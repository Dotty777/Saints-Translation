## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Direct address to Jesus, including the heavenly voice and the messenger quotation, uses Thou/Thee/Thy; ordinary commands use you or an unexpressed imperative subject. Keep become, the fishing image, repeated immediately, ongoing baptism and the heavens being torn open. Restore the explicit synagogue entry in 1:21. Son of God (1:1) and kneeling (1:40) are retained where NA28 uses single brackets. Moved with compassion follows the already recorded NA28 correction at 1:41; says retains that verse’s historical present.

Pending: Review Son of God, kneeling, the compassion/anger variant, into Him in 1:10, and the supplied appointed/net wording. Divine-address capitalization expresses this edition’s convention, not the speaker’s belief. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize third-person verb forms and direct address to Jesus. Preserve the forgiveness/passive clauses, singular mat and plural friends, bridegroom repetition, wineskins, the Sabbath/man reversal, and the explicit Abiathar designation without harmonizing it. Existing narrative past remains an English presentation of the Greek story; no mechanical tense substitution is made.

Pending: Review the referent of his house in 2:15, the make their way idiom in 2:23 and the chronological force of in the time of Abiathar. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Include whom He also named apostles (3:14) and Thy sisters (3:32), which NA28 prints in single brackets; their textual uncertainty remains in these notes. Retain the bracketed Twelve appointment at 3:16. Use people for the human-reference idiom sons of humankind at 3:28; retain sins/blasphemies and the following qualification. Preserve eternal sin, the divided kingdom/house repetitions, bread and the relatives’ reported speech.

Pending: Review the single-bracketed clauses, the name/names form at 3:17, who His own people and they denote at 3:21, and the interpretation of eternal sin. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Retain the repeated sowing/hearing language, seed yields and lamp personification. Clarify the boat setting in 4:1 and the ripe-crop idiom in 4:29 while preserving sends in the sickle. Use modern third-person verbs; address Jesus with Thou. Preserve the purpose and forgiveness clauses in 4:12 and the forceful commands to wind and sea.

Pending: Review the purpose of the parables in 4:12, the modius grain-measure term at 4:21, the full/full grain grammatical variation in 4:28, and the crop idiom. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserve Gerasenes, the singular/plural movement of the possessed speaker, about two thousand pigs, twelve years and the repeated immediately. Keep Talitha koum and the supplied translation together. In the boat (5:21) and the second immediately (5:42) are retained despite NA28 single brackets.

Pending: Review overhearing/ignoring at 5:36, the source of her blood image, saved in its local healing context, and the variants noted above. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Retain carpenter, the family names and the statement that He could do no mighty work there, without harmonization. Preserve staff/sandals, two by two, food numbers, repeated giving, the fourth watch and intended to pass by. His (6:41), the loaves (6:44) and intensified astonishment (6:51) remain with their textual uncertainty recorded.

Pending: Review the consequential daughter/Herodias variant and the force of repeatedly, the breadth of carpenter, and the interpretation of pass by and It is I. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.
