## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Retain Thou/Thee/Thine and the matching second-person forms in direct prayer; modernize third-person verbs and ordinary address to despisers. Render Thou hast appointed at 1:12 to reflect the perfect result and Thou wilt not be able to at 1:13 to make the future explicit. Preserve the judge takes without a supplied object, scattered law, despisers/vanish, Chaldean warriors, wolves of Arabia, tyrants as toys, spirit/propitiation/my God, He formed me/his discipline, fish/creeping things, distinct fishing nets, choice foods and continual killing.

Pending: Review the judge’s unstated object, claim at 1:7, collective nation/person-number treatment, spirit and propitiation at 1:11 and the pronouns/discipline clause at 1:12. No bribe, Rock, moral harmonization or identification of the spirit as the Holy Spirit is added. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person forms; restore the question boundary and explicit future They will say at 2:6, with the short quotation nested within the outer speech. Retain rock, speak within me, boxwood tablet and pursuit, masculine he/him for the awaited one, coming he will come, both first-person possessives and shall at 2:4, Hades/death, heavy collar, biters and him-to-you shift, reciprocal plunder, beetle, water covering them, turbid overthrow/caves, cup/tossing/shaking, false appearance/mute idols and revere the Lord.

Pending: Review boxwood as a material gloss, the pursued object, faith/trust/faithfulness and the relation of My at 2:4, the beetle, the referent of them at 2:14 and caves/turbid overthrow at 2:15. Hebrews, Galatians and other reception preserve their own wording; no alternative possessive or sea object is imported. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize only third-person arrives/says/scatters; retain the direct prayer’s Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine, its unusual imagery and all Diapsalma markers. Preserve two living creatures and years/season, Teman and wooded mountain, horns in hands and mighty love, preceding word/sandals, everlasting journeys once at 3:7, riding as salvation, bow/scepters, peoples/abyss/appearance, missiles, plural anointed ones, death/heads/bonds, poor eater/bridles, horses disturbing water, prayer/trembling/sojourning, agricultural loss, rejoicing and completion/heights/His song.

Pending: Review the creatures’ identification, mighty love, the change of speaker at 3:9–10, abyss/appearance, ruler/bridle clauses, bodily condition/sojourning and completion at 3:19. No deer, plague, named Paran, singular anointed figure or fixed typological interpretation is substituted for the selected Greek. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
