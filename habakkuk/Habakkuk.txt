HABAKKUK 1

1:1 The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.
1:2 How long, Lord, shall I cry and Thou wilt not listen? I shall cry to Thee while being wronged, and Thou wilt not save?
1:3 Why didst Thou show me toil and pain, to look upon misery and impiety? Judgment has come against me, and the judge takes.
1:4 Therefore law has been scattered, and judgment is not brought to completion; the impious oppresses the righteous, so judgment will go forth distorted.
1:5 Look, you despisers, observe, marvel at wonders and vanish! For I am doing a work in your days that you will certainly not believe if someone recounts it.
1:6 For look, I am raising up against you the Chaldean warriors, the bitter and swift nation, who traverse the breadth of the earth to possess dwellings not their own.
1:7 Fearsome and conspicuous are they; their judgment will come from themselves, and their claim will proceed from themselves.
1:8 Their horses will leap beyond leopards and be fiercer than the wolves of Arabia. Their horsemen will ride out and rush from afar, flying like an eagle eager to eat.
1:9 An end will come upon the impious who stand facing them in opposition, and they will gather captives like sand.
1:10 They will revel among kings, and tyrants will be their toys; they will mock every fortress, throw up a mound and seize it.
1:11 Then the spirit will change and pass through and make propitiation. This strength belongs to my God.
1:12 Art Thou not from the beginning, Lord, my holy God? We shall certainly not die. Lord, Thou hast appointed him for judgment, and He formed me to reprove his discipline.
1:13 Thine eye is pure, not to see evil things, and Thou wilt not be able to look upon distress. Why dost Thou look upon despisers? Wilt Thou remain silent when the impious swallows the righteous?
1:14 Thou wilt make people like the fish of the sea and like creeping things with no leader.
1:15 They hauled up an entire catch with a hook, dragged it in a casting net and gathered it in their seines; therefore they will rejoice and their heart will be glad.
1:16 Therefore they will sacrifice to their seine and burn incense to their casting net, for through them they fattened their portion, and their foods are choice.
1:17 Therefore they will cast their casting net and will not spare nations from continual killing.

HABAKKUK 2

2:1 I will stand at my watch and step onto a rock; I will look out to see what He will speak within me and what I shall answer concerning my reproof.
2:2 The Lord answered me: “Write a vision clearly on a boxwood tablet, so that the one reading these things may pursue them.
2:3 For the vision is still for a season, and it will rise toward the end and not toward emptiness. If he delays, wait for him, for coming he will come and certainly will not linger.
2:4 If he draws back, My soul has no pleasure in him; but the righteous one shall live by My faithfulness.
2:5 But the wine-soaked, contemptuous, boastful man will accomplish nothing. He widened his appetite like Hades and is like death, never filled; he will gather all nations and receive all peoples to himself.
2:6 Will not all these take up a parable against him and a riddle for his account? They will say, ‘Woe to the one multiplying for himself what is not his—how long?—and heavily weighting his collar!’
2:7 For suddenly those biting him will rise, and those plotting against you will awake; you will become their plunder.
2:8 Because you despoiled many nations, all the remaining peoples will despoil you, because of human bloodshed and impiety against land, city and all its inhabitants.
2:9 O you who acquire evil gain for your house, setting your nest on high to escape the hand of evils!
2:10 You planned shame for your house, brought many peoples to an end, and your soul sinned.
2:11 For a stone will cry from a wall, and a beetle from wood will utter these things.
2:12 Woe to the one building a city with bloodshed and preparing a city with injustices!
2:13 Are these things not from the Lord Almighty? Many peoples failed in fire, and many nations lost heart.
2:14 For the earth will be filled with knowing the Lord’s glory, as water will cover them.
2:15 O you who make your neighbor drink with a turbid overthrow and intoxicate him, so that you may look upon their caves!
2:16 Drink, you too, a fullness of dishonor instead of glory; be tossed and shaken! The cup of the Lord’s right hand has come around to you, and dishonor has gathered upon your glory.
2:17 For Lebanon’s impiety will cover you, and the misery of wild animals will terrify you, because of human bloodshed and impiety against land, city and all its inhabitants.
2:18 What use is a carved image, that they carved it? They formed it as a cast object, a false appearance, because its maker trusted his creation, making mute idols.
2:19 Woe to the one saying to wood, ‘Wake and rise!’ and to stone, ‘Be lifted up!’ It is an appearance, a plate of gold and silver, and no breath at all is in it.
2:20 But the Lord is in His holy sanctuary; let all the earth revere Him.”

HABAKKUK 3

3:1 A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, with a song.
3:2 Lord, I have heard Thy report and was afraid; I considered Thy works and was astonished. In the midst of two living creatures Thou wilt be known; when the years draw near Thou wilt be recognized; when the season arrives Thou wilt be revealed. When my soul is troubled, in wrath Thou wilt remember mercy.
3:3 God will come from Teman, the Holy One from a mountain shaded and thickly wooded. Diapsalma. His excellence covered the heavens, and the earth is full of His praise.
3:4 His brightness will be like light; horns are in His hands, and He established mighty love of His strength.
3:5 A word will go before Him, and He will go out, His feet in sandals.
3:6 He stood, and the earth shook; He looked, and nations melted. Mountains were crushed violently; everlasting hills melted.
3:7 I saw His everlasting journeys in place of toils; the dwellings of Ethiopians will be terrified, and the tents of the land of Midian.
3:8 Wast Thou angry with rivers, Lord? Was Thy fury against rivers, or Thine onrush against the sea, that Thou wilt mount Thy horses and Thy riding is salvation?
3:9 Thou wilt surely bend Thy bow against scepters, says the Lord. Diapsalma. The earth will be torn apart by rivers.
3:10 Peoples will see Thee and travail, as He scatters waters in His passage. The abyss gave its voice, the height of its appearance.
3:11 The sun was raised, and the moon stood in its appointed place. Thy missiles will go into light, into the brightness of Thy flashing weapons.
3:12 With threat Thou wilt diminish the earth, and in fury Thou wilt bring nations down.
3:13 Thou didst go out for Thy people’s salvation, to save Thine anointed ones. Thou didst cast death upon the heads of lawless people and didst raise bonds up to the neck. Diapsalma.
3:14 Thou didst cut the heads of rulers in bewilderment; they will be shaken in it. They will open their bridles like a poor person eating secretly.
3:15 Thou didst drive Thy horses into the sea as they troubled much water.
3:16 I kept watch, and my belly was terrified at the sound of the prayer of my lips. Trembling entered my bones, and my bodily condition beneath me was troubled. I will rest on a day of affliction, to go up to the people of my sojourning.
3:17 For the fig tree will not bear fruit, and vines will have no produce; the olive’s labor will fail, and fields will not produce food. Sheep have failed for lack of food, and there are no cattle at feeding troughs.
3:18 But I will exult in the Lord; I will rejoice in God my Savior.
3:19 The Lord God is my strength; He will set my feet toward completion and lead me onto the heights, to conquer in His song.
