## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved the repeated gospel, grace, human/divine contrast, ages, flesh and blood, womb and named itinerary. Corrected 1:3 from inherited God the Father and our Lord to God our Father and the Lord, following displayed NA28. Included bracketed God at 1:15; retained bracketed Christ at 1:6 and to you at 1:8. Smoothed persecution and recognition clauses; preserved three years and fifteen days.

Pending: Review revelation of Jesus Christ and reveal His Son in me, the call chronology, the exception concerning James and the pronoun placement variant in 1:3. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved the running, slavery/freedom, pillars, face-to-face, rebuilding and crucifixion images, all works/law/faith repetitions and negations. Modernized does, smoothed the topic opening in 2:6 and rendered plural right hands in 2:9. Included bracketed also at 2:13. Kept the inherited provisional faith in Christ and faith in the Son of God readings; Christ’s faithfulness remains a live interpretive alternative. Retained the existing quotation boundaries and placement of crucified with Christ at 2:19.

Pending: Review the broken construction in 2:4–6, faith/faithfulness genitives in 2:16 and 2:20, the extent of the reported speech to Cephas and the historical visit chronology. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved every law/faith/promise repetition, hearing with faith, curse/tree, the seed/seeds argument and four hundred and thirty years. Modernized does/has and rendered mighty deeds as acts of power. Smoothed the terse mediator phrase to not for one alone without adding a specific party. Retained guardian until Christ, of God at 3:21 (bracketed in NA28), the provisional faith in Jesus Christ at 3:22, and the distinctive no male and female at 3:28.

Pending: Review hearing with faith, suffering/experience in 3:4, covenant/testament, the mediator saying, faith/faithfulness in 3:22, guardian until/unto Christ and the quotation context of male and female. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved elements, fullness of time, born of a woman, adoption as sons, our hearts, Abba Father, slavery/freedom and the labor/birth images. Smoothed the trial clause at 4:14 and modernized third-person verbs in quotations. Retained the allegorical statement, Hagar/Sinai, Jerusalem above and you at 4:28.

Pending: Review elements as principles/powers and related senses, the bodily weakness without diagnosing Paul, blessedness in 4:15, the passive zeal construction and the Hagar/Sinai text and interpretation. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserved freedom/yoke, running, leaven, the cross, serving as slaves, biting/devouring, flesh/Spirit and the singular fruit list. Replaced ordinary ye and archaic third-person verbs; clarified dough in the leaven image and smoothed desire wording. Included bracketed Jesus at 5:24. Kept faithfulness at 5:22 provisionally, with faith as an alternative, and retained the force of the difficult 5:12 saying.

Pending: Review faith working through love, the future/conditional nuance of 5:11, the cutting/castration saying, base of operations, the vice-list senses and faith/faithfulness in 5:22. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Preserved the shift from plural spiritual people to singular watching yourself at 6:1, mutual burdens and individual load, sowing/reaping, flesh/Spirit, household, large letters, crucifixion and marks. Replaced ordinary ye with you and kept the future you will fulfill at 6:2. Smoothed doing good and the elliptical new-creation contrast. Retained future will keep in step, the Israel of God wording and the final Amen.

Pending: Review the nuances of burden/load, through which/whom in 6:14, the Israel of God, rule, the marks of Jesus and the large-letter saying without diagnosing its cause. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.
