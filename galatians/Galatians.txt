GALATIANS 1

1:1 Paul, an apostle, not from human beings nor through a human being, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised Him from the dead,
1:2 and all the brothers with me, to the Churches of Galatia:
1:3 grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ,
1:4 who gave Himself for our sins, to rescue us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,
1:5 to whom be the glory for the ages of ages. Amen.
1:6 I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting the One who called you in the grace of Christ for a different gospel,
1:7 which is not another gospel, except that there are some who trouble you and want to distort the gospel of Christ.
1:8 But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim to you a gospel contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.
1:9 As we have said before, so now I say again: if anyone proclaims to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.
1:10 For am I now seeking the approval of human beings, or of God? Or am I seeking to please human beings? If I were still pleasing human beings, I would not be Christ’s slave.
1:11 For I make known to you, brothers, that the gospel proclaimed by me is not of human origin.
1:12 For I neither received it from a human being nor was taught it, but received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ.
1:13 For you have heard of my former conduct in Judaism, how I persecuted the Church of God beyond measure and ravaged it,
1:14 and I advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, being far more zealous for the traditions of my ancestors.
1:15 But when God, who set me apart from my mother’s womb and called me through His grace, was pleased
1:16 to reveal His Son in me, so that I might proclaim Him among the nations, I did not immediately consult flesh and blood,
1:17 nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away to Arabia and returned again to Damascus.
1:18 Then, after three years, I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas, and I stayed with him fifteen days.
1:19 But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord’s brother.
1:20 Now in what I am writing to you, behold, before God, I am not lying.
1:21 Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.
1:22 But I was unknown by sight to the Churches of Judea that are in Christ.
1:23 They only kept hearing, “The one who once persecuted us now proclaims the faith he once ravaged.”
1:24 And they glorified God because of me.

GALATIANS 2

2:1 Then, after fourteen years, I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along as well.
2:2 I went up in accordance with a revelation, and I set before them the gospel I proclaim among the nations, privately before those held in esteem, lest somehow I was running, or had run, in vain.
2:3 But not even Titus, who was with me, was compelled to be circumcised, though he was a Greek.
2:4 Yet because of the false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out our freedom which we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might enslave us—
2:5 we did not yield to them in submission even for an hour, so that the truth of the gospel might remain with you.
2:6 But as for those held to be something—whatever they once were makes no difference to me; God does not favor a person’s outward standing—those held in esteem added nothing to me.
2:7 On the contrary, seeing that I had been entrusted with the gospel for the uncircumcised, just as Peter with that for the circumcised,
2:8 for the One who worked through Peter for an apostleship to the circumcised worked also through me for the nations,
2:9 and recognizing the grace given to me, James and Cephas and John, who were regarded as pillars, gave me and Barnabas their right hands in fellowship, so that we should go to the nations and they to the circumcised.
2:10 Only we were to remember the poor, the very thing I was also eager to do.
2:11 But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he stood condemned.
2:12 For before certain people came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles. But when they came, he began drawing back and separating himself, fearing those of the circumcision.
2:13 And the other Jews also joined him in hypocrisy, so that even Barnabas was carried away by their hypocrisy.
2:14 But when I saw that they were not walking straight in line with the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas before them all, “If you, though a Jew, live like the Gentiles and not like the Jews, how can you compel the Gentiles to live as Jews?
2:15 “We are Jews by birth and not sinners from the Gentiles,
2:16 yet knowing that a human being is not justified by works of law but through faith in Jesus Christ, we too believed in Christ Jesus, so that we might be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of law, because by works of law no flesh will be justified.
2:17 But if, in seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves were also found to be sinners, is Christ then a servant of sin? May it never be!
2:18 For if I rebuild the things I tore down, I show myself to be a transgressor.
2:19 For through law I died to law, so that I might live to God. I have been crucified with Christ.
2:20 And I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me.
2:21 I do not set aside the grace of God. For if righteousness comes through law, then Christ died for nothing.”

GALATIANS 3

3:1 O foolish Galatians, who bewitched you, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed as crucified?
3:2 This alone I want to learn from you: did you receive the Spirit by works of law or by hearing with faith?
3:3 Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being brought to completion by the flesh?
3:4 Did you suffer so much in vain—if indeed it was in vain?
3:5 So then, does the One who supplies you with the Spirit and performs acts of power among you do so by works of law or by hearing with faith?
3:6 Just as Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.
3:7 Know, then, that those who are of faith are the sons of Abraham.
3:8 And Scripture, foreseeing that God justifies the nations by faith, proclaimed the gospel beforehand to Abraham: “In you all the nations will be blessed.”
3:9 So then, those who are of faith are blessed with believing Abraham.
3:10 For all who are of works of law are under a curse, for it is written, “Accursed is everyone who does not abide by all the things written in the book of the law, to do them.”
3:11 Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by law, because “The righteous one will live by faith.”
3:12 But the law is not of faith; rather, “The one who does these things will live by them.”
3:13 Christ bought us free from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, because it is written, “Accursed is everyone who hangs upon a tree,”
3:14 so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the nations, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.
3:15 Brothers, I speak in human terms: even when a human covenant has been ratified, no one sets it aside or adds provisions to it.
3:16 Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed. It does not say, “And to seeds,” as concerning many, but as concerning one, “And to your seed,” who is Christ.
3:17 What I mean is this: the law, which came four hundred and thirty years later, does not invalidate a covenant previously ratified by God, so as to nullify the promise.
3:18 For if the inheritance is from law, it is no longer from promise. But God has graciously given it to Abraham through promise.
3:19 Why, then, the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the seed should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.
3:20 Now a mediator is not for one alone, but God is one.
3:21 Is the law, then, against the promises of God? May it never be! For if a law had been given that could give life, righteousness really would have come from law.
3:22 But Scripture confined everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.
3:23 Now before faith came, we were guarded under law, confined until the faith that was to be revealed.
3:24 So the law became our guardian until Christ, so that we might be justified by faith.
3:25 But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.
3:26 For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.
3:27 For as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.
3:28 There is neither Jew nor Greek; there is neither slave nor free; there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.
3:29 And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s seed, heirs according to promise.

GALATIANS 4

4:1 Now I say that as long as the heir is a child, he differs in no way from a slave, though he is master of everything.
4:2 Rather, he is under guardians and stewards until the date appointed by his father.
4:3 So also we, when we were children, were enslaved under the elements of the world.
4:4 But when the fullness of time came, God sent forth His Son, born of a woman, born under law,
4:5 to redeem those under law, that we might receive adoption as sons.
4:6 And because you are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, “Abba, Father!”
4:7 So you are no longer a slave but a son; and if a son, then also an heir through God.
4:8 But at that time, not knowing God, you were enslaved to those that by nature are not gods.
4:9 Now, however, having come to know God—or rather, having been known by God—how do you turn back again to the weak and impoverished elements, to which you want to be enslaved all over again?
4:10 You carefully observe days and months and seasons and years.
4:11 I fear for you, lest somehow I have labored over you in vain.
4:12 Become as I am, because I too became as you are, brothers; I beg you. You did me no wrong.
4:13 And you know that it was because of a weakness of the flesh that I first proclaimed the gospel to you.
4:14 And though my flesh was a trial for you, you did not despise or reject me, but you welcomed me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.
4:15 Where, then, is your sense of blessedness? For I testify to you that, if possible, you would have dug out your eyes and given them to me.
4:16 So have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?
4:17 They are zealous for you, but not for good; they want to shut you out, so that you may be zealous for them.
4:18 But it is good to be sought zealously for a good purpose at all times, and not only when I am present with you,
4:19 my children, for whom I am again in labor until Christ is formed in you.
4:20 I wish I could be present with you now and change my tone, because I am perplexed about you.
4:21 Tell me, you who want to be under law, do you not listen to the law?
4:22 For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and one by the free woman.
4:23 But the one by the slave woman was born according to the flesh, and the one by the free woman through promise.
4:24 These things are being interpreted allegorically, for these women are two covenants: one from Mount Sinai, bearing children into slavery, which is Hagar.
4:25 Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is enslaved with her children.
4:26 But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.
4:27 For it is written, “Rejoice, barren woman who does not bear; break out and cry aloud, you who are not in labor, because the children of the deserted woman are more numerous than those of the woman who has a husband.”
4:28 Now you, brothers, are children of promise, like Isaac.
4:29 But just as then the one born according to the flesh persecuted the one born according to the Spirit, so it is also now.
4:30 But what does Scripture say? “Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman shall certainly not inherit with the son of the free woman.”
4:31 Therefore, brothers, we are not children of a slave woman but of the free woman.

GALATIANS 5

5:1 For freedom Christ set us free. Stand firm, therefore, and do not be held again under a yoke of slavery.
5:2 Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you receive circumcision, Christ will be of no benefit to you.
5:3 And I testify again to every man who receives circumcision that he is obligated to do the whole law.
5:4 You who seek to be justified by law have been severed from Christ; you have fallen from grace.
5:5 For we, by the Spirit, through faith, eagerly await the hope of righteousness.
5:6 For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any strength, but faith working through love.
5:7 You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?
5:8 This persuasion is not from the One who calls you.
5:9 A little leaven leavens the whole batch of dough.
5:10 I am confident about you in the Lord that you will think nothing else. But the one troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever he may be.
5:11 But I, brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished.
5:12 I wish those who unsettle you would even castrate themselves!
5:13 For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not make freedom a base of operations for the flesh, but through love serve one another as slaves.
5:14 For the whole law is fulfilled in one saying: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
5:15 But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.
5:16 But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will certainly not fulfill the desire of the flesh.
5:17 For the flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit what is against the flesh; for these oppose one another, so that you do not do the things you want.
5:18 But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under law.
5:19 Now the works of the flesh are evident: sexual immorality, impurity, licentiousness,
5:20 idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambitions, divisions, factions,
5:21 envies, drunkenness, revelries, and things like these. I tell you beforehand, just as I told you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.
5:22 But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,
5:23 gentleness, self-control. Against such things there is no law.
5:24 And those who belong to Christ Jesus crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.
5:25 If we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit.
5:26 Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.

GALATIANS 6

6:1 Brothers, even if a person is overtaken in some transgression, you who are spiritual are to restore such a person in a spirit of gentleness, watching yourself, lest you too be tempted.
6:2 Carry one another’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.
6:3 For if anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.
6:4 But let each test his own work, and then he will have his reason for boasting in relation to himself alone, and not in relation to someone else.
6:5 For each will carry his own load.
6:6 Let the one instructed in the word share all good things with the one who instructs.
6:7 Do not be deceived; God is not mocked. For whatever a person sows, that he will also reap.
6:8 For the one who sows into his own flesh will reap corruption from the flesh, but the one who sows into the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.
6:9 And let us not grow weary in doing good, for in the proper season we will reap, if we do not give up.
6:10 So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, especially to those of the household of faith.
6:11 See with what large letters I have written to you in my own hand.
6:12 All who want to make a good outward show in the flesh compel you to receive circumcision, only so that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.
6:13 For even those who receive circumcision do not themselves keep the law, but they want you to receive circumcision so that they may boast in your flesh.
6:14 But may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.
6:15 For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but a new creation does.
6:16 And as many as will keep in step with this rule, peace and mercy be upon them, and upon the Israel of God.
6:17 From now on, let no one cause me trouble, for I carry the marks of Jesus in my body.
6:18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
