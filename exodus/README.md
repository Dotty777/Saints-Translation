# Exodus — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 40 chapters, containing 1,165 existing verse units, received an English editorial pass and were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. 531 verse units changed; 634 retained their wording. This is a provisional reading edition, with specialist verification still pending.

The newer *Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf* takes precedence over the older *Saints English Founding Style Guide*, particularly their different pronoun rules. Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your. Accepted Genesis 1–11 provides the working English benchmark. The PDF mentions Exodus 15 as a voice benchmark but supplies no accepted full text of that chapter; this revision does not claim otherwise.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 is the user's authorized provisional base. Selected passages were inspected in the [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/EXO.1). The full apparatus and Göttingen text were not obtained or collated. Every source-check claim is limited by the scope recorded in source-checks.json. Earlier study records and notes are historical evidence, not new verification claims.

## Preservation and explicit corrections

All existing verse IDs, links, navigation, earlier notes, images and scripts were preserved. Exodus 15 retains every existing poetic line break. The combined export prefixes repeated note IDs with their chapter only to keep internal links unique; staging IDs and all verse IDs remain unchanged.

The 1,165-unit structure retains 28:29a and 35:12a, all original gaps, and the Greek arrangement of chapters 36–39. No replacement verses were invented. The existing Decalogue order is retained.

Exodus 1:7 and 1:12 restore the source's doubled intensifier; 15:16 restores its second possessive. Exodus 25:22 removes a duplicated English word. These are documented corrections, not new manuscript choices. Difficult constructions were retained provisionally and identified for review. In particular, the from-there expression at 29:42 and 30:6 remains a question where the inherited English does not express it separately.

Saints Translation literal-smooth, comparison streams and their notes were not edited. The three backed-up reference pages passed unchanged-content checks. This archive is an Exodus backup, not a full-site export. No live-site changes were made.
