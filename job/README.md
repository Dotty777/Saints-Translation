# Job — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 42 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 1083 verse units received an English editorial review: 450 changed and 633 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/JOB.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize God’s address to the devil and ordinary human address; retain Thou/Thee/Thy when the devil speaks directly to God. Smooth the syntax of Job’s blessing while retaining its repeated Lord and the departure-there image.

Pending: 1:3 eastern nobility/enterprises, 1:5 additional calf, 1:10 enclosure imagery, 1:11 blessing/curse euphemism and full apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize ordinary speech and third-person verbs; preserve direct divine address at 2:5, skin for skin, Job’s wife’s full expanded speech, the dung heap and the friends’ royal titles.

Pending: 2:3 belongings, 2:5 blessing/curse euphemism, 2:9e word to the Lord, and the critical signs in the publisher’s running text require apparatus review.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize third-person verbs and my eyes; preserve the singular day-curser, sea creature, morning star, swords, age-old ones, tax collector and repeated negations.

Pending: 3:17 kindled/burned-out sense, 3:18 age-old ones, 3:23 rest and surrounding syntax remain for specialist review.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize ordinary address, third-person verbs and my ear/my eyes; smooth you admonished. Preserve the ant-lion, dragons, houses of clay and the inherited accusation and vision sequence. Final syntax review aligned encouraged with admonished.

Pending: 4:2–6 conditional syntax and foolishness, 4:11 ant-lion, 4:12 extraordinary things, 4:18 angel wording and 4:19 moth image need specialist review.

**Chapter 5.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person descriptions of God. Retain the wishes against the wicked, vultures, hand of iron, lash of the tongue, numerical progression and threshing-floor image.

Pending: 5:4 weaker people, 5:11 rousing the perished, 5:17–18 tense shifts, 5:24 tent livelihood and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Modernize ordinary plural address, third-person verbs and my anger. Preserve the weighing, arrows drinking blood, lion smell, city/grave, snow/ice and throat images, the negative petition at 6:9 and repeated worthless words.

Pending: 6:7 food and stench, 6:9 negative petition, 6:10 city/grave, 6:16 ice comparison, 6:20 cities/possessions and 6:29 righteous-person reference need specialist review.

**Chapter 7.** Retain Thou/Thee/Thy in direct address to God; modernize third-person sees and my eye, and use on my couch. Preserve chatter, dragon, saliva, accuser, release from breath and bones from death.

Pending: 7:5 clods/discharge, 7:13 private speech, 7:15 release, 7:18 future/subjunctive force, 7:20 accuser and 7:21 dawn need specialist review.

**Chapter 8.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; preserve first/last things, papyrus drinking, moist growth from decay, stones, the disowning place and the refusal of an ungodly gift.

Pending: 8:12 plant drinking, 8:16 decay, 8:18–19 subject/referents, 8:20 gift and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 9.** Retain direct divine address in the quoted question and Job’s appeal. Modernize third-person verbs and my injuries; use wears the mountains out. Preserve constellations, sea creatures, singular/plural shift at 9:25, washing, robe, mediator and rod imagery.

Pending: 9:3 speaker/referents, 9:13 anger, 9:24 its judges, 9:25 plural they, 9:33–35 mediator and consciousness require specialist review.

**Chapter 10.** Preserve the sustained direct address to God, modernizing only the third-person human verbs and my examination. Retain milk/cheese, clay, sinews, lion, womb/tomb and the difficult accusations.

Pending: 10:3 first-person accusation, 10:8 changing, 10:13 construction, 10:16–17 examination, and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 11.** Modernize ordinary address while preserving Thou in the quotation addressed to God. Clarify speak at such length, open His lips to speak with you and sins you committed; retain swimming in words, wild donkey, doubled wisdom and morning-star prayer.

Pending: 11:2 blessing, 11:5 question, 11:6 doubled wisdom, 11:13 tense and 11:17 prayer/morning star need specialist review.

**Chapter 12.** Modernize address to people and third-person verbs. Preserve the heart, speaking animals/earth, ear/throat, captive counselors and priests, seating kings and the inherited tense shift to bound.

Pending: 12:2–6 abbreviated clauses, 12:15–24 tense/aspect choices, 12:18 seating kings and 12:21 healing the lowly require specialist review.

**Chapter 13.** Modernize ordinary address, third-person verbs and my eye/my ear; retain divine address from 13:20 onward. Keep both exchanges initiated by God at 13:22, the twice-repeated Listen, physicians, teeth, leaf, roots and wineskin.

Pending: 13:9–10 attaching/admiring persons, 13:15 Mighty One and subduing, 13:19 failing, 13:25 wary and 13:27–28 referents require specialist review.

**Chapter 14.** Retain direct divine address and modernize third-person descriptions. Preserve the tree’s scent of water, heaven stitched together, Hades, the affirmative hope at 14:14, bag of sins and flowing waters.

Pending: 14:12 stitched-heaven clause, 14:14 life/restoration statement, 14:17 unwilling transgression and 14:19 flat earth-surface wording need specialist review.

**Chapter 15.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; render the emphatic charge as spoken far too much. Keep punishment for few sins, elders, hands of iron, vultures, shield-back, thigh collar, botanical images and the wish forms. Use burst/brought in 15:13.

Pending: 15:2 spirit/belly, 15:10 age, 15:18 fathers, 15:24 general and 15:27 collar image need specialist review.

**Chapter 16.** Modernize ordinary speech while retaining Thou in the brief divine address at 16:8. Use my eyelids/my eye; preserve shifts between He, Thou and they, comforters of evils, eyes as points, kidneys, gall, skin and the heavenly witness.

Pending: 16:3 singular address amid plurals, 16:6 pain statement, 16:8 falsehood, 16:9–10 subjects, 16:19 witness and 16:21 hearing need specialist review.

**Chapter 17.** Retain direct divine address at 17:4 and 17:6; use my eyes and all of you. Preserve stolen belongings, hardened eyes, stench, Hades, kinship with death/decay and shifts from they to we.

Pending: 17:3–5 referents, 17:12 light near darkness and 17:16 number shifts need specialist review.

**Chapter 18.** Make the negative question idiomatic as How long before you stop? Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs. Retain thirsty ones, branches of feet, royal charge and the affirmative name clause.

Pending: 18:9 thirsty ones, 18:13 branches, 18:14 royal charge, 18:17 affirmative name and 18:18 subject need specialist review.

**Chapter 19.** Modernize ordinary address and verbs while retaining the Greek addition 4a, concubines’ sons, bones held in teeth, repeated Have mercy, everlasting deliverer wording and covering at 19:29.

Pending: 19:12 trials, 19:20 teeth, 19:25–27 deliverance/resurrection syntax and 19:29 covering/substance need specialist review.

**Chapter 20.** Modernize ordinary address and verbs. Mark the Greek plural in the second clause of 20:2 with you all, while leaving the first address singular. Preserve asp, dragons, angel, gristly meat, iron hand, bronze bow, walking lightning and unkindled fire.

Pending: 20:3 question, 20:4 temporal phrase, 20:9 eye/place, 20:17 milk/pasture wording and 20:23–28 wish forms need specialist review.

**Chapter 21.** Modernize ordinary speech and third-person verbs, retain Thy in the quotation to the Lord, and correct covered to the past form in 21:26. Preserve repeated Listen, everlasting sheep, Hades rest, murder judgment, coffin vigil and sweet pebbles.

Pending: 21:2 negative consolation, 21:16 subject, 21:21 divided months and 21:30–33 relief/vigil/pebbles require specialist review.

**Chapter 22.** Modernize address to Job, remove awkward did plus infinitive in affirmative narrative clauses, and preserve past/perfect shifts. Retain river foundations, Ophir torrents, hardening/endurance, refined silver and final imperative. Final syntax review aligned removed with took in 22:6.

Pending: 22:3 conditional tense, 22:16 foundations, 22:23 perfect, 22:24 missing object and 22:29–30 referents/imperative need specialist review.

**Chapter 23.** Use my own and modern third-person verbs. Preserve left/right, first/last, softened heart, inherited time shifts, added 15a and the existing absence of a 23:14 unit.

Pending: 23:3 end, 23:8 first/last, 23:9 wrapped right and 23:11–12 divided syntax need specialist review.

**Chapter 24.** Modernize remaining third-person verbs without smoothing away changes of subject or tense. Retain hours, shepherd with flock, breast, rock shelter, shadow of death, wood and mallow images.

Pending: 24:5 beyond me, 24:12 infants, 24:14 and 17 subjects, 24:19 armful and 24:21–24 pronouns/aspect need specialist review.

**Chapter 25.** Modernize third-person verbs and retain the short chapter’s prelude, raiders, commanded moon, decay and worm language.

Pending: 25:2 elliptical whole, 25:3 raiders/ambush and 25:6 Leave it require specialist review.

**Chapter 26.** Use ordinary you for Job’s answer to his interlocutor and modern third-person verbs. Preserve giants, Hades, earth upon nothing, command circled over waters, wounded sea creature, killed rebellious dragon and drop of speech.

Pending: 26:2–3 rhetorical direction, 26:5 neighbors, 26:9 throne face and 26:14 thunder syntax need specialist review.

**Chapter 27.** Modernize ordinary address, third-person verbs and my innocence/my enemies. Keep the oath, divine breath, empty things upon empty things, moths/spider and shifting subjects at the end.

Pending: 27:8 holding on, 27:13 possession, 27:18 house comparison and 27:19–23 tense/subjects need specialist review.

**Chapter 28.** Modernize third-person verbs and my eye. Join 28:25 to 26 with a semicolon and close the outer quotation at the end. Preserve repeated wisdom questions, sappheiros/gabis/topazion, gold/glass, earth/fire and divine acts.

Pending: 28:3–4 stone/torrent syntax, 28:6 mound, 28:15 locked treasure, 28:18 gabis and inner things, and 28:26 sounds/shaking require specialist review.

**Chapter 29.** Use My age and retain the other inherited wording. Preserve lamp, butter/milk, blessing repetition, molars, palm trunk, bow in His hand and king among lightly equipped soldiers.

Pending: 29:2 month, 29:4 weighty, 29:11 eye turning, 29:18 age, 29:20 bow referent and 29:25 soldiers require specialist review.

**Chapter 30.** Retain direct address to God in 30:19–22 and modernize third-person wishes and has. Keep salt plants, resounding things, shoot, quiver, shifting subjects, sirens and ostriches.

Pending: 30:2–4 completion/resounding, 30:7 resounding, 30:12 shoot, 30:20 they and 30:24 self-mastery need specialist review.

**Chapter 31.** Use my before nouns, modern third-person verbs and capitals at selected sentence starts in the oath. Preserve its long conditional sequence, infants, virgin, fiery adultery, balance, covenant, debt bond and agricultural imprecations.

Pending: 31:12 aspect, 31:16 need, 31:18 implicit objects, 31:23 taking hold, 31:26 sun/moon, 31:31 flesh and 31:35–39 debt/landowner syntax require specialist review.

**Chapter 32.** Modernize ordinary address, third-person verbs and my own/my understanding. Preserve Elihu’s ancestry, three friends, spirit and breath, fermenting wineskin, smith’s bellows and moths.

Pending: 32:3 condemnation, 32:9 categorical age statement, 32:11–16 speech transitions and 32:18 destroying spirit require specialist review.

**Chapter 33.** Modernize address to Job, third-person verbs and my ears; end 33:21 with a period before the following past-tense statement. Preserve clay, wooden restraint, thousand death-bearing angels, plaster, marrow, confession and three ways.

Pending: 33:14–18 dream/aspect, 33:23 angel and human subjects, 33:26 righteousness recipient and 33:29 three ways need specialist review.

**Chapter 34.** Modernize ordinary address and verbs; preserve Thou in the quoted appeal to the Strong One at 34:32. Retain no-sinning statement at 34:8, singular/plural shifts, hypocritical king, pledges and the concluding we/us. Reorder the emphatic prayer as Thou, show me.

Pending: 34:6 arrow, 34:8 negation, 34:18–20 subjects, 34:23 omitted object, 34:31–33 pledges/recompense and 34:37 we/us require specialist review.

**Chapter 35.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; replace did sin/did act with simple past in the conditional. Preserve night watches, distinction from beasts/birds, save me and weighing words.

Pending: 35:3 conditional, 35:9–12 speakers, 35:14 save me and 35:15 anger/inspection syntax require specialist review.

**Chapter 36.** Modernize ordinary address and verbs, retain additions 28a–b and transliterated ēdō, and repair the sentence join at 36:24–25. Preserve arrows/angels, enemy-mouth deception, abyss/table, old things and difficult closing clauses.

Pending: 36:3 works, 36:9 strength, 36:16 deception/abyss, 36:19–21 syntax, 36:24–25 subjects, 36:28 old things and 36:30–33 ēdō/friend/possession require specialist review.

**Chapter 37.** Modernize third-person verbs and address to Job; capitalize the sentence start at 37:17. Preserve earth’s wings, thunder, repeated whether, theeboulathō, old things, book/scribe and northern golden clouds.

Pending: 37:4 subjects, 37:6 storms, 37:11 chosen thing, 37:12 theeboulathō, 37:18 old things and 37:20 book/scribe require specialist review.

**Chapter 38.** Use ordinary you for the Lord’s address to Job and to the sea, My angels, modern verbs and you must answer Me for the emphatic command. Preserve earth’s rings/wings, dawn-bearer, clay creature, Hades gatekeepers, mazourōth, women’s weaving and dragons.

Pending: 38:2 concealed counsel, 38:6 rings, 38:14 clay creature, 38:20 conditional, 38:32 plural them and 38:38 dust/cube syntax require specialist review.

**Chapter 39.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person animal descriptions. Preserve one-horned beast, neelasa/asida/nessa, tax collector, full horse armor, trumpet Well done and bloodied nestlings.

Pending: 39:1 goat-deer, 39:3 offspring/fear, 39:13 transliterations and syntax, 39:16 hardening, 39:18 lifting up and 39:26 hawk pose need specialist review.

**Chapter 40.** Modernize the Lord’s address to Job and use you must answer Me. Preserve beasts-to-it shift, cypress, bronze ribs, angels, Tartarus, Jordan, dragon covenant, Phoenicians and existing Greek chapter numbering.

Pending: 40:2 judgment, 40:8 shown righteous, 40:15 number shift, 40:20 Tartarus, 40:24 subjects, 40:31 tail hide and 40:32 war require specialist review.

**Chapter 41.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; use My angels but retain independent Mine. Preserve dragon imagery, emery, brother comparison, fire-hearths, anvil, bronze bow, ointment jar, captive Tartarus and king of waters.

Pending: 41:4 equal/pity, 41:7 inward shields, 41:15 pouring subject, 41:18 weapons and 41:21 fire-bearing weapon need specialist review.

**Chapter 42.** Retain Thou/Thee in Job’s prayer; modernize third-person verbs and divine first-person possessive My. Make hearing with ear/seeing with eye speak naturally while retaining singular imagery. Preserve doubled restitution, names, ages, four generations and all additions 17a–e.

Pending: 42:3 speaker/question, 42:8 truth against Job, 42:10 doubling, 42:15 better, and 42:17b–e ancestry/names require specialist review.
