Luke 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Purpose of This Account

1:1Since many have undertaken to compile an account of the events fulfilled among us,

1:2just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us,

1:3it seemed good to me also, having followed everything carefully from the beginning, to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus,

1:4so that you may know the certainty of the matters in which you have been instructed.

Zechariah and Elizabeth

1:5In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah, of the division of Abijah. He had a wife from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.

1:6Both were righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the Lord’s commandments and ordinances.

1:7They had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both were advanced in years.

The Birth of John Foretold

1:8While he was serving as priest before God in the order of his division,

1:9according to the priesthood’s custom, he was chosen by lot to enter the Lord’s sanctuary and burn incense.

1:10The whole multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of incense.

1:11An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right of the altar of incense.

1:12Zechariah was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him.

1:13But the angel said to him, “Do not fear, Zechariah, for your petition has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall name him John.

1:14You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,

1:15for he will be great before the Lord. He will certainly drink neither wine nor strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother’s womb.

1:16He will turn many of the sons of Israel to the Lord their God.

1:17He himself will go before Him in Elijah’s spirit and power, to turn fathers’ hearts toward children and the disobedient to the understanding of the righteous, to prepare for the Lord a people made ready.”

1:18Zechariah said to the angel, “How will I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in years.”

1:19The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands before God, and I was sent to speak to you and announce this good news to you.

1:20And behold, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their appointed time.”

1:21The people were waiting for Zechariah and wondering at his delay in the sanctuary.

1:22When he came out, he could not speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary. He kept making signs to them and remained mute.

1:23When the days of his service were completed, he went home.

1:24After those days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she kept herself secluded for five months, saying,

1:25“This is what the Lord has done for me in these days, when He looked upon me to take away my disgrace among people.”

In the Sixth Month, the Angel Was Sent

1:26In the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,

1:27to a virgin betrothed to a man named Joseph, of David’s house. The virgin’s name was Mary.

1:28And he entered and said to her, “Rejoice, you who have been favoured! The Lord is with you.” †

1:29She was greatly troubled at the saying and considered what sort of greeting this might be.

1:30The angel said to her, “Do not fear, Mary, for you have found grace with God.

1:31And behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a Son, and you shall name Him Jesus.

1:32He will be great and will be called Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David,

1:33and He will reign over Jacob’s house forever, and His kingdom will have no end.”

1:34Mary said to the angel, “How will this be, since I do not know a man?”

1:35The angel answered her, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; and so the holy One to be born will be called Son of God. †

1:36And behold, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren,

1:37for no word from God will be powerless.”

1:38Mary said, “Behold, I am the Lord’s servant. May it happen to me according to your word.” Then the angel departed from her.

Mary Visits Elizabeth

1:39In those days Mary arose and went in haste into the hill country, to a city of Judah.

1:40She entered Zechariah’s house and greeted Elizabeth.

1:41When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the infant leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

1:42She cried out with a loud shout and said, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!

1:43And how is it that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

1:44For behold, when the sound of your greeting entered my ears, the infant in my womb leaped with gladness.

1:45And blessed is she who believed that there would be a fulfillment of the things spoken to her from the Lord.”

My Soul Magnifies the Lord

1:46Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord,

1:47and my spirit rejoiced in God my Savior,

1:48because He looked upon the lowly condition of His servant. For behold, from now on all generations will call me blessed,

1:49because the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is His name.

1:50His mercy is for generations and generations upon those who fear Him.

1:51He has performed a mighty deed with His arm; He scattered the proud in the thought of their heart.

1:52He brought rulers down from thrones and raised up the lowly.

1:53He filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty.

1:54He helped Israel His servant, remembering mercy,

1:55as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and his offspring forever.”

1:56Mary stayed with her about three months and returned to her home.

The Birth of John

1:57When Elizabeth’s time to give birth had come, she bore a son.

1:58Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy toward her, and they rejoiced with her.

1:59On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child and were calling him Zechariah after his father’s name.

1:60But his mother answered, “No; he shall be called John.”

1:61They said to her, “No one among your relatives is called by this name.”

1:62They made signs to his father to ask what he wanted the child to be called.

1:63He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” And they all wondered.

1:64Immediately his mouth and tongue were opened, and he spoke, blessing God.

1:65Fear came upon all who lived around them, and all these matters were discussed throughout the whole hill country of Judea.

1:66Everyone who heard took these matters to heart, saying, “What then will this child become?” For the Lord’s hand was indeed with him.

The Prophecy of Zechariah

1:67His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,

1:68“Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, because He has visited and accomplished redemption for His people,

1:69and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of David His servant,

1:70as He spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets from long ago:

1:71salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

1:72to show mercy to our fathers and remember His holy covenant,

1:73the oath He swore to Abraham our father, to grant us

1:74that, rescued from our enemies’ hand, we might serve Him without fear,

1:75in holiness and righteousness before Him all our days.

1:76And you also, child, will be called prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the Lord to prepare His ways,

1:77to give His people knowledge of salvation in the forgiveness of their sins,

1:78through the tender mercy of our God, by which the dawn from on high will visit us,

1:79to shine upon those who sit in darkness and death’s shadow, to direct our feet into a way of peace.”

The Child in the Wilderness

1:80The child grew and became strong in spirit. He remained in the wilderness until the day of his public appearance to Israel.

Luke 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Birth of Jesus

2:1In those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus for all the inhabited world to be registered.

2:2This first registration took place while Quirinius was governing Syria.

2:3Everyone went to be registered, each to his own city.

2:4Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to David’s city, which is called Bethlehem, because he belonged to David’s house and family,

2:5to be registered with Mary, who was betrothed to him and was pregnant.

2:6While they were there, the time came for her to give birth.

2:7She bore her firstborn Son, wrapped Him in swaddling cloths, and laid Him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the lodging place. †

The Shepherds and the Angels

2:8In that same region there were shepherds living in the fields and keeping watch over their flock through the watches of the night.

2:9An angel of the Lord stood beside them, and the Lord’s glory shone around them; they feared with a great fear.

2:10The angel said to them, “Do not fear, for behold, I announce to you good news of great joy, which will be for all the people:

2:11today in David’s city a Savior has been born for you, who is Christ the Lord.

2:12This is the sign for you: you will find an infant wrapped in swaddling cloths and lying in a manger.”

2:13Suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army, praising God and saying,

2:14“Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace among people of His favour.” †

The Shepherds Go to Bethlehem

2:15When the angels had departed from them into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, “Let us go over to Bethlehem and see this event that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”

2:16They came in haste and found Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in the manger.

2:17When they saw Him, they made known the saying spoken to them about this Child.

2:18Everyone who heard wondered at what the shepherds told them.

2:19But Mary kept all these matters, pondering them in her heart.

2:20The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for everything they had heard and seen, just as it had been spoken to them.

The Circumcision and Naming of Jesus

2:21When eight days were completed for His circumcision, He was named Jesus, the name given by the angel before He was conceived in the womb.

The Presentation in the Temple

2:22When the days of their purification according to Moses’ law were completed, they brought Him up to Jerusalem to present Him to the Lord,

2:23as it is written in the Lord’s law: “Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the Lord,”

2:24and to give a sacrifice according to what is said in the Lord’s law: “A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.”

Simeon

2:25And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem named Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, awaiting Israel’s consolation, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.

2:26It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he saw the Lord’s Christ.

2:27He came in the Spirit into the temple. When the parents brought in the Child Jesus to do for Him what was customary under the law,

2:28he received Him into his arms, blessed God, and said,

2:29“Now, Master, Thou art releasing Thy servant in peace according to Thy word,

2:30because my eyes have seen Thy salvation,

2:31which Thou didst prepare in the sight of all peoples:

2:32a light for revelation to the nations and glory for Thy people Israel.”

2:33His father and mother were wondering at what was being said about Him.

2:34Simeon blessed them and said to His mother Mary, “Behold, this One is appointed for the fall and rising of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against—

2:35and a sword will pass through your own soul also—so that thoughts from many hearts may be revealed.”

Anna the Prophetess

2:36There was also a prophetess, Anna, daughter of Phanuel, from the tribe of Asher. She was very old. She had lived with her husband for seven years after marrying as a virgin,

2:37and had been a widow until the age of eighty-four. She did not leave the temple, serving with fasts and petitions night and day.

2:38Coming up at that very hour, she gave thanks to God and spoke about Him to everyone awaiting Jerusalem’s redemption.

The Return to Nazareth

2:39When they had completed everything according to the Lord’s law, they returned to Galilee, to their own city of Nazareth.

2:40The Child grew and became strong, being filled with wisdom, and God’s grace was upon Him.

The Boy Jesus in the Temple

2:41His parents went each year to Jerusalem for the Festival of Passover.

2:42When He became twelve years old, they went up according to the festival’s custom.

2:43When the festival days were over and they were returning, the boy Jesus remained in Jerusalem, and His parents did not know.

2:44Supposing Him to be in the traveling company, they went a day’s journey and searched for Him among their relatives and acquaintances.

2:45When they did not find Him, they returned to Jerusalem searching for Him.

2:46After three days they found Him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and questioning them.

2:47All who heard Him were astonished at His understanding and answers.

2:48When His parents saw Him, they were overwhelmed. His mother said to Him, “Child, why hast Thou treated us this way? Behold, Thy father and I have been searching for Thee in anguish.”

2:49He said to them, “Why were you searching for Me? Did you not know that I must be in what belongs to My Father?”

2:50But they did not understand the saying He spoke to them.

2:51He went down with them and came to Nazareth, and He remained subject to them. His mother kept all these matters in her heart.

2:52Jesus advanced in wisdom and age, and in favor with God and people.

The Preaching of John

3:1In the fifteenth year of Tiberius Caesar’s rule, when Pontius Pilate governed Judea, Herod was tetrarch of Galilee, his brother Philip tetrarch of the region of Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilene,

3:2during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, God’s word came to John, Zechariah’s son, in the wilderness.

3:3He went into all the region around the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins,

3:4as it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet: “A voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare the Lord’s way; make His paths straight.

3:5Every ravine will be filled, and every mountain and hill brought low. The crooked will become straight, and the rough ways smooth roads,

3:6and all flesh will see God’s salvation.”

Fruits Worthy of Repentance

3:7So he said to the crowds coming out to be baptized by him, “Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee the coming wrath?

3:8Produce fruits worthy of repentance, and do not begin saying among yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as father.’ For I tell you that God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.

3:9Already the axe also lies at the root of the trees. Therefore every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire.”

The Crowds Asked Him, What Then Shall We Do?

3:10The crowds asked him, “What then should we do?”

3:11He answered them, “Let the one who has two tunics share with the one who has none, and let the one who has food do likewise.”

3:12Tax collectors also came to be baptized and said to him, “Teacher, what should we do?”

3:13He said to them, “Collect nothing beyond what has been appointed for you.”

3:14Soldiers also asked him, “And we, what should we do?” He said to them, “Extort from no one, do not make false accusations, and be content with your pay.”

One Mightier Is Coming

3:15As the people were waiting expectantly and everyone was considering in their hearts whether John might be the Christ,

3:16John answered them all, “I baptize you with water, but One stronger than I is coming, whose sandal strap I am not worthy to untie. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.

3:17His winnowing fork is in His hand, to cleanse His threshing floor thoroughly and gather the wheat into His storehouse; but the chaff He will burn with unquenchable fire.”

3:18With many other exhortations also, he announced good news to the people.

John Imprisoned by Herod

3:19But Herod the tetrarch, rebuked by him concerning Herodias, his brother’s wife, and all the evil things Herod had done,

3:20added this also to them all: he shut John up in prison.

The Baptism of Jesus

3:21When all the people were baptized, and Jesus also had been baptized and was praying, heaven was opened,

3:22and the Holy Spirit descended upon Him in bodily form like a dove. A voice came from heaven: “Thou art My beloved Son; in Thee I have taken delight.”

The Genealogy of Jesus

3:23Jesus Himself was about thirty years old when He began His ministry. He was the son, as was supposed, of Joseph, of Heli,

3:24of Matthat, of Levi, of Melchi, of Jannai, of Joseph,

3:25of Mattathias, of Amos, of Nahum, of Esli, of Naggai,

3:26of Maath, of Mattathias, of Semein, of Josech, of Joda,

3:27of Joanan, of Rhesa, of Zerubbabel, of Shealtiel, of Neri,

3:28of Melchi, of Addi, of Cosam, of Elmadam, of Er,

3:29of Jesus, of Eliezer, of Jorim, of Matthat, of Levi,

3:30of Simeon, of Judah, of Joseph, of Jonam, of Eliakim,

3:31of Melea, of Menna, of Mattatha, of Nathan, of David,

3:32of Jesse, of Obed, of Boaz, of Sala, of Nahshon,

3:33of Amminadab, of Admin, of Arni, of Hezron, of Perez, of Judah,

3:34of Jacob, of Isaac, of Abraham, of Terah, of Nahor,

3:35of Serug, of Reu, of Peleg, of Eber, of Shelah,

3:36of Cainan, of Arphaxad, of Shem, of Noah, of Lamech,

3:37of Methuselah, of Enoch, of Jared, of Mahalalel, of Cainan,

3:38of Enosh, of Seth, of Adam, of God.

Luke 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Temptation in the Wilderness

4:1Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan and was led in the Spirit in the wilderness

4:2for forty days, being tested by the devil. He ate nothing during those days, and when they were completed He was hungry.

4:3The devil said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, tell this stone to become bread.”

4:4Jesus answered him, “It is written: ‘A human being shall not live on bread alone.’”

4:5Taking Him up, the devil showed Him all the kingdoms of the inhabited world in a moment of time.

4:6The devil said to Him, “I will give Thee all this authority and their glory, because it has been handed over to me, and I give it to whomever I wish.

4:7Therefore, if Thou dost bow down before me, it will all be Thine.”

4:8Jesus answered him, “It is written: ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him alone shall you serve.’”

4:9He brought Him to Jerusalem, placed Him on the temple’s pinnacle, and said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, throw Thyself down from here,

4:10for it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning Thee, to guard Thee,’

4:11and, ‘They will lift Thee upon their hands, lest Thou strike Thy foot against a stone.’”

4:12Jesus answered him, “It has been said: ‘You shall not put the Lord your God to the test.’”

4:13Having completed every test, the devil departed from Him until an opportune time.

Jesus Returns to Galilee

4:14Jesus returned in the Spirit’s power into Galilee, and a report about Him spread throughout the whole surrounding region.

4:15He taught in their synagogues, being glorified by everyone.

Rejection at Nazareth

4:16He came to Nazareth, where He had been brought up, and according to His custom entered the synagogue on the Sabbath day. He stood up to read.

4:17The scroll of the prophet Isaiah was given to Him. Unrolling it, He found the place where it was written:

4:18“The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He anointed Me to proclaim good news to the poor. He has sent Me to proclaim release to captives and recovery of sight to the blind, to send the oppressed away in release, †

4:19to proclaim the Lord’s acceptable year.”

4:20He rolled up the scroll, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of everyone in the synagogue were fixed on Him.

4:21He began to say to them, “Today this Scripture has been fulfilled in your ears.”

4:22All bore witness to Him and wondered at the words of grace coming from His mouth. They said, “Is this not Joseph’s son?”

4:23He said to them, “Surely you will tell Me this proverb: ‘Physician, heal yourself.’ ‘Do here in Thy homeland also everything we heard happened in Capernaum.’”

4:24He said, “Amen, I tell you, no prophet is acceptable in his homeland.

4:25But in truth I tell you, there were many widows in Israel in Elijah’s days, when heaven was shut for three years and six months and a great famine came over the whole land.

4:26Yet Elijah was sent to none of them except to a widow at Zarephath in Sidon’s territory.

4:27And there were many leprous people in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet, but none of them was cleansed except Naaman the Syrian.”

4:28Everyone in the synagogue was filled with anger when they heard these things.

4:29They rose, threw Him out of the city, and led Him to the brow of the hill on which their city was built, intending to hurl Him down.

4:30But He passed through their midst and went on His way.

An Unclean Demon in the Synagogue

4:31He went down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee, and was teaching them on the Sabbaths.

4:32They were overwhelmed at His teaching, because His word was with authority.

4:33In the synagogue was a man who had the spirit of an unclean demon. He cried out with a loud voice,

4:34“Ah! What have we to do with Thee, Jesus of Nazareth? Hast Thou come to destroy us? I know who Thou art: God’s Holy One.”

4:35Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent and come out of him!” The demon threw him down in their midst and came out of him without harming him.

4:36Amazement came upon everyone, and they said to one another, “What is this word? With authority and power He commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!”

4:37A report about Him went out into every place in the surrounding region.

Simon’s Mother-in-Law Healed

4:38Leaving the synagogue, He entered Simon’s house. Simon’s mother-in-law was gripped by a high fever, and they asked Him about her.

4:39Standing over her, He rebuked the fever, and it left her. Immediately she rose and served them.

Healings at Sunset

4:40As the sun was setting, everyone who had people sick with various diseases brought them to Him. He laid His hands on each one and healed them.

4:41Demons also came out of many, crying, “Thou art God’s Son!” He rebuked them and would not let them speak, because they knew He was the Christ.
