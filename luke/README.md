# Luke — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 24 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 1149 verse units received an English editorial review: 224 changed and 925 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

NA28 is the user-authorized provisional New Testament base. Focused checks of the German Bible Society’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not complete critical-text or apparatus verification. The newer Bespoke PDF’s preferred UBS6 text has not been obtained; UBS6/ECM comparison, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/LUK.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 24 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Smooth the opening account and age/time idioms without changing their sequence. Use you for Mary and Gabriel: the older thou/thee in 1:28,35 conflicts with the newer PDF. Retain bestowed favour, grace, virgin, overshadow, no word from God powerless, and the first-person speech and song. Modernize third-person verbs; preserve the arm, horn, heart, oath, dawn and shadow images. The child addressed in 1:76 is John, so you remains appropriate.

Pending: Review the force of fulfilled in 1:1, followed from the beginning in 1:3, the best rendering of the female servant terms, holy/to be born in 1:35, and the mouth/tongue expression in 1:64. The future visit in 1:78 and the source’s quoted-song tense distinctions remain. No blessed among women clause is added at 1:28. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Retain lodging place, first registration, their purification, His father and mother, the two birds and all stated ages and intervals. Naturalize Mary’s pondering and Anna’s age/marriage wording while retaining virginity and seven years. Simeon’s prayer addresses God with Thou/Thy; Mary addresses Jesus with Thou/Thee under the edition’s referent convention. Preserve the prayer’s present release and past preparation. His in 2:14 remains an explicitly supplied contextual referent.

Pending: Quirinius and census chronology need historical review; no harmonizing date is inserted. Anna’s eighty-four years may be read as age or duration of widowhood; the inherited age reading remains provisional. Keep the ambiguity of what belongs to My Father in 2:49 and age in 2:52 pending lexical review. The small NA28 brackets at 2:26,35,52 do not change the current natural English sense. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize third-person verbs and use Thou/Thee in the Father’s address to Jesus. Smooth the opening of the genealogy; His ministry makes the contextual object of began explicit and is not a separate Greek noun. Preserve every genealogical name and its order, including Jesus in 3:29, Admin and Arni, and both Cainan entries. Retain the repeated fruits, root, fire, winnowing fork, all flesh, bodily form and about thirty.

Pending: The chronology and genealogy require specialist review, including the supposed sonship clause and textual witnesses at 3:22–38. The small bracketed article at 3:3 and conjunction at 3:20 do not introduce a separate English clause. Do not harmonize Luke’s genealogy or baptismal voice with other witnesses. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Apply Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine to speech addressed to Jesus, including the devil’s quotation applied to Him; this editorial convention does not imply reverence by the speaker. The ordinary scriptural commands at 4:8,12 and the generic physician proverb keep you/yourself. Modernize has and to, retaining the two release expressions, temptation order, singular stone, three years and six months, and Judea in 4:44.

Pending: The inherited release phrasing at 4:18 remains deliberately close to its repeated Greek term and needs further English/lexical review. Do not add heal the brokenhearted or the longer bread quotation from another Gospel. Retain Nazareth as the established English place-name despite the displayed Nazara spelling at 4:16; no new place is inferred. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Apply divine address to Jesus and modernize third-person verbs. Use bridegroom’s companions for the wedding idiom, consistent with Mark 2:19, retaining the bridegroom and fasting imagery. Preserve the plural nets, catching people alive, little bed, forgiveness passive, healthy/ill contrast, new/fresh wineskins and the saying that the old is good, not better.

Pending: The put-out command at 5:4 is singular to Simon; let down your nets is plural to the group. Modern you/your relies on that stated context. Retain Him as the subject of healing at 5:17, the single-bracketed him at 5:18 and And at 5:39. Independent review of these grammatical and lexical decisions remains due. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.
