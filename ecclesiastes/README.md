## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Keep the name Ecclesiastes, king of Israel, the repeated futility formula, surplus/toil and the sun-to-wind transition across 5–6. Preserve the future filling/seeing/hearing clauses, comparisons and understanding in 17 and knowledge rather than grief in 18. Modernize verbs and add clear speech punctuation in 10. Resolve of spirit remains a provisional lexical rendering, not an inserted wind-chasing paraphrase. The future hypothetical speaker in Greek 10 is expressed by the English if anyone speaks construction.

Pending: Review resolve of spirit throughout the book, the subject transition in 5–6, and exact force of the future hypothetical in 10. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary self-address to the heart and address to gladness use you. Retain male cupbearer/female cupbearers, counsel in 12, the extended excess speech in 15 and bidding the heart farewell in 20. Keep the difficult no good opening in 24 with its inherited note; do not insert a comparative better. Make the future eating/drinking/showing in 24 and past gave twice in 26 explicit. Preserve the contextual God-reference apart from Him in 25.

Pending: Priority review of counsel syntax in 12 and the difficult syntax of 24–25; also farewell in 20 and cupbearer terminology. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserve every time/season pair, active give birth, bodily mourning, age in hearts, the pursued person, beasts even to themselves, repeated one and shared breath. Keep the explicit whether questions in 21 and the distinction between breath in 19 and spirit in 21, with inherited notes. The eating/drinking in 13 retains an English whenever construction for the Greek prospective/generic wording; no doctrinal resolution is added.

Pending: Review age in 11, the referent of the pursued one in 15, speech and even to themselves in 18 and the questions in 21. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 4.** Retain the repeated absence of comfort, the fool’s past folded/ate, all one/two/three contrasts, gain strength in 12 and the threefold cord. Preserve verse 17 here, corresponding to common English 5:1; ordinary address is singular you/your. Its sacrifice/fools contrast is not harmonized. Greek tear in 1 is singular, represented by the inherited English tears as a collective expression.

Pending: Review extortion terminology in 1, the strengthened-one relation in 12, the ruler succession in 14–16 and the difficult sacrifice/evil clause in 17. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 5.** Use you/your for ordinary singular address, preserving mouth/flesh/hands and the repeated vow a vow/pay pattern. Add quotation marks around the excuse in 5. Keep God rather than an angel, the difficult king/field statement in 8, the question in 9, sickness and wind images and numbered days. In 10 the Greek past multiplication is made explicit; increased expresses the surrounding abundance as an English clause.

Pending: Priority review of 8–9 and the construction of 10; also whose hand in 13 and the force of divine distraction in 19. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 6.** Make will give/will not empower in 2 and the report I said in 3 explicit. Preserve the stillborn comparison and its rest, courses of a thousand years without adding two, the statement about the poor in 8, and resolve of spirit. Has already been named and will not be able in 10 retain passive naming and future inability; will tell in 12 preserves the rhetorical future.

Pending: Review the difficult conditional tenses in 3, courses in 6, appetite/soul in 7,9 and the shadow clause in 12. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 7.** Preserve anger rather than a softened sadness, extortion as the sole cause in 7, shade of silver, the excessive-righteousness warning, and the slave as actor in 22. Ordinary singular address uses you/your; bewildered clarifies confounded. Asked in 10 reflects the Greek past question. Keep the 23–24 verse boundary, deep depth, the woman/traps/nets/bonds image and the man/woman findings without adding interpretive qualifications.

Pending: Review heart of his vigor in 7, concerning speech in 14, the force of excessive righteousness in 16 and the difficult 25–29 search statements. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 8.** Keep wise people plural, the king’s mouth and future departure, knowledge as burden in 6, spirit repeated in 8, praised in 10 and contradiction in 11. Had authority in 9, became fully assured in 11 and gave in 15 preserve past forms. One with a shameless face in 1 improves English order. Retain the difficult sinner/length clause in 12 and the uninterrupted 16–17 sentence.

Pending: Priority review of oath/speaker relations in 2–4, the contradiction construction in 11 and length for him in 12. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

Preserve the possible siege in 14–15 and its future deliverance, with past remembered.

Pending: Review the opening question in 4, the scope of the mortality statements, the mixed hypothetical/past narration in 14–15 and the force of rulers in follies in 17. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

Pending: Priority review of the imagery and tense sequence in 3–7, the wheel wording in 6, the ear/arrangement in 9 and the collection/shepherd relation in 11. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.
