## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize ordinary you and third-person has, and smooth the vine/fig-tree destruction clause. Retain Thee in the closing direct address to God, the repeated insect/blight sequence, their wine, lion teeth, searching repetition, bride and first husband, separate sacrifice/drink offering, dried farmers and addressed estates, threefold alas and suffering from suffering, leaping heifers, winepresses, weeping cattle and the repeated beautiful places of the wilderness.

Pending: Review the bruchus/blight terminology, searching the vine, first-husband phrase, service at 1:14 and suffering-from-suffering at 1:15. No modern species identification or Shaddai substitution is supplied. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs while keeping Thy/Thine in the prayer at 2:17. Render confounded as thrown into turmoil, plain of disappearance as desolate plain, and not be finished as not be destroyed; smooth the negative each clause and make the shared future explicit for winepresses. Preserve the prospective verbs in 2:4–10, scorched-pot image, repeated great, Lord’s past jealousy and sparing in 2:18, the two seas and repeated rising decay/stench, food for righteousness, all four destructive agents and repeated never-shamed promises.

Pending: Review the pot image, brother/weapon/missile clauses, sufficient for the day, altar platform, past narrative at 2:18 and especially food for righteousness at 2:23. Retain food rather than substituting teacher or rain. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Retain the accepted 3:1 cadence, including shall and of My Spirit, all flesh, sons/daughters, elders and young men. Match the repeated outpouring phrase at 3:2 to of My Spirit and modernize third-person calls/escapes/has in 3:5. Preserve all five source-numbered verses, signs, great and manifest day, the passive salvation promise, singular one who escapes and plural bearers of good news.

Pending: Review the force of of My Spirit and the relationship of escape and good-news clauses. Acts’ last-days wording, altered group order and additional clauses are reception material and have not been inserted into Joel. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize ordinary address, has and attributive My, and smooth What have you to do with Me. Make the shared future explicit in the flowing-water clauses, and render the repeated disappearance terms as desolate/desolate plain. Preserve repeated recompense, scattering and captivity, boys/girls and sons, Galilee of foreigners, Jehoshaphat, plows into swords and sickles into lances, the meek fighter, sickles/vintage/winepress, repeated Valley of Judgment, sweetness, Wadi of Rushes, Idumea/Judea and the final avenge/not-acquit pair with the Zion sentence included once.

Pending: Review the force of recompense and sharp grudge, the meek fighter, the geographic Rushes designation, blood and responsibility at 4:19, and the objects and legal force of avenge/acquit at 4:21. Do not import the peace-direction transformation from Isaiah/Micah or a different textual witness’s cleansing formula. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
