MATTHEW 1

1:1 A book of the origin of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.
1:2 Abraham begot Isaac; and Isaac begot Jacob; and Jacob begot Judah and his brothers;
1:3 Judah begot Perez and Zerah by Tamar; Perez begot Hezron; Hezron begot Ram;
1:4 Ram begot Amminadab; Amminadab begot Nahshon; Nahshon begot Salmon;
1:5 Salmon begot Boaz by Rahab; Boaz begot Obed by Ruth; Obed begot Jesse;
1:6 Jesse begot David the king. David begot Solomon by the wife of Uriah;
1:7 Solomon begot Rehoboam; Rehoboam begot Abijah; Abijah begot Asaph;
1:8 Asaph begot Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat begot Joram; Joram begot Uzziah;
1:9 Uzziah begot Jotham; Jotham begot Ahaz; Ahaz begot Hezekiah;
1:10 Hezekiah begot Manasseh; Manasseh begot Amos; Amos begot Josiah;
1:11 Josiah begot Jechoniah and his brothers at the deportation to Babylon.
1:12 After the deportation to Babylon, Jechoniah begot Shealtiel; Shealtiel begot Zerubbabel;
1:13 Zerubbabel begot Abiud; Abiud begot Eliakim; Eliakim begot Azor;
1:14 Azor begot Zadok; Zadok begot Achim; Achim begot Eliud;
1:15 Eliud begot Eleazar; Eleazar begot Matthan; Matthan begot Jacob;
1:16 and Jacob begot Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.
1:17 So all the generations from Abraham to David were fourteen generations, from David to the deportation to Babylon fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon to the Christ fourteen generations.
1:18 The origin of Jesus Christ was like this. When His mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child from the Holy Spirit.
1:19 Joseph her husband, being righteous and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace, decided to divorce her quietly.
1:20 As he considered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary your wife home, for what has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.
1:21 She shall bear a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He shall save His people from their sins.”
1:22 All this has happened so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled:
1:23 “Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall call His name Emmanuel”; which means “God with us.”
1:24 When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him and took his wife home.
1:25 He did not have relations with her until she bore a Son; and he called His name Jesus.

MATTHEW 2

2:1 After Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, behold, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem,
2:2 saying, “Where is the One born King of the Jews? For we saw His star at its rising and have come to bow before Him.”
2:3 When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.
2:4 He gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people and asked them where the Christ was to be born.
2:5 They told him, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is what has been written through the prophet:
2:6 ‘And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd My people Israel.’”
2:7 Then Herod secretly called the magi and learned from them the exact time when the star appeared.
2:8 He sent them to Bethlehem, saying, “Go and inquire carefully about the Child. When you find Him, report to me, so that I too may come and bow before Him.”
2:9 After hearing the king, they went on their way. And behold, the star they had seen at its rising went ahead of them until it came and stood over the place where the Child was.
2:10 When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.
2:11 They entered the house and saw the Child with Mary His mother. Falling down, they bowed before Him; and opening their treasures, they offered Him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
2:12 Having been warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they withdrew to their country by another way.
2:13 After they withdrew, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, “Rise, take the Child and His mother, and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is about to seek the Child to destroy Him.”
2:14 He rose, took the Child and His mother by night, and withdrew to Egypt.
2:15 He remained there until Herod’s death, so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled: “Out of Egypt I called My Son.”
2:16 Then Herod, seeing that the magi had outwitted him, became exceedingly angry. He sent men and killed all the boys in Bethlehem and throughout its district who were two years old and under, according to the exact time he had learned from the magi.
2:17 Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:
2:18 “A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and great lamentation: Rachel weeping for her children, and she would not be comforted, because they are no more.”
2:19 After Herod died, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,
2:20 saying, “Rise, take the Child and His mother, and go to the land of Israel, for those seeking the Child’s life have died.”
2:21 He rose, took the Child and His mother, and entered the land of Israel.
2:22 But hearing that Archelaus reigned over Judea in place of his father Herod, he feared to go there. Warned in a dream, he withdrew to the regions of Galilee.
2:23 He came and settled in a city called Nazareth, so that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled: “He will be called a Nazarene.”

MATTHEW 3

3:1 In those days John the Baptist appeared, proclaiming in the wilderness of Judea
3:2 and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.”
3:3 For this is the one spoken of through Isaiah the prophet: “A voice of one crying in the wilderness: ‘Prepare the way of the Lord; make His paths straight.’”
3:4 John himself wore clothing of camel’s hair and a leather belt around his waist; his food was locusts and wild honey.
3:5 Then Jerusalem, all Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him,
3:6 and they were baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.
3:7 When he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, “Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?
3:8 Bear fruit, then, worthy of repentance,
3:9 and do not suppose you can say among yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as father.’ For I tell you that God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.
3:10 Already the axe lies at the root of the trees. Every tree, therefore, that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire.
3:11 I baptize you in water for repentance, but the One coming after me is stronger than I, whose sandals I am not fit to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.
3:12 His winnowing shovel is in His hand, and He will thoroughly clear His threshing floor and gather His wheat into the storehouse; but He will burn the chaff with unquenchable fire.”
3:13 Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.
3:14 John tried to prevent Him, saying, “I need to be baptized by Thee, and dost Thou come to me?”
3:15 Jesus answered him, “Allow it now, for in this way it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then he allowed Him.
3:16 When Jesus was baptized, He immediately came up from the water. And behold, the heavens were opened, and He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and coming upon Him.
3:17 And behold, a voice from the heavens said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”

MATTHEW 4

4:1 Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit to be tempted by the devil.
4:2 After fasting forty days and forty nights, He became hungry.
4:3 The tempter came and said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, command these stones to become loaves of bread.”
4:4 But He answered, “It is written: ‘A human being shall not live on bread alone, but on every word proceeding through the mouth of God.’”
4:5 Then the devil took Him into the holy city and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple,
4:6 and said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, throw Thyself down. For it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning Thee,’ and ‘They will lift Thee on their hands, lest Thou strike Thy foot against a stone.’”
4:7 Jesus said to him, “Again it is written: ‘You shall not put the Lord your God to the test.’”
4:8 Again the devil took Him to an exceedingly high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory.
4:9 He said to Him, “I will give Thee all these things if Thou fall down and worship me.”
4:10 Then Jesus said to him, “Go away, Satan! For it is written: ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him alone shall you serve.’”
4:11 Then the devil left Him, and behold, angels came and ministered to Him.
4:12 When He heard that John had been handed over, He withdrew to Galilee.
4:13 Leaving Nazareth, He came and settled in Capernaum beside the sea, in the territories of Zebulun and Naphtali,
4:14 so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled:
4:15 “Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations:
4:16 the people sitting in darkness saw a great light, and for those sitting in the region and shadow of death a light rose.”
4:17 From then on Jesus began to proclaim, “Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.”
4:18 As He walked beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.
4:19 He said to them, “Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of people.”
4:20 Immediately they left their nets and followed Him.
4:21 Going on from there, He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and He called them.
4:22 Immediately they left the boat and their father and followed Him.
4:23 He went throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the good news of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every infirmity among the people.
4:24 The report about Him spread throughout Syria, and they brought Him all who were ill, afflicted with various diseases and torments, those oppressed by demons, those suffering seizures, and paralytics; and He healed them.
4:25 Great crowds followed Him from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan.

MATTHEW 5

5:1 And seeing the crowds, He went up the mountain; and when He had sat down, His disciples came to Him.
5:2 And He opened His mouth and taught them, saying:
5:3 “Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.
5:4 Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.
5:5 Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.
5:6 Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.
5:7 Blessed are the merciful, for they shall receive mercy.
5:8 Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.
5:9 Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God.
5:10 Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness’ sake, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.
5:11 Blessed are you when they revile you and persecute you and falsely speak every evil thing against you for My sake.
5:12 Rejoice and be glad, for great is your reward in the heavens; for thus they persecuted the prophets before you.
5:13 You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt becomes tasteless, how shall it be made salty? It is no longer good for anything except to be thrown out and trampled underfoot by people.
5:14 You are the light of the world. A city set upon a mountain cannot be hidden.
5:15 Nor do people light a lamp and place it beneath the modius, but upon the lampstand; and it shines for everyone in the house.
5:16 So let your light shine before people, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in the heavens.
5:17 Do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to abolish, but to fulfil.
5:18 For truly I tell you: until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota nor one stroke shall pass from the Law, until all things come to pass.
5:19 Whoever therefore loosens one of these least commandments and teaches people to do so shall be called least in the kingdom of the heavens; but whoever does and teaches them shall be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.
5:20 For I tell you: unless your righteousness exceeds that of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall by no means enter the kingdom of the heavens.
5:21 You have heard that it was said to those of old: ‘You shall not murder’; and ‘whoever murders shall be liable to judgment.’
5:22 But I tell you that everyone who is angry with his brother shall be liable to judgment; and whoever says to his brother, ‘Raca,’ shall be liable to the council; and whoever says, ‘Fool,’ shall be liable to the Gehenna of fire.
5:23 If therefore you offer your gift upon the altar, and there remember that your brother has something against you,
5:24 leave your gift there before the altar, and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.
5:25 Come to terms with your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the way, lest your adversary hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.
5:26 Truly I tell you: you shall by no means come out of there until you have paid the last quadrans.
5:27 You have heard that it was said: ‘You shall not commit adultery.’
5:28 But I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.
5:29 If your right eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body be thrown into Gehenna.
5:30 And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body go into Gehenna.
5:31 And it was said: ‘Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce.’
5:32 But I tell you that everyone who divorces his wife, except on account of sexual immorality, causes her to commit adultery; and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.
5:33 Again, you have heard that it was said to those of old: ‘You shall not swear falsely, but shall fulfil your oaths to the Lord.’
5:34 But I tell you: do not swear at all, neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God,
5:35 nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of His feet, nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.
5:36 Nor shall you swear by your head, for you cannot make one hair white or black.
5:37 But let your word be ‘Yes, yes’; ‘No, no.’ Whatever is beyond these is of evil.
5:38 You have heard that it was said: ‘An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.’
5:39 But I tell you: do not resist the evildoer; but whoever strikes you upon your right cheek, turn the other to him also.
5:40 And if anyone would sue you and take your tunic, let him have your cloak also.
5:41 And whoever compels you to go one Roman mile, go with him two.
5:42 Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who would borrow from you.
5:43 You have heard that it was said: ‘You shall love your neighbour and hate your enemy.’
5:44 But I tell you: love your enemies, and pray for those who persecute you,
5:45 that you may become sons of your Father who is in the heavens; for He makes His sun rise upon the evil and the good, and sends rain upon the righteous and the unrighteous.
5:46 For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same?
5:47 And if you greet only your brothers, what are you doing beyond others? Do not even the Gentiles do the same?
5:48 You shall therefore be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.

MATTHEW 6

6:1 Take care not to practice your righteousness before people in order to be seen by them; otherwise you have no reward with your Father in the heavens.
6:2 So when you give alms, do not sound a trumpet before you as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and streets, so that people may glorify them. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.
6:3 But when you give alms, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,
6:4 so that your almsgiving may be in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.
6:5 And when you pray, you shall not be like the hypocrites, because they love to pray standing in synagogues and at street corners, so that people may see them. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.
6:6 But you, when you pray, enter your inner room, shut your door, and pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.
6:7 When praying, do not babble like the Gentiles, for they think they will be heard through their many words.
6:8 So do not become like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask Him.
6:9 Pray, then, in this way: ‘Our Father who art in the heavens, hallowed be Thy name.
6:10 Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done, as in heaven, so also upon earth.
6:11 Give us this day our daily bread;
6:12 and forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors;
6:13 and bring us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.’
6:14 For if you forgive people their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.
6:15 But if you do not forgive people, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.
6:16 And when you fast, do not become gloomy like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces so that people may see they are fasting. Truly I tell you, they have received their reward in full.
6:17 But you, when fasting, anoint your head and wash your face,
6:18 so that your fasting may be seen by your Father who is in secret, rather than by people; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.
6:19 Do not store up treasures for yourselves upon earth, where moth and consumption destroy and where thieves break through and steal.
6:20 But store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where neither moth nor consumption destroys and where thieves neither break through nor steal.
6:21 For where your treasure is, there your heart will also be.
6:22 The lamp of the body is the eye. If therefore your eye is sound, your whole body will be full of light.
6:23 But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be dark. So if the light within you is darkness, how great is the darkness!
6:24 No one can serve as a slave to two masters: for he will either hate the one and love the other, or hold to one and despise the other. You cannot serve as a slave to God and mammon.
6:25 Therefore I tell you: do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, or about your body, what you will wear. Is life not more than food, and the body more than clothing?
6:26 Look at the birds of the sky: they do not sow or reap or gather into storehouses, yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth more than they?
6:27 Which of you by being anxious can add one cubit to his lifespan?
6:28 And why are you anxious about clothing? Observe the lilies of the field, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin.
6:29 Yet I tell you that not even Solomon in all his glory clothed himself like one of these.
6:30 If God so clothes the grass of the field, which exists today and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, will He not much more clothe you, people of little faith?
6:31 So do not be anxious, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’
6:32 For the nations seek all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them all.
6:33 But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.
6:34 So do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious about itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble.

MATTHEW 7

7:1 Do not judge, so that you may not be judged.
7:2 For with the judgment you use, you will be judged, and with the measure you use, it will be measured to you.
7:3 Why do you see the splinter in your brother’s eye, but do not notice the beam in your own eye?
7:4 Or how will you say to your brother, ‘Let me remove the splinter from your eye,’ when behold, the beam is in your own eye?
7:5 Hypocrite! First remove the beam from your own eye, and then you will see clearly to remove the splinter from your brother’s eye.
7:6 Do not give what is holy to dogs or throw your pearls before pigs, lest they trample them with their feet and turn and tear you apart.
7:7 Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.
7:8 For everyone who asks receives, the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.
7:9 Or who among you, if his son asks for bread, will give him a stone?
7:10 Or if he asks for a fish, will give him a snake?
7:11 If you, then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in the heavens give good things to those who ask Him!
7:12 So in everything, whatever you wish people to do for you, you also do likewise for them; for this is the Law and the Prophets.
7:13 Enter through the narrow gate, because the gate is wide and the road broad that leads to destruction, and many enter through it.
7:14 How narrow is the gate and how constricted the road that leads to life, and few find it!
7:15 Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing but inwardly are ravenous wolves.
7:16 You will recognize them by their fruits. Do people gather grapes from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?
7:17 In the same way, every good tree bears good fruit, but the rotten tree bears evil fruit.
7:18 A good tree cannot bear evil fruit, nor can a rotten tree bear good fruit.
7:19 Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire.
7:20 So then, you will recognize them by their fruits.
7:21 Not everyone who says to Me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter the kingdom of the heavens, but the one who does the will of My Father in the heavens.
7:22 Many will say to Me on that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in Thy name, cast out demons in Thy name, and do many mighty works in Thy name?’
7:23 Then I will declare to them, ‘I never knew you. Depart from Me, you who practice lawlessness.’
7:24 Therefore everyone who hears these words of Mine and puts them into practice will be likened to a prudent man who built his house upon the rock.
7:25 The rain came down, the rivers came, and the winds blew and fell upon that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded upon the rock.
7:26 And everyone who hears these words of Mine and does not put them into practice will be likened to a foolish man who built his house upon the sand.
7:27 The rain came down, the rivers came, and the winds blew and struck that house, and it fell; and its fall was great.”
7:28 When Jesus finished these words, the crowds were astonished at His teaching,
7:29 for He taught them as One having authority, and not as their scribes.

MATTHEW 8

8:1 When He came down from the mountain, great crowds followed Him.
8:2 And behold, a leper came and bowed before Him, saying, “Lord, if Thou art willing, Thou canst cleanse me.”
8:3 He stretched out His hand and touched him, saying, “I am willing; be cleansed.” Immediately his leprosy was cleansed.
8:4 Jesus said to him, “See that you tell no one, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift Moses commanded as a testimony to them.”
8:5 When He entered Capernaum, a centurion came to Him, pleading with Him
8:6 and saying, “Lord, my servant lies at home paralyzed, terribly tormented.”
8:7 He said to him, “I will come and heal him.”
8:8 The centurion answered, “Lord, I am not fit for Thee to enter under my roof. Only speak a word, and my servant will be healed.
8:9 For I too am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I say to this one, ‘Go,’ and he goes; to another, ‘Come,’ and he comes; and to my slave, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.”
8:10 When Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those following, “Truly I tell you, with no one in Israel have I found such faith.
8:11 I tell you that many will come from east and west and recline with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of the heavens,
8:12 but the sons of the kingdom will be cast into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.”
8:13 Jesus said to the centurion, “Go; as you have believed, let it happen for you.” And his servant was healed at that hour.
8:14 Jesus entered Peter’s house and saw his mother-in-law lying down with a fever.
8:15 He touched her hand, and the fever left her. She arose and served Him.
8:16 When evening came, they brought Him many who were oppressed by demons. He cast out the spirits with a word and healed all who were ill,
8:17 so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled: “He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases.”
8:18 Seeing a crowd around Him, Jesus gave orders to depart to the other side.
8:19 One scribe came and said to Him, “Teacher, I will follow Thee wherever Thou goest.”
8:20 Jesus said to him, “Foxes have dens and the birds of the sky have resting places, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay His head.”
8:21 Another of His disciples said to Him, “Lord, first permit me to go and bury my father.”
8:22 But Jesus said to him, “Follow Me, and let the dead bury their own dead.”
8:23 And when He entered the boat, His disciples followed Him.
8:24 And behold, a great tempest arose in the sea, so that the boat was covered by the waves; but He was sleeping.
8:25 And they came to Him and awoke Him, saying, “Lord, save; we are perishing.”
8:26 And He says to them, “Why are you fearful, you of little faith?” Then He arose and rebuked the winds and the sea; and there was a great calm.
8:27 And the men marvelled, saying, “What kind of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey Him?”
8:28 When He came to the other side, to the country of the Gadarenes, two men oppressed by demons met Him, coming out of the tombs. They were exceedingly fierce, so that no one could pass by that road.
8:29 And behold, they cried out, “What have we to do with Thee, Son of God? Hast Thou come here to torment us before the appointed time?”
8:30 Far from them a herd of many pigs was feeding.
8:31 The demons pleaded with Him, saying, “If Thou castest us out, send us into the herd of pigs.”
8:32 He said to them, “Go.” They came out and went into the pigs. And behold, the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the waters.
8:33 The herdsmen fled, and going into the city they reported everything, including what had happened to the men oppressed by demons.
8:34 And behold, the whole city came out to meet Jesus. When they saw Him, they begged Him to depart from their territory.

MATTHEW 9

9:1 He entered a boat, crossed over, and came to His own city.
9:2 And behold, they brought Him a paralytic lying on a bed. Seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralytic, “Take courage, child; your sins are forgiven.”
9:3 And behold, some of the scribes said among themselves, “This Man blasphemes.”
9:4 Seeing their thoughts, Jesus said, “Why do you think evil things in your hearts?
9:5 For which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Rise and walk’?
9:6 But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”—then He said to the paralytic—“rise, take your bed, and go to your house.”
9:7 He rose and went to his house.
9:8 When the crowds saw this, they feared and glorified God, who had given such authority to human beings.
9:9 As Jesus passed on from there, He saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax station, and He said to him, “Follow Me.” He rose and followed Him.
9:10 As He reclined at table in the house, behold, many tax collectors and sinners came and reclined with Jesus and His disciples.
9:11 When the Pharisees saw this, they said to His disciples, “Why does your Teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”
9:12 Hearing this, He said, “The strong have no need of a physician, but those who are ill do.
9:13 Go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy and not sacrifice.’ For I did not come to call righteous people, but sinners.”
9:14 Then John’s disciples came to Him, saying, “Why do we and the Pharisees fast often, but Thy disciples do not fast?”
9:15 Jesus said to them, “Can the wedding guests mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast.
9:16 No one puts a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment, for its patch pulls away from the garment, and a worse tear results.
9:17 Nor do people put new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise the skins burst, the wine spills out, and the skins are ruined. Instead, they put new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved.”
9:18 While He was saying these things to them, behold, a ruler came and bowed before Him, saying, “My daughter has just died. But come, lay Thy hand upon her, and she will live.”
9:19 Jesus rose and followed him, and so did His disciples.
9:20 And behold, a woman who had suffered a flow of blood for twelve years came behind Him and touched the fringe of His garment.
9:21 For she said within herself, “If I only touch His garment, I will be saved.”
9:22 Jesus turned, saw her, and said, “Take courage, daughter; your faith has saved you.” And the woman was saved from that hour.
9:23 When Jesus entered the ruler’s house and saw the flute players and the crowd in an uproar,
9:24 He said, “Withdraw, for the girl has not died but is sleeping.” And they laughed at Him.
9:25 When the crowd had been put outside, He entered and took her hand, and the girl arose.
9:26 This report went out into all that land.
9:27 As Jesus passed on from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!”
9:28 When He entered the house, the blind men came to Him, and Jesus said to them, “Do you believe that I can do this?” They said to Him, “Yes, Lord.”
9:29 Then He touched their eyes, saying, “According to your faith, let it happen for you.”
9:30 Their eyes were opened. Jesus sternly warned them, saying, “See that no one knows.”
9:31 But they went out and spread the report about Him throughout all that land.
9:32 As they were going out, behold, people brought Him a mute man oppressed by a demon.
9:33 When the demon was cast out, the mute man spoke. The crowds marveled, saying, “Never has anything like this appeared in Israel.”
9:34 But the Pharisees said, “By the ruler of the demons He casts out demons.”
9:35 And Jesus went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every infirmity.
9:36 And seeing the crowds, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed and cast down, as sheep having no shepherd.
9:37 Then He says to His disciples, “The harvest indeed is plentiful, but the labourers are few.
9:38 Pray therefore to the Lord of the harvest, that He send out labourers into His harvest.”

MATTHEW 10

10:1 He summoned His twelve disciples and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out and to heal every disease and every infirmity.
10:2 These are the names of the twelve apostles: first Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother;
10:3 Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;
10:4 Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, who also handed Him over.
10:5 Jesus sent these twelve, commanding them, “Do not go along a road of the Gentiles, and do not enter a city of Samaritans.
10:6 Go instead to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
10:7 As you go, proclaim, ‘The kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.’
10:8 Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. Freely you received; freely give.
10:9 Do not acquire gold, silver, or copper for your belts,
10:10 nor a bag for the journey, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.
10:11 Whatever city or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and remain there until you leave.
10:12 As you enter the house, greet it.
10:13 If the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.
10:14 Whoever will not receive you or hear your words, as you leave that house or city, shake the dust off your feet.
10:15 Truly I tell you, it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that city.
10:16 Behold, I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves. So become prudent as snakes and innocent as doves.
10:17 Beware of people, for they will hand you over to councils and flog you in their synagogues.
10:18 You will also be brought before governors and kings because of Me, as a testimony to them and to the nations.
10:19 When they hand you over, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak, for what you should speak will be given to you at that hour.
10:20 For it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you.
10:21 Brother will hand brother over to death, and a father his child; children will rise against parents and have them put to death.
10:22 You will be hated by all because of My name, but the one who endures to the end will be saved.
10:23 When they persecute you in this city, flee to the other. For truly I tell you, you will certainly not finish going through the cities of Israel until the Son of Man comes.
10:24 A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a slave above his master.
10:25 It is enough for the disciple to become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more his household members!
10:26 So do not fear them. For nothing is covered that will not be revealed, or hidden that will not become known.
10:27 What I tell you in the darkness, say in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim upon the housetops.
10:28 Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear Him who can destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.
10:29 Are not two sparrows sold for an assarion? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground apart from your Father.
10:30 Even the hairs of your head are all numbered.
10:31 So do not fear; you are worth more than many sparrows.
10:32 Therefore everyone who acknowledges Me before people, I too will acknowledge before My Father in the heavens.
10:33 But whoever denies Me before people, I too will deny before My Father in the heavens.
10:34 Do not suppose that I came to bring peace upon the earth. I did not come to bring peace, but a sword.
10:35 For I came to divide a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law,
10:36 and a person’s enemies will be the members of his own household.
10:37 Whoever loves father or mother more than Me is not worthy of Me; and whoever loves son or daughter more than Me is not worthy of Me.
10:38 Whoever does not take his cross and follow after Me is not worthy of Me.
10:39 Whoever finds his life will lose it, and whoever loses his life because of Me will find it.
10:40 Whoever receives you receives Me, and whoever receives Me receives the One who sent Me.
10:41 Whoever receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward, and whoever receives a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will receive a righteous person’s reward.
10:42 And whoever gives one of these little ones even a cup of cold water in the name of a disciple, truly I tell you, will certainly not lose his reward.”

MATTHEW 11

11:1 When Jesus finished directing His twelve disciples, He moved on from there to teach and proclaim in their cities.
11:2 John, hearing in prison about the works of the Christ, sent through his disciples
11:3 and said to Him, “Art Thou the One who is coming, or should we expect another?”
11:4 Jesus answered them, “Go and report to John what you hear and see:
11:5 the blind receive sight and the lame walk, lepers are cleansed and the deaf hear, the dead are raised and the poor have good news proclaimed to them.
11:6 And blessed is whoever does not stumble because of Me.”
11:7 As they went away, Jesus began to speak to the crowds about John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to behold? A reed shaken by the wind?
11:8 But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft clothing? Behold, those wearing soft clothing are in kings’ houses.
11:9 But why did you go out? To see a prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.
11:10 This is the one about whom it is written: ‘Behold, I send My messenger before Thy face, who will prepare Thy way before Thee.’
11:11 Truly I tell you, among those born of women none greater than John the Baptist has arisen; yet the least in the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.
11:12 From the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of the heavens suffers violence, and violent men seize it.
11:13 For all the Prophets and the Law prophesied until John.
11:14 And if you are willing to receive it, he is Elijah who is to come.
11:15 Let the one who has ears hear.
11:16 To what shall I compare this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces who call to the others,
11:17 ‘We played the flute for you, and you did not dance; we sang a lament, and you did not beat your breasts.’
11:18 For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon.’
11:19 The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Behold, a gluttonous man and a wine drinker, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ Yet wisdom was vindicated by her works.”
11:20 Then He began to reproach the cities in which most of His mighty works had occurred, because they did not repent:
11:21 “Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the mighty works that occurred in you had occurred in Tyre and Sidon, they would long ago have repented in sackcloth and ashes.
11:22 Nevertheless I tell you, it will be more bearable for Tyre and Sidon on the day of judgment than for you.
11:23 And you, Capernaum, will you be exalted to heaven? You will descend to Hades! For if the mighty works that occurred in you had occurred in Sodom, it would have remained until today.
11:24 Nevertheless I tell you, it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom on the day of judgment than for you.”
11:25 At that time Jesus answered, “I acknowledge Thee, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because Thou didst hide these things from the wise and understanding and reveal them to infants.
11:26 Yes, Father, because this was pleasing before Thee.
11:27 All things have been handed over to Me by My Father. No one knows the Son except the Father, nor does anyone know the Father except the Son and anyone to whom the Son wishes to reveal Him.
11:28 Come to Me, all you who labour and are burdened, and I will give you rest.
11:29 Take My yoke upon you, and learn from Me; for I am meek and lowly in heart, and you shall find rest for your souls.
11:30 For My yoke is easy, and My burden is light.”

MATTHEW 12

12:1 At that time Jesus went through the grainfields on the Sabbath; and His disciples grew hungry, and began to pluck heads of grain and eat.
12:2 And the Pharisees, seeing it, said to Him, “Behold, Thy disciples do what is not lawful on the Sabbath.”
12:3 He said to them, “Have you not read what David did when he and those with him became hungry:
12:4 how he entered the house of God, and they ate the bread of the Presence, which it was not lawful for him or those with him to eat, but only for the priests?
12:5 Or have you not read in the Law that on Sabbaths the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath and are guiltless?
12:6 But I tell you that something greater than the temple is here.
12:7 If you had known what ‘I desire mercy and not sacrifice’ means, you would not have condemned the guiltless.
12:8 For the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.”
12:9 Moving on from there, He entered their synagogue.
12:10 And behold, a man had a withered hand. They asked Him, “Is it lawful to heal on Sabbaths?” so that they might accuse Him.
12:11 He said to them, “Who among you, if he has one sheep and it falls into a pit on a Sabbath, will not grasp it and lift it out?
12:12 How much more valuable, then, is a human being than a sheep! So it is lawful to do good on Sabbaths.”
12:13 Then He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He stretched it out, and it was restored, healthy like the other.
12:14 The Pharisees went out and took counsel against Him, how they might destroy Him.
12:15 But Jesus, knowing this, withdrew from there. Great crowds followed Him, and He healed them all.
12:16 He sternly warned them not to make Him known,
12:17 so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled:
12:18 “Behold, My Servant whom I chose, My beloved in whom My soul was well pleased. I will put My Spirit upon Him, and He will proclaim justice to the nations.
12:19 He will not quarrel or cry out, nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets.
12:20 He will not break a bruised reed or extinguish a smoldering wick, until He brings justice forth to victory.
12:21 And nations will hope in His name.”
12:22 Then a man oppressed by a demon, blind and mute, was brought to Him. He healed him, so that the mute man spoke and saw.
12:23 All the crowds were amazed and said, “Could this be the Son of David?”
12:24 But when the Pharisees heard it, they said, “This Man casts out demons only by Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons.”
12:25 Knowing their thoughts, He said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand.
12:26 If Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his kingdom stand?
12:27 And if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they will be your judges.
12:28 But if I cast out demons by the Spirit of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.
12:29 Or how can someone enter the strong man’s house and seize his belongings unless he first binds the strong man? Then he will plunder his house.
12:30 Whoever is not with Me is against Me, and whoever does not gather with Me scatters.
12:31 Therefore I tell you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven.
12:32 Whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the coming one.
12:33 Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree rotten and its fruit rotten; for the tree is known by the fruit.
12:34 Brood of vipers! How can you speak good things when you are evil? For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.
12:35 The good person brings forth good things from the good treasure, and the evil person brings forth evil things from the evil treasure.
12:36 But I tell you that people will give account on the day of judgment for every idle word they speak.
12:37 For by your words you will be justified, and by your words you will be condemned.”
12:38 Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered Him, saying, “Teacher, we wish to see a sign from Thee.”
12:39 He answered them, “An evil and adulterous generation seeks a sign, but no sign will be given it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.
12:40 For just as Jonah was in the sea creature’s belly three days and three nights, so the Son of Man will be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.
12:41 Men of Nineveh will rise at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at Jonah’s proclamation; and behold, something greater than Jonah is here.
12:42 The queen of the south will be raised at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon’s wisdom; and behold, something greater than Solomon is here.
12:43 When the unclean spirit leaves a person, it passes through waterless places seeking rest, and finds none.
12:44 Then it says, ‘I will return to my house from which I came.’ When it comes, it finds it vacant, swept, and put in order.
12:45 Then it goes and takes with itself seven other spirits more evil than itself; they enter and dwell there, and that person’s last condition becomes worse than the first. So it will also be with this evil generation.”
12:46 While He was still speaking to the crowds, behold, His mother and brothers stood outside, seeking to speak with Him.
12:47 Someone said to Him, “Behold, Thy mother and Thy brothers stand outside, seeking to speak with Thee.”
12:48 He answered the one speaking to Him, “Who is My mother, and who are My brothers?”
12:49 Stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, “Behold, My mother and My brothers!
12:50 For whoever does the will of My Father in the heavens is My brother and sister and mother.”

MATTHEW 13

13:1 On that day Jesus went out of the house and sat beside the sea.
13:2 Great crowds gathered to Him, so that He entered a boat and sat down, while all the crowd stood on the shore.
13:3 He spoke many things to them in parables, saying, “Behold, the sower went out to sow.
13:4 As he sowed, some seeds fell beside the road, and the birds came and devoured them.
13:5 Others fell on rocky places, where they did not have much soil, and immediately sprang up because they had no depth of soil.
13:6 But when the sun rose, they were scorched, and because they had no root they withered.
13:7 Others fell among thorns; the thorns grew up and choked them.
13:8 Others fell on the good soil and yielded fruit: some a hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.
13:9 Let the one who has ears hear.”
13:10 The disciples came and said to Him, “Why dost Thou speak to them in parables?”
13:11 He answered them, “To you it has been given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of the heavens, but to them it has not been given.
13:12 For whoever has, to him more will be given, and he will have abundance; but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.
13:13 Therefore I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear or understand.
13:14 In them is fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy, which says: ‘You will hear and hear but certainly not understand; you will look and look but certainly not see.
13:15 For the heart of this people has grown fat, and with their ears they heard with difficulty, and they closed their eyes, lest they see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their heart, and turn, and I will heal them.’
13:16 But blessed are your eyes, because they see, and your ears, because they hear.
13:17 For truly I tell you, many prophets and righteous people desired to see what you see and did not see it, and to hear what you hear and did not hear it.
13:18 You, then, hear the parable of the sower.
13:19 When anyone hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand, the evil one comes and snatches what was sown in his heart. This is the one sown beside the road.
13:20 The one sown on rocky places is the one who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy,
13:21 yet has no root in himself, but lasts only a short time. When affliction or persecution arises because of the word, he immediately stumbles.
13:22 The one sown among the thorns is the one who hears the word, but the anxiety of this age and the deceit of riches choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.
13:23 The one sown upon the good soil is the one who hears the word and understands; he indeed bears fruit and produces, some a hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.”
13:24 He set another parable before them, saying, “The kingdom of the heavens was likened to a man who sowed good seed in his field.
13:25 While people slept, his enemy came and sowed darnel among the wheat and went away.
13:26 When the crop sprouted and bore fruit, then the darnel appeared also.
13:27 The householder’s slaves came and said to him, ‘Master, did you not sow good seed in your field? From where, then, does it have darnel?’
13:28 He said to them, ‘An enemy did this.’ The slaves said to him, ‘Do you want us, then, to go and gather it?’
13:29 He said, ‘No, lest while gathering the darnel you uproot the wheat with it.
13:30 Let both grow together until the harvest. At harvest time I will tell the reapers: Gather the darnel first and bind it into bundles to burn it, but gather the wheat into my storehouse.’”
13:31 He set another parable before them, saying, “The kingdom of the heavens is like a mustard seed, which a man took and sowed in his field.
13:32 It is smaller than all the seeds, but when it grows it is greater than the garden plants and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the sky come and rest in its branches.”
13:33 He spoke another parable to them: “The kingdom of the heavens is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three sata of flour until the whole was leavened.”
13:34 Jesus spoke all these things to the crowds in parables, and He spoke nothing to them without a parable,
13:35 so that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled: “I will open My mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from the foundation of the world.”
13:36 Then He left the crowds and entered the house. His disciples came to Him, saying, “Explain to us the parable of the darnel in the field.”
13:37 He answered, “The One sowing the good seed is the Son of Man.
13:38 The field is the world; the good seed are the sons of the kingdom; the darnel are the sons of the evil one;
13:39 the enemy who sowed them is the devil; the harvest is the consummation of the age; and the reapers are angels.
13:40 Just as the darnel is gathered and burned with fire, so it will be at the consummation of the age.
13:41 The Son of Man will send His angels, and they will gather from His kingdom all causes of stumbling and those who practice lawlessness.
13:42 They will cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.
13:43 Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. Let the one who has ears hear.
13:44 The kingdom of the heavens is like treasure hidden in a field, which a man found and hid. In his joy he goes and sells everything he has and buys that field.
13:45 Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a merchant seeking fine pearls.
13:46 Finding one pearl of great value, he went and sold everything he had and bought it.
13:47 Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a dragnet cast into the sea and gathering fish of every kind.
13:48 When it was full, they drew it onto the shore, sat down, and gathered the good into containers, but threw the rotten away.
13:49 So it will be at the consummation of the age: the angels will go out and separate the evil from the midst of the righteous
13:50 and cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.
13:51 Have you understood all these things?” They said to Him, “Yes.”
13:52 He said to them, “Therefore every scribe made a disciple for the kingdom of the heavens is like a householder who brings out of his treasure things new and old.”
13:53 When Jesus finished these parables, He departed from there.
13:54 Coming to His hometown, He taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished and said, “Where did this Man get this wisdom and these mighty works?
13:55 Is He not the craftsman’s son? Is His mother not called Mary, and His brothers James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas?
13:56 And are not all His sisters with us? Where, then, did this Man get all these things?”
13:57 They stumbled because of Him. But Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor except in his hometown and his own house.”
13:58 He did not do many mighty works there because of their unbelief.

MATTHEW 14

14:1 At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the report about Jesus
14:2 and said to his attendants, “This is John the Baptist. He has been raised from the dead, and therefore the powers are at work in him.”
14:3 For Herod had seized John, bound him, and put him in prison because of Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip.
14:4 For John kept saying to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”
14:5 Although he wanted to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they regarded him as a prophet.
14:6 When Herod’s birthday celebration came, Herodias’s daughter danced in their midst and pleased Herod.
14:7 Therefore he promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked.
14:8 Prompted by her mother, she said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist.”
14:9 Although the king was grieved, because of his oaths and those reclining with him, he ordered it to be given.
14:10 He sent and had John beheaded in the prison.
14:11 His head was brought on a platter and given to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.
14:12 His disciples came, took the corpse, and buried him; then they went and reported to Jesus.
14:13 When Jesus heard this, He withdrew from there by boat to a deserted place privately. Hearing of it, the crowds followed Him on foot from the cities.
14:14 Coming out, He saw a great crowd, was moved with compassion for them, and healed their sick.
14:15 When evening came, the disciples approached Him, saying, “The place is deserted, and the hour has already passed. Dismiss the crowds so that they may go to the villages and buy food for themselves.”
14:16 But Jesus said to them, “They have no need to go away. You give them something to eat.”
14:17 They said to Him, “We have here only five loaves and two fish.”
14:18 He said, “Bring them here to Me.”
14:19 He ordered the crowds to recline on the grass. Taking the five loaves and the two fish, He looked up to heaven, blessed, broke the loaves, and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.
14:20 They all ate and were filled, and they took up the surplus fragments: twelve baskets full.
14:21 Those eating were about five thousand men, besides women and children.
14:22 Immediately He compelled the disciples to enter a boat and go ahead of Him to the other side, while He dismissed the crowds.
14:23 After dismissing the crowds, He went up the mountain privately to pray. When evening came, He was there alone.
14:24 The boat was already many stadia from land, battered by the waves, for the wind was against them.
14:25 In the fourth watch of the night He came toward them, walking upon the sea.
14:26 When the disciples saw Him walking upon the sea, they were troubled and said, “It is an apparition!” They cried out in fear.
14:27 Immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, “Take courage; it is I. Do not fear.”
14:28 Peter answered Him, “Lord, if it is Thou, command me to come to Thee upon the waters.”
14:29 He said, “Come.” Peter got down from the boat, walked upon the waters, and came toward Jesus.
14:30 But seeing the strong wind, he became afraid; beginning to sink, he cried out, “Lord, save me!”
14:31 Immediately Jesus stretched out His hand, grasped him, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?”
14:32 When they climbed into the boat, the wind ceased.
14:33 Those in the boat worshiped Him, saying, “Truly Thou art God’s Son.”
14:34 Having crossed over, they came to land at Gennesaret.
14:35 The men of that place recognized Him and sent into all the surrounding region. They brought Him all who were ill
14:36 and begged Him that they might only touch the fringe of His garment. As many as touched it were made well.

MATTHEW 15

15:1 Then Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem came to Jesus, saying,
15:2 “Why do Thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread.”
15:3 He answered them, “Why do you also transgress God’s commandment because of your tradition?
15:4 For God said, ‘Honor father and mother,’ and ‘Whoever reviles father or mother must surely die.’
15:5 But you say, ‘Whoever tells his father or mother, “Whatever benefit you might receive from me is a gift,”
15:6 will certainly not honor his father.’ You have thus nullified the word of God because of your tradition.
15:7 Hypocrites! Isaiah prophesied well about you, saying,
15:8 ‘This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me.
15:9 In vain they worship Me, teaching human commandments as teachings.’”
15:10 He summoned the crowd and said to them, “Hear and understand:
15:11 what enters the mouth does not defile a person, but what comes out of the mouth—this defiles a person.”
15:12 Then the disciples came and said to Him, “Dost Thou know that the Pharisees stumbled when they heard this saying?”
15:13 He answered, “Every plant that My heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.
15:14 Leave them; they are blind guides of the blind. If a blind person guides a blind person, both will fall into a pit.”
15:15 Peter answered Him, “Explain this parable to us.”
15:16 He said, “Are you also still without understanding?
15:17 Do you not understand that everything entering the mouth goes into the stomach and is expelled into the latrine?
15:18 But the things coming out of the mouth come from the heart, and those defile a person.
15:19 For out of the heart come evil reasonings, murders, adulteries, sexual immoralities, thefts, false testimonies, and blasphemies.
15:20 These are the things that defile a person, but eating with unwashed hands does not defile a person.”
15:21 Jesus went out from there and withdrew to the regions of Tyre and Sidon.
15:22 And behold, a Canaanite woman from those territories came out and cried, “Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is terribly oppressed by a demon.”
15:23 But He did not answer her a word. His disciples came and asked Him, “Dismiss her, because she is crying out behind us.”
15:24 He answered, “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”
15:25 She came and bowed before Him, saying, “Lord, help me.”
15:26 He answered, “It is not good to take the children’s bread and throw it to the little dogs.”
15:27 She said, “Yes, Lord, for even the little dogs eat from the crumbs falling from their masters’ table.”
15:28 Then Jesus answered her, “Woman, great is your faith! Let it happen for you as you wish.” Her daughter was healed from that hour.
15:29 Moving on from there, Jesus came beside the Sea of Galilee. He went up the mountain and sat there.
15:30 Great crowds came to Him, bringing with them lame, blind, maimed, mute, and many others. They laid them at His feet, and He healed them,
15:31 so that the crowd marveled when they saw mute people speaking, maimed people healthy, lame people walking, and blind people seeing. They glorified the God of Israel.
15:32 Jesus summoned His disciples and said, “I am moved with compassion for the crowd, because they have already stayed with Me three days and have nothing to eat. I am unwilling to dismiss them fasting, lest they faint on the road.”
15:33 The disciples said to Him, “Where can we get so many loaves in a deserted place, enough to fill so great a crowd?”
15:34 Jesus said to them, “How many loaves do you have?” They said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”
15:35 He instructed the crowd to recline on the ground,
15:36 took the seven loaves and the fish, gave thanks, broke them, and kept giving them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.
15:37 They all ate and were filled, and they took up the surplus fragments: seven large baskets full.
15:38 Those eating were four thousand men, besides women and children.
15:39 After dismissing the crowds, He entered the boat and came to the territory of Magadan.

MATTHEW 16

16:1 The Pharisees and Sadducees came and, testing Him, asked Him to show them a sign from heaven.
16:2 He answered them, “When evening comes, you say, ‘Fair weather, for the sky is red.’
16:3 And in the morning, ‘Today a storm, for the sky is red and gloomy.’ You know how to distinguish the face of the sky, but can you not distinguish the signs of the times?
16:4 An evil and adulterous generation seeks a sign, but no sign will be given it except the sign of Jonah.” He left them and went away.
16:5 When the disciples came to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread.
16:6 Jesus said to them, “Look out and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”
16:7 They reasoned among themselves, saying, “We did not take bread.”
16:8 Knowing this, Jesus said, “Why do you reason among yourselves, people of little faith, because you have no bread?
16:9 Do you not yet understand or remember the five loaves for the five thousand, and how many baskets you took up?
16:10 Or the seven loaves for the four thousand, and how many large baskets you took up?
16:11 How do you not understand that I did not speak to you about bread? But beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”
16:12 Then they understood that He did not tell them to beware of the leaven of bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.
16:13 When Jesus came into the regions of Caesarea Philippi, He asked His disciples, “Who do people say the Son of Man is?”
16:14 They said, “Some John the Baptist; others Elijah; still others Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”
16:15 He said to them, “But you, who do you say I am?”
16:16 Simon Peter answered, “Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God.”
16:17 Jesus answered him, “Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but My Father in the heavens.
16:18 And I tell you that you are Peter, and upon this rock I will build My Church, and the gates of Hades shall not prevail against it.
16:19 I will give you the keys of the kingdom of the heavens. Whatever you bind upon earth will have been bound in the heavens, and whatever you loose upon earth will have been loosed in the heavens.”
16:20 Then He instructed the disciples to tell no one that He was the Christ.
16:21 From then on Jesus began to show His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem, suffer many things from the elders, chief priests, and scribes, be killed, and on the third day be raised.
16:22 Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him, saying, “May mercy be Thine, Lord! This shall certainly never happen to Thee.”
16:23 But He turned and said to Peter, “Get behind Me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to Me, because you do not set your mind on the things of God, but on the things of human beings.”
16:24 Then Jesus said to His disciples, “If anyone wishes to come after Me, let him deny himself, take up his cross, and follow Me.
16:25 For whoever wishes to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life because of Me will find it.
16:26 For what will a person gain if he gains the whole world but forfeits his life? Or what will a person give in exchange for his life?
16:27 For the Son of Man is to come in the glory of His Father with His angels, and then He will repay each person according to his conduct.
16:28 Truly I tell you that some standing here will certainly not taste death until they see the Son of Man coming in His kingdom.”

MATTHEW 17

17:1 After six days Jesus took Peter, James, and John his brother, and led them up a high mountain privately.
17:2 He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun, and His garments became white like the light.
17:3 And behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, conversing with Him.
17:4 Peter answered Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If Thou wishest, I will make three tents here: one for Thee, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”
17:5 While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. And behold, a voice from the cloud said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Hear Him.”
17:6 When the disciples heard, they fell on their faces and were exceedingly afraid.
17:7 Jesus came, touched them, and said, “Rise, and do not fear.”
17:8 Lifting their eyes, they saw no one except Jesus Himself alone.
17:9 As they came down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them, “Tell the vision to no one until the Son of Man is raised from the dead.”
17:10 The disciples asked Him, “Why, then, do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”
17:11 He answered, “Elijah indeed comes and will restore all things.
17:12 But I tell you that Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did to him whatever they wished. In the same way the Son of Man is also to suffer at their hands.”
17:13 Then the disciples understood that He spoke to them about John the Baptist.
17:14 When they came to the crowd, a man approached Him, kneeling before Him
17:15 and saying, “Lord, have mercy on my son, because he suffers seizures and is badly afflicted; for he often falls into fire and often into water.
17:16 I brought him to Thy disciples, but they could not heal him.”
17:17 Jesus answered, “O faithless and perverted generation, how long will I be with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him here to Me.”
17:18 Jesus rebuked him, and the demon came out of him; the boy was healed from that hour.
17:19 Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, “Why could we not cast it out?”
17:20 He said to them, “Because of your little faith. For truly I tell you, if you have faith like a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible for you.”
17:22 While they gathered in Galilee, Jesus said to them, “The Son of Man is about to be handed over into human hands.
17:23 They will kill Him, and on the third day He will be raised.” They were exceedingly grieved.
17:24 And when they came to Capernaum, those who collected the didrachmas came to Peter and said, “Does not your Teacher pay the didrachmas?”
17:25 He says, “Yes.” And when he entered the house, Jesus spoke first to him, saying, “What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth receive toll or tribute: from their sons, or from strangers?”
17:26 And when he said, “From strangers,” Jesus said to him, “Then the sons are free.
17:27 But lest we cause them to stumble, go to the sea and cast a hook, and take the first fish that comes up; and when you open its mouth, you will find a stater. Take it and give it to them for Me and you.”

MATTHEW 18

18:1 At that hour the disciples came to Jesus, saying, “Who, then, is greatest in the kingdom of the heavens?”
18:2 He called a child and stood the child in their midst,
18:3 and said, “Truly I tell you, unless you turn and become like children, you will certainly not enter the kingdom of the heavens.
18:4 Therefore whoever humbles himself like this child is greatest in the kingdom of the heavens.
18:5 And whoever receives one such child in My name receives Me.
18:6 But whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to stumble, it is better for him that a donkey-driven millstone be hung around his neck and he be sunk in the open sea.
18:7 Woe to the world because of stumbling blocks! For stumbling blocks must come, but woe to the person through whom the stumbling block comes!
18:8 If your hand or foot causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you. It is better for you to enter life maimed or lame than, having two hands or two feet, to be cast into the eternal fire.
18:9 And if your eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away from you. It is better for you to enter life with one eye than, having two eyes, to be cast into the Gehenna of fire.
18:10 See that you do not despise one of these little ones, for I tell you that their angels in the heavens continually see the face of My Father in the heavens.
18:12 What do you think? If someone has a hundred sheep and one of them strays, will he not leave the ninety-nine on the mountains and go seeking the one that strays?
18:13 If he succeeds in finding it, truly I tell you that he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine that have not strayed.
18:14 In the same way, it is not the will of your Father in the heavens that one of these little ones perish.
18:15 If your brother sins against you, go and reprove him between you and him alone. If he hears you, you have gained your brother.
18:16 But if he does not hear, take one or two more with you, so that every matter may be established upon the mouth of two or three witnesses.
18:17 If he refuses to hear them, tell the Church. And if he refuses to hear even the Church, let him be to you like the Gentile and the tax collector.
18:18 Truly I tell you, whatever you bind upon earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever you loose upon earth will have been loosed in heaven.
18:19 Again, truly I tell you that if two of you agree upon earth concerning any matter they ask, it will be done for them by My Father in the heavens.
18:20 For where two or three are gathered into My name, there I am in their midst.”
18:21 Then Peter came and said to Him, “Lord, how often will my brother sin against me and I forgive him? Up to seven times?”
18:22 Jesus says to him, “I tell you, not up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven.
18:23 Therefore the kingdom of the heavens was likened to a king who wished to settle accounts with his slaves.
18:24 And when he began to settle them, one was brought to him who owed ten thousand talents.
18:25 And since he did not have the means to repay, his master commanded that he be sold, with his wife and children and all that he had, and that payment be made.
18:26 The slave therefore fell down and did him homage, saying, ‘Have patience with me, and I will repay you everything.’
18:27 And the master of that slave, moved with compassion, released him and forgave him the loan.
18:28 But that slave went out and found one of his fellow slaves who owed him a hundred denarii. He seized him and began choking him, saying, ‘Repay whatever you owe.’
18:29 So his fellow slave fell down and pleaded with him, saying, ‘Be patient with me, and I will repay you.’
18:30 But he was unwilling. Instead, he went and threw him into prison until he repaid what was owed.
18:31 When his fellow slaves saw what had happened, they were exceedingly grieved. They came and explained to their master everything that had happened.
18:32 Then his master summoned him and said, ‘Evil slave! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me.
18:33 Should you not also have had mercy on your fellow slave, as I had mercy on you?’
18:34 His master became angry and handed him over to the torturers until he repaid everything owed.
18:35 So also My heavenly Father will do to you, unless each of you forgives his brother from your hearts.”

MATTHEW 19

19:1 When Jesus finished these words, He departed from Galilee and came into the territories of Judea beyond the Jordan.
19:2 Great crowds followed Him, and He healed them there.
19:3 Pharisees came to Him, testing Him and saying, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any cause?”
19:4 He answered, “Have you not read that the Creator from the beginning made them male and female,
19:5 and said, ‘For this reason a man will leave father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh’?
19:6 So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has yoked together, let no human being separate.”
19:7 They said to Him, “Why then did Moses command giving a certificate of divorce and divorcing her?”
19:8 He said to them, “Because of your hardness of heart Moses permitted you to divorce your wives, but from the beginning it has not been so.
19:9 I tell you that whoever divorces his wife, except on account of sexual immorality, and marries another commits adultery.”
19:10 His disciples said to Him, “If this is the situation of the man with the wife, it is better not to marry.”
19:11 He said to them, “Not everyone can receive this saying, but only those to whom it has been given.
19:12 For there are eunuchs who were born so from their mother’s womb, there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by human beings, and there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs because of the kingdom of the heavens. Let the one able to receive it receive it.”
19:13 Then children were brought to Him so that He might lay His hands upon them and pray. But the disciples rebuked them.
19:14 Jesus said, “Let the children come, and do not hinder them from coming to Me, for the kingdom of the heavens belongs to such as these.”
19:15 After laying His hands upon them, He went on from there.
19:16 And behold, someone came and said to Him, “Teacher, what good thing shall I do to have eternal life?”
19:17 He said to him, “Why do you ask Me about what is good? One is good. But if you wish to enter life, keep the commandments.”
19:18 He said to Him, “Which ones?” Jesus said, “‘You shall not murder; you shall not commit adultery; you shall not steal; you shall not bear false witness;
19:19 honor father and mother’; and ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’”
19:20 The young man said to Him, “I have kept all these. What do I still lack?”
19:21 Jesus said to him, “If you wish to be perfect, go, sell your belongings, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in the heavens. Then come, follow Me.”
19:22 When the young man heard the saying, he went away grieved, for he had many properties.
19:23 Jesus said to His disciples, “Truly I tell you that a rich person will enter the kingdom of the heavens with difficulty.
19:24 Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to enter through the hole of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”
19:25 When the disciples heard this, they were exceedingly astonished, saying, “Who then can be saved?”
19:26 Looking at them, Jesus said, “With human beings this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.”
19:27 Then Peter answered Him, “Behold, we have left everything and followed Thee. What then will there be for us?”
19:28 Jesus said to them, “Truly I tell you that you who have followed Me, in the regeneration, when the Son of Man sits upon the throne of His glory, shall also sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
19:29 Everyone who has left houses, brothers, sisters, father, mother, children, or fields because of My name will receive a hundredfold and inherit eternal life.
19:30 But many who are first will be last, and the last first.

MATTHEW 20

20:1 For the kingdom of the heavens is like a householder who went out at dawn to hire labourers for his vineyard.
20:2 And having agreed with the labourers for a denarius for the day, he sent them into his vineyard.
20:3 And going out about the third hour, he saw others standing idle in the marketplace;
20:4 and to them he said, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and whatever is just I will give you.’
20:5 They went. Again he went out about the sixth and ninth hours and did the same.
20:6 About the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing, and said to them, ‘Why have you stood here idle the whole day?’
20:7 They said to him, ‘Because no one hired us.’ He said to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard.’
20:8 When evening came, the master of the vineyard said to his steward, ‘Call the workers and pay them the wage, beginning with the last up to the first.’
20:9 Those hired about the eleventh hour came and received a denarius each.
20:10 When the first came, they supposed they would receive more, but they too received a denarius each.
20:11 On receiving it, they grumbled against the householder,
20:12 saying, ‘These last worked one hour, and you made them equal to us who bore the day’s burden and scorching heat.’
20:13 He answered one of them, ‘Friend, I am not wronging you. Did you not agree with me for a denarius?
20:14 Take what is yours and go. I wish to give to this last one the same as to you.
20:15 Am I not permitted to do what I wish with what is mine? Or is your eye evil because I am good?’
20:16 So the last will be first, and the first last.”
20:17 As Jesus went up to Jerusalem, He took the twelve disciples privately, and on the road He said to them,
20:18 “Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and scribes. They will condemn Him to death
20:19 and hand Him over to the Gentiles to mock, flog, and crucify Him; and on the third day He will be raised.”
20:20 Then the mother of Zebedee’s sons came to Him with her sons, bowing before Him and asking something from Him.
20:21 He said to her, “What do you wish?” She said to Him, “Command that these two sons of mine sit, one at Thy right and one at Thy left, in Thy kingdom.”
20:22 Jesus answered, “You do not know what you ask. Are you able to drink the cup that I am about to drink?” They said to Him, “We are able.”
20:23 He said to them, “You will indeed drink My cup, but to sit at My right and left is not Mine to give, except to those for whom it has been prepared by My Father.”
20:24 When the ten heard, they became indignant about the two brothers.
20:25 Jesus summoned them and said, “You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them.
20:26 It shall not be so among you. Instead, whoever wishes to become great among you will be your servant,
20:27 and whoever wishes to be first among you will be your slave,
20:28 just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve and to give His life as a ransom in place of many.”
20:29 As they went out from Jericho, a great crowd followed Him.
20:30 And behold, two blind men sitting beside the road heard that Jesus was passing by and cried out, “Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!”
20:31 The crowd rebuked them to make them silent, but they cried out all the more, “Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!”
20:32 Jesus stopped, called them, and said, “What do you wish Me to do for you?”
20:33 They said to Him, “Lord, that our eyes may be opened.”
20:34 Moved with compassion, Jesus touched their eyes. Immediately they received sight and followed Him.

MATTHEW 21

21:1 When they drew near Jerusalem and came to Bethphage, to the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples,
21:2 saying to them, “Go into the village opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied up and a colt with her. Untie them and bring them to Me.
21:3 If anyone says anything to you, say, ‘The Lord has need of them,’ and immediately he will send them.”
21:4 This happened so that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled:
21:5 “Tell the daughter of Zion: ‘Behold, your King comes to you, meek and mounted upon a donkey, and upon a colt, the foal of a beast of burden.’”
21:6 The disciples went and did as Jesus directed them.
21:7 They brought the donkey and the colt, placed the garments upon them, and He sat upon them.
21:8 Most of the crowd spread their garments on the road, while others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.
21:9 The crowds going before Him and those following cried out, “Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is the One coming in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!”
21:10 When He entered Jerusalem, the whole city was shaken, saying, “Who is this?”
21:11 The crowds said, “This is the prophet Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee.”
21:12 Jesus entered the temple and cast out all those selling and buying in the temple. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves.
21:13 He said to them, “It is written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer,’ but you make it a robbers’ cave.”
21:14 Blind and lame people came to Him in the temple, and He healed them.
21:15 But when the chief priests and scribes saw the wonders He did and the children crying in the temple, “Hosanna to the Son of David,” they became indignant
21:16 and said to Him, “Dost Thou hear what these are saying?” Jesus said to them, “Yes. Have you never read, ‘Out of the mouth of infants and nursing children Thou didst prepare praise’?”
21:17 Leaving them, He went outside the city to Bethany and spent the night there.
21:18 In the morning, as He returned to the city, He became hungry.
21:19 Seeing a fig tree beside the road, He came to it and found nothing on it except leaves alone. He said to it, “Let no fruit ever come from you again.” Immediately the fig tree withered.
21:20 When the disciples saw it, they marveled, saying, “How did the fig tree wither immediately?”
21:21 Jesus answered them, “Truly I tell you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will do not only what was done to the fig tree; even if you say to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and cast into the sea,’ it will happen.
21:22 Everything you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive.”
21:23 When He entered the temple, the chief priests and elders of the people approached Him as He taught, saying, “By what authority dost Thou do these things? And who gave Thee this authority?”
21:24 Jesus answered them, “I too will ask you one thing. If you tell Me, I too will tell you by what authority I do these things.
21:25 John’s baptism—from where was it? From heaven or from human beings?” They reasoned among themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will say to us, ‘Why, then, did you not believe him?’
21:26 But if we say, ‘From human beings,’ we fear the crowd, for all regard John as a prophet.”
21:27 They answered Jesus, “We do not know.” He also said to them, “Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.
21:28 But what do you think? A man had two children. He came to the first and said, ‘Child, go work in the vineyard today.’
21:29 He answered, ‘I do not want to,’ but afterward he changed his mind and went.
21:30 He came to the second and said the same. He answered, ‘I will, sir,’ but did not go.
21:31 Which of the two did the father’s will?” They said, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Truly I tell you that tax collectors and prostitutes go ahead of you into the kingdom of God.
21:32 For John came to you in the way of righteousness and you did not believe him, but tax collectors and prostitutes believed him. Even after seeing this, you did not afterward change your minds and believe him.
21:33 Hear another parable. A householder planted a vineyard, put a fence around it, dug a winepress in it, and built a tower. He leased it to farmers and traveled abroad.
21:34 When the season of fruits drew near, he sent his slaves to the farmers to receive his fruits.
21:35 The farmers seized his slaves: one they beat, another they killed, another they stoned.
21:36 Again he sent other slaves, more than the first, and they did the same to them.
21:37 Last he sent his son to them, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’
21:38 But when the farmers saw the son, they said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him and take his inheritance.’
21:39 They seized him, cast him outside the vineyard, and killed him.
21:40 So when the master of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?”
21:41 They said to Him, “He will miserably destroy those miserable men, and lease the vineyard to other farmers who will give him the fruits in their seasons.”
21:42 Jesus said to them, “Have you never read in the Scriptures: ‘The stone that the builders rejected became the head of the corner; this came from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes’?
21:43 Therefore I tell you that the kingdom of God will be taken from you and given to a people producing its fruits.
21:44 And the one falling upon this stone will be shattered; but on whomever it falls, it will crush him.”
21:45 When the chief priests and Pharisees heard His parables, they knew He was speaking about them.
21:46 Although they sought to seize Him, they feared the crowds, because they regarded Him as a prophet.

MATTHEW 22

22:1 Jesus again answered them in parables, saying,
22:2 “The kingdom of the heavens was likened to a king who made a wedding feast for his son.
22:3 He sent his slaves to call those invited to the wedding feast, but they were unwilling to come.
22:4 Again he sent other slaves, saying, ‘Tell those invited: Behold, I have prepared my meal. My bulls and fattened animals have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.’
22:5 But they disregarded it and went away, one to his own field, another to his business.
22:6 The rest seized his slaves, mistreated them, and killed them.
22:7 The king became angry. He sent his troops, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.
22:8 Then he said to his slaves, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy.
22:9 So go to the outlets of the roads and invite to the wedding feast as many as you find.’
22:10 Those slaves went out into the roads and gathered everyone they found, both evil and good, and the wedding feast was filled with people reclining.
22:11 The king entered to see those reclining and saw there a man not clothed in a wedding garment.
22:12 He said to him, ‘Friend, how did you enter here without a wedding garment?’ He was speechless.
22:13 Then the king said to the attendants, ‘Bind his feet and hands and cast him into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.’
22:14 For many are called, but few chosen.”
22:15 Then the Pharisees went and took counsel how to trap Him in a statement.
22:16 They sent Him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that Thou art truthful and dost teach God’s way in truth. Thou art not swayed by anyone, for Thou dost not look at people’s outward status.
22:17 Tell us, then, what Thou thinkest: is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?”
22:18 Knowing their evil intent, Jesus said, “Why do you test Me, hypocrites?
22:19 Show Me the coin of the tribute.” And they brought Him a denarius.
22:20 And He says to them, “Whose image is this, and whose inscription?”
22:21 They say to Him, “Caesar’s.” Then He says to them, “Render therefore to Caesar the things of Caesar, and to God the things of God.”
22:22 When they heard, they marveled, left Him, and went away.
22:23 On that day Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Him and questioned Him,
22:24 saying, “Teacher, Moses said, ‘If someone dies without children, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.’
22:25 There were seven brothers among us. The first married and died, and having no offspring, left his wife to his brother.
22:26 Likewise the second and the third, up to the seventh.
22:27 Last of all, the woman died.
22:28 In the resurrection, then, whose wife of the seven will she be? For all had her.”
22:29 Jesus answered them, “You are mistaken, because you know neither the Scriptures nor the power of God.
22:30 For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.
22:31 But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God:
22:32 ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? He is not God of the dead, but of the living.”
22:33 When the crowds heard, they were astonished at His teaching.
22:34 Hearing that He had silenced the Sadducees, the Pharisees gathered together.
22:35 One of them, an expert in the Law, questioned Him to test Him:
22:36 “Teacher, which commandment is great in the Law?”
22:37 He said to him, “‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’
22:38 This is the great and first commandment.
22:39 A second is like it: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’
22:40 On these two commandments hang the whole Law and the Prophets.”
22:41 While the Pharisees were gathered, Jesus questioned them,
22:42 saying, “What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is He?” They said to Him, “David’s.”
22:43 He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call Him Lord, saying,
22:44 ‘The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at My right until I put Thy enemies beneath Thy feet’?
22:45 If David calls Him Lord, how is He his son?”
22:46 No one could answer Him a word, nor from that day did anyone dare question Him any longer.

MATTHEW 23

23:1 Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and His disciples,
23:2 saying, “The scribes and Pharisees have seated themselves in Moses’ chair.
23:3 Therefore do and observe everything they tell you, but do not act according to their works, for they say and do not do.
23:4 They bind heavy burdens, hard to carry, and lay them upon people’s shoulders, but they themselves are unwilling to move them with their finger.
23:5 They do all their works to be seen by people. They broaden their phylacteries and enlarge their fringes.
23:6 They love the foremost place at dinners and the foremost seats in the synagogues,
23:7 greetings in the marketplaces, and being called ‘Rabbi’ by people.
23:8 But you shall not be called ‘Rabbi,’ for One is your Teacher, and you are all brothers.
23:9 And call no one upon earth your father, for One is your Father, the heavenly One.
23:10 Nor shall you be called guides, because One is your Guide, the Christ.
23:11 But the greatest among you will be your servant.
23:12 Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.
23:13 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of the heavens before people! For you do not enter, nor do you permit those entering to enter.
23:15 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you travel around sea and dry land to make one proselyte, and when he becomes one, you make him a son of Gehenna twice as much as yourselves!
23:16 Woe to you, blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the sanctuary, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the sanctuary is bound.’
23:17 Fools and blind men! For which is greater, the gold or the sanctuary that sanctified the gold?
23:18 And, ‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift upon it is bound.’
23:19 Blind men! For which is greater, the gift or the altar that sanctifies the gift?
23:20 Therefore the one swearing by the altar swears by it and by everything upon it.
23:21 The one swearing by the sanctuary swears by it and by the One dwelling in it.
23:22 The one swearing by heaven swears by God’s throne and by the One seated upon it.
23:23 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you tithe mint, dill, and cumin, but have left aside the weightier matters of the Law: justice, mercy, and faithfulness! These you ought to have done without leaving those aside.
23:24 Blind guides, straining out the gnat but swallowing the camel!
23:25 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you cleanse the outside of the cup and dish, but inside they are full of plunder and self-indulgence!
23:26 Blind Pharisee, first cleanse the inside of the cup, so that its outside may also become clean.
23:27 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you resemble whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead people’s bones and all uncleanness!
23:28 So you too outwardly appear righteous to people, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.
23:29 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you build the prophets’ tombs and adorn the monuments of the righteous,
23:30 and say, ‘If we had lived in our fathers’ days, we would not have shared with them in the blood of the prophets.’
23:31 So you testify concerning yourselves that you are sons of those who murdered the prophets.
23:32 You, then, fill up the measure of your fathers!
23:33 Snakes, brood of vipers! How will you flee from the judgment of Gehenna?
23:34 Therefore behold, I send you prophets, wise people, and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and some you will flog in your synagogues and persecute from city to city,
23:35 so that upon you may come all righteous blood poured out upon earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar.
23:36 Truly I tell you, all these things will come upon this generation.
23:37 Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those sent to her! How often I wished to gather your children as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you were unwilling!
23:38 Behold, your house is left to you desolate.
23:39 For I tell you, you will certainly not see Me from now until you say, ‘Blessed is the One coming in the name of the Lord.’”

MATTHEW 24

24:1 Jesus went out from the temple and was going away, when His disciples came to show Him the temple buildings.
24:2 He answered them, “Do you not see all these things? Truly I tell you, not one stone will be left here upon another that will not be thrown down.”
24:3 As He sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately, saying, “Tell us, when will these things happen, and what is the sign of Thy coming and the consummation of the age?”
24:4 Jesus answered them, “See that no one leads you astray.
24:5 For many will come in My name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will lead many astray.
24:6 You will hear wars and reports of wars. See that you are not alarmed, for these things must happen, but the end is not yet.
24:7 For nation will rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines and earthquakes in various places.
24:8 All these things are the beginning of birth pains.
24:9 Then they will hand you over to affliction and kill you, and you will be hated by all nations because of My name.
24:10 Then many will stumble, hand one another over, and hate one another.
24:11 Many false prophets will arise and lead many astray.
24:12 Because lawlessness increases, the love of many will grow cold.
24:13 But the one who endures to the end will be saved.
24:14 This good news of the kingdom will be proclaimed in the whole inhabited world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come.
24:15 So when you see the abomination of desolation spoken of through Daniel the prophet standing in a holy place—let the reader understand—
24:16 then let those in Judea flee to the mountains.
24:17 Let the one on the housetop not go down to take things from his house,
24:18 and let the one in the field not turn back to take his cloak.
24:19 Woe to those with child and those nursing infants in those days!
24:20 Pray that your flight may not occur in winter or on a Sabbath.
24:21 For then there will be great affliction such as has not happened from the beginning of the world until now, and certainly will never happen again.
24:22 If those days had not been shortened, no flesh would have been saved; but because of the chosen, those days will be shortened.
24:23 Then if anyone tells you, ‘Behold, here is the Christ!’ or ‘Here!’ do not believe it.
24:24 For false christs and false prophets will arise and give great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen.
24:25 Behold, I have told you beforehand.
24:26 So if they tell you, ‘Behold, He is in the wilderness,’ do not go out; or ‘Behold, in the inner rooms,’ do not believe it.
24:27 For just as lightning comes from the east and shines to the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man.
24:28 Wherever the corpse is, there the eagles will gather.
24:29 Immediately after the affliction of those days, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its brightness, the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.
24:30 Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth will mourn; they will see the Son of Man coming upon the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.
24:31 He will send His angels with a great trumpet, and they will gather His chosen from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other.
24:32 Learn the parable from the fig tree: when its branch has already become tender and puts out leaves, you know that summer is near.
24:33 So also you, when you see all these things, know that it is near, at the doors.
24:34 Truly I tell you that this generation will certainly not pass away until all these things happen.
24:35 Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will certainly not pass away.
24:36 Concerning that day and hour, no one knows, not even the angels of the heavens, nor the Son, but the Father alone.
24:37 For just as the days of Noah were, so will be the coming of the Son of Man.
24:38 For in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day Noah entered the ark.
24:39 They did not know until the flood came and took them all away. So also will be the coming of the Son of Man.
24:40 Then two men will be in the field: one is taken and one is left.
24:41 Two women will be grinding at the mill: one is taken and one is left.
24:42 So keep watch, because you do not know on what day your Lord comes.
24:43 But know this: if the householder had known in what watch the thief was coming, he would have kept watch and would not have allowed his house to be broken into.
24:44 Therefore you too must be ready, because the Son of Man comes at an hour you do not expect.
24:45 Who, then, is the faithful and prudent slave whom the master appointed over his household to give them food at the proper time?
24:46 Blessed is that slave whom his master, when he comes, will find doing so.
24:47 Truly I tell you that he will appoint him over all his belongings.
24:48 But if that evil slave says in his heart, ‘My master delays,’
24:49 and begins to beat his fellow slaves and eats and drinks with drunkards,
24:50 the master of that slave will come on a day he does not expect and at an hour he does not know.
24:51 He will cut him in two and assign his portion with the hypocrites. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.

MATTHEW 25

25:1 Then the kingdom of the heavens will be likened to ten virgins who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom.
25:2 Five of them were foolish and five prudent.
25:3 For the foolish took their lamps but did not take oil with them.
25:4 The prudent took oil in containers along with their lamps.
25:5 As the bridegroom delayed, they all became drowsy and slept.
25:6 At midnight a cry arose: ‘Behold, the bridegroom! Come out to meet him.’
25:7 Then all those virgins arose and put their lamps in order.
25:8 The foolish said to the prudent, ‘Give us some of your oil, because our lamps are going out.’
25:9 The prudent answered, ‘There may not be enough for us and you. Go instead to those selling and buy for yourselves.’
25:10 While they went away to buy, the bridegroom came. Those who were ready entered with him into the wedding feast, and the door was shut.
25:11 Afterward the other virgins also came, saying, ‘Lord, lord, open to us.’
25:12 He answered, ‘Truly I tell you, I do not know you.’
25:13 Therefore keep watch, because you know neither the day nor the hour.
25:14 For it is like a man traveling abroad, who called his own slaves and handed over his belongings to them.
25:15 To one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one, each according to his own ability, and traveled abroad. Immediately
25:16 the one who received the five talents went and traded with them and gained another five.
25:17 Likewise the one with two gained another two.
25:18 But the one who received one went away, dug in the ground, and hid his master’s silver.
25:19 After a long time, the master of those slaves came and settled accounts with them.
25:20 The one who received five talents came and brought another five talents, saying, ‘Master, you handed over five talents to me. Look, I gained another five talents.’
25:21 His master said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a few things; I will appoint you over many. Enter into your master’s joy.’
25:22 The one with two talents also came and said, ‘Master, you handed over two talents to me. Look, I gained another two talents.’
25:23 His master said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a few things; I will appoint you over many. Enter into your master’s joy.’
25:24 The one who received one talent also came and said, ‘Master, I knew you to be a hard man, reaping where you did not sow and gathering from where you did not scatter.
25:25 I was afraid, so I went away and hid your talent in the ground. Look, you have what is yours.’
25:26 His master answered him, ‘Evil and idle slave! You knew that I reap where I did not sow and gather from where I did not scatter?
25:27 Then you ought to have deposited my silver with the bankers, and when I came I would have recovered what is mine with interest.
25:28 So take the talent from him and give it to the one who has the ten talents.
25:29 For to everyone who has, more will be given and he will have abundance; but from the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken.
25:30 Cast the useless slave into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.’
25:31 When the Son of Man comes in His glory, and all the angels with Him, then He will sit upon the throne of His glory.
25:32 All the nations will be gathered before Him, and He will separate them from one another as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.
25:33 He will stand the sheep at His right and the young goats at His left.
25:34 Then the King will say to those at His right, ‘Come, you blessed by My Father; inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.
25:35 For I was hungry and you gave Me food, I was thirsty and you gave Me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed Me,
25:36 naked and you clothed Me, I was sick and you visited Me, I was in prison and you came to Me.’
25:37 Then the righteous will answer Him, ‘Lord, when did we see Thee hungry and feed Thee, or thirsty and give Thee drink?
25:38 When did we see Thee a stranger and welcome Thee, or naked and clothe Thee?
25:39 When did we see Thee sick or in prison and come to Thee?’
25:40 The King will answer them, ‘Truly I tell you, insofar as you did it to one of the least of these My brothers, you did it to Me.’
25:41 Then He will also say to those at His left, ‘Depart from Me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.
25:42 For I was hungry and you did not give Me food, I was thirsty and you did not give Me drink,
25:43 I was a stranger and you did not welcome Me, naked and you did not clothe Me, sick and in prison and you did not visit Me.’
25:44 Then they too will answer, ‘Lord, when did we see Thee hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and did not serve Thee?’
25:45 Then He will answer them, ‘Truly I tell you, insofar as you did not do it to one of the least of these, neither did you do it to Me.’
25:46 And these shall depart into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.”

MATTHEW 26

26:1 When Jesus finished all these words, He said to His disciples,
26:2 “You know that after two days the Passover takes place, and the Son of Man is handed over to be crucified.”
26:3 Then the chief priests and elders of the people gathered in the courtyard of the high priest called Caiaphas.
26:4 They took counsel to seize Jesus by deceit and kill Him.
26:5 But they said, “Not during the feast, lest an uproar occur among the people.”
26:6 While Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,
26:7 a woman came to Him with an alabaster flask of very costly perfume and poured it upon His head as He reclined at table.
26:8 Seeing it, the disciples became indignant, saying, “Why this waste?
26:9 For this could have been sold for much and given to the poor.”
26:10 Knowing this, Jesus said to them, “Why do you trouble the woman? For she has done a good work toward Me.
26:11 For you always have the poor with you, but you do not always have Me.
26:12 By pouring this perfume upon My body, she did it to prepare Me for burial.
26:13 Truly I tell you, wherever this good news is proclaimed in the whole world, what she did will also be told in remembrance of her.”
26:14 Then one of the twelve, called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests
26:15 and said, “What are you willing to give me, and I will hand Him over to you?” They weighed out for him thirty silver coins.
26:16 From then on he sought a suitable opportunity to hand Him over.
26:17 On the first day of Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying, “Where dost Thou wish us to prepare for Thee to eat the Passover?”
26:18 He said, “Go into the city to a certain man and tell him, ‘The Teacher says: My appointed time is near. I keep the Passover at your house with My disciples.’”
26:19 The disciples did as Jesus directed them, and prepared the Passover.
26:20 When evening came, He reclined at table with the twelve.
26:21 As they ate, He said, “Truly I tell you that one of you will hand Me over.”
26:22 Exceedingly grieved, each began saying to Him, “Surely it is not I, Lord?”
26:23 He answered, “The one who dipped his hand with Me in the dish will hand Me over.
26:24 The Son of Man indeed goes as it is written concerning Him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is handed over! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born.”
26:25 Judas, who was handing Him over, answered, “Surely it is not I, Rabbi?” He said to him, “You have said it.”
26:26 And as they were eating, Jesus took bread, and having blessed, He broke it; and giving it to the disciples, He said, “Take, eat; this is My body.”
26:27 And He took a cup, and having given thanks, He gave it to them, saying, “Drink from it, all of you;
26:28 for this is My blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many for forgiveness of sins.
26:29 And I tell you: from this time I shall by no means drink of this fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new with you in the kingdom of My Father.”
26:30 After singing a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.
26:31 Then Jesus said to them, “All of you will stumble because of Me this night, for it is written: ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’
26:32 But after I am raised, I will go ahead of you to Galilee.”
26:33 Peter answered Him, “Even if everyone stumbles because of Thee, I will never stumble.”
26:34 Jesus said to him, “Truly I tell you that this night, before a rooster crows, you will deny Me three times.”
26:35 Peter said to Him, “Even if I must die with Thee, I will certainly not deny Thee.” All the disciples said likewise.
26:36 Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and said to the disciples, “Sit here while I go over there and pray.”
26:37 Taking Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, He began to be grieved and distressed.
26:38 Then He said to them, “My soul is deeply grieved, even to death. Remain here and keep watch with Me.”
26:39 Going forward a little, He fell upon His face and prayed, saying, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from Me. Nevertheless, not as I wish, but as Thou wishest.”
26:40 He came to the disciples and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “So, could you not keep watch with Me for one hour?
26:41 Keep watch and pray, so that you may not enter temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”
26:42 Again He went away a second time and prayed, saying, “My Father, if this cannot pass unless I drink it, let Thy will be done.”
26:43 He came and again found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.
26:44 He left them and went away again, praying a third time and saying the same words again.
26:45 Then He came to the disciples and said to them, “Sleep on now and rest. Behold, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is handed over into sinners’ hands.
26:46 Rise, let us go. Behold, the one handing Me over has drawn near.”
26:47 While He was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came with a great crowd carrying swords and clubs, from the chief priests and elders of the people.
26:48 The one handing Him over had given them a sign, saying, “The One I kiss is He; seize Him.”
26:49 Immediately he came to Jesus and said, “Greetings, Rabbi,” and kissed Him.
26:50 Jesus said to him, “Friend, do what you have come for.” Then they came forward, laid their hands upon Jesus, and seized Him.
26:51 And behold, one of those with Jesus stretched out his hand, drew his sword, struck the high priest’s slave, and cut off his ear.
26:52 Then Jesus said to him, “Return your sword to its place, for all who take a sword will perish by a sword.
26:53 Or do you think I cannot plead with My Father, and He will provide Me at once more than twelve legions of angels?
26:54 How, then, would the Scriptures be fulfilled, that it must happen this way?”
26:55 At that hour Jesus said to the crowds, “Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs, to arrest Me? Daily I sat teaching in the temple, and you did not seize Me.
26:56 But all this has happened so that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples left Him and fled.
26:57 Those who seized Jesus led Him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and elders had gathered.
26:58 Peter followed Him at a distance as far as the high priest’s courtyard. Going inside, he sat with the officers to see the outcome.
26:59 The chief priests and the whole council sought false testimony against Jesus to put Him to death,
26:60 but found none, although many false witnesses came forward. Finally two came forward
26:61 and said, “This Man said, ‘I can destroy God’s sanctuary and build it in three days.’”
26:62 The high priest rose and said to Him, “Dost Thou answer nothing? What is this that they testify against Thee?”
26:63 But Jesus remained silent. The high priest said to Him, “I adjure Thee by the living God to tell us whether Thou art the Christ, the Son of God.”
26:64 Jesus said to him, “You have said it. Nevertheless I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man seated at the right of the Power and coming upon the clouds of heaven.”
26:65 Then the high priest tore his garments, saying, “He has blasphemed! What further need do we have of witnesses? Look, now you heard the blasphemy.
26:66 What do you think?” They answered, “He is liable to death.”
26:67 Then they spat into His face and struck Him with their fists; others slapped Him,
26:68 saying, “Prophesy to us, Christ! Who is the one who struck Thee?”
26:69 Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. One servant girl approached him, saying, “You too were with Jesus the Galilean.”
26:70 But he denied it before everyone, saying, “I do not know what you are saying.”
26:71 When he went out to the gateway, another girl saw him and said to those there, “This man was with Jesus the Nazarene.”
26:72 Again he denied it with an oath: “I do not know the Man.”
26:73 After a little while, those standing there came and said to Peter, “Truly you too are one of them, for even your speech makes you recognizable.”
26:74 Then he began to invoke curses and swear, “I do not know the Man.” Immediately a rooster crowed.
26:75 Peter remembered Jesus’ word: “Before a rooster crows, you will deny Me three times.” He went outside and wept bitterly.

MATTHEW 27

27:1 When morning came, all the chief priests and elders of the people took counsel against Jesus to put Him to death.
27:2 They bound Him, led Him away, and handed Him over to Pilate the governor.
27:3 Then Judas, who had handed Him over, saw that He had been condemned. Filled with regret, he returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and elders,
27:4 saying, “I sinned by handing over innocent blood.” They said, “What is that to us? See to it yourself.”
27:5 He threw the silver coins into the sanctuary and withdrew; then he went away and hanged himself.
27:6 The chief priests took the silver coins and said, “It is not lawful to put them into the treasury, because they are the price of blood.”
27:7 After taking counsel, they bought the potter’s field with them as a burial place for strangers.
27:8 Therefore that field has been called Field of Blood until today.
27:9 Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled: “They took the thirty silver coins, the price of the One priced, whom some of the sons of Israel priced,
27:10 and gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord directed me.”
27:11 Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor questioned Him, saying, “Art Thou the King of the Jews?” Jesus said, “You say it.”
27:12 While He was accused by the chief priests and elders, He answered nothing.
27:13 Then Pilate said to Him, “Dost Thou not hear how many things they testify against Thee?”
27:14 He did not answer him even one word, so that the governor marveled greatly.
27:15 At the feast the governor customarily released to the crowd one prisoner whom they wanted.
27:16 At that time they had a notorious prisoner called Jesus Barabbas.
27:17 So when they gathered, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you want me to release to you: Jesus Barabbas, or Jesus called Christ?”
27:18 For he knew that they had handed Him over because of envy.
27:19 While he sat on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, “Have nothing to do with that righteous Man, for I suffered many things today in a dream because of Him.”
27:20 But the chief priests and elders persuaded the crowds to ask for Barabbas and destroy Jesus.
27:21 The governor answered them, “Which of the two do you want me to release to you?” They said, “Barabbas.”
27:22 Pilate said to them, “What then shall I do with Jesus called Christ?” They all said, “Let Him be crucified!”
27:23 He said, “Why, what evil did He do?” But they cried out all the more, “Let Him be crucified!”
27:24 When Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing, but rather an uproar was arising, he took water and washed his hands before the crowd, saying, “I am innocent of this blood. See to it yourselves.”
27:25 All the people answered, “His blood be upon us and upon our children.”
27:26 Then he released Barabbas to them; after flogging Jesus, he handed Him over to be crucified.
27:27 Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the praetorium and gathered the whole cohort around Him.
27:28 They stripped Him and put a scarlet cloak around Him.
27:29 They wove a crown from thorns, placed it upon His head, and put a reed in His right hand. Kneeling before Him, they mocked Him, saying, “Greetings, King of the Jews!”
27:30 They spat on Him, took the reed, and kept striking Him upon the head.
27:31 After mocking Him, they stripped off the cloak, dressed Him in His own garments, and led Him away to crucify Him.
27:32 As they went out, they found a Cyrenian man named Simon. They compelled him to carry His cross.
27:33 They came to a place called Golgotha, which is called Place of a Skull,
27:34 and gave Him wine mixed with gall to drink. After tasting it, He was unwilling to drink.
27:35 After crucifying Him, they divided His garments by casting a lot,
27:36 and sat there keeping watch over Him.
27:37 Above His head they placed the written charge against Him: “This is Jesus, the King of the Jews.”
27:38 Then two robbers were crucified with Him, one at His right and one at His left.
27:39 Those passing by reviled Him, shaking their heads
27:40 and saying, “Thou who destroyest the sanctuary and buildest it in three days, save Thyself! If Thou art God’s Son, come down from the cross.”
27:41 Likewise the chief priests, mocking with the scribes and elders, said,
27:42 “He saved others; Himself He cannot save. He is King of Israel! Let Him come down from the cross now, and we will believe in Him.
27:43 He trusts in God; let Him rescue Him now, if He desires Him, for He said, ‘I am God’s Son.’”
27:44 The robbers crucified with Him also reproached Him in the same way.
27:45 From the sixth hour darkness came upon all the land until the ninth hour.
27:46 And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, “Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?” That is, “My God, My God, why hast Thou forsaken Me?”
27:47 Some standing there heard and said, “This Man is calling Elijah.”
27:48 Immediately one of them ran, took a sponge, filled it with sour wine, placed it on a reed, and gave Him a drink.
27:49 But the rest said, “Leave it; let us see whether Elijah comes to save Him.”
27:50 Jesus again cried out with a loud voice and released His spirit.
27:51 And behold, the veil of the sanctuary was torn in two from top to bottom. The earth shook, and the rocks split.
27:52 The tombs were opened, and many bodies of the holy ones who had fallen asleep were raised.
27:53 Coming out of the tombs after His rising, they entered the holy city and appeared to many.
27:54 The centurion and those with him guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and what had happened, and were exceedingly afraid, saying, “Truly this was God’s Son.”
27:55 Many women were there, watching from a distance. They had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving Him.
27:56 Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee’s sons.
27:57 When evening came, a rich man from Arimathea named Joseph came, who himself had also become Jesus’ disciple.
27:58 He went to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body. Then Pilate ordered it to be given.
27:59 Joseph took the body and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth.
27:60 He laid it in his own new tomb, which he had cut in the rock. Rolling a great stone to the tomb’s entrance, he went away.
27:61 Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, sitting opposite the tomb.
27:62 On the next day, which was after the Preparation, the chief priests and Pharisees gathered before Pilate,
27:63 saying, “Sir, we remembered that while He was still alive, that deceiver said, ‘After three days I am raised.’
27:64 So command that the tomb be made secure until the third day, lest His disciples come, steal Him, and tell the people, ‘He has been raised from the dead,’ and the last deception will be worse than the first.”
27:65 Pilate said to them, “You have a guard. Go and make it secure as you know how.”
27:66 They went and secured the tomb by sealing the stone, with the guard.

MATTHEW 28

28:1 After the Sabbath, as it dawned toward the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to view the tomb.
28:2 And behold, a great earthquake occurred, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, came, rolled away the stone, and sat upon it.
28:3 His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow.
28:4 From fear of him the guards shook and became like dead men.
28:5 The angel answered the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you seek Jesus who was crucified.
28:6 He is not here, for He was raised, as He said. Come, see the place where He lay.
28:7 Go quickly and tell His disciples that He was raised from the dead; and behold, He goes ahead of you to Galilee. There you will see Him. Behold, I have told you.”
28:8 They departed quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy and ran to report to His disciples.
28:9 And behold, Jesus met them, saying, “Greetings!” They came, grasped His feet, and worshiped Him.
28:10 Then Jesus said to them, “Do not fear. Go and report to My brothers that they should go to Galilee, and there they will see Me.”
28:11 As they went, behold, some of the guard came into the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had happened.
28:12 They gathered with the elders and took counsel, then gave a considerable amount of silver to the soldiers,
28:13 saying, “Say, ‘His disciples came by night and stole Him while we slept.’
28:14 If this is heard before the governor, we will persuade him and make you free from worry.”
28:15 They took the silver and did as they were instructed. This account was spread among Jews until this very day.
28:16 The eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had directed them.
28:17 When they saw Him, they worshiped, but some doubted.
28:18 And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, “All authority in heaven and upon earth has been given to Me.
28:19 Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
28:20 teaching them to keep all that I commanded you; and behold, I am with you all the days, until the consummation of the age.”
