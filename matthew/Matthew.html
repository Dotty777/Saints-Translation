Matthew 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Book of the Origin of Jesus Christ

1:1A book of the origin of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham. †

1:2Abraham begot Isaac; and Isaac begot Jacob; and Jacob begot Judah and his brothers; †

1:3Judah begot Perez and Zerah by Tamar; Perez begot Hezron; Hezron begot Ram;

1:4Ram begot Amminadab; Amminadab begot Nahshon; Nahshon begot Salmon;

1:5Salmon begot Boaz by Rahab; Boaz begot Obed by Ruth; Obed begot Jesse;

1:6Jesse begot David the king. David begot Solomon by the wife of Uriah;

1:7Solomon begot Rehoboam; Rehoboam begot Abijah; Abijah begot Asaph;

1:8Asaph begot Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat begot Joram; Joram begot Uzziah;

1:9Uzziah begot Jotham; Jotham begot Ahaz; Ahaz begot Hezekiah;

1:10Hezekiah begot Manasseh; Manasseh begot Amos; Amos begot Josiah;

1:11Josiah begot Jechoniah and his brothers at the deportation to Babylon.

1:12After the deportation to Babylon, Jechoniah begot Shealtiel; Shealtiel begot Zerubbabel;

1:13Zerubbabel begot Abiud; Abiud begot Eliakim; Eliakim begot Azor;

1:14Azor begot Zadok; Zadok begot Achim; Achim begot Eliud;

1:15Eliud begot Eleazar; Eleazar begot Matthan; Matthan begot Jacob;

1:16and Jacob begot Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ. †

1:17So all the generations from Abraham to David were fourteen generations, from David to the deportation to Babylon fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon to the Christ fourteen generations.

The Birth of Jesus Christ

1:18The origin of Jesus Christ was like this. When His mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child from the Holy Spirit.

1:19Joseph her husband, being righteous and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace, decided to divorce her quietly.

1:20As he considered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary your wife home, for what has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.

1:21She shall bear a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He shall save His people from their sins.” †

1:22All this has happened so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled:

1:23“Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall call His name Emmanuel”; which means “God with us.” †

1:24When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him and took his wife home.

1:25He did not have relations with her until she bore a Son; and he called His name Jesus.

Matthew 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Magi from the East

2:1After Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, behold, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem,

2:2saying, “Where is the One born King of the Jews? For we saw His star at its rising and have come to bow before Him.”

2:3When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.

2:4He gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people and asked them where the Christ was to be born.

2:5They told him, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is what has been written through the prophet:

2:6‘And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd My people Israel.’”

2:7Then Herod secretly called the magi and learned from them the exact time when the star appeared.

2:8He sent them to Bethlehem, saying, “Go and inquire carefully about the Child. When you find Him, report to me, so that I too may come and bow before Him.”

2:9After hearing the king, they went on their way. And behold, the star they had seen at its rising went ahead of them until it came and stood over the place where the Child was.

2:10When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.

2:11They entered the house and saw the Child with Mary His mother. Falling down, they bowed before Him; and opening their treasures, they offered Him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

2:12Having been warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they withdrew to their country by another way.

The Flight to Egypt

2:13After they withdrew, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, “Rise, take the Child and His mother, and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is about to seek the Child to destroy Him.”

2:14He rose, took the Child and His mother by night, and withdrew to Egypt.

2:15He remained there until Herod’s death, so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled: “Out of Egypt I called My Son.”

The Children of Bethlehem

2:16Then Herod, seeing that the magi had outwitted him, became exceedingly angry. He sent men and killed all the boys in Bethlehem and throughout its district who were two years old and under, according to the exact time he had learned from the magi.

2:17Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:

2:18“A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and great lamentation: Rachel weeping for her children, and she would not be comforted, because they are no more.”

The Return from Egypt

2:19After Herod died, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,

2:20saying, “Rise, take the Child and His mother, and go to the land of Israel, for those seeking the Child’s life have died.”

2:21He rose, took the Child and His mother, and entered the land of Israel.

2:22But hearing that Archelaus reigned over Judea in place of his father Herod, he feared to go there. Warned in a dream, he withdrew to the regions of Galilee.

2:23He came and settled in a city called Nazareth, so that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled: “He will be called a Nazarene.”

John the Baptist in the Wilderness

3:1In those days John the Baptist appeared, proclaiming in the wilderness of Judea

3:2and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.” †

3:3For this is the one spoken of through Isaiah the prophet: “A voice of one crying in the wilderness: ‘Prepare the way of the Lord; make His paths straight.’”

3:4John himself wore clothing of camel’s hair and a leather belt around his waist; his food was locusts and wild honey.

3:5Then Jerusalem, all Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him,

3:6and they were baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.

The Pharisees and Sadducees at the Baptism

3:7When he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, “Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

3:8Bear fruit, then, worthy of repentance,

3:9and do not suppose you can say among yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as father.’ For I tell you that God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.

3:10Already the axe lies at the root of the trees. Every tree, therefore, that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire.

3:11I baptize you in water for repentance, but the One coming after me is stronger than I, whose sandals I am not fit to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.

3:12His winnowing shovel is in His hand, and He will thoroughly clear His threshing floor and gather His wheat into the storehouse; but He will burn the chaff with unquenchable fire.”

The Baptism of Jesus

3:13Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan, to John, to be baptized by him.

3:14John tried to prevent Him, saying, “I need to be baptized by Thee, and dost Thou come to me?”

3:15Jesus answered him, “Allow it now, for in this way it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then he allowed Him.

3:16When Jesus was baptized, He immediately came up from the water. And behold, the heavens were opened, and He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and coming upon Him.

3:17And behold, a voice from the heavens said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”

The Temptation in the Wilderness

4:1Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit to be tempted by the devil.

4:2After fasting forty days and forty nights, He became hungry.

4:3The tempter came and said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, command these stones to become loaves of bread.”

4:4But He answered, “It is written: ‘A human being shall not live on bread alone, but on every word proceeding through the mouth of God.’” †

4:5Then the devil took Him into the holy city and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple,

4:6and said to Him, “If Thou art God’s Son, throw Thyself down. For it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning Thee,’ and ‘They will lift Thee on their hands, lest Thou strike Thy foot against a stone.’”

4:7Jesus said to him, “Again it is written: ‘You shall not put the Lord your God to the test.’”

4:8Again the devil took Him to an exceedingly high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory.

4:9He said to Him, “I will give Thee all these things if Thou fall down and worship me.”

4:10Then Jesus said to him, “Go away, Satan! For it is written: ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him alone shall you serve.’”

4:11Then the devil left Him, and behold, angels came and ministered to Him.

Jesus Withdraws to Galilee

4:12When He heard that John had been handed over, He withdrew to Galilee.

4:13Leaving Nazareth, He came and settled in Capernaum beside the sea, in the territories of Zebulun and Naphtali,

4:14so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled:

4:15“Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations:

4:16the people sitting in darkness saw a great light, and for those sitting in the region and shadow of death a light rose.”

Repent, for the Kingdom Has Drawn Near

4:17From then on Jesus began to proclaim, “Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.”

The Calling of the First Disciples

4:18As He walked beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.

4:19He said to them, “Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of people.”

4:20Immediately they left their nets and followed Him.

4:21Going on from there, He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and He called them.

4:22Immediately they left the boat and their father and followed Him.

Teaching and Healing in Galilee

4:23He went throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the good news of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every infirmity among the people.

4:24The report about Him spread throughout Syria, and they brought Him all who were ill, afflicted with various diseases and torments, those oppressed by demons, those suffering seizures, and paralytics; and He healed them.

4:25Great crowds followed Him from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan.

Matthew 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Sermon on the Mountain Begins

5:1And seeing the crowds, He went up the mountain; and when He had sat down, His disciples came to Him.

5:2And He opened His mouth and taught them, saying:

The Beatitudes

5:3“Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens. †

5:4Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted. †

5:5Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth. †

5:6Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.

5:7Blessed are the merciful, for they shall receive mercy. †

5:8Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

5:9Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God. †

5:10Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness’ sake, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens. †

5:11Blessed are you when they revile you and persecute you and falsely speak every evil thing against you for My sake. †

5:12Rejoice and be glad, for great is your reward in the heavens; for thus they persecuted the prophets before you.

Salt and Light

5:13You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt becomes tasteless, how shall it be made salty? It is no longer good for anything except to be thrown out and trampled underfoot by people. †

5:14You are the light of the world. A city set upon a mountain cannot be hidden.

5:15Nor do people light a lamp and place it beneath the modius, but upon the lampstand; and it shines for everyone in the house. †

5:16So let your light shine before people, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in the heavens.

I Did Not Come to Abolish the Law

5:17Do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to abolish, but to fulfil. †

5:18For truly I tell you: until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota nor one stroke shall pass from the Law, until all things come to pass. †

5:19Whoever therefore loosens one of these least commandments and teaches people to do so shall be called least in the kingdom of the heavens; but whoever does and teaches them shall be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.

5:20For I tell you: unless your righteousness exceeds that of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall by no means enter the kingdom of the heavens.

You Shall Not Murder

5:21You have heard that it was said to those of old: ‘You shall not murder’; and ‘whoever murders shall be liable to judgment.’ †

5:22But I tell you that everyone who is angry with his brother shall be liable to judgment; and whoever says to his brother, ‘Raca,’ shall be liable to the council; and whoever says, ‘Fool,’ shall be liable to the Gehenna of fire. †

5:23If therefore you offer your gift upon the altar, and there remember that your brother has something against you,

5:24leave your gift there before the altar, and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.

5:25Come to terms with your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the way, lest your adversary hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.

5:26Truly I tell you: you shall by no means come out of there until you have paid the last quadrans. †

You Shall Not Commit Adultery

5:27You have heard that it was said: ‘You shall not commit adultery.’

5:28But I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

5:29If your right eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body be thrown into Gehenna. †

5:30And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away from you; for it is better for you that one of your members perish than that your whole body go into Gehenna.

Whoever Divorces His Wife

5:31And it was said: ‘Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce.’

5:32But I tell you that everyone who divorces his wife, except on account of sexual immorality, causes her to commit adultery; and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery. †

You Shall Not Swear Falsely

5:33Again, you have heard that it was said to those of old: ‘You shall not swear falsely, but shall fulfil your oaths to the Lord.’

5:34But I tell you: do not swear at all, neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God,

5:35nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of His feet, nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.

5:36Nor shall you swear by your head, for you cannot make one hair white or black.

5:37But let your word be ‘Yes, yes’; ‘No, no.’ Whatever is beyond these is of evil. †

An Eye for an Eye

5:38You have heard that it was said: ‘An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.’

5:39But I tell you: do not resist the evildoer; but whoever strikes you upon your right cheek, turn the other to him also. †

5:40And if anyone would sue you and take your tunic, let him have your cloak also. †

5:41And whoever compels you to go one Roman mile, go with him two. †

5:42Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who would borrow from you.

Love Your Enemies

5:43You have heard that it was said: ‘You shall love your neighbour and hate your enemy.’

5:44But I tell you: love your enemies, and pray for those who persecute you, †

5:45that you may become sons of your Father who is in the heavens; for He makes His sun rise upon the evil and the good, and sends rain upon the righteous and the unrighteous.

5:46For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same?

5:47And if you greet only your brothers, what are you doing beyond others? Do not even the Gentiles do the same? †

5:48You shall therefore be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect. †
