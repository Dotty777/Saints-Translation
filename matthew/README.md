# Matthew — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 28 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 1068 verse units received an English editorial review: 228 changed and 840 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

NA28 is the user-authorized provisional New Testament base. Focused checks of the German Bible Society’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not complete critical-text or apparatus verification. The newer Bespoke PDF’s preferred UBS6 text has not been obtained; UBS6/ECM comparison, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/MAT.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 28 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Use begot consistently with the accepted Genesis benchmark; keep Asaph and Amos, repeated genealogy clauses, Mary as the feminine antecedent and the passive was born. Joseph receives ordinary you; has replaces hath. Retain the until clause without a doctrinal inference. Home in 1:20,24 remains the inherited contextual rendering of taking Mary as wife, not a separately expressed Greek destination.

Pending: Review the supplied home, genealogy names and the interpretation of 1:25. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Keep magi, at its rising, the great joy, the age limit and the plural those seeking the child’s life. Learned the exact time makes the inquiry wording more natural. No number of magi or royal title is added. Nazarene and the plural prophets remain.

Pending: Review the star expression, chronology and quotation relationships. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Repent replaces ordinary plural Repent ye; the crowd address remains plural in context. John’s direct address to Jesus takes Thee/Thou under this edition’s divine-address convention. Keep water, Spirit, fire, axe, fruit and winnowing images. The opening and and 3:16 to Him are retained; NA28 brackets the corresponding elements, alongside small article/conjunction uncertainties in 3:16.

Pending: Review the brackets in 3:2,16, the baptism dialogue and the divine-address convention. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Direct address to Jesus, including the tempter’s speech and the quotation applied to Him, uses Thou/Thee/Thy. This is an editorial referent convention, not a claim that the speaker confesses His divinity. Jesus’ address to Satan uses you. Preserve the bread/mouth quotation, forty days and nights, handed over, fishers of people, places and healings. Has drawn near retains the resulting nearness.

Pending: Review quotation wording, historic presents in narrative and the seizure rendering in 4:24. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; retain the beatitudes’ repetition, poor in spirit, sons, have been persecuted, iota/stroke, modius, quadrans, Raca, Gehenna, garments and Roman mile. Perfect retains its directive future form.

Pending: Review porneia and the passive infinitive in 5:32, evil/evil one, evildoer, perfect/complete and ancient units. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

Pending: Review epiousios, evil/evil one, sound/single eye, consumption, cubit/lifespan and bracketed clauses; preserve prayer cadence. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.

Pending: Review 7:14 against the apparatus and the referent convention in the judgment dialogue. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek review and human read-aloud review remain pending.
