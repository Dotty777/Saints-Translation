# Ruth — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All four chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 85 verses received an English editorial review: 78 changed and seven retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer *Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf* governs and supersedes the older *Saints English Founding Style Guide* where their pronoun policies conflict. Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your. Accepted Genesis 1–11 is the working English benchmark. Successful earlier wording is retained.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. The publisher’s running text for Ruth 1–4 was read in the [German Bible Society edition](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/RUT.1); focused checks and decisions are recorded in source-checks.json. Göttingen and the full apparatus were not obtained. No complete critical-text verification, independent scholarly approval or human read-aloud review is claimed.

## Preserved readings and review queue

Every original verse ID, link, navigation destination, image, dagger and existing review note is preserved. All five existing dagger notes remain linked and unchanged. Notes are separated from Scripture. The revision does not harmonize the selected Greek text with Hebrew or familiar English versions.

Ruth’s pledge to Naomi uses ordinary you/your. Third-person blessings about the Lord remain distinct from direct address to God. The elliptical grant in 1:9 receives the English object this, with the addition explicitly recorded. The hypothetical wording in 1:12–13 and the affirmative death shall part in 1:17 remain for further review. Bitter, Sufficient One and full/empty retain their connections.

Chapter 2 retains morning until evening, no rest even a little, affirmative I shall be, barley meal and oiphi. Stay close links the repeated gleaning instructions. The carry/cast expressions, the encounter idiom in 2:22 and the uncertain he/his in 2:20 remain in the review queue. No fixed modern quantity is supplied for oiphi.

Chapter 3 retains the wing image, go up/went down, drinking/eating order, what is at his feet, the 3:11–12 continuation, and repeated kinship language. He entered the city in 3:15 is inherited interpretation, not an explicit Greek gendered pronoun. The negative matter shall not fall through in 3:18 remains visible with its existing note. The unit of the six measures is not supplied.

Chapter 4 preserves the field given to Naomi, uncover your ear, the kinship rights, hand, inheritance, seed, and raising up the dead man’s name. Verse 4:3 turns a relative clause into a full English statement without adding a sale. The plural Rachel/Leah construction in 4:11 and your name with lowercase he in 4:14 remain under review. Abimelech, Arran and Salman are retained. Begot follows the accepted Genesis genealogy cadence.

The five inherited dagger notes concern 2:20, 3:15, 3:18, 4:11 and 4:14. Chapter records identify additional specialist questions. The inherited comparison records are historical evidence, not a new verification claim.

Saints Translation literal-smooth and all six study/comparison pages were left unchanged and passed content-hash checks. No live-site update was made. This is a book-level archive, not a full-site backup.

Joshua, Judges and Ruth have completed this division’s Bespoke English/staging pass. 1 Samuel is next; twelve books in the division remain pending. [Repository progress](../progress.json) supersedes next-book statements in earlier snapshots.
