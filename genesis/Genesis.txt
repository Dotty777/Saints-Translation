GENESIS 1

1:1 In the beginning God made heaven and earth.
1:2 Now the earth was unseen and unformed, and darkness was over the deep; and the Spirit of God was moving over the water.
1:3 And God said, “Let there be light.” And there was light.
1:4 And God saw that the light was good, and God divided the light from the darkness.
1:5 And God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. And there was evening, and there was morning: one day.
1:6 And God said, “Let there be a firmament in the midst of the water, and let it divide water from water.” And it was so.
1:7 And God made the firmament, and God divided the water beneath the firmament from the water above the firmament.
1:8 And God called the firmament Heaven. And God saw that it was good. And there was evening, and there was morning: a second day.
1:9 And God said, “Let the water beneath heaven be gathered into one place, and let the dry land appear.” And it was so. And the water beneath heaven was gathered into its gathering places, and the dry land appeared.
1:10 And God called the dry land Earth, and the gathered waters He called Seas. And God saw that it was good.
1:11 And God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation sowing seed according to its kind and likeness, and fruit-bearing trees upon the earth, each yielding fruit with its seed within it, according to its kind.” And it was so.
1:12 And the earth brought forth vegetation sowing seed according to its kind and likeness, and fruit-bearing trees upon the earth, each yielding fruit with its seed within it, according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.
1:13 And there was evening, and there was morning: a third day.
1:14 And God said, “Let there be lights in the firmament of heaven to give light upon the earth and to divide the day from the night. Let them serve for signs and seasons, for days and years.
1:15 And let them be lights in the firmament of heaven, to shine upon the earth.” And it was so.
1:16 And God made the two great lights: the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night; He made the stars also.
1:17 And God set them in the firmament of heaven to shine upon the earth,
1:18 to rule the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.
1:19 And there was evening, and there was morning: a fourth day.
1:20 And God said, “Let the waters bring forth living creatures that move, and birds that fly above the earth across the firmament of heaven.” And it was so.
1:21 And God made the great sea creatures, and every living creature that moves, which the waters brought forth according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that they were good.
1:22 And God blessed them, saying, “Grow and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas; and let the birds multiply upon the earth.”
1:23 And there was evening, and there was morning: a fifth day.
1:24 And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kind: four-footed animals, creeping things, and wild beasts of the earth according to their kind.” And it was so.
1:25 And God made the wild beasts of the earth according to their kind, and the cattle according to their kind, and all the creeping things of the earth according to their kinds. And God saw that they were good.
1:26 And God said, “Let Us make man in Our image and likeness; and let them rule over the fish of the sea, the birds of heaven, the cattle, all the earth, and every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth.”
1:27 And God made man; in the image of God He made him; male and female He made them.
1:28 And God blessed them, saying, “Grow and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish of the sea, the birds of heaven, all the cattle, all the earth, and every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth.”
1:29 And God said, “Behold, I have given you every plant sowing seed upon all the earth, and every tree that bears fruit containing seed. These shall be yours for food.
1:30 And to every wild beast of the earth, every bird of heaven, and every creeping thing upon the earth that has life within it, I have given every green plant for food.” And it was so.
1:31 And God saw all that He had made, and behold, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning: a sixth day.

GENESIS 2

2:1 Thus heaven and earth were finished, and all their ordered array.
2:2 And on the sixth day God completed the works He had made, and on the seventh day He rested from all the works He had made.
2:3 And God blessed the seventh day and hallowed it, because on it He rested from all the works that God had begun to make.
2:4 This is the book of the origin of heaven and earth, when they came to be, on the day when God made heaven and earth,
2:5 and every green plant of the field before it appeared upon the earth, and every plant of the field before it sprang up. For God had not sent rain upon the earth, and there was no man to till the ground.
2:6 But a spring rose from the earth and watered the whole face of the ground.
2:7 And God formed man from the dust of the earth, and breathed into his face the breath of life; and man became a living soul.
2:8 And the Lord God planted a garden in Eden, toward the east, and there He placed the man whom He had formed.
2:9 And God caused every tree that was beautiful to behold and good for food to spring up from the earth, with the tree of life in the midst of the garden, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
2:10 And a river flows out of Eden to water the garden, and from there it divides into four branches.
2:11 The name of the first is Pishon; it flows around the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.
2:12 And the gold of that land is good; there also are the carbuncle and the green stone.
2:13 The name of the second river is Gihon; it flows around the whole land of Ethiopia.
2:14 The third river is the Tigris; it flows opposite Assyria. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.
2:15 And the Lord God took the man whom He had formed and placed him in the garden, to tend it and keep it.
2:16 And the Lord God commanded Adam, saying, “You may freely eat from every tree in the garden;
2:17 but from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat. For on the day you eat from it, you shall surely die.”
2:18 And the Lord God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. Let Us make him a helper corresponding to him.”
2:19 And God also formed from the earth every wild beast of the field and every bird of heaven, and brought them to Adam to see what he would call them. And whatever Adam called each living creature, that was its name.
2:20 And Adam gave names to all the cattle, all the birds of heaven, and all the wild beasts of the field. But for Adam no helper like himself was found.
2:21 And God cast a deep sleep upon Adam, and he slept. And He took one of his ribs and filled its place with flesh.
2:22 And the Lord God fashioned the rib He had taken from Adam into a woman, and brought her to Adam.
2:23 And Adam said: This, at last, is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh. She shall be called Woman, for she was taken from her husband.
2:24 For this reason a man shall leave his father and his mother and cleave to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh.
2:25 And the two were naked, both Adam and his wife, and they were not ashamed.

GENESIS 3

3:1 Now the serpent was more cunning than all the wild beasts upon the earth that the Lord God had made. And the serpent said to the woman, “Why has God said, ‘You shall not eat from any tree in the garden’?”
3:2 And the woman said to the serpent, “We may eat of the fruit of the trees in the garden.
3:3 But of the fruit of the tree in the midst of the garden, God said, ‘You shall not eat of it, nor shall you touch it, lest you die.’”
3:4 And the serpent said to the woman, “You shall surely not die.
3:5 For God knew that on the day you ate of it, your eyes would be opened, and you would be as gods, knowing good and evil.”
3:6 And the woman saw that the tree was good for food, pleasing to the eyes, and beautiful to contemplate. And she took of its fruit and ate; she also gave some to her husband with her, and they ate.
3:7 And the eyes of both were opened, and they knew that they were naked. And they sewed fig leaves together and made coverings for themselves.
3:8 And they heard the sound of the Lord God walking in the garden toward evening. And Adam and his wife hid themselves from the face of the Lord God among the trees of the garden.
3:9 And the Lord God called to Adam and said to him, “Adam, where are you?”
3:10 And he said to Him, “I heard Thy voice as Thou didst walk in the garden, and I was afraid because I am naked, and I hid myself.”
3:11 And He said to him, “Who told you that you are naked? Have you eaten from the tree of which alone I commanded you not to eat?”
3:12 And Adam said, “The woman whom Thou didst give to be with me—she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate.”
3:13 And the Lord God said to the woman, “What is this you have done?” And the woman said, “The serpent deceived me, and I ate.”
3:14 And the Lord God said to the serpent: Because you have done this, cursed are you above all cattle and all the wild beasts of the earth. Upon your breast and belly you shall go, and earth you shall eat all the days of your life.
3:15 And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed. He shall watch for your head, and you shall watch for his heel.
3:16 And to the woman He said: I will greatly multiply your sorrows and your groaning; in sorrow you shall bear children. You shall turn toward your husband, and he shall rule over you.
3:17 And to Adam He said: Because you listened to the voice of your wife and ate from the tree of which alone I commanded you not to eat, cursed is the earth in your labours; in pain you shall eat of it all the days of your life.
3:18 Thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you, and you shall eat the plants of the field.
3:19 By the sweat of your face you shall eat your bread, until you return to the earth from which you were taken; for earth you are, and unto earth you shall return.
3:20 And Adam called his wife’s name Life, because she was the mother of all the living.
3:21 And the Lord God made garments of skin for Adam and his wife, and clothed them.
3:22 And God said, “Behold, Adam has become as one of Us, knowing good and evil. And now, lest he stretch forth his hand and take also from the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever—”
3:23 And the Lord God sent him out of the garden of delight to till the earth from which he had been taken.
3:24 And He drove Adam out and settled him opposite the garden of delight. And He stationed the cherubim and the flaming sword that turns about, to guard the way to the tree of life.

GENESIS 4

4:1 Now Adam knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and bore Cain, and said, “I have gained a man through God.”
4:2 And again she bore his brother Abel. And Abel became a shepherd of sheep, but Cain tilled the earth.
4:3 And after some time Cain brought an offering to the Lord from the fruits of the earth.
4:4 And Abel also brought an offering from the firstborn of his sheep and from their fat. And God looked with favour upon Abel and his gifts,
4:5 but He gave no heed to Cain and his offerings. And Cain was greatly distressed, and his face fell.
4:6 And the Lord God said to Cain, “Why are you so distressed, and why has your face fallen?
4:7 If you offer rightly but do not divide rightly, have you not sinned? Be still. His turning shall be toward you, and you shall rule over him.”
4:8 And Cain said to Abel his brother, “Let us go out into the field.” And while they were in the field, Cain rose against Abel his brother and slew him.
4:9 And God said to Cain, “Where is Abel your brother?” And he said, “I do not know. Am I my brother’s keeper?”
4:10 And God said, “What have you done? The voice of your brother’s blood cries unto Me from the earth.
4:11 And now you are cursed from the earth, which opened its mouth to receive your brother’s blood from your hand.
4:12 When you till the earth, it shall no longer yield its strength to you. You shall be groaning and trembling upon the earth.”
4:13 And Cain said to the Lord, “My guilt is too great for me to be forgiven.
4:14 If Thou dost cast me out this day from the face of the earth, I shall be hidden from Thy face; and I shall be groaning and trembling upon the earth, and whoever finds me shall slay me.”
4:15 And the Lord God said to him, “Not so. Whoever slays Cain shall incur sevenfold vengeance.” And the Lord God set a mark upon Cain, that no one finding him should slay him.
4:16 And Cain went out from the face of God and dwelt in the land of Nod, opposite Eden.
4:17 And Cain knew his wife, and she conceived and bore Enoch. And he was building a city, and named the city after his son Enoch.
4:18 And to Enoch was born Gaidad; and Gaidad begot Maiel; and Maiel begot Methuselah; and Methuselah begot Lamech.
4:19 And Lamech took two wives. The name of the first was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah.
4:20 And Adah bore Jabal; he was the father of those who dwell in tents and keep livestock.
4:21 His brother’s name was Jubal; he introduced the psaltery and the lyre.
4:22 And Zillah also bore Thobel, a smith who worked bronze and iron with the hammer. And Thobel’s sister was Naamah.
4:23 And Lamech said to his wives: Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; wives of Lamech, give ear to my words. For I have slain a man to my own hurt, and a young man to my own bruising.
4:24 For Cain has been avenged sevenfold, but Lamech seventy times seven.
4:25 And Adam knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and named him Seth, saying, “For God has raised up another seed for me in place of Abel, whom Cain slew.”
4:26 And to Seth also a son was born, and he named him Enosh. He hoped to call upon the name of the Lord God.

GENESIS 5

5:1 This is the book of the generations of mankind. On the day when God made Adam, He made him in the image of God.
5:2 Male and female He made them, and He blessed them, and named them Adam on the day when He made them.
5:3 And Adam lived two hundred and thirty years, and begot a son in his own likeness and image, and named him Seth.
5:4 And the days of Adam after he begot Seth were seven hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters.
5:5 And all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.
5:6 And Seth lived two hundred and five years, and begot Enosh.
5:7 And Seth lived seven hundred and seven years after he begot Enosh, and begot sons and daughters.
5:8 And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.
5:9 And Enosh lived one hundred and ninety years, and begot Cainan.
5:10 And Enosh lived seven hundred and fifteen years after he begot Cainan, and begot sons and daughters.
5:11 And all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years, and he died.
5:12 And Cainan lived one hundred and seventy years, and begot Mahalalel.
5:13 And Cainan lived seven hundred and forty years after he begot Mahalalel, and begot sons and daughters.
5:14 And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.
5:15 And Mahalalel lived one hundred and sixty-five years, and begot Jared.
5:16 And Mahalalel lived seven hundred and thirty years after he begot Jared, and begot sons and daughters.
5:17 And all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.
5:18 And Jared lived one hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Enoch.
5:19 And Jared lived eight hundred years after he begot Enoch, and begot sons and daughters.
5:20 And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.
5:21 And Enoch lived one hundred and sixty-five years, and begot Methuselah.
5:22 And Enoch pleased God for two hundred years after he begot Methuselah, and begot sons and daughters.
5:23 And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.
5:24 And Enoch pleased God, and was not found, for God transferred him.
5:25 And Methuselah lived one hundred and sixty-seven years, and begot Lamech.
5:26 And Methuselah lived eight hundred and two years after he begot Lamech, and begot sons and daughters.
5:27 And all the days that Methuselah lived were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.
5:28 And Lamech lived one hundred and eighty-eight years, and begot a son.
5:29 And he named him Noah, saying, “This one shall give us rest from our works and from the toil of our hands, and from the earth which the Lord God has cursed.”
5:30 And Lamech lived five hundred and sixty-five years after he begot Noah, and begot sons and daughters.
5:31 And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred and fifty-three years, and he died.
5:32 And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah begot three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

GENESIS 6

6:1 And it came to pass, when mankind began to increase upon the earth and daughters were born to them,
6:2 that the sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful, and took wives for themselves from all whom they chose.
6:3 And the Lord God said, “My Spirit shall not abide in these men for ever, for they are flesh; and their days shall be a hundred and twenty years.”
6:4 Now giants were upon the earth in those days, and afterward also, whenever the sons of God went in to the daughters of men and they bore children to them. These were the giants of old, the men of renown.
6:5 And the Lord God saw that the wickedness of mankind had multiplied upon the earth, and that each one continually set his heart upon evil all his days.
6:6 And God took to heart that He had made man upon the earth, and pondered it.
6:7 And God said, “I will blot out from the face of the earth the mankind I have made, together with the cattle, the creeping things, and the birds of heaven; for I am angered that I made them.”
6:8 But Noah found grace before the Lord God.
6:9 These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation; Noah pleased God.
6:10 And Noah begot three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
6:11 And the earth was corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with unrighteousness.
6:12 And the Lord God looked upon the earth, and it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth.
6:13 And God said to Noah, “The time of all mankind has come before Me, for the earth is filled with unrighteousness because of them; and behold, I am destroying them and the earth.
6:14 “Therefore make yourself an ark of squared timber. Make compartments within the ark, and coat it inside and out with pitch.
6:15 And this is how you shall make it: the ark shall be three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.
6:16 Make the ark taper toward the top, and finish it to a cubit above. Set the door of the ark in its side, and make it with lower, second, and third storeys.
6:17 “And behold, I am bringing a flood of water upon the earth, to destroy all flesh beneath heaven in which is the breath of life. Everything upon the earth shall die.
6:18 But I will establish My covenant with you. And you shall enter the ark, you and your sons, your wife and your sons’ wives with you.
6:19 “And from all the cattle, all the creeping things, all the wild beasts, and all flesh, you shall bring two of each into the ark, to sustain them with you. They shall be male and female.
6:20 Of all the winged birds according to their kind, all the cattle according to their kind, and all the creeping things upon the earth according to their kinds, two of each shall come to you, male and female, to be sustained with you.
6:21 “And take for yourself every kind of food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself. It shall be food for you and for them.”
6:22 And Noah did all that the Lord God commanded him; so he did.

GENESIS 7

7:1 And the Lord God said to Noah, “Enter the ark, you and all your household, for I have seen you righteous before Me in this generation.
7:2 “Of the clean animals, bring in with you by sevens, male and female; and of the unclean animals, by twos, male and female.
7:3 Of the clean birds of heaven also, by sevens, male and female; and of the unclean birds, by twos, male and female, to preserve their seed upon all the earth.
7:4 “For in seven days I will bring rain upon the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out from the face of the earth every living thing that I have made.”
7:5 And Noah did all that the Lord God commanded him.
7:6 Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of water came upon the earth.
7:7 And Noah entered the ark with his sons, his wife, and his sons’ wives, because of the waters of the flood.
7:8 Of the birds, the clean animals, the unclean animals, and all the creeping things upon the earth,
7:9 two by two they entered the ark with Noah, male and female, as God had commanded him.
7:10 And after seven days the waters of the flood came upon the earth.
7:11 In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, on that day all the fountains of the deep burst open, and the floodgates of heaven were opened.
7:12 And rain fell upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.
7:13 On that very day Noah entered the ark, with Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah’s wife and the three wives of his sons with him.
7:14 And every wild beast according to its kind, all the cattle according to their kind, every creeping thing that moves upon the earth according to its kind, and every bird according to its kind
7:15 entered the ark with Noah, two by two of all flesh in which is the breath of life.
7:16 Those that entered were male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded Noah. And the Lord God closed the ark from the outside.
7:17 And the flood was upon the earth for forty days and forty nights. And the waters increased and lifted the ark, and it rose above the earth.
7:18 And the waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the earth, and the ark floated upon the waters.
7:19 And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth, and covered all the high mountains beneath heaven.
7:20 The waters rose fifteen cubits above them and covered all the high mountains.
7:21 And all flesh that moved upon the earth died: birds, cattle, wild beasts, every creeping thing that moves upon the earth, and all mankind.
7:22 Everything that had the breath of life, everything upon the dry land, died.
7:23 And He blotted out every living thing upon the face of the whole earth: mankind, cattle, creeping things, and the birds of heaven. They were blotted out from the earth, and only Noah remained, and those with him in the ark.
7:24 And the waters remained high upon the earth for one hundred and fifty days.

GENESIS 8

8:1 And God remembered Noah, and all the wild beasts, all the cattle, all the birds, and all the creeping things that were with him in the ark. And God brought a wind upon the earth, and the waters subsided.
8:2 And the fountains of the deep and the floodgates of heaven were closed, and the rain from heaven was restrained.
8:3 And the waters steadily withdrew from the earth; after one hundred and fifty days the waters had receded and diminished.
8:4 And in the seventh month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the ark rested upon the mountains of Ararat.
8:5 And the waters continued to diminish until the tenth month. In the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.
8:6 And after forty days Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made.
8:7 And he sent forth the raven to see whether the waters had subsided. It went out and did not return until the waters had dried from the earth.
8:8 And he sent forth the dove after it, to see whether the waters had subsided from the face of the earth.
8:9 But the dove found no resting place for its feet, and returned to him in the ark, for water covered the whole face of the earth. And he stretched forth his hand, took it, and brought it to himself within the ark.
8:10 And he waited another seven days, and again sent forth the dove from the ark.
8:11 And the dove returned to him toward evening, bearing an olive sprig with a leaf in its beak. And Noah knew that the waters had subsided from the earth.
8:12 And he waited another seven days, and again sent forth the dove; and it returned to him no more.
8:13 And in the six hundred and first year of Noah’s life, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters had disappeared from the earth. And Noah removed the covering of the ark which he had made, and saw that the waters had disappeared from the face of the earth.
8:14 And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.
8:15 And the Lord God spoke to Noah, saying,
8:16 “Come out of the ark, you and your wife, your sons and your sons’ wives with you.
8:17 Bring out with you all the wild beasts that are with you, and all flesh, both birds and cattle, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth. And grow and multiply upon the earth.”
8:18 And Noah came out, with his wife, his sons, and his sons’ wives.
8:19 And all the wild beasts, all the cattle, every bird, and every creeping thing that moves upon the earth came out of the ark according to their kinds.
8:20 And Noah built an altar to God. And he took of every kind of clean animal and every kind of clean bird, and offered whole burnt offerings upon the altar.
8:21 And the Lord God smelled the sweet fragrance, and the Lord God considered and said, “I will never again curse the earth because of the deeds of mankind, for the mind of man is bent intently upon evil from his youth. Therefore I will never again strike down all living flesh as I have done.
8:22 All the days of the earth, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and spring shall not cease by day or night.”

GENESIS 9

9:1 And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, “Grow and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it.
9:2 The dread and fear of you shall fall upon every wild beast of the earth, every bird of heaven, everything that moves upon the earth, and every fish of the sea. Into your hands I have given them.
9:3 “Every living thing that moves shall be food for you. As I gave the green plants, so I have given you all things.
9:4 But flesh with its lifeblood you shall not eat.
9:5 “And surely I will require your blood, the blood of your lives. From every beast I will require it, and from the hand of a fellow man I will require the life of man.
9:6 Whoever sheds the blood of man, his own blood shall be shed in return; for in the image of God I made man.
9:7 “But you, grow and multiply, and fill the earth and multiply upon it.”
9:8 And God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying,
9:9 “Behold, I establish My covenant with you and with your seed after you,
9:10 and with every living creature that is with you: the birds, the cattle, and all the wild beasts of the earth with you, all that came out of the ark.
9:11 “And I will establish My covenant with you: never again shall all flesh perish by the waters of a flood, and never again shall there be a flood of water to destroy the whole earth.”
9:12 And the Lord God said to Noah, “This is the sign of the covenant which I give between Me and you and every living creature with you, for everlasting generations:
9:13 I set My bow in the cloud, and it shall be a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth.
9:14 “And when I gather clouds over the earth, My bow shall appear in the cloud.
9:15 And I will remember My covenant between Me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall never again become a flood to blot out all flesh.
9:16 “My bow shall be in the cloud, and I will look upon it and remember the everlasting covenant between Me and every living creature of all flesh upon the earth.”
9:17 And God said to Noah, “This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between Me and all flesh upon the earth.”
9:18 Now the sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. And Ham was the father of Canaan.
9:19 These three were the sons of Noah, and from them people were scattered over all the earth.
9:20 And Noah began to till the ground, and planted a vineyard.
9:21 And he drank of the wine and became drunk, and lay uncovered in his house.
9:22 And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father’s nakedness, and went out and told his two brothers outside.
9:23 And Shem and Japheth took a garment, laid it across their shoulders, and walked backward to cover their father’s nakedness. Their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father’s nakedness.
9:24 And Noah awoke from his wine and knew what his younger son had done to him.
9:25 And he said: Cursed be Canaan; a household slave shall he be to his brothers.
9:26 And he said: Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant.
9:27 May God enlarge Japheth, and may he dwell in the houses of Shem; and let Canaan be their servant.
9:28 And Noah lived three hundred and fifty years after the flood.
9:29 And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.

GENESIS 10

10:1 These are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. And sons were born to them after the flood.
10:2 The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elishah, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
10:3 And the sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.
10:4 And the sons of Javan were Elishah and Tarshish, the Kittians and the Rhodians.
10:5 From these the islands of the nations were divided in their lands, each according to its language, by their tribes and their nations.
10:6 And the sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.
10:7 And the sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. And the sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan.
