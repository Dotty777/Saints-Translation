LAMENTATIONS 1

1:1 After Israel was taken captive and Jerusalem laid waste, Jeremiah sat weeping and lamented this lament over Jerusalem, saying:
How the city once full of peoples sat alone!
She has become like a widow, she who was populous among the nations.
A ruler among the regions has become subject to tribute.
1:2 Weeping, she wept in the night, her tears upon her cheeks.
Among all those who love her there is no one to comfort her.
All her friends have dealt faithlessly with her; they have become her enemies.
1:3 Judea was deported because of her humiliation and her great slavery.
She dwelt among the nations; she found no rest.
All her pursuers overtook her among those who afflicted her.
1:4 Zion’s roads mourn because no one comes at the festival.
All her gates are destroyed; her priests groan.
Her virgins are led away, and she is embittered within herself.
1:5 Those afflicting her have become the head, and her enemies prospered,
because the Lord humbled her for her many acts of ungodliness.
Her infants went into captivity before the oppressor.
1:6 All her beauty has departed from Daughter Zion.
Her rulers have become like rams finding no pasture;
they went without strength before the pursuer.
1:7 Jerusalem remembered the days of her humiliation and rejection,
all her desired things from ancient days.
When her people fell into the oppressor’s hands and no one helped her,
her enemies saw her and laughed at her deportation.
1:8 Jerusalem has sinned grievously; therefore she has come into upheaval.
All who glorified her humbled her, for they saw her shame.
She too groaned and turned backward.
1:9 Her uncleanness is at her feet; she did not remember her last things.
He brought her down exceedingly; there is no one to comfort her.
See, Lord, my humiliation, for the enemy has grown great!
1:10 The oppressor spread out his hand over all her desired things.
She saw nations enter her sanctuary,
those whom Thou didst command not to enter Thine assembly.
1:11 All her people groan, seeking bread.
They gave her desired things for food to restore life.
See, Lord, and behold, for I have become dishonored!
1:12 Not to you, all who pass along the road!
Turn and see whether there is pain like my pain, which came to be.
Speaking against me, the Lord humbled me on the day of His furious anger.
1:13 From His height He sent fire; He brought it down into my bones.
He spread a net for my feet; He turned me backward.
He made me desolate, suffering pain all day long.
1:14 A watch was kept over my acts of ungodliness;
they were intertwined in my hands and rose upon my neck.
My strength weakened, because the Lord put pains into my hands; I will not be able to stand.
1:15 The Lord removed all my strong men from my midst.
He called a time against me to crush my chosen men.
The Lord trod a winepress for the virgin Daughter Judah; for these things I weep.
1:16 My eye brought down water,
for the one who comforts me, who restores my life, is far from me.
My sons have been destroyed, for the enemy has grown strong.
1:17 Zion spread out her hands; there is no one to comfort her.
The Lord commanded concerning Jacob; around him are those who afflict him.
Jerusalem has become like a woman sitting apart among them.
1:18 The Lord is righteous, for I embittered His mouth.
Hear now, all you peoples, and see my pain.
My virgins and my young men went into captivity.
1:19 I called my lovers, but they deceived me.
My priests and my elders failed in the city,
for they sought food for themselves to restore their lives, and found none.
1:20 See, Lord, how I am afflicted! My belly is troubled,
and my heart has turned within me, for I have bitterly provoked.
Outside the sword bereaved me, like death in the house.
1:21 Hear now that I groan; there is no one to comfort me.
All my enemies heard of my calamities and rejoiced because Thou didst do it.
Thou didst bring a day, Thou didst call a time, and they became like me.
1:22 May all their wickedness come before Thee,
and glean them as they made a gleaning for all my sins;
for my groans are many and my heart is grieved.

LAMENTATIONS 2

2:1 How the Lord has darkened Daughter Zion in His anger!
He hurled Israel’s glory from heaven to earth
and did not remember His footstool on the day of His anger.
2:2 The Lord drowned without sparing all Jacob’s beautiful things.
In His fury He brought down Daughter Judah’s strongholds;
He pressed them to the earth and profaned her king and her rulers.
2:3 In His furious anger He broke every horn of Israel.
He turned back His right hand before the enemy
and kindled a flame in Jacob like fire, consuming everything around.
2:4 He drew His bow like an enemy and made His right hand firm like an opponent.
He killed everything desired by my eyes in Daughter Zion’s tent;
He poured out His fury like fire.
2:5 The Lord became like an enemy; He drowned Israel.
He drowned all her citadels and destroyed his strongholds;
He multiplied humiliation and abasement for Daughter Judah.
2:6 He spread out His dwelling like a vine and destroyed His festival.
The Lord forgot what He had made in Zion of festival and Sabbath;
in His angry indignation He provoked king, priest and ruler.
2:7 The Lord rejected His altar and shook off His sanctuary.
He shattered the wall of her citadels in the enemy’s hand.
They raised a shout in the Lord’s house as on a festival day.
2:8 The Lord turned to destroy Daughter Zion’s wall.
He stretched out a measure; He did not withdraw His hand from trampling.
The outer defense mourned, and the wall weakened together with it.
2:9 Her gates were fixed in the earth; He destroyed and broke her bars.
Her king and her rulers are among the nations.
There is no law; even her prophets saw no vision from the Lord.
2:10 Daughter Zion’s elders sat on the ground in silence.
They put dust on their heads and girded themselves with sackcloth.
They brought the leading virgins in Jerusalem down to the ground.
2:11 My eyes failed with tears; my heart was troubled.
My glory was poured onto the ground at the shattering of the daughter of my people,
as infant and nursing child failed in the city’s squares.
2:12 They said to their mothers, “Where are grain and wine?”
They weakened like the wounded in the city’s squares,
and their lives poured out into their mothers’ bosoms.
2:13 What can I testify to you, or what can I liken to you, Daughter Jerusalem?
Who will save you and comfort you, virgin Daughter Zion?
For the cup of your crushing has grown great; who will heal you?
2:14 Your prophets saw futile things and folly for you.
They did not uncover your injustice to turn back your captivity;
they saw for you futile pronouncements and expulsions.
2:15 All who pass along the road clapped their hands at you.
They hissed and shook their heads at Daughter Jerusalem:
“Is this the city they will call a crown of glory, the gladness of all the earth?”
2:16 All your enemies opened their mouths against you.
They hissed and gnashed their teeth; they said, “We have swallowed her!
Indeed, this is the day we expected; we found it, we saw it!”
2:17 The Lord did what He intended; He fulfilled His words,
which He commanded from ancient days. He brought down without sparing;
He made the enemy rejoice over you and exalted your oppressor’s horn.
2:18 Their heart cried to the Lord:
Walls of Zion, bring down tears like torrents day and night!
Give yourself no sobering pause; daughter, may your eye not fall silent.
2:19 Rise, exult in the night at the beginning of your watch!
Pour out your heart like water before the Lord.
Lift your hands to Him for the lives of your infants,
who weaken with hunger at the head of every way out.
2:20 See, Lord, and look: whom hast Thou gleaned in this way?
Will women eat the fruit of their womb?
A butcher made a gleaning; will infants nursing at breasts be murdered?
Wilt Thou kill priest and prophet in the Lord’s sanctuary?
2:21 Child and elder lay at the way out.
My virgins and my young men went into captivity.
With sword and famine Thou didst kill; on the day of Thine anger Thou didst slaughter without sparing.
2:22 He called a festival day of my sojourning from all around.
On the day of the Lord’s anger there was neither one rescued nor one left,
as I prevailed and multiplied all my enemies.

LAMENTATIONS 3

3:1 I am the man who sees poverty under the rod of His fury against me.
3:2 He took me and led me away into darkness, not light.
3:3 Indeed, against me He turned His hand all day.
3:4 He made my flesh and my skin old; He shattered my bones.
3:5 He built against me, surrounded my head and toiled.
3:6 He made me dwell in dark places like the dead of ages.
3:7 He built against me, and I will not go out; He made my bronze heavy.
3:8 Indeed, I will cry and call out; He blocked my prayer.
3:9 He built up my roads, blocked my paths and troubled them.
3:10 He is a bear lying in wait for me, a lion in hidden places.
3:11 He pursued me when I turned away and brought me to a stop; He made me desolate.
3:12 He drew His bow and fixed me as a target for a missile.
3:13 He drove the arrows of His quiver into my kidneys.
3:14 I became a laughingstock to all my people, their psalm all day.
3:15 He filled me with bitterness and made me drunk with gall.
3:16 He knocked out my teeth with a pebble and fed me ashes.
3:17 He drove my soul away from peace; I forgot good things.
3:18 I said, “My victory has perished, and my hope from the Lord.”
3:19 I remembered, from my poverty and my persecution, my bitterness and gall.
3:20 My soul will remember and brood over me.
3:21 I will set this in my heart; therefore I will endure.
3:25 The Lord is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul that will seek Him as good
3:26 and will endure and be quiet for the Lord’s salvation.
3:27 It is good for a man when he takes up a yoke in his youth.
3:28 He will sit alone and be silent, because he has taken it upon himself.
3:30 He will give his cheek to the one striking him; he will be filled with reproaches.
3:31 For the Lord will not reject for ever.
3:32 For the One who humbled will have compassion according to His abundant mercy.
3:33 For He did not answer from His heart, and He humbled human beings.
3:34 To humble under one’s feet all the prisoners of the earth,
3:35 to turn aside a man’s judgment before the face of the Most High,
3:36 to condemn a person when he is judged—the Lord did not say to do this.
3:37 Who spoke in this way and it came to be? The Lord did not command,
3:38 will not calamities and good come from the mouth of the Most High?
3:39 Why should a living person grumble, a man concerning his sin?
3:40 Our way was searched and examined; let us return to the Lord.
3:41 Let us lift our hearts upon our hands to the Exalted One in heaven.
3:42 We have sinned and acted godlessly, and Thou wast not propitiated.
3:43 Thou didst cover Thyself with fury and drive us away; Thou didst kill without sparing.
3:44 Thou didst spread a cloud over Thyself because of prayer,
3:45 to close my eyes; Thou didst place us to be rejected among the peoples.
3:46 All our enemies opened their mouths against us.
3:47 Fear and fury have come upon us, exaltation and crushing.
3:48 My eye will bring down outpourings of water at the shattering of the daughter of my people.
3:49 My eye was swallowed up, and I will not be silent, for there is no recovery,
3:50 until the Lord bends down and sees from heaven.
3:51 My eye will glean my soul beyond all the daughters of a city.
3:52 My enemies hunted and hunted me like a sparrow without cause.
3:53 They put my life to death in a pit and laid a stone upon me.
3:54 Water overflowed my head; I said, “I have been rejected.”
3:55 I called upon Thy name, Lord, from the lowest pit.
3:56 Thou didst hear my voice: “Do not hide Thine ears from my supplication.”
3:57 Thou didst draw near to help me on the day I called Thee; Thou didst say to me, “Do not fear.”
3:58 Thou didst judge the cases of my soul, Lord; Thou didst redeem my life.
3:59 Thou didst see my troubles, Lord; Thou didst judge my case.
3:60 Thou didst see all their vengeance in all their reasonings against me.
3:61 Thou didst hear their reproach, all their reasonings against me,
3:62 the lips of those rising against me and their meditations against me all day,
3:63 their sitting and their rising; look upon their eyes.
3:64 Thou wilt repay them a recompense, Lord, according to the works of their hands.
3:65 Thou wilt give them a shielding of heart, Thy toil for them.
3:66 Thou wilt pursue in anger and consume them from beneath heaven, Lord.

LAMENTATIONS 4

4:1 How the gold will grow dim, how the good silver will change!
Holy stones were poured out at the head of every way out.
4:2 Zion’s precious sons, exalted in gold—
how they were regarded as earthen vessels, works of a potter’s hands!
4:3 Even dragons bared breasts, and their young suckled;
the daughters of my people have become incurable, like a sparrow in the wilderness.
4:4 The nursing child’s tongue clung to its throat with thirst.
Infants asked for bread; there is no one to break it for them.
4:5 Those who ate luxuries were destroyed at the ways out;
those nursed upon scarlet wrapped themselves in dung heaps.
4:6 The lawlessness of the daughter of my people became greater than Sodom’s lawlessness,
which was overthrown as in haste, and they did not labor their hands upon it.
4:7 Her Nazirites were made purer than snow and shone beyond milk;
they glowed red beyond sapphire stones in their cut portion.
4:8 Their appearance grew darker than soot; they were unrecognized at the ways out.
Their skin clung to their bones; they dried up and became like wood.
4:9 Those wounded by the sword were better off than those wounded by famine;
they went away pierced through, deprived of the fields’ produce.
4:10 The hands of compassionate women boiled their children;
they became food for them at the shattering of the daughter of my people.
4:11 The Lord completed His fury and poured out His furious anger.
He kindled a fire in Zion, and it consumed her foundations.
4:12 The kings of the earth, all who dwell in the inhabited world, did not believe
that enemy and oppressor would enter through Jerusalem’s gates.
4:13 It was because of her prophets’ sins and her priests’ injustices,
those who shed righteous blood in her midst.
4:14 Her watchmen were shaken at the ways out, polluted with blood;
when they were unable, they touched their garments.
4:15 Keep away from unclean people—call them—keep away, keep away! Do not touch,
for they were set aflame and shaken. Say among the nations, “They will sojourn no longer.”
4:16 The Lord’s face is their portion; He will look upon them no longer.
They showed no respect to priests and no mercy to elders.
4:17 While we still existed, our eyes failed, looking vainly for our help.
Keeping watch, we watched for a nation that does not save.
4:18 We hunted our little ones so they would not walk in our squares.
Our time has drawn near, our days were fulfilled, our time is here.
4:19 Those pursuing us became swifter than the eagles of heaven.
They were kindled upon the mountains and lay in wait for us in the wilderness.
4:20 The spirit of our face, the Lord’s anointed, was seized in their destructions,
of whom we said, “In his shadow we will live among the nations.”
4:21 Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Idumea, dwelling upon the land!
To you too the Lord’s cup will pass; you will become drunk and pour it out.
4:22 Your lawlessness has ended, Daughter Zion; He will deport you no longer.
He has visited your acts of lawlessness, Daughter Edom; He has uncovered your acts of ungodliness.

LAMENTATIONS 5

5:1 Remember, Lord, what has happened to us;
look and see our reproach.
5:2 Our inheritance has passed to outsiders,
our houses to strangers.
5:3 We have become orphans; there is no father;
our mothers are like widows.
5:4 From our days, our wood came in exchange.
5:5 We were pursued at our neck;
we toiled and did not rest.
5:6 Egypt gave a hand,
Assyria for their satisfaction.
5:7 Our fathers sinned and no longer exist;
we bore their lawless acts.
5:8 Slaves ruled over us;
there is no one to redeem us from their hand.
5:9 At the risk of our lives we will bring in our bread,
because of the sword of the wilderness.
5:10 Our skin turned livid like an oven;
they shriveled because of famine’s storms.
5:11 They humiliated women in Zion,
virgins in Judah’s cities.
5:12 Rulers were hung by their hands;
elders were not glorified.
5:13 Chosen men took up weeping,
and young men weakened under wood.
5:14 Elders ceased from the gate,
chosen men ceased from their songs.
5:15 The joy of our heart ended;
our dancing turned to mourning.
5:16 The crown fell from our head;
woe indeed to us, for we sinned!
5:17 For this our heart became sorrowful;
for this our eyes grew dim,
5:18 because Mount Zion was destroyed;
foxes passed through her.
5:19 But Thou, Lord, wilt dwell for ever;
Thy throne endures from generation to generation.
5:20 Why wilt Thou forget us for ever
and forsake us for so many days?
5:21 Turn us back unto Thee, O Lord, and we shall be turned back; and renew our days as before.
5:22 For Thou hast utterly rejected us
and hast been exceedingly angry with us.
