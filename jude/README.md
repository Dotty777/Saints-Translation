## Chapter decisions and review queue

Keep/kept in 1:6 and the distinct judgment images remain. Michael addresses the devil with you in 1:9; May the Lord rebuke you is a third-person wish, not direct address to God. Without fear in 1:12 now accompanies feasting, following the displayed punctuation.
