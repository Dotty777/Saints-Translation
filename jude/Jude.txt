JUDE 1

1:1 Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, beloved in God the Father and kept for Jesus Christ:
1:2 May mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you.
1:3 Beloved, while making every effort to write to you about the salvation we share, I found it necessary to write and urge you to contend for the faith delivered once for all to the saints.
1:4 For certain people have slipped in unnoticed, those written about long ago for this judgment: ungodly people who turn the grace of our God into shameless indulgence and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.
1:5 Now I wish to remind you, although you have come to know all this once for all, that Jesus, after saving a people from the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe.
1:6 And He has kept the angels who did not keep their own domain, but abandoned their proper dwelling, in everlasting chains under gloom for the judgment of the great day.
1:7 In the same way, Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, which practiced sexual immorality and went after other flesh in a manner like these, are set before us as an example, undergoing the penalty of eternal fire.
1:8 Yet in the same way these people too, in their dreaming, defile flesh, reject dominion, and slander glorious ones.
1:9 But Michael the archangel, when he was contending with the devil and disputing about the body of Moses, did not dare to bring a slanderous judgment against him, but said, “May the Lord rebuke you.”
1:10 But these people slander whatever they do not know; and what they understand by instinct, like unreasoning animals, becomes the very thing by which they are corrupted.
1:11 Woe to them! For they have gone in the way of Cain, have poured themselves into Balaam’s error for pay, and have perished in Korah’s rebellion.
1:12 These are reefs at your love feasts, feasting together with you without fear, shepherding themselves; waterless clouds carried along by winds; late-autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, uprooted;
1:13 wild waves of the sea foaming up their own shames; wandering stars for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept forever.
1:14 Enoch also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about these people, saying, “Look, the Lord came with His holy myriads,
1:15 to execute judgment against all and to convict every soul concerning all their ungodly works that they committed in ungodliness, and concerning all the harsh things that ungodly sinners spoke against Him.”
1:16 These are grumblers, faultfinders, walking according to their own desires; their mouth speaks boastful things, and they flatter people for the sake of advantage.
1:17 But you, beloved, remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,
1:18 for they used to tell you, “In the last time there will be mockers, walking according to their own ungodly desires.”
1:19 These are the ones who create divisions, unspiritual people who do not have the Spirit.
1:20 But you, beloved, building yourselves up upon your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit,
1:21 keep yourselves in the love of God, awaiting the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that leads to eternal life.
1:22 And have mercy on some who are doubting;
1:23 save others, snatching them out of the fire; and have mercy on others with fear, hating even the tunic stained by the flesh.
1:24 Now to Him who is able to guard you from stumbling and to make you stand blameless before His glory with exultation,
1:25 to the only God our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, might, and authority before all time, and now, and into all the ages. Amen.
