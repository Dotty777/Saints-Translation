Romans 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus, called as an apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,

1:2which He promised beforehand through His prophets in holy Scriptures,

1:3concerning His Son, who came from David’s seed according to the flesh,

1:4who was designated Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by resurrection from the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,

1:5through whom we received grace and apostleship for the obedience of faith among all the nations for His name,

1:6among whom you also are, called by Jesus Christ:

1:7to all who are in Rome, beloved of God, called as saints: grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Paul’s Desire to Visit Rome

1:8First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is proclaimed in the whole world.

1:9For God, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of His Son, is my witness that I remember you without ceasing,

1:10always in my prayers asking whether somehow now at last I may be granted a way, by the will of God, to come to you.

1:11For I long to see you, to share with you some spiritual gift so that you may be strengthened,

1:12that is, to be encouraged together among you through one another’s faith, both yours and mine.

1:13And I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that I often planned to come to you—and was hindered until now—so that I might have some fruit among you also, just as among the other nations.

1:14I am a debtor both to Greeks and to those who do not speak Greek, both to the wise and to the senseless.

1:15Thus, for my part, I am eager to proclaim the gospel also to you who are in Rome.

The Righteous Will Live by Faith

1:16For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is God’s power for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.

1:17For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith, as it is written, “But the righteous one will live by faith.”

The Wrath of God Revealed

1:18For God’s wrath is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of people who suppress the truth in unrighteousness,

1:19because what is known of God is plain among them, for God made it plain to them.

1:20For His invisible qualities, both His eternal power and divinity, have been clearly seen since the creation of the world, understood through the things made, so that they are without excuse,

1:21because, although they knew God, they did not glorify Him as God or give thanks, but became futile in their thoughts, and their senseless heart was darkened.

1:22Claiming to be wise, they became foolish,

1:23and exchanged the glory of the incorruptible God for the likeness of an image of corruptible humanity, and of birds and four-footed animals and creeping things.

God Handed Them Over

1:24Therefore God handed them over, in the desires of their hearts, to impurity, so that their bodies would be dishonored among them,

1:25who exchanged the truth of God for the lie, and revered and worshiped the creation instead of the Creator, who is blessed throughout the ages. Amen.

1:26For this reason God handed them over to dishonorable passions; for their females exchanged natural relations for those contrary to nature,

1:27and likewise the males, leaving natural relations with the female, burned in their desire for one another, males with males committing what is shameful and receiving in themselves the due penalty for their error.

A Debased Mind

1:28And just as they did not see fit to retain God in their knowledge, God handed them over to an unfit mind, to do what is not fitting:

1:29filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, greed, evil; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, malice; whisperers,

1:30slanderers, haters of God, insolent, proud, boastful, inventors of evils, disobedient to parents,

1:31without understanding, faithless, without natural affection, merciless;

1:32who, knowing God’s righteous decree, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do them, but also approve those who practice them.

Romans 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Judgment of God Is Impartial

2:1Therefore you are without excuse, O man—whoever you are who judges. For when you judge another, you condemn yourself, because you who judge practice the same things.

2:2And we know that God’s judgment against those who practice such things is according to truth.

2:3But do you think, O man—you who judge those who practice such things and do them yourself—that you will escape God’s judgment?

2:4Or do you despise the wealth of His kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that God’s kindness leads you to repentance?

2:5But by your hardness and unrepentant heart, you store up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath and revelation of God’s righteous judgment,

2:6who will repay each according to his works:

2:7to those who, through perseverance in good work, seek glory and honor and incorruptibility, eternal life;

2:8but to those acting from selfish ambition, who disobey the truth but obey unrighteousness, wrath and fury,

2:9affliction and distress upon every human soul that does evil—the Jew first and also the Greek;

2:10but glory and honor and peace to everyone who does good, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.

2:11For there is no partiality with God.

The Law Written on Hearts

2:12For all who sinned without law will also perish without law; and all who sinned under law will be judged through law.

2:13For it is not the hearers of law who are righteous before God, but the doers of law will be justified.

2:14For whenever Gentiles who do not have law do by nature the things of the law, these, though not having law, are a law to themselves,

2:15who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness with it, and their reasonings among themselves accusing or even defending,

2:16on the day when God judges people’s secrets according to my gospel through Christ Jesus.

The Jew and the Law

2:17But if you bear the name Jew, and rest upon law and boast in God,

2:18and know His will and approve the things that matter, being instructed from the law,

2:19and are confident that you yourself are a guide of the blind, a light to those in darkness,

2:20an instructor of the senseless, a teacher of infants, having in the law the embodiment of knowledge and truth—

2:21you then who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who proclaim that one must not steal, do you steal?

2:22You who say that one must not commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?

2:23You who boast in law, do you dishonor God through transgression of the law?

2:24For “the name of God is blasphemed among the nations because of you,” just as it is written.

Circumcision of the Heart

2:25For circumcision benefits you if you practice law; but if you are a transgressor of law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.

2:26So if the uncircumcised person keeps the law’s righteous requirements, will not his uncircumcision be reckoned as circumcision?

2:27And the one uncircumcised by nature who fulfills the law will judge you who, with the written letter and circumcision, are a transgressor of law.

2:28For the Jew is not the one outwardly so, nor is circumcision what is outward in flesh;

2:29but the Jew is the one inwardly so, and circumcision is of the heart, in Spirit, not in letter, whose praise is not from people but from God.

Romans 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Advantage of the Jew

3:1What, then, is the Jew’s advantage, or what is the benefit of circumcision?

3:2Much in every way. First, indeed, because they were entrusted with the oracles of God.

3:3For what if some were unfaithful? Will their unfaithfulness nullify God’s faithfulness?

3:4May it never be! But let God be true, and every human being a liar, just as it is written, “So that Thou mayest be justified in Thy words, and wilt prevail when Thou art judged.”

3:5But if our unrighteousness demonstrates God’s righteousness, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous when He brings wrath? I speak in human terms.

3:6May it never be! For then how will God judge the world?

3:7But if God’s truth abounded through my falsehood to His glory, why am I still judged as a sinner?

3:8And why not say—as we are slandered and as some claim we say—“Let us do evil things so that good things may come”? Their condemnation is just.

None Is Righteous

3:9What, then? Are we better off? Not at all; for we have already charged both Jews and Greeks with all being under sin,

3:10just as it is written: “There is no righteous one, not even one;

3:11there is no one who understands; there is no one who seeks God.

3:12All turned aside; together they became useless. There is no one who shows kindness; there is not even one.

3:13Their throat is an opened tomb; with their tongues they practiced deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips;

3:14their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.

3:15Their feet are swift to pour out blood;

3:16ruin and misery are in their ways,

3:17and the way of peace they did not know.

3:18There is no fear of God before their eyes.”

Every Mouth Silenced

3:19And we know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those under the law, so that every mouth may be stopped and the whole world may become answerable to God;

3:20because by works of law no flesh will be justified before Him, for through law comes knowledge of sin.

Righteousness Through Faith in Jesus Christ

3:21But now, apart from law, God’s righteousness has been revealed, witnessed by the law and the prophets:

3:22God’s righteousness through faith in Jesus Christ, for all who believe. For there is no distinction,

3:23for all sinned and fall short of God’s glory,

3:24being justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus,

3:25whom God set forth as a propitiation in His blood, through faith, to demonstrate His righteousness because of the passing over of sins committed beforehand,

3:26in God’s forbearance, to demonstrate His righteousness at the present time, so that He may be righteous and justify the one who has faith in Jesus.

Boasting Excluded

3:27Where, then, is boasting? It was excluded. Through what kind of law? Of works? No, but through a law of faith.

3:28For we reckon that a person is justified by faith apart from works of law.

3:29Or is God the God of Jews only? Is He not also of Gentiles? Yes, also of Gentiles,

3:30since God is one, who will justify the circumcised from faith and the uncircumcised through faith.

3:31Do we then nullify law through faith? May it never be! Rather, we establish law.

Romans 4

Read this chapter on staging

What, Then, Shall We Say Abraham Found?

4:1What, then, shall we say Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh, has found?

4:2For if Abraham was justified by works, he has something to boast of—but not before God.

4:3For what does the Scripture say? “And Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness.”

4:4Now to the one who works, the wage is not reckoned according to grace, but according to what is owed.

4:5But to the one who does not work, but believes in the One who justifies the ungodly, his faith is reckoned as righteousness,

4:6just as David also speaks of the blessedness of the person to whom God reckons righteousness apart from works:

4:7“Blessed are those whose lawless deeds were forgiven and whose sins were covered;

4:8blessed is the man whose sin the Lord certainly will not reckon.”

Is This Blessedness upon the Circumcised Only?

4:9Is this blessedness, then, upon the circumcised, or also upon the uncircumcised? For we say, “Faith was reckoned to Abraham as righteousness.”

4:10How, then, was it reckoned? While he was circumcised, or uncircumcised? Not while circumcised, but while uncircumcised.

4:11And he received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith he had while uncircumcised, so that he might be father of all who believe while uncircumcised, so that righteousness might be reckoned to them also,

4:12and father of the circumcised, to those who are not only circumcised but also walk in the footsteps of the faith our father Abraham had while uncircumcised.

The Promise Through the Righteousness of Faith

4:13For the promise to Abraham or to his seed that he would be heir of the world was not through law, but through the righteousness of faith.

4:14For if those from law are heirs, faith has been emptied and the promise has been nullified;

4:15for the law brings wrath, but where there is no law, neither is there transgression.

4:16For this reason it is from faith, so that it may be according to grace, so that the promise may be secure to all the seed—not only to the one from the law, but also to the one from Abraham’s faith. He is father of us all,

4:17just as it is written, “I have made you father of many nations,” before the God whom he believed, who gives life to the dead and calls the things that are not as though they are.

The Faith of Abraham

4:18Against hope, he believed in hope, so that he might become father of many nations, according to what had been said, “So shall your seed be.”

4:19And without growing weak in faith, he considered his own body, already deadened as it was, being about a hundred years old, and the deadness of Sarah’s womb;

4:20but with regard to God’s promise he did not waver in unbelief, but was strengthened in faith, giving glory to God,

4:21and being fully assured that what He has promised He is also able to do.

4:22Therefore it was also reckoned to him as righteousness.

4:23But “it was reckoned to him” was not written for his sake alone,

4:24but also for our sake, to whom it is to be reckoned, who believe in the One who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead,

4:25who was handed over for our trespasses and was raised for our justification.

Romans 5

Read this chapter on staging

Peace with God Through Faith

5:1Therefore, having been justified from faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ,

5:2through whom we have also obtained access by faith into this grace in which we stand; and we boast in hope of God’s glory.

5:3And not only this, but we also boast in afflictions, knowing that affliction produces perseverance,

5:4and perseverance, proven character, and proven character, hope.

5:5And hope does not put us to shame, because God’s love has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us.

Christ Died for the Ungodly

5:6For while we were still weak, even then Christ died at the appointed time for the ungodly.

5:7For rarely will someone die for a righteous person; though perhaps for the good person someone even dares to die.

5:8But God demonstrates His own love toward us in that, while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

5:9Much more, then, having now been justified in His blood, we will be saved through Him from wrath.

5:10For if, while we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we will be saved in His life.

5:11And not only this, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received reconciliation.

Adam and Christ

5:12For this reason, just as through one person sin entered the world, and through sin, death, and so death passed to all people, because all sinned—

5:13for before the law came, sin was in the world, but sin is not charged to account when there is no law;

5:14nevertheless death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those who did not sin in the likeness of Adam’s transgression, who is a type of the One to come.

Romans 15

Read this chapter on staging

Bearing with the Weak

15:1And we who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of those without strength, and not to please ourselves.

15:2Let each of us please our neighbor for his good, to build him up.

15:3For even Christ did not please Himself; but just as it is written, “The reproaches of those who reproach Thee fell upon Me.”

15:4For whatever was written beforehand was written for our instruction, so that through perseverance and through the encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.

15:5And may the God of perseverance and encouragement grant you to have the same mind among one another according to Christ Jesus,

15:6so that with one accord and one mouth you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Christ the Hope of Jews and Gentiles

15:7Therefore welcome one another, just as Christ also welcomed you, to God’s glory.

15:8For I say that Christ has become a servant of the circumcision for God’s truth, to confirm the promises of the fathers,

15:9and for the Gentiles to glorify God for mercy, just as it is written, “For this reason I will give thanks to Thee among the Gentiles, and sing praise to Thy name.”

15:10And again it says, “Rejoice, Gentiles, with His people.”

15:11And again, “Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples praise Him.”

15:12And again Isaiah says, “There will be the root of Jesse, and the One who rises to rule Gentiles; upon Him Gentiles will hope.”

15:13And may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may abound in hope in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Paul’s Ministry to the Gentiles

15:14And I myself am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.

15:15But I wrote more boldly to you on some points, to remind you again, because of the grace given me by God

15:16to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, performing priestly service with God’s gospel, so that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified in the Holy Spirit.

15:17Therefore I have reason to boast in Christ Jesus concerning the things of God;

15:18for I will not dare to speak of anything that Christ did not work through me for the Gentiles’ obedience, in word and deed,

15:19in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit of God, so that from Jerusalem and around as far as Illyricum I have fulfilled the proclamation of Christ’s gospel,

15:20and so making it my ambition to proclaim the gospel where Christ has not been named, so that I would not build on another’s foundation,

15:21but just as it is written, “Those to whom nothing about Him was announced will see, and those who have not heard will understand.”

Paul’s Plan to Visit Rome

15:22For this reason I was also often hindered from coming to you;

15:23but now, no longer having a place in these regions, and having a longing for many years to come to you,

15:24whenever I travel to Spain—for I hope to see you as I pass through, and to be sent onward there by you, if I may first be filled in some measure by your company—

15:25but now I am traveling to Jerusalem, serving the saints.

15:26For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.

15:27For they were pleased, and they are their debtors; for if the Gentiles shared in their spiritual things, they also owe them service in material things.

15:28Therefore, having completed this and sealed this fruit for them, I will depart by way of you for Spain.

15:29And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of Christ’s blessing.

An Appeal for Prayer

15:30And I urge you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to struggle together with me in prayers to God for me,

15:31so that I may be rescued from those who disobey in Judea, and my service for Jerusalem may become acceptable to the saints,

15:32so that, coming to you in joy through God’s will, I may find rest together with you.

15:33And may the God of peace be with you all. Amen.

Romans 16

Read this chapter on staging

Phoebe Commended

16:1And I commend to you Phoebe our sister, who is also a deacon of the Church in Cenchreae,

16:2so that you may welcome her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and stand by her in whatever matter she may need from you; for she herself has been a patron of many, and of me personally.

Greetings to the Saints in Rome

16:3Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

16:4who risked their own neck for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the Churches of the Gentiles;

16:5and greet the Church in their house. Greet Epaenetus my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Asia for Christ.

16:6Greet Mary, who labored much for you.

16:7Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and fellow prisoners, who are distinguished among the apostles, who also were in Christ before me.

16:8Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord.

16:9Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys my beloved.

16:10Greet Apelles, approved in Christ. Greet those of Aristobulus’s household.

16:11Greet Herodion my kinsman. Greet those of Narcissus’s household who are in the Lord.

16:12Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet Persis the beloved, who labored much in the Lord.

16:13Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine.

16:14Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.

16:15Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas and all the saints with them.

16:16Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the Churches of Christ greet you.

Watch Out for Those Who Cause Divisions

16:17And I urge you, brothers, to watch those who cause divisions and snares contrary to the teaching you learned, and turn away from them;

16:18for such people do not serve our Lord Christ, but their own belly, and through smooth speech and flattery they deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.

16:19For your obedience has reached everyone; therefore I rejoice over you, but I want you to be wise about what is good and innocent about what is evil.

16:20And the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet soon. The grace of our Lord Jesus be with you.

Greetings from Paul’s Companions

16:21Timothy my fellow worker greets you, and Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.

16:22I, Tertius, who wrote the letter, greet you in the Lord.

16:23Gaius, my host and host of the whole Church, greets you. Erastus the city steward greets you, and Quartus the brother.

16:24[[The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.]]
