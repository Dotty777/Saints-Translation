2 Chronicles 1

Read this chapter on staging

Solomon at the High Place in Gabaon

1:1Solomon son of David grew strong over his kingdom. The Lord his God was with him and made him exceedingly great.

1:2Solomon spoke to all Israel: the commanders of thousands and hundreds, the judges, and all the rulers before Israel, the ancestral rulers.

1:3Solomon and the whole assembly with him went to the high place in Gabaon, where God’s Tent of Testimony was, which Moses, the Lord’s servant, had made in the wilderness.

1:4But David had brought God’s ark up from the city of Kariathiarim, because he had prepared a tent for it in Jerusalem.

1:5The bronze altar that Beseleel son of Ouriou son of Or had made was there before the Lord’s tent, and Solomon and the assembly sought it out.

1:6Solomon offered there upon the bronze altar before the Lord, the one in the tent; he offered a thousandfold whole burnt offering upon it.

Give Me Wisdom and Understanding

1:7That night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, “Ask what I should give you.”

1:8Solomon said to God, “Thou didst show great mercy to David my father, and Thou didst make me king in his place.

1:9Now, Lord God, let Thy name be confirmed upon David my father, for Thou hast made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.

1:10Now give me wisdom and understanding, and I shall go out before this people and come in; for who can judge this great people of Thine?” †

1:11God said to Solomon, “Because this came into your heart, and you did not ask for wealth of possessions, glory, or the life of opponents, and did not ask for many days, but did ask for wisdom and understanding to judge My people over whom I made you king,

1:12I give you wisdom and understanding. I will also give you wealth, possessions, and glory, so that none like you has existed among the kings before you, nor will it be so after you.”

1:13Solomon came from the bama in Gabaon to Jerusalem, from before the Tent of Testimony, and he reigned over Israel. †

The Chariots, Horses and Wealth of Solomon

1:14Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had a thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen. He left them in the chariot cities, and the people were with the king in Jerusalem.

1:15The king made gold and silver in Jerusalem like stones, and cedars in Judea as abundant as the sycamores in the plain.

1:16Solomon’s horses came out of Egypt, and there was the price of the king’s merchants; they bought them to trade,

1:17and they went up and exported from Egypt one chariot for six hundred in silver and a horse for a hundred fifty. Likewise they brought them through their hands for all the kings of the Chettites and the kings of Syria.

1:18Solomon resolved to build a house for the Lord’s name and a house for his kingdom.

2 Chronicles 2

Read this chapter on staging

Solomon Gathers the Workers

2:1Solomon gathered seventy thousand burden-bearing men and eighty thousand quarrymen in the mountain, with three thousand six hundred overseers over them.

The Letter to Chiram King of Tyre

2:2Solomon sent to Chiram king of Tyre, saying, “As you dealt with my father David and sent him cedars to build himself a house to live in,

2:3behold, I his son am building a house for the name of the Lord my God, to consecrate it to Him, to burn incense before Him, with continual presentation and the continual offering of whole burnt offerings morning and evening, on sabbaths, new moons, and the feasts of the Lord our God. This is an everlasting obligation upon Israel.

2:4The house I am building is great, for our God is greater than all the gods.

2:5Who will be able to build Him a house, since heaven and the heaven of heaven cannot bear His glory? Who am I to build Him a house, except to burn incense before Him?

2:6Now send me a wise and knowledgeable man to work in gold, silver, bronze, and iron, in purple, scarlet, and hyacinth blue, skilled at engraving, with the skilled men with me in Judah and Jerusalem whom David my father prepared.

2:7Send me cedar, juniper, and pine timbers from Lebanon, for I know your slaves know how to cut timber from Lebanon. Behold, your servants will be with my servants

2:8and will go to prepare abundant timber for me, for the house I am building is great and glorious.

2:9Behold, I give the workers cutting timber grain for food, as gifts to your servants: twenty thousand kors, and twenty thousand kors of barley, twenty thousand measures of wine, and twenty thousand measures of oil.”

The Reply of Chiram

2:10Chiram king of Tyre replied in writing and sent it to Solomon: “Because the Lord loves His people, He has appointed you king over them.”

2:11Chiram said, “Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who gave King David a wise son, skilled in understanding and knowledge, who will build a house for the Lord and a house for his kingdom.

2:12Now I have sent you a wise man, possessing understanding: Chiram, my father,

2:13his mother is from Dan’s daughters, and his father is a Tyrian man. He knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, and timber; to weave in purple, hyacinth blue, fine linen, and scarlet; to engrave engravings and devise every design, whatever you give him, with your skilled men and the skilled men of David my lord, your father.

2:14Now let my lord send his servants the grain, barley, oil, and wine of which he spoke.

2:15We will cut timber from Lebanon according to all your need and bring it in rafts to Joppa’s sea. You shall bring it to Jerusalem.”

The Resident Foreigners Numbered

2:16Solomon gathered all the resident foreign men in Israel’s land after the numbering by which David his father had numbered them. They were found to be a hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred.

2:17He assigned seventy thousand of them as burden bearers, eighty thousand as quarrymen, and three thousand six hundred as taskmasters over the people.

2 Chronicles 3

The Building of the House Begun

3:1Solomon began building the Lord’s house in Jerusalem on Mount Amoria, where the Lord appeared to David his father, at the place David had prepared, at the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.

3:2He began building in the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

3:3These were the measurements when Solomon began building God’s house: the length in cubits, by the first measurement, was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

3:4The ailam before the house extended twenty cubits across the house’s width, and its height was a hundred twenty cubits. He gilded it inside with pure gold. †

3:5He paneled the great house with cedar wood, gilded it with pure gold, and carved palm trees and hanging ornaments upon it.

3:6He adorned the house with precious stones for glory, and with gold from Pharouaim,

3:7and he gilded the house, the walls, gateways, ceilings, and doorways with gold, and carved cherubim upon the walls.

The House of the Holy of Holies

3:8He made the House of the Holy of Holies. Its length across the width was twenty cubits, and its width twenty cubits. He gilded it with pure gold for cherubim, amounting to six hundred talents.

3:9As for the weight of the nails, the weight of one was fifty shekels of gold. He gilded the upper room with gold.

The Two Cherubim

3:10He made two cherubim in the House of the Holy of Holies, work made from wood, and he gilded them with gold.

3:11The cherubim’s wings extended twenty cubits in length. One wing, five cubits, touched the house’s wall, and the other wing, five cubits, touched the other cherub’s wing.

3:12One wing of the other cherub, five cubits, touched the house’s wall, and the other wing, five cubits, touched the wing of the other cherub.

3:13The cherubim’s wings were spread twenty cubits. They stood upon their feet, with their faces toward the house.

3:14He made the curtain of hyacinth blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, and wove cherubim into it.

The Two Pillars

3:15He made two pillars before the house, thirty-five cubits in height, and their capitals five cubits.

3:16He made serseroth in the dabir and placed them upon the pillars’ capitals. He made a hundred pomegranates and placed them upon the hanging ornaments. †

3:17He erected the pillars before the temple, one on the right and one on the left. He named the one on the right Establishment and the one on the left Strength.

The Bronze Altar and the Cast Sea

4:1He made the bronze altar, twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high.

4:2He made the cast sea, ten cubits in diameter, round all around, and five cubits high, with a circumference of thirty cubits.

4:3Likenesses of calves were beneath it, circling it all around; ten cubits surrounded the basin. They cast the calves in two groups when they were cast,

4:4in which they made them, twelve calves: three looking north, three looking west, three looking south, and three looking east. The sea was above them, with their hindquarters inward.

4:5Its thickness was a handbreadth, and its rim was like a cup’s rim, carved with lily shoots, holding three thousand metretes. He completed it. †

The Basins, Lampstands and Tables

4:6He made ten basins and placed five on the right and five on the left, to wash and rinse in them the preparations for the whole burnt offerings. The sea was for the priests to wash in.

4:7He made ten golden lampstands according to their ordinance and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left.

4:8He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left. He made a hundred golden bowls.

4:9He made the priests’ court, the great court, and doors for the court, with their doorways covered in bronze.

4:10He placed the sea at the house’s right-hand corner, facing toward the east.

The Work of Chiram

4:11Chiram made the meat hooks, fire pans, the altar’s grate, and all its vessels. Chiram finished doing all the work he performed for Solomon the king in God’s house:

4:12two pillars, with golath for the chothareth upon the tops of the two pillars, and two networks covering the heads of the chothareth that are upon the pillars’ tops; †

4:13four hundred golden bells for the two networks, and two groups of pomegranates on each network, covering the two golath of the chothareth above the pillars.

4:14He made ten mechonoth and made the basins upon the mechonoth, †

4:15and the one sea and the twelve calves beneath it,

4:16and the foot pieces, lifting tools, cauldrons, meat hooks, and all their vessels, which Chiram made and brought up to King Solomon in the Lord’s house, of pure bronze.

4:17The king cast them in the Jordan region, in the thick earth, in the house of Sokchoth and between Sirdatha.

4:18Solomon made all these vessels in very great abundance, because the supply of bronze by weight did not run out.

The Vessels of Gold

4:19Solomon made all the vessels of the Lord’s house, the golden altar, and the tables with the bread of presentation upon them;

4:20the lampstands and the lamps for light according to the ordinance before the dabir, of pure gold,

4:21and their tongs, their lamps, the bowls, the incense cups, and the fire pans of pure gold;

4:22and the inner door of the house into the Holy of Holies, for the golden doors of the temple house.

The Ark Brought into the House

5:1All the work that Solomon made in the Lord’s house was finished. Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father—the silver, the gold, and the vessels—and put them in the treasury of the Lord’s house.

5:2Then Solomon assembled Israel’s elders and all the rulers of the tribes, the leaders of the ancestral houses of Israel’s sons, at Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the Lord’s covenant from David’s city, which is Zion.

5:3All the men of Israel assembled before the king at the feast; this was the seventh month.

5:4All Israel’s elders came, and all the Levites took up the ark.

5:5They brought up the ark, the Tent of Testimony, and all the holy vessels that were in the tent; the priests and the Levites brought it up.

5:6King Solomon and all Israel’s congregation, and those who feared, and their assembled ones, were before the ark, sacrificing calves and sheep that could neither be numbered nor reckoned because of their multitude.

5:7The priests brought the ark of the Lord’s covenant to its place, into the dabir of the house, into the Holy of Holies, beneath the cherubim’s wings.

5:8The cherubim spread out their wings over the ark’s place, and the cherubim covered the ark and its carrying poles from above.

5:9The carrying poles projected, and their ends could be seen from the holy places in front of the dabir, but they could not be seen outside. They have been there to this day.

5:10There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses had placed at Choreb, which the Lord covenanted with Israel’s sons when they came out of the land of Egypt.

The Glory Fills the House

5:11When the priests came out of the holy places—for all the priests who were found there were sanctified, without being arranged by their courses—

5:12and all the Levitical singers belonging to the sons of Asaph, to Heman, and to Idithoun, and their sons and their brethren, clothed in fine-linen robes, with cymbals, nablai, and kinyrai, stood opposite the altar, and with them were a hundred and twenty priests sounding trumpets,

5:13there was one voice in trumpeting, singing psalms, and shouting, to give thanks and praise to the Lord. When they raised their voice with trumpets, cymbals, and musical instruments, they said, “Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good, for His mercy endures for ever.” The house was filled with a cloud of the Lord’s glory. †

5:14The priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the Lord’s glory filled God’s house.

2 Chronicles 6

Read this chapter on staging

Solomon Blesses the Assembly

6:1Then Solomon said, “The Lord said that He would dwell in thick darkness.

6:2I have built a house for Thy name, holy to Thee and ready for dwelling throughout the ages.”

6:3The king turned his face and blessed all Israel’s assembly, while all Israel’s assembly stood by.

6:4He said, “Blessed be the Lord, Israel’s God, who spoke with His mouth to David my father and fulfilled it with His hands, saying,

6:5‘Since the day I brought My people up from Egypt’s land, I chose no city from all Israel’s tribes in which to build a house for My name to be there, and I chose no man to be leader over My people Israel.

6:6But I chose Jerusalem for My name to be there, and I chose David to be over My people Israel.’

6:7It came upon the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the Lord, Israel’s God.

6:8The Lord said to David my father, ‘Because it came upon your heart to build a house for My name, you did well that it came upon your heart.

6:9Nevertheless, you will not build the house. Your son who will come from your loins—he will build the house for My name.’

6:10The Lord has established the word He spoke. I have come to be in place of David my father and sat on Israel’s throne, as the Lord spoke, and I have built the house for the name of the Lord, Israel’s God.

6:11I placed there the ark, in which is the Lord’s covenant that He made with Israel.”

The Prayer of Dedication

6:12He stood before the Lord’s altar, opposite all Israel’s assembly, and spread out his hands.

6:13For Solomon had made a bronze base and put it in the middle of the temple court, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high. He stood on it, fell on his knees opposite all Israel’s assembly, and spread out his hands toward heaven.

6:14He said, “Lord, Israel’s God, there is no god like Thee in heaven or on earth, keeping covenant and mercy for Thy servants who walk before Thee with their whole heart.

6:15Thou hast kept for Thy servant David my father the things Thou didst speak to him, saying them. Thou didst speak with Thy mouth and didst fulfill them with Thy hands, as on this day.

6:16Now, O Lord, the God of Israel, keep for Thy servant David my father that which Thou didst speak to him: ‘You shall never lack a man before Me sitting upon the throne of Israel, provided your sons guard their way, walking in My law as you walked before Me.’

6:17Now, Lord, Israel’s God, let Thine utterance that Thou didst speak to Thy servant David indeed be confirmed.

6:18But will God indeed dwell with men upon earth? If heaven and the heaven of heaven will not suffice for Thee, what is this house that I have built? †

6:19Yet look upon Thy servant’s prayer and my supplication, Lord God, to hear the supplication and prayer that Thy servant prays before Thee today.

6:20Let Thine eyes be open toward this house day and night, toward this place where Thou didst say Thy name would be invoked, to hear the prayer that Thy servant prays toward this place.

6:21Hear the supplication of Thy servant and Thy people Israel, whatever they pray toward this place. Hear in Thy dwelling place, from heaven; hear and be propitious.

When a Man Sins Against His Neighbor

6:22If a man sins against his neighbor and takes upon himself an oath-curse to bind himself by it, and comes and swears before the altar in this house,

6:23then hear from heaven, act, and judge Thy servants, repaying the lawless and returning his ways upon his own head, and justifying the righteous, giving him according to his righteousness.

When Israel Is Crushed Before the Enemy

6:24If Thy people Israel are crushed before the enemy, if they sin against Thee, and they return, confess Thy name, and pray and supplicate before Thee in this house,

6:25then hear from heaven and be propitious toward the sins of Thy people Israel. Return them to the land Thou didst give to them and their fathers.

When Heaven Is Shut and Famine Comes

6:26When heaven is shut and there is no rain because they will sin against Thee, and they pray toward this place, praise Thy name, and turn from their sins because Thou wilt humble them,

6:27then hear from heaven and be propitious toward the sins of Thy servants and Thy people Israel, for Thou wilt make known to them the good way in which they should walk. Give rain upon Thy land that Thou didst give Thy people as an inheritance.

6:28If there is famine upon the land, if there is death, damage from wind or yellowing, locust or consuming locust; if the enemy oppresses them before their cities; in every plague and every suffering,

6:29and every prayer and every supplication that comes to any person or to all Thy people Israel, if a man knows his own affliction and weakness and spreads out his hands toward this house,

6:30then hear from heaven, from Thy prepared dwelling place, and be propitious. Give to each man according to his ways, as Thou knowest his heart; for Thou alone knowest the heart of men,

6:31so that they may fear Thy ways all the days they live upon the land Thou didst give our fathers.

Concerning the Foreigner

6:32And every foreigner who is not of Thy people Israel, who comes from a distant land because of Thy great name, Thy mighty hand, and Thine uplifted arm—when they come and pray toward this place,

6:33then hear from heaven, from Thy prepared dwelling place, and do everything for which the foreigner invokes Thee, so that all earth’s peoples may know Thy name and fear Thee as Thy people Israel do, and know that Thy name has been invoked over this house that I have built.

When They Go Out to War

6:34If Thy people go out to war against their enemies by the way Thou sendest them, and pray to Thee toward this city Thou hast chosen and the house I have built for Thy name,

6:35then hear from heaven their supplication and prayer and uphold their right.

When They Are Carried into Captivity

6:36For they will sin against Thee—for there will be no human being who will not sin—and Thou wilt strike them and hand them over before enemies, and their captors will carry them captive to an enemy land, a land far away or near,

6:37and they turn their heart in their land where they were deported, and indeed return and supplicate Thee in their captivity, saying, ‘We have sinned, done wrong, and acted lawlessly,’

6:38and return to Thee with all their heart and all their soul in the land of those who took them captive, and pray toward their land that Thou didst give their fathers, the city Thou didst choose, and the house I built for Thy name,

6:39then hear from heaven, from Thy prepared dwelling place, their prayer and supplication, execute judgments, and be propitious toward the people who sinned against Thee.

Arise, Lord God, to Your Rest

6:40Now, O Lord, I beseech Thee, let Thine eyes be open, and Thine ears attentive to the supplication of this place.

6:41Now arise, O Lord God, to Thy rest, Thou and the ark of Thy strength. Let Thy priests, O Lord God, clothe themselves with salvation, and let Thy sons rejoice in good things.

6:42Lord God, do not turn away the face of Thine anointed one. Remember the mercies of David Thy servant.”

Fire from Heaven and the Glory of the Lord

7:1When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the whole burnt offerings and sacrifices, and the Lord’s glory filled the house.

7:2The priests could not enter the Lord’s house at that time, because the Lord’s glory had filled the house.

7:3All Israel’s sons saw the fire descending and the Lord’s glory upon the house. They fell face down to the earth upon the stone pavement, worshiped, and praised the Lord: “For it is good, for His mercy endures for ever.”

The Sacrifices and the Feast

7:4The king and all the people were sacrificing offerings before the Lord.

7:5Solomon offered the sacrifice: twenty-two thousand calves and a hundred and twenty thousand flock animals. The king and all the people dedicated God’s house.

7:6The priests stood at their watches, and the Levites had instruments for the Lord’s songs belonging to King David, to give thanks before the Lord, “For His mercy endures for ever,” with David’s hymns through their hands. The priests sounded trumpets on the trumpets opposite them, while all Israel stood.

7:7Solomon sanctified the middle of the court in the Lord’s house, for he offered the whole burnt offerings and the fat of the salvation offerings there, because the bronze altar Solomon had made was not sufficient to receive the whole burnt offerings, the manaa, and the fat portions. †

7:8Solomon kept the feast at that time for seven days, with all Israel, a very great assembly, from the entrance of Aimath to Egypt’s torrent.

7:9On the eighth day he held a closing celebration, for he had celebrated the altar’s dedication as a feast for seven days.

7:10On the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people to their dwellings, rejoicing and with a good heart over the good things the Lord had done for David, Solomon, and His people Israel.

The Lord Appears to Solomon a Second Time

7:11Solomon completed the Lord’s house and the king’s house. Everything Solomon desired in his soul to do in the Lord’s house and in his own house prospered.

Josiah King of Judah

34:1Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.

34:2He did what was upright before the Lord and walked in the ways of his father David, turning aside neither to the right nor to the left.

The Cleansing of Judah and Israel

34:3In the eighth year of his reign, while still a boy, he began to seek the Lord, the God of his father David. In the twelfth year of his reign he began to cleanse Judah and Jerusalem of the high places, sacred groves, and cast objects.

34:4He pulled down before him the altars of the Baals and the high places upon them. He cut down the sacred groves, shattered and pulverized the carved and cast objects, and scattered them over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

34:6And in the cities of Ephraim, Manasseh, Simeon, and Naphtali, and in their surrounding places,

The Repair of the House and the Book Found

34:8In the eighteenth year of his reign, to cleanse the land and the house, he sent Shaphan son of Eselias, Maasias the city ruler, and Joah son of Joahaz his recorder to strengthen the house of the Lord his God.

34:9They came to Hilkiah the high priest and handed over the silver brought into God’s house, which the Levites guarding the gate had gathered from Manasseh, Ephraim, the rulers, all the remnant in Israel, the sons of Judah and Benjamin, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

34:10Those appointed in the Lord’s house put it into the hands of the workers, and they gave it to the workers laboring in the Lord’s house, to repair and strengthen the house.

34:11They gave it to the craftsmen and builders to buy four-foot stones and timber for beams, to roof the buildings the kings of Judah had destroyed. †

34:28I am adding you to your fathers, and you will be added to your tombs in peace. Your eyes will not see all the evils I am bringing upon this place and its inhabitants.’” They brought word back to the king.

34:32He made everyone found in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand. The inhabitants of Jerusalem made a covenant in the house of the Lord, the God of their fathers.

34:33Josiah removed all the abominations from all the land belonging to the sons of Israel and made everyone present in Jerusalem and Israel serve the Lord their God throughout his days. He did not turn aside from following the Lord, the God of his fathers.

The Contributions for the People

35:7Josiah set apart for the sons of the people sheep, lambs, and kids from the offspring of goats, all for the Passover for everyone present, thirty thousand in number, and three thousand calves. These came from the king’s property.

35:8His rulers set apart contributions for the people, priests, and Levites. Hilkiah, Zacharias, and Jiiel, rulers of God’s house, gave to the priests; they gave for the Passover two thousand six hundred sheep, lambs, and kids, and three hundred calves.

35:9Chonenias, Banaias, Samaias, his brother Nathanael, and Asabias, Jiiel, and Jozabad, rulers of the Levites, set apart for the Levites five thousand sheep for the Passover and five hundred calves.

The Passover Sacrificed

35:10The ministry was set in order. The priests stood at their station and the Levites in their divisions, according to the king’s command.

35:11They sacrificed the Passover, and the priests poured the blood from their hands while the Levites skinned the animals.

35:13They roasted the Passover with fire according to the ruling, and boiled the holy things in bronze vessels and cauldrons. It went well, and they ran to all the sons of the people.

35:14After preparing for themselves and the priests—because the priests were offering the fat and burnt offerings until night—the Levites prepared for themselves and for their brethren, Aaron’s sons.

35:15The psalm-singers, Asaph’s sons, were at their station according to the commands of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, the king’s prophets, together with the rulers and the gatekeepers at each gate. 35:23The archers shot at King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, “Bring me out, for I am in great pain.”

Jehoiakim King of Judah

36:4Pharaoh Neco installed Eliakim son of Josiah as king of Judah in place of his father Josiah and changed his name to Jehoiakim. Pharaoh Neco took his brother Joahaz and brought him into Egypt, and he died there.

36:4aThey gave the silver and gold to Pharaoh. Then the land began to be taxed to provide the silver at Pharaoh’s command, and each, according to his capacity, exacted the silver and gold from the people of the land to give to Pharaoh Neco.

36:5Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Zechora, daughter of Nerias from Rama. He did what was evil before the Lord, according to everything his fathers had done.

Nebuchadnezzar Comes into the Land

36:5aIn his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came into the land, and he served him for three years and then revolted against him.

36:5bThe Lord sent against them the Chaldeans, bands of Syrian raiders, bands of Moabite raiders, and the sons of Ammon and of Samaria. They withdrew after this event, according to the Lord’s word through His servants the prophets.

36:5cNevertheless the Lord’s anger was against Judah, to remove him from His presence because of Manasseh’s sins in everything he had done,

36:5dand because of the innocent blood that Jehoiakim shed, filling Jerusalem with innocent blood; yet the Lord did not wish to destroy them.

Jehoiakim Bound and the Vessels Carried Off

36:6Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against him, bound him with bronze fetters, and took him away to Babylon.

36:7He carried away part of the vessels of the Lord’s house to Babylon and put them in his temple in Babylon.

36:8The rest of Jehoiakim’s deeds and everything he did—are these not written in the book of the accounts of the days for the kings of Judah? Jehoiakim lay down with his fathers and was buried in Ganoza with his fathers, and his son Jechonias reigned in his place.

Jechonias King of Judah

36:9Jechonias was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem. He did what was evil before the Lord.

36:10At the turn of the year King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him into Babylon with the desirable vessels of the Lord’s house. He made Zedekiah, his father’s brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem.

Zedekiah King of Judah

36:11Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.

36:12He did what was evil before the Lord his God. He felt no shame before Jeremiah the prophet, and at what came from the Lord’s mouth,

36:13in breaking his obligations to King Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God. He hardened his neck and strengthened his heart so as not to return to the Lord God of Israel.

36:14All the distinguished men of Judah, the priests, and the people of the land multiplied breaches of faith in the nations’ abominations and defiled the Lord’s house in Jerusalem.

The Messengers Mocked

36:15The Lord, the God of their fathers, sent through prophets, rising early and sending His messengers, because He spared His people and His sanctuary.

36:16They kept sneering at His messengers, treating His words as nothing, and mocking His prophets, until the Lord’s anger rose among His people, until there was no healing.

The Burning of the House and the Deportation

36:17He brought against them a king of the Chaldeans, who killed their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary and did not spare Zedekiah. They had no mercy on their virgins and carried away their elders. He handed everything into their hands.

36:18All the vessels of God’s house, great and small, the treasures, and all the treasures of the king and the magnates—he brought everything into Babylon.

36:19He burned the Lord’s house, demolished Jerusalem’s wall, and burned its citadels with fire, consigning every beautiful article to destruction.

36:20He deported the survivors to Babylon, and they became slaves to him and his sons until the kingdom of the Medes,

36:21to fulfill the Lord’s word through Jeremiah’s mouth, until the land accepted its Sabbaths, to keep Sabbath. All the days of its desolation it kept Sabbath, to complete seventy years.

The Proclamation of Cyrus

36:22In the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, after the Lord’s word through Jeremiah’s mouth had been fulfilled, the Lord stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of the Persians. He ordered a proclamation throughout his kingdom in writing, saying,
