# 2 Chronicles — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 36 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 833 verse units received an English editorial review: 183 changed and 650 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. Fresh selected-verse checks succeeded in 35 of 36 chapters; chapter 5 could not be retrieved in this pass and received English-only editing. Fresh access to chapters 32 and 36 succeeded despite the historical retrieval limitations. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/2CH.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.
