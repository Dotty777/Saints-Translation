Malachi 1

Read this chapter on staging

I Have Loved You, Says the Lord

1:1A pronouncement of the word of the Lord concerning Israel through His messenger. Take it to your hearts!

1:2“I have loved you,” says the Lord. But you said, “How hast Thou loved us?” “Was not Esau Jacob’s brother?” says the Lord. “Yet I loved Jacob,

1:3but Esau I hated. I appointed his territories for desolation and his inheritance as gifts of a wilderness.”

1:4Because Idumea will say, “It has been overthrown; let us return and rebuild the deserted places,” thus says the Lord Almighty: “They will build, but I will overthrow. They will be called ‘Territories of Lawlessness’ and ‘A People Against Whom the Lord Has Drawn Up His Forces Forever.’”

1:5Your eyes will see, and you yourselves will say, “The Lord has been magnified beyond Israel’s territories!”

Where Is My Honor?

1:6“A son honors his father, and a slave his master. If I am a father, where is My honor? If I am a master, where is the fear of Me?” says the Lord Almighty. You priests who belittle My name! But you said, “How have we belittled Thy name?”

1:7“By bringing polluted loaves to My altar.” But you said, “How have we polluted them?” “By your saying, ‘The Lord’s table is despised, and the foods placed upon it are despised.’”

1:8“For if you bring a blind animal for sacrifice, is that not evil? If you bring one that is lame or sick, is that not evil? Bring it, then, to your ruler! Will he accept it? Will he show you favor?” says the Lord Almighty.

1:9“And now appease the face of your God and plead with Him. These things have happened in your hands. Will I show favor to any of you?” says the Lord Almighty.

1:10“For even among you doors will be shut, and you will not kindle My altar for nothing. I have no desire for you,” says the Lord Almighty, “and I will not accept a sacrifice from your hands.”

From the Sun's Rising to Its Setting

1:11“For from the sun’s rising to its setting, My name has been glorified among the nations, and in every place incense is brought to My name, together with a pure sacrifice, because My name is great among the nations,” says the Lord Almighty.

1:12“But you profane it by your saying, ‘The Lord’s table is polluted, and the foods placed upon it are despised.’

1:13“And you said, ‘These things are from hardship,’ and I blew them away,” says the Lord Almighty. “You were bringing things seized by force, the lame and the afflicted. If you bring the sacrifice, shall I accept these things from your hands?” says the Lord Almighty.

1:14“Cursed is one who was able, and had a male in his flock and a vow concerning it, yet sacrifices a damaged animal to the Lord. For I am a great King,” says the Lord Almighty, “and My name is manifest among the nations.”

Current Bespoke editorial notes

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Ordinary address uses you/your and third-person verbs use modern forms; Thou/Thy with matching verbs remain where people address God directly. Attributive My altar replaces Mine altar. Desolation and favor to any of you improve spoken English while retaining the stated ruin and favor. His messenger and take it to your hearts, Jacob/Esau contrasts, gifts of a wilderness, Idumea, repeated honor/fear and despised food, worldwide incense and pure sacrifice, perfect has been glorified, and first-person I blew them away remain.

Malachi 2

Read this chapter on staging

This Command Is for You, Priests

2:1“And now this command is for you, priests.

2:2If you do not listen and do not take it into your hearts to give glory to My name,” says the Lord Almighty, “I will send out the curse upon you, and I will curse your blessing, and I will curse it. I will scatter your blessing, and it will not be among you, because you do not take it into your hearts.

2:3Look, I am setting apart the shoulder for you, and I will scatter the stomach on your faces, the stomach of your festivals, and I will take you to the same place.

2:4You will recognize that I have sent out this command to you, so that My covenant might be with the Levites,” says the Lord Almighty.

The Covenant of Life and Peace

2:5“My covenant with him was of life and peace, and I gave it to him in fear, to fear Me and to shrink back before My name.

2:6A law of truth was in his mouth, and injustice was not found on his lips. Directing his way in peace, he walked with Me and turned many back from injustice.

2:7For a priest’s lips will guard knowledge, and people will seek law from his mouth, because he is the messenger of the Lord Almighty.

2:8But you have turned aside from the way and weakened many through the law; you have corrupted the covenant of Levi,” says the Lord Almighty.

2:9“And I have made you despised and neglected among all the nations, because you did not guard My ways but showed partiality in the law.”

Judah Has Broken Faith

2:10Did not one God create you? Is there not one Father of you all? Why have you each abandoned your brother, so as to profane the covenant of your fathers?

2:11Judah has been abandoned, and an abomination has occurred in Israel and Jerusalem, because Judah profaned the Lord’s holy things, in which he delighted, and devoted himself to foreign gods.

2:12The Lord will destroy the person who does these things, until he is also humbled away from Jacob’s dwellings and from those who bring a sacrifice to the Lord Almighty.

The Wife of Your Youth

2:13And you were doing these things that I hated: you covered the Lord’s altar with tears, with weeping and groaning from hardships. Is it still fitting to look toward a sacrifice or accept it from your hands?

2:14And you said, “For what reason?” Because the Lord bore witness between you and the wife of your youth, whom you abandoned, though she is your partner and the wife of your covenant.

2:15And no other did it, and there was a remnant of his spirit. And you said, “What else does God seek but offspring?” So guard your spirit, and do not abandon the wife of your youth.

2:16But if, after hating her, you send her away, says the Lord, the God of Israel, impiety will cover your thoughts, says the Lord Almighty. So guard your spirit, and you must not abandon.

2:17You who provoke God with your words! And you said, “How have we provoked Him?” By your saying, “Everyone who does evil is good before the Lord, and He has delighted in them,” and, “Where is the God of righteousness?”

Malachi 3

Read this chapter on staging

I Am Sending Out My Messenger

3:1“Look, I am sending out My messenger, and he will look to a way before My face. Suddenly the Lord whom you seek will come to His own sanctuary, and the messenger of the covenant whom you desire. Look, he is coming,” says the Lord Almighty.

3:2And who will endure the day of his entrance? Or who will stand at his appearance? For he enters like a smelting fire and like launderers’ herb.

3:3He will sit, smelting and purifying as with silver and gold. He will cleanse the sons of Levi and melt them like gold and silver, and they will be those who bring a sacrifice to the Lord in righteousness.

3:4And the sacrifice of Judah and Jerusalem will please the Lord, as in the days of old and as in the former years.

I Will Draw Near to You in Judgment

3:5“I will draw near to you in judgment, and I will be a swift witness against sorceresses, adulteresses and those who swear falsely by My name, against those who withhold a hired worker’s wages, those who oppress a widow, those who strike orphans with their fists, those who turn aside the judgment of a sojourner, and those who do not fear Me,” says the Lord Almighty.

3:6“For I am the Lord your God, and I have not changed. But you, sons of Jacob, do not refrain

3:7from the injustices of your fathers. You have turned aside from My ordinances and have not guarded them. Return to Me, and I will return to you,” says the Lord Almighty. And you said, “How shall we return?”

Will a Human Defraud God?

3:8“Will a human defraud God? Yet you are defrauding Me.” And you will say, “How have we defrauded Thee?” “Because the tithes and the firstfruits are with you.

3:9And you keep looking away, yet you are defrauding Me. The nation has been brought to an end.

3:10And you brought all the produce dues into the storehouses, and his plunder will be in his house. Examine, then, by this,” says the Lord Almighty, “whether I will not open heaven’s floodgates for you and pour out My blessing for you until there is enough.

3:11And I will give you instructions for food, and I will certainly not destroy the fruit of your land, and your vine in the field will certainly not grow weak,” says the Lord Almighty.

3:12“All the nations will call you blessed, because you will be a desirable land,” says the Lord Almighty.

Your Words Have Been Heavy Against Me

3:13“You have made your words heavy against Me,” says the Lord. And you said, “How have we spoken against Thee?”

3:14You said, “The one who serves God is futile. What advantage is there because we guarded His observances and because we walked as supplicants before the Lord Almighty?

3:15And now we call outsiders blessed. All who practice lawlessness are being built up; they opposed God and were saved.”

3:16Those who feared the Lord spoke these things, each to his neighbor. The Lord paid attention and listened, and He wrote a book of remembrance before Him for those who feared the Lord and revered His name.

3:17“They will be Mine,” says the Lord Almighty, “for a day that I am making for an acquisition, and I will choose them as a man chooses his son who serves him.

3:18And you will return and see the distinction between the righteous and the lawless, and between one who serves God and one who does not serve.

The Day Is Coming, Burning Like an Oven

3:19For look, the day of the Lord is coming, burning like an oven, and it will burn them. All the foreigners and all who practice lawlessness will be straw, and the coming day will ignite them,” says the Lord Almighty, “and neither root nor branch will remain from them.

3:20But for you who fear My name, a sun of righteousness will rise, and healing in its wings; you will go out and leap like little calves released from bonds.

3:21You will trample the lawless, because they will be ashes beneath your feet on the day that I am making,” says the Lord Almighty.

I Am Sending You Elijah the Tishbite

3:22“And look, I am sending you Elijah the Tishbite before the coming of the Lord’s great and manifest day,

3:23who will restore a father’s heart toward a son and a person’s heart toward his neighbor, lest I come and strike the land utterly.”

3:24“All of you, remember the law of Moses My servant, as I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel, ordinances and judgments.”

Current Bespoke editorial notes
