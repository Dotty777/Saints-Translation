## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Ordinary address uses you/your and third-person verbs use modern forms; Thou/Thy with matching verbs remain where people address God directly. Attributive My altar replaces Mine altar. Desolation and favor to any of you improve spoken English while retaining the stated ruin and favor. His messenger and take it to your hearts, Jacob/Esau contrasts, gifts of a wilderness, Idumea, repeated honor/fear and despised food, worldwide incense and pure sacrifice, perfect has been glorified, and first-person I blew them away remain.

Pending: Review gifts of a wilderness, the envoy designation, face/favor idioms and the context of the worldwide offering. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary address and reporting verbs are modernized. Has sent out preserves the completed commission at 2:4; the questions and accusation at 2:10 and 2:13 are reordered for speech. The shoulder/stomach images, repeated curse, life and peace, lips/knowledge/law/mouth, Levites/Levi, lowercase he delighted, and difficult remnant-of-spirit wording remain. At 2:16 the second-person condition remains a condition, with her supplied from the wife context, followed by the plural injunction against abandonment.

Pending: Review shoulder/stomach terminology, he delighted, the syntax and referents of 2:15, and the condition and plural injunction at 2:16. No broader ruling on divorce is inserted into Scripture. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Ordinary address and verbs are modernized, while Thee remains in direct questions to God at 3:8 and 3:13 and absolute Mine remains at 3:17. All of you, remember makes the final plural summons explicit in natural English. The messenger’s looking verb, His own sanctuary, smelting and herb, gendered accusations, fists against orphans, perfect I have not changed and the continuous 3:6–7 clause, tithes/firstfruits, past brought, his plunder/his house, food instructions, book written by the Lord, acquisition and choosing, foreigners, sun/healing/wings/calves, Elijah the Tishbite, father/son and person/neighbor, and Moses last at 3:24 remain.

Pending: Review the looking verb and messenger identities, launderers’ herb, his plunder/his house, food instructions, acquisition, foreigners and the closing order. Later reception remains separate from the selected source wording. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
