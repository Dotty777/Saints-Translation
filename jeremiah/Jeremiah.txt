JEREMIAH 1

1:1 The word of God that came upon Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests, who lived in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,
1:2 which became God’s word to him in the days of Josiah son of Amos, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.
1:3 It also came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, until Jerusalem’s captivity in the fifth month.
1:4 The Lord’s word came to me, saying,
1:5 Before I formed you in the belly, I know you; before you came out of the womb, I have consecrated you. I have appointed you a prophet to nations.
1:6 I said, O Master Lord! Behold, I do not know how to speak, because I am young.
1:7 The Lord said to me, Do not say, I am young, because you will go to everyone to whom I send you, and you will speak according to everything I command you.
1:8 Do not fear them, because I am with you to rescue you, says the Lord.
1:9 The Lord stretched out His hand toward me and touched my mouth. The Lord said to me, Behold, I have put My words into your mouth.
1:10 Behold, today I have appointed you over nations and kingdoms, to uproot, demolish, destroy, build and plant.
1:11 The Lord’s word came to me, saying, What do you see, Jeremiah? I said, A nut-wood staff.
1:12 The Lord said to me, You have seen well, because I am watchful over My words to perform them.
1:13 The Lord’s word came to me a second time, saying, What do you see? I said, A cauldron heated from beneath, its face away from the north.
1:14 The Lord said to me, From the north calamities will be kindled upon all who inhabit the land.
1:15 Because behold, I am summoning all the kingdoms from the north of the land, says the Lord. They will come and each set his throne at the forecourts of Jerusalem’s gates, against all her surrounding walls and against all Judah’s cities.
1:16 I will speak to them with judgment concerning all their evil, because they abandoned Me, sacrificed to foreign gods and bowed down to the works of their hands.
1:17 But you, gird your waist, rise and tell them everything I command you. Do not fear them or be terrified before them, because I am with you to rescue you, says the Lord.
1:18 Behold, on this day I have made you like a fortified city and like a strong bronze wall against all Judah’s kings, its rulers and the people of the land,
1:19 and they will fight you, but they will certainly not prevail against you, because I am with you to rescue you, said the Lord.

JEREMIAH 2

2:2 And He said, Thus says the Lord: I remembered the mercy of your youth and the love of your completion, when you followed the Holy One of Israel, says the Lord.
2:3 Israel is holy. To the Lord he is the first of His produce. All who devour him will offend; calamities will come upon them, declares the Lord.
2:4 Hear the Lord’s word, house of Jacob and every clan of the house of Israel.
2:5 Thus says the Lord: What offense did your fathers find in Me, that they withdrew far from Me, followed futile things and became futile?
2:6 They did not say, Where is the Lord who brought us up from Egypt’s land, who guided us in the wilderness, in a boundless and impassable land, a waterless and fruitless land, a land through which nothing passed and where no human being lived?
2:7 I brought you into Carmel to eat its fruits and good things, but you entered and polluted My land and made My inheritance an abomination.
2:8 The priests did not say, Where is the Lord? Those holding to the law did not know Me; the shepherds acted ungodly toward Me; the prophets prophesied by Baal and followed what is useless.
2:9 Therefore I will still enter judgment with you, says the Lord, and with your children’s children I will enter judgment.
2:10 Cross over to the islands of Kittim and see; send to Kedar and consider carefully. See whether such things have happened.
2:11 Will nations exchange their gods, though these are not gods? But My people exchanged their glory, from which they will not benefit.
2:12 Heaven was astonished at this and shuddered exceedingly, says the Lord.
2:13 For My people did two evil things: they abandoned Me, a spring of water of life, and dug broken cisterns for themselves that will not be able to hold water.
2:14 Is Israel a slave, or is he house-born? Why has he become spoil?
2:15 Lions roared over him and raised their voice. They made his land a wilderness, and his cities were demolished so that none inhabited them.
2:16 The sons of Memphis and Tahpanhes knew you and mocked you.
2:17 Has abandoning Me not brought these things upon you? says the Lord your God.
2:18 Now what do you have to do with Egypt’s road, to drink Gihon’s water? And what do you have to do with the Assyrians’ road, to drink river waters?
2:19 Your apostasy will discipline you, and your evil will rebuke you. Know and see that abandoning Me is bitter for you, says the Lord your God; and I took no pleasure in you, says the Lord your God.
2:20 For long ago you shattered your yoke and tore apart your bonds, and said, I will not be enslaved, but I will go upon every high hill and beneath every shady tree; there I will abandon myself to my prostitution.
2:21 But I planted you as a fruitful vine, wholly genuine. How have you changed into bitterness, you foreign vine?
2:22 Even if you wash with natron and multiply cleansing herb for yourself, you remain stained in your injustices before Me, says the Lord.
2:23 How will you say, I was not polluted and did not follow Baal? See your ways in the burial ground and know what you did. Late her voice wailed; her ways
2:24 she broadened upon wilderness waters. In her soul’s desires she was carried by the wind; she was handed over. Who will turn her back? All who seek her will not toil; in her humiliation they will find her.
2:25 Turn your foot from a rough road and your throat from thirst. But she said, I will be strong, because she had loved strangers and went after them.
2:26 As a thief is ashamed when caught, so the sons of Israel will be ashamed: they, their kings, their rulers, their priests and their prophets.
2:27 They said to wood, You are my father, and to stone, You gave birth to me. They turned their backs toward Me, not their faces; and in the time of their calamities they will say, Rise and save us!
2:28 Where are your gods that you made for yourself? Will they rise and save you in the time of your affliction? For your gods were as numerous as your cities, Judah, and according to the number of Jerusalem’s streets they sacrificed to Baal.
2:29 Why do you speak to Me? All of you acted ungodly, and all of you acted lawlessly against Me, says the Lord.
2:30 In vain I struck your children; you accepted no discipline. A sword devoured your prophets like a destroying lion, and you did not fear.
2:31 Hear the Lord’s word. Thus says the Lord: Have I become a wilderness to Israel, or a land left barren? Why did My people say, We will not be ruled, and we will come to Thee no more?
2:32 Will a bride forget her adornment, or a virgin her breastband? Yet My people forgot Me for days beyond counting.
2:33 What good practice will you still pursue in your ways to seek love? Not so; you too acted wickedly to pollute your ways.
2:34 Even on your hands was found the blood of innocent lives. I did not find them in break-ins, but at every oak.
2:35 And you said, I am innocent; let His fury turn away from me. Behold, I enter judgment with you because you say, I did not sin.
2:36 Why did you show such contempt by repeating your ways? You will also be put to shame by Egypt, as you were put to shame by Assyria.
2:37 For from here too you will go out with your hands on your head, because the Lord rejected your hope, and you will not prosper in it.

JEREMIAH 3

3:1 If a man sends his wife away, and she leaves him and becomes another man’s, surely she will not return to him again? Would not that woman be utterly polluted? And you prostituted yourself with many shepherds—and were you returning to Me? says the Lord.
3:2 Lift your eyes straight ahead and see: where have you not been defiled? You sat for them on the roads like a solitary crow, and polluted the land with your prostitution and your evils.
3:3 You had many shepherds as a stumbling block for yourself. You had a prostitute’s appearance; you were shameless toward everyone.
3:4 Did you not call Me a household, a father and the leader of your virginity?
3:5 Will He remain for ever, or will it be guarded always? Behold, you spoke and did these evil things and were able.
3:6 The Lord said to me in the days of King Josiah, You saw what the dwelling of Israel did to Me. They went upon every high mountain and beneath every wooded tree and prostituted themselves there.
3:7 After she prostituted herself in all these ways, I said, Return to Me. But she did not return, and faithless Judah saw her faithlessness.
3:8 I saw that, concerning all the things in which the dwelling of Israel was caught committing adultery, I sent her away and gave a certificate of divorce into her hands. Yet faithless Judah did not fear; she too went and prostituted herself.
3:9 Her prostitution came to be regarded as nothing, and she committed adultery with wood and stone.
3:10 In all these things faithless Judah did not return to Me with her whole heart, but in falsehood.
3:11 The Lord said to me, Israel has justified himself in comparison with faithless Judah.
3:12 Go and read these words toward the north: Return to Me, dwelling of Israel, says the Lord. I will not set My face firmly against you, because I am merciful, says the Lord, and I will not be angry with you for ever.
3:13 Only recognize your injustice: you acted ungodly toward the Lord your God and spread your ways to strangers beneath every wooded tree, but you did not obey My voice, says the Lord.
3:14 Return, children who have withdrawn, says the Lord, because I will rule over you. I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and bring you into Zion,
3:15 and I will give you shepherds according to My heart, and they will shepherd you, shepherding with knowledge.
3:16 When you multiply and increase upon the land in those days, says the Lord, they will no longer say, The ark of the covenant of the Holy One of Israel. It will not come to mind, be named or be attended to, and it will not be made again.
3:17 In those days and at that time they will call Jerusalem the Lord’s throne, and all the nations will gather to her. They will no longer follow the thoughts of their evil heart.
3:18 In those days the house of Judah will join the house of Israel, and together they will come from the northern land and from all the regions to the land I gave their fathers as an inheritance.
3:19 And I said, May it be, Lord! For I will place you among children and give you a chosen land, an inheritance of God Almighty of nations. I said, You will call Me Father and will not turn away from Me.
3:20 But as a woman deals faithlessly with the one living with her, so the house of Israel dealt faithlessly with Me, says the Lord.
3:21 A voice was heard from lips, the weeping and supplication of Israel’s children, because they acted unjustly in their ways and forgot their holy God.
3:22 Return, returning children, and I will heal your fractures. Behold, we will be Thy servants, because Thou art the Lord our God.
3:23 Truly the hills and the power of the mountains were false; but through the Lord our God is Israel’s salvation.
3:24 Shame consumed our fathers’ labors from our youth: their sheep and calves, their sons and daughters.
3:25 We lay down in our shame, and our dishonor covered us, because we sinned before our God, we and our fathers, from our youth until this day, and did not obey the voice of the Lord our God.

JEREMIAH 4

4:1 If Israel returns, says the Lord, he will return to Me; if he removes his abominations from his mouth and shows reverence before Me,
4:2 and swears, ‘The Lord lives,’ with truth and in judgment and righteousness, then nations will bless in her, and in Him they will praise God in Jerusalem.
4:3 For thus says the Lord to the men of Judah and those dwelling in Jerusalem: Break up fallow ground for yourselves, and do not sow among thorns.
4:4 Be circumcised to your God, and circumcise your hardness of heart, men of Judah and those dwelling in Jerusalem, lest My fury come out like fire and burn, and there be no one to extinguish it because of the evil of your practices.
4:5 Announce in Judah, and let it be heard in Jerusalem; say: Signal with a trumpet over the land, and cry loudly! Say: Gather, and let us enter the walled cities!
4:6 Take up and flee to Zion; hurry, do not stand still, for I am bringing evils from the north and great crushing.
4:7 A lion has gone up from his enclosure; destroying nations, he has set out and left his place to make the land a desolation, and cities will be demolished so that they are not inhabited.
4:8 For these things, gird yourselves with sackcloth, beat yourselves in mourning and wail, because the Lord’s fury has not turned away from you.
4:9 And it will be on that day, says the Lord: the king’s heart and the rulers’ heart will perish, and the priests will be beside themselves and the prophets astonished.
4:10 And I said: O Master, Lord, surely Thou hast indeed deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, ‘You will have peace,’ and behold, the sword has reached to their soul!
4:11 At that time they will say to this people and Jerusalem: A spirit of wandering in the wilderness; the daughter of My people’s way is not toward purity or holiness.
4:12 A spirit of filling will come to Me; but now I speak judgments against them.
4:13 Behold, he will come up like a cloud, and his chariots like a storm; his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, because we suffer misery!
4:14 Wash your heart clean of evil, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will the schemes of your troubles exist within you?
4:15 For the voice of one announcing will come from Dan, and trouble will be heard from Mount Ephraim.
4:16 Remind the nations: Behold, they have come! Announce in Jerusalem: Bands come from a distant land and have raised their voice against the cities of Judah.
4:17 Like those guarding a field they came against her all around, because you neglected Me, says the Lord.
4:18 Your ways and your practices have done these things to you. This is your evil: it is bitter; it has reached to your heart.
4:19 My belly, my belly, I am in pain, and the senses of my heart! My soul rages, my heart is torn; I will not be silent, because my soul heard the sound of a trumpet, a cry of war.
4:20 It calls out misery and crushing, because the whole land has suffered misery; suddenly the tent has suffered misery, and my tent coverings have been torn apart.
4:21 How long will I see people fleeing while hearing the sound of trumpets?
4:22 For the leaders of My people did not know Me; they are foolish sons and not discerning. They are wise at doing evil, but did not learn to do well.
4:23 I looked upon the earth, and behold, nothing; and into heaven, and its lights were not there.
4:24 I saw the mountains, and they were trembling, and all the hills were being shaken.
4:25 I looked, and behold, there was no human, and all the birds of heaven were terrified.
4:26 I saw, and behold, Carmel was a wilderness, and all the cities were burned with fire before the Lord, and they vanished before His furious anger.
4:27 Thus says the Lord: The whole land will be a wilderness, but I will certainly not make a complete end.
4:28 For these things let the earth mourn and let heaven above grow dark, because I have spoken and will not change My mind; I have set Myself in motion and will not turn back from it.
4:29 At the sound of a horseman and a drawn bow the whole countryside withdrew; they entered caves and hid in groves and climbed onto the rocks. Every city was abandoned; no human dwells in them.
4:30 And you, what will you do if you put on scarlet and adorn yourself with golden ornaments, and if you paint your eyes with antimony? Your adornment is in vain; your lovers have rejected you; they seek your soul.
4:31 For I heard a voice like a woman in labor, your groaning like one giving birth for the first time, the voice of the daughter of Zion. She will grow faint and let her hands fall: Woe to me, because my soul fails over those killed!

JEREMIAH 5

5:1 Run through Jerusalem’s streets and see and learn, and search her squares: if you find a man, if there is one doing justice and seeking faithfulness, then I will be merciful to them, says the Lord.
5:2 They say, ‘The Lord lives’; do they not therefore swear on falsehoods?
5:3 Lord, Thine eyes are toward faithfulness. Thou didst scourge them, and they did not suffer; Thou broughtest them to an end, and they would not accept instruction. They hardened their faces beyond rock and would not return.
5:4 And I said: Perhaps they are poor, because they had no ability, because they did not know the Lord’s way and God’s judgment.
5:5 I will go to the prosperous and speak to them, for they recognized the Lord’s way and God’s judgment. And behold, with one accord they broke the yoke and tore the bonds.
5:6 Therefore a lion from the woodland struck them, and a wolf destroyed them as far as the houses, and a leopard kept watch over their cities. All who leave them will be hunted, because they multiplied their impieties and grew strong in their turnings away.
5:7 For which of these things should I be merciful to you? Your sons abandoned Me and swore by those who are not gods. And I fed them fully, and they committed adultery and lodged in prostitutes’ houses.
5:8 They became horses mad for mares; each neighed after his neighbor’s wife.
5:9 Shall I not visit for these things? says the Lord. Or will not My soul take vengeance on such a nation?
5:10 Go up against her battlements and tear them down, but do not make a complete end. Leave her supports, because they belong to the Lord.
5:11 For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have utterly rejected Me, says the Lord.
5:12 They lied to their own Lord and said: These things are not so; evils will not come upon us, and we will not see sword or famine.
5:13 Our prophets became wind, and the Lord’s word was not in them. Thus it will be for them.
5:14 Therefore thus says the Lord Almighty: Because you spoke this utterance, behold, I have made My words fire in your mouth, and this people wood, and it will devour them.
5:15 Behold, I am bringing a nation upon you from far away, house of Israel, says the Lord, a nation whose language’s sound you will not understand.
5:16 All are strong,
5:17 and they will devour your harvest and your bread, and devour your sons and daughters, and devour your sheep and calves, and devour your vineyards and fig orchards and olive groves; and with a sword they will thresh your fortified cities, in which you have trusted.
5:18 And it will be in those days, says the Lord your God: I will certainly not bring you to a complete end.
5:19 And when you say, ‘For what reason has the Lord our God done all these things to us?’ you will say to them: Because you served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve foreigners in a land not yours.
5:20 Announce these things to the house of Jacob, and let it be heard in Judah.
5:21 Hear these things now, foolish and senseless people: they have eyes and do not see; they have ears and do not hear.
5:22 Will you not fear Me? says the Lord. Or will you not show reverence before Me, who appointed sand as the sea’s boundary, an eternal command it will not cross? It will be stirred and will not prevail, and its waves will roar, and it will not cross it.
5:23 But this people acquired an unheeding and disobedient heart, and they turned aside and went away.
5:24 And they did not say in their heart: Let us fear the Lord our God, who gives us early and late rain at the time of fulfillment of the harvest’s command, and He kept it for us.
5:25 Your lawless acts turned these things aside, and your sins removed good things from you.
5:26 For impious people were found among My people, and they set traps to destroy men and were catching them.
5:27 Like a set trap full of birds, so their houses are full of deceit; therefore they became great and rich.
5:28 And they transgressed judgment; they did not judge an orphan’s case, and they were not judging a widow’s case.
5:29 Shall I not visit for these things? says the Lord. Or will not My soul take vengeance on such a nation?
5:30 Astonishment and horrifying things happened upon the land:
5:31 The prophets prophesy unjust things, and the priests applauded with their hands, and My people loved it so. And what will you do after these things?

JEREMIAH 6

6:1 Take courage, sons of Benjamin, from within Jerusalem, and signal with a trumpet in Tekoa, and raise a sign above Beth-haccherem, because evils have looked out from the north, and great crushing is taking place,
6:2 and your height will be taken away, daughter of Zion.
6:3 Shepherds and their flocks will come to her, and they will pitch tents against her all around, and each will shepherd with his own hand.
6:4 Prepare yourselves for war against her! Rise, and let us go up against her at noon! Woe to us, because the day has declined, because evening’s shadows are failing!
6:5 Rise, and let us go up in the night and destroy her foundations!
6:6 For thus says the Lord: Cut down her trees; pour out a force upon Jerusalem. O false city, all oppression is within her!
6:7 As a cistern cools water, so her evil cools; impiety and misery will be heard in her, continually before her. With pain and scourging
6:8 you will be instructed, Jerusalem, lest My soul depart from you, lest I make you an impassable land that will not be inhabited.
6:9 For thus says the Lord: Glean, glean Israel’s remnants like a vine; turn back like the grape-gatherer to his basket.
6:10 To whom shall I speak and testify, and he will hear? Behold, their ears are uncircumcised, and they cannot hear. Behold, the Lord’s utterance became a reproach to them; they certainly will not wish to hear it.
6:11 And I filled up My fury and held back and did not bring them to an end. I will pour it upon little children outside and upon a gathering of young men together, because man and woman will be seized, the elder with one full of days.
6:12 And their houses will be transferred to others, fields and their wives together, because I will stretch out My hand against those dwelling in this land, says the Lord.
6:13 For from their small to their great, all have carried out lawless deeds; from priest to false prophet, all have done false things.
6:14 And they treated My people’s fracture with contempt, saying, ‘Peace, peace’; and where is peace?
6:15 They were put to shame because they failed, and even so they were not at all ashamed and did not recognize their dishonor. Therefore they will fall in their fall, and at the time of their visitation they will perish, said the Lord.
6:16 Thus says the Lord: Stand upon the roads and see, and ask for the Lord’s eternal paths and see which is the good way, and walk in it, and you will find purification for your souls. And they said, ‘We will not go.’
6:17 I have appointed watchers over you: Hear the trumpet’s sound! And they said, ‘We will not hear.’
6:18 Therefore the nations heard, and those shepherding their flocks.
6:19 Hear, earth! Behold, I am bringing evils upon this people, the fruit of their turning away, because they did not attend to My words and rejected My law.
6:20 Why do you bring Me frankincense from Sheba and cinnamon from a distant land? Your whole burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices did not please Me.
6:21 Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am placing weakness upon this people, and fathers and sons together will become weak in it; neighbor and his neighbor will perish.
6:22 Thus says the Lord: Behold, a people comes from the north, and nations will be roused from the earth’s extremity.
6:23 They will grasp bow and spear; it is ruthless and will show no mercy. Its voice is like a surging sea; on horses and chariots it will draw up like fire for war against you, daughter of Zion.
6:24 We heard the report of them; our hands were paralyzed. Affliction seized us, labor pains like a woman giving birth.
6:25 Do not go out into a field or walk on the roads, because the enemies’ sword dwells all around.
6:26 Daughter of My people, gird yourself with sackcloth; sprinkle yourself with ashes. Make mourning for yourself for a beloved one, a pitiful lament, because misery will suddenly come upon you.
6:27 I have made you a tester among tested peoples, and you will know Me when I test their way.
6:28 All are unheeding, walking crookedly, bronze and iron; all are corrupted.
6:29 The bellows failed from the fire; the lead failed. In vain the silversmith works silver; their wickedness was not melted away.
6:30 Call them rejected silver, because the Lord rejected them.

JEREMIAH 7

7:2 Hear the Lord’s word, all Judea!
7:3 Thus says the Lord, Israel’s God: Correct your ways and your practices, and I will settle you in this place.
7:4 Do not put your confidence in yourselves on false words, because they will not benefit you at all when they say, ‘The Lord’s temple, the Lord’s temple it is.’
7:5 For if you truly correct your ways and practices and truly execute judgment between a man and his neighbor,
7:6 and do not oppress a newcomer, orphan, or widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not follow foreign gods to your own harm,
7:7 then I will settle you in this place, in the land I gave your fathers, from age to age.
7:8 But if you have trusted false words from which you will receive no benefit,
7:9 and murder and commit adultery and steal and swear unjustly, and you burned incense to Baal and followed foreign gods whom you do not know, to your own harm,
7:10 and you came and stood before Me in the house over which My name has been called and said, ‘We have kept away, so as not to do all these abominations,’
7:11 is My house, over which My name has been called there, a robbers’ cave before you? And behold, I have seen, says the Lord.
7:12 For go to My place in Shiloh, where I formerly made My name dwell, and see what I did to it because of the evil of My people Israel.
7:13 And now, because you did all these works, and I spoke to you and you did not hear Me, and I called you and you did not answer,
7:14 I will also do to this house over which My name has been called, in which you have trusted, and to the place I gave you and your fathers, as I did to Shiloh.
7:15 And I will throw you away from My presence, as I threw away your brothers, all Ephraim’s offspring.
7:16 And you, do not pray for this people, and do not ask that they receive mercy, and do not pray or approach Me on their behalf, because I will not listen.
7:17 Do you not see what they are doing in Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets?
7:18 Their sons gather wood, and their fathers burn a fire, and their women work dough to make cakes for heaven’s host; and they poured drink offerings to foreign gods to provoke Me to anger.
7:19 Is it Me they provoke to anger? says the Lord. Is it not themselves, so that their faces are put to shame?
7:20 Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, My wrath and fury are poured upon this place, upon the people and the livestock, upon every tree of their field and all the earth’s produce; and it will burn and not be extinguished.
7:21 Thus says the Lord: Gather your whole burnt offerings with your sacrifices, and eat meat!
7:22 For I did not speak to your fathers or command them on the day I brought them up from Egypt’s land concerning whole burnt offerings and sacrifice.
7:23 But this utterance I commanded them, saying: Hear My voice, and I will be your God and you will be My people; and walk in all My ways that I command you, so that it may be well with you.
7:24 And they did not hear Me, and their ear did not attend, but they walked in the thoughts of their evil heart and went backward rather than forward.
7:25 From the day their fathers left Egypt’s land until this day, I sent out to you all My servants the prophets by day and at dawn, and I sent them.
7:26 And they did not hear Me, and their ear did not attend, and they hardened their neck beyond their fathers.
7:27–28 And you will say this word to them: This is the nation that did not hear the Lord’s voice or accept instruction; faithfulness failed from their mouth.
7:29 Shear your head and cast it away, and take up a lament on your lips, because the Lord rejected and thrust away the generation doing these things.
7:30 For Judah’s sons did evil before Me, says the Lord; they set their abominations in the house over which My name has been called, to defile it.
7:31 And they built Topheth’s altar, which is in the ravine of the son of Hinnom, to burn up their sons and daughters in fire, which I did not command them and did not conceive in My heart.
7:32 Therefore behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and they will no longer say Topheth’s Altar or Ravine of the Son of Hinnom, but Ravine of the Slain; and they will bury in Topheth because there is no space.
7:33 And this people’s dead will be food for heaven’s birds and the earth’s wild beasts, and there will be no one to drive them off.
7:34 And I will stop the sound of those rejoicing and the sound of those glad, the voice of bridegroom and the voice of bride, from Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s thoroughfares, because the whole land will become a desolation.

JEREMIAH 8

8:1 At that time, says the Lord, they will bring the bones of Judah’s kings and the bones of its rulers and the bones of the priests and the bones of the prophets and the bones of those dwelling in Jerusalem out of their tombs,
8:2 and expose them to cool before the sun and moon and all the stars and all heaven’s host, which they loved and served and followed and clung to and bowed down to. They will not be mourned or buried, and they will be an example upon the earth’s surface,
8:3 because they chose death rather than life, and for all the remnants left from that generation in every place where I drive them away.
8:4 For thus says the Lord: Does one who falls not rise? Or does one who turns away not return?
8:5 Why has this people of Mine turned away with a shameless turning away, and been held fast in their own choice, and been unwilling to return?
8:6 Give ear now and hear: they will not speak thus; there is no person repenting of his evil, saying, ‘What have I done?’ The runner ceased from his course like a sweating horse in its neighing.
8:7 Even the stork in heaven knew her time; turtledove and swallow, field sparrows, kept the times of their coming, but My people did not know the Lord’s judgments.
8:8 How will you say, ‘We are wise, and the Lord’s law is with us’? A false reed-pen came to be in vain for scribes.
8:9 The wise were ashamed and terrified and captured, because they rejected the Lord’s word. What wisdom is in them?
8:10 Therefore I will give their wives to others and their fields to inheritors,
8:13 and they will gather their produce, says the Lord. There is no grape on the vines and no figs on the fig trees, and the leaves have fallen.
8:14 Why are we sitting? Gather, and let us enter the fortified cities and be cast away, because God cast us away and made us drink gall-water, because we sinned before Him.
8:15 We gathered for peace, and there were no good things; for a time of healing, and behold, haste!
8:16 From Dan we will hear the shrill sound of his horses; at the sound of the neighing of his riding horses the whole earth shook. And he will come and devour the earth and its fullness, a city and those dwelling in it.
8:17 Because behold, I am sending out among you deadly snakes that cannot be charmed, and they will bite you.
8:18 Incurable things, with pain, while your heart is bewildered.
8:19 Behold, the crying voice of the daughter of My people from a distant land: ‘Is the Lord not in Zion? Is a King not there?’ Why did they provoke Me with their carved things and foreign futile things?
8:20 Summer passed, harvest went by, and we were not delivered.
8:21 At the crushing of the daughter of My people I was darkened; in bewilderment, pains like a woman giving birth overpowered me.
8:22 Is there no resin in Gilead, or is there no physician there? Why did healing not rise for the daughter of My people?
8:23 Who will give my head water and my eyes a fountain of tears, and I will weep day and night for this people of Mine, for the wounded of the daughter of My people?

JEREMIAH 9

9:1 Who would give me a remote station in the wilderness, and I will leave my people and go away from them? For all commit adultery, an assembly of those rejecting faithfulness.
9:2 And they stretched their tongue like a bow; falsehood and not faithfulness grew strong upon the earth, because they went from evils to evils and did not know Me.
9:3 Each of you, guard against his neighbor, and do not put trust in their brothers, because every brother will trip with the heel, and every friend will walk deceitfully.
9:4 Each will mock his friend; they certainly will not speak truth. Their tongue has learned to speak false things; they did wrong and did not cease turning back.
9:5 Interest upon interest, deceit upon deceit: they were unwilling to know Me.
9:6 Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I will put them through fire and test them, because I will act on account of the evil of the daughter of My people.
9:7 Their tongue is a wounding missile; the utterances of their mouth are deceitful. One speaks peaceful things to his neighbor, and within himself he holds enmity.
9:8 Shall I not visit for these things? says the Lord. Or will not My soul take vengeance on such a people?
9:9 Take up mourning for the mountains and a dirge for the wilderness paths, because they failed for lack of people. They did not hear the sound of possessions; from heaven’s birds to livestock, they were displaced and went away.
9:10 And I will give Jerusalem over to deportation and as a dwelling of dragons, and make Judah’s cities disappear, so that they are not inhabited.
9:11 Who is the discerning person? Let him understand this; and the one to whom the word of the Lord’s mouth comes, let him announce it to you: for what reason did the land perish, kindled like a wilderness so that no one passes through it?
9:12 And the Lord said to me: Because they abandoned My law that I set before them and did not hear My voice,
9:13 but walked after the things pleasing to their evil heart and after the idols that their fathers taught them,
9:14 therefore thus says the Lord, Israel’s God: Behold, I will feed them distresses and make them drink gall-water,
9:15 and I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their fathers knew, and send the sword upon them until I consume them by it.
9:16 Thus says the Lord: Call the women who lament, and let them come; send for the skilled women, and let them speak,
9:17 and let them take up a dirge over you; let your eyes bring down tears, and let your eyelids flow with water.
9:18 For a voice of lament was heard in Zion: How we suffered misery and were greatly ashamed, because we abandoned the land and threw away our dwellings!
9:19 Hear God’s word now, women, and let your ears receive His mouth’s words; teach your daughters lament, and each woman her neighbor a dirge.
9:20 For death climbed through your windows and entered your land, to wipe out little children outside and young men from the squares.
9:21 And people’s dead will become an example on the surface of your land’s plain, and like grass behind one reaping, and there will be no one gathering.
9:22 Thus says the Lord: Let no wise man boast in his wisdom, let no strong man boast in his strength, and let no rich man boast in his wealth,
9:23 but let whoever boasts boast in this: understanding and knowing that I am the Lord, doing mercy and judgment and righteousness upon the earth, because in these is My will, says the Lord.
9:24 Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and I will visit all who have been circumcised in their foreskins:
9:25 Egypt and Judea and Edom and the sons of Ammon and the sons of Moab and everyone who clips the hair around his face, those dwelling in the wilderness; because all the nations are uncircumcised in flesh, and all Israel’s house are uncircumcised in their hearts.

JEREMIAH 10

10:1 Hear the Lord’s word that He spoke concerning you, house of Israel.
10:2 Thus says the Lord: Do not learn according to the nations’ ways, and do not fear heaven’s signs, because they fear them before their faces.
10:3 For the nations’ customs are futile: it is wood cut from the woodland, a carpenter’s work and a cast object.
10:4 They are adorned with silver and gold; with hammers and nails they fastened them, and they will not move.
10:5 They must be lifted and carried, because they will not take a step. Do not fear them, because they certainly will not do evil, and no good is in them.
10:9 It is worked silver; they will not walk. Applied silver will come from Tarshish, gold from Mophaz, and the goldsmiths’ hand; all are artisans’ works. They will dress them in blue and purple.
10:11 This is what you will say to them: Gods who did not make heaven and earth, let them perish from the earth and from beneath this heaven.
10:12 The Lord who made the earth by His strength, who set the inhabited world upright by His wisdom, also stretched out heaven by His understanding,
10:13 and an abundance of water in heaven; and He brought clouds up from the earth’s extremity, made lightning for rain, and brought light out of His treasuries.
10:14 Every human became foolish, without knowledge; every goldsmith was put to shame at his carved things, because they cast false things; there is no spirit in them.
10:15 They are futile, mocked works; at the time of their visitation they will perish.
10:16 Jacob’s portion is not such as this, because the One who formed everything is himself his inheritance; Lord is His name.
10:17 He gathered your possessions from outside, you who dwell among chosen ones.
10:18 For thus says the Lord: Behold, I am tripping those dwelling in this land with affliction, so that your wound may be found.
10:19 Woe for your crushing! Your wound is painful. And I said: Truly this is my wound, and it has seized me.
10:20 My tent suffered misery and perished, and all my tent coverings were torn apart. My sons and my sheep are no more; there is no longer a place for my tent, a place for my coverings.
10:21 For the shepherds acted foolishly and did not seek the Lord; therefore all the pasture did not perceive, and they were scattered.
10:22 A sound of a report—behold, it comes! And a great shaking from the northern land, to lay Judah’s cities waste and make them a resting place for ostriches.
10:23 I know, Lord, that a human’s way does not belong to him, nor will a man go and make his course straight.
10:24 Instruct us, Lord, but with judgment and not in fury, so that Thou dost not make us few.
10:25 Pour Thy fury upon nations who do not know Thee and upon generations who did not call on Thy name, because they devoured Jacob and consumed him and made his pasture desolate.

JEREMIAH 11

11:1 The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah, saying:
11:2 Hear the words of this covenant. And you will speak to the men of Judah and those dwelling in Jerusalem,
11:3 and you will say to them: Thus says the Lord, Israel’s God: Cursed is the person who will not hear the words of this covenant,
11:4 which I commanded your fathers on the day I brought them up from Egypt’s land, from the iron furnace, saying: Hear My voice and do everything I command you, and you will be My people and I will be your God,
11:5 so that I may establish My oath that I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as on this day. And I answered and said: May it be, Lord.
11:6 And the Lord said to me: Read these words in Judah’s cities and outside Jerusalem, saying: Hear the words of this covenant and do them.
11:8 And they did not do them.
11:9 And the Lord said to me: A conspiracy was found among Judah’s men and those dwelling in Jerusalem.
11:10 They returned to the injustices of their earlier fathers, who would not listen to My words; and behold, they walk after foreign gods to serve them. And the house of Israel and the house of Judah shattered My covenant that I established with their fathers.
11:11 Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am bringing evils upon this people from which they will not be able to escape; and they will cry to Me, and I will not listen to them.
11:12 And Judah’s cities and those dwelling in Jerusalem will go and cry to the gods to whom they burn incense. Will those gods save them in the time of their evils?
11:13 For your gods were according to the number of your cities, Judah; and according to the number of Jerusalem’s exits you set up altars to burn incense to Baal.
11:14 And you, do not pray for this people or petition for them with supplication and prayer, because I will not listen when they call upon Me, in their time of affliction.
11:15 Why has the beloved woman committed an abomination in My house? Will prayers and holy meats remove your evils from you, or will you escape by these?
11:16 The Lord called your name a beautiful olive tree, well-shaded in appearance. At the sound of its pruning, fire was kindled upon it; great is the affliction upon you, and its branches became useless.
11:17 And the Lord who planted you spoke evils against you in return for the evil of the house of Israel and the house of Judah, because they acted for themselves to provoke Me by burning incense to Baal.
11:18 Lord, make it known to me, and I will know; then I saw their practices.
11:19 But I, like a guileless lamb led to be slaughtered, did not know. Against me they devised an evil design, saying: Come, let us put wood into his bread and wipe him out from the land of the living, and his name will certainly be remembered no more.
11:20 Lord, who judges justly, who tests kidneys and hearts, may I see the vengeance from Thee upon them, because to Thee I revealed my claim of right.
11:21 Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the men of Anathoth who seek my soul, who say: You must certainly not prophesy in the Lord’s name; otherwise you will die by our hands.
11:22 Behold, I will visit them; their young men will die by the sword, and their sons and daughters will end their lives in famine,
11:23 and no remnant of them will remain, because I will bring evils upon those dwelling in Anathoth in the year of their visitation.

JEREMIAH 12

12:1 Thou art righteous, Lord, as I make my defense before Thee; nevertheless I will speak judgments with Thee. Why does the impious person’s way prosper? Why did all who commit acts of rejection flourish?
12:2 Thou didst plant them, and they took root; they produced children and bore fruit. Thou art near their mouth and far from their kidneys.
12:3 And Thou, Lord, knowest me; Thou hast tested my heart before Thee. Purify them for their day of slaughter.
12:4 How long will the earth mourn and all the field’s grass dry up because of the evil of those dwelling in it? Livestock and birds vanished, because they said, ‘God will not see our ways.’
12:5 Your feet run and exhaust you; how will you prepare yourself against horses? And in a land of peace you have confidence; how will you fare in the Jordan’s raging?
12:6 For even your brothers and your father’s house rejected you, and they shouted; they gathered behind you. Do not trust them, because they will speak good things to you.
12:7 I have abandoned My house, let go My inheritance, and given My beloved soul into her enemies’ hands.
12:8 My inheritance became to Me like a lion in woodland; she raised her voice against Me, therefore I hated her.
12:9 Is My inheritance to Me a hyena’s cave, or a cave around her? Go, gather all the field’s wild beasts, and let them come to eat her!
12:10 Many shepherds ruined My vineyard, defiled My portion, and made My desirable portion an impassable wilderness.
12:11 It was reduced to ruin and destruction; because of Me the whole land utterly vanished, because there is no man who lays it to heart.
12:12 Upon every passage through the wilderness those bringing misery came, because the Lord’s sword will devour from one end of the earth to the other; there is no peace for any flesh.
12:13 Sow wheat and harvest thorns! Their allotments will not benefit them. Be ashamed of your boasting, of reproach before the Lord.
12:14 For thus says the Lord concerning all the evil neighbors who touch My inheritance that I apportioned to My people Israel: Behold, I am tearing them from their land, and I will cast Judah out from their midst.
12:15 And after I cast them out, I will return and have mercy on them and settle each in his inheritance and each in his land.
12:16 And if they truly learn My people’s way, to swear by My name, ‘The Lord lives,’ as they taught My people to swear by Baal, then they will be built up in the midst of My people.
12:17 But if they do not return, I will remove that nation with removal and destruction.

JEREMIAH 13

13:1 Thus says the Lord: Go, acquire a linen waistcloth for yourself, and put it around your waist, and it will not pass through water.
13:2 And I acquired the waistcloth according to the Lord’s word and put it around my waist.
13:3 And the Lord’s word came to me, saying:
13:4 Take the waistcloth around your waist, and rise and go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in the rock’s opening.
13:5 And I went and hid it at the Euphrates, as the Lord commanded me.
13:6 And after many days the Lord said to me: Rise, go to the Euphrates, and take from there the waistcloth that I commanded you to hide there.
13:7 And I went to the Euphrates River and dug and took the waistcloth from the place where I had buried it. And behold, it was ruined, so that it could certainly be used for nothing.
13:8 And the Lord’s word came to me, saying:
13:9 Thus says the Lord: Thus I will ruin Judah’s arrogance and Jerusalem’s arrogance,
13:10 this great arrogance, those unwilling to obey My words and who went after foreign gods to serve them and bow down to them; and they will be like this waistcloth, which will be used for nothing.
13:11 For just as the waistcloth clings around a man’s waist, so I made Israel’s house and all Judah’s house cling to Myself, to become for Me a renowned people, a boast and a glory; and they did not listen to Me.
13:12 And you will say to this people: Every wineskin will be filled with wine. And if they say to you, ‘Do we not surely know that every wineskin will be filled with wine?’
13:13 then you will say to them: Thus says the Lord: Behold, I am filling those dwelling in this land, and their kings, David’s sons who sit on his throne, and the priests and prophets and Judah and all those dwelling in Jerusalem, with intoxication,
13:14 and I will scatter them, a man and his brother, their fathers and their sons together. I will not yearn for them, says the Lord, nor spare nor pity them so as to withhold their destruction.
13:15 Hear and give ear, and do not exalt yourselves, because the Lord has spoken.
13:16 Give glory to the Lord your God before darkness comes and before your feet stumble on dark mountains; you will wait for light, and there is death’s shadow, and they will be placed in darkness.
13:17 But if you do not hear, your soul will weep in secret because of arrogance, and your eyes will bring down tears, because the Lord’s flock was crushed.
13:18 Say to the king and those exercising power: Humble yourselves and sit down, because your crown of glory was taken down from your head.
13:19 The southern cities were shut, and there was no one opening them. Judah was deported; a complete deportation was accomplished.
13:20 Lift your eyes, Jerusalem, and see those coming from the north. Where is the flock given to you, the sheep of your glory?
13:21 What will you say when they visit you? And you taught them lessons against yourself for rule. Will not labor pains seize you like a woman giving birth?
13:22 And if you say in your heart, ‘Why have these things happened to me?’ because of your great injustice your backside was uncovered, so that your heels were put on display.
13:23 If an Ethiopian changes his skin and a leopard her markings, then you too will be able to do well, having learned evil things.
13:24 And I scattered them like brushwood carried by wind into a wilderness.
13:25 This is your lot and portion for your disobeying Me, says the Lord, because you forgot Me and hoped in falsehoods.
13:26 And I will uncover your backside over your face, and your dishonor will be seen.
13:27 And your adultery and your neighing and the estrangement of your prostitution—on the hills and in the fields I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, Jerusalem, because you were not cleansed after Me! How much longer?

JEREMIAH 14

14:1 And the Lord’s word came to Jeremiah concerning the lack of rain:
14:2 Judea mourned, and her gates were emptied and darkened upon the earth, and Jerusalem’s cry rose.
14:3 Her magnates sent their younger ones for water; they came to wells and found no water and brought back their vessels empty.
14:4 And the land’s works failed because there was no rain; the farmers were ashamed and covered their heads.
14:5 And deer in the field gave birth and abandoned their young, because there was no pasture.
14:6 Wild donkeys stood in wooded valleys; they drew in wind, their eyes failed, because there was no grass, because of a people of injustice.
14:7 If our sins stood against us, Lord, act for us for Thine own sake, because our sins before Thee are many, because we sinned against Thee.
14:8 Israel’s patient expectation, Lord, Thou savest in a time of evils; why didst Thou become like a sojourner upon the earth and like a native turning aside to a lodging?
14:9 Wilt Thou be like a sleeping man, or like a man unable to save? Yet Thou art among us, Lord, and Thy name has been called over us; do not forget us.
14:10 Thus says the Lord to this people: They loved to move their feet and did not restrain them, and God was not pleased with them; now He will remember their injustices.
14:11 And the Lord said to me: Do not pray for good things for this people,
14:12 because if they fast, I will not listen to their supplication, and if they bring whole burnt offerings and sacrifices, I will not be pleased with them, because by sword and famine and death I will bring them to an end.
14:13 And I said: O Lord, behold, their prophets prophesy and say, ‘You will not see sword, nor will famine be among you, because I will give truth and peace upon the land and in this place.’
14:14 And the Lord said to me: The prophets prophesy false things in My name; I did not send them or command them or speak to them, because they prophesy false visions, divinations, omens, and their heart’s choices to you.
14:15 Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the prophets prophesying false things in My name, though I did not send them, who say, ‘Sword and famine will not be upon this land’: They will die a diseased death, and the prophets will be consumed by famine.
14:16 And the people to whom they prophesy will be thrown into Jerusalem’s thoroughfares because of sword and famine, and there will be no one burying them, and their wives and sons and daughters; and I will pour their evils upon them.
14:17 And you will say this word to them: Bring tears down upon your eyes day and night, and let them not cease, because the daughter of My people was utterly crushed, with a very painful wound.
14:18 If I go out to the plain, behold, those wounded by the sword; and if I enter the city, behold, the pain of famine! For priest and prophet went to a land they did not know.
14:19 Hast Thou utterly rejected Judah, and has Thy soul departed from Zion? Why didst Thou strike us, and there is no healing for us? We waited for peace, and there were no good things; for a time of healing, and behold, turmoil!
14:20 We recognized, Lord, our sins and our fathers’ injustices, because we sinned before Thee.
14:21 Abate for Thy name’s sake; do not destroy Thy throne of glory. Remember; do not shatter Thy covenant with us.
14:22 Is there among the nations’ idols one who brings rain? Can heaven give its fullness? Is it not Thou Thyself? And we will wait for Thee, because Thou madest all these things.

JEREMIAH 15

15:1 And the Lord said to me: If Moses and Samuel stand before Me, My soul is not toward them. Send this people away, and let them go out.
15:2 And if they say to you, ‘Where shall we go out?’ you will say to them: Thus says the Lord: As many as are for death, to death; as many for sword, to sword; as many for famine, to famine; as many for captivity, to captivity.
15:3 And I will exact vengeance upon them in four kinds, says the Lord: the sword for slaughter, dogs for tearing apart, the earth’s wild beasts and heaven’s birds for eating and destruction.
15:4 And I will hand them over to distresses for all the earth’s kingdoms because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, concerning all he did in Jerusalem.
15:5 Who will spare you, Jerusalem? Who will be afraid for you? Or who will turn back for your peace?
15:6 You turned away from Me, says the Lord; you will go backward. And I will stretch out My hand and destroy you, and I will no longer let them go.
15:7 And I will scatter them in a dispersion; at My people’s gates they were bereaved; they destroyed My people because of their evils.
15:8 Their widows multiplied beyond the sea’s sand. I brought misery upon a young man’s mother at noon; suddenly I cast trembling and haste upon her.
15:9 She who bore seven was emptied; her soul grew faint. Her sun set while the day was still at its middle; she was ashamed and reproached. Their remnants I will give to the sword before their enemies.
15:10 Woe to me, mother! What sort of man did you give birth to—a man litigating and disputing with the whole earth? I benefited no one, nor did anyone benefit me; my strength failed among those cursing me.
15:11 May it be, Master, as they are directed—if I did not stand before Thee in the time of their evils and their affliction, for good toward the enemy!
15:12 Will iron be recognized? And a bronze enclosure
15:13 is your strength. And I will give your treasures as plunder, an exchange for all your sins and in all your borders.
15:14 And I will enslave you all around to your enemies in the land you did not know, because a fire has been kindled from My fury; it will burn upon you.
15:15 Lord, remember me and visit me and vindicate me against those pursuing me; do not delay. Know how I received reproach concerning Thee
15:16 from those rejecting Thy words. Bring them to an end, and Thy word will become to me rejoicing and my heart’s joy, because Thy name has been called over me, Lord Almighty.
15:17 I did not sit in their gathering as they played, but I was reverent before Thy hand. I sat alone because I was filled with bitterness.
15:18 Why do those grieving me overpower me? My wound is firm; from where will I be healed? It indeed became to me like false water having no faithfulness.
15:19 Therefore thus says the Lord: If you return, I will restore you, and you will stand before Me. If you bring out the honored from the unworthy, you will be as My mouth; and they will turn to you, and you will not turn to them.
15:20 And I will make you to this people like a fortified bronze wall, and they will fight against you but certainly will not prevail against you, because I am with you to save you
15:21 and to rescue you from the hand of evil people, and I will ransom you from the hand of pestilent people.

JEREMIAH 16

16:1 And you must not take a wife, says the Lord, Israel’s God,
16:2 and no son or daughter will be born to you in this place.
16:3 For thus says the Lord concerning the sons and daughters born in this place, and their mothers who bore them, and their fathers who begot them in this land:
16:4 They will die a diseased death; they will not be mourned or buried. They will be an example upon the earth’s surface, and for the earth’s wild beasts and heaven’s birds; they will fall by the sword and be consumed by famine.
16:5 Thus says the Lord: Do not enter their gathering or go to beat yourself in mourning, and do not mourn them, because I have removed My peace from this people.
16:6 They certainly will not mourn them or make cuts, and they will not shave themselves,
16:7 and bread certainly will not be broken in their mourning for consolation over one dead; they will not give him a cup for consolation over his father and mother.
16:8 You must not enter a house of drinking to sit with them to eat and drink.
16:9 Because thus says the Lord, Israel’s God: Behold, before your eyes and in your days I am stopping from this place the sound of joy and rejoicing, the voice of bridegroom and bride.
16:10 And when you announce all these utterances to this people and they say to you, ‘Why did the Lord speak all these evils against us? What is our injustice, and what is our sin that we sinned before the Lord our God?’
16:11 you will say to them: Because your fathers abandoned Me, says the Lord, and went after foreign gods and served them and bowed down to them, and abandoned Me and did not keep My law,
16:12 and you acted more wickedly than your fathers, and behold, each of you follows what pleases your evil heart, so as not to obey Me,
16:13 and I will cast you away from this land into the land neither you nor your fathers knew, and there you will serve other gods who will give you no mercy.
16:14 Therefore behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and they will no longer say, ‘The Lord lives, who brought Israel’s sons up from Egypt’s land,’
16:15 but, ‘The Lord lives, who brought Israel’s house up from the northern land and all the regions where they were driven.’ And I will restore them to their land that I gave their fathers.
16:16 Behold, I am sending the many fishermen, says the Lord, and they will fish for them; and after these things I will send the many hunters, and they will hunt them on every mountain and hill and from the rocks’ openings.
16:17 Because My eyes are upon all their ways, and their wrongdoings were not hidden before My eyes.
16:18 And I will repay double their injustices and sins, for which they profaned My land with the corpses of their abominations and with their lawless acts, by which they offended against My inheritance.
16:19 Lord, my strength and help and refuge in a day of evils, nations will come to Thee from the earth’s extremity and say: How our fathers acquired false idols, and there is no benefit in them!
16:20 Will a human make gods for himself? And these are not gods.
16:21 Therefore behold, at this time I will show them My hand and make My power known to them, and they will know that My name is Lord.

JEREMIAH 17

17:5 Cursed is the person who places his hope in a human and will rest the flesh of his arm upon him, and whose heart departs from the Lord;
17:6 and he will be like a wild tamarisk in the wilderness; he will not see when good things come, and will dwell among salt-plants and in a wilderness, in a salt land that is not inhabited.
17:7 And blessed is the person who has trusted in the Lord, and the Lord will be his hope;
17:8 and he will be like a tree flourishing beside waters, and will send its roots toward moisture, and will not fear when heat comes. Wooded stems will be upon it; in a rainless year it will not fear or cease bearing fruit.
17:9 The heart is deep beyond all things, and so is a human; and who will know him?
17:10 I, the Lord, examine hearts and test kidneys, to give each according to his ways and the fruits of his practices.
17:11 A partridge called; it gathered what it did not bear. One makes his wealth without judgment; in the middle of his days they will abandon him, and at his last he will be foolish.
17:12 An exalted throne of glory is our sanctuary,
17:13 Israel’s patient expectation, Lord, let all who abandoned Thee be ashamed; let those who departed be written upon the earth, because they abandoned the Lord, the fountain of life.
17:14 Heal me, Lord, and I will be healed; save me, and I will be saved, because Thou art my boast.
17:15 Behold, they say to me: Where is the Lord’s word? Let it come!
17:16 But I did not grow weary following after Thee, and did not desire a human’s day; Thou knowest. The things going out through my lips are before Thee.
17:17 Do not become an estrangement to me, sparing me in an evil day.
17:18 Let those pursuing me be ashamed, and may I not be ashamed; may they be terrified, and may I not be terrified. Bring an evil day upon them; crush them with double crushing.
17:19 Thus says the Lord: Go and stand in the gates of your people’s sons, through which Judah’s kings enter and leave, and in all Jerusalem’s gates,
17:20 and you will say to them: Hear the Lord’s word, kings of Judah, all Judea and all Jerusalem, who enter these gates:
17:21 Thus says the Lord: Guard your souls, and do not lift burdens on the Sabbath day or go out through Jerusalem’s gates,
17:22 and do not bring burdens out of your houses on the Sabbath day, and you must do no work; consecrate the Sabbath day, as I commanded your fathers. And they did not hear or incline their ear,
17:23 and they hardened their neck beyond their fathers, so as not to hear Me or accept instruction.
17:24 And if you truly listen to Me, says the Lord, not bringing burdens through this city’s gates on the Sabbath day and consecrating the Sabbath day by doing no work,

JEREMIAH 22

22:1 Thus says the Lord: Go and descend to the house of Judah’s king and speak this word there,
22:2 and say: Hear the Lord’s word, king of Judah who sits on David’s throne—you, your household, your people, and those entering these gates.
22:3 Thus says the Lord: Do justice and righteousness; rescue the plundered from the hand of the one wronging him. Do not oppress the newcomer, orphan, or widow, and do not act impiously or shed innocent blood in this place.
22:4 Because if you truly carry out this word, kings sitting upon David’s throne and riding chariots and horses will enter this house’s gates—they, their servants, and their people.
22:5 But if you do not carry out these words, I swear by Myself, says the Lord, that this house will become a desolation.

JEREMIAH 23

23:1 O shepherds who scatter and destroy the sheep of My pasture!

23:3 And I will receive the remnant of My people from all the land where I drove them out, and establish them in their pasture; they will increase and multiply.
23:4 And I will raise shepherds for them who will tend them, and they will no longer fear or be frightened, says the Lord.
23:5 Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and I will raise up a righteous rising for David. A king will reign and understand, doing judgment and righteousness upon the earth.
23:6 In his days Judah will be saved and Israel will dwell confidently. This is his name, which the Lord will call him: Josedek.
23:7 Therefore behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and they will no longer say, As the Lord lives, who brought Israel’s house up from Egypt’s land,
23:8 but, As the Lord lives, who gathered all Israel’s offspring from the northern land and all the countries where He drove them out, and restored them to their land.
23:9 Among the prophets my heart was shattered; within me all my bones were shaken. I became like a shattered man, like a person gripped by wine, before the Lord and the splendor of His glory.
23:10 Because of these things the land mourned and the wilderness pastures dried up. Their course became evil, and their strength was not so.
23:11 Because priest and prophet were defiled, and in My house I saw their evils.
23:12 Therefore let their way become a slippery place for them in darkness, and they will be tripped and fall in it, because I will bring evils upon them in their year of visitation, says the Lord.
23:13 And among Samaria’s prophets I saw lawless acts: they prophesied through Baal and led My people Israel astray.
23:14 And among Jerusalem’s prophets I have seen horrifying things: committing adultery, walking in lies, and supporting evil hands so that no one turns from his evil way. They all became like Sodom to Me, and her inhabitants like Gomorrah.
23:15 Therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I will feed them pain and give them bitter water to drink, because from Jerusalem’s prophets defilement went out into all the land.
23:16 Thus says the Lord Almighty: Do not listen to the prophets’ words, because they make a vision futile for themselves. They speak from their own heart and not from the Lord’s mouth.
23:17 They say to those rejecting the Lord’s word, You will have peace. To all who walk according to their own wishes, to everyone walking in the wandering of his heart, they said, No evils will come upon you.
23:18 Because who stood in the Lord’s standing place and saw His word? Who gave ear and heard?
23:19 Behold, a shaking comes from the Lord, and anger goes out into a convulsion; swirling, it will come upon the ungodly.
23:20 And the Lord’s fury will no longer turn back until He does it and raises it up from His heart’s undertaking. At the end of the days they will understand these things.
23:21 I was not sending the prophets, yet they ran; I did not speak to them, yet they prophesied.
23:22 And if they had stood in My standing presence and listened to My words, they would have turned My people from their evil practices.
23:23 I am a God drawing near, says the Lord, and not a God far away.
23:24 If a person hides in hidden places, will I not see him? Do I not fill the heaven and the earth? says the Lord.
23:25 I heard what the prophets speak, the false things they prophesy in My name, saying, I dreamed a dream.
23:26 How long will it be in the heart of the prophets prophesying false things, and in their prophesying the wishes of their own heart?
23:27 They plan to forget My law through their dreams, which each told his neighbor, just as their fathers forgot My name through Baal.
23:28 Let the prophet in whom there is a dream tell his dream, and the one to whom My word comes tell My word in truth. What has chaff to do with wheat? So are My words, says the Lord.
23:29 Are not My words like blazing fire, says the Lord, and like an axe cutting rock?
23:30 Therefore behold, I am against the prophets, says the Lord God, who steal My words, each from his neighbor.
23:31 Behold, I am against the prophets who cast out prophecies of the tongue and doze their own dozing.
23:32 Behold, I am against the prophets who prophesy false dreams, who told them and led My people astray by their lies and wanderings. I did not send or command them, and they will bring this people no benefit at all.
23:33 And if this people, a priest, or a prophet asks you, What is the Lord’s burden? say to them, You are the burden, and I will dash you down, says the Lord.
23:34 And the prophet, priest, or people who say, The Lord’s burden—I will exact justice from that person and his household.
23:35 For this is what you will say, each to his neighbor and his brother: What did the Lord answer? What did the Lord speak?
23:36 And do not name the Lord’s burden again, because a person’s own word will be his burden.
23:37 And why did the Lord our God speak?
23:38 Therefore thus says the Lord God: Because you said this word, The Lord’s burden, though I sent to you saying, Do not say The Lord’s burden,
23:39 therefore behold, I seize you and dash you down, along with the city I gave to you and your fathers,
23:40 and I will put eternal reproach and eternal dishonor upon you, which will not be forgotten.

28:3 Against her let the archer stretch his bow, and let the man who has armor put it on. Do not spare her young men; obliterate her whole force.
28:4 Wounded men will fall in the Chaldeans’ land, and pierced men outside her.
28:5 For Israel and Judah have not been widowed of their God, of the Lord Almighty, because their land was filled with injustice from Israel’s holy ones.
28:6 Flee from Babylon’s midst; each of you save your life. Do not be cast away in her injustice, for her time of vengeance is from the Lord; He is repaying her recompense.
28:7 Babylon was a golden cup in the Lord’s hand, making the whole earth drunk. Nations drank her wine; therefore they were shaken.
28:8 Suddenly Babylon fell and was shattered. Lament her! Take resin for her corruption, if somehow she may be healed.
28:9 We treated Babylon, but she was not healed. Let us leave her; let each of us go to our own land, for her judgment reached heaven and rose to the stars.
28:10 The Lord has brought forth His judgment. Come, let us announce in Zion the works of the Lord our God.
28:11 Prepare the arrows; fill the quivers. The Lord roused the spirit of the Medes’ king, for His anger against Babylon is to destroy her. This is the Lord’s vengeance, vengeance for His people.
28:12 Raise a signal on Babylon’s walls; set up quivers, raise guards, prepare weapons, for the Lord has undertaken and will do what He spoke against Babylon’s inhabitants,
28:13 who dwell beside many waters and her abundance of treasures. Your end has truly come to your inward parts.
28:14 For the Lord swore by His arm: I will fill you with people like locusts, and those coming down will speak out against you.
28:15 He makes the earth by His strength, prepares the inhabited world by His wisdom, and stretched out heaven by His understanding.
28:16 At His voice He set a sound of water in heaven and brought clouds up from the earth’s end. He made lightning for rain and brought light out of His treasuries.
28:17 Every person became foolish, without knowledge; every goldsmith was disgraced by his carvings, because they cast false things, and there is no spirit in them.
28:18 They are vain, mocked works; at the time of their visitation they will perish.
28:19 Jacob’s portion is not like this, for the One who formed all things is Himself his inheritance. The Lord is His name.
28:20 You scatter instruments of war for Me. In you I will scatter nations, and from you I will remove kings.
28:21 In you I will scatter horse and rider; in you I will scatter chariots and their riders.
28:22 In you I will scatter young man and virgin; in you I will scatter man and woman.
28:23 In you I will scatter shepherd and flock; in you I will scatter farmer and farmland; in you I will scatter your leaders and generals.
28:24 I will repay Babylon and all the Chaldeans who live there for all the evils they did against Zion before your eyes, says the Lord.
28:25 I am against you, ruined mountain that ruins the whole earth. I will stretch My hand against you, roll you down from the rocks, and make you like a burned mountain.
28:26 They will certainly not take from you a cornerstone or a foundation stone, for you will be a ruin for ever, says the Lord.
28:27 Raise a signal on the earth; sound a trumpet among the nations. Consecrate nations against her; give orders against her to Ararat’s kingdoms from Me and to the Ashkenazites. Set up arrow batteries against her; bring up horses against her like a multitude of locusts.
28:28 Consecrate nations against her: the king of the Medes and of all the earth, his leaders and all his generals.
28:29 The earth was shaken and suffered, for the Lord’s purpose rose against Babylon, to make Babylon’s land a ruin, no longer inhabited.
28:30 Babylon’s warrior has ceased fighting; they will sit there in confinement. Their power was crushed; they became like women. Her dwellings were burned; her bars were shattered.
28:31 Pursuer will pursue to meet pursuer, and announcer to meet announcer, to tell Babylon’s king that his city has been captured;
28:32 from the extremity of his crossings they were taken, and their gatherings were burned with fire, and his warriors are going out.
28:33 For thus says the Lord: The houses of Babylon’s king will be threshed like a threshing floor ready for use; a little longer, and her harvest will come.
28:34 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon devoured me, divided me, seized me, a thin vessel. He swallowed me like a dragon, filled his belly, and drove me from my luxury.
28:35 My toils and miseries be upon Babylon, the woman dwelling in Zion will say; my blood be upon the Chaldeans who live there, Jerusalem will say.
28:36 Therefore thus says the Lord: I will judge your adversary and avenge you; I will lay her sea waste and dry her spring.
28:37 Babylon will become a ruin and will not be inhabited.
28:38 Together they were roused like lions and like lions’ cubs.
28:39 In their heat I will give them a drink and make them drunk, so that they are stupefied and sleep an everlasting sleep and certainly do not rise, says the Lord.
28:40 I will bring them down like lambs to slaughter and like rams with young goats.
28:41 How the boast of the whole earth was captured and hunted! How Babylon became a ruin among the nations!
28:42 The sea rose over Babylon with the sound of its waves, and she was covered.
28:43 Her cities became a waterless and untrodden land; not one person will live there, nor will any human lodge there.
28:44 I will exact vengeance on Babylon and bring what she swallowed out of her mouth. Nations will certainly no longer gather to her.
28:49 And in Babylon wounded men from all the earth will fall.
28:50 You who escape from the land, go on; do not stand still. You who are far away, remember the Lord, and let Jerusalem rise in your hearts.
28:51 We were disgraced, for we heard our reproach; dishonor covered our face. Foreigners entered our holy places, the Lord’s house.
28:52 Therefore days are coming, says the Lord, when I will exact vengeance on her carvings, and wounded men will fall throughout her land.
28:53 Even if Babylon rises as high as heaven and fortifies the height of her strength, those who destroy her will come from Me, says the Lord.
28:54 A sound of crying in Babylon, a great shattering in the Chaldeans’ land!
28:55 For the Lord destroyed Babylon and silenced her loud voice sounding like many waters; He gave her voice over to destruction.
28:56 For misery came upon Babylon. Her warriors were captured; their bow is terrified, because God repays them; the Lord repays her recompense.
28:57 He will make her leaders and wise men and generals thoroughly drunk, says the King, whose name is the Lord Almighty.
28:58 Thus says the Lord: Babylon’s wall was broadened; it will be utterly demolished, and her high gates burned. Peoples will not labor in vain, and nations will perish at the beginning.
28:59 The word the Lord commanded Jeremiah the prophet to tell Seraiah son of Neriah son of Maaseiah when he went from Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. Seraiah was an official over gifts.
28:60 Jeremiah wrote in one scroll all the evils that would come upon Babylon, all these words written against Babylon.
28:61 Jeremiah said to Seraiah: When you arrive in Babylon, you will see and read all these words,
28:62 and say, Lord, Lord, Thou spakest against this place to destroy it and leave no inhabitants in it, from human to livestock, for it will be a ruin for ever.
28:63 When you finish reading this scroll, bind a stone to it and throw it into the Euphrates,
28:64 and say, Thus Babylon will sink and certainly not rise because of the evils I am bringing upon her.

33:4 And say: Thus said the Lord: If you do not hear Me, walking in My ordinances that I set before your face,
33:5 listening to the words of My servants the prophets whom I send to you from early morning, and have sent—though you did not listen to Me—
33:6 then I will make this house like Shiloh and make the city a curse to all the nations of all the earth.

33:10 And Judah’s rulers heard this word and went up from the king’s house to the Lord’s house. They sat before the Lord’s New Gate.
33:11 And the priests and false prophets said to the rulers and all the people: A sentence of death for this man, because he prophesied against this city, as you heard with your own ears!
33:12 And Jeremiah said to the rulers and all the people: The Lord sent me to prophesy against this house and this city all these words you heard.
33:13 And now make your ways and works better and hear the Lord’s voice, and the Lord will cease from the evils He spoke against you.
33:14 And behold, I am in your hands. Do to me what is advantageous and better for you.
33:15 But know for certain that if you kill me, you place innocent blood upon yourselves, this city, and its inhabitants, because the Lord truly sent me to you to speak all these words in your ears.
33:16 And the rulers and all the people said to the priests and false prophets: There is no sentence of death for this man, because he spoke to us in the name of the Lord our God.
33:17 And men from the land’s elders rose and said to the whole congregation of the people:
33:18 Micah the Morathite lived in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah and said to all Judah’s people: Thus said the Lord: Zion will be plowed as a field, Jerusalem will become an untrodden place, and the house’s mountain a grove of woodland.

33:20 And there was a man prophesying in the Lord’s name, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied concerning this land according to all Jeremiah’s words.
33:21 And King Jehoiakim and all the rulers heard all his words and sought to kill him. Uriah heard and entered Egypt.
33:22 And the king sent men to Egypt,
33:23 and they brought him out from there and brought him to the king. He struck him with the sword and threw him into the tomb of the sons of his people.
33:24 Nevertheless, Ahikam son of Shaphan’s hand was with Jeremiah, so that he was not handed into the people’s hands and they did not kill him.

JEREMIAH 37

37:5 Thus said the Lord: You will hear a sound of fear—fear, and there is no peace.

37:7 so it became. For that day is great, and there is none like it; it is a narrow time for Jacob, and he will be saved from it.

37:23 Because the Lord’s anger went out furious; swirling anger went out and will come upon the ungodly.
37:24 The anger of the Lord’s fury will certainly not turn back until He acts and establishes His heart’s undertaking. At the end of the days you will know these things.

JEREMIAH 38

38:1 At that time, said the Lord, I will be God to Israel’s family, and they will be My people.
38:2 Thus said the Lord: I found warmth in the wilderness among those who perished by the sword. Go, and do not destroy Israel.
38:3 The Lord appeared to him from afar: I loved you with an eternal love; therefore I drew you into compassion.
38:4 Again I will build you, and you will be built, virgin Israel. Again you will take your tambourine and go out with a gathering of people playing.
38:5 Again plant vineyards on Samaria’s mountains; plant and praise.
38:6 Because there is a day of calling by those making their defense in Ephraim’s mountains: Rise and go up to Zion, to the Lord our God.
38:7 Because thus said the Lord to Jacob: Rejoice and neigh aloud toward the head of the nations; make it heard and praise. Say: The Lord saved His people, Israel’s remnant!
38:8 Behold, I am bringing them from the north and will gather them from the earth’s farthest end at the Passover festival. You will bear a great crowd, and they will return here.
38:9 They went out with weeping, and with encouragement I will bring them up, lodging them beside water channels on a straight road where they will certainly not wander, because I became Israel’s father, and Ephraim is My firstborn.
38:10 Hear the Lord’s word, nations, and announce it to the farther islands. Say: He who winnowed Israel will gather him and guard him like one tending his flock.
38:11 Because the Lord ransomed Jacob and rescued him from the hand of those stronger than he.
38:12 They will come and rejoice on Mount Zion; they will come for the Lord’s good things, to a land of grain, wine, fruits, livestock, and sheep. Their life will be like a fruitful tree, and they will hunger no more.
38:13 Then virgins will rejoice in a gathering of young men, and elders will rejoice. I will turn their mourning into gladness and make them joyful.
38:14 I will magnify and fill the priests’ souls to abundance, the sons of Levi, and My people will be filled with My good things.
38:15 Thus said the Lord: A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, weeping, and wailing. Rachel, weeping aloud, would not stop over her sons, because they are no more.
38:16 Thus said the Lord: Let your voice cease from weeping and your eyes from your tears, because your works have a reward, and they will return from enemies’ land,
38:17 a lasting stay for your children.
38:18 I surely heard Ephraim wailing: Thou didst discipline me, and I was disciplined; like a calf I was not taught. Turn me back, and I will return, because Thou art the Lord my God.
38:19 Because after my captivity I repented, and after I came to know, I groaned over days of shame and showed Thee that I received reproach from my youth.
38:20 Ephraim is a beloved son to Me, a child taking delight, because My words are in him; I will surely remember him. Therefore I hastened toward him; I will surely have mercy on him, says the Lord.
38:21 Stand firm, Zion; carry out punishment; set your heart upon the shoulders. Turn back along the road you traveled, virgin Israel; turn back to your cities in mourning.
38:22 How long will you turn away, dishonored daughter? Because the Lord created salvation for a new planting; people will go about in salvation.
38:23 Thus said the Lord: They will again say this word in Judah’s land and its cities when I return its captivity: Blessed is the Lord over the righteous mountain, His holy mountain,
38:24 and those dwelling in Judah’s cities and all its land together with a farmer; and he will be lifted up with a flock.
38:25 Because I filled every thirsty soul to abundance and filled every hungry soul.
38:26 Therefore I awoke and saw, and my sleep became sweet to me.
38:27 Therefore behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and I will sow Israel and Judah with human seed and livestock seed.
38:28 And just as I watched over them to tear down and harm, so I will watch over them to build and plant, says the Lord.
38:29 In those days they will certainly not say: The fathers ate an unripe grape, and the children’s teeth were set on edge.
38:30 Instead, each will die in his own sin, and the one eating the unripe grape will have his own teeth set on edge.
38:31 Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah,
38:32 not according to the covenant I made with their fathers on the day I took their hand to bring them out of Egypt’s land, because they did not remain in My covenant, and I disregarded them, says the Lord.
38:33 Because this is the covenant I will make with Israel’s house after those days, says the Lord: I will surely put My laws into their understanding and write them upon their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be My people.
38:34 And they will certainly not teach each his fellow citizen or his brother, saying, Know the Lord, because they will all know Me, from their least to their greatest. I will be merciful toward their injustices and certainly remember their sins no more.
38:35 If the heaven is raised to the height, says the Lord, and the earth’s ground lowered beneath, even then I do not reject Israel’s family, says the Lord, for all they did.
38:36 Thus said the Lord, who gave the sun for daylight, moon and stars for night light, and a cry in the sea so its waves boomed—the Lord Almighty is His name:
38:37 If these laws cease from before Me, says the Lord, then Israel’s family will cease to be a nation before Me for all days.
38:38 Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and a city will be built for the Lord from Hanamel’s tower to the Corner Gate.
38:39 And its measurement will go out opposite them as far as Gareb’s hills, and it will be enclosed all around with chosen stones.
38:40 And all Asaremoth as far as the Kidron wadi, to the corner of the eastern Horse Gate, will be a sanctuary for the Lord. It will certainly never disappear or be torn down for ever.

JEREMIAH 39

39:1 The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah in King Zedekiah’s tenth year, which was the eighteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.
39:2 The king of Babylon’s force had built siegeworks against Jerusalem, and Jeremiah was kept in the prison court at the king’s house.
39:3 King Zedekiah had shut him in there, saying, Why do you prophesy, saying, Thus said the Lord: I am handing this city over to Babylon’s king, and he will take it?
39:4 Zedekiah will certainly not be saved from the Chaldeans’ hand, but will certainly be handed over to Babylon’s king. His mouth will speak to his mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes.
39:5 Zedekiah will enter Babylon and remain there.

39:7 Hanamel son of Shallum, your father’s brother, is coming to you, saying, Acquire my field at Anathoth for yourself, because you have the right to receive it as a possession.

39:9 I acquired the field of Hanamel, my father’s brother’s son, and weighed out to him seventeen shekels of silver.
39:10 I wrote a document, sealed it, called witnesses, and weighed the silver on a balance.
39:11 I took the purchase document, the sealed one and the one that had been read.
39:12 I gave it to Baruch son of Neriah son of Maaseiah, before Hanamel my father’s brother’s son, before those standing there and writing in the purchase document, and before the Judeans in the prison court.
39:13 Before their eyes I instructed Baruch:
39:14 Thus said the Lord Almighty: Take this purchase document and the document that has been read, and put it in an earthenware vessel so that it may last many days.
39:15 For thus said the Lord: Fields, houses, and vineyards will again be acquired in this land.
39:16 After giving the purchase document to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the Lord:
39:17 O Lord, Thou madest heaven and earth by Thy great strength and Thy high, uplifted arm. Nothing can be hidden from Thee.
39:18 Thou showest mercy to thousands and repayest fathers’ sins into their children’s laps after them, the great and strong God,
39:19 Lord of great counsel and powerful in works, the great God, the Almighty and renowned Lord. Thine eyes are upon the ways of human beings, to give each according to his way.
39:20 Thou didst perform signs and wonders in Egypt down to this day, in Israel and among earthborn people, and madest a name for Thyself as it is this day.
39:21 Thou broughtest Thy people Israel out of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a mighty hand, a high arm, and great sights.
39:22 Thou gavest them this land, which Thou hadst sworn to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.
39:23 They entered and received it, but did not obey Thy voice or walk in Thine ordinances. They did none of all that Thou didst command them, and Thou didst cause all these calamities to befall them.
39:24 A multitude has reached this city to seize it. The city was handed over to the Chaldeans fighting it because of sword and famine. As Thou spakest, so it happened.
39:25 Yet Thou sayest to me, Acquire a field with silver. And I wrote a document, sealed it, and called witnesses—even though the city was handed over to the Chaldeans.
39:26 The Lord’s word came to me:
39:27 I am the Lord, the God of all flesh. Can anything be hidden from Me?
39:28 Therefore thus said the Lord, Israel’s God: This city will certainly be handed over to Babylon’s king, and he will take it.
39:29 The Chaldeans fighting this city will come and burn this city with fire. They will burn down the houses on whose roofs they burned incense to Baal and poured libations to other gods to provoke Me bitterly.
39:30 The children of Israel and Judah alone were doing evil in My sight from their youth.
39:31 This city has aroused My anger and wrath from the day they built it until this day, so that I would remove it from My presence,
39:32 because of all the wicked deeds the children of Israel and Judah committed to embitter Me—they and their kings, their rulers, their priests, their prophets, the men of Judah and Jerusalem’s inhabitants.
39:33 They turned their backs to Me, not their faces. I taught them from early morning and kept teaching, but they did not listen and take hold of discipline.
39:34 They set their pollutions in the house over which My name is invoked, amid their impurities.
39:35 They built the altars to Baal in the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, to offer their sons and daughters to Molech the king. I had not instructed them, nor had it entered My heart, to do this abomination and cause Judah to sin.
39:36 And now, thus said the Lord, Israel’s God, concerning the city of which you say, It will be handed over to Babylon’s king by sword, famine, and a sent affliction:
39:37 I am gathering them from all the land where I scattered them in My anger, wrath, and great indignation. I will bring them back to this place and settle them in confidence.
39:38 They will be My people, and I will be their God.
39:39 I will give them another way and another heart, to fear Me always, for their good and their children’s good after them.
39:40 I will establish an eternal covenant with them, which I will certainly not turn back from behind them. I will put My fear in their heart so that they do not depart from Me.
39:41 I will attend to them to do them good and will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all heart and all soul.
39:42 For thus said the Lord: Just as I brought all these great calamities upon this people, so I will bring upon them all the good things I spoke concerning them.
39:43 Fields will again be acquired in the land of which you say, It is untrodden by people and animals, and they were handed over to the Chaldeans.
39:44 They will acquire fields with silver, and you will write a document, seal it, and call witnesses in Benjamin’s land, around Jerusalem, in Judah’s cities, the hill-country cities, the cities of the Shephelah, and the cities of the Negeb, because I will bring back their exiles.

JEREMIAH 43

43:1 In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, the Lord’s word came to me:
43:2 Take a writing roll and write on it all the words I have delivered to you as oracles concerning Jerusalem, Judah, and all the nations, from the day I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah king of Judah until today.
43:3 Perhaps Judah’s house will hear all the calamities I intend to bring upon them, so that they turn from their evil way and I may be merciful toward their injustices and sins.
43:4 Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and he wrote on a writing roll, from Jeremiah’s mouth, all the Lord’s words which He had delivered to him as oracles.
43:5 Jeremiah instructed Baruch, I am under guard and cannot enter the Lord’s house.
43:6 Read from this roll in the people’s hearing at the Lord’s house on a fast day, and read it to all Judah’s people coming from their city.
43:7 Perhaps their appeal for mercy will fall before the Lord, and they will turn from their evil way, for great are the Lord’s wrath and anger which He has pronounced against this people.
43:8 Baruch did everything Jeremiah instructed him, reading the Lord’s words from the document in the Lord’s house.
43:9 In King Jehoiakim’s eighth year, in the ninth month, all the people in Jerusalem and Judah’s house assembled for a fast before the Lord.
43:10 Baruch read Jeremiah’s words from the document in the Lord’s house, in the house of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court at the entrance of the New Gate of the Lord’s house, in all the people’s hearing.
43:11 Micaiah son of Gemariah son of Shaphan heard all the Lord’s words from the document.
43:12 He went down to the king’s house, to the scribe’s house. All the rulers were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shelemiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the rulers.
43:13 Micaiah reported to them all the words he had heard when Baruch read in the people’s hearing.
43:14 All the rulers sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah son of Shelemiah son of Cushi to Baruch son of Neriah, saying, Take in your hand the roll from which you are reading to the people, and come. Baruch took the roll and went down to them.
43:15 They said to him, Read it again to us. Baruch read it.
43:16 When they heard all the words, they consulted one another and said, Let us certainly report all these words to the king.
43:17 They asked Baruch, Where did all these words you wrote come from?
43:18 Baruch said, Jeremiah reported all these words to me from his mouth, and I wrote them in a document.
43:19 They said to Baruch, Go and hide, you and Jeremiah. Let no one know where you are.
43:20 They entered the court to see the king, leaving the roll for safekeeping in Elishama’s house, and reported all the words to the king.
43:21 The king sent Jehudi to fetch the roll. He took it from Elishama’s house, and Jehudi read it to the king and all the rulers standing around him.
43:22 The king was sitting in a winter house, with a fire brazier before him.
43:23 When Jehudi read three or four columns, the king cut them off with the scribe’s knife and threw them into the fire on the brazier, until the whole roll was consumed in the brazier’s fire.
43:24 Neither the king nor his servants who heard all these words sought anything or tore their garments.
43:25 Elnathan, Gedaliah, and Gemariah advised the king not to burn the roll.
43:26 The king ordered Jerahmeel the king’s son and Seraiah son of Azriel to arrest Baruch and Jeremiah. But they were hidden.
43:27 After the king burned the roll—all the words Baruch had written from Jeremiah’s mouth—the Lord’s word came to Jeremiah:
43:28 Take another roll again and write all the words that were on the roll which King Jehoiakim burned.
43:29 Say, Thus said the Lord: You burned this roll, saying, Why did you write in it that Babylon’s king will certainly enter and destroy this land, and people and animals will cease from it?
43:30 Therefore thus said the Lord concerning Jehoiakim king of Judah: He will have no one sitting on David’s throne. His carcass will be thrown out in the day’s heat and the night’s frost.
43:31 I will visit him, his family, and his servants, and bring upon them, Jerusalem’s inhabitants, and Judah’s land all the calamities I pronounced to them, but they did not listen.
43:32 Baruch took another roll and wrote on it, from Jeremiah’s mouth, all the words of the document Jehoiakim burned. Still more words like these were added to it.

JEREMIAH 44

44:1 Zedekiah son of Josiah reigned in place of Jehoiakim; Nebuchadnezzar had made him king over Judah.
44:2 Neither he nor his servants nor the people of the land obeyed the Lord’s words, which He spoke through Jeremiah.
44:3 King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest to Jeremiah, saying, Please pray for us to the Lord.
44:4 Jeremiah came and passed through the midst of the city; they had not put him in prison.
44:5 Pharaoh’s force came out of Egypt. The Chaldeans heard the report about them and withdrew from Jerusalem.
44:6 The Lord’s word came to Jeremiah:
44:7 Thus said the Lord: Tell Judah’s king, who sent to you to seek Me, Pharaoh’s force, which came out to help you, will return to Egypt’s land.
44:8 The Chaldeans themselves will return, fight this city, seize it, and burn it with fire.
44:9 For thus said the Lord: Do not suppose within yourselves, The Chaldeans will flee and leave us. They certainly will not leave.
44:10 Even if you struck the whole Chaldean force fighting you and some were left pierced through, each in his place, these would rise and burn this city with fire.
44:11 When the Chaldean force withdrew from Jerusalem because of Pharaoh’s force,
44:12 Jeremiah left Jerusalem to go to Benjamin’s land, to buy from there among the people.
44:13 At Benjamin’s Gate there was a man with whom he lodged, Sarouiah son of Shelemiah son of Hananiah. He arrested Jeremiah, saying, You are fleeing to the Chaldeans!
44:14 He said, A lie! I am not fleeing to the Chaldeans. But Sarouiah would not listen to him; he arrested Jeremiah and brought him to the rulers.
44:15 The rulers were embittered against Jeremiah. They struck him and sent him into the house of Jonathan the scribe, which they had made into a prison house.
44:16 Jeremiah entered the pit-house and the chereth and stayed there many days.
44:17 Zedekiah sent and summoned him. The king secretly asked him whether there was a word from the Lord. He said, There is. You will be handed over to Babylon’s king.
44:18 Jeremiah said to the king, How have I wronged you, your servants, or this people, that you are putting me in prison?
44:19 Where are your prophets who prophesied to you, saying, Babylon’s king certainly will not come against this land?
44:20 Now, lord king, let my appeal for mercy fall before you. Why send me back to Jonathan the scribe’s house—and will I not die there?
44:21 The king gave orders, and they put him into the prison house, giving him one loaf a day from outside where they bake, until the city’s loaves ran out. Jeremiah remained in the prison court.

JEREMIAH 45

45:1 Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, and Jehucal son of Shelemiah heard the words Jeremiah was speaking to the people:
45:2 Thus said the Lord: Whoever remains in this city will die by sword and famine. Whoever goes out to the Chaldeans will live; his life will be his gain, and he will live.
45:3 For thus said the Lord: This city will certainly be handed over to the force of Babylon’s king, and he will seize it.
45:4 They said to the king, Let that man be killed! By speaking these words to them he weakens the hands of the fighters left in the city and of all the people. This man delivers oracles of evil to this people, not peace.
45:5 The king said, He is in your hands—for the king was unable to oppose them.
45:6 They threw him into the pit of Malchiah the king’s son in the prison court and lowered him into the pit. There was no water in it, only mire, and he was in the mire.
45:7 Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, who was in the king’s house, heard that they had put Jeremiah in the pit. The king was at Benjamin’s Gate.
45:8 He went out to him, spoke to the king, and said:
45:9 You have acted wickedly in what you did, killing this man through hunger, for there are no more loaves in the city.
45:10 The king instructed Ebed-melech, Take thirty men from here under your command and bring him up from the pit so that he does not die.
45:11 Ebed-melech took the men and entered the king’s underground house. He took old rags and old ropes from there and threw them down to Jeremiah in the pit.
45:12 He said, Put these beneath the ropes. Jeremiah did so.
45:13 They pulled him with the ropes and brought him up from the pit. Jeremiah remained in the prison court.
45:14 The king sent and summoned him to himself, to the aselisi house in the Lord’s house. The king said to him, I will ask you something. Please hide no word from me.
45:15 Jeremiah said to the king, If I tell you, will you not certainly put me to death? And if I advise you, you certainly will not listen to me.
45:16 The king swore to him, As the Lord lives, who made this life for us, I will not kill you or hand you over to these men.
45:17 Jeremiah said to him, Thus said the Lord: If you go out to Babylon’s king’s commanders, your life will be spared and this city will certainly not be burned with fire. You and your household will live.
45:18 But if you do not go out, this city will be handed over to the Chaldeans and they will burn it with fire. You certainly will not be saved.
45:19 The king said to Jeremiah, I am concerned about the Judeans who have fled to the Chaldeans, lest they hand me over to them and mock me.
45:20 Jeremiah said, They certainly will not hand you over. Hear the Lord’s word which I speak to you, and it will be better for you; your life will be spared.
45:21 But if you are unwilling to go out, this is the word the Lord showed me:
45:22 All the women left in Judah’s king’s house were being brought out to Babylon’s king’s rulers. They said, Your peaceful friends deceived you and will overpower you. They will bring your feet to rest in slippery places; they turned away from you.
45:23 They will bring your wives and children out to the Chaldeans. You certainly will not be saved, for Babylon’s king will seize you, and this city will be burned.
45:24 The king said to him, Let no one learn anything of these words, and you certainly will not die.
45:25 If the rulers hear that I spoke to you, come to you, and say, Tell us what the king said to you. Hide nothing from us and we certainly will not kill you. What did the king say to you?
45:26 tell them, I am casting my appeal for mercy before the king’s eyes, not to send me back to Jonathan’s house to die there.
45:27 All the rulers came to Jeremiah and questioned him. He reported everything the king had instructed him, and they fell silent, because the Lord’s word had not been heard.
45:28 Jeremiah remained in the prison court until the time Jerusalem was captured.

JEREMIAH 46

46:1 In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his whole force arrived against Jerusalem and besieged it.
46:2 In Zedekiah’s eleventh year, on the ninth day of the fourth month, the city was breached.
46:3 All Babylon’s king’s commanders entered and sat at the Middle Gate: Nargalasar, Samagoth, Nabousachar, Nabousaris, Nagargasnaser Rabamag, and the remaining commanders of Babylon’s king.
46:14 They sent and took Jeremiah from the prison court and handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan. They brought him out, and he lived among the people.
46:15 The Lord’s word came to Jeremiah in the prison court:
46:16 Go and tell Ebed-melech the Ethiopian, Thus said the Lord, Israel’s God: I am bringing My words upon this city for calamity, not good.
46:17 I will save you that day and certainly will not hand you over to the men you fear.
46:18 I will certainly save you. You certainly will not fall by the sword; your life will be your gain because you trusted in Me, says the Lord.

JEREMIAH 47

47:1 The word that came from the Lord to Jeremiah afterward, after Nebuzaradan, the chief guard from Daman, sent him away, when he took him in handcuffs among Judah’s exiles who were being led to Babylon.
47:2 The chief guard took him and said to him, The Lord your God spoke these calamities against this place.
47:3 And the Lord acted, because you sinned against Him and did not obey His voice.
47:4 Behold, I have released you from the handcuffs on your hands. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will look after you.
47:5 But if not, go off and return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, whom Babylon’s king appointed in Judah’s land, and live with him among the people in Judah’s land. Go wherever it seems good in your eyes to go. The chief guard gave him gifts and sent him away.
47:6 He came to Gedaliah at Mizpah and lived among the people left in the land.
47:7 All the commanders of the force in the countryside, they and their men, heard that Babylon’s king had appointed Gedaliah in the land and had entrusted to him men and their wives whom he had not deported to Babylon.
47:8 Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ophe the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite came to Gedaliah at Mizpah, they and their men.
47:9 Gedaliah swore to them and their men, saying, Do not be afraid of the Chaldeans’ servants. Live in the land and work for Babylon’s king, and it will be better for you.
47:10 Behold, I am staying before you at Mizpah to stand before the Chaldeans who come against you. Gather wine and summer fruit, gather oil, put them in your containers, and live in the cities you have taken over.
47:11 All the Judeans in Moab’s land, among Ammon’s people, in Idumea, and in all the land heard that Babylon’s king had left a remnant for Judah and appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over them.
47:12 They came to Gedaliah in Judah’s land at Mizpah and gathered a very great amount of wine and summer fruit, and oil.
47:13 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the force in the countryside came to Gedaliah at Mizpah.
47:14 They said to him, Do you really know that Belisa king of Ammon’s people has sent Ishmael to you to take your life? But Gedaliah did not believe them.
47:15 Johanan spoke secretly to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Let me go and strike down Ishmael, and let no one know, lest he take your life and all Judah gathered to you be scattered, and Judah’s survivors perish.
47:16 Gedaliah said to Johanan, Do not do this thing, for you are speaking falsehoods about Ishmael.

JEREMIAH 48

48:1 In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah son of Elasa, of the king’s family, came with ten men to Gedaliah at Mizpah. They ate bread together there.
48:2 Ishmael and the ten men with him rose and struck Gedaliah, whom Babylon’s king had appointed over the land,
48:3 and all the Judeans with him at Mizpah, and all the Chaldeans found there.
48:4 On the second day after he struck Gedaliah, when no one knew,
48:5 eighty men came from Shechem, Salem, and Samaria, with shaved beards and torn clothes, beating themselves, carrying grain offerings and frankincense to bring to the Lord’s house.
48:6 Ishmael went out to meet them. They were walking and weeping, and he said to them, Come in to Gedaliah.
48:7 When they entered the middle of the city, he slaughtered them into the cistern.
48:8 Ten men found there said to Ishmael, Do not kill us, for we have stores in the countryside: wheat, barley, honey, and oil. He passed them by and did not kill them among their brothers.
48:9 The cistern into which Ishmael threw all those he had struck down is a large cistern that King Asa made because of Baasha king of Israel. Ishmael filled it with the slain.
48:10 Ishmael turned back all the people left at Mizpah and the king’s daughters whom the chief guard had entrusted to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, and he went away to the far side of Ammon’s people.
48:11 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the force with him heard of all the evils Ishmael had done.
48:12 They led their entire army and went away to fight him. They found him beside abundant water at Gibeon.
48:13 When all the people with Ishmael saw Johanan and the commanders of the force with him,
48:14 they returned to Johanan.
48:15 Ishmael escaped with eight men and went away to Ammon’s people.
48:16 Johanan and all the commanders of the force with him took all the people left whom he had brought back from Ishmael: men mighty in war, the women, the rest, and the eunuchs whom he brought back from Gibeon.
48:17 They went away and stayed at Gaberoth-chamaam near Bethlehem, intending to go into Egypt,
48:18 away from the Chaldeans, for they feared them because Ishmael had struck Gedaliah, whom Babylon’s king had appointed in the land.

JEREMIAH 49

49:1 All the commanders of the force, Johanan, Azariah son of Maaseiah, and all the people, from the least to the greatest, approached
49:2 Jeremiah the prophet and said to him, Please let our appeal for mercy come before you. Pray to the Lord your God for these survivors, for few of us remain out of many, as your eyes see.
49:3 Let the Lord your God tell us the way we should go and the matter we should carry out.
49:4 Jeremiah said to them, I have heard. I will pray to the Lord our God as you have said. Whatever word the Lord answers, I will tell you; I certainly will not hide a word from you.
49:5 They said to Jeremiah, May the Lord be a righteous and faithful witness among us if we do not act according to every word with which the Lord sends you to us.
49:6 Whether good or bad, we will obey the voice of the Lord our God, to whom we are sending you, so that it may be better for us, because we will obey the voice of the Lord our God.
49:7 After ten days, the Lord’s word came to Jeremiah.
49:8 He called Johanan, the commanders of the force, and all the people, from the least to the greatest.
49:9 He said to them, Thus said the Lord:
49:10 If you remain in this land, I will build you up and certainly not tear you down; I will plant you and certainly not pluck you up. For I have rested from the calamities I brought upon you.
49:11 Do not fear Babylon’s king, whom you fear. Do not fear, says the Lord, for I am with you to rescue you and save you from his hand.
49:12 I will grant you mercy, have mercy on you, and return you to your land.
49:13 But if you say, We certainly will not remain in this land, so as not to obey the Lord’s voice,
49:14 because we will enter Egypt’s land, certainly not see war or hear the trumpet’s sound or hunger for bread, and there we will live—
49:15 therefore hear the Lord’s word. Thus said the Lord: If you set your faces toward Egypt and go in to live there,
49:16 the sword you fear will find you in Egypt’s land, and the famine you are concerned about will catch up with you from behind in Egypt, and there you will die.
49:17 All the people and all the foreigners who set their faces toward Egypt’s land to live there will perish by sword and famine. None of them will be saved from the calamities I am bringing upon them.
49:18 For thus said the Lord: As My wrath dripped upon Jerusalem’s inhabitants, so My wrath will drip upon you when you enter Egypt. You will become an untrodden place, subject to others, a curse, and a reproach, and you certainly will never see this place again.
49:19 These are the things the Lord spoke concerning you, Judah’s survivors: Do not enter Egypt. And now know for certain
49:20 that you acted wickedly in your souls when you sent me, saying, Pray for us to the Lord, and we will do everything the Lord tells you.
49:21 And you did not obey the Lord’s voice, with which He sent me to you.
49:22 And now you will perish by sword and famine in the place where you want to go and live.

JEREMIAH 50

50:1 When Jeremiah stopped speaking to the people all the Lord’s words, with which the Lord had sent him to them—all these words—
50:2 Azariah son of Maaseiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the men who spoke said to Jeremiah, You are lying! The Lord did not send you to us to say, Do not enter Egypt to live there.
50:3 Rather, Baruch son of Neriah is setting you against us, so that you will hand us over to the Chaldeans, to put us to death and have us deported to Babylon.
50:4 Johanan, all the commanders of the force, and all the people did not obey the Lord’s voice by remaining in Judah’s land.
50:5 Johanan and all the commanders of the force took all Judah’s survivors who had returned to live in the land:
50:6 the mighty men, the women, the little children, the king’s daughters, the people Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, and Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.
50:7 They entered Egypt, for they had not obeyed the Lord’s voice. They entered Taphnas.
50:8 The Lord’s word came to Jeremiah at Taphnas:
50:9 Take large stones for yourself and hide them in the forecourt at the gate of Pharaoh’s house at Taphnas, before the eyes of Judah’s men.
50:10 Say, Thus said the Lord: I am sending and will bring Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will place his throne above these stones that you hid and raise his weapons over them.
50:11 He will enter and strike Egypt’s land: those for death, to death; those for deportation, to deportation; and those for the sword, to the sword.
50:12 He will kindle fire in the houses of their gods, burn the houses, and deport the gods. He will delouse Egypt’s land as a shepherd delouses his garment, and he will go out in peace.
50:13 He will shatter the pillars of the City of the Sun, those in On, and burn their houses with fire.

JEREMIAH 51

51:1 The word that came to Jeremiah for all the Judeans living in Egypt’s land and those staying at Migdol, Taphnas, and in Pathoures’s land:
51:2 Thus said the Lord, Israel’s God: You have seen all the calamities I brought upon Jerusalem and Judah’s cities. Behold, they are deserted of inhabitants,
51:3 because of the wickedness they committed to embitter Me, going to burn incense to other gods whom you did not know.
51:4 I sent you My servants the prophets at dawn, and I sent them saying, Do not commit this act of defilement that I hate.
51:5 But they did not listen to Me or incline their ear to turn from their evils and stop burning incense to other gods.
51:6 My anger and wrath dripped out and blazed in Judah’s cities and outside Jerusalem, and they became a desolation and an untrodden place, as on this day.
51:7 Now thus said the Lord Almighty: Why are you doing great evils against yourselves, cutting off from yourselves man and woman, little child and nursing infant from Judah, so that none of you remains,
51:8 embittering Me with the work of your hands by burning incense to other gods in Egypt’s land, which you entered to live there, so that you are cut off and become a curse and a reproach among all the earth’s nations?
51:9 Have you forgotten your fathers’ evils, the evils of Judah’s kings, your rulers’ evils, and your wives’ evils, which they committed in Judah’s land and outside Jerusalem?
51:10 They have not stopped to this day and have not held fast to My ordinances, which I set before their fathers.
51:11 Therefore thus said the Lord: I am setting My face
51:12 to destroy all the survivors in Egypt, and they will fall. By sword and famine they will perish, from the least to the greatest, and become a reproach, a destruction, and a curse.
51:13 I will visit those staying in Egypt’s land as I visited Jerusalem, by sword, famine, and death.
51:14 None of Judah’s remainder residing in Egypt’s land will be saved to return to Judah’s land, where they hope in their souls to return. They certainly will not return except those rescued.
51:15 All the men who knew that their wives burned incense to other gods, all the women, a great assembly, and all the people staying in Egypt’s land at Pathoures answered Jeremiah:
51:16 As for the word you spoke to us in the Lord’s name, we will not listen to you.
51:17 We will certainly do everything that will come from our mouths: burn incense to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, as we, our fathers, our kings, and our rulers did in Judah’s cities and outside Jerusalem. We were filled with bread, were well off, and saw no calamities.
51:18 But when we stopped burning incense to the queen of heaven, we were all diminished and perished by sword and famine.
51:19 And when we burn incense to the queen of heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, did we make cakes for her and pour out drink offerings to her without our husbands?
51:20 Jeremiah said to all the people—to the mighty men, the women, and all the people who had answered him:
51:21 Was it not the incense you burned in Judah’s cities and outside Jerusalem—you, your fathers, your kings, your rulers, and the people of the land—that the Lord remembered, and did it not come into His heart?
51:22 The Lord could no longer bear the wickedness of your actions and the abominations you had committed. Your land became a desolation, an untrodden place, and a curse, as on this day,
51:23 because you burned incense, sinned against the Lord, did not obey the Lord’s voice, and did not walk in His ordinances, law, and testimonies. These calamities have seized you.
51:24 Jeremiah said to the people and the women, Hear the Lord’s word:
51:25 Thus said the Lord, Israel’s God: You women spoke with your mouths and fulfilled it with your hands, saying, We will certainly carry out our pledges that we pledged, to burn incense to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings to her. You firmly persisted in your pledges and indeed carried them out.
51:26 Therefore hear the Lord’s word, all Judah staying in Egypt’s land: I have sworn by My great name, said the Lord, that My name will no longer be in any Judean’s mouth to say, As the Lord, the Lord, lives, throughout Egypt’s land.
51:27 For I am watching over them to harm them, not do them good. All Judah living in Egypt’s land will perish by sword and famine until they perish.
51:28 Those saved from the sword will return to Judah’s land, few in number. Judah’s survivors who settled in Egypt’s land to live there will know whose word will endure.
51:29 This will be the sign for you that I will visit you for evils:
51:30 Thus said the Lord: I am handing Ouaphre king of Egypt over to his enemy and those seeking his life, as I handed Zedekiah king of Judah over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy who sought his life.
51:31 The word Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah when he wrote these words in the scroll from Jeremiah’s mouth, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:
51:32 Thus said the Lord concerning you, Baruch:
51:33 For you said, Woe to me, woe to me! The Lord has added toil to my pain. I lay down amid groanings and found no rest.
51:34 Tell him, Thus said the Lord: Those I have built up I am tearing down, and those I have planted I am plucking up.
51:35 Are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them, for I am bringing calamities upon all flesh, says the Lord. I will give your life as gain wherever you walk.

JEREMIAH 52

52:1 Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Amitaal daughter of Jeremiah, from Lobena.
52:4 In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his force came against Jerusalem. They enclosed her with a palisade and built around her with squared stones.
52:5 The city was under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.
52:6 On the ninth of the month, the famine grew severe in the city, and there were no loaves for the people of the land.
52:7 The city was breached, and all the warriors went out at night by the gate between the wall and the outer wall beside the king’s garden, while the Chaldeans surrounded the city. They went by the road to Arabah,
52:8 and the Chaldean force pursued the king and overtook him beyond Jericho. All his servants scattered away from him.
52:9 They captured the king and brought him to the king of Babylon at Deblatha, and he spoke to him in judgment.
52:10 The king of Babylon slaughtered Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes, and he slaughtered all Judah’s rulers at Deblatha.
52:11 He blinded Zedekiah’s eyes and bound him in fetters. The king of Babylon brought him to Babylon and put him in a mill house until the day he died.
52:12 In the fifth month, on the tenth of the month, Nebuzaradan, the chief guard who stood before the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.
52:13 He burned the Lord’s house, the king’s house, and all the city’s houses; every great house he burned with fire.
52:14 The Chaldean force with the chief guard pulled down the whole wall of Jerusalem all around.
52:16 The chief guard left the remaining people as vinedressers and farmers.
52:17 The Chaldeans shattered the bronze pillars in the Lord’s house, the bases, and the bronze Sea in the Lord’s house. They took their bronze and carried it to Babylon.
52:18 And the rim, the bowls, the meat forks, and all the bronze utensils with which they performed service,
52:19 and the saphphoth, the masmaroth, the pouring vessels, the lampstands, the incense bowls, and the ladles—those of gold, gold, and those of silver, silver—the chief guard took.
52:20 The two pillars, the one Sea, and the twelve bronze calves beneath the Sea, which King Solomon made for the Lord’s house—their bronze was beyond weighing.
52:21 As for the pillars, one pillar was thirty-five cubits high, and a cord of twelve cubits encircled it. Its thickness was four fingerbreadths all around,
52:22 and a bronze cornice was on them. The projection of one cornice was five cubits in length, and a net and pomegranates surrounded the cornice, all of bronze. The second pillar had the same: eight pomegranates per cubit over the twelve cubits.
52:23 There were ninety-six pomegranates on one side; all the pomegranates around the net numbered one hundred.
52:24 The chief guard took the first priest, the second priest, and the three men guarding the road,
52:25 and one eunuch who supervised the warriors, seven prominent men in the king’s presence who were found in the city, the scribe of the forces who kept records for the people of the land, and sixty people from the people of the land found inside the city.
52:26 Nebuzaradan, the chief guard, took them and brought them to the king of Babylon at Deblatha,
52:27 and the king of Babylon struck them at Deblatha in Aimath’s land.
52:31 In the thirty-seventh year after Ioakim king of Judah had been deported, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-fourth of the month, Oulaimaradach king of Babylon, in the year he became king, took up the head of Ioakim king of Judah and brought him out of the house where he was guarded.
52:32 He spoke kindly to him and placed his throne above the thrones of the kings with him in Babylon.
52:33 He changed his prison robe, and he ate bread continually before him all the days he lived.
52:34 His allowance was given to him continually by the king of Babylon, from day to day until the day he died.
