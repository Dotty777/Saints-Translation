Ephesians 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints who are [in Ephesus] and faithful in Christ Jesus:

1:2grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Spiritual Blessings in Christ

1:3Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,

1:4just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, so that we should be holy and without blemish before Him in love,

1:5having predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,

1:6to the praise of the glory of His grace, with which He favored us in the Beloved.

1:7In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of transgressions, according to the riches of His grace,

1:8which He lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding,

1:9having made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure which He purposed in Him,

1:10for an administration of the fullness of the times, to sum up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth, in Him.

1:11In Him we were also allotted an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of the One who works all things according to the counsel of His will,

1:12so that we who had hoped beforehand in Christ should be to the praise of His glory.

1:13In Him you also, having heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and having believed in Him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,

1:14who is the pledge of our inheritance, toward the redemption of the possession, to the praise of His glory.

Prayer for Wisdom and Revelation

1:15For this reason I too, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love toward all the saints,

1:16do not cease giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,

1:17so that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him,

1:18with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance among the saints,

1:19and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of His strength,

1:20which He exercised in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places,

1:21far above every rule and authority and power and dominion and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come.

1:22And He subjected all things under His feet and gave Him as head over all things to the Church,

1:23which is His body, the fullness of the One who fills all things in all.

Ephesians 1:7 · redemption

Meaning. Release expressed as redemption and explained through forgiveness.

Decision. Retain redemption, blood, forgiveness of transgressions, and riches of grace. Explain the noun without assigning an unstated ransom recipient.

Comparison. Saints and KJV forgiveness; DRC remission. Saints transgressions is more specific than the historical sins.

Excluded. blood imagery omitted for smoother abstraction

Ephesians 2

Read this chapter on staging

Dead in Trespasses

2:1And you were dead in your transgressions and sins,

2:2in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit now working in the sons of disobedience—

2:3among whom we too all once lived in the desires of our flesh, carrying out the wishes of the flesh and of our thoughts, and we were by nature children of wrath, like the rest.

Made Alive with Christ

2:4But God, being rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us,

2:5even when we were dead in transgressions, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—

2:6and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,

2:7so that in the ages to come He might show the surpassing riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

2:8For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God,

2:9not from works, so that no one may boast.

2:10For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, so that we should walk in them.

One New Humanity in Christ

2:11Therefore remember that once you, the Gentiles in the flesh, called “uncircumcision” by what is called “circumcision,” made by hands in the flesh—

2:12that at that time you were without Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.

2:13But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far away have become near by the blood of Christ.

2:14For He Himself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the dividing wall of the barrier, the hostility, in His flesh,

2:15having abolished the law of commandments in decrees, so that He might create in Him one new human being out of the two, making peace,

2:16and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, having killed the hostility by it.

2:17And He came and proclaimed peace to you who were far away, and peace to those who were near,

2:18because through Him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father.

Fellow Citizens with the Saints

2:19So then you are no longer strangers and resident foreigners, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of God’s household,

2:20having been built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus Himself being the cornerstone,

2:21in whom the whole building, fitted together, grows into a holy sanctuary in the Lord,

2:22in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.

Ephesians 2:8 · grace

Meaning. Grace named as the basis of the saving gift.

Decision. Retain the completed saving expression, through faith, and not from yourselves. The neuter this should not be assigned exclusively to the feminine noun faith on grammatical gender alone.

Comparison. All three preserve grace, faith, and God's gift. Saints makes the completed passive construction explicit.

Excluded. a disputed antecedent concealed in the rendering

Ephesians 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Mystery Revealed

3:1For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for you Gentiles—

3:2if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of God’s grace that was given to me for you,

3:3that the mystery was made known to me by revelation, as I wrote briefly before.

3:4By reading this you can perceive my understanding of the mystery of Christ,

3:5which in other generations was not made known to the sons of human beings as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:

3:6that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the body and fellow sharers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,

3:7of which I became a servant according to the gift of God’s grace given to me according to the working of His power.

To Me, the Very Least of All the Saints

3:8To me, the very least of all saints, this grace was given: to proclaim to the nations the untraceable riches of Christ,

3:9and to enlighten everyone about the administration of the mystery hidden from the ages in God, who created all things,

3:10so that now, through the Church, the richly varied wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places,

3:11according to the purpose of the ages which He accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord,

3:12in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in Him.

3:13Therefore I ask you not to lose heart at my afflictions for you, which are your glory.

Prayer to Be Strengthened

3:14For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,

3:15from whom every family in the heavens and on earth is named,

3:16so that according to the riches of His glory He may grant you to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner person,

3:17so that Christ may dwell through faith in your hearts, being rooted and founded in love,

3:18so that with all the saints you may have strength to grasp what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

3:19and to know the love of Christ which surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.

3:20Now to the One who is able to do far more abundantly than all we ask or think, according to the power working in us,

3:21to Him be glory in the Church and in Christ Jesus for all generations of the age of ages. Amen.

Ephesians 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Unity of the Spirit

4:1Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called,

4:2with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,

4:3making every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace:

4:4one body and one Spirit, just as you were also called in one hope of your calling;

4:5one Lord, one faith, one baptism;

4:6one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.

Gifts for Building Up the Body

4:7But grace was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ’s gift.

4:8Therefore it says, “Having ascended on high, He took captivity captive; He gave gifts to human beings.”

4:9Now what does “He ascended” mean, except that He also descended into the lower parts of the earth?

4:10The One who descended is also the One who ascended far above all the heavens, so that He might fill all things.

4:11And He Himself gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as shepherds and teachers,

4:12for the equipping of the saints for the work of service, for building up the body of Christ,

4:13until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,

4:14so that we may no longer be children, tossed by waves and carried about by every wind of teaching, by human trickery, by cunning in deceitful schemes,

4:15but, being truthful in love, we may grow in all things into Him who is the head, Christ,

4:16from whom the whole body, fitted and held together through every supporting joint, as each individual part works in its proper measure, produces the growth of the body for building itself up in love.

The Old Life Put Off

4:17So I say this and testify in the Lord: you must no longer walk as the Gentiles also walk, in the futility of their mind,

4:18being darkened in understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance in them, because of the hardness of their heart.

4:19Having become unfeeling, they handed themselves over to licentiousness for the practice of every impurity in greed.

4:20But you did not learn Christ in this way,

4:21if indeed you heard Him and were taught in Him, as truth is in Jesus:

4:22that, concerning your former conduct, you put off the old human being, which is being corrupted according to deceitful desires,

4:23and be renewed in the spirit of your mind,

4:24and put on the new human being, created according to God in righteousness and holiness of truth.

The New Life Put On

4:25Therefore, putting away falsehood, let each speak truth with his neighbor, because we are members of one another.

4:26Be angry and do not sin. Do not let the sun go down upon your anger,

4:27nor give a place to the devil.

4:28Let the one who steals steal no longer, but rather let him labor, doing what is good with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with the one in need.

4:29Let no rotten word come out of your mouth, but only what is good for building up as needed, so that it may give grace to those who hear.

4:30And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.

4:31Let all bitterness and rage and anger and shouting and slander be taken away from you, with all malice.

4:32But be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you.

Comparison. Saints equipping and service; KJV and DRC perfecting and ministry.

Ephesians 5

Read this chapter on staging

Imitators of God

5:1Therefore become imitators of God, as beloved children,

5:2and walk in love, just as Christ also loved us and gave Himself for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for a fragrant aroma.

Children of Light

5:3But sexual immorality and every impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints,

5:4nor shamefulness and foolish talk or coarse joking, which are unfitting, but rather thanksgiving.

5:5For know this with certainty: no sexually immoral or impure or greedy person, that is, an idolater, has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.

5:6Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

5:7Therefore do not become their fellow sharers.

5:8For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light,

5:9for the fruit of the light is in all goodness and righteousness and truth,

5:10testing what is pleasing to the Lord.

5:11And do not share in the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them,

5:12for it is shameful even to speak of the things done by them in secret.

5:13But all things, when exposed by the light, become visible,

5:14for everything that becomes visible is light. Therefore it says, “Awake, you who sleep, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine upon you.”

Walk Carefully

5:15So watch carefully how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,

5:16buying up the opportunity, because the days are evil.

5:17For this reason do not become foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

5:18And do not become drunk with wine, in which there is reckless excess, but be filled in the Spirit,

5:19speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord,

5:20always giving thanks for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God the Father,

Wives and Husbands

5:21submitting to one another in the fear of Christ:

5:22wives, to your own husbands as to the Lord,

5:23because a husband is head of the wife as Christ also is head of the Church, He Himself being Savior of the body.

5:24But as the Church submits to Christ, so also wives to their husbands in everything.

Husbands, Love Your Wives

5:25Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the Church and gave Himself for her,

5:26so that He might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with a word,

5:27so that He Himself might present the Church to Himself glorious, having no spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but so that she should be holy and without blemish.

5:28In this way husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. The one who loves his own wife loves himself.

5:29For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ also does the Church,

5:30because we are members of His body.

5:31“For this reason a man will leave father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.”

5:32This mystery is great, but I am speaking with reference to Christ and the Church.

5:33Nevertheless, let each one of you also love his own wife as himself, and let the wife respect her husband.

Ephesians 6

Read this chapter on staging

Children and Parents

6:1Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.

6:2“Honor your father and mother”—which is the first commandment with a promise—

6:3“so that it may be well with you and you may live long upon the earth.”

6:4And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but nourish them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.

Slaves and Masters

6:5Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in the sincerity of your heart, as you would Christ,

6:6not serving only when their eyes are on you, as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the soul,

6:7serving with goodwill, as to the Lord and not to human beings,

6:8knowing that whatever good each one does, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free.

6:9And masters, act in the same way toward them, giving up threats, knowing that both their Master and yours is in the heavens, and there is no partiality with Him.

The Whole Armor of God

6:10Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of His strength.

6:11Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.

6:12For our wrestling is not against blood and flesh, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly places.

6:13For this reason take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand in the evil day and, having accomplished everything, to stand.

6:14Stand, therefore, having fastened truth around your waist and put on the breastplate of righteousness,

6:15and having shod your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace,

6:16in all circumstances taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.

6:17And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,

6:18with every prayer and petition, praying at every time in the Spirit, and to this end keeping watch with all perseverance and petition for all the saints,

6:19and for me, so that a word may be given to me when I open my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,

6:20for which I serve as an ambassador bound by a chain, so that in it I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.

Tychicus Sent and Final Blessing

6:21Now so that you also may know my circumstances, what I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make everything known to you.

6:22I sent him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know about us and he may encourage your hearts.

6:23Peace to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

6:24Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruptibility.
