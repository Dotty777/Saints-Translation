## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved the bracketed in Ephesus, repeated blessing/glory/grace, adoption, blood, inheritance, sealing/pledge, heart’s eyes, the several power terms and head/body/fullness images. Smoothed abundance and prayer wording. At 1:10–11 kept both in-Him expressions while joining the first to 1:10, as in the displayed NA28 punctuation, and beginning 1:11 with an English main clause. Retained your at 1:18, bracketed in NA28.

Pending: Review in Ephesus and the address syntax, the attachment of in love and in all wisdom, administration/fullness, allotted inheritance versus being made an inheritance, possession in 1:14, spirit in 1:17 and the fullness construction in 1:23. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved dead/alive, walking, age/world/air, flesh, wrath, the together repetitions, completed have been saved, workmanship, blood, peace/hostility, two/one and household/building images. Smoothed the opening sentence and wishes clause, replaced ordinary ye, and clarified the new-human-being construction. Retained this at 2:8 without naming a disputed antecedent. At 2:15, in Him reflects the consulted NA28 pronoun; the earlier in Himself is archived and its possible reflexive sense remains noted.

Pending: Review age of this world and the ruler/air syntax, the scope of this in 2:8, the placement of in His flesh and hostility in 2:14–15, decrees, and whole/every building in 2:21. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved the interrupted opening, mystery/stewardship, three fellow relationships, untraceable riches, varied wisdom, knees/family, roots/foundation, dimensions and fullness. Modernized has and smoothed several phrases and the doxology. Retained Jesus at 3:1 and everyone at 3:9, bracketed in NA28, and the shorter Father wording at 3:14. Kept the inherited provisional faith in Him at 3:12. Added that at 3:3 for bracketed NA28 hoti, with a comma at 3:2 to connect the clause.

Pending: Review stewardship/administration, faith in Him versus His faithfulness, the subject of not losing heart, every/whole family and the dimensions/fullness language. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved all seven one declarations, captivity captive, descent/ascent, the gifts and service clauses, mature man, waves/wind, head/body/joints/measure, walking, old/new humanity, clothing, rotten speech and sealing. Smoothed selected exhortations, cunning and the body-growth clause. Retained parts at 4:9 and own at 4:28 from bracketed NA28 wording; included bracketed But at 4:32.

Pending: Review the lower parts of the earth, shepherds/teachers and the service-clause relationships, stature/age, the joint/supply construction, truthfulness in 4:15 and holiness of truth in 4:24. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserved imitation, walking, offering/aroma, darkness/light, fruit, buying up opportunity, songs, heart, submission to one another, washing, body/bride, spot/wrinkle and two/one flesh. Modernized third-person verbs, rendered fellow partakers as fellow sharers, and clarified dissipation as reckless excess. Retained fruit of light at 5:9, fear of Christ at 5:21, the verbless continuation at 5:22, and the shorter 5:30. Kept in at 5:19 and also at 5:28, bracketed in NA28.

Pending: Review the light/visibility construction, be filled in/by/with the Spirit, reciprocal submission and the 5:22 ellipsis, washing with a word, and respect/fear at 5:33. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Preserved children/parents and slaves/masters, fear/trembling, eyes, soul, no partiality, blood and flesh, repeated against, standing and every armor item, all/every prayer language and the chain. Modernized does and smoothed eye-service, reciprocity, waist-fastening, petition and bound by a chain. Retained in the Lord at 6:1, bracketed in NA28, the shorter 6:10 opening and the closing incorruptibility without adding Amen.

Pending: Review the historical household language, first commandment with a promise, readiness at 6:15, in all circumstances, in it at 6:20 and the attachment and sense of incorruptibility at 6:24. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.
