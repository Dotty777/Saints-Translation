EPHESIANS 1

1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints who are [in Ephesus] and faithful in Christ Jesus:
1:2 grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,
1:4 just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, so that we should be holy and without blemish before Him in love,
1:5 having predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,
1:6 to the praise of the glory of His grace, with which He favored us in the Beloved.
1:7 In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of transgressions, according to the riches of His grace,
1:8 which He lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding,
1:9 having made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure which He purposed in Him,
1:10 for an administration of the fullness of the times, to sum up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth, in Him.
1:11 In Him we were also allotted an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of the One who works all things according to the counsel of His will,
1:12 so that we who had hoped beforehand in Christ should be to the praise of His glory.
1:13 In Him you also, having heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and having believed in Him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,
1:14 who is the pledge of our inheritance, toward the redemption of the possession, to the praise of His glory.
1:15 For this reason I too, having heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love toward all the saints,
1:16 do not cease giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,
1:17 so that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him,
1:18 with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance among the saints,
1:19 and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of His strength,
1:20 which He exercised in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places,
1:21 far above every rule and authority and power and dominion and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come.
1:22 And He subjected all things under His feet and gave Him as head over all things to the Church,
1:23 which is His body, the fullness of the One who fills all things in all.

EPHESIANS 2

2:1 And you were dead in your transgressions and sins,
2:2 in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, of the spirit now working in the sons of disobedience—
2:3 among whom we too all once lived in the desires of our flesh, carrying out the wishes of the flesh and of our thoughts, and we were by nature children of wrath, like the rest.
2:4 But God, being rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us,
2:5 even when we were dead in transgressions, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—
2:6 and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,
2:7 so that in the ages to come He might show the surpassing riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.
2:8 For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God,
2:9 not from works, so that no one may boast.
2:10 For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, so that we should walk in them.
2:11 Therefore remember that once you, the Gentiles in the flesh, called “uncircumcision” by what is called “circumcision,” made by hands in the flesh—
2:12 that at that time you were without Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.
2:13 But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far away have become near by the blood of Christ.
2:14 For He Himself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the dividing wall of the barrier, the hostility, in His flesh,
2:15 having abolished the law of commandments in decrees, so that He might create in Him one new human being out of the two, making peace,
2:16 and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, having killed the hostility by it.
2:17 And He came and proclaimed peace to you who were far away, and peace to those who were near,
2:18 because through Him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father.
2:19 So then you are no longer strangers and resident foreigners, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of God’s household,
2:20 having been built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus Himself being the cornerstone,
2:21 in whom the whole building, fitted together, grows into a holy sanctuary in the Lord,
2:22 in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.

EPHESIANS 3

3:1 For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for you Gentiles—
3:2 if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of God’s grace that was given to me for you,
3:3 that the mystery was made known to me by revelation, as I wrote briefly before.
3:4 By reading this you can perceive my understanding of the mystery of Christ,
3:5 which in other generations was not made known to the sons of human beings as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:
3:6 that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the body and fellow sharers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,
3:7 of which I became a servant according to the gift of God’s grace given to me according to the working of His power.
3:8 To me, the very least of all saints, this grace was given: to proclaim to the nations the untraceable riches of Christ,
3:9 and to enlighten everyone about the administration of the mystery hidden from the ages in God, who created all things,
3:10 so that now, through the Church, the richly varied wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places,
3:11 according to the purpose of the ages which He accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord,
3:12 in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in Him.
3:13 Therefore I ask you not to lose heart at my afflictions for you, which are your glory.
3:14 For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,
3:15 from whom every family in the heavens and on earth is named,
3:16 so that according to the riches of His glory He may grant you to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner person,
3:17 so that Christ may dwell through faith in your hearts, being rooted and founded in love,
3:18 so that with all the saints you may have strength to grasp what is the breadth and length and height and depth,
3:19 and to know the love of Christ which surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.
3:20 Now to the One who is able to do far more abundantly than all we ask or think, according to the power working in us,
3:21 to Him be glory in the Church and in Christ Jesus for all generations of the age of ages. Amen.

EPHESIANS 4

4:1 Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called,
4:2 with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,
4:3 making every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace:
4:4 one body and one Spirit, just as you were also called in one hope of your calling;
4:5 one Lord, one faith, one baptism;
4:6 one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.
4:7 But grace was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ’s gift.
4:8 Therefore it says, “Having ascended on high, He took captivity captive; He gave gifts to human beings.”
4:9 Now what does “He ascended” mean, except that He also descended into the lower parts of the earth?
4:10 The One who descended is also the One who ascended far above all the heavens, so that He might fill all things.
4:11 And He Himself gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as shepherds and teachers,
4:12 for the equipping of the saints for the work of service, for building up the body of Christ,
4:13 until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,
4:14 so that we may no longer be children, tossed by waves and carried about by every wind of teaching, by human trickery, by cunning in deceitful schemes,
4:15 but, being truthful in love, we may grow in all things into Him who is the head, Christ,
4:16 from whom the whole body, fitted and held together through every supporting joint, as each individual part works in its proper measure, produces the growth of the body for building itself up in love.
4:17 So I say this and testify in the Lord: you must no longer walk as the Gentiles also walk, in the futility of their mind,
4:18 being darkened in understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance in them, because of the hardness of their heart.
4:19 Having become unfeeling, they handed themselves over to licentiousness for the practice of every impurity in greed.
4:20 But you did not learn Christ in this way,
4:21 if indeed you heard Him and were taught in Him, as truth is in Jesus:
4:22 that, concerning your former conduct, you put off the old human being, which is being corrupted according to deceitful desires,
4:23 and be renewed in the spirit of your mind,
4:24 and put on the new human being, created according to God in righteousness and holiness of truth.
4:25 Therefore, putting away falsehood, let each speak truth with his neighbor, because we are members of one another.
4:26 Be angry and do not sin. Do not let the sun go down upon your anger,
4:27 nor give a place to the devil.
4:28 Let the one who steals steal no longer, but rather let him labor, doing what is good with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with the one in need.
4:29 Let no rotten word come out of your mouth, but only what is good for building up as needed, so that it may give grace to those who hear.
4:30 And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.
4:31 Let all bitterness and rage and anger and shouting and slander be taken away from you, with all malice.
4:32 But be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you.

EPHESIANS 5

5:1 Therefore become imitators of God, as beloved children,
5:2 and walk in love, just as Christ also loved us and gave Himself for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for a fragrant aroma.
5:3 But sexual immorality and every impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints,
5:4 nor shamefulness and foolish talk or coarse joking, which are unfitting, but rather thanksgiving.
5:5 For know this with certainty: no sexually immoral or impure or greedy person, that is, an idolater, has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.
5:6 Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.
5:7 Therefore do not become their fellow sharers.
5:8 For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light,
5:9 for the fruit of the light is in all goodness and righteousness and truth,
5:10 testing what is pleasing to the Lord.
5:11 And do not share in the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them,
5:12 for it is shameful even to speak of the things done by them in secret.
5:13 But all things, when exposed by the light, become visible,
5:14 for everything that becomes visible is light. Therefore it says, “Awake, you who sleep, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine upon you.”
5:15 So watch carefully how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,
5:16 buying up the opportunity, because the days are evil.
5:17 For this reason do not become foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.
5:18 And do not become drunk with wine, in which there is reckless excess, but be filled in the Spirit,
5:19 speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord,
5:20 always giving thanks for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God the Father,
5:21 submitting to one another in the fear of Christ:
5:22 wives, to your own husbands as to the Lord,
5:23 because a husband is head of the wife as Christ also is head of the Church, He Himself being Savior of the body.
5:24 But as the Church submits to Christ, so also wives to their husbands in everything.
5:25 Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the Church and gave Himself for her,
5:26 so that He might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with a word,
5:27 so that He Himself might present the Church to Himself glorious, having no spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but so that she should be holy and without blemish.
5:28 In this way husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. The one who loves his own wife loves himself.
5:29 For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ also does the Church,
5:30 because we are members of His body.
5:31 “For this reason a man will leave father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.”
5:32 This mystery is great, but I am speaking with reference to Christ and the Church.
5:33 Nevertheless, let each one of you also love his own wife as himself, and let the wife respect her husband.

EPHESIANS 6

6:1 Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.
6:2 “Honor your father and mother”—which is the first commandment with a promise—
6:3 “so that it may be well with you and you may live long upon the earth.”
6:4 And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but nourish them in the discipline and admonition of the Lord.
6:5 Slaves, obey your masters according to the flesh with fear and trembling, in the sincerity of your heart, as you would Christ,
6:6 not serving only when their eyes are on you, as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the soul,
6:7 serving with goodwill, as to the Lord and not to human beings,
6:8 knowing that whatever good each one does, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free.
6:9 And masters, act in the same way toward them, giving up threats, knowing that both their Master and yours is in the heavens, and there is no partiality with Him.
6:10 Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the might of His strength.
6:11 Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.
6:12 For our wrestling is not against blood and flesh, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly places.
6:13 For this reason take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand in the evil day and, having accomplished everything, to stand.
6:14 Stand, therefore, having fastened truth around your waist and put on the breastplate of righteousness,
6:15 and having shod your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace,
6:16 in all circumstances taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.
6:17 And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,
6:18 with every prayer and petition, praying at every time in the Spirit, and to this end keeping watch with all perseverance and petition for all the saints,
6:19 and for me, so that a word may be given to me when I open my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,
6:20 for which I serve as an ambassador bound by a chain, so that in it I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.
6:21 Now so that you also may know my circumstances, what I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make everything known to you.
6:22 I sent him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know about us and he may encourage your hearts.
6:23 Peace to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
6:24 Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruptibility.
