PROVERBS 1

1:1 The proverbs of Solomon son of David, who reigned in Israel,
1:2 for knowing wisdom and discipline and understanding words of good sense,
1:3 for receiving turns of speech, understanding true righteousness, and directing judgment rightly,
1:4 so that he may give shrewdness to the innocent, perception and thought to a young child.
1:5 For a wise person who hears these things will become wiser, and the discerning will acquire guidance,
1:6 and understand a comparison and an obscure saying, the utterances of the wise and riddles.
1:7 The fear of God is the beginning of wisdom, and good understanding belongs to all who practice it. Reverence toward God is the beginning of perception, but the ungodly will despise wisdom and discipline.
1:8 Listen, son, to your father’s discipline, and do not reject your mother’s ordinances,
1:9 for you will receive a crown of graces for your head and a golden collar around your neck.
1:10 Son, do not let ungodly men lead you astray; do not agree if they invite you, saying,
1:11 Come with us; share in bloodshed. Let us hide a righteous man in the earth unjustly;
1:12 let us swallow him alive like Hades and remove his memory from the earth.
1:13 Let us seize his costly property and fill our houses with spoils.
1:14 Cast your lot among us; let us all obtain a common purse, and let there be one moneybag for us.
1:15 Do not walk in the way with them; turn your foot away from their paths,
1:16 for their feet run toward evil, and they are quick to shed blood.
1:17 For nets are not unjustly stretched out for winged creatures.
1:18 For those who share in murder store up evils for themselves, and the overthrow of lawless men is evil.
1:19 These are the ways of all who carry out lawless deeds, for through ungodliness they take away their own life.
1:20 Wisdom is celebrated at the exits, and in the squares she speaks openly.
1:21 At the tops of walls she is proclaimed; at rulers’ gates she sits nearby, and at city gates she speaks boldly:
1:22 As long as the innocent hold to righteousness, they will not be ashamed. But the senseless, desiring insolence, became ungodly and hated perception,
1:23 and became liable to reproofs. Behold, I will send forth to you an utterance of my breath, and I will teach you my word.
1:24 Since I kept calling and you did not obey, and kept extending words and you paid no attention,
1:25 but you were making my counsels ineffective and disobeyed my reproofs,
1:26 therefore I too will laugh at your ruin and exult when destruction comes to you,
1:27 and when sudden tumult reaches you and overthrow arrives like a storm, when affliction and siege come upon you, or when destruction comes to you.
1:28 For when you call upon me, I will not listen to you; evil people will seek me and will not find me,
1:29 for they hated wisdom and did not choose the fear of the Lord,
1:30 nor did they want to attend to my counsels, but they were sneering at my reproofs.
1:31 Therefore they will eat the fruits of their own way and be filled with their own ungodliness.
1:32 For because they wronged the innocent, they will be murdered, and examination will destroy the ungodly.
1:33 But whoever listens to me will dwell in hope and rest fearlessly from every evil.

PROVERBS 2

2:1 Son, if you receive an utterance of my commandment and hide it within yourself,
2:2 your ear will obey wisdom, and you will apply your heart to understanding; you will also apply it to admonishing your son.
2:3 for if you call upon wisdom and give your voice to understanding, and seek perception with a loud voice,
2:4 and if you seek it like silver and search it out like treasures,
2:5 then you will understand the fear of the Lord and find the knowledge of God.
2:6 For the Lord gives wisdom, and from His presence come knowledge and understanding.
2:7 He stores up salvation for those acting rightly; He will shield their journey,
2:8 to guard the ways of righteous ordinances, and He will protect the way of those who revere Him.
2:9 Then you will understand righteousness and judgment, and you will make all good courses straight.
2:10 For if wisdom enters your mind and perception seems good to your soul,
2:11 good counsel will guard you, and holy thought will keep you,
2:12 so that it may rescue you from an evil way and from a man who says nothing trustworthy.
2:13 O you who abandon straight ways to walk in ways of darkness,
2:14 who delight in evils and rejoice in evil distortion,
2:15 whose paths are crooked and whose tracks are bent,
2:16 to put you far from a straight way and make you alien to a righteous judgment.
2:17 Son, do not let evil counsel seize you, counsel that abandons the teaching of youth and has forgotten a divine covenant.
2:18 For she has set her house beside death and her courses beside Hades with the earthborn.
2:19 All who travel in her will not return, nor will they reach straight paths, for they are not reached by years of life.
2:20 For if they had walked good paths, they would have found smooth paths of righteousness.
2:21 Good people will inhabit the land, and the innocent will remain in it, for the upright will dwell in the land and the holy will remain in it.
2:22 The ways of the ungodly will perish from the land, and the lawless will be driven out of it.

PROVERBS 3

3:1 Son, do not forget my ordinances; let your heart keep my words,
3:2 for they will add length of life, years of living, and peace to you.
3:3 Do not let acts of mercy and faithfulness fail you. Fasten them around your neck, and you will find favor.
3:4 And take thought for good things before the Lord and people.
3:5 Trust God with your whole heart; do not exalt yourself in your own wisdom.
3:6 In all your ways acknowledge her, that she may cut your ways straight; and your foot will surely not stumble.
3:7 Do not be prudent in your own estimation; fear God and turn away from every evil.
3:8 Then there will be healing for your body and care for your bones.
3:9 Honor the Lord from your righteous labors, and offer Him firstfruits from your fruits of righteousness,
3:10 so that your storerooms may be filled with an abundance of grain and your vats gush with wine.
3:11 Son, do not make light of the Lord’s discipline or lose heart when corrected by Him,
3:12 for the Lord disciplines the one He loves and scourges every son He receives.
3:13 Blessed is the person who found wisdom, the mortal who saw good sense,
3:14 for trading in her is better than treasures of gold and silver.
3:15 She is more valuable than costly stones; nothing evil will stand against her. She is readily known to all who approach her, and nothing precious is worthy of her.
3:16 For length of life and years of living are in her right hand, and wealth and glory in her left.
3:16a Righteousness proceeds from her mouth, and she carries law and mercy on her tongue.
3:17 Her ways are good ways, and all her paths are in peace.
3:18 She is a tree of life to all who hold her fast, and secure for those who lean on her as on the Lord.
3:19 God founded the earth by wisdom and prepared the heavens by good sense.
3:20 By perception the deeps were split open, and clouds flowed with dew.
3:21 Son, do not drift away; keep my counsel and thought,
3:22 so that your soul may live and grace be around your neck.
3:22a There will be healing for your flesh and care for your bones,
3:23 so that you may walk all your ways confidently in peace, and your foot will certainly not stumble.
3:24 For if you sit, you will be fearless, and if you sleep, you will sleep pleasantly.
3:25 You will not fear an approaching fright or the oncoming assaults of the ungodly,
3:26 for the Lord will be upon all your ways and support your foot so that you are not shaken.
3:27 Do not refrain from doing good to someone in need whenever your hand has the means to help.
3:28 Do not say, ‘Go away and come back; tomorrow I will give,’ when you are able to do good, for you do not know what the coming day will bring forth.
3:29 Do not devise evils against your friend who lives nearby and trusts in you.
3:30 Do not seek enmity with anyone in vain, lest he do you some harm.
3:31 Do not acquire the reproaches of evil men or imitate their ways,
3:32 for every lawless person is unclean before the Lord, and he does not sit in council among the righteous.
3:33 God’s curse is in the houses of the ungodly, but the dwellings of the righteous are blessed.
3:34 The Lord opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.
3:35 The wise will inherit glory, but the ungodly raised up dishonor.

PROVERBS 4

4:1 Listen, children, to a father’s discipline; pay attention to gain discernment,
4:2 for I give you a good gift; do not abandon my law.
4:3 For I too became a son obedient to a father and loved in a mother’s presence,
4:4 who used to speak to me and teach me: Let our word take hold in your heart;
4:5 keep commandments; do not forget or disregard an utterance of my mouth,
4:6 and do not abandon her, and she will hold fast to you; love her, and she will keep you.
4:8 Fortify her, and she will exalt you; honor her so that she may embrace you,
4:9 so that she may give your head a crown of graces and shield you with a crown of delight.
4:10 Listen, son, and receive my words, and your years of life will multiply, so that many ways of living may be yours,
4:11 for I teach you ways of wisdom and lead you onto straight tracks.
4:12 For if you walk, your steps will not be shut in, and if you run, you will not grow weary.
4:13 Take hold of my discipline; do not release it, but keep it for yourself for your life.
4:14 Do not enter the ways of the ungodly or imitate the ways of the lawless.
4:15 Wherever they encamp, do not go there; turn away from them and pass by.
4:16 For they will not sleep unless they do harm; their sleep has been taken away, and they do not lie down.
4:17 For these people eat food of ungodliness and get drunk on lawless wine.
4:18 But the ways of the righteous shine like light; they go forward and illuminate until the day comes right.
4:19 But the ways of the ungodly are dark; they do not know how they stumble.
4:20 Son, attend to my utterance; apply your ear to my words,
4:21 so that your springs do not fail you; keep them in your heart,
4:22 for there is life for those who find them and healing for all flesh.
4:23 With every guard keep your heart, for from these come life’s outlets.
4:24 Remove a crooked mouth from yourself and thrust unjust lips far away from you.
4:25 Let your eyes look straight, and let your eyelids incline toward righteous things.
4:26 Make straight tracks for your feet and direct your ways rightly.
4:27 Do not turn to the right or the left; turn your foot away from an evil way.
4:27a For God knows the ways on the right, but those on the left are perverted.
4:27b He Himself will make your tracks straight and advance your journeys in peace.

PROVERBS 5

5:1 Son, attend to my wisdom, and incline your ear to my words,
5:2 so that you may guard good understanding. I command you to heed the discernment of my lips.
5:3 Do not attend to a base woman, for honey drips from a prostitute’s lips; for a time she oils your throat.
5:4 Afterward, however, you will find it more bitter than gall and sharper than a two-edged blade.
5:5 For folly’s feet lead those who engage with her down with death into Hades; her tracks are not firmly set.
5:6 For she does not travel life’s ways; her tracks are slippery and not easy to recognize.
5:7 Now therefore, son, listen to me, and do not make my words ineffective.
5:8 Put your way far from her; do not approach the doors of her houses,
5:9 so that you do not surrender your life to others and your livelihood to the merciless,
5:10 so that outsiders are not filled with your strength, and your labors enter outsiders’ houses,
5:11 and you will come to regret it at the end, when the flesh of your body is worn away,
5:12 and you will say, “How I hated discipline! My heart turned aside from rebukes.
5:13 I did not listen to the voice of the one disciplining and teaching me, nor incline my ear.
5:14 I was nearly in every kind of evil in the midst of the assembly and congregation.”
5:15 Drink waters from your vessels and from your spring wells.
5:16 Do not let waters overflow for you from your spring; but let your waters travel into your own squares.
5:17 Let them belong to you alone, and let no outsider share with you.
5:18 Let your fountain of water be your own, and share joy with the wife of your youth.
5:19 Let a deer of affection and a colt of your graces keep company with you. Let your own woman lead you and be with you at every time, for as you move in the company of her affection, you will be one among many.
5:20 Do not be much involved with another woman, nor be held in the arms of one who is not your own.
5:21 For a man’s ways are before God’s eyes, and He watches all his tracks.
5:22 Lawless acts hunt a man, and each is bound tight by the cords of his own sins.
5:23 He dies among the undisciplined; from the abundance of his own life he was cast out and perished through folly.

PROVERBS 6

6:1 Son, if you act as guarantor for your friend, you will give your hand over to an enemy.
6:2 For a man’s own lips are a strong trap, and he is caught by the lips of his own mouth.
6:3 Son, do what I command you and save yourself—for through your friend you have come into evil men’s hands. Go without growing slack, and press the friend for whom you acted as guarantor.
6:4 Give no sleep to your eyes, nor doze with your eyelids,
6:5 so that you may save yourself like a gazelle from snares and like a bird from a trap.
6:6 Go to the ant, sluggard; see its ways and emulate them, and become wiser than it.
6:7 For although it has no cultivated field, and has no one forcing it, and is under no master,
6:8 it prepares its food in summer and makes abundant provision at harvest.
6:8a Or go to the bee and learn how industrious she is, and how nobly she performs her work,
6:8b whose labors kings and private people use for health; she is desired by everyone and held in esteem.
6:8c Although weak in bodily strength, she was advanced because she honored wisdom.
6:9 How long will you remain lying down, sluggard? When will you rise from sleep?
6:10 You sleep a little, sit a little, doze a little, and clasp your chest with your hands a little;
6:11 then poverty arrives beside you like an evil traveler, and want like a good runner.
6:11a But if you are diligent, your harvest will come like a spring, and want will desert you like a bad runner.
6:12 A foolish and lawless man travels ways that are not good;
6:13 the same man signals with his eye and gives signs with his foot, and instructs by gestures of his fingers;
6:14 with a twisted heart he devises evils at every time. Such a person stirs up disturbances for a city.
6:15 Therefore his destruction comes suddenly: a cutting down and an incurable crushing,
6:16 because he rejoices in everything the Lord hates, and is crushed because of his soul’s uncleanness:
6:17 an insolent person’s eye, an unjust tongue, hands shedding a righteous person’s blood,
6:18 and a heart devising evil thoughts, and feet hastening to do harm;
6:19 an unjust witness kindles falsehoods and sends disputes among brothers.
6:20 Son, guard your father’s laws, and do not reject your mother’s ordinances.
6:21 Fasten them always upon your soul, and collar them around your neck.
6:22 Whenever you walk, take her along and let her be with you; when you sleep, let her guard you, so that when you rise she may converse with you,
6:23 because the commandment of law is a lamp and a light, and correction and discipline are life’s way,
6:24 to preserve you from a married woman and from an alien tongue’s slander.
6:25 Do not let desire for beauty overcome you; do not be hunted by your own eyes, nor seized by her eyelids.
6:26 For a prostitute’s price is as much as one loaf, but a woman hunts men’s precious lives.
6:27 Will someone bind fire in his bosom without burning his garments?
6:28 Or will someone walk on fiery coals without burning his feet?
6:29 So is the one who goes in to a married woman: he will not be acquitted, nor will anyone touching her.
6:30 It is no wonder if someone is caught stealing, for he steals to fill his appetite when hungry.
6:31 But if caught, he will repay sevenfold, and by giving all his possessions he will rescue himself.
6:32 But the adulterer, through lack of sense, brings destruction upon his own life;
6:33 he bears pains and dishonors, and his reproach will never be wiped away.
6:34 For her husband’s anger is full of jealousy; he will not spare on the day of judgment,
6:35 he will not trade his enmity for any ransom, nor be reconciled by many gifts.

PROVERBS 7

7:1 Son, guard my words, and hide my commandments with yourself.
7:1a Son, honor the Lord and you will be strong; apart from Him, fear no other.
7:2 Guard my commandments and you will live, and my words like the pupils of eyes.
7:3 Put them around your fingers; inscribe them on the broad surface of your heart.
7:4 Say that wisdom is your sister, and acquire understanding as your familiar companion,
7:5 so that she may keep you from an alien and wicked woman, if she catches you with pleasing words.
7:6 For she leans out from a window of her house toward the squares,
7:7 and whenever she sees among the foolish children a young man lacking sense,
7:8 passing by a corner in the passages of her houses
7:9 and talking in evening darkness, when night is still and murky,
7:10 the woman meets him, with a prostitute’s appearance, making young men’s hearts fly away.
7:11 She is fluttering with excitement and dissolute; her feet do not rest in the house.
7:12 For at one time she roams outside, and at another she lies in wait in the squares, at every corner.
7:13 Then she took hold of him and kissed him; with a shameless face she addressed him:
7:14 “I have a peace sacrifice; today I am paying my vows.
7:15 For this reason I came out to meet you; longing for your face, I have found you.
7:16 I have stretched my bed with bands and spread it with double-sided rugs from Egypt.
7:17 I have sprinkled my bed with saffron and my house with cinnamon.
7:18 Come, let us enjoy affection until dawn; come here, let us roll in love.
7:19 For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey,
7:20 taking a pouch of silver in his hand; after many days he will return to his house.”
7:21 She led him astray with much conversation and drove him off course with snares from her lips.
7:22 He followed her, made a fool, as an ox is led to slaughter and as a dog to bonds,
7:23 or as a deer struck in the liver by an arrow. He hurries like a bird into a trap, not knowing that he runs for his life.
7:24 Now therefore, son, listen to me, and attend to my mouth’s sayings.
7:25 Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways.
7:26 For she has wounded and brought down many, and those she has murdered are beyond counting.
7:27 Her house is ways to Hades, leading down to death’s chambers.

PROVERBS 8

8:1 You will proclaim wisdom, so that understanding may heed you.
8:2 For she is on the high summits, and stands between the paths.
8:3 For she sits beside the gates of rulers, and is praised at the entrances:
8:4 “You, O human beings, I summon, and I send forth my voice to the children of humankind.
8:5 Understand resourcefulness, you innocent ones; and you uneducated, put your heart into it.
8:6 Listen to me, for I will speak noble things, and bring forth upright things from my lips.
8:7 For my throat will meditate on truth, and lying lips are detestable before me.
8:8 All the words of my mouth are with righteousness; there is nothing crooked or entangled in them.
8:9 They are all plain to those who understand, and upright to those who find knowledge.
8:10 Take discipline rather than silver, and knowledge above tested gold; choose discernment rather than pure gold.
8:11 For wisdom is better than costly stones, and nothing precious is worthy of her.
8:12 I, Wisdom, have made counsel my dwelling, and I have called upon knowledge and reflection.
8:13 The fear of the Lord hates injustice, insolence and arrogance and the ways of wicked people; and I hate the perverted ways of evil people.
8:14 Counsel and security are mine; understanding is mine, and strength is mine.
8:15 Through me kings reign, and rulers write righteousness.
8:16 Through me great men become great, and through me sovereigns rule the earth.
8:17 I love those who love me, and those who seek me will find me.
8:18 Wealth and glory belong to me, and possession of many things and righteousness.
8:19 It is better to reap me than gold and precious stone, and my produce is better than choice silver.
8:20 I walk in ways of righteousness, and move in the midst of paths of just judgment,
8:21 so that I may apportion substance to those who love me, and fill their storehouses with good things.
8:21a If I announce to you the things that happen day by day, I will remember to recount the things from eternity.
8:22 The Lord created me as the beginning of His ways, for His works.
8:23 Before the age He founded me, in the beginning,
8:24 before making the earth and before making the deeps, before the springs of the waters came forth,
8:25 before the mountains were established, and before all hills He gives me birth.
8:26 The Lord made countries and uninhabited places, and the inhabited extremities of the world beneath heaven.
8:27 When He was preparing heaven, I was present with Him, and when He was marking out His own throne upon winds.
8:28 When He was making the clouds above strong, and when He was setting the springs of the world beneath heaven secure,
8:29 and was making the foundations of the earth strong,
8:30 I was beside Him, fitting things together; I was the one in whom He delighted. Day by day I rejoiced before Him at every time,
8:31 when He rejoiced after completing the inhabited world, and delighted in the children of humankind.
8:32 Now, therefore, son, listen to me.
8:34 Happy is the man who will listen to me, and the person who will keep my ways, staying awake at my doors day by day, guarding the posts of my entrances.
8:35 For my ways out are ways out to life, and favor is prepared by the Lord.
8:36 But those who sin against me act impiously against their own lives, and those who hate me love death.”

PROVERBS 9

9:1 Wisdom built herself a house, and set seven pillars beneath it.
9:2 She slaughtered her own sacrificial animals, mixed her own wine in a mixing-bowl, and prepared her own table.
9:3 She sent her own servants, summoning with a lofty proclamation to the mixing-bowl, saying:
9:4 “Whoever is foolish, let him turn aside to me!” And to those lacking understanding she said:
9:5 “Come, eat of my loaves, and drink wine that I mixed for you.
9:6 Leave foolishness, and you will live; seek understanding, so that you may live, and set insight right in knowledge.”
9:7 One who disciplines evil people will receive dishonor for himself, and one who reproves an impious person will bring blame on himself.
9:8 Do not reprove evil people, so that they may not hate you; reprove a wise person, and he will love you.
9:9 Give a wise person a starting point, and he will be wiser; inform a righteous person, and he will receive still more.
9:10 The beginning of wisdom is the fear of the Lord, and the counsel of holy ones is understanding.
9:10a For knowing the law belongs to a good mind.
9:11 For in this way you will live a long time, and years of your life will be added to you.
9:12 Son, if you become wise for yourself, you will also be wise for your neighbors; but if you turn out evil, you alone will bear evils.
9:12a One who leans on lies—he will shepherd winds, and that same person will pursue flying birds.
9:12b For he has left the ways of his own vineyard, and strayed from the paths of his own cultivated field.
9:12c He travels through a waterless wilderness and land allotted among thirsty places, and gathers fruitlessness with his hands.
9:13 A foolish and brazen woman comes to lack a morsel, she who does not know shame.
9:14 She sat at the doors of her own house, on a stool openly in the squares,
9:15 calling to those passing by and going straight along their ways:
9:16 “Whoever among you is most foolish, let him turn aside to me!” And I urge those lacking understanding, saying:
9:17 “Taste hidden loaves with pleasure, and sweet stolen water.”
9:18 But he does not know that earth-born ones perish beside her, and he comes upon the ledge of Hades.
9:18a But leap away; do not linger in the place, and do not fix your eye upon her.
9:18b For in this way you will cross foreign water and pass over a foreign river.
9:18c But keep away from foreign water, and do not drink from a foreign spring,
9:18d so that you may live a long time, and years of life may be added to you.

PROVERBS 10

10:1 A wise son makes a father glad, but a foolish son is grief to his mother.
10:2 Treasures will not profit lawless people, but righteousness will rescue from death.
10:3 The Lord will not starve a righteous life, but He will overturn the life of the impious.
10:4 Poverty humbles a man, but the hands of the courageous make rich.
10:4a A disciplined son will be wise, and he will use the foolish one as a servant.
10:5 An intelligent son was preserved from scorching heat, but a lawless son is ruined by wind at harvest.
10:6 The Lord’s blessing is upon the head of the righteous, but untimely mourning will cover the mouth of the impious.
10:7 The memory of righteous people is with praises, but the name of the impious is extinguished.
10:8 One wise in heart will receive commands, but the unrestrained person who twists things with his lips will be tripped up.
10:9 One who walks sincerely walks confidently, but one who perverts his ways will be known.
10:10 One who signals with his eyes deceitfully gathers griefs for people, but one who reproves with frankness makes peace.
10:11 A fountain of life is in the hand of the righteous, but destruction will cover the mouth of the impious.
10:12 Hatred raises strife, but friendship covers all who do not love strife.
10:13 One who brings forth wisdom from his lips strikes a man lacking heart with a rod.
10:14 Wise people will hide discernment, but the mouth of a rash person draws near to ruin.
10:15 The possession of the rich is a fortified city, but the ruin of the impious is poverty.
10:16 The works of the righteous produce life, but the fruits of the impious produce sins.
10:17 Discipline guards righteous paths of life, but discipline that goes unreproved wanders.
10:18 Righteous lips conceal enmity, but those who bring forth abuse are most foolish.
10:19 Through much talking you will not escape sin, but by sparing your lips you will be intelligent.
10:20 The tongue of the righteous is fire-tested silver, but the heart of the impious will fail.
10:21 The lips of the righteous know lofty things, but fools die in want.
10:22 The Lord’s blessing is upon the head of the righteous; it makes rich, and sorrow in the heart will certainly not be added to it.
10:23 A fool does evil things in laughter, but wisdom gives birth to understanding for a man.
10:24 An impious person is swept about in destruction, but the desire of the righteous is acceptable.
10:25 When a storm passes by, the impious disappears, but the righteous turns aside and is saved for ever.
10:26 As an unripe grape is harmful to teeth and smoke to eyes, so is lawlessness to those who practice it.
10:27 The fear of the Lord adds days, but the years of the impious will become few.
10:28 Gladness stays long with the righteous, but the hope of the impious perishes.
10:29 The fear of the Lord is a stronghold for the devout, but ruin for those who do evil.
10:30 The righteous will never give way, but the impious will not inhabit the earth.
10:31 The mouth of the righteous drips wisdom, but the tongue of the unjust will be destroyed.
10:32 The lips of righteous men drip gracious things, but the mouth of the impious turns away.

PROVERBS 11

11:1 Deceitful scales are an abomination before the Lord, but a just weight is acceptable to Him.
11:2 Wherever insolence enters, dishonor is there too, but the mouth of the humble meditates on wisdom.
11:3 A righteous person, when he died, left regret, but the destruction of the impious is ready at hand and welcomed with joy.
11:5 Righteousness cuts blameless paths straight, but impiety falls into injustice.
11:6 The righteousness of upright men rescues them, but lawless people are caught in their own destruction.
11:7 When a righteous man dies, hope does not perish, but the boast of the impious perishes.
11:8 The righteous escapes from the hunt, but the impious is handed over in his place.
11:9 In the mouth of the impious is a trap for citizens, but the discernment of the righteous has a good course.
11:10 Through the good things of the righteous a city prospered,
11:11 but by the mouths of the impious it was razed.
11:12 One lacking understanding sneers at citizens, but an intelligent man keeps quiet.
11:13 A double-tongued man reveals plans in a council, but one faithful in breath conceals matters.
11:14 Those who have no steering fall like leaves, but deliverance is in much counsel.
11:15 An evil person does harm when he mixes with a righteous person, and he hates the sound of security.
11:16 A gracious woman raises glory for her husband, but a woman who hates righteous things is a throne of dishonor. The lazy become lacking in wealth, but the courageous lean upon wealth.
11:17 A merciful man does good to his own life, but the merciless destroys his own body.
11:18 The impious does unjust works, but the seed of righteous people is a reward of truth.

PROVERBS 12

12:3 A person will not prosper through a lawless person, but the roots of the righteous will not be uprooted.
12:4 A courageous woman is a crown to her husband; but as a worm in wood, so a woman who does evil destroys a man.
12:5 The reasonings of the righteous are judgments, but the impious steer deceits.
12:6 The words of the impious are deceitful, but the mouth of the upright will rescue them.
12:7 Wherever he turns, the impious disappears, but the houses of the righteous remain.
12:8 The mouth of an understanding person is praised by a man, but one sluggish of heart is sneered at.
12:9 Better is a man in dishonor who serves himself than one who clothes himself in honor but needs bread.

12:11 One who works his own land will be filled with loaves, but those who pursue empty things lack understanding.
12:11a One who delights in time spent over wine will leave dishonor in his own strongholds.
12:12 The desires of the impious are evil, but the roots of the devout are in strongholds.
12:13 Through the sin of lips a sinner falls into traps, but the righteous escapes from them.
12:13a One who looks gently will receive mercy, but one who confronts people at the gates will crush lives.
12:14 From fruits of a mouth a man’s life will be filled with good things, and the recompense of his lips will be given to him.
12:15 The ways of fools are upright before them, but a wise person listens to counsel.
12:16 A fool announces his anger that same day, but a resourceful person hides his own dishonor.
12:17 A righteous person reports demonstrated faithfulness, but the witness of unjust people is deceitful.
12:18 There are those who wound with a sword as they speak, but the tongues of the wise heal.
12:19 Truthful lips set testimony right, but a hasty witness has an unjust tongue.
12:20 Deceit is in the heart of one devising evil things, but those who desire peace will rejoice.
12:21 Nothing unjust will please the righteous, but the impious will be filled with evils.
12:22 Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord, but one who performs faithful deeds is acceptable to Him.
12:23 An understanding man is a throne of discernment, but the heart of fools will meet curses.
12:24 The hand of chosen people will rule easily, but deceitful people will become plunder.
12:25 A frightening word troubles the heart of a righteous man, but good news makes him glad.
12:26 A discerning righteous person will be his own friend, but the judgments of the impious are unreasonable. Evils will pursue those who sin, and the way of the impious will lead them astray.
12:27 A deceitful person will not obtain game, but a pure man is a precious possession.
12:28 In ways of righteousness is life, but the ways of those who remember wrongs lead to death.

PROVERBS 13

13:1 A resourceful son is obedient to a father, but a disobedient son is in destruction.
13:2 A good person will eat from fruits of righteousness, but the lives of lawless people will perish before their time.
13:3 One who guards his own mouth keeps his own life, but one rash with his lips will terrify himself.
13:4 Every idle person is amid desires, but the hands of the courageous are engaged in care.
13:5 The righteous hates an unjust word, but the impious is ashamed and will have no frankness.
13:6 Righteousness guards the innocent, but sin makes the impious worthless.
13:7 There are those who make themselves rich while having nothing, and those who humble themselves amid much wealth.
13:8 A man’s own wealth is the ransom of his life, but a poor person does not face a threat.
13:9 Light is for the righteous continually, but the light of the impious is extinguished.
13:9a Deceitful lives wander in sins, but the righteous have compassion and show mercy.
13:10 An evil person does evil things with insolence, but those who understand themselves are wise.
13:11 Property pursued eagerly with lawlessness becomes less, but one who gathers for himself with piety will increase; a righteous person has compassion and lends.
13:12 Better is one who begins to help the heart than one who promises and leads it into hope; for a good desire is a tree of life.
13:13 One who despises a matter will be despised by it, but one who fears a command is sound.
13:13a Nothing good will belong to a deceitful son, but a wise household servant’s actions will prosper, and his way will be made straight.
13:14 The law of a wise person is a fountain of life, but the senseless will die by a trap.
13:15 Good understanding gives favor, and knowing the law belongs to a good mind, but the ways of the contemptuous are in destruction.
13:16 Every resourceful person acts with knowledge, but the fool has spread out his own wickedness.
13:17 A rash king will fall into evils, but a faithful messenger will rescue him.
13:18 Discipline takes away poverty and dishonor, and one who keeps rebukes will be glorified.
13:19 The desires of the devout sweeten the life, but the works of the impious are far from knowledge.
13:20 One who walks with the wise will be wise, but one who walks with fools will be known.
13:21 Evils will pursue those who sin, but good things will overtake the righteous.
13:22 A good man will inherit children’s children, but the wealth of the impious is stored up for the righteous.
13:23 The righteous will spend many years in wealth, but the unjust will perish swiftly.
13:24 One who spares the staff hates his son, but one who loves him disciplines carefully.
13:25 The righteous, by eating, fills his life, but the lives of the impious are in want.

PROVERBS 14

14:1 Wise women built houses, but the foolish woman tore them down with her hands.
14:2 One who walks uprightly fears the Lord, but one who is crooked in his ways will be dishonored.
14:3 From the mouth of fools comes a staff of insolence, but the lips of the wise guard them.
14:4 Where there are no oxen, mangers are clean; but where there is much produce, an ox’s strength is evident.
14:5 A faithful witness does not lie, but an unjust witness kindles lies.
14:6 You will seek wisdom among evil people and not find it, but discernment is readily found among intelligent people.
14:7 Everything is against a foolish man, but wise lips are weapons of discernment.
14:8 The wisdom of resourceful people will recognize their ways, but the senselessness of fools is in wandering.
14:9 The houses of lawless people will owe purification, but the houses of the righteous are acceptable.
14:10 A man’s heart is perceptive; his life is sorrowful, but when he rejoices, he does not mingle with insolence.
14:11 The houses of the impious will disappear, but the tents of those who act rightly will stand.
14:12 There is a way that seems upright to people, but its final outcome comes to the bottom of Hades.
14:13 In gladness sorrow does not mingle, but in the end joy comes to mourning.
14:14 One rash in heart will be filled from his own ways, but a good man from his own thoughts.
14:15 The innocent person believes every word, but the resourceful comes to reconsideration.
14:16 The wise, becoming afraid, turned from evil, but the fool, trusting himself, mixes with a lawless person.
14:17 One quick to anger acts without counsel, but an intelligent man endures many things.
14:18 Fools will receive a share of wickedness, but the resourceful will lay hold of discernment.
14:19 Evil people will slip before good people, and the impious will attend the doors of the righteous.
14:20 Friends will hate poor friends, but the friends of the rich are many.
14:21 One who dishonors the needy sins, but one who has mercy on the poor is blessed.
14:22 Those who wander devise evil things, but good people devise mercy and truth. Makers of evils do not know mercy and faithfulness, but acts of mercy and faithfulness are with good makers.
14:23 In everyone who takes care there is abundance, but the pleasure-loving and unfeeling person will be in want.
14:24 A resourceful person is the crown of the wise, but the occupation of fools is evil.
14:25 A faithful witness will rescue a life from evils, but a deceitful person kindles lies.
14:26 In the fear of the Lord is hope of strength, and he leaves a support for his children.
14:27 The command of the Lord is a fountain of life, and it causes one to turn from a trap of death.
14:28 In a numerous nation is a king’s glory, but in the failing of a people is a ruler’s ruin.
14:29 A patient man is great in understanding, but one faint of spirit is exceedingly foolish.
14:30 A gentle-tempered man is a physician of the heart, but a perceptive heart is a moth to the bones.
14:31 One who extorts from the needy provokes the One who made him, but one who honors Him has mercy on the poor.
14:32 The impious will be thrust away in his wickedness, but one trusting in his own piety is righteous.
14:33 Wisdom is in a man’s good heart, but in the heart of fools it is not discerned.
14:34 Righteousness exalts a nation, but sins diminish tribes.
14:35 An intelligent attendant is acceptable to a king, and by his own skill he removes dishonor.

PROVERBS 15

15:1 Anger destroys even intelligent people; but a yielding answer turns away wrath, and a painful word stirs up anger.
15:2 The tongue of the wise knows good things, but the mouth of fools will announce evil things.
15:3 The eyes of the Lord are in every place; they observe both evil people and good people.
15:4 Healing of a tongue is a tree of life, but one who keeps watch over it will be filled with spirit.
15:5 A fool sneers at a father’s discipline, but one who keeps commands is more resourceful.
15:6 In abundant righteousness is great strength, but the impious will perish from the earth, roots and all. In the houses of the righteous is great strength, but the fruits of the impious will perish.
15:7 The lips of the wise are bound to discernment, but the hearts of fools are not secure.
15:8 The sacrifices of the impious are an abomination to the Lord, but the prayers of those going straight are acceptable to Him.
15:9 The ways of the impious are an abomination to the Lord, but He loves those who pursue righteousness.
15:10 The discipline of an innocent person is recognized by passersby, but those who hate rebukes die shamefully.
15:11 Hades and destruction are manifest before the Lord; how are human hearts not also?
15:12 An uneducated person will not love those who reprove him, and he will not associate with the wise.
15:13 When the heart rejoices, the face flourishes, but when it is in sorrows, the face grows gloomy.
15:14 An upright heart seeks discernment, but the mouth of the uneducated will know evil things.
15:15 All the time the eyes of evil people expect evils, but good people rest quietly continually.
15:16 Better a small portion with the fear of the Lord than great storehouses with fearlessness.
15:17 Better hospitality of vegetables with friendship and favor than a serving of calves with enmity.
15:18 A quick-tempered man prepares battles, but a patient person calms even a battle about to begin.
15:18a A patient man will extinguish disputes, but the impious stirs them up still more.
15:19 The ways of the idle are strewn with thorns, but those of the courageous are well worn.
15:20 A wise son makes a father glad, but a foolish son sneers at his mother.
15:21 The paths of a senseless person lack understanding, but an intelligent man walks making his course straight.
15:22 Those who do not honor councils put plans off, but counsel remains in the hearts of those who deliberate.
15:23 The evil person will certainly not heed it, nor say anything timely and good for the community.
15:24 The thoughts of an understanding person are ways of life, so that, turning aside from Hades, he may be saved.
15:25 The Lord pulls down the houses of the insolent, but He established the boundary of a widow.
15:26 An unjust thought is an abomination to the Lord, but the sayings of pure people are noble.
15:27 One who takes bribes destroys himself, but one who hates taking gifts is saved.
15:27a By acts of mercy and faithfulness sins are thoroughly cleansed, and by the fear of the Lord everyone turns from evil.
15:28 The hearts of the righteous meditate on faithful things, but the mouth of the impious answers evil things.
15:28a The ways of righteous men are acceptable to the Lord, and through them even enemies become friends.
15:29 God is far distant from the impious, but He listens to the prayers of the righteous.
15:29a Better a small gain with righteousness than much produce with injustice.
15:29b Let a man’s heart consider righteous things, so that his steps may be set right by God.
15:30 An eye looking at good things gladdens the heart, and a good report makes bones full.
15:32 One who thrusts discipline away hates himself, but one who keeps rebukes loves his own life.
15:33 The fear of God is discipline and wisdom, and the beginning of glory will answer her.

PROVERBS 16

16:5 Everyone high of heart is unclean before God, and one who unjustly puts hands into a hand will not be acquitted.

16:8 One who seeks the Lord will find knowledge with righteousness, and those who seek Him rightly will find peace.
16:9 All the works of the Lord are with righteousness, but the impious is kept for an evil day.

16:31 Old age is a crown of boasting, and it is found in ways of righteousness.

16:33 Everything comes into the laps of the unjust, but all righteous things are from the Lord.

PROVERBS 17

17:1 Better a morsel with pleasure in peace than a house full of many good things and unjust sacrifices with battle.
17:2 An intelligent household servant will rule foolish masters, and divide portions among brothers.
17:3 As silver and gold are tested in a furnace, so hearts are chosen before the Lord.
17:4 An evil person heeds the tongue of lawless people, but a righteous person pays no attention to lying lips.
17:5 One who laughs at the poor provokes the One who made him, and one who rejoices over someone perishing will not be acquitted; but one who has compassion will receive mercy.
17:6 The crown of elders is children’s children, and the boast of children is their fathers.
17:6a The whole world of possessions belongs to the faithful person, but not even an obol belongs to the unfaithful.
17:7 Faithful lips will not suit a fool, nor lying lips a righteous person.
17:8 Discipline is a reward of favors to those who use it, and wherever it turns, it will prosper.
17:9 One who hides wrongdoings seeks friendship, but one who hates hiding them separates friends and household members.
17:10 A threat crushes an intelligent person’s heart, but a fool, though whipped, does not perceive.
17:11 Every evil person raises disputes, but the Lord will send a merciless messenger to him.

17:14 The beginning of righteousness gives authority to words, but faction and battle lead the way to want.
17:15 One who judges the unjust righteous and the righteous unjust is unclean and detestable before God.

17:16a One who makes his own house high seeks ruin, and one who turns crookedly away from learning will fall into evils.

17:20 but one hard of heart does not meet good things. A man changeable in tongue will fall into evils,
17:21 and a fool’s heart is pain to the one who possesses it. A father does not rejoice over an uneducated son, but an intelligent son gladdens his mother.
17:22 A rejoicing heart makes one flourish, but a sorrowful man’s bones dry up.

17:24 An understanding face belongs to a wise man, but the fool’s eyes are upon the extremities of the earth.
17:25 A foolish son is anger to a father, and pain to the one who bore him.
17:26 To penalize a righteous man is not good, nor is it holy to plot against righteous rulers.
17:27 One who refrains from sending forth a harsh word is discerning, and a patient man is intelligent.
17:28 Wisdom will be reckoned to a senseless person who asks for wisdom, and someone who makes himself mute will seem intelligent.

PROVERBS 18

18:1 A man who wishes to separate from friends seeks pretexts, and at every time he will be subject to reproach.

18:3 When the impious comes into the depth of evils, he despises, and dishonor and reproach come upon him.

18:14 An intelligent attendant calms a man’s anger, but who will bear a fainthearted man?

31:20 She opened her hands to a needy person and stretched out her wrist to a poor person.
31:21 Her husband is not anxious about those at home when he stays away somewhere, for all those with her are clothed.
31:22 She made double cloaks for her husband, and garments of fine linen and purple for herself.
31:23 Her husband becomes distinguished in the gates when he sits in council with the elder inhabitants of the land.
31:24 She made linen cloths and sold them, and girdles for the Canaanites.
31:25 She opened her mouth attentively and lawfully and prescribed order for her tongue.
31:26 She clothed herself in strength and dignity and rejoiced in the last days.
31:27 The occupations of her houses are secure, and she did not eat idle food.
31:28 She opens her mouth wisely and according to law, and her mercy raised up her children, and they became wealthy, and her husband praised her:
31:29 “Many daughters acquired wealth, many did mighty things, but you stand above and have surpassed them all.”
31:30 A woman’s pleasing qualities are false and beauty is vain; for an understanding woman is blessed, and let her praise the fear of the Lord.
31:31 Give her from the fruits of her hands, and let her husband be praised in the gates.
