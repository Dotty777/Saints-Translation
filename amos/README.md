All 9 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 146 verse units received an English editorial review: 91 changed and 55 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize third-person says while retaining past said/spoke where the source has past speech. Preserve nakkarim, Jerusalem as the vision’s object, two years, three/four repeated formulas, differing him/them/her objects, pregnant women and iron saws, foundations, son of Ader, Harran, Syria called by name, Solomon’s captives, all source-specific place names and plural kings/priests/rulers.

Pending: Review the untranslated nakkarim, called-by-name adjective, Solomon reference, and the pronoun shifts and womb/testimony/shuddering/onrush clause at 1:11. Do not reconstruct a historical event or substitute the Hebrew readings. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize ordinary you and third-person says/rolls; clarify the trailing things-their-fathers-followed clause at 2:4. Preserve lime, weakness, foundations, the sandal clause crossing 2:6–7, fist blows, young slave woman and their God’s name, tied hangings and extorted wine, cedar/oak/fruit/roots, forty years, consecration, rolling straw-filled wagon, repeated negatives and the naked one pursuing at 2:16.

Pending: Review the antecedent of which at 2:7, consecration terminology, wagon syntax and the heart-among-powers/naked-pursuer clause. Do not import Nazirite, pledged garments or fleeing from comparison witnesses. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person has/reveals/snatches/says. Preserve known and therefore judgment, mutual recognition, every question, fowler, calamity, discipline disclosed to prophets, future lion’s roar, Assyrians, explicit Tyre, two legs/earlobe, tribe and priests in Damascus, colonnaded house and the final addition of many other houses.

Pending: Review 3:10’s detached storing-group clause, unnamed agent at 3:11, 3:12’s tribe/priest syntax, and the force of confound at 3:15. Source-specific readings remain provisional rather than being rewritten as familiar Hebrew-based phrases. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize ordinary address, third-person swears/says and attributive My. Preserve heifers, fiery pestilences/cauldrons, nakedness and Mount Remman, past Bethel/Gilgal acts, three-day period, third-person law-reading clause, future aching teeth, past withheld rain followed by future rain, repeated refusal to return, Egypt’s road, call upon God, thunder and His anointed.

Pending: Review the number shifts, especially singular your horses amid plural address at 4:10, whose Greek distinction is recorded here; the captivity and fire clauses; and the first-person I with His anointed/His name at 4:13. Do not replace call upon with meet or insert a named messianic identification. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; close the direct quotation after 5:5 before Seek the Lord. Preserve the doubled Lord title, Well of the Oath, Joseph/Israel distinctions, the Lord as agent establishing judgment, making/refashioning all things, fist blows, first-person perfect claim at 5:15, past said at 5:17, plural hands, impassable torrent and shall at 5:24, Moloch/Raiphan and beyond Damascus.

Pending: Review the distributing-shattering phrase, farmer/mourning syntax and salvation offering of your display. The 5:15 words remain a quoted claim rather than rewritten commands; the relation to Acts remains reception evidence, with no Babylon substitution. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Modernize ordinary address and commands; make young goats explicit, smooth nothing and correct the sentence boundary between 6:9–10. Preserve Zion’s despisers, firstfruits/entered-themselves clause, all of you rather than Calneh, Emath Rabba/Geth, false sabbaths, lasting instruments without an inserted David, strained wine, horses’ neighing/Ephraim, repeated survivors, horns and the Wadi of the West.

Pending: Review the firstfruits and entered-themselves syntax, strongest kingdoms, captivity from the foremost, survivors and speaker/number shifts at 6:9–10, and horses remaining silent among females at 6:12. Difficult readings are retained rather than silently replaced from Hebrew-based versions. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Modernize ordinary address to Amos, Amaziah and the king, and third-person says/finishes; smooth laid waste and one who scratches sycamore figs, and explicitly carry the future through 7:17. Preserve the prospective locust clause, Gog the king, two prayers that ask God to relent, man/adamant repeated, altars of laughter, Jeroboam’s house versus the reported accusation about Jeroboam, future prophecy, past I was, goat herding/sheep and crowd agitation.

Pending: Review the bruchus terminology and Gog, the fewness of corporate Jacob, the exceptionally hard material called adamant, the laughing altars and sycamore treatment. Do not convert the requests to relent into narration or identify the material as a settled metal/mineral. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 8.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person swears/has/lives/says; render failure from thirst as fainting. Preserve the fowler’s vessel twice, coffered ceilings, casting silence, until morning, month/weights, every kind of produce, third-person He with your in the oath, consummation/river image, midday darkness, beloved one and those with him, singular Lord’s word, shaken waters, and both gods named in the Dan/Beersheba oaths.

Pending: Review the fowler’s vessel and implied objects, punctuation of the commerce question, consummation, unnamed male referent at 8:10, and Samaria’s propitiation. Do not substitute summer fruit, an only child, sea-to-sea wandering or Beersheba’s way from another witness. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 9.** Modernize ordinary address, attributive My and third-person verbs; render establishing the promise and ruined cities naturally. Preserve altar/atonement cover/foregates, Hades/heaven/Carmel/dragon, evil and good, doubled Lord title, consummation, ascent/promise, Ethiopians/Cappadocia/pit, Jacob’s preservation, winnowing basket and fragment, David’s tent, remaining humanity and nations seeking without an expressed object, threshing/vintage/ripening/seedtime, hills planted together and permanent planting.

Pending: Review temple imagery and foregates, consummation and the established promise, the winnowed fragment, the unexpressed object of seek at 9:12 and the agricultural sequence. Acts’ explicit Lord and changed introduction remain reception material; no Edom-possession reading is substituted. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
