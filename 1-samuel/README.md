# 1 Samuel — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 31 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 773 verse units received an English editorial review: 367 changed and 406 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/1SA.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Publisher retrieval failed for 1 Samuel 7; that chapter received an English review of the inherited text, with no new Greek-check claim. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserve the distinctive names, repeated affliction, one portion, Eli’s servant as speaker, Adonai/Lord/Eloai Sabaoth, the no-wine/no-iron vow, three-year calf and loan language. Ordinary dialogue uses you/your; Hannah’s prayer at 1:11 retains Thou/Thy/Thee. Smooth Elkanah’s questions and Eli’s blessing without changing the heart image or request.

Pending: Names, quantities, transliterations and vow variants; independent full Greek and apparatus review.

**Chapter 2.** Preserve the song’s lineation, established heart/horn, hungry leaving the land, divine undertakings, plural our kings and expanded wisdom passage. Thy/Thee remain in 2:1–2. Retain ephoud bar, loan, faithful house/priest and silver obol; modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs.

Pending: Song syntax and images; ephoud bar and sacrificial terminology; full apparatus.

**Chapter 3.** Keep the lamp being put in order, repeated calling, reviling God and expiated wrongdoing. In 3:9 distinguish Eli addressing Samuel (you) from the nested prayer (Thy servant hears). The same prayer wording appears at 3:10.

Pending: Lamp idiom, reviling and expiation; narrative additions.

**Chapter 4.** Retain the foreigners’ appeal to the Lord, Eli’s age ninety and twenty years of judging, crushed back and Woe-barchaboth. Name the man and Eli in 4:18 to clarify the pronoun sequence; split 4:20 into spoken English sentences.

Pending: Names and death narrative; unusual numbers and source differences.

**Chapter 5.** Retain soles of hands, amapheth, ships, seats, buttocks and Ashkelon. Modernize dialogue without substituting familiar readings.

Pending: Dagon description, amapheth, disease vocabulary and geographical variants.

**Chapter 6.** Preserve propitiation for the personal beneficiaries, bersechthan/ergab, labouring cows, Hosea, Jeconiah and the separate seventy and fifty thousand. Keep all repeated ark language and source-specific details.

Pending: Cultic vocabulary, opaque terms, cow description and numerical variants.

**Chapter 7.** English editorial review only in this chapter. Retain the inherited Greek-based readings including looking after the Lord, groves, Ashkelon to Azob and the old city. No fresh Greek verification is claimed for chapter 7.

Pending: Fresh publisher-text consultation, geographical readings and full Greek review.

**Chapter 8.** Retain after completion at 8:3 and good cattle herds at 8:16. Preserve the repeated claims of the king and meaningful plural address while modernizing grammar.

Pending: After completion, royal levy details and cattle terminology.

**Chapter 9.** Retain you give the quarter-shekel, Bama, beautiful things, seventy guests and gnaw it. Keep emphatic But you stand here at 9:27.

Pending: Money terminology, meal description, names and unusual lexical readings.

**Chapter 10.** Preserve the tombs, leaping men, lavish expenditure, Nasib, nabla/kinyra and you both. In 10:19 the quoted request to appoint a king uses ordinary you, reflecting the request made to Samuel in chapter 8.

Pending: Prophetic idioms, instrument names and the quotation’s addressee.

**Chapter 11.** Retain plural you all in 11:3, after morning, Abiezek, six hundred thousand/seventy thousand and Samuel’s anointing and sacrifice. Do not harmonize figures.

Pending: Time expression, muster location and numbers, anointing sequence.

**Chapter 12.** Preserve will sit down, appeasing payment and sandal, the unfinished conditional at 12:14 and will be added at 12:25. Restore Thee in the direct prayer quoted in 12:10.

Pending: Payment/sandal vocabulary, conditional syntax and final threat.

**Chapter 13.** Keep the absence of 13:1, Nasib, thirty thousand chariots, could not advance, testimony of the days and tool prices per tooth. Reorder the list of tools at the forge without losing any item.

Pending: 13:6 compressed infinitive, days expression, tool names/prices and textual gaps.

**Chapter 14.** Preserve messab, the unusual weapon/stone description, servitude, clear signs/holiness and the people’s prayer for Jonathan. Direct prayer uses Wilt Thou and hast Thou. At 14:41 render the embedded saying indirectly as if Thou sayest it is in Thy people Israel; this is an English quotation decision, not a claim about ancient punctuation.

Pending: 14:14 measure/image, 14:40 servitude and 14:41 syntax and lot terminology; quotation interpretation.

**Chapter 15.** Keep Jerim, four hundred thousand/thirty thousand, consoled at 15:11 distinct from regretted at 15:35, hand monument, people’s voice and Israel divided in two. Preserve the violence and ban without embellishment. Modernize ordinary dialogue and shared auxiliaries.

Pending: Divine consolation/regret, hand monument, 15:20 and divided kingdom wording.

**Chapter 16.** Use Are these all the boys? at 16:11 for natural English; retain all participants and the youngest. Preserve good before the Lord, choking spirit, gomor and kinyra.

Pending: Spirit terminology, quantities and names; the question at 16:11.

**Chapter 17.** Retain the shorter Greek chapter with its numbering gaps, four cubits and a span, bronze/iron, the worse-than-a-dog response and stone passing the helmet. You all at 17:47 marks the plural audience, distinct from singular address to Goliath.

Pending: Short/long narrative witnesses, stature, armour and stone description.

**Chapter 18.** Keep the shorter chapter, source-specific gaps, the foreigners’ hand against Saul, one hundred foreskins and all Israel loving David. Ordinary commands and court dialogue use you/your.

Pending: Short/long witness differences, subjects and numbers.

**Chapter 19.** Preserve sleeping Saul, funerary images and goat’s liver, naked prophesying and the king/your servant honorific shift in 19:4. Modernize ordinary dialogue without removing the source’s ambiguity.

Pending: Image and liver vocabulary, spirit wording and honorific syntax.

**Chapter 20.** Retain the filled space between life and death, and if not ellipsis, cities, bringing evils, ergab/amattari, son of deserting girls and completion. Preserve covenant repetition and explicit speakers.

Pending: 20:3 idiom, 20:9 broken conditional, opaque terms, insult and completion.

**Chapter 21.** Keep 21:1 at the source’s chapter boundary, Nomba/Abimelech, Phellani Allemoni, singular he may eat, neessaran, Syrian Doeg, mules and the feigned physical behaviour. Modernize dialogue only where warranted.

Pending: Holy bread and equipment syntax, names, neessaran and feigned behaviour.

**Chapter 22.** Retain three hundred and five priests, Syrian Doeg, Abimelech and protection for the soul. Preserve uncover my ear as a repeated image; use natural imperative order.

Pending: Priestly tally, names, disclosure idiom and protection wording.

**Chapter 23.** Restore Thy servant in David’s direct prayer. Retain the missing 23:12, four hundred men and difficult Will it be shut up? without choosing a new object. Ordinary speech remains you/your.

Pending: Shut-up question, missing verse, numbers and 23:22 syntax.

**Chapter 24.** Keep the source’s chapter boundary, heart striking, cloak flap, dead dog and single flea. May the Lord forbid that I should gives natural English for the oath at 24:7; Whom are you pursuing? smooths the question.

Pending: Oath idiom and source numbering; no harmonization with common chapter divisions.

**Chapter 25.** Retain doglike Nabal, To the seasons!, oiphi/gomor, urinating against a wall and hand/soul/sling images. Reorder 25:29 for clarity while retaining person, pursuit, bond of life and enemies’ soul.

Pending: Greeting, quantities, violent oath and soul/sling grammar.

**Chapter 26.** Use lying asleep at 26:7 for the Greek sleep expression without adding soundly. Keep covered carriage, night-raven and stupor. Match 24:7’s oath formula at 26:11; retain the magnified soul and future rescue.

Pending: Carriage, sleep expression, night-raven and divine stupor.

**Chapter 27.** Retain four hundred men, four months and the narrated killing. At 27:12 use David was greatly trusted by Achish, who said to clarify the English passive and the speaker already implied by the quotation.

Pending: Chronological/numerical differences and the subject of saying at 27:12.

**Chapter 28.** Keep the narrator’s Samuel, plural gods, physical standing upright, future transfer of the kingdom and sons will fall rather than importing with me. Modernize ordinary conversation; retain all attributed reception separately.

Pending: Apparition language and interpretive history, future tenses and death prediction.

**Chapter 29.** Preserve second year despite the different duration in chapter 27, and guarding the foreigners’ land. Modernize the dismissal and testimony without harmonization.

Pending: Duration discrepancy, satraps’ speech and final action.

**Chapter 30.** Retain six hundred where given, four hundred camel riders, geddour and the full added 30:28a list in place. Keep shared spoil, recipients and place names.

Pending: Names, geddour, numbers and the additional place list.

**Chapter 31.** Preserve missile throwers/archers, wounded ribs, turned him over, Jonathan’s body, burning and cultivated ground burial. Do not import beheading or sons’ bodies from another witness.

Pending: Military/wound vocabulary, body treatment, burial and witness differences.
