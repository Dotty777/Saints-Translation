## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize ordinary you/your and third-person verbs; render ambushes as plural at 1:7, smooth the emphatic prohibition at 1:13 and explicitly carry the future possess through 1:19. Preserve first-person I heard and third-person He sent, the shift to his dwelling/his heart at 1:3, upward/downward eagle and nest imagery, Idumea/Teman, the 1:9–10 continuation with slaughter and impiety, repeated day phrases and prohibitions, recompense upon the head, wine and descent, possessing former possessors, fire/flame/stubble and bearer of fire, Ephrathah and the accepted 1:21 cadence with saved men ascending from Zion and shall.
