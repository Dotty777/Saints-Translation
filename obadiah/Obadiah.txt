OBADIAH 1

1:1 The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord God to Idumea: I heard a report from the Lord, and He sent a dispatch to the nations: “Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!”
1:2 Look, I have made you insignificant among the nations; you are greatly dishonored.
1:3 The pride of your heart has lifted you up, you who camp in holes in the rocks, raising his dwelling high and saying in his heart, “Who will bring me down to the ground?”
1:4 If you soar like an eagle, and if you place your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down, says the Lord.
1:5 If thieves came in to you, or robbers by night, where would you have been cast away? Would they not have stolen enough for themselves? And if grape gatherers came in to you, would they not have left a gleaning?
1:6 How Esau has been searched out, and his hidden things seized!
1:7 All the men of your covenant sent you out as far as your borders; the men at peace with you opposed you and prevailed over you. They set ambushes beneath you. There is no understanding in them.
1:8 On that day, says the Lord, I will destroy the wise from Idumea and understanding from Mount Esau.
1:9 Your warriors from Teman will be terrified, so that a person may be removed from Mount Esau,
1:10 because of the slaughter and the impiety toward your brother Jacob; and shame will cover you, and you will be removed forever.
1:11 From the day you stood opposed, on the day foreigners took his strength captive and strangers entered his gates and cast lots over Jerusalem, you too were like one of them.
1:12 Do not look on your brother’s day, on the day of strangers; do not rejoice over the sons of Judah on the day of their destruction; do not speak boastfully on the day of affliction.
1:13 Do not enter the gates of peoples on the day of their suffering. You, too, must not look on their assembly on the day of their destruction, and do not join in attacking their strength on the day of their destruction.
1:14 Do not stand at their escape routes to destroy those of them who are being saved, and do not shut in those fleeing from them on the day of affliction.
1:15 For the day of the Lord is near against all the nations. Just as you acted, so it will be for you; your recompense will be repaid upon your own head.
1:16 For just as you drank on My holy mountain, all the nations will drink wine; they will drink and go down, and they will be as though they did not exist.
1:17 But on Mount Zion there will be salvation, and it will be holy; the house of Jacob will possess those who possessed them.
1:18 The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble. They will blaze against them and consume them, and there will be no bearer of fire in the house of Esau, because the Lord has spoken.
1:19 Those in the Negeb will possess the mountain of Esau, and those in the Shephelah will possess the foreigners. They will possess the mountain of Ephraim, the plain of Samaria, Benjamin and the land of Gilead.
1:20 And this is the beginning of the deportation: for the sons of Israel, the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath; and the deportation of Jerusalem as far as Ephrathah. They will inherit the cities of the Negeb.
1:21 And men who have been saved shall ascend from Mount Zion to execute judgment upon the mountain of Esau; and the kingdom shall be the Lord’s.
