The Vision of Obadiah Against Idumea

1:1The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord God to Idumea: I heard a report from the Lord, and He sent a dispatch to the nations: “Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!”

1:2Look, I have made you insignificant among the nations; you are greatly dishonored.

1:3The pride of your heart has lifted you up, you who camp in holes in the rocks, raising his dwelling high and saying in his heart, “Who will bring me down to the ground?”

1:4If you soar like an eagle, and if you place your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down, says the Lord.

1:5If thieves came in to you, or robbers by night, where would you have been cast away? Would they not have stolen enough for themselves? And if grape gatherers came in to you, would they not have left a gleaning?

1:6How Esau has been searched out, and his hidden things seized!

1:7All the men of your covenant sent you out as far as your borders; the men at peace with you opposed you and prevailed over you. They set ambushes beneath you. There is no understanding in them.

1:8On that day, says the Lord, I will destroy the wise from Idumea and understanding from Mount Esau.

1:9Your warriors from Teman will be terrified, so that a person may be removed from Mount Esau,

Because of the Slaughter of Your Brother Jacob

1:10because of the slaughter and the impiety toward your brother Jacob; and shame will cover you, and you will be removed forever.

1:11From the day you stood opposed, on the day foreigners took his strength captive and strangers entered his gates and cast lots over Jerusalem, you too were like one of them.

1:12Do not look on your brother’s day, on the day of strangers; do not rejoice over the sons of Judah on the day of their destruction; do not speak boastfully on the day of affliction.

1:13Do not enter the gates of peoples on the day of their suffering. You, too, must not look on their assembly on the day of their destruction, and do not join in attacking their strength on the day of their destruction.

1:14Do not stand at their escape routes to destroy those of them who are being saved, and do not shut in those fleeing from them on the day of affliction.

The Day of the Lord Is Near

1:15For the day of the Lord is near against all the nations. Just as you acted, so it will be for you; your recompense will be repaid upon your own head.

1:16For just as you drank on My holy mountain, all the nations will drink wine; they will drink and go down, and they will be as though they did not exist.

On Mount Zion There Will Be Salvation

1:17But on Mount Zion there will be salvation, and it will be holy; the house of Jacob will possess those who possessed them.

1:18The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble. They will blaze against them and consume them, and there will be no bearer of fire in the house of Esau, because the Lord has spoken.

1:19Those in the Negeb will possess the mountain of Esau, and those in the Shephelah will possess the foreigners. They will possess the mountain of Ephraim, the plain of Samaria, Benjamin and the land of Gilead.

1:20And this is the beginning of the deportation: for the sons of Israel, the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath; and the deportation of Jerusalem as far as Ephrathah. They will inherit the cities of the Negeb.

1:21And men who have been saved shall ascend from Mount Zion to execute judgment upon the mountain of Esau; and the kingdom shall be the Lord’s.

Current Bespoke editorial notes

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Modernize ordinary you/your and third-person verbs; render ambushes as plural at 1:7, smooth the emphatic prohibition at 1:13 and explicitly carry the future possess through 1:19. Preserve first-person I heard and third-person He sent, the shift to his dwelling/his heart at 1:3, upward/downward eagle and nest imagery, Idumea/Teman, the 1:9–10 continuation with slaughter and impiety, repeated day phrases and prohibitions, recompense upon the head, wine and descent, possessing former possessors, fire/flame/stubble and bearer of fire, Ephrathah and the accepted 1:21 cadence with saved men ascending from Zion and shall.

Greek consultation in this revision: All 21 verses read with focused comparison of pronouns, ambushes, the cross-verse slaughter clause, source images and names, and the final saved-ones declaration.
