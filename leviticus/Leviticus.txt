Leviticus 1

1:1 And He called unto Moses, and the Lord spoke unto him from the Tent of Testimony, saying,
1:2 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: If a man among you brings gifts unto the Lord, you shall bring your gifts from the livestock, from the cattle and from the flock.
1:3 If his gift is a whole burnt offering from the cattle, he shall bring an unblemished male; he shall bring it unto the door of the Tent of Testimony, acceptable before the Lord.
1:4 And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the offering, acceptable for him, to make atonement for him.
1:5 And they shall slaughter the calf before the Lord; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall bring the blood, and pour the blood round about upon the altar which is at the doors of the Tent of Testimony.
1:6 And when they have skinned the whole burnt offering, they shall divide it limb by limb.
1:7 And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall put fire upon the altar, and pile wood upon the fire.
1:8 And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pile the divided parts, and the head, and the fat upon the wood upon the fire which is upon the altar.
1:9 But they shall wash the entrails and the feet with water; and the priests shall place all upon the altar. It is an offering, a sacrifice, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
1:10 And if his gift unto the Lord is from the flock, from lambs and young goats, for a whole burnt offering, he shall bring an unblemished male, and lay his hand upon its head.
1:11 And they shall slaughter it at the north side of the altar before the Lord; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour its blood round about upon the altar.
1:12 And they shall divide it limb by limb, with the head and the fat; and the priests shall pile them upon the wood upon the fire which is upon the altar.
1:13 And they shall wash the entrails and the feet with water; and the priest shall bring all, and place it upon the altar. It is an offering, a sacrifice, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
1:14 And if you bring an offering of birds as a gift unto the Lord, he shall bring his gift from the turtledoves or the pigeons.
1:15 And the priest shall bring it unto the altar, and pinch off the head; and the priest shall place it upon the altar, and drain the blood at the base of the altar.
1:16 And he shall remove the crop with the feathers, and cast it beside the altar toward the east, into the place of the ashes.
1:17 And he shall break it open by its wings, but shall not divide it; and the priest shall place it upon the altar, upon the wood upon the fire. It is an offering, a sacrifice, a sweet savour unto the Lord.

Editorial note: The changes of ritual agent are retained, including the plural priests in 1:9 and the singular priest in 1:13. The change from “you” to “he” in 1:14 is also retained. “Acceptable” and the repeated “sweet savour” remain.

Leviticus 2

2:1 And if a soul brings a gift as a sacrifice unto the Lord, his gift shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil upon it, and put frankincense upon it. It is a sacrifice.
2:2 And he shall bring it unto the sons of Aaron, the priests; and when he has taken from it a full handful of the fine flour with the oil and all its frankincense, the priest shall place its memorial portion upon the altar: a sacrifice, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
2:3 And what remains of the sacrifice shall be Aaron’s and his sons’; it is most holy among the sacrifices of the Lord.
2:4 And if he brings a gift as a sacrifice baked in an oven, a gift unto the Lord from fine flour, it shall be unleavened loaves mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil.
2:5 And if your gift is a sacrifice from a griddle, it shall be fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened.
2:6 And you shall break them into pieces, and pour oil upon them. It is a sacrifice unto the Lord.
2:7 And if your gift is a sacrifice from a hearth, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.
2:8 And he shall bring the sacrifice which he makes from these things unto the Lord; and he shall bring it unto the priest; and when he has drawn near unto the altar,
2:9 the priest shall take its memorial portion from the sacrifice, and the priest shall place it upon the altar: an offering, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
2:10 But what is left of the sacrifice shall be Aaron’s and his sons’; these are most holy things among the offerings of the Lord.
2:11 You shall not make with leaven any sacrifice which you bring unto the Lord; for you shall bring neither any leaven nor any honey from it as an offering unto the Lord.
2:12 As a gift of firstfruits you shall bring them unto the Lord; but it shall not be brought up upon the altar for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
2:13 And every gift of your sacrifice shall be seasoned with salt. You shall not let the salt of the covenant of the Lord cease from your sacrifices; with every gift of yours you shall bring salt unto the Lord your God.
2:14 And if you bring a sacrifice of first produce unto the Lord, new roasted and crushed groats unto the Lord, you shall bring the sacrifice of the first produce,
2:15 and pour oil upon it, and put frankincense upon it. It is a sacrifice.
2:16 And the priest shall offer up its memorial portion, some of the groats with the oil and all its frankincense. It is an offering unto the Lord.

Editorial note: The grain-offering instructions retain their repeated references to oil, frankincense, salt and the memorial portion. The prohibition in 2:11 has clearer English word order; its scope is retained.

Leviticus 3

3:1 And if his gift unto the Lord is a sacrifice of deliverance, if he brings it from his cattle, whether male or female, he shall bring it unblemished before the Lord.
3:2 And he shall lay his hands upon the head of the gift, and slaughter it beside the doors of the Tent of Testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood round about upon the altar of whole burnt offerings.
3:3 And from the sacrifice of deliverance they shall bring an offering unto the Lord: the fat which covers the belly, and all the fat upon the belly,
3:4 and the two kidneys, and the fat upon them which is by the thighs, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.
3:5 And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall offer them up upon the altar, upon the whole burnt offerings, upon the wood upon the fire upon the altar: an offering, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
3:6 And if his gift is from the flock, as a sacrifice of deliverance unto the Lord, whether male or female, he shall bring it unblemished.
3:7 If he brings a lamb as his gift, he shall bring it before the Lord,
3:8 and lay his hands upon the head of his gift, and slaughter it beside the doors of the Tent of Testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood round about upon the altar.
3:9 And from the sacrifice of deliverance he shall bring an offering unto God: the fat and the unblemished loin, which he shall remove with the muscles of the lower back, and the fat of the belly,
3:10 and both kidneys, and the fat upon them which is by the thighs, and the lobe upon the liver, removing it with the kidneys;
3:11 the priest shall offer it up upon the altar: a sweet savour, an offering unto the Lord.
3:12 And if his gift is from the goats, he shall bring it before the Lord,
3:13 and lay his hands upon its head; and they shall slaughter it before the Lord beside the doors of the Tent of Testimony; and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall pour the blood round about upon the altar.
3:14 And from it he shall offer up an offering unto the Lord: the fat which covers the belly, and all the fat upon the belly,
3:15 and both kidneys, and all the fat upon them which is by the thighs, and the lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.
3:16 And the priest shall offer it up upon the altar: an offering, a sweet savour unto the Lord. All the fat is the Lord’s.
3:17 It is an everlasting statute throughout your generations, in every dwelling place of yours: you shall eat neither any fat nor any blood.

Editorial note: The repeated descriptions of fat and the perpetual prohibition on eating fat and blood remain. Conditional clauses now use ordinary present-tense English.

Leviticus 4

4:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
4:2 Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying: If a soul sins unintentionally before the Lord against the ordinances of the Lord concerning things which must not be done, and does any one of them:
4:3 if the anointed high priest sins so that the people sin, he shall bring, for his sin which he has committed, an unblemished calf from the cattle unto the Lord, for his sin.
4:4 And he shall bring the calf beside the door of the Tent of Testimony before the Lord, and lay his hand upon the calf’s head before the Lord, and slaughter the calf in the presence of the Lord.
4:5 And the anointed priest whose hands have been consecrated shall take some of the calf’s blood, and bring it into the Tent of Testimony.
4:6 And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the Lord, toward the holy veil.
4:7 And the priest shall put some of the calf’s blood upon the horns of the altar of compounded incense before the Lord, which is in the Tent of Testimony; and he shall pour out all the calf’s blood beside the base of the altar of whole burnt offerings, which is beside the doors of the Tent of Testimony.
4:8 And he shall remove all the fat from the calf of the sin offering: the fat which covers the inward parts, and all the fat upon the inward parts,
4:9 and the two kidneys, and the fat upon them which is by the thighs, and the lobe upon the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys,
4:10 as it is taken from the calf of the sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall offer it up upon the altar of offering.
4:11 But the calf’s hide, and all its flesh, with the head and the extremities, and the belly and the dung:
4:12 they shall carry the whole calf outside the camp unto a clean place, where they pour out the ashes, and burn it upon wood with fire; it shall be burned upon the place where the ashes are poured out.
4:13 And if the whole congregation of Israel is unintentionally ignorant, and a matter escapes the eyes of the congregation, and they do any of the things which the commandments of the Lord forbid, and commit an offence,
4:14 and the sin which they have committed becomes known unto them, the congregation shall bring an unblemished calf from the cattle for sin, and bring it beside the doors of the Tent of Testimony.
4:15 And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands upon the calf’s head before the Lord, and they shall slaughter the calf before the Lord.
4:16 And the anointed priest shall bring some of the calf’s blood into the Tent of Testimony.
4:17 And the priest shall dip his finger in some of the calf’s blood, and sprinkle it seven times before the Lord, in front of the holy veil.
4:18 And the priest shall put some of the blood upon the horns of the altar of compounded incense, which is before the Lord, which is in the Tent of Testimony; and he shall pour out all the blood at the base of the altar of offerings beside the door of the Tent of Testimony.
4:19 And he shall remove all the fat from it, and offer it up upon the altar.
4:20 And he shall do with the calf as he did with the calf of the sin offering; thus it shall be done. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and their sin shall be forgiven them.
4:21 And they shall carry the whole calf outside the camp, and burn the calf as they burned the former calf. It is the sin offering of the congregation.
4:22 And if the ruler sins, and unintentionally does any of the things which the commandments of the Lord their God forbid, and sins and commits an offence,
4:23 and the sin which he has committed becomes known unto him, he shall bring as his gift an unblemished male young goat from the goats.
4:24 And he shall lay his hand upon the young goat’s head; and they shall slaughter it in the place where they slaughter the whole burnt offerings before the Lord. It is a sin offering.
4:25 And the priest shall put some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger upon the horns of the altar of whole burnt offerings; and he shall pour out all its blood beside the base of the altar of whole burnt offerings.
4:26 And he shall offer up all its fat upon the altar, as the fat of a sacrifice of deliverance. And the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin, and he shall be forgiven.
4:27 And if one soul from the people of the land sins unintentionally, by doing any of the things which the commandments of the Lord forbid, and commits an offence,
4:28 and the sin which he has committed becomes known unto him, he shall bring an unblemished female young goat from the goats; he shall bring it for the sin which he has committed.
4:29 And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his sin offering; and they shall slaughter the female young goat of the sin offering in the place where they slaughter the whole burnt offerings.
4:30 And the priest shall take some of its blood with his finger, and put it upon the horns of the altar of whole burnt offerings; and he shall pour out all its blood beside the base of the altar.
4:31 And he shall remove all the fat, as fat is removed from a sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall offer it up upon the altar for a sweet savour unto the Lord. And the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.
4:32 And if he brings a sheep as his gift for a sin offering, he shall bring an unblemished female.
4:33 And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering; and they shall slaughter it in the place where they slaughter the whole burnt offerings.
4:34 And the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger, and put it upon the horns of the altar of whole burning; and he shall pour out all its blood beside the base of the altar of whole burning.
4:35 And he shall remove all its fat, as the fat of a sheep is removed from the sacrifice of deliverance; and the priest shall place it upon the altar, upon the whole burnt offering of the Lord. And the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin which he has committed, and he shall be forgiven.

Editorial note: The distinct offerings for the anointed priest, congregation, ruler and individual are retained. Reordered clauses make the agent of pouring the blood clearer without changing the sequence of actions.

Leviticus 5

5:1 And if a soul sins, and hears the voice of a solemn adjuration, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or has knowledge of the matter, if he does not declare it, he shall bear the sin.
5:2 Or if a soul touches any unclean thing, whether a dead thing, or an unclean thing torn by wild beasts, or one of the dead things, or one of the unclean abominations, or a carcass of unclean livestock,
5:3 or touches human uncleanness, any uncleanness of a man by touching which he is defiled, and it escapes his notice, but afterward he knows it and incurs guilt;
5:4 or if a soul swears, declaring with his lips to do evil or to do good, whatsoever a man declares with an oath, and it escapes his notice, and he knows it and sins in any one of these matters,
5:5 he shall confess the sin concerning that wherein he has sinned.
5:6 And he shall bring unto the Lord, for the offence and the sin which he has committed, a female from the flock, a ewe lamb or a female young goat from the goats, for sin. And the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin which he has committed, and his sin shall be forgiven him.
5:7 And if his hand cannot provide enough for a sheep, he shall bring unto the Lord for the sin which he has committed two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for sin and one for a whole burnt offering.
5:8 And he shall bring them unto the priest, and the priest shall bring the one for sin first; and the priest shall pinch its head at the neck joint, but shall not divide it.
5:9 And he shall sprinkle some of the blood of the offering for sin upon the wall of the altar; but the remaining blood he shall drain at the base of the altar, for it is for sin.
5:10 And he shall prepare the second as a whole burnt offering, as is fitting. And the priest shall make atonement for the sin which he has committed, and he shall be forgiven.
5:11 And if his hand cannot obtain a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons, he shall bring his gift for the sin which he has committed: a tenth of an oiphi of fine flour for sin. He shall pour no oil upon it, neither shall he put frankincense upon it, for it is for sin.
5:12 And he shall bring it unto the priest; and the priest shall take from it a full handful, and place its memorial portion upon the altar of whole burnt offerings unto the Lord. It is a sin offering.
5:13 And the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin which he has committed in any one of these matters, and he shall be forgiven. But what remains shall be the priest’s, as the sacrifice of fine flour.
5:14 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
5:15 If a soul is overtaken by forgetfulness, and sins unintentionally concerning the holy things of the Lord, he shall bring unto the Lord for his offence an unblemished ram from the flock, valued in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel, for the offence which he has committed.
5:16 And he shall repay that wherein he has sinned against the holy things, and add a fifth part unto it, and give it unto the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he shall be forgiven.
5:17 And if a soul sins, and does any of the things which the commandments of the Lord forbid, though he did not know it, and incurs guilt and bears the sin,
5:18 he shall bring unto the priest an unblemished ram from the flock, valued in silver, for a guilt offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him for his ignorance, wherein he acted ignorantly and did not know it, and he shall be forgiven.
5:19 For he has indeed committed an offence before the Lord.
5:20 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
5:21 If a soul sins, and utterly disregards the commandments of the Lord, and deals falsely with his neighbour concerning a deposit, or a partnership, or robbery, or any wrong which he has done unto his neighbour,
5:22 or finds a lost thing and deals falsely concerning it, and swears unjustly concerning any of all the things which a man may do so as to sin therein,
5:23 then it shall be, when he has sinned and incurred guilt, that he shall return the plunder which he seized, or that which he obtained by wrong, or the deposit which was entrusted unto him, or the lost thing which he found,
5:24 concerning every matter about which he swore unjustly. And he shall repay the principal, and add a fifth part unto it; he shall repay it unto the one to whom it belongs, on the day when he is convicted.
5:25 And for his offence he shall bring unto the Lord an unblemished ram from the flock, of a value corresponding to the offence which he has committed against Him.
5:26 And the priest shall make atonement for him before the Lord, and he shall be forgiven concerning any of all the things which he has done and wherein he has offended Him.

Editorial note: The source numbering continues through 5:26. Clauses about knowledge, guilt, restitution and the added fifth are retained; modern verb forms clarify their English connections.

Leviticus 6

6:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
6:2 Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of whole burning. The whole burnt offering shall remain upon its burning place upon the altar all night until morning; and the fire of the altar shall burn upon it, and shall not be quenched.
6:3 And the priest shall put on a linen tunic, and he shall put linen undergarments about his body; and he shall remove from the altar the burnt remains of the whole burning which the fire has consumed, and place them beside the altar.
6:4 And he shall take off his robe, and put on another robe, and carry the burnt remains outside the camp unto a clean place.
6:5 And the fire upon the altar shall burn from it, and shall not be quenched; and the priest shall burn wood upon it in the morning, and pile the whole burnt offering upon it, and place upon it the fat of the offering of deliverance.
6:6 And a fire shall burn continually upon the altar; it shall not be quenched.
6:7 This is the law of the sacrifice which the sons of Aaron shall bring before the Lord, opposite the altar.
6:8 And he shall take a handful from the fine flour of the sacrifice, with its oil and the frankincense upon the sacrifice, and offer it up upon the altar as an offering: a sweet savour, its memorial portion unto the Lord.
6:9 But Aaron and his sons shall eat what remains of it. It shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place; they shall eat it in the court of the Tent of Testimony.
6:10 It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it unto them as a portion from the offerings of the Lord; it is most holy, as the offering for sin and the guilt offering.
6:11 Every male among the priests shall eat it; it is an everlasting statute throughout your generations, from the offerings of the Lord. Whosoever touches them shall be sanctified.
6:12 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
6:13 This is the gift of Aaron and his sons which they shall bring unto the Lord on the day when you anoint him: a tenth of an oiphi of fine flour as a continual sacrifice, half in the morning and half in the evening.
6:14 It shall be made upon a griddle with oil; he shall bring it mixed, in rolled pieces, a sacrifice of fragments, a sacrifice for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
6:15 The priest from among his sons who is anointed in his stead shall prepare it. It is an everlasting law; it shall all be fully performed.
6:16 And every sacrifice of a priest shall be wholly burned, and shall not be eaten.
6:17 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
6:18 Speak unto Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of the sin offering. In the place where they slaughter the whole burnt offering, they shall slaughter the offerings for sin before the Lord; they are most holy.
6:19 The priest who offers it up shall eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Testimony.
6:20 Whosoever touches its flesh shall be sanctified; and if any of its blood is sprinkled upon a garment, what it was sprinkled upon shall be washed in a holy place.
6:21 And the earthenware vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; but if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, he shall scour it and rinse it with water.
6:22 Every male among the priests shall eat them; they are most holy unto the Lord.
6:23 And none of the offerings for sin whose blood is brought into the Tent of Testimony to make atonement in the holy place shall be eaten; they shall be burned with fire.

Editorial note: Source numbering begins this chapter with the altar instructions. Reordering clarifies who eats the remainder and where blood is washed; the ritual objects and repeated commands remain.

Leviticus 7

7:1 And this is the law of the ram for the guilt offering; it is most holy.
7:2 In the place where they slaughter the whole burnt offering, they shall slaughter the ram of the guilt offering before the Lord; and he shall pour the blood round about upon the base of the altar.
7:3 And he shall bring all its fat from it: the loin, and all the fat which covers the inward parts, and all the fat upon the inward parts,
7:4 and the two kidneys, and the fat upon them which is by the thighs, and the lobe upon the liver; these he shall remove with the kidneys.
7:5 And the priest shall offer them up upon the altar as an offering unto the Lord. It is for guilt.
7:6 Every male among the priests shall eat them; they shall eat them in a holy place. They are most holy.
7:7 As the offering for sin, so also the guilt offering: one law is theirs. It shall belong unto the priest who makes atonement with it.
7:8 And the priest who brings a man’s whole burnt offering shall have the hide of the whole burnt offering which he brings.
7:9 And every sacrifice made in an oven, and every sacrifice made upon a hearth or upon a griddle, shall belong unto the priest who brings it.
7:10 And every sacrifice prepared with oil, or not so prepared, shall belong unto all the sons of Aaron, unto each an equal share.
7:11 This is the law of the sacrifice of deliverance which they shall bring unto the Lord.
7:12 If he brings it for praise, he shall bring with the sacrifice of praise loaves of fine flour prepared with oil, unleavened wafers spread with oil, and fine flour mixed with oil.
7:13 He shall bring his gifts with leavened loaves, with a sacrifice of praise and deliverance.
7:14 And he shall bring one of all his gifts as a portion set apart unto the Lord; it shall belong unto the priest who pours the blood of the offering of deliverance.
7:15 And the flesh of the sacrifice of praise and deliverance shall be his, and shall be eaten on the day when it is given; they shall leave none of it until morning.
7:16 And if he sacrifices his gift as a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day when he brings his sacrifice, and on the morrow.
7:17 And what remains of the flesh of the sacrifice until the third day shall be burned with fire.
7:18 But if he eats any of the flesh on the third day, it shall not be accepted for him who brings it, neither shall it be reckoned unto him; it is a defilement. And the soul that eats of it shall bear the sin.
7:19 And flesh which touches any unclean thing shall not be eaten; it shall be burned with fire. Whosoever is clean shall eat flesh.
7:20 But the soul that eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance which is the Lord’s, while his uncleanness is upon him, that soul shall perish from among his people.
7:21 And if a soul touches any unclean thing, human uncleanness, or unclean four-footed beasts, or any unclean abomination, and eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of deliverance which is the Lord’s, that soul shall perish from among his people.
7:22 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
7:23 Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying: You shall eat no fat of cattle, or sheep, or goats.
7:24 And fat from animals that have died, or from those torn by wild beasts, may be used for any work; but it shall in no wise be eaten as food.
7:25 Whosoever eats fat from the livestock from which an offering is brought unto the Lord, that soul shall perish from among his people.
7:26 You shall eat no blood in any dwelling place of yours, whether of birds or of livestock.
7:27 Every soul that eats blood, that soul shall perish from among his people.
7:28 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying,
7:29 And you shall speak unto the sons of Israel, saying: He who brings a sacrifice of deliverance unto the Lord shall bring his gift unto the Lord from the sacrifice of deliverance.
7:30 His hands shall bring the offerings unto the Lord: the fat upon the breast and the lobe of the liver; he shall bring them to place them as a gift before the Lord.
7:31 And the priest shall offer up the fat upon the altar; and the breast shall be Aaron’s and his sons’.
7:32 And the right foreleg you shall give as a portion set apart unto the priest from your sacrifices of deliverance.
7:33 He among the sons of Aaron who brings the blood of the offering of deliverance and the fat shall have the right foreleg as his portion.
7:34 For the breast of the presentation and the foreleg of the portion set apart have I taken from the sons of Israel, from your sacrifices of deliverance; and I have given them unto Aaron the priest and unto his sons as an everlasting statute from the sons of Israel.
7:35 This is Aaron’s anointing and the anointing of his sons from the offerings of the Lord, on the day when he brought them forward to serve as priests unto the Lord,
7:36 as the Lord commanded to be given them from the sons of Israel on the day when he anointed them: an everlasting statute throughout their generations.
7:37 This is the law of the whole burnt offerings, and the sacrifice, and the offering for sin, and the guilt offering, and the consecration, and the sacrifice of deliverance,
7:38 as the Lord commanded Moses on Mount Sinai on the day when He commanded the sons of Israel to bring their gifts before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai.

Editorial note: The distinctions among offerings, participants and permitted days of eating remain. “Morrow” is retained where it is clear; present-tense verb endings follow the current Bespoke convention.

Leviticus 8

8:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
8:2 Take Aaron and his sons, his robes, the anointing oil, the calf for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread.
8:3 And assemble the whole congregation at the entrance of the Tent of Testimony.
8:4 And Moses did as the Lord directed him, and he assembled the congregation at the door of the Tent of Testimony.
8:5 And Moses said unto the congregation: This is the word which the Lord commanded to be done.
8:6 And Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.
8:7 And he clothed him with the tunic, girded him with the belt, clothed him with the under-robe, and put the shoulder-piece on him. He girded him according to the shoulder-piece’s workmanship and fastened him securely in it.
8:8 And he placed the oracle-piece on it and put Manifestation and Truth upon the oracle-piece.
8:9 And he put the turban on his head and placed on its front the gold plate, the consecrated holy object, as the Lord directed Moses.
8:10 And Moses took some of the anointing oil,
8:11 and sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times. He anointed the altar and consecrated it, and all its utensils, the basin and its stand, and consecrated them. He anointed the tent and everything in it and consecrated it.
8:12 And Moses poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron’s head, anointed him, and consecrated him.
8:13 And Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward, clothed them with tunics, girded them with belts, and put turbans on them, as the Lord directed Moses.
8:14 And Moses brought the calf for the sin offering forward, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the calf of the sin offering.
8:15 And he slaughtered it, and Moses took some of the blood and put it with his finger all around on the altar’s horns, cleansing the altar. He poured out the blood at the altar’s base and consecrated it, to make atonement upon it.
8:16 And Moses took all the fat on the inward parts, the lobe on the liver, both kidneys and the fat on them, and Moses offered them up on the altar.
8:17 And he burned up the calf, its hide, its flesh, and its dung with fire outside the camp, as the Lord directed Moses.
8:18 And Moses brought the ram for the whole burnt offering forward, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram’s head.
8:19 And Moses slaughtered the ram, and Moses poured the blood all around against the altar.
8:20 And he divided the ram limb by limb, and Moses offered up the head, the limbs, and the fat.
8:21 And he washed the belly and feet with water, and Moses offered up the whole ram on the altar: a whole burnt offering for a sweet savour, an offering unto the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.
8:22 And Moses brought the second ram forward, a ram of consecration, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram’s head.
8:23 And he slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it upon Aaron’s right earlobe, the extremity of his right hand, and the extremity of his right foot.
8:24 And Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward, and Moses put some of the blood on their right earlobes, the extremities of their right hands, and the extremities of their right feet. Moses poured the blood all around against the altar.
8:25 And he took the fat, the loin, the fat on the belly, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat on them, and the right foreleg.
8:26 And from the basket of consecration before the Lord he took one unleavened loaf, one loaf with oil, and one wafer, and placed them on the fat and the right foreleg.

Leviticus 11

11:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:
11:2 Speak to the sons of Israel, saying: These are the animals you may eat among all the animals on the earth.
11:3 Every animal among the livestock that divides the hoof, and cleaves its claws into two hoofs, and brings up the cud: these you may eat.
11:4 Nevertheless, of these you shall not eat: among those that bring up the cud and those that divide the hoof and cleave the claws, the camel, because it brings up the cud, but does not divide the hoof; it is unclean unto you;
11:5 and the hare, because it brings up the cud but does not divide the hoof; it is unclean for you;
11:6 and the choirogryllios, because it brings up the cud but does not divide the hoof; it is unclean for you;
11:7 and the pig, because it divides the hoof and has cleft hoof-claws but does not bring up the cud; it is unclean for you.
11:8 You shall not eat their flesh or touch their carcasses; they are unclean for you.
11:9 And these you may eat among all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas and streams, you may eat.
11:10 And whatever has neither fins nor scales in the water, in the seas and streams, among all that the waters bring forth and every living creature in the water, is an abomination.
11:11 And they shall be abominations for you. You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall abhor their carcasses.
11:12 And whatever among the things in the water has no fins and scales is an abomination for you.
11:13 And these you shall abhor among the birds; they shall not be eaten, for they are an abomination: the eagle, the gryps, and the sea-eagle;
11:14 and the vulture, the kite, and those like it;
11:15 and the raven and those like it;
11:16 and the ostrich, the owl, the gull and those like it, and the hawk and those like it;
11:17 and the night-raven, the diving bird, and the ibis;
11:18 and the purple waterbird, the pelican, and the swan;
11:19 and the owl, the heron, the plover and those like it, the hoopoe, and the bat.
11:20 And every winged creeping thing that walks on four is an abomination for you.
11:21 But among the winged creeping things that walk on four, you may eat those that have legs above their feet for leaping on the earth.
11:22 And these among them you may eat: the brouchos and those like it, the attakes and those like it, the locust and those like it, and the snake-fighter and those like it.
11:23 Every winged creeping thing that has four feet is an abomination for you.
11:24 And by these you shall become defiled: everyone who touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening,
11:25 and whosoever carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening.
11:26 Every animal that divides the hoof and has cleft claws but does not chew the cud shall be unclean for you. Whosoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening.
11:27 And among all wild animals that walk on four, whatever walks on its hands shall be unclean for you. Whosoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean until evening.
11:28 And whosoever carries their carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening. They are unclean for you.
11:29 And these are unclean for you among the creeping things that creep on the earth: the weasel, the mouse, and the land crocodile;
11:30 the shrew, the chameleon, the spotted lizard, the lizard, and the mole.
11:31 These are unclean for you among all the creeping things on the earth. Whosoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening.
11:32 And anything on which one of them falls when dead shall become unclean: any wooden utensil, garment, skin, or sack, any article used for work. It shall be dipped in water and remain unclean until evening; then it shall be clean.
11:33 And if one of them falls inside any earthenware vessel, everything in it shall become unclean, and the vessel shall be shattered.
11:34 And any food that may be eaten, on which water comes, shall become unclean, and any drink that may be drunk in any vessel shall become unclean.
11:35 And anything on which any of their carcasses falls shall become unclean. Ovens and pot-stands shall be demolished; these are unclean and shall be unclean for you.
11:36 But springs of water, a cistern, and a gathering of water shall remain clean; whosoever touches their carcasses shall be unclean.
11:37 If any of their carcasses falls on seed intended for sowing, it remains clean.
11:38 But if water is poured on any seed and any of their carcasses falls on it, it is unclean for you.
11:39 If an animal that you may eat dies, whosoever touches its carcass shall be unclean until evening.
11:40 And whosoever eats from these carcasses shall wash his garments and be unclean until evening. Whosoever carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
11:41 And every creeping thing that creeps upon the earth shall be an abomination for you; it shall not be eaten.
11:42 And whatever moves on its belly, whatever walks on four continually, whatever has many feet among all the creeping things that creep upon the earth, you shall not eat, for it is an abomination for you.
11:43 And you shall in no wise make your souls abominable through any creeping things that creep upon the earth; neither shall you be defiled through them, nor shall you be unclean through them;
11:44 for I am the Lord your God; and you shall be sanctified and you shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord your God. And you shall not defile your souls through any creeping things that move upon the earth;
11:45 for I am the Lord who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; and you shall be holy, for I am holy, the Lord.
11:46 This is the law concerning animals, birds, every creature that moves in the water, and every creature that creeps upon the earth,
11:47 to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creatures that are eaten and the living creatures that are not eaten.

Editorial note: The received animal names and classification language remain provisional. “Hands”, “cud” and the distinctions among touching, carrying and eating are retained; these names have not received a new specialist zoological review.

Leviticus 13

13:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:
13:2 If anyone has on the skin of his body a bright scar-like mark, and it becomes a lesion of leprosy on his skin, he shall be brought unto Aaron the priest or to one of his sons, the priests.
13:3 And the priest shall examine the lesion on the skin of his body. If the hair in the lesion has turned white and the lesion appears lower than the skin of his body, it is a lesion of leprosy. The priest shall examine him and declare him unclean.
13:4 If the bright spot on his skin is white but does not appear lower than the skin, and its hair has not turned white, but it is faint, the priest shall isolate the lesion for seven days.
13:5 And the priest shall examine the lesion on the seventh day; and, behold, if the lesion remains before him, and the lesion has not shifted in the skin, the priest shall isolate him for a second seven days.
13:6 And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day a second time; and, behold, if the lesion is faint, and the lesion has not shifted in the skin, the priest shall declare him clean, for it is a mark; and having washed his garments, he shall be clean.
13:7 But if the mark changes and shifts in the skin after the priest has examined him for cleansing, he shall appear before the priest a second time,
13:8 and the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the mark has shifted in the skin, the priest shall declare him unclean; it is leprosy.
13:9 And when a lesion of leprosy occurs in anyone, he shall come unto the priest.
13:10 And the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if there is a white scar in the skin, and it has turned the hair white, and there is some sound living flesh in the scar,
13:11 it is longstanding leprosy on the skin of his body. The priest shall declare him unclean and isolate him, for he is unclean.
13:12 But if the leprosy breaks out abundantly in the skin, and the leprosy covers all the skin of the lesion from head to foot, throughout the whole view of the priest,
13:13 the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the leprosy has covered all the skin of his body, the priest shall declare him clean as to the lesion, because it has all turned white; it is clean.
13:14 And on whatever day living skin appears on him, he shall be defiled.
13:15 And the priest shall examine the sound skin, and the sound skin shall make him unclean, because it is unclean; it is leprosy.
13:16 But if the sound skin is restored and turns white, he shall come unto the priest,
13:17 and the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the lesion has turned white, the priest shall declare the lesion clean; he is clean.
13:18 And if an ulcer occurs on the skin of the body and heals,
13:19 and a white scar or a whitish or fiery-red bright spot appears in the place of the ulcer, he shall appear before the priest.
13:20 And the priest shall examine it; and, behold, if its appearance is lower than the skin, and its hair has turned white, the priest shall declare him unclean; it is leprosy which has blossomed in the ulcer.
13:21 But if the priest examines it, and, behold, there is no white hair in it, and it is not lower than the skin of the body, but is faint, the priest shall isolate him seven days.
13:22 If it spreads in the skin, the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a lesion of leprosy that has blossomed in the ulcer.
13:23 But if the bright spot stays in its place and does not spread, it is the scar of the ulcer; the priest shall declare him clean.
13:24 And if the skin of the body has a burn from fire, and the healed area of the burn on his skin becomes a shining, bright, whitish-red or completely white spot,
13:25 the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the hair in the shining spot has turned white, and its appearance is lower than the skin, it is leprosy which has blossomed in the burn; and the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a lesion of leprosy.
13:26 But if the priest examines it, and, behold, there is no white hair in the shining spot, and it is not lower than the skin, but is faint, the priest shall isolate him seven days.
13:27 And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day; but if it has indeed spread in the skin, the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a lesion of leprosy which has blossomed in the ulcer.
13:28 But if the shining spot stays in its place, has not spread in the skin, and is faint, it is the scar of the burn. The priest shall declare him clean, for it is the mark of the burn.
13:29 And if a man or woman has a lesion of leprosy on the head or beard,
13:30 the priest shall examine the lesion; and, behold, if its appearance is more hollow than the skin, and there is fine yellowish hair in it, the priest shall declare him unclean; it is a broken patch, leprosy of the head or leprosy of the beard.
13:31 And if the priest examines the lesion of the broken patch, and, behold, its appearance is not more hollow than the skin, and there is no yellowish hair in it, the priest shall isolate the lesion of the broken patch seven days.
13:32 And the priest shall examine the lesion on the seventh day; and, behold, if the broken patch has not spread, and there is no yellowish hair in it, and the appearance of the broken patch is not hollow below the skin,
13:33 then the skin shall be shaved, but the broken patch shall not be shaved. The priest shall isolate the broken patch for a second seven days.
13:34 And the priest shall examine the broken patch on the seventh day; and, behold, if the broken patch has not spread in the skin after he was shaved, and its appearance is not hollow below the skin, the priest shall declare him clean; and having washed his garments, he shall be clean.
13:35 But if the broken patch has indeed spread in the skin after he has been cleansed,
13:36 the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the broken patch has spread in the skin, the priest shall not inspect for yellow hair, for he is unclean.
13:37 But if before him the broken patch has remained in place and black hair has grown in it, the broken patch has healed. He is clean, and the priest shall declare him clean.
13:38 And if a man or woman has shining, whitish patches on the skin of the body,
13:39 the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if there are shining, whitish patches on the skin of his body, it is a white eruption; he is clean. It blossoms in the skin of his body; he is clean.
13:40 If anyone loses the hair of his head, he is bald; he is clean.
13:41 If he loses the hair at the front of his head, he is bald at the forehead; he is clean.
13:42 But if there is a white or fiery-red lesion on his bald head or bald forehead, it is leprosy on his bald head or bald forehead.
13:43 And the priest shall examine him; and, behold, if the appearance of the lesion on his bald head or his bald forehead is white tinged with red, like the appearance of leprosy on the skin of his body,
13:44 he is a man with leprosy; the priest shall surely declare him unclean; his lesion is upon his head.
13:45 And he that has leprosy, in whom the lesion is, shall have his garments loosened and his head uncovered, and shall put a covering about his mouth, and shall be called unclean;
13:46 all the days in which the lesion is upon him, being unclean, he shall be unclean; he shall dwell apart; his dwelling shall be outside the camp.
13:47 And if a garment has a lesion of leprosy, whether a woollen garment or a garment of coarse flax,
13:48 or in warp or weft, or in linen or wool, or in leather or any worked leather,
13:49 and the lesion in the leather, garment, warp, weft, or any worked leather article is greenish or fiery red, it is a lesion of leprosy, and he shall show it unto the priest.
13:50 And the priest shall examine the lesion, and the priest shall isolate the lesion for seven days.
13:51 And on the seventh day the priest shall examine the lesion. If it has spread in the garment, warp, weft, or leather, in whatever use is made of leather, the lesion is persistent leprosy; it is unclean.
13:52 He shall burn up the garment, the warp or weft in wool or linen, or any leather article that has the lesion, for it is persistent leprosy; it shall be burned up with fire.
13:53 But if the priest examines it and the lesion has not spread in the garment, warp, weft, or any leather article,
13:54 then the priest shall give instructions, and he shall wash the article with the lesion; the priest shall isolate the lesion for a second seven days.
13:55 And after it has been washed, the priest shall examine the lesion. If it has not changed its appearance and has not spread, it is unclean; it shall be burned up with fire. It has become established in the garment, warp, or weft.
13:56 And if the priest examines it and the lesion is faint after washing, he shall tear it out of the garment, leather, warp, or weft.
13:57 If it appears again in the garment, warp, weft, or any leather article, it is blossoming leprosy; the affected article shall be burned up with fire.
13:58 And a garment, warp, weft, or any leather article that is washed, from which the lesion departs, shall be washed a second time and be clean.
13:59 This is the law of a lesion of leprosy in a garment of wool or coarse flax, or in warp or weft, or in any leather article, for declaring it clean or unclean.

Editorial note: The sequences of inspection, waiting and re-examination remain. The received descriptions of lesions, hair, colour and garments are retained without claiming a modern medical diagnosis.

14:30 And he shall prepare one of the turtledoves or young pigeons, as his hand can provide,
14:31 one for sin and one for a whole burnt offering, together with the sacrifice. The priest shall make atonement before the Lord for the person being cleansed.

14:33 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:

Leviticus 23

23:4 These are the feasts for the Lord, holy convocations, which you shall proclaim at their proper times.
23:5 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the evenings, is the Lord’s Passover.
23:6 And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread unto the Lord. You shall eat unleavened bread for seven days.
23:7 And on the first day you shall have a holy convocation. You shall do no laborious work.
23:8 And you shall bring whole burnt offerings unto the Lord for seven days. The seventh day shall be a holy convocation for you; you shall do no laborious work.
23:9 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
23:10 Say unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: When you enter the land which I give unto you, and reap its harvest, you shall bring a sheaf as the first portion of your harvest unto the priest;
23:11 and he shall offer up the sheaf before the Lord, acceptable for you; the priest shall offer it up on the morrow after the first day.
23:12 And on the day you bring the sheaf, you shall offer an unblemished year-old sheep as a whole burnt offering unto the Lord.
23:13 And its sacrifice shall be two tenths of fine flour kneaded with oil, a sacrifice unto the Lord, a sweet savour unto the Lord. Its drink offering shall be a quarter of a hin of wine.
23:14 And you shall eat neither bread nor roasted fresh grain until that very day, until you bring the gifts to your God. This is an everlasting ordinance throughout your generations in every dwelling of yours.
23:15 And you shall count from the day after the Sabbath, from the day you bring the sheaf of the presentation, seven complete weeks.
23:16 Until the day after the last week you shall count fifty days, and then present a new sacrifice unto the Lord.

23:18 And with the loaves you shall bring seven unblemished year-old lambs, one calf from the herd, and two unblemished rams. They shall be a whole burnt offering unto the Lord, with their sacrifices and drink offerings, a sacrifice, a sweet savour unto the Lord.
23:19 And they shall offer one male young goat from the goats for sin and two year-old lambs as a sacrifice of deliverance with the firstfruits loaves.
23:20 And the priest shall present them with the firstfruits loaves as a presentation before the Lord, with the two lambs. They shall be holy unto the Lord; they shall belong unto the priest who presents them.
23:21 And you shall proclaim this day a convocation; it shall be holy for you. You shall do no laborious work on it. This is an everlasting ordinance throughout your generations in every dwelling of yours.

23:27 And on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement; it shall be a holy convocation unto you, and you shall humble your souls and bring a whole burnt offering unto the Lord.
23:28 You shall do no work on that very day, for this is a Day of Atonement for you, to make atonement for you before the Lord your God.
23:29 Every soul that shall not be humbled on that very day shall be destroyed from among his people.
23:30 And every soul that does work on that very day, that soul shall perish from among his people.

Leviticus 25

25:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses on Mount Sinai, saying:
25:2 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: When you enter the land which I give unto you, the land which I give unto you shall rest, a Sabbath unto the Lord.
25:3 For six years you shall sow your field, and for six years you shall prune your vine and gather its fruit.
25:4 But in the seventh year the land shall have a Sabbath, a rest, a Sabbath unto the Lord. You shall not sow your field or prune your vine.
25:5 And you shall not reap the things growing of themselves in your field or gather the grapes of your consecration. It shall be a year of rest for the land.
25:6 And the land’s Sabbath produce shall be food for you, your male slave, your female slave, your hired worker, and the lodger attached to you.
25:7 And all its produce shall also be food for your livestock and the wild animals in your land.
25:8 And you shall count unto yourself seven rests of years, seven years seven times; and the seven weeks of years shall be unto you forty-nine years.
25:9 And you shall make proclamation with a trumpet sound throughout your land in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month. On the Day of Atonement you shall make proclamation with a trumpet throughout your land.
25:10 And you shall consecrate the fiftieth year, and proclaim release throughout the land unto all who dwell therein; this shall be a year of release, a signal unto you; and each one shall depart unto his possession, and each of you shall depart unto his ancestral homeland.
25:11 This is a signal of release: the fiftieth year shall be a year for you. You shall not sow, reap what grows of itself, or gather its consecrated produce.
25:12 For it is a signal of release; it shall be holy to you. You shall eat its produce from the fields.
25:13 In the year of release, at its signal, each shall return to his possession.
25:14 But if one sells property unto your neighbour, or you acquire it from your neighbour, let no man oppress his neighbour.
25:15 According to the number of years after the signal you shall acquire from your neighbour; according to the number of harvest years he shall sell to you.
25:16 As the years increase, you shall increase its purchase price; as the years decrease, you shall decrease its purchase price, for he is selling you a number of harvests.
25:17 Let no man oppress his neighbour, and you shall fear the Lord your God; I am the Lord your God.
25:18 And you shall do all My statutes and all My judgments, keep them, and do them; then you shall dwell confidently on the land.
25:19 And the land shall yield its produce, and you shall eat to fullness and dwell confidently upon it.
25:20 But if you say, “What shall we eat in this seventh year, if we neither sow nor gather our produce?”
25:21 then I will send My blessing to you in the sixth year, and it shall produce its harvest for the three years.
25:22 And you shall sow in the eighth year and eat old produce. Until the ninth year, until its produce comes in, you shall eat old stores from the old.
25:23 And the land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is Mine; for you are resident foreigners and lodgers before Me;
25:24 and throughout all the land of your possession, you shall provide redemption for the land.
25:25 But if your brother who is with you becomes poor, and sells some of his possession, and his nearest kinsman comes, he shall redeem that which his brother sold.
25:26 If someone has no kinsman but prospers by his hand and enough is found for his redemption,
25:27 then he shall calculate the years of his sale and repay the excess to the man to whom he sold himself; then he shall depart to his possession.
25:28 If his hand does not prosper enough to repay him, the sale shall remain with the one who acquired it until the sixth year of release. It shall go out at the release, and he shall depart to his possession.
25:29 But if anyone sells a dwelling house in a walled city, its redemption shall be possible until a year of days is fulfilled; that shall be its redemption period.
25:30 If it is not redeemed before its whole year is complete, the house in the city that has a wall shall be confirmed permanently to its buyer throughout his generations. It shall not go out at the release.
25:31 But houses in settlements that have no surrounding wall shall be reckoned with the field of the land. They shall always be redeemable and shall go out at the release.
25:32 And the cities of the Levites—the houses in their cities of possession—shall always be redeemable by the Levites.
25:33 And whosoever redeems from the Levites, the sale of their houses in the city of their possession shall go out at the release; for the houses in the cities of the Levites are their possession among the sons of Israel.
25:34 And the fields set apart for their cities shall not be sold, for this is their everlasting possession.
25:35 If your brother becomes poor and grows weak in his hands beside you, you shall support him as a resident foreigner and a lodger, and your brother shall live with you.
25:36 You shall take no interest or increase from him, and you shall fear your God; I am the Lord; and your brother shall live with you.
25:37 You shall not give him your silver at interest or give your food for an increase.
25:38 I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to be your God.
25:39 If your brother is brought low beside you and is sold to you, he shall not serve you with the service of a household slave.
25:40 He shall be with you as a hired worker or a lodger; he shall work beside you until the year of release.
25:41 And he shall go out at the release, and his children with him. He shall depart to his kin and return to his ancestral possession.
25:42 For these are My household slaves, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt; one shall not be sold with the sale of a household slave.
25:43 You shall not wear him down with toil, but you shall fear the Lord your God.
25:44 And your male slave and your female slave, as many as you have from the nations which are round about you: from them you may acquire a male slave and a female slave.
25:45 And you may also acquire from the sons of the lodgers among you and from their relatives who come to be in your land; they shall become your possession.
25:46 And you shall distribute them to your children after you, and they shall be held by you permanently. But among your brethren, the sons of Israel, no one shall wear down his brother with toils.
25:47 If the hand of the resident foreigner or lodger beside you gains means, and your brother becomes needy and is sold to the resident foreigner or lodger beside you, a foreigner by birth,
25:48 after he is sold to him, he shall have a right of redemption. One of his brethren may redeem him,
25:49 his father’s brother or the son of his father’s brother may redeem him, or one of his close kin from his tribe may redeem him; but if, having prospered by his hands, he redeems himself,
25:50 then he shall calculate with the one who acquired him from the year he sold himself to him until the year of release. His sale price shall be like a hired worker’s pay; he shall be with him year by year.
25:51 If many years remain for someone, he shall repay his redemption from his sale money in proportion to them.
25:52 If few years remain until the year of release, he shall calculate with him according to his years and repay his redemption.
25:53 He shall be with him as a hired worker year by year; you shall not wear him down with toil before you.
25:54 If he is not redeemed by these means, he shall go out in the year of release, he and his children with him.
25:55 For the sons of Israel are household slaves to Me; these are My servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.

Editorial note: Release, redemption, land and labour remain distinct. The inherited “sixth year of release” in 25:28 is retained after a selected Rahlfs–Hanhart main-text check. It is not silently replaced by a more familiar reading.

Leviticus 26

26:45 And I will remember for them the former covenant, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of a house of slavery, before the nations to be their God. I am the Lord.
26:46 These are the judgments and the ordinances and the law which the Lord gave between Himself and the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

Editorial note: The repeated conditional structure, consequences and covenant language remain. “You” and “your” serve ordinary address; divine first-person capitals are retained as an editorial convention.

Leviticus 27

27:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
27:2 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: Whosoever vows a vow for the valuation of his soul unto the Lord,
27:3 the valuation of a male from twenty to sixty years old shall be fifty didrachmas of silver, by the sacred weight.
27:4 For a female the assessed value shall be thirty didrachmas.
27:5 From five to twenty years old, the valuation shall be twenty didrachmas for a male and ten didrachmas for a female.
27:6 From one month to five years old, the valuation shall be five didrachmas of silver for a male and three didrachmas for a female.
27:7 From sixty years old and upward, the valuation shall be fifteen didrachmas of silver for a male and ten didrachmas for a female.
27:8 But if he is too poor for the valuation, he shall stand before the priest, and the priest shall value him; the priest shall value him according to the strength of the hand of the one who vowed.
27:9 If it concerns livestock of a kind presented as a gift unto the Lord, whatever one gives from these unto the Lord shall be holy.
27:10 He shall not exchange it, good for bad or bad for good. If he does exchange it, animal for animal, both it and its substitute shall be holy.
27:11 If it concerns any unclean livestock of a kind not presented as a gift unto the Lord, he shall set the animal before the priest.
27:12 And the priest shall value it, between good and bad. As the priest values it, so it shall stand.
27:13 If he does redeem it, he shall add a fifth to its valuation.
27:14 And if a man consecrates his house as holy unto the Lord, the priest shall value it, between good and bad. As the priest values it, so it shall stand.
27:15 If the one who consecrated it redeems his house, he shall add a fifth of the silver of the valuation to it, and it shall be his.
27:16 If a man consecrates unto the Lord part of the field of his possession, the valuation shall be according to its seed: fifty didrachmas of silver for a kor of barley.
27:17 If he consecrates his field from the year of release, it shall stand according to its valuation.
27:18 If he consecrates his field later, after the release, the priest shall calculate the silver for him according to the years remaining until the year of release, and a deduction shall be made from its assessment.
27:19 If the one who consecrated the field redeems it, he shall add a fifth of the silver to its valuation, and it shall be his.
27:20 If he does not redeem the field and sells the field to another man, he shall no longer redeem it.
27:21 When the release has passed, the field shall be holy unto the Lord like land set apart. It shall become the priest’s possession.
27:22 If he consecrates unto the Lord part of a field he acquired, which is not part of the field of his inherited possession,
27:23 the priest shall calculate for him the total valuation from the year of release, and he shall repay the valuation on that day as holy unto the Lord.
27:24 And in the year of release the field shall be returned to the man from whom he acquired it, whose possession the land was.
27:25 And every valuation shall be by sacred weights; the didrachma shall be twenty obols.
27:26 And every firstborn which is born among your livestock shall belong unto the Lord, and none shall consecrate it; whether calf or sheep, it is the Lord’s.
27:27 If it is one of the unclean four-footed animals, he shall exchange it according to its valuation and add a fifth to it, and it shall be his. If he does not redeem it, it shall be sold according to its assessed value.
27:28 But every devoted thing which a man dedicates unto the Lord, of all that he has, from man unto livestock, and from the field of his possession, he shall neither sell nor redeem; every devoted thing shall be most holy unto the Lord.
27:29 And whatsoever is devoted from among men shall not be redeemed, but shall surely be put to death.
27:30 Every tithe of the land, from the seed of the land and the fruit of trees, belongs unto the Lord; it is holy unto the Lord.
27:31 But if a man indeed redeems his tithe, he shall add a fifth part unto it, and it shall be his.
27:32 And every tithe of cattle and sheep, and everything that comes under the staff in the count—the tenth shall be holy unto the Lord.
27:33 You shall not exchange a good one for a bad one; but if you do indeed exchange it, its substitute also shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.
27:34 These are the commandments that the Lord commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai.

Editorial note: The distinct rules for valuations, redemption, devoted things and tithes remain. The added fifth and the stated exceptions are retained; 27:8 has clearer subject–verb order.
