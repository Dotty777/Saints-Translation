# Leviticus — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 27 chapters and the book index were revised, saved and read back on WordPress staging on 14 October 2026. The English pass covers all 859 existing verse units: 537 changed and 322 retained. This is a provisional editorial edition, with independent specialist review still pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English policy and the Genesis–Exodus manner govern this pass. Ordinary address uses you/your; Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine are reserved for direct address to God. Modern verb forms, clearer conditional grammar and restrained changes to sentence order improve readability. Ritual agents, imagery, repeated commands, technical terms and inherited source-specific readings remain.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Selected main-text checks are recorded in [source-checks.json](source-checks.json). No full Göttingen or apparatus collation, independent Greek specialist review or human read-aloud review is claimed. Earlier source and reception notes remain historical records, with their superseded voice policy clearly labelled.

## Preservation

The 859-unit source numbering is unchanged: chapter 5 has 26 units and chapter 6 has 23. Every existing verse ID, link, navigation element, image, script, style, serialized block comment and earlier note paragraph was preserved. The combined HTML export prefixes repeated historical note IDs with their chapter to keep the export's IDs unique; staging IDs are unchanged.

All 1,265 linked word-study rows still match the revised text. Only the matching phrase field changed in 93 rows; explanations, original-language fields, themes, colours, references and other fields were preserved. The revised 1:9 highlight was opened successfully in the browser, and the current note was visible in the Translation notes workspace.

The inherited negative in 14:39 and sixth-year reading in 25:28 remain after selected main-text inspection. These were not silently harmonized with a more familiar textual tradition.

The separate literal-smooth Saints Translation and comparison streams were not edited. No production-site changes were made. This is a Leviticus archive, not a full-site backup.
