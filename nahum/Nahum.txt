NAHUM 1

1:1 An oracle concerning Nineveh; the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.
1:2 The Lord is a jealous and avenging God, the Lord avenging with fury, the Lord avenging Himself on His adversaries, and He Himself removes His enemies.
1:3 The Lord is patient, and His strength is great; the Lord certainly will not acquit. His way is in consummation and shaking; clouds are the dust of His feet.
1:4 He threatens the sea and dries it up, and makes all the rivers desolate. Bashan and Carmel have dwindled, and Lebanon’s flowering things have failed.
1:5 Mountains shook before Him and hills were shaken; the whole earth recoiled before Him, and all who dwell in it.
1:6 Who will stand before His anger? Who will resist His furious wrath? His fury melts rulers, and rocks were crushed before Him.
1:7 The Lord is good to those who wait for Him in a day of affliction, and He knows those who revere Him.
1:8 With an advancing flood He will make a complete end of those who rise up, and darkness will pursue His enemies.
1:9 What do you devise against the Lord? He will make a complete end; He will not exact vengeance twice upon the same thing in affliction.
1:10 For it will be laid waste to their foundation, consumed like an entwining thorn-vine and like stubble full of dryness.
1:11 From you will come a scheme, devising evil things in opposition to the Lord.
1:12 Thus says the Lord who rules many waters: Even so they will be separated, and your report will no longer be heard.
1:13 And now I will crush his rod away from you and tear apart your bonds.
1:14 The Lord will command concerning you: No more will be sown from your name. From the house of your god I will destroy carved and cast images. I will make your burial, for they are swift.

NAHUM 2

2:1 Look, upon the mountains are the feet of one bringing good news and announcing peace! Celebrate your feasts, Judah; repay your vows. For they will no longer pass through you into decay. It has been completed; it has been removed.
2:2 One who breathes into your face has come up, rescuing from affliction. Watch the road; hold your waist firm; act courageously with great strength,
2:3 because the Lord turned away Jacob’s arrogance, as Israel’s arrogance; for those shaking them out shook out both them and their vine branches; they ruined
2:4 the weapons of their might from among people, mighty men playing in fire. The reins of their chariots are on the day of his preparation, and the horsemen will be thrown into uproar
2:5 in the roads, and the chariots will be thrown into confusion and entangled in the squares; their appearance is like torches of fire and lightning flashing past.
2:6 Their nobles will remember and flee by day; they will weaken on their journey, hurry to the walls and prepare their defenses.
2:7 The river gates were opened, and the royal palace collapsed.
2:8 The foundation was uncovered, and she was going up, and her slave women were being led away like doves murmuring in their hearts.
2:9 Nineveh—her waters are like a swimming pool; they were fleeing and did not stop, and there was no one looking back.
2:10 They were plundering silver; they were plundering gold. There was no limit to her adornment; they have been weighed down beyond all her desirable vessels.
2:11 Shaking out, tossing up and boiling over; broken hearts, loosening knees, pangs at every waist, and every face like the scorching of a pot.
2:12 Where is the lions’ dwelling and the pasture that belonged to the cubs, where a lion went to enter there, a lion’s cub, and no one frightened them?
2:13 A lion seized enough for his cubs and strangled for his lions; he filled his nest with prey and his dwelling with plunder.
2:14 Look, I am against you, says the Lord Almighty. I will burn your multitude in smoke; the sword will consume your lions. I will destroy your prey from the earth, and your deeds will never again be heard.

NAHUM 3

3:1 O city of bloodshed, wholly false, full of injustice! Prey will not be touched.
3:2 The sound of whips and the sound of shaking wheels, of a pursuing horse and a jolting chariot,
3:3 and of a mounting horseman, a flashing sword and glittering weapons, a multitude of slain and a grievous fall! There was no limit to her nations, and they will weaken in their bodies
3:4 because of the multitude of prostitution. A prostitute, beautiful and pleasing, mistress of potions, selling nations through her prostitution and tribes through her potions,
3:5 look, I am against you, says the Lord God Almighty. I will expose your hind parts over your face and show nations your shame and kingdoms your dishonor,
3:6 and cast abomination upon you according to your impurities, and make you an example,
3:7 and everyone seeing you will spring away and say, “Miserable Nineveh! Who will lament her? Where shall I seek consolation for her?”
3:8 Prepare a portion; tune a string; prepare a portion, Amon, who dwells among rivers, water around her, whose dominion was the sea and whose walls were water,
3:9 and Ethiopia was her strength, and Egypt, and there is no limit to the flight; Libyans became her helpers.
3:10 She too will go into deportation as a captive; they will dash her infants to the ground at the head of all her roads, cast lots for all her glorious things; and all her nobles will be bound in handcuffs.
3:11 You too will become drunk and be overlooked; you too will seek a place to stand away from enemies.
3:12 All your fortresses are fig trees having lookouts; if shaken, they will fall into the eater’s mouth.
3:13 Look, your people within you are like women! The gates of your land will open wide to your enemies, and fire will consume your bars.
3:14 Draw water for yourself for the siege, hold your fortresses firmly; step into mud, be trampled in straw, hold fast over the brick.
3:15 There fire will consume you; a sword will destroy you, devouring you like a locust, and you will become heavy like a crawling locust.
3:16 You multiplied your trading beyond the stars of heaven. A crawling locust rushed out and flew away.
3:17 Your mixed company leaped away like a young locust, like a locust perched on a fence on frosty days. The sun rose, it leaped away, and it did not know its place. Woe to them!
3:18 Your shepherds slumbered; the Assyrian king put your rulers to sleep. Your people departed upon the mountains, and there was no one receiving them.
3:19 There is no healing for your crushing; your wound has become inflamed. All who hear the report of you will clap their hands over you, for upon whom has your evil not continually come?
