Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; render Game as Prey at 3:1 to maintain the predatory image. The sexual and violent images remain in the text without added explanations presented as Scripture. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
