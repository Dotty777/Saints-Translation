# 1 Maccabees — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 16 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 924 verse units received an English editorial review: 110 changed and 814 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/1MA.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Clarified the law-book destruction and possession clauses in 56–57, preserving their sequence and recurring force. Retained lifted heart, made foreskins, wounded, put on shame, Jerusalem personification and altar distinction.

Pending: Opening sequence, anatomical/assimilation expression, tribute title, source dates, body/household referents in 61 and full critical collation.

**Chapter 2.** Modernized ordinary address and narrative verbs; made the command to listen at 2:65 natural. Preserved inward-parts, horn and burning-zeal images, present/past distinctions at 2:19 and 63, little children at 2:9, and ancestral names and counts.

Pending: Specialist assessment of kidneys/inward parts and anger according to judgment at 2:24, fate sequence at 2:63, and complete apparatus collation.

**Chapter 3.** Retained Thou/Thee/Thy in the appeal to Heaven at 3:50–53. Clarified the army-size contrast at 3:19 and the will-in-heaven sentence at 3:60; preserved giant, breastplate and hand imagery, and source subject shifts at 3:40. A historical query about 3:40 was checked: the accessed running text has the singular departure verb followed by plural arrival and encampment verbs, matching the retained subject shift.

Pending: Meaning of consulting the law book/idol likenesses at 3:48; subject shifts and elliptical divine reference at 3:40,60; full collation.

**Chapter 4.** Retained divine address at 4:30–33, melt imagery and repeated fell at 4:34. Modernized third-person verbs, clarified the decision to pull down the altar, and used impersonal “it is good” for the printed neuter formula at 4:24. Left the distinct altar stones and difficult fortification sentence intact.

Pending: The elliptical praise formula at 4:24, heaven-directed blessing at 4:55, and fortification/Beth-zur syntax at 4:61 require specialist review; no apparatus consultation.

**Chapter 5.** Modernized ordinary address and cross/become/encamp forms. Preserved devoted-to-destruction and daughter-town images, reported-to-direct speech shift, distinct stream/river terms, all counts, and “The man Judas”. The city is addressed in the singular for “your land” at 5:48, followed by a plural assurance; modern English your/you is number-neutral. The historical query at 5:32 was resolved for this running text: it prints our brothers, which is retained.

Pending: Place-name and location syntax at 5:26, military-geographic vocabulary, and lineage/deliverance statement at 5:62; complete source collation.

**Chapter 6.** Modernized royal address, my eyes, and the wording of rule and dwindling. Retained the blood-of-grapes image, hand-of-peace gesture, present willingness at 6:23, territorial pronoun at 6:25, and four fighting men plus the Indian driver at 6:37 as printed in the accessed running text.

Pending: Existing comparison note reports competing thirty/thirty-two/four readings at 6:37; that apparatus evidence has NOT been independently consulted. Specialist review of elephant equipment, siege vocabulary, royal titles and chronological details remains pending.

**Chapter 7.** Modernized ordinary royal address and trust. Retained Thy in the quoted prayer at 7:17 and divine forms in 7:37–42, while modernizing third-person “he has”. Made the quotation at 7:17 more natural without suppressing flesh and blood. Preserved the pit, defilement and battle imagery and all figures. The 500 casualties at 7:32 match the accessed running text; the competing 5,000 figure remains a historical comparison note pending apparatus review.

Pending: Psalm quotation alignment, defiled them at 7:34, request to grant no continuance at 7:38, and full apparatus collation require specialist review.

**Chapter 8.** Modernized treaty and diplomatic address; clarified the paired reign/remove statement. Preserved the doubled mighty-in-strength statement, India/Media/Lydia, 320 councillors, one annual ruler, the yoke and soul imagery, and the difficult allies clause at 8:28 without adding an unstated enemy.

Pending: Treaty obligation referents at 8:26–28, the help/reign syntax at 8:13, and historical-geographic and governmental claims; no harmonization to reconstructed history or unseen apparatus.

**Chapter 9.** Modernized ordinary address, third-person verbs and conferred. Preserved the lament for the mighty one, go-out/come-in and hand imagery, the wedding-to-mourning reversal, loss of prophecy, sword ceasing, place names and counts. The plural command to cry to Heaven in 9:46 addresses people and uses you.

Pending: Prophetic works/wall sequence at 9:54, subject and small-number phrasing at 9:65–66, geographical identifications and full collation.

**Chapter 10.** Modernized royal and ordinary address and trust, and recast the greeting at 10:55 for spoken English. Preserved four-foot stones, all financial amounts and proportions, repeated time expressions, the letter’s parenthesis, pestilent image, and court titles. The golden clasp and Kinsmen title at 10:89 were checked in a separate retrieved excerpt.

Pending: Scope of tax remission, fiscal terminology, subject transitions, and distinctions among court ranks require specialist review; full collation pending.

**Chapter 11.** Modernized ordinary diplomatic address while preserving the letter structure, repeated ownership phrases, three/four district distinction, right-hand imagery, royal titles and all battle figures. Prayer is narrated at 11:71; no words of prayer were invented.

Pending: Territorial and fiscal syntax at 11:28,34–35, diplomatic father/Kinsman titles, narrative subject shifts and full apparatus collation.

**Chapter 12.** Modernized ordinary address and shows/threatens. Preserved holy books in our hands, repeated help/helping and mourning, the diplomatic claims of brotherhood, numerical division of forces, Chaphenatha, and the report of Jonathan’s death without harmonizing the later death account.

Pending: Historical and textual evaluation of Spartan correspondence, the unexpressed departure at 12:29, fortification terminology at 12:37, and report versus narrative chronology at 12:50; complete collation.

**Chapter 13.** Modernized ordinary address and tell/stands, including the plea to Simon at 13:46; it is human address, not prayer. Preserved spirit rekindled, the first-person shift at 13:18, seven pyramids despite six named family members, the right-hand gesture, yoke image, legal terms and festival date.

Pending: Speech perspective at 13:17–18, memorial architecture and count, royal financial terminology, and source chronology need specialist review; no harmonization or apparatus claim.

**Chapter 14.** Modernized acts and annuls in the decree. Preserved the vine-and-fig-tree image, repeated glory and holy places, 1,000 minas, Asaramel, gathered-to-his-people idiom, hands imagery, and “for ever, until a faithful prophet should arise”. Kept inscription hierarchy and court offices.

Pending: Asaramel interpretation, the hands/prosperity and purity phrasing at 14:36, temporal scope of the perpetual appointment, and documentary history/full collation.

**Chapter 15.** Modernized ordinary address, hold/demand and unto; corrected the possessive to yours where it stands alone. Preserved remissions, independent coinage, all place names and sums, pestilent imagery, the repeated inheritance claim and treaty reversal.

Pending: Royal-debt scope, the second-day expression and assault imagery at 15:25, geographical names, decree syntax and full collation.

**Chapter 16.** Modernized ordinary address and third-person verbs. Preserved hands/prosperity, blessing of help from Heaven, heart lifted up, evil-for-good reversal, repeated destruction language, ambiguous subject/referent shifts, and the closing reference to the high-priestly record. Restored “behold” in the closing record formula at 16:24 after the historical study flagged its omission and the accessed running text confirmed it; the marker points the reader to the cited record.

Pending: Subject at 16:4, the singular object burned at 16:10, military totals, reign chronology and closing-source formula require specialist review; full collation and human read-aloud remain pending.
