## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved every/all, joy, fellowship, chains, fruit, flesh/body, life/death, common-life and united-striving language. Smoothed remembrance, petitions, progress and the choice/struggle clauses; modernized has. Retained love at 1:16 and ambition at 1:17 in the NA28 order, the shorter word at 1:14, and the bracketed for at 1:23 and in at 1:24.

Pending: Review the subject/object ambiguity of 1:7, praetorium and the in-Christ chains construction at 1:13, salvation/deliverance at 1:19, know/make known at 1:22, and the civic and spirit senses at 1:27. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved repeated if, same/one thinking, form/appearance distinctions, equality with God, emptying Himself without an added object, slave, death/cross, every knee/tongue, and working in 2:12–13. Updated ordinary you/my and smoothed their sentence. Like mind at 2:20 expresses the inherited like soul idiom; soul language remains explicit in its note. Retained the bracketed also at 2:4 and all poured-out, sacrifice, running, soldier and sorrow images.

Pending: Review something to exploit at 2:6 alongside grasp/seize/retain interpretations, the elliptical syntax of 2:5, holding fast/holding forth at 2:16, and the epistolary past of sent at 2:28. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved the three watch out commands, mutilation/circumcision contrast, lineage and eighth day, gain/loss/refuse, flesh, resurrection, repeated taking hold, race/goal/prize, belly and citizenship/body images. Smoothed several sentence structures. Jesus at 3:12 is added from bracketed NA28 wording; the shorter earlier form is archived. But at 3:7 and the bracketed articles at 3:10 are reflected in the retained English. The shorter 3:16 remains.

Pending: Review faith in Christ at 3:9 beside Christ’s faithfulness, the strength of refuse at 3:8, the relation of mature/perfected at 3:12–15, and citizenship/commonwealth at 3:20. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved the repeated appeals to Euodia and Syntyche, yoke-fellow, repeated rejoice, every whatever clause, joy/crown, future will guard, contentment in plenty and need, once and twice, fruit/account, fragrance/sacrifice and fill language. Recovered the blossoming image at 4:10. Modernized surpasses/will and smoothed practice and circumstances. Retained the shorter 4:13 without adding Christ, and no final Amen at 4:23.

Pending: Review yoke-fellow as a description or possible name, the senses of the Lord is near and content, the accounting language of 4:15–18 and the scope of all things at 4:13 in its immediate context. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
