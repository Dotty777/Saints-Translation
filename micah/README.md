# Micah — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 7 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 105 verse units received an English editorial review: 59 changed and 46 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. ## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize ordinary address and has; smooth water flowing downhill. Preserve Michaiah/Morashthi and the royal names, testimony and holy house, heights and wax, repeated questions, fruit watcher’s hut, chasm/foundations, repeated prostitution wages, female lamenter, dragons and daughters of sirens, Gath/Akim/Sennaan/Lachish/Odollam, laughter phrases, the clause across 1:12–13, heirs/inheritance and eagle/widowhood.

Pending: Review the woman/inhabitant and person shifts in 1:11–15, laughter-house syntax, sent-ones and vain-houses clause, and widowhood at 1:16. These difficult source readings are not replaced with Hebrew place-name wordplays or different animals. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modernize ordinary address and says/has. Preserve daybreak planning and hands not raised to God, explicit orphans, repeated man/house/inheritance, necks and sudden upright walking, lament and measuring cord, weeping/tears, his pronouns in 2:7–8, flayed skin/hope, everlasting mountains, corruption with corruption, pursuit without a pursuer, spirit/falsehood/dripping, sure gathering/receiving, afflicted sheep and the past breach/king followed by future Lord’s leading.

Pending: Review pronoun antecedents and speaker changes, the flayed-skin/shattering clause, leaders’ expulsion tense, and dripping/turning-back/flock phrases. At 2:10 the initial address is singular and the corrupted group is plural; 2:11 moves from plural pursuit to singular to you. English you covers both, with the distinctions recorded here. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize ordinary address and says while retaining all other reviewed wording. Preserve future He will say, authorities/remaining ones, the graphic skin/flesh/bones cooking image, repeated pronouns, peace/war and mouths, dream-seers and mockery, unless I fill up strength with the Spirit of the Lord and of judgment and might, past paid judgments/divination and the field/fruit watcher’s hut/forest grove.

Pending: Review the referents of He/them, peace-recipient clause and especially the active I fill and unless construction at 3:8. Do not replace the difficult Greek conditional with the familiar Hebrew-based I am filled affirmation. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize ordinary address and has/says, and use hooves. Preserve manifest mountain and hurrying peoples, plural They will show, law from Zion, swords to plows and spears to sickles, repeated each and no fear, Lord Almighty, each people’s own way, forever and beyond, crushed/expelled/rejected, squalid tower and kingdom from Babylon, counsel rather than counselor, birth/courage, iron horns/bronze hooves, melting/grinding, and the daughter of Ephraim and Israel’s tribes at 4:14.

Pending: Review the Babylon kingdom phrase, squalid tower, speaker and gender changes, the named Ephraim daughter and unnamed agent at 4:14. Preserve 4:14 here rather than renumbering it as common English 5:1; do not merge the peace oracle with Isaiah’s separate wording. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs; render she gives birth naturally. Preserve Bethlehem/house of Ephrathah, thousands, plural goings out and both time phrases, stand/see/shepherd, plural they/their God with singular he magnified, seven shepherds/eight bites, Assour/Nebrod/ditch, repeated remnant setting, dew/lambs/couch grass, lion/flock, horses/chariots/cities, sorceries/oracles, pillars/groves and paired anger/wrath.

Pending: Review eight bites of people, the ditch, the lambs/couch-grass clause and person/number shifts. Goings out from the beginning and days of old are retained without declaring a later interpretation the only lexical meaning. This chapter’s 5:1–14 corresponds to common English 5:2–15. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Modernize human address and third-person verbs, including the question at 6:8; retain O man, justice, mercy and readiness to walk with the Lord your God. Preserve hills/ravines, repeated divine questions, Moses/Aaron/Miriam, rushes/Galgal, take hold/grasp, rams/fat-rich torrents/firstborn, saving those who fear, fire, balance and deceptive weights, darkening/turning aside, sowing/pressing/drinking and Zambri/Achaab. Make the shared future of pressing wine explicit.

Pending: Review the taking-hold imagery and fat-rich torrents, who adorns the city and fire clauses, the object of turning aside, and the changes between singular and plural in 6:15–16. At 6:8 the address is singular and human; readiness is the selected Greek reading, while humbly remains a comparison reading. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Modernize ordinary address, attributive my and third-person verbs; smooth the weeping clause, explicitly carry will rise into daughter-in-law, and render confounded as thrown into turmoil. Retain Thy/Thine/Thee/Thou in prayer at 7:14,17–18,20; preserve stubble/gleaning, repeated alas and crushing, peaceful words, the 7:3–4 continuation, moth/measuring rod, female enemy, light/righteousness, brick plastering and destructive day, Assyrians/Tyre/Syria, shepherd/rod, wonderful things, dust/snakes/confinement, testimony, mercy and all our sins sunk/cast into the sea.

Pending: Review the moth/measuring-rod clause, the 7:10–12 continuations and pronoun referents, singular your departure with plural you will see at 7:15, and the closing irregular syntax at 7:19. The female enemy is explicit in 7:10; no restoration promise is substituted for the difficult destructive-day wording. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
