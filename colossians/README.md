The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

This is an English editorial revision, not complete critical-text or apparatus verification. ## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved fruit/growth, power, light/darkness, inheritance, image/firstborn, body/head, fullness, blood/cross, flesh and mystery images. Modernized third-person forms and smoothed several clauses. At 1:7 changed our behalf to your behalf, and at 1:22 changed the inherited passive to He has reconciled you, following the consulted NA28 running text. Kept the bracketed repetition through Him at 1:20 and every person repeated three times at 1:28.

Pending: Review the attachment of with joy in 1:11–12, the supplied God subject and fullness construction at 1:19, the implications of reconciliation at 1:20, the referents in 1:21–22, what is lacking in 1:24, and Christ in/among you at 1:27. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved knit-together, treasure, walking/rooting/building, spoil, elements, bodily deity, circumcision, burial/raising, handwritten record, nailing/cross, triumph, shadow/body, head/joints/ligaments and growth. Smoothed ordinary idioms and archaic forms. The repeated you in 2:13 is explicit, while forgiven us remains; its in reflects bracketed NA28 wording. Eating and drinking at 2:16 follows NA28 and replaces inherited or. Retained what he has seen in 2:18 without adding not. At 2:2, supplied come to makes the goal construction readable in English; the supply is separately noted.

Pending: Review the syntax and apposition of 2:2, elements in 2:8 and 2:20, circumcision of Christ, the agent and stripping/triumph imagery in 2:15, the referent of in it, worship of angels and dwelling on visions in 2:18, and the contested final construction of 2:23. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved above/earth, death/life, hidden/revealed, members, old/new clothing, image, the full list of social identities, bond, peace ruling, one body, songs and household instructions. Modernized ordinary address and third-person forms, clarified forgiving and service under the eyes, and used reward for recompense. Placed in all wisdom with teaching/admonishing at 3:16. Your life at 3:4, the Lord at 3:13, peace of Christ at 3:15 and God at 3:16 remain.

Pending: Review sons of disobedience in the bracketed clause at 3:6, the scope of all and in all at 3:11, grace/gratitude and punctuation in 3:16, and indicative/imperative interpretations of serve the Lord Christ in 3:24. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved justice/equity, watchfulness, the door, walking/buying opportunity, salt, names and relationships, hand and chains. Smoothed ordinary you, disclosure and the circumcision clause. Jesus at 4:12 is included from bracketed NA28 wording. Nympha and her house at 4:15 remain; the letter from Laodicea is left unidentified. No final Amen has been added.

Pending: Review equity/equality in 4:1, the epistolary past sent at 4:8, the scope of the circumcision clause at 4:11, and the pain/labor sense at 4:13. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
