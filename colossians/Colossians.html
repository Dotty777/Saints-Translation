Colossians 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Timothy our brother,

1:2to the saints and faithful brothers in Christ who are in Colossae: grace to you and peace from God our Father.

Thanksgiving for the Colossians

1:3We thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, always praying for you,

1:4having heard of your faith in Christ Jesus and the love you have toward all the saints,

1:5because of the hope laid up for you in the heavens, of which you heard beforehand in the word of truth, the gospel

1:6that has come to you, just as also in all the world it is bearing fruit and growing, as it does also among you from the day you heard and came to know the grace of God in truth,

1:7just as you learned from Epaphras, our beloved fellow slave, who is a faithful servant of Christ on your behalf,

1:8who also made known to us your love in the Spirit.

Prayer for Knowledge of His Will

1:9For this reason we too, since the day we heard, have not ceased praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of His will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,

1:10to walk worthily of the Lord, pleasing Him in every way, bearing fruit in every good work and growing in the knowledge of God,

1:11being empowered with every power according to the might of His glory for all endurance and patience with joy,

1:12giving thanks to the Father who made you fit for a share of the inheritance of the saints in the light,

1:13who rescued us from the authority of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of His love,

1:14in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins.

The Image of the Invisible God

1:15He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation,

1:16because in Him all things were created, in the heavens and upon the earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things have been created through Him and for Him.

1:17And He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.

1:18And He is the head of the body, the Church; He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, so that He Himself might have first place in all things,

1:19because God was pleased that all the fullness should dwell in Him,

1:20and through Him to reconcile all things to Himself, having made peace through the blood of His cross, [through Him], whether the things upon the earth or the things in the heavens.

Reconciled Through His Death

1:21And you, who were once alienated and enemies in mind in your evil works—

1:22yet now He has reconciled you in the body of His flesh through death, to present you holy and without blemish and beyond accusation before Him,

1:23if indeed you continue in the faith, founded and steadfast and not shifting from the hope of the gospel which you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven, of which I, Paul, became a servant.

Paul’s Ministry to the Church

1:24Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking in the afflictions of Christ for His body, which is the Church,

1:25of which I became a servant according to the stewardship from God given to me for you, to fulfill the word of God,

1:26the mystery hidden from the ages and from the generations, but now revealed to His saints,

1:27to whom God willed to make known what are the riches of the glory of this mystery among the nations, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.

1:28Him we announce, admonishing every person and teaching every person in all wisdom, so that we may present every person mature in Christ.

1:29For this I also labor, struggling according to His working, which works in me with power.

Colossians 1:15 · firstborn

Meaning. Firstborn as a relational title within the account of creation and supremacy.

Decision. Preserve firstborn and image of the invisible God. The following creation-through-Him clauses belong to interpretation; neither first-created nor creator is a word-for-word substitute here.

Comparison. Saints all creation; KJV and DRC every creature. All retain firstborn.

Excluded. first-created substituted for firstborn

Colossians 2

Read this chapter on staging

Paul’s Struggle for Them

2:1For I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you and those in Laodicea and all who have not seen my face in the flesh,

2:2so that their hearts may be encouraged as they are knit together in love, and that they may come to all the riches of full assurance in understanding, to the knowledge of the mystery of God—Christ,

2:3in whom all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.

2:4I say this so that no one may deceive you with persuasive argument.

2:5For even if I am absent in the flesh, yet in spirit I am with you, rejoicing and seeing your order and the firmness of your faith in Christ.

Rooted and Built Up in Him

2:6Therefore, as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in Him,

2:7rooted and being built up in Him and being established in the faith just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.

2:8Watch out that no one carries you off as spoil through philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the elements of the world, and not according to Christ,

2:9for in Him all the fullness of deity dwells bodily,

2:10and you have been filled in Him, who is the head of every ruler and authority,

2:11in whom you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands, in the stripping off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,

2:12having been buried together with Him in baptism, in which you were also raised together through faith in the working of God, who raised Him from the dead.

2:13And you, who were dead in transgressions and the uncircumcision of your flesh, He made you alive together with Him, having forgiven us all the transgressions,

2:14having wiped out the handwritten record with its decrees that was against us, which was opposed to us, and He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross.

2:15Having stripped the rulers and authorities, He displayed them openly, leading them in triumph in it.

Let No One Judge You

2:16Therefore let no one judge you in eating and drinking, or in the matter of a festival or new moon or Sabbaths,

2:17which are a shadow of the things to come, but the body belongs to Christ.

2:18Let no one disqualify you, delighting in humility and worship of angels, dwelling on what he has seen, puffed up without cause by the mind of his flesh,

2:19and not holding fast to the head, from whom the whole body, supplied and knit together through joints and ligaments, grows with the growth of God.

Died with Christ

2:20If you died with Christ to the elements of the world, why, as though living in the world, do you submit to decrees:

2:21“Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch,”

2:22which all concern things that perish with use, according to human commandments and teachings?

2:23These indeed have a reputation of wisdom in self-chosen worship and humility and severe treatment of the body, but are of no value against the indulgence of the flesh.

Colossians 3

Read this chapter on staging

Seek the Things Above

3:1If, then, you were raised together with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God.

3:2Set your minds on the things above, not on the things upon the earth,

3:3for you died, and your life has been hidden with Christ in God.

3:4When Christ, your life, is revealed, then you also will be revealed with Him in glory.

Put to Death What Is Earthly

3:5Therefore put to death the members that are upon the earth: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed, which is idolatry.

3:6Because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience,

3:7in which you also once walked when you lived in these things.

3:8But now you too put away all these: anger, rage, malice, slander, foul speech from your mouth.

3:9Do not lie to one another, having stripped off the old human being with his practices

3:10and put on the new, who is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of the One who created him,

3:11where there is no Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, slave, free, but Christ is all and in all.

Put On Love

3:12Therefore put on, as God’s chosen ones, holy and beloved, tender compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience,

3:13bearing with one another and forgiving one another if anyone has a complaint against someone. Just as the Lord forgave you, so you also must forgive.

3:14And above all these things put on love, which is the bond of completeness.

3:15And let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts, to which you were also called in one body; and be thankful.

3:16Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, teaching and admonishing one another in all wisdom with psalms, hymns, spiritual songs, singing with gratitude in your hearts to God.

3:17And whatever you do in word or in deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks through Him to God the Father.

Wives, Husbands, Children, Fathers

3:18Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

3:19Husbands, love your wives and do not be bitter toward them.

3:20Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well pleasing in the Lord.

3:21Fathers, do not provoke your children, so that they may not lose heart.

Slaves and Masters

3:22Slaves, obey in all things your masters according to the flesh, not serving only when their eyes are on you, as people-pleasers, but in sincerity of heart, fearing the Lord.

3:23Whatever you do, work from the soul, as for the Lord and not for human beings,

3:24knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance. You serve the Lord Christ.

3:25For the one who does wrong will receive back the wrong he did, and there is no partiality.

Colossians 4

Read this chapter on staging

Masters, Provide Your Slaves with Justice

4:1Masters, provide your slaves with what is just and equitable, knowing that you too have a Master in heaven.

Persevere in Prayer

4:2Persevere in prayer, keeping watch in it with thanksgiving,

4:3praying at the same time for us also, so that God may open for us a door for the word, to speak the mystery of Christ, for which I am also bound,

4:4so that I may make it clear as I ought to speak.

4:5Walk in wisdom toward those outside, buying up the opportunity.

4:6Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer each one.

Tychicus and Onesimus

4:7Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant and fellow slave in the Lord, will make known to you all my circumstances.

4:8I sent him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know about us and he may encourage your hearts,

4:9with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will make known to you all the things here.

4:10Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, and Mark, the cousin of Barnabas, concerning whom you received instructions—if he comes to you, welcome him—

4:11and Jesus who is called Justus. From among those of the circumcision, these alone are fellow workers for the kingdom of God; they have become a comfort to me.

4:12Epaphras, who is one of you, a slave of Christ Jesus, greets you, always struggling for you in his prayers, so that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God.

4:13For I testify about him that he takes great pains for you and those in Laodicea and those in Hierapolis.

4:14Luke, the beloved physician, greets you, and Demas.

Final Greetings

4:15Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and Nympha and the Church in her house.

4:16And when the letter has been read among you, arrange for it also to be read in the Church of the Laodiceans, and for you also to read the one from Laodicea.

4:17And say to Archippus, “Attend to the service which you received in the Lord, so that you may fulfill it.”

4:18The greeting is in my own hand, Paul’s. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
