ISAIAH 1

1:1 The vision that Isaiah son of Amos saw, which he saw against Judea and against Jerusalem during the reign of Oziah, Joatham, Achaz and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judea.
1:2 Listen, heaven, and give ear, earth, because the Lord has spoken: “I begot sons and raised them high, but they rejected Me.
1:3 An ox knew the one who acquired it, and a donkey its lord’s manger; but Israel did not know Me, and the people did not understand Me.”
1:4 Woe, sinful nation, people full of sins, evil offspring, lawless sons! You abandoned the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger.
1:5 Why should you be struck again as you add lawlessness? Every head is in pain, and every heart in grief.
1:6 From feet to head: for neither a wound nor a bruise nor an inflamed blow is there a poultice to apply, nor oil nor bandages.
1:7 Your land is deserted, your cities burned by fire. Your country—foreigners consume it before you; it has been deserted, overturned by foreign peoples.
1:8 Daughter Zion will be left like a tent in a vineyard, like a fruit-watcher’s shelter in a cucumber field, like a besieged city.
1:9 If the Lord Sabaoth had not left us seed, we would have become like Sodom and been made like Gomorrah.
1:10 Hear the Lord’s word, rulers of Sodom! Attend to God’s law, people of Gomorrah!
1:11 “What is the multitude of your sacrifices to Me?” says the Lord. “I am full of burnt offerings of rams; fat of lambs and blood of bulls and male goats I do not desire,
1:12 not even if you come to appear before Me. For who demanded these things from your hands? To trample My court,
1:13 you will no longer continue. If you bring fine flour, it is futile. Incense is an abomination to Me. Your new moons and sabbaths and a great day I cannot endure; fasting and idleness,
1:14 and your new moons and your festivals My soul hates. You have become more than enough for Me; I will no longer let your sins go.
1:15 Whenever you stretch your hands toward Me, I will turn My eyes away from you. Even if you multiply supplication, I will not listen to you, for your hands are full of blood.
1:16 Wash yourselves, become clean. Remove the evils from your souls, before My eyes. Stop your evil deeds.
1:17 Learn to do good, seek judgment, rescue the wronged. Give judgment for the orphan and vindicate the widow.
1:18 Come, let us reason together, says the Lord. Though your sins are like purple-red, I will whiten them like snow; though they are like scarlet, I will whiten them like wool.
1:19 If you are willing and listen to Me, you will eat the land’s good things.
1:20 But if you are unwilling and do not listen to Me, a sword will devour you, for the Lord’s mouth has spoken these things.”
1:21 How the faithful city Zion has become a prostitute, full of judgment, in which righteousness lay at rest, but now murderers!
1:22 Your silver is unapproved; your retailers mix the wine with water.
1:23 Your rulers disobey, partners of thieves, loving gifts and pursuing repayment, not judging for orphans and not attending to widows’ judgment.
1:24 Therefore thus says the Master, the Lord Sabaoth: “Woe, mighty ones of Israel! For My anger will not cease among My opponents, and I will exact judgment from My enemies.
1:25 I will bring My hand against you and burn you to purity. I will destroy the disobedient, remove all lawless people from you and humble all the arrogant.
1:26 I will establish your judges as before and your counselors as at the beginning. After these things you will be called City of Righteousness, Faithful Metropolis Zion.
1:27 For her captivity will be saved through judgment and through mercy.
1:28 The lawless and sinners will be shattered together, and those abandoning the Lord will be brought to an end.
1:29 For they will be ashamed of their idols, which they themselves wanted, and they were disgraced by their gardens, which they desired.
1:30 For they will be like a terebinth that has shed its leaves, like a garden that has no water.
1:31 Their strength will become a stalk of tow and their works sparks of fire. The lawless and sinners will be burned together, with no one to quench.”

ISAIAH 2

2:1 The word that came from the Lord to Isaiah son of Amos concerning Judea and Jerusalem.
2:2 In the last days the Lord’s mountain and God’s house will be clearly visible on the summits of the mountains and be raised above the hills; all the nations will come to it,
2:3 and many nations will go and say, “Come, let us go up to the Lord’s mountain and to the house of Jacob’s God. He will announce His way to us, and we will walk in it.” For law will go out from Zion, and the Lord’s word from Jerusalem.
2:4 He will judge between the nations and reprove a numerous people. They will beat their swords into plows and their spears into sickles. Nation will no longer take sword against nation, and they will certainly no longer learn to wage war.
2:5 And now, house of Jacob, come, let us walk by the Lord’s light!
2:6 For He abandoned His people, Israel’s house, because their country was filled, as from the beginning, with omens like the foreigners’ country, and many foreign children were born to them.
2:7 For their country was filled with silver and gold, and their treasures had no number; the land was filled with horses, and their chariots had no number.
2:8 The land was filled with abominations, the works of their hands, and they bowed before what their fingers made.
2:9 A human being bowed down, and a man was humbled; I will certainly not let them go.
2:10 And now enter the rocks and hide in the earth from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.
2:11 For the Lord’s eyes are high, but the human being is low; humanity’s height will be humbled, and the Lord alone will be exalted on that day.
2:12 For the Lord Sabaoth has a day against every insolent and arrogant person, against everyone high and elevated; they will be humbled,
2:13 against every cedar of Lebanon among the high and elevated, and against every oak tree of Bashan,
2:14 against every mountain and every high hill,
2:15 against every high tower and every high wall,
2:16 against every ship of the sea and every beautiful display of ships.
2:17 Every human being will be humbled, humanity’s height will fall, and the Lord alone will be exalted on that day.
2:18 They will hide away all the handmade things,
2:19 carrying them into caves, into clefts in the rocks and into holes in the earth, from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.
2:20 For on that day a person will cast out his silver and gold abominations, which they made to worship, to the worthless ones and the bats,
2:21 to enter holes in the solid rock and clefts in the rocks from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.

ISAIAH 3

3:1 Look now, the Master, the Lord Sabaoth, will remove from Judea and Jerusalem strong man and strong woman, the strength of bread and the strength of water,
3:2 giant and strong man and warrior and judge and prophet and conjecturer and elder,
3:3 commander of fifty and admirable counselor and wise master builder and discerning listener.
3:4 I will establish young men as their rulers, and mockers will lord it over them.
3:5 The people will clash, person against person and each against his neighbor. The child will collide with the elder, the dishonored with the honored.
3:6 For a man will seize his brother or a member of his father’s household, saying, “You have a cloak; become our leader, and let my food be under you!”
3:7 Answering on that day, he will say, “I will not be your leader, for there is neither bread nor cloak in my house. I will not be leader of this people.”
3:8 For Jerusalem has been abandoned and Judea has collapsed; their tongues accompany lawlessness, disobeying what concerns the Lord, because now their glory has been humbled,
3:9 and the shame of their face stood against them. They announced their sin like Sodom’s and made it manifest. Woe to their soul, for they have planned an evil plan against themselves,
3:10 saying, “Let us bind the righteous person, because he is troublesome to us.” Therefore they will eat the produce of their works.
3:11 Woe to the lawless! Evils according to the works of his hands will happen to him.
3:12 My people, your collectors glean you, and those demanding payment lord it over you. My people, those pronouncing you blessed lead you astray and disturb the path of your feet.
3:13 But now the Lord will establish Himself for judgment and will set His people before judgment.
3:14 The Lord Himself will come to judgment with the people’s elders and its rulers: “Why have you burned My vineyard, while the poor person’s plunder is in your houses?
3:15 Why do you wrong My people and shame the face of the poor?”
3:16 Thus says the Lord: “Because Zion’s daughters were exalted and walked with high neck and glancing eyes, dragging their tunics as their feet moved and playing with their feet,
3:17 God will humble Zion’s ruling daughters, and the Lord will uncover their appearance
3:18 on that day, and the Lord will remove the glory of their clothing and their ornaments, hair braids, fringes and crescents,
3:19 the necklace and the adornment of their face,
3:20 the arrangement of glorious adornment, armlets, bracelets, a hair braid, armbands, rings and earrings,
3:21 the garments bordered with purple and those purple at the center,
3:22 the coverings used about the house and the transparent Laconian garments,
3:23 the fine-linen, blue-purple and scarlet garments, and fine linen interwoven with gold and blue-purple, and trailing summer wraps.
3:24 Instead of sweet fragrance there will be dust; instead of a belt you will gird yourself with a cord; instead of golden head adornment you will have baldness because of your deeds; instead of the tunic with purple at its center you will gird yourself with sackcloth.
3:25 Your most beautiful son, whom you love, will fall by the sword, and your strong men will fall by the sword.
3:26 The cases of your adornment will be humbled and mourn; you will be left alone and dashed to the ground.”

ISAIAH 4

4:1 Seven women will take hold of one man, saying, “We will eat our own bread and put on our own clothes; only let your name be called over us. Take away our disgrace!”
4:2 On that day God will shine out in counsel with glory upon the earth, to exalt and glorify what remains of Israel,
4:3 and those remaining in Zion and left in Jerusalem will be called holy, all those recorded for life in Jerusalem,
4:4 because the Lord will wash away the filth of Zion’s sons and daughters and thoroughly cleanse the blood from their midst, by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.
4:5 He will come, and it will be: a cloud will shade every place of Mount Zion and everything around her by day, and as of smoke and as of a light of fire burning by night; with all the glory it will be covered.
4:6 It will be shade from heat, shelter and concealment from harshness and rain.

ISAIAH 5

5:1 I will sing now to the beloved a song of the loved one for my vineyard. The beloved had a vineyard on a horn, in a fertile place.
5:2 I put a fence around it, surrounded it with a palisade, planted a Sorech vine, built a tower in its midst and dug a wine vat in it. I waited for it to produce grapes, but it produced thorns.
5:3 Now, man of Judah and residents of Jerusalem, judge My case and the matter of My vineyard.
5:4 What more shall I do for My vineyard that I did not do for it? For I waited for it to produce grapes, but it produced thorns.
5:5 Now I will announce to you what I will do to My vineyard: I will remove its fence, and it will be plundered; I will pull down its wall, and it will be trampled,
5:6 and I will abandon My vineyard. It will certainly not be pruned or dug, and thorns will rise in it as in fallow ground. I will command the clouds not to rain upon it.
5:7 For the vineyard of the Lord Sabaoth is Israel’s house, and the man of Judah a beloved new planting. I waited for it to produce judgment, but it produced lawlessness, not righteousness but an outcry.
5:8 Woe to those joining house to house and bringing field beside field, so that they may take something from their neighbor! Surely you will not dwell alone upon the earth?
5:9 For these things were heard in the ears of the Lord Sabaoth: “If many houses come to be, great and beautiful, they will become deserted, and no one will inhabit them.
5:10 For where ten pairs of oxen work, it will yield one jar, and the one sowing six artabas will yield three measures.”
5:11 Woe to those rising in the morning and pursuing strong drink, lingering into the evening, for wine will burn them up!
5:12 For with lyre and harp, drums and flutes they drink wine, but they do not look at the Lord’s works or consider the works of His hands.
5:13 Therefore My people became captive because they did not know the Lord, and there was a multitude of dead through famine and thirst for water.
5:14 Hades enlarged its appetite and opened its mouth without ceasing, and her glorious ones, great ones, rich ones and pestilent ones will go down.
5:15 A human being will be humbled, a man dishonored, and lofty eyes humbled.
5:16 The Lord Sabaoth will be exalted through judgment, and the holy God will be glorified through righteousness.
5:17 Those who were plundered will be pastured like bulls, and lambs will feed upon the deserted places of those wiped out.
5:18 Woe to those drawing sins as with a long cord and lawless deeds as with the strap of a heifer’s yoke,
5:19 who say, “Let what He will do approach quickly, so that we may see, and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come, so that we may know!”
5:20 Woe to those calling evil good and good evil, making darkness light and light darkness, making bitter sweet and sweet bitter!
5:21 Woe to those who are discerning in their own judgment and knowledgeable in their own view!
5:22 Woe to your strong ones who drink wine and the powerful who mix strong drink,
5:23 who justify the ungodly for gifts and take away the righteous person’s right!
5:24 Therefore, as a stalk is burned by a fiery coal and consumed by a rising flame, their root will become like chaff and their blossom rise like dust. For they did not desire the law of the Lord Sabaoth, but provoked the utterance of the Holy One of Israel.
5:25 The Lord Sabaoth was enraged with wrath against His people, brought His hand against them and struck them. The mountains were roused, and their carcasses became like dung in the middle of the road. In all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.
5:26 Therefore He will raise a signal among the distant nations and whistle to them from the earth’s end. Behold, they come quickly and lightly!
5:27 They will not hunger or grow weary, slumber or sleep, nor loosen the belts from their waists; their sandal straps will certainly not break.
5:28 Their arrows are sharp and their bows drawn. Their horses’ feet were reckoned like solid rock, and their chariot wheels like a storm.
5:29 They rush like lions and have taken their stand like a lion’s cub. It will seize and cry out as a beast does and cast out, and no one will rescue them.
5:30 It will cry out because of them on that day like the sound of a surging sea. They will look to the earth, and behold, harsh darkness in their perplexity.

ISAIAH 6

6:1 In the year that King Oziah died, I saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and lifted up; and the house was full of His glory.
6:2 And seraphim stood around Him: each one had six wings, and each one had six wings; with two they covered their faces, and with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew.
6:3 And one cried to another, saying:
Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Sabaoth;
all the earth is full of His glory.
6:4 And the lintel was lifted at the sound of their cry, and the house was filled with smoke.
6:5 And I said, Woe is me, for I am pierced to the heart! For I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell among a people of unclean lips; and my eyes have seen the King, the Lord Sabaoth.
6:6 And one of the seraphim was sent to me, with a coal in his hand that he had taken from the altar with the tongs;
6:7 and he touched my mouth and said, Behold, this has touched your lips, and will take away your iniquities and cleanse your sins.
6:8 And I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go to this people? And I said, Behold, here I am; send me.
6:9 And He said, Go, and say to this people:
Hearing, you will hear and certainly not understand;
and seeing, you will see and certainly not perceive.
6:10 For the heart of this people has grown dull,
and with their ears they have heard heavily,
and they have closed their eyes;
lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears,
and understand with their heart, and turn,
and I will heal them.
6:11 And I said, How long, O Lord? And He said:
Until cities lie waste, without inhabitants,
and houses, without people,
and the land is left desolate.
6:12 And after these things God will remove people far away;
and those who remain will multiply upon the land.
6:13 And yet a tenth remains upon it;
and again it will be for plunder,
as a terebinth, and as an acorn
when it falls from its husk.

ISAIAH 7

7:1 In the days of Achaz, son of Joatham, son of Oziah, king of Judea, Raasson king of Aram and Phakee son of Romelias, king of Israel, went up against Jerusalem to wage war on her, but they could not besiege her.
7:2 It was reported to David’s house, “Aram has come to agreement with Ephraim.” His soul and his people’s soul were distraught, as when a tree in a wood is shaken by wind.
7:3 The Lord said to Isaiah, “Go out to meet Achaz, you and the remaining Jasub, your son, toward the pool of the upper road of the fuller’s field,
7:4 and you will say to him, ‘Take care to be quiet, and do not fear; do not let your soul grow weak before these two smoking firebrand logs. For whenever the wrath of My anger comes, I will heal again.
7:5 And the son of Aram and the son of Romelias, because they counseled an evil counsel concerning you, saying,
7:6 “We will go up into Judea and, after speaking with them, turn them toward ourselves and make Tabeel’s son king over her,”
7:7 thus says the Lord Sabaoth: “This counsel will certainly not endure, nor will it happen.
7:8 But the head of Aram is Damascus; yet within another sixty-five years Ephraim’s kingdom will cease from being a people,
7:9 and the head of Ephraim is Somoron, and the head of Somoron Romelias’s son. If you do not believe, you will certainly not understand.”’”
7:10 And the Lord spoke again to Achaz, saying,
7:11 Ask for yourself a sign from the Lord your God, in the depth or in the height.
7:12 And Achaz said, I will certainly not ask, nor will I put the Lord to the test at all.
7:13 And he said, Hear now, O house of David: is it a small thing for you to trouble people? And how is it that you trouble the Lord?
7:14 Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign:
behold, the virgin will conceive and bear a son,
and you will call his name Emmanuel.
7:15 Curds and honey he will eat;
before he knows or chooses evil,
he will choose the good.
7:16 For before the child knows good or evil, he refuses wickedness, to choose the good; and the land which you fear because of the two kings will be forsaken.
7:17 But God will bring upon you, upon your people and upon your father’s house days that have not yet come since the day He removed Ephraim from Judah—the king of the Assyrians.”
7:18 On that day the Lord will whistle for flies—what rules a part of Egypt’s river—and for the bee that is in the Assyrians’ country,
7:19 and they will all come and rest in the country’s ravines, in holes of the rocks, in the caves, in every fissure and in every tree.
7:20 On that day the Lord will shave with the great and drunken razor that is beyond the river of the king of the Assyrians—the head and the hair of the feet—and He will remove the beard.
7:21 On that day a person will raise a heifer from the cattle and two sheep,
7:22 and because they produce very much milk, everyone left upon the land will eat butter and honey.
7:23 On that day every place where there are a thousand vines worth a thousand shekels will become fallow ground and thorn.
7:24 With missile and arrow they will enter there, because all the land will be fallow ground and thorn.
7:25 Every mountain that is plowed will be plowed, and fear will certainly not come there; for from fallow ground and thorn it will become pasture for sheep and a trampling place for cattle.

ISAIAH 8

8:1 The Lord said to me, “Take a large new sheet and write on it with a human stylus: ‘To make plunder of spoils swiftly; for it is at hand.’”
8:2 “Make reliable men witnesses for Me, Uriah and Zechariah son of Barachias.”
8:3 I approached the prophetess; she became pregnant and bore a son. The Lord said to me, “Call his name Plunder-quickly, Spoil-swiftly,
8:4 because before the child knows how to call father or mother, he will take Damascus’s power and Samaria’s spoils before the king of the Assyrians.”
8:5 The Lord spoke to me yet again,
8:6 “Because this people does not want Siloam’s water, which flows quietly, but wants to have Raasson and Romelias’s son as king over you,
8:7 therefore, behold, the Lord brings up against you the river’s strong and abundant water—the king of the Assyrians and his glory. He will ascend every ravine of yours and walk over every wall of yours,
8:8 and he will remove from Judea a person able to lift a head or someone strong enough to accomplish anything. His encampment will fill the breadth of your country. God is with us.”
8:9 Know, nations, and be defeated! Listen, you at the farthest edge of the earth! You who have grown strong, be defeated; for even if you grow strong again, you will again be defeated.
8:10 Whatever counsel you devise, the Lord will scatter; whatever word you speak will certainly not endure for you, because the Lord God is with us.
8:11 Thus says the Lord: “With a strong hand they resist the course of this people’s way, saying,
8:12 ‘Never say hard; for everything this people says is hard.’ But do not fear its fear, and do not be troubled.
8:13 Sanctify the Lord Himself, and He will be your fear.
8:14 If you trust in Him, He will become a sanctuary for you, and you will not encounter Him as the stumbling of a stone or as the falling of a rock. But Jacob’s house is in a trap, and those settled in Jerusalem in a pit.
8:15 Therefore many among them will be powerless, fall and be broken; people who are secure will draw near and be captured.”
8:16 Then those sealing up the law so that it may not be learned will be revealed.
8:17 He will say, “I will wait for God, who turned His face away from Jacob’s house, and I will put my trust in Him.”
8:18 Behold, I and the children God gave me—and it will be for signs and wonders in Israel’s house, from the Lord Sabaoth, who dwells on Mount Zion.
8:19 If they say to you, “Seek those who speak from the earth and the belly-speakers, the empty-talkers who speak from the belly,” should not a nation turn to its God? Why do they seek the dead concerning the living?
8:20 For He gave law for help, so that they might speak unlike this utterance, concerning which there are no gifts to give for it.
8:21 A harsh famine will come upon you; when you hunger, you will be grieved and speak evil of the ruler and the cult images, and they will look up toward heaven above,
8:22 and they will look down to the earth, and behold, affliction and confinement and darkness, constricted perplexity and darkness so that they cannot see,
8:23 and the one in confinement will not remain perplexed for a time. “Do this first, do it quickly, country of Zebulun, land of Naphtali, way of the sea, and the rest who dwell by the sea and beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations, the regions of Judea.”

ISAIAH 9

9:1 You people who walk in darkness, behold a great light;
you who dwell in a land and shadow of death,
a light will shine upon you.
9:2 The multitude of the people whom Thou hast brought down in Thy gladness,
they will rejoice before Thee,
as people rejoice at harvest,
and as those who divide the spoils.
9:3 For the yoke laid upon them has been taken away,
and the rod upon their neck;
for the Lord has shattered the rod of their oppressors,
as in the day against Midian.
9:4 For every robe gathered by deceit,
and every garment, they will repay with restitution;
and they will wish that they had been burned with fire.
9:5 For a child was born to us,
a son also was given to us,
whose rule was laid upon his shoulder;
and his name is called Messenger of Great Counsel.
For I will bring peace upon the rulers,
peace and health to him.
9:6 Great is his rule, and his peace has no limit,
upon the throne of David and his kingdom,
to establish it and uphold it in righteousness and judgment,
from now on and for ever.
The zeal of the Lord Sabaoth will do these things.
9:7 The Lord sent death against Jacob, and it came upon Israel,
9:8 and all Ephraim’s people and those settled in Samaria will know, saying in insolence and a lofty heart,
9:9 “Bricks have fallen, but come, let us hew stones, cut down sycamores and cedars, and build ourselves a tower.”
9:10 God will dash those rising against Mount Zion upon them, and He will scatter their enemies,
9:11 Syria from the sun’s rising and the Greeks from the sun’s setting, who devour Israel with their whole mouth. For all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.
9:12 The people did not turn back until they were struck, and they did not seek the Lord.
9:13 The Lord removed from Israel head and tail, great and small, in one day,
9:14 the elder and those who show favoritism—this is the leadership—and the prophet teaching lawless things—this is the tail.
9:15 Those blessing this people will lead astray, and they lead astray in order to swallow them up.
9:16 Therefore God will not rejoice over their young men, and He will not pity their orphans or widows, because all are lawless and evil, and every mouth speaks unjust things. For all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.
9:17 Lawlessness will burn like fire and be consumed by fire like dry grass; it will burn in the forest’s thickets and consume together everything around the hills.
9:18 Through the fury of the Lord’s anger the whole earth has been burned, and the people will be as though burned by fire. A person will not pity his brother,
9:19 but will turn to the right because he will hunger, and eat from the left; a person eating the flesh of his own arm will certainly not be filled.
9:20 For Manasseh will eat from Ephraim, and Ephraim from Manasseh, because together they will besiege Judah. For all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.

ISAIAH 10

10:1 Woe to those writing wickedness! For when they write, they write wickedness,
10:2 turning aside the judgment of the poor, seizing the justice of My people’s needy, so that a widow becomes their prey and an orphan their plunder.
10:3 What will they do on the day of visitation? For affliction will come to you from far away. To whom will you flee for help, and where will you leave your glory,
10:4 so as not to fall into captivity? For all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.
10:5 Woe to the Assyrians! The rod of My anger and wrath is in their hands.
10:6 I will send My anger against a lawless nation, and I will command My people to take spoils and plunder, trample the cities and reduce them to dust.
10:7 But he did not consider it this way and has not reckoned it this way in his soul; rather, his mind will turn aside, even to destroy not a few nations.
10:8 If they say to him, “You alone are ruler,”
10:9 he will say, “Did I not take the country above Babylon and Chalanneh, where the tower was built? I also took Arabia, Damascus and Samaria.
10:10 As I took these in my hand, I will also take all the dominions. Wail, carved images in Jerusalem and Samaria!
10:11 For as I did to Samaria and her handmade objects, so I will also do to Jerusalem and her idols.”
10:12 When the Lord has finished doing all things on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, He will bring judgment upon the great mind—the ruler of the Assyrians—and upon the height of the glory of his eyes.
10:13 For he said, “By strength I will act, and by the wisdom of understanding. I will remove nations’ boundaries, plunder their strength and shake inhabited cities.
10:14 I will seize the whole inhabited world in my hand like a nest, and lift it like abandoned eggs. There is no one who will escape me or contradict me.”
10:15 Will an ax be glorified without the one cutting with it, or a saw be raised without the one drawing it? Likewise, if someone lifts a rod or a piece of wood.
10:16 And it will not be so; rather, the Lord Sabaoth will send dishonor against your honor, and against your glory a burning fire will burn.
10:17 Israel’s light will become fire, and He will sanctify him in burning fire and consume the woodland like grass. On that day,
10:18 the mountains, hills and forests will be extinguished, and it will consume from soul to flesh. The one fleeing will be like someone fleeing from a burning flame.
10:19 Those left from them will be a number, and a child will write them down.
10:20 On that day Israel’s remainder will no longer continue, and Jacob’s survivors will certainly no longer trust those who wronged them; rather, they will trust God, Israel’s Holy One, in truth,
10:21 and Jacob’s remainder will be toward a mighty God.
10:22 Even if Israel’s people becomes like the sea’s sand, their remnant will be saved; for He is completing and cutting short a word in righteousness,
10:23 because God will carry out a shortened word in the whole inhabited world.

and a blossom will rise from the root.

the spirit of wisdom and understanding,
the spirit of counsel and might,
the spirit of knowledge and godliness.
11:3 The spirit of the fear of God will fill him.
He will not judge according to appearance,
nor reprove according to report;
11:4 but he will judge the cause of the lowly,
and reprove the lowly of the earth;
and he will strike the earth with the word of his mouth,
and with breath through his lips he will slay the ungodly.
11:5 His loins will be girded with righteousness,
and his sides bound with truth.
11:6 And the wolf will feed with the lamb,
and the leopard will rest with the young goat;
and the calf and the bull and the lion will feed together,
and a little child will lead them.
11:7 And the ox and the bear will feed together,
and their young will be together;
and the lion and the ox will eat straw together.
11:8 And an infant will stretch out his hand over the hole of asps,
and over the nest of their young.
11:9 And they will certainly do no harm,
nor will they be able at all to destroy anyone upon My holy mountain;
for the whole earth has been filled with the knowledge of the Lord,
as abundant water covers the seas.
11:10 On that day there will be Jesse’s root and the one rising to rule nations; nations will hope in him, and his rest will be honor.
11:11 On that day the Lord will again show His hand, to be zealous for the remaining remnant of the people left from the Assyrians, Egypt, Babylonia, Ethiopia, the Elamites, the sun’s rising and Arabia.
11:12 He will raise a signal to the nations and gather Israel’s lost ones; He will gather Judah’s scattered ones from the earth’s four wings.
11:13 Ephraim’s jealousy will be removed, and Judah’s enemies will perish. Ephraim will not envy Judah, and Judah will not afflict Ephraim.
11:14 They will fly in foreigners’ ships toward the sea; together they will plunder those from the sun’s rising and Idumea. Upon Moab they will first lay their hands, and Ammon’s sons will be the first to obey.
11:15 The Lord will lay Egypt’s sea waste and bring His hand upon the river with a violent wind. He will strike seven ravines so that one may pass through it in sandals.
11:16 There will be a passage for My remaining people in Egypt, and it will be for Israel as on the day it came out of Egypt’s land.

ISAIAH 13

13:1 The vision that Isaiah son of Amos saw against Babylon.
13:2 Raise a signal on a level mountain, lift your voice to them, do not fear, beckon with the hand: “Open, rulers!”
13:3 I command, and I lead them; they are consecrated, and I lead them. Giants are coming to fulfill My wrath, rejoicing and acting insolently together.
13:4 A sound of many nations upon the mountains, like many nations; a sound of kings and gathered nations. The Lord Sabaoth has commanded an armed nation,
13:5 to come from a distant land, from the farthest edge of heaven’s foundation—the Lord and His armed fighters—to destroy the whole inhabited world.
13:6 Wail, for the Lord’s day is near, and shattering will come from God.
13:7 Therefore every hand will slacken, and every human soul grow cowardly;
13:8 the envoys will be troubled, and labor pains will hold them like those of a woman giving birth. They will lament to one another and be distraught, and change their face as to flame.
13:9 For behold, the Lord’s incurable day comes, of wrath and anger, to make the whole inhabited world deserted and destroy its sinners from it.
13:10 For heaven’s stars, Orion and all heaven’s array will not give light; it will grow dark as the sun rises, and the moon will not give its light.
13:11 I will ordain evils for the whole inhabited world, and for the ungodly their sins; I will destroy the insolence of the lawless and lower the insolence of the proud.
13:12 Those left will be more precious than unrefined gold, and a human being more precious than the stone from Souphir.
13:13 For heaven will be enraged, and the earth shaken from its foundations through the fury of the Lord Sabaoth’s anger, on the day when His wrath comes upon it.
13:14 Those left will be like a fleeing little gazelle and a wandering sheep, with no one to gather them, so that each turns back to his own people and hastens to his own country.
13:15 Whoever is captured will be defeated, and those gathered together will fall by a sword.
13:16 They will dash their children before them, plunder their houses and take possession of their wives.
13:17 Behold, I am rousing the Medes against you, who do not value silver or have need of gold.
13:18 They will shatter young men’s arrows and certainly not pity your children; their eyes will not spare the children.
13:19 Babylon, which is called glorious by the king of the Chaldeans, will be as when God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah.
13:20 She will not be inhabited for everlasting time, nor will people ever enter her through many generations. Arabs will certainly not pass through her, and shepherds will certainly not rest in her.
13:21 Wild beasts will rest there, and the houses fill with sound; sirens will rest there, and demons dance there.
13:22 Donkey-centaurs will dwell there, and hedgehogs make nests in their houses. It comes quickly and will not delay.

ISAIAH 14

14:1 The Lord will pity Jacob and again choose Israel; they will rest upon their land, and the resident foreigner will be added to them and added to Jacob’s house,
14:2 and nations will take them and bring them into their place; they will inherit and multiply upon God’s land as male and female slaves. Those who took them captive will be captives, and those who ruled them will be ruled.
14:3 On that day God will give you rest from pain, your wrath and the harsh slavery in which you served them,
14:4 and you will take up this lament against Babylon’s king and say on that day, “How the one who made demands has rested, and the taskmaster rested!
14:5 God shattered the sinners’ yoke, the rulers’ yoke;
14:6 having struck a nation in wrath with an incurable blow, striking a nation with a blow of anger that did not spare,
14:7 he rested confidently. The whole earth cries out with gladness,
14:8 and Lebanon’s trees rejoiced over you, and Lebanon’s cedar: ‘Since you have fallen asleep, the one cutting us has not come up.’
14:9 Hades below grew bitter on meeting you. All the giants who ruled the earth rose together for you, those who raised all the nations’ kings from their thrones.
14:10 They will all answer and say to you, ‘You too were captured as we were, and were numbered among us.’
14:11 Your glory went down to Hades, your abundant gladness. They will spread decay beneath you, and your covering is a worm.
14:12 How the dawn-bearer who rises in the morning fell from heaven! The one dispatching to all the nations was shattered to earth.
14:13 You said in your mind, ‘I will ascend to heaven, set my throne above heaven’s stars, and sit on a high mountain, upon the high mountains toward the north.
14:14 I will ascend above the clouds; I will be like the Most High.’
14:15 But now you will descend to Hades and to the earth’s foundations.
14:16 Those who see you will marvel at you and say, ‘Is this the person who provoked the earth, shook kings,
14:17 who made the whole inhabited world deserted and pulled down the cities, who did not release those in captivity?’
14:18 All the nations’ kings fell asleep in honor, each in his own house;
14:19 but you will be thrown into the mountains like an abhorred dead man, with many dead pierced by swords, descending to Hades. As a garment stained with blood will not be clean,
14:20 so neither will you be clean, because you destroyed My land and killed My people. You will certainly not endure for everlasting time, evil offspring.
14:21 Prepare your children to be slaughtered because of your father’s sins, so that they do not rise, inherit the earth and fill the earth with cities.”
14:22 “I will rise against them,” says the Lord Sabaoth, “and destroy their name, remnant and offspring”—thus says the Lord—
14:23 “and make Babylonia deserted, so that hedgehogs dwell there; she will become nothing. I will make her a muddy abyss for destruction.”
14:24 Thus says the Lord Sabaoth: “As I said, so it will be, and as I counseled, so it will endure,
14:25 to destroy the Assyrians from My land and from My mountains. They will become a trampling place; their yoke will be removed from them, and their glory removed from the shoulders.”
14:26 This is the counsel the Lord has counseled concerning the whole inhabited world, and this the high hand over all the world’s nations.
14:27 For what the holy God has counseled, who will scatter? And who will turn back the high hand?
14:28 In the year King Achaz died, this utterance came:
14:29 “Do not rejoice, all you foreigners, because the yoke of the one striking you was shattered; for from snakes’ seed asp offspring will come, and their offspring will emerge as flying snakes.
14:30 The poor will be fed through him, and poor men will rest in peace; but he will destroy your seed with famine and destroy your remnant.
14:31 Wail, gates of cities! Let troubled cities cry out, all the foreigners, because smoke comes from the north, and there is no surviving.
14:32 What will the nations’ kings answer? That the Lord founded Zion, and through Him the humble among the people will be saved.”

15:2 Grieve over yourselves, for Debon too will perish. Where your altar is, there you will go up to weep. Over Nabau of Moab’s country, wail: baldness upon every head, every arm cut.

15:5 Moab’s heart cries within her as far as Segor, for she is a three-year-old heifer. On Louith’s ascent they will go up toward you weeping; along Aroniim’s road destruction and shaking cry out.

ISAIAH 16

16:1 I will send them like creeping creatures upon the earth. Surely Mount Zion is not a deserted rock?
16:2 For you will be like a fledgling removed from a bird taking flight, Daughter Moab. Then, Arnon,
16:3 take further counsel. Make a shelter of mourning for her continually. In midday darkness they flee; they were distraught. Do not be carried away.
16:4 Moab’s fugitives will sojourn with you; they will be a shelter for you from the pursuer, because your alliance was removed, and the ruler trampling the earth perished.
16:5 A throne will be set right with mercy, and one will sit upon it with truth in David’s tent, judging, seeking judgment and hastening righteousness.
16:6 We heard Moab’s insolence—exceedingly insolent. You raised up your arrogance. Your divination is not so,
16:7 not so. Moab will wail, for all in Moab’s country will wail. You will ponder for Deseth’s residents and not be ashamed.
16:8 Hesebon’s plains will mourn, and Sebama’s vine. You who devour the nations, trample her vineyards as far as Iazer; you will certainly not reach them. Wander in the wilderness! Those sent out were abandoned, for they crossed the wilderness.

16:13 This is the utterance the Lord spoke against Moab, whenever He spoke it.

ISAIAH 17

17:1 The utterance against Damascus. Behold, Damascus will be removed from the cities and become a ruin,
17:2 abandoned for everlasting time, a bed and resting place for flocks, with no one pursuing.
17:3 There will no longer be a stronghold for Ephraim to flee to, nor a kingdom in Damascus, and the Syrians’ remainder will perish; for you are no better than Israel’s sons and their glory. Thus says the Lord Sabaoth.
17:4 On that day Jacob’s glory will fail, and the fat parts of his glory will be shaken.
17:5 It will be as when someone gathers a standing harvest and reaps seed from the ears in his arm, as when someone gathers an ear in a firm ravine,
17:6 and a stalk is left in it, or like olive berries, two or three on a lofty top, or four or five left on their branches. Thus says the Lord, Israel’s God.
17:7 On that day a person will trust in the One who made him, and his eyes will look toward Israel’s Holy One,
17:8 and they will certainly not trust the altars or their hands’ works, which their fingers made, nor look at their trees or abominations.
17:9 On that day your cities will be abandoned as the Amorites and Hivites abandoned theirs before Israel’s sons, and they will be deserted,

17:11 on the day you plant, you will wander. In the morning, if you sow, it will blossom for harvest on whatever day you allot, and like a human father you will allot to your sons.
17:12 Woe, the multitude of many nations! Like a surging sea, so you will be troubled, and the back of many nations will roar like water.
17:13 Many nations are like abundant water, like much water violently rushing down. He will drive him away and pursue him far off, like chaff-dust when people winnow before wind, and like wheel-dust carried by a squall.
17:14 Toward evening there will be mourning; before morning it will be gone. This is the portion of those who plundered you, an inheritance for those who took possession of you.

ISAIAH 18

18:1 Woe, wings of a land of ships, beyond Ethiopia’s rivers,
18:2 the one sending hostages by sea and papyrus letters over the water! For swift messengers will go to a lofty nation and a foreign and harsh people—who is beyond it?—a hopeless and trampled nation. Now the land’s rivers,
18:3 all are like an inhabited country; their country will be inhabited as when a signal is raised from a mountain, and it will be audible like a trumpet’s voice.
18:4 For thus the Lord said to me, “There will be security in My city, like the light of midday heat, and it will be like a dew cloud on a harvest day.”
18:5 Before harvest, when the bloom is complete and the unripe grape blossoms as its flower forms unripe fruit, He will remove the small grape clusters with sickles, remove the vine branches and cut them down,
18:6 and leave them together for heaven’s birds and the earth’s wild beasts. Heaven’s birds will gather upon them, and all the earth’s wild beasts will come upon him.
18:7 At that time gifts will be brought to the Lord Sabaoth from a people afflicted and plucked, and from a great people from now on and for everlasting time—a hoping and trampled nation, which is in a part of its country’s river—to the place where the Lord Sabaoth’s name was called, Mount Zion.

ISAIAH 19

19:1 A vision of Egypt. Behold, the Lord sits upon a light cloud and will come into Egypt; Egypt’s handmade objects will be shaken before Him, and their heart overcome within them.
19:2 Egyptians will be roused against Egyptians; each will fight his brother and each his neighbor, city against city and district against district.
19:3 The Egyptians’ spirit will be troubled within them, and I will scatter their counsel. They will inquire of their gods, their statues, those speaking from the earth and the belly-speakers.
19:4 I will hand Egypt into the hands of harsh human masters, and harsh kings will rule them. Thus says the Lord Sabaoth.
19:5 The Egyptians will drink water beside the sea, but the river will fail and dry up.
19:6 Rivers and the river’s canals will fail, and every gathering of water dry up, in every marsh of reed and papyrus,
19:7 and all the green marsh growth around the river and everything sown by the river will dry up, destroyed by wind.
19:8 Fishermen will groan, and all casting a hook into the river will groan; those casting seines and those using casting nets will mourn.
19:9 Shame will seize those working split flax and those working fine linen,
19:10 and those warping them will be in pain, and all who make beer will be grieved and suffer in their souls.
19:11 Tanis’s rulers will be fools; the king’s wise counselors—their counsel will become foolish. How will you say to the king, “We are sons of the discerning, sons of kings from the beginning”?
19:12 Where are your wise men now? Let them announce to you and say what the Lord Sabaoth has counseled against Egypt.
19:13 Tanis’s rulers failed, and Memphis’s rulers were exalted; they will lead Egypt astray tribe by tribe.
19:14 For the Lord mixed for them a spirit of wandering; they led Egypt astray in all their works, as someone wanders while drunk and vomiting.
19:15 There will be no work for the Egyptians that will produce a head and tail, a beginning and end.
19:16 On that day the Egyptians will be like women in fear and trembling before the Lord Sabaoth’s hand, which He Himself will bring upon them.
19:17 The Judeans’ country will become a terror to the Egyptians; whenever anyone names her to them, they will fear because of the counsel the Lord has counseled against her.
19:18 On that day five cities in Egypt will speak the Canaanite language and swear by the Lord’s name. One city will be called City-Asedek.
19:19 On that day there will be an altar to the Lord in the Egyptians’ country and a pillar to the Lord by its border,
19:20 and it will be an everlasting sign to the Lord in Egypt’s country, because they will cry to the Lord on account of their oppressors. The Lord will send them a man who will save them; judging, he will save them.
19:21 The Lord will be known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians will know the Lord on that day. They will make sacrifices, vow vows to the Lord and pay them.
19:22 The Lord will strike the Egyptians with a great blow and heal them with healing. They will turn back to the Lord, and He will listen to them and heal them.
19:23 On that day there will be a road from Egypt toward the Assyrians. Assyrians will enter Egypt, and Egyptians go toward the Assyrians, and the Egyptians will serve the Assyrians.
19:24 On that day Israel will be third among the Assyrians and Egyptians, blessed upon the earth,
19:25 which the Lord Sabaoth blessed, saying, “Blessed be My people in Egypt and among the Assyrians, and My inheritance Israel.”

ISAIAH 20

20:4 for thus the king of the Assyrians will lead Egypt’s and the Ethiopians’ captivity, young men and elders, naked and without sandals, exposed as Egypt’s shame.
20:5 The Egyptians will be ashamed in defeat because of the Ethiopians, in whom the Egyptians had trusted, for they were their glory.
20:6 Those dwelling on this island will say, ‘Behold, we had trusted that we could flee to them for help, but they could not be saved from the king of the Assyrians. How, then, shall we be saved?’”

ISAIAH 22

22:3 All your rulers have fled; those captured are harshly bound, and the strong among you have fled far away.
22:4 Therefore I said, “Leave me; I will weep bitterly. Do not insist on comforting me over the shattering of the daughter of my race.”

22:6 The Elamites took quivers—mounted men upon horses and an assembled battle line.

22:18 and your glorious crown, and throw you into a great and immeasurable country. There you will die; He will make your fine chariot dishonored and your ruler’s house a trampling place,

ISAIAH 23

23:1 The vision of Tyre. Wail, ships of Carthage, because she has perished, and they no longer come from the Kitians’ land; she has been led captive.
23:2 Whom have the island-dwelling traders of Phoenicia become like, crossing the sea
23:3 upon much water, seed of traders? Like a harvest being brought in are the nations’ traders.
23:4 “Be ashamed, Sidon,” said the sea; and the sea’s strength said, “I did not labor or give birth or rear young men or raise maidens.”
23:5 When it becomes known to Egypt, pain will seize them concerning Tyre.
23:6 Go away to Carthage! Wail, residents of this island!
23:7 Was this not your insolence from the beginning, before she was handed over?
23:8 Who planned these things against Tyre? Is she weaker, or has she no strength? Her merchants are glorious, rulers of the earth.
23:9 The Lord Sabaoth planned it, to dissolve all the insolence of the glorious and dishonor everything glorious upon the earth.
23:10 Work your land, for ships no longer come from Carthage.
23:11 Your hand no longer has strength at sea, the hand that provoked kings. The Lord Sabaoth commanded concerning Canaan, to destroy her strength.
23:12 They will say, “You shall no longer continue in insolence and in wronging Sidon’s daughter.” Even if you go to the Kitians, there too you will have no rest,
23:13 and into the Chaldeans’ land: this too has been devastated by the Assyrians. There too you will have no rest, because her wall has fallen.
23:14 Wail, ships of Carthage, because your fortress has perished!
23:15 On that day Tyre will be left for seventy years, like a king’s time, like a person’s time. After seventy years, Tyre will be like a prostitute’s song:
23:16 “Take a lyre, wander through cities, forgotten prostitute! Play the lyre well, sing much, so that you may be remembered.”
23:17 After seventy years God will visit Tyre, and she will again be restored to her former state and become a trading center for all the inhabited world’s kingdoms.
23:18 Her trade and her wage will be holy to the Lord. It will not be collected for them; instead, all her trade will be for those dwelling before the Lord, to eat and drink and be filled, as a contribution, a memorial before the Lord.

ISAIAH 24

24:1 Behold, the Lord is destroying the inhabited world; He will make it desolate, uncover its face and scatter those dwelling in it.
24:2 The people will be like the priest, the slave like the master, the maidservant like the mistress; the buyer will be like the seller, the lender like the borrower, and the debtor like the one to whom he owes a debt.
24:3 The earth will be utterly destroyed, and the earth utterly plundered, for the Lord’s mouth has spoken these things.
24:4 The earth mourned, the inhabited world was destroyed, and the earth’s high ones mourned.
24:5 The earth acted lawlessly because of those dwelling in it, because they transgressed the law and changed the ordinances, an everlasting covenant.
24:6 Therefore a curse will consume the earth, because those dwelling in it sinned. Therefore those dwelling on the earth will be poor, and few people will be left.
24:7 Wine will mourn, the vine will mourn, and all who gladden the soul will groan.
24:8 The joy of tambourines has ceased; the impudence and wealth of the ungodly have ceased; the lyre’s sound has ceased.
24:9 They were ashamed and did not drink wine; sikera became bitter to those drinking it.
24:10 Every city was deserted; he will shut a house so that none may enter.
24:11 Wail over wine everywhere! All the earth’s gladness has ceased.
24:12 Cities will be left deserted, and abandoned houses will perish.
24:13 All these things will happen on the earth, among the nations: as when someone gleans an olive tree, so they will glean them, even when the grape harvest has ceased.
24:14 These will cry aloud, and those left upon the earth will rejoice together with the Lord’s glory. The sea’s water will be troubled;
24:15 therefore the Lord’s glory will be in the sea’s islands; the Lord’s name will be glorious—O Lord, Israel’s God!
24:16 From the earth’s wings we heard wonders: “Hope for the devout!” And they will say, “Woe to those who reject, those who reject the law!”
24:17 Fear, pit and trap are upon you who dwell on the earth!
24:18 Whoever flees the fear will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs from the pit will be caught by the trap, because windows from heaven have opened and the earth’s foundations will be shaken.
24:19 The earth will be utterly agitated, and the earth will be utterly at a loss;
24:20 The earth has tilted and will be shaken like a fruit-watcher’s shelter, like someone drunk and hungover. It will fall and will never be able to rise, for lawlessness has prevailed upon it.
24:21 God will bring His hand against heaven’s array and against the earth’s kings.
24:22 They will gather and shut them into a fortress and a prison; after many generations there will be a visitation of them.
24:23 The brick will melt and the wall will fall, because the Lord will reign in Zion and in Jerusalem and will be glorified before the elders.

ISAIAH 25

25:1 Lord my God, I will glorify Thee and sing Thy name, because Thou hast done wondrous things, an ancient, true plan. May it be so, Lord!
25:2 For Thou hast made cities a mound, fortified cities so that their foundations fall. The city of the ungodly will certainly never be rebuilt.
25:3 Therefore the poor people will bless Thee, and cities of wronged people will bless Thee.
25:4 For Thou didst become a helper to every lowly city and a shelter to those discouraged by need. Thou wilt rescue them from evil people, a shelter for the thirsty and a breath for wronged people.
25:5 They will bless Thee like fainthearted people thirsting in Zion because of ungodly people, to whom Thou didst hand us over.
25:6 The Lord Sabaoth will act for all the nations upon this mountain. They will drink gladness, drink wine and anoint themselves with perfume.
25:7 On this mountain, hand all these things over to the nations, for this plan concerns all the nations.
25:8 Death swallowed, having prevailed; and again God removed every tear from every face. He removed the people’s reproach from all the earth, for the Lord’s mouth has spoken.
25:9 On that day they will say, “Behold, our God, in whom we hoped and exulted; and we will rejoice in our salvation.”
25:10 For God will give rest upon this mountain, and Moab’s land will be trampled as people tread a threshing floor with wagons,
25:11 and he will release his hands, just as he himself brought low for destruction; he will bring low his insolence over what he laid his hands on,
25:12 and he will bring low the height of your wall’s refuge, and it will descend to the ground.

ISAIAH 26

26:1 On that day they will sing this song in Judah’s land, saying, “Behold, a fortified city! He will make our salvation a wall and an encircling wall.
26:2 Open the gates; let a people enter, keeping righteousness and keeping truth,
26:3 holding fast to truth and keeping peace. For in Thee
26:4 they hoped, Lord, for ever, the great, everlasting God,
26:5 Thou who didst humble and bring down those dwelling on high. Thou wilt cast down fortified cities and bring them to the ground,
26:6 and the feet of the meek and lowly will trample them.
26:7 The way of the devout became straight, and the way of the devout is prepared.
26:8 For the Lord’s way is judgment. We hoped in Thy name and in the remembrance
26:9 which our soul desires. From night my spirit rises early toward Thee, God, because Thine ordinances are light upon the earth. Learn righteousness, you dwelling upon the earth!
26:10 For the ungodly has ceased; he will certainly not learn righteousness upon the earth or practice truth. Let the ungodly be removed, so that he may not see the Lord’s glory.
26:11 Lord, Thine arm is high, and they did not know; when they know, they will be ashamed. Zeal will seize an undisciplined people, and now fire will consume the opponents.
26:12 Lord our God, give us peace, for Thou hast repaid everything to us.
26:13 Lord our God, acquire us! Lord, we know no other besides Thee; we name Thy name.
26:14 The dead will certainly not see life, nor will physicians ever raise them. Therefore Thou didst bring calamity, destroy and remove every male of theirs.
26:15 Add evils to them, Lord; add evils to all the earth’s glorious people!
26:16 Lord, in affliction I remembered Thee; in slight affliction Thy discipline was for us.
26:17 Like a woman in labor nearing childbirth and crying out in her pang, so we became toward Thy beloved because of fear of Thee, Lord.
26:18 We conceived, labored and gave birth; we produced the spirit of Thy salvation upon the earth, but those dwelling upon the earth will fall.
26:19 The dead will rise, those in the tombs will be awakened, and those in the earth will rejoice, for the dew from Thee is healing to them; but the earth of the ungodly will fall.”
26:20 Go, My people, enter your inner rooms, shut your door, hide for a little, a very little while, until the Lord’s wrath passes.
26:21 For behold, the Lord brings wrath from the holy place upon those dwelling on the earth. The earth will uncover its blood and will not cover those killed.

ISAIAH 27

27:1 On that day God will bring the holy, great and strong sword against the dragon, the fleeing serpent, against the dragon, the crooked serpent, and will kill the dragon.
27:2 On that day, a beautiful vineyard; a desire to lead a song against her.
27:3 “I am a strong city, a besieged city; in vain I will water her, for she will be captured by night, and by day the wall will fall.
27:4 There is none who has not seized her. Who will set me to guard stubble in a field? Because of this hostility I rejected her.” Therefore, for this reason the Lord God has done everything He commanded. “I have been burned!”
27:5 the residents in her will cry. “Let us make peace with Him; let us make peace.”
27:6 Those coming, Jacob’s children—Israel will sprout and flower, and the inhabited world will be filled with his fruit.
27:7 Surely he will not be struck just as he himself struck, and be killed just as he himself killed?
27:8 Fighting and reproaching, he will send them away. Wast Thou not the One planning with a harsh spirit to destroy them with a spirit of wrath?
27:9 Therefore Jacob’s lawlessness will be removed, and this is his blessing: when I remove his sin, when they make all altar stones cut down like fine dust. Their trees will certainly not remain, and their idols will be cut down like a forest far away.
27:10 The inhabited flock will be released like an abandoned flock; it will be pasture for a long time, and there they will rest.
27:11 After a time there will be nothing green in her because it has dried. Women coming from a spectacle, come! For this is a people without understanding; therefore their Maker will certainly not pity them, nor will the One who formed them ever have mercy.
27:12 On that day the Lord will fence off from the River’s canal to Rhinocorura; but you, gather Israel’s sons one by one.
27:13 On that day they will sound the great trumpet. Those lost in the Assyrians’ country and those lost in Egypt will come and bow to the Lord upon the holy mountain in Jerusalem.

ISAIAH 28

28:1 Woe to the crown of insolence, Ephraim’s hired men; the flower fallen from glory on the summit of the fertile mountain, those drunk without wine!
28:2 Behold, the Lord’s wrath is strong and harsh, like falling hail with no covering, falling violently. Like a great mass of water dragging a country, He will give the land rest with His hands.
28:3 Underfoot will be trampled the crown of insolence, Ephraim’s hired men.
28:4 The flower fallen from the hope of glory, on the high mountain’s summit, will be like an early fig: whoever sees it will want to swallow it before taking it into his hand.
28:5 On that day the Lord Sabaoth will be the crown of hope, woven of glory, for My people who remain;
28:6 they will be left with a spirit of judgment, for judgment and strength, as He prevents destruction.
28:7 For these have been led astray by wine and wandered because of sikera. Priest and prophet lost their senses because of wine, reeled from sikera’s intoxication and went astray. This is an apparition.
28:8 A curse will consume this counsel, for this counsel is for greed.
28:9 To whom did we announce evils, and to whom did we announce a message—you weaned from milk, torn from a breast?
28:10 Expect affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little, yet a little,
28:11 because of contemptuous lips, through another tongue, for they will speak to this people,
28:12 saying to it, “This is the rest for the hungry, and this is the shattering.” But they did not want to listen.
28:13 The utterance of the Lord God will become for them affliction upon affliction, hope upon hope, yet a little, yet a little, so that they go and fall backward, come into danger, be shattered and captured.
28:14 Therefore hear the Lord’s word, afflicted men and rulers of this people in Jerusalem:
28:15 Because you said, “We made a covenant with Hades and agreements with death; if a rushing storm passes, it will certainly not come upon us. We made falsehood our hope and will be sheltered by deceit,”
28:16 therefore thus says the Lord: “Behold, I will place in Zion’s foundations a costly, chosen, precious cornerstone, in her foundations, and whoever trusts in it will certainly not be ashamed.
28:17 I will make judgment a hope, and My mercy will be weights; those trusting deceit do so in vain, for a storm will certainly not pass you by—
28:18 might it also remove your covenant with death? Your hope toward Hades will certainly not remain. If a rushing storm comes, you will become its trampling ground.
28:19 When it passes, it will take you; morning after morning it will pass during the day, and at night there will be evil hope. Learn to listen!
28:20 Cramped, we cannot fight, and we ourselves are too weak to be gathered.
28:21 Like a mountain of ungodly people he will rise and be in Gabaon’s ravine. With wrath he will do his deeds, a work of bitterness; his wrath will act strangely, and his bitterness is foreign.”
28:22 And you, do not rejoice, nor let your bonds grow stronger, because I heard from the Lord Sabaoth completed and shortened deeds, which He will do upon all the earth.
28:23 Give ear and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my words.
28:24 Will the plowman plow all day? Or will he prepare seed before working the ground?
28:25 When he has leveled its surface, does he not then sow a little black cumin and cumin, and again sow wheat, barley and emmer within your borders?
28:26 You will be disciplined by your God’s judgment and will rejoice.
28:27 For black cumin is not cleaned with harshness, nor will a wagon wheel roll over cumin; black cumin is shaken out with a rod, but cumin
28:28 will be eaten with bread. For I will not be angry with you for ever, nor will the sound of My bitterness trample you.
28:29 These wonders too came from the Lord Sabaoth. Take counsel; raise up futile consolation!

ISAIAH 29

29:1 Woe, city Ariel, against which David fought! Gather produce year after year, for you will eat with Moab.
29:2 For I will press Ariel hard, and her strength and wealth will be Mine.
29:3 I will surround you like David, set a palisade around you and set towers around you,
29:4 and your words will be humbled into the earth and sink into the earth. Your voice will be like those who speak from the earth, and your voice will weaken toward the ground.
29:5 The wealth of the ungodly will be like dust from a wheel, like drifting chaff, and it will be like a moment, immediately,
29:6 from the Lord Sabaoth. For there will be a visitation with thunder, earthquake and a great sound, a rushing storm and a consuming flame of fire.
29:7 Like someone dreaming in sleep will be the wealth of all nations who marched against Ariel, all who marched against Jerusalem, and all assembled against her and afflicting her.
29:8 They will be like people drinking and eating in sleep; when they rise, their dream is futile. As a thirsty person dreams of drinking but wakes still thirsty, his soul having hoped in vain, so will be the wealth of all nations who marched against Mount Zion.
29:9 Grow faint, lose your senses and suffer a hangover, neither from sikera nor from wine,
29:10 because the Lord has made you drink a spirit of stupor, and He will close their eyes and those of their prophets and rulers, those who see hidden things.
29:11 All these sayings will be for you like the words of this sealed book. If they give it to a person who knows how to read, saying, “Read these,” he will say, “I cannot read, for it is sealed.”
29:12 This book will be given into the hands of a person who does not know how to read. Someone will say to him, “Read this,” and he will say, “I do not know how to read.”
29:13 The Lord said, “This people draws near Me; with their lips they honor Me, but their heart is far from Me. They revere Me in vain, teaching human commands and teachings.
29:14 Therefore, behold, I will proceed to remove this people; I will remove them, destroy the wise people’s wisdom and hide the discerning people’s understanding.”
29:15 Woe to those making deep counsel, but not through the Lord! Woe to those making counsel in secret! Their deeds will be in darkness, and they will say, “Who has seen us, and who will know us or what we do?”
29:16 Will you not be reckoned as the potter’s clay? Will the formed thing say to its former, “You did not form me,” or the work to its maker, “You did not make me intelligently”?
29:17 Is it not yet a little while until Lebanon is changed like Mount Chermel, and Mount Chermel is reckoned as forest?
29:18 On that day the deaf will hear a book’s words, and the blind people’s eyes in darkness and mist will see.
29:19 The poor will exult with joy because of the Lord, and the hopeless among people will be filled with gladness.
29:20 The lawless one has failed, the arrogant one has perished, and those acting lawlessly for evil have been destroyed,
29:21 and those who cause people to sin by a word. They will make all who reprove at the gates a stumbling block, and have turned the righteous aside through injustices.
29:22 Therefore thus says the Lord concerning Jacob’s house, which He set apart from Abraham: “Jacob will not now be ashamed, nor will Israel now change his face;
29:23 but when their children see My works, because of Me they will sanctify My name, sanctify Jacob’s Holy One and fear Israel’s God.
29:24 Those wandering in spirit will know understanding; grumblers will learn obedience, and stammering tongues will learn to speak peace.”

ISAIAH 30

30:1 “Woe, rebellious children,” thus says the Lord, “you made a plan not through Me and agreements not through My spirit, to add sins upon sins,
30:2 you who go down to Egypt, but they did not ask Me, to be helped by Pharaoh and sheltered by Egyptians.
30:3 For Pharaoh’s shelter will become your shame, and reproach for those trusting in Egypt.
30:4 For there are leaders in Tanis, evil messengers; they will labor in vain
30:5 toward a people that will not benefit them, neither for assistance nor for profit, but for shame and reproach.”
30:6 The vision of the four-footed creatures in the wilderness. In affliction and hardship, a lion and lion cub from there, asps and offspring of flying asps; they carried their wealth on donkeys and camels to a nation that will not benefit them with help, but with shame and reproach.
30:7 Egyptians will benefit you with futile and empty things. Announce to them, “This consolation of yours is futile.”
30:8 Now then, sit and write these things on a tablet and in a book, because these things will endure for days of appointed times and for ever.
30:9 For this is a disobedient people, lying sons who did not want to hear God’s law,
30:10 who say to the prophets, “Do not announce to us,” and to those seeing visions, “Do not speak to us. Instead, speak to us and announce another deception,
30:11 and turn us from this way, remove this path from us, and remove Israel’s Holy One from us.”
30:12 Therefore thus says the Lord, Israel’s Holy One: “Because you disobeyed these words and hoped in falsehood, and because you grumbled and came to trust in this word,
30:13 therefore this sin will be for you like a wall falling immediately in a captured fortified city, whose fall is immediately at hand,
30:14 and its fall will be like the shattering of an earthen vessel, fine pieces from a jar, so that no shard is found among them with which to lift fire or draw a little water.”
30:15 Thus says the Lord, Israel’s Holy One: “When you turn back and groan, then you will be saved and know where you were. When you trusted in futile things, your strength became futile.” But you did not want to listen,
30:16 but you said, “We will flee on horses.” Therefore you will flee. You said, “We will ride swift mounts.” Therefore those pursuing you will be swift.
30:17 At the voice of one, a thousand will flee; at the voice of five, many will flee, until you are left like a mast upon a mountain, like someone carrying a standard upon a hill.
30:18 Again God will wait to pity you, and therefore He will be exalted to have mercy on you, because the Lord our God is a judge. Where will you leave your glory? Blessed are those remaining in Him.
30:19 For a holy people will dwell in Zion, and Jerusalem wept bitterly, “Have mercy on me!” He will have mercy on you at the sound of your cry; when He saw, He listened to you.
30:20 The Lord will give you bread of affliction and scant water; those misleading you will certainly no longer approach you, for your eyes will see those misleading you,
30:21 and your ears will hear the words of those behind you who led you astray, saying, “This is the way; let us walk in it, whether right or left.”
30:22 You will remove the silver-covered and gold-covered idols, reduce them to fine pieces and winnow them like a menstruating woman’s fluid; like dung you will push them away.
30:23 Then there will be rain for your land’s seed; bread from your land’s produce will be plentiful and rich, and on that day your livestock will graze a fertile, spacious place,
30:24 your bulls and cattle working the earth will eat straw prepared with barley and winnowed.
30:25 On every high mountain and every lofty hill there will be flowing water on that day, when many perish and towers fall.
30:26 The moon’s light will be like the sun’s light, and the sun’s light will be sevenfold on the day when the Lord heals His people’s fracture; He will heal the pain of your wound.
30:27 Behold, the Lord’s name comes after a long time, wrath burning. His lips’ utterance comes with glory, an utterance full of anger, and the anger of His wrath will consume like fire.
30:28 His spirit will come like water sweeping through a ravine, reaching the neck, and will divide to trouble nations in futile wandering. Delusion will pursue them and seize them in their faces.
30:29 Must you always rejoice and always enter My holy places like festival-keepers, like people rejoicing as they enter with a flute to the Lord’s mountain, to Israel’s God?
30:30 God will make His voice’s glory heard and show His arm’s wrath with fury, anger and consuming flame. He will strike violently with lightning, like water and hail falling together violently.
30:31 For at the Lord’s voice the Assyrians will be defeated by the blow with which He strikes them.
30:32 It will be all around him, from where he hoped for help, on which he himself relied; they will turn and fight him with flutes and a lyre.
30:33 For you will be called to account ahead of time. Has it also been prepared for you to rule a deep ravine, with wood laid out, fire and much wood? The Lord’s wrath is like a ravine burning with sulfur.

ISAIAH 31

31:1 Woe to those going down to Egypt for help, trusting in horses and chariots because there are many, and in horses, a very great multitude, but not trusting Israel’s Holy One or seeking God!
31:2 He Himself, wise, brought evils upon them; His word will certainly not be set aside. He will rise against evil people’s houses and against their futile hope,
31:3 an Egyptian human being and not God, horses’ flesh, with no help. The Lord will bring His hand against them; the helpers will grow weary, and all will perish together.
31:4 For thus the Lord said to me: “As a lion or lion cub cries over prey it has caught and shouts over it until the mountains fill with its voice, and they are defeated and terrified by the greatness of its wrath, so the Lord Sabaoth will descend to march upon Mount Zion, upon her mountains.
31:5 Like flying birds, so the Lord will shield Jerusalem, rescue, preserve and save.”
31:6 Turn back, you planning a deep and lawless plan.
31:7 For on that day people will renounce their silver and golden handmade things, which their hands made.
31:8 Assour will fall. Neither a man’s sword nor a human’s sword will devour him, and he will flee, but not from a sword. The young men will be defeated,
31:9 for they will be enclosed by a rock as by a palisade and defeated, and the fugitive will be captured. Thus says the Lord: “Blessed is the one who has seed in Zion and household members in Jerusalem.”

ISAIAH 32

32:1 For behold, a righteous king will reign, and rulers will rule with judgment.
32:2 The man will conceal his words and hide as from rushing water; he will appear in Zion like a rushing river, glorious in a thirsty land.
32:3 They will no longer trust in human beings, but will give their ears to listen.
32:4 The heart of the weak will pay attention and listen, and stammering tongues will quickly learn to speak peace.
32:5 They will no longer tell the fool to rule, and your attendants will no longer say, “Be silent.”
32:6 For the fool will speak foolish things, and his heart will think futile things, carrying out lawless acts and speaking deception toward the Lord, scattering hungry souls and making thirsty souls empty.
32:7 For evil people’s counsel will devise lawless things, to destroy the lowly with unjust words and scatter the lowly people’s words in judgment.
32:8 The devout made wise plans, and this counsel will remain.
32:9 Wealthy women, rise and hear my voice! Daughters in hope, hear my words!
32:10 Remember the days of a year in pain with hope; the vintage has been exhausted, the sowing has ceased and will certainly no longer come.
32:11 Be dismayed, grieve, you confident women; undress, become naked and wrap sackcloth around your waists,
32:12 and beat your breasts because of the desirable field and fruitful vine.
32:13 My people’s earth will grow thorn and grass, and joy will be removed from every house. Wealthy city,
32:14 houses abandoned! They will leave the city’s wealth and desirable houses; villages will become caves for ever, wild donkeys’ delight, pastures for shepherds,
32:15 until a spirit from on high comes upon you. Chermel will become wilderness, and Chermel will be reckoned as forest.
32:16 Judgment will rest in the wilderness, and righteousness will dwell in Carmel.
32:17 The works of righteousness will be peace; righteousness will secure rest, and they will be confident for ever.
32:18 His people will dwell in a city of peace and live confidently, and they will rest with wealth.
32:19 If hail comes down, it will not come upon you. Those dwelling in the forests will be confident like those in the plain.
32:20 Blessed are those sowing beside every water, where ox and donkey tread!

ISAIAH 33

33:1 Woe to those afflicting you—yet no one afflicts you—and the one rejecting you does not reject. The rejecters will be captured and handed over; like a moth upon a cloak, so they will be defeated.
33:2 Lord, have mercy on us, for we have trusted in Thee. The disobedient people’s seed came to destruction, but our salvation is in a time of affliction.
33:3 At the sound of Thy terror, peoples were dismayed by fear of Thee, and nations scattered.
33:4 Now your spoils, small and great, will be gathered; as someone gathers locusts, so they will mock you.
33:5 Holy is God, who dwells on high; Zion has been filled with judgment and righteousness.
33:6 They will be handed over under law; our salvation is among treasures. There are wisdom, knowledge and reverence toward the Lord; these are treasures of righteousness.
33:7 Behold, in your terror they themselves will fear; those you feared will fear you. For messengers will be sent asking for peace, weeping bitterly, pleading for peace.
33:8 For their roads will be deserted; fear of the nations has ceased, the covenant with these people is removed, and you will certainly not reckon them as human beings.
33:9 The earth mourned; Lebanon was ashamed; Sharon became marshes. Galilee and Carmel will be clearly visible.
33:10 “Now I will rise,” says the Lord. “Now I will be glorified; now I will be exalted.
33:11 Now you will see, now you will perceive; your spirit’s strength will be futile. Fire will consume you.
33:12 Nations will be burned, like a thorn thrown in a field and burned.
33:13 Those far away will hear what I did; those drawing near will know My strength.”
33:14 The lawless in Zion departed; trembling will seize the ungodly. Who will announce to you that fire burns? Who will announce to you the everlasting place?
33:15 One walking in righteousness, speaking a straight way, hating lawlessness and injustice, shaking his hands free of gifts, making his ears heavy so as not to hear a judgment of blood, and closing his eyes so as not to see injustice,
33:16 this one will dwell in a high cave of strong rock. Bread will be given him, and his water is trustworthy.
33:17 You will see a king with glory, and your eyes will see a land from afar.
33:18 Your soul will ponder fear: “Where are the scholars? Where are the counselors? Where is the one counting those being fed,
33:19 a people small and great?” With whom they did not deliberate, nor did he know a deep voice, so that a despised people would not hear; the hearer has no understanding.
33:20 Behold, Zion, the city, our salvation! Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a wealthy city, tents that will certainly not be shaken; her tent’s pegs will never be moved, nor her ropes torn.
33:21 For the Lord’s name is great for you. There will be a place for you, rivers and broad, spacious canals. You will not go this way, nor will a rowing ship go.
33:22 For my God is great; the Lord will not pass me by. The Lord is our judge, the Lord our ruler, the Lord our king; this One will save us.
33:23 Your ropes tore because it was not strong; your mast tilted. It will not lower the sails or raise a signal until it is handed over for plunder. Then many lame people will take spoil.
33:24 The people dwelling among them will certainly not say, “I am weary,” for their sin has been forgiven.

ISAIAH 34

34:1 Draw near, nations, and hear, rulers! Let the earth and those in it hear, the inhabited world and the people in it!
34:2 For the Lord’s wrath is against all nations, and anger against their number, to destroy them and hand them over to slaughter.
34:3 Their wounded and dead will be thrown out; their odor will rise, and mountains will be soaked with their blood.
34:4 Heaven will roll up like a scroll, and all the stars will fall like leaves from a vine, like leaves falling from a fig tree.
34:5 My sword became drunk in heaven. Behold, it will descend upon Idumea and upon the people of destruction, with judgment.
34:6 The Lord’s sword was filled with blood, thickened with lambs’ fat and the fat of male goats and rams, for the Lord has a sacrifice in Bosor and a great slaughter in Idumea.
34:7 The stout ones will fall with them, and rams and bulls; the earth will become drunk with blood and be filled with their fat.
34:8 For it is the Lord’s day of judgment and a year of repayment for Zion’s judgment.
34:9 Her ravines will turn to pitch, her earth to sulfur, and her land will burn like pitch
34:10 night and day. It will not be quenched for an everlasting time, and her smoke will rise upward. For generations and a long time she will be deserted.
34:11 Birds, hedgehogs, ibises and ravens will dwell in her; a measuring cord for deserted land will be cast upon her, and donkey-centaurs will dwell in her.
34:12 Her rulers will be no more, for her kings, her rulers and her magnates will come to destruction.
34:13 Thorny trees will sprout in their cities and her fortifications; she will become a dwelling for sirens and a courtyard for ostriches.
34:14 Demons will meet donkey-centaurs and cry one to another. There donkey-centaurs will rest, for they found rest for themselves.
34:15 There a hedgehog nested, and the earth kept her children safe. There deer met and saw one another’s faces;
34:16 they passed by in number, and not one of them perished; none sought another, for the Lord commanded them and His spirit gathered them.
34:17 He Himself will cast lots for them, and His hand apportioned grazing. You will inherit for an everlasting time; for generations of generations they will rest upon her.

ISAIAH 35

35:1 Rejoice, thirsty wilderness! Let the wilderness exult and flower like a lily,
35:2 and the Jordan’s desert places will flower and exult. Lebanon’s glory and Carmel’s honor were given to her, and My people will see the Lord’s glory and God’s height.
35:3 Be strong, limp hands and paralyzed knees!
35:4 Give encouragement, you fainthearted in mind. Be strong; do not fear! Behold, our God repays judgment and will repay; He Himself will come and save us.
35:5 Then blind people’s eyes will open, and deaf people’s ears will hear.
35:6 Then the lame person will leap like a deer, and the tongue of those with difficulty speaking will be clear, for water burst out in the wilderness and a ravine in a thirsty land,
35:7 the waterless place will become marshes, and a water spring will be in the thirsty land. There will be birds’ gladness, a dwelling of reeds and marshes.
35:8 There will be a clean road, called a Holy Way. No unclean person will ever pass there, nor will there be an unclean way there; the scattered will walk upon it and will certainly not wander.
35:9 No lion will be there, nor will any evil wild beast ever go up on it or be found there; instead, the redeemed will walk in it.
35:10 Those gathered through the Lord will return and come to Zion with gladness, everlasting gladness above their heads; for praise and exultation are upon their heads, and gladness will overtake them. Pain, grief and groaning have fled.

ISAIAH 36

36:1 In the fourteenth year of Hezekiah’s reign, Sennacherib king of the Assyrians came up against Judea’s fortified cities and took them.
36:2 The king of the Assyrians sent Rapsakes from Lachis to Jerusalem, to King Hezekiah, with a large force. He stood at the upper pool’s aqueduct, on the road of the fuller’s field.
36:3 Eliakim son of Chelkias, the steward, Somnas the scribe, and Joach son of Asaph, the record-keeper, went out to him.
36:4 Rapsakes said to them, “Tell Hezekiah: Thus says the great king, the king of the Assyrians: ‘What do you trust in?
36:5 Does a battle line arise through counsel or words of lips? Now whom have you trusted, that you disobey me?
36:6 Behold, you trust this broken reed staff, Egypt. Whoever leans on it, it will enter his hand. Such are Pharaoh king of Egypt and all who trust in him.
36:7 But if you say, “We trust the Lord our God,”
36:8 now join with my master, the king of the Assyrians, and I will give you two thousand horses, if you can provide riders for them.
36:9 How can you face even one district governor? Those trusting Egyptians for horses and riders are servants.
36:10 Now have we come up without the Lord against this country to wage war upon it?’”
36:11 Eliakim, Somnas and Joach said to him, “Speak Syrian to your servants, for we understand, and do not speak Judean to us. Why do you speak within hearing of the people on the wall?”
36:12 Rapsakes said to them, “Has my master sent me to your master or to you to speak these words? Was it not to the people sitting on the wall, so that they eat dung and drink urine together with you?”
36:13 Rapsakes stood and cried in a loud voice in Judean: “Hear the words of the great king, the king of the Assyrians:
36:14 Thus says the king: ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you with words, which will not be able to rescue you,
36:15 and do not let Hezekiah tell you, “God will rescue you, and this city will certainly not be handed into the king of the Assyrians’ hand.”
36:16 Do not listen to Hezekiah. Thus says the king of the Assyrians: If you wish to be blessed, come out to me; each will eat from his vine and fig trees, and you will drink your cistern’s water,
36:17 until I come and take you to a land like your land, a land of grain, wine, bread and vineyards.
36:18 Do not let Hezekiah deceive you, saying, “Your God will rescue you.” Have the nations’ gods each rescued his own country from the king of the Assyrians’ hand?
36:19 Where is the god of Aimath and Arphath? Where is the god of Seppharim’s city? Were they able to rescue Samaria from my hand?
36:20 Which of all these nations’ gods rescued his land from my hand, that God should rescue Jerusalem from my hand?’”
36:21 They were silent, and no one answered him a word, because the king had ordered that no one answer.
36:22 Eliakim son of Chelkias, the steward, Somnas the army’s scribe, and Joach son of Asaph, the record-keeper, came to Hezekiah with torn tunics and reported Rapsakes’ words.

ISAIAH 37

37:1 When King Hezekiah heard, he tore his garments, wrapped himself in sackcloth, and went up to the Lord’s house.
37:2 He sent Eliakim the steward, Somnas the scribe, and the elders of the priests, wrapped in sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amos.
37:3 They said to him, “Thus says Hezekiah: Today is a day of distress, reproach, reproof, and wrath, for labor has come upon the woman giving birth, but she has no strength to give birth.
37:4 May the Lord your God hear Rapsakes’ words, which the king of the Assyrians sent to reproach the living God and to reproach words that the Lord your God has heard. You shall plead with the Lord your God for those who remain.”
37:5 The king’s servants came to Isaiah,
37:6 and Isaiah said to them, “You shall say this to your master: Thus says the Lord: Do not fear the words you heard, with which the king of the Assyrians’ envoys reproached Me.
37:7 Behold, I will put a spirit into him. On hearing a report he will return to his country, and he will fall by the sword in his own land.”
37:8 Rapsakes returned and found the king besieging Lomna. The king of the Assyrians heard that
37:9 Tharaka king of the Ethiopians had come out to besiege him. On hearing this he turned back and sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,
37:10 “You shall say this to Hezekiah king of Judea: Do not let your God, on whom you rely, deceive you by saying, ‘Jerusalem will certainly not be handed into the king of the Assyrians’ hands.’
37:11 Or have you not heard what the kings of the Assyrians did—how they destroyed the whole earth?
37:12 Did the gods of the nations my fathers destroyed rescue them—Gozan, Charran, and Raphes, which are in the country of Themad?
37:13 Where are the kings of Aimath and Arphath, and of the city of Seppharim, Anag, and Ougaua?”
37:14 Hezekiah took the document from the messengers and opened it before the Lord.
37:15 Hezekiah prayed to the Lord, saying,
37:16 “Lord Sabaoth, God of Israel, seated upon the cherubim, Thou alone art God of every kingdom of the inhabited world. Thou didst make heaven and earth.
37:17 Hear, Lord; look, Lord, and see the words that Sennacherib sent to reproach the living God.
37:18 For the kings of the Assyrians have truly laid waste the whole inhabited world and their country,
37:19 and cast their idols into the fire. For they were not gods, but works of human hands, wood and stones, and they destroyed them.
37:20 But Thou, Lord our God, save us from their hand, so that every kingdom of the earth may know that Thou alone art God.”
37:21 Isaiah son of Amos was sent to Hezekiah and said to him, “Thus says the Lord, God of Israel: I heard what you prayed to Me about Sennacherib king of the Assyrians.
37:22 This is the word God spoke concerning him: Virgin daughter Zion despised and sneered at you; daughter Jerusalem shook her head over you.
37:23 Whom did you reproach and provoke? Against whom did you raise your voice? Yet you did not lift your eyes on high toward the Holy One of Israel.
37:24 For through messengers you reproached the Lord. You said, ‘With my many chariots I climbed the mountain heights and the furthest reaches of Lebanon. I cut down its cedar’s height and the cypress’s beauty, and entered the height of a part of the woodland.
37:25 I laid a bridge and dried up waters and every gathering of water.’
37:26 Did you not hear long ago about these things I did? From ancient days I arranged them; now I have shown them: to desolate nations in strongholds and those dwelling in fortified cities.
37:27 I let the hands go, and they withered and became like dry grass on roofs and like wild grass.
37:28 But now I know your rest, your going out and your coming in.
37:29 Your wrath with which you raged, and your bitterness, have risen to Me. I will put a muzzle in your nose and a bridle in your lips, and turn you back by the way you came.
37:30 This is your sign: This year eat what you have sown, in the second year what remains, and in the third, after sowing, reap and plant vineyards; you will eat their fruit.
37:31 Those remaining in Judea will grow a root below and produce seed above.
37:32 For those remaining will go out from Jerusalem, and those being saved from Mount Zion. The zeal of the Lord Sabaoth will do these things.
37:33 Therefore thus says the Lord against the king of the Assyrians: He will certainly not enter this city or shoot a missile against it; he will certainly not bring a shield against it or surround it with a siegework.
37:34 By the way he came he will return. Thus says the Lord.
37:35 I will shield this city to save it, because of Me and because of David My servant.”
37:36 An angel of the Lord went out and killed one hundred eighty-five thousand from the Assyrians’ camp. On rising early in the morning, they found all the bodies dead.
37:37 The king of the Assyrians turned back, went away, and lived in Nineveh.
37:38 While he was worshiping his cult-image in the house of Nasarach, his sons Adramelech and Sarasar struck him with swords. They escaped safely to Armenia, and his son Asordan reigned in his place.

ISAIAH 38

38:1 At that time Hezekiah became ill to the point of death. The prophet Isaiah son of Amos came to him and said, “Thus says the Lord: Arrange your household’s affairs, for you are dying and will not live.”
38:2 Hezekiah turned his face toward the wall and prayed to the Lord,
38:3 saying, “Remember, Lord, how I walked before Thee with truth, in a true heart, and did what was pleasing before Thee.” Hezekiah wept greatly.
38:4 The Lord’s word came to Isaiah, saying,
38:5 “Go and tell Hezekiah: Thus says the Lord, God of David your father: I heard the voice of your prayer and saw your tears. Behold, I am adding fifteen years to your time.
38:6 I will save you from the king of the Assyrians’ hand, and I will shield this city.
38:7 This is your sign from the Lord that God will do this thing:”
38:8 “As for the shadow of the steps the sun descended—the ten steps of your father’s house—I will turn the sun back ten steps.” The sun went up the ten steps which the shadow had descended.
38:9 A prayer of Hezekiah king of Judea, when he became ill and rose from his illness:
38:10 I said, “At the height of my days, at Hades’ gates, I will leave my remaining years behind.”
38:11 I said, “I will certainly no longer see God’s salvation upon the earth; I will certainly no longer see a person
38:12 from my family. I left the rest of my life; it went out and departed from me like someone taking down a tent he had pitched. My spirit beside me became like a weaver’s web as she approached to cut it off. On that day I was handed over
38:13 until morning, as to a lion; thus he crushed my bones, for from day to night I was handed over.
38:14 Like a swallow I will cry out, and like a dove I will murmur. For my eyes failed from looking toward heaven’s height, toward the Lord who rescued me
38:15 and took away my soul’s pain.
38:16 For, Lord, a report about it was brought to Thee, and Thou didst raise my breath; comforted, I lived.
38:17 For Thou didst choose my soul so that it would not perish, and didst cast all my sins behind me.
38:18 For those in Hades will not praise Thee, nor will the dead bless Thee, nor will those in Hades hope for Thy mercy.
38:19 The living will bless Thee, as I do. For from today I will have children who will announce Thy righteousness,
38:20 Lord of my salvation. I will not cease blessing Thee with a harp all the days of my life before God’s house.”
38:21 Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Take a cake made of figs, crush it and apply it as a poultice, and you will be healthy.”
38:22 Hezekiah said, “This is the sign that I will go up to the house of the Lord God.”

ISAIAH 39

39:1 At that time Marodach son of Laadan, king of Babylonia, sent letters, envoys, and gifts to Hezekiah, for he heard that he had been ill to the point of death and had recovered.
39:2 Hezekiah rejoiced greatly over them and showed them the store of nekhotha, liquid myrrh, incense, perfumed ointment, silver, gold, all the storerooms of the treasury’s equipment, and everything in his treasuries. There was nothing in his house that Hezekiah did not show.
39:3 The prophet Isaiah came to King Hezekiah and said, “What are these men saying, and from where have they come to you?” Hezekiah said, “They have come to me from a distant land, from Babylon.”
39:4 Isaiah said, “What did they see in your house?” Hezekiah said, “They saw everything in my house. There is nothing in my house they did not see, and also the things in my treasuries.”
39:5 Isaiah said to him, “Hear the word of the Lord Sabaoth:
39:6 Behold, days are coming, says the Lord, and they will take everything in your house. What your fathers gathered until this day will reach Babylon, and they will certainly leave nothing behind. God also said
39:7 that they will also take some of your children whom you fathered and make them eunuchs in the house of the Babylonians’ king.”
39:8 Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The Lord’s word that He spoke is good. Let there indeed be peace and righteousness in my days.”

ISAIAH 40

40:1 Comfort, comfort My people, says God.
40:2 You priests, speak to the heart of Jerusalem; comfort her.
For her humiliation is fulfilled, her sin is loosed;
for from the hand of the Lord she has received double for her sins.
40:3 A voice of one crying in the wilderness:
Prepare the way of the Lord;
make straight the paths of our God.
40:4 Every valley will be filled,
and every mountain and hill will be brought low;
all the crooked places will become straight,
and the rough country will become plains.
40:5 And the glory of the Lord will be seen,
and all flesh will see the salvation of God;
for the Lord has spoken.
40:6 A voice says, “Cry out!”
And I said, “What shall I cry?”
All flesh is grass,
and all the glory of man is as the flower of grass.
40:7 The grass has withered, and the flower has fallen;
40:8 but the word of our God remains for ever.
40:9 Go up on a high mountain, you who bring good news to Zion;
lift up your voice with strength, you who bring good news to Jerusalem.
Lift up; do not fear.
Say to the cities of Judah, Behold your God!
40:10 Behold, the Lord comes with might, and His arm with dominion;
behold, His reward is with Him, and His work before Him.
40:11 Like a shepherd, He will shepherd His flock;
and with His arm He will gather lambs,
and comfort those that are with young.
40:12 Who measured the water with His hand, heaven with a span, and all the earth with a handful? Who weighed mountains with a weight and wooded valleys with a balance?
40:13 Who knew the Lord’s mind, and who became His counselor to instruct Him?
40:14 With whom did He take counsel, and who instructed Him? Who showed Him judgment, or who showed Him the way of understanding?
40:15 If all the nations were counted like a drop from a bucket and like a scale’s slight tipping, they will also be counted like spittle.
40:16 Lebanon is not sufficient for burning, nor all the four-footed animals sufficient for a whole burnt offering,
40:17 and all the nations are as nothing and were counted as nothing.
40:18 To whom did you liken the Lord, and to what likeness did you liken Him?
40:19 Did a craftsman make an image, or did a goldsmith melt gold and gild it, fashioning it as a likeness?
40:20 For a craftsman chooses wood that will not rot and skillfully seeks how to set up his image so that it will not shake.
40:21 Will you not know? Will you not hear? Was it not announced to you from the beginning? Did you not know the earth’s foundations?
40:22 He holds the earth’s circuit, and its inhabitants are like locusts. He set heaven like a vault and stretched it out like a tent to dwell in.
40:23 He gives rulers nothing to rule and made the earth as nothing.
40:24 For they will certainly not sow or plant, nor will their root take hold in the earth. He blew on them, and they dried; a storm will carry them away like brushwood.
40:25 “Now then, to whom did you liken Me, and shall I be exalted?” said the Holy One.
40:26 Lift your eyes on high and see: Who displayed all these? He brings out His array by number and will call all by name. From abundant glory and in strength’s might, nothing escaped you.
40:27 Do not say, Jacob—and why did you declare, Israel—“My way was hidden from God, and my God removed judgment and departed”?
40:28 And now, did you not know, if you have not heard? God is eternal, the God who fashioned the earth’s extremities. He will not hunger or grow weary, and His understanding cannot be discovered.
40:29 He gives strength to the hungry, and grief to those who are not in pain.
40:30 For younger people will hunger, young men will grow weary, and chosen men will be without strength.
40:31 But those enduring for God will exchange their strength and grow wings like eagles. They will run without growing weary and walk without hunger.

ISAIAH 41

41:1 “Be renewed toward Me, islands, for rulers will exchange their strength. Let them approach and speak together; then let them announce judgment.
41:2 Who roused righteousness from the east, called her to His feet, and she will go? He will set her before nations, astonish kings, and put their swords into the earth and their bows like brushwood driven away.
41:3 He will pursue them, and the way of his feet will pass through in peace.
41:4 Who acted and did these things? He called her, the One calling her from the generations’ beginning: I, God, am first, and for the things coming I am.
41:5 Nations saw and feared; the earth’s extremities drew near and came together,
41:6 each deciding to help his neighbor and brother, and he will say,
41:7 ‘A craftsman grew strong, and a smith striking with a hammer while also beating out the work.’ At one time he will say, ‘The joining is good.’ They strengthened them with nails; they will set them in place, and they will not move.
41:8 But you, Israel, My servant Jacob whom I chose, offspring of Abraham whom I loved,
41:9 whom I took hold of from the earth’s extremities, and from its lookout heights called you and said, ‘You are My servant; I chose you and did not abandon you’—
41:10 do not fear, for I am with you; do not wander, for I am your God who strengthened you. I helped you and secured you with My righteous right hand.
41:11 Behold, all opposing you will be ashamed and abashed, for all your adversaries will be as though they did not exist and will perish.
41:12 You will seek them but certainly not find the people who insult you. For those warring against you will be as though they did not exist, and will not exist.
41:13 For I am your God who holds your right hand and says to you, ‘Do not fear,
41:14 Jacob, little Israel. I helped you,’ says God who redeems you, Israel.
41:15 Behold, I made you like new, saw-toothed wagon wheels for threshing. You will thresh mountains, grind hills fine, and make them like dust.
41:16 You will winnow, and wind will take them and a storm scatter them. But you will rejoice among Israel’s holy ones. And they will exult—
41:17 the poor and needy. For they will seek water, and there will be none; their tongue dried from thirst. I am the Lord God; I will listen, the God of Israel, and will not abandon them,
41:18 but will open rivers on mountains and springs amid plains. I will make the wilderness marshes and the thirsty land watercourses,
41:19 and will place cedar, box tree, myrtle, cypress, and white poplar in the waterless land,
41:20 so that they may see and know, consider and understand together that the Lord’s hand did all these things and Israel’s Holy One displayed them.
41:21 Your judgment draws near, says the Lord God; your plans have drawn near, says Jacob’s King.
41:22 Let them approach and announce to you what will happen. Or say what the former things were, and we will apply our mind and know what the last things are; tell us also the coming things.
41:23 Announce to us the things coming at the end, and we will know that you are gods. Do good and cause harm, and we will marvel and see together.
41:24 For where are you from, and where is your work from? From the earth! They chose you as an abomination.
41:25 But I roused the one from the north and the one from the sun’s rising; they will be called by My name. Let rulers come, and like a potter’s clay, as a potter tramples clay, so you will be trampled.
41:26 For who will announce the things from the beginning so we may know, and the earlier things so we may say, ‘They are true’? There is no one predicting, nor anyone hearing your words.
41:27 I will give Zion a beginning and comfort Jerusalem for a journey.
41:28 For behold, there is no one from the nations, and none among their idols was an announcer. If I ask them, ‘Where are you from?’ they will certainly not answer Me.
41:29 For there are those making you, and those misleading you do so in vain.”

ISAIAH 42

42:1 “Jacob is My servant; I will uphold him. Israel is My chosen one; My soul accepted him. I put My spirit upon him; he will bring judgment to the nations.
42:2 He will not cry out or raise his voice, nor will his voice be heard outside.
42:3 He will not crush a bruised reed or extinguish a smoking flax wick; rather, he will bring judgment in truth.
42:4 He will shine forth and will not be broken until he sets judgment upon the earth, and nations will hope in his name.”
42:5 Thus says the Lord God, who made heaven and fixed it in place, established the earth and its contents, and gives breath to the people upon it and spirit to those treading it:
42:6 “I, the Lord God, called you in righteousness; I will hold your hand and strengthen you. I gave you as a covenant for a people, a light for nations,
42:7 to open blind people’s eyes, bring those bound out of bonds, and those sitting in darkness out of the prison house.
42:8 I am the Lord God; this is My name. I will not give My glory to another or My praiseworthy deeds to carved images.
42:9 Behold, the things from the beginning have arrived, and new things I will announce; before they spring up, they were disclosed to you.”
42:10 Sing a new hymn to the Lord—its beginning: “Glorify His name from the earth’s extremity, you who go down to the sea and sail it, islands and their inhabitants.”
42:11 Rejoice, wilderness and its villages, settlements and those inhabiting Kedar. Those inhabiting Petra will rejoice and cry out from the mountain tops.
42:12 They will give God glory and announce His praiseworthy deeds in the islands.
42:13 The Lord God of Powers will go out and crush war. He will rouse zeal and cry out mightily against His enemies.
42:14 “I was silent; surely I will not always be silent and hold back? I endured like a woman giving birth; I will astonish and dry up together.
42:15 I will turn rivers into islands and dry up marshes.
42:16 I will lead the blind on a road they did not know and make them tread paths they did not know. I will turn darkness into light for them and crooked places straight. These things I will do, and I will not abandon them.
42:17 But they turned backward. Be utterly ashamed, you who trust carved images, who say to cast images, ‘You are our gods.’”
42:18 Deaf people, listen; blind people, look up and see!
42:19 Who is blind except My servants, or deaf except those ruling them? God’s slaves have become blind.
42:20 You saw often but did not keep watch; the ears were opened, but you did not listen.
42:21 The Lord God wished to be justified and to magnify praise. And I saw,
42:22 and the people became plundered and despoiled. For the snare is in the storerooms everywhere and in houses together, where they hid them. They became plunder, and no one rescued the spoil; no one said, ‘Give it back!’
42:23 Who among you will give ear to these things and listen for what is coming?
42:24 Who gave Jacob over to plunder and Israel to those despoiling him? Was it not God, against whom they sinned, refusing to walk in His ways or listen to His law?
42:25 He brought His wrathful anger upon them; war overpowered them, as did those burning them all around. None of them understood, nor did they take it to soul.

43:4 Since you became honored before Me, you were glorified, and I loved you. I will give many people on your behalf and rulers for your head.

ISAIAH 44

44:1 “But now listen, My servant Jacob, and Israel whom I chose.
44:2 Thus says the Lord God who made you and formed you from the womb: You will yet be helped. Do not fear, My servant Jacob and beloved Israel whom I chose.
44:3 For I will give water in thirst to those traveling through waterless land. I will place My spirit upon your offspring and My blessings upon your children,
44:4 and they will spring up like grass amid water and like a willow beside flowing water.
44:5 One will say, ‘I belong to God’; another will cry out in Jacob’s name; another will inscribe, ‘I belong to God,’ in Israel’s name.”
44:6 Thus says God, Israel’s King, who rescued him, God Sabaoth: “I am first, and I am after these things; apart from Me there is no God.
44:7 Who is like Me? Let him stand, call out, and prepare for Me, from when I made humankind for eternity. Let them announce to you the coming things before they arrive.
44:8 Do not cover yourselves. Did you not hear from the beginning, and did I not announce it to you? You are witnesses whether there is a God apart from Me; there were none then.”
44:9 All who fashion and carve are futile, making what pleases them, things that will not help them. But they will be ashamed—
44:10 all who fashion a god and carve useless things,
44:11 and all from whom they came have dried up. Let the deaf from among humankind all assemble and stand together; let them be abashed and ashamed together.
44:12 For a craftsman sharpened iron, worked it with an adze, bored it with a drill, and worked it with his strong arm. He will hunger and grow weak and certainly not drink water. Having selected
44:13 wood, a craftsman set it by measure and arranged it with glue. He made it like a man’s form and human beauty, to set it in a house.
44:14 The wood he cut from the woodland was what the Lord planted and rain made grow,
44:15 so that it might serve people for burning. Taking some, he warmed himself; after burning it they baked loaves over the pieces. They worked the rest into gods and worship them.

as a root in thirsty ground.

by his bruise we were healed.

each person has strayed in his own way.

and give your toil for what does not satisfy?

55:3 Listen with your ears, and follow My ways;
listen to Me, and your soul will live amid good things.

the holy and sure blessings of David.
55:4 Behold, I have given him as a testimony among the nations,
a ruler and commander of nations.
55:5 Nations that did not know you will call upon you,
and peoples that do not know you will flee to you for refuge,
because of your God, the Holy One of Israel,
for He has glorified you.
55:6 Seek God, and when you find Him, call upon Him;
and when He draws near to you,
55:7 let the ungodly forsake his ways,
and the lawless man his counsels;
and let him return to the Lord, and he will receive mercy;
for He will abundantly forgive your sins.

nor are My ways like your ways, says the Lord.

so is My way distant from your ways,
and your thoughts from My mind.

and given seed to the sower and bread for food,

it will certainly not return until all that I have willed is accomplished;

57:13 When you cry aloud, let them rescue you in your distress! Wind will take all these, and a storm carry them away. But those holding fast to Me will acquire land and inherit My holy mountain.

ISAIAH 58

58:2 Day after day they seek Me and desire to know My ways. Like a people who practiced righteousness and did not abandon their God’s judgment, they now ask Me righteous judgment and desire to draw near to God,
58:3 saying, ‘Why did we fast, and Thou didst not see? We humbled our lives, and Thou didst not notice?’ For on your fasting days you pursue your wishes and prod all those under your control.
58:4 If you fast for lawsuits and fights and strike a humble person with fists, why do you fast for Me as today, to make your voice heard in an outcry?
58:5 I did not choose this fast, or a day for a person to humble his life. Even if you bend your neck like a ring and spread sackcloth and ashes beneath you, you will not thereby call it an acceptable fast.
58:6 ‘It is not such a fast that I chose,’ says the Lord. ‘Instead, loosen every bond of injustice, undo the strangling ties of coercive agreements, release the crushed, and tear up every unjust written contract.
58:7 Break your bread for the hungry and bring homeless poor people into your house. If you see someone naked, clothe him; do not overlook those belonging to your own family.
58:8 Then your light will break out early, and your healings quickly rise. Your righteousness will go ahead of you, and God’s glory will enfold you.
58:9 Then you will cry, and God will hear you. While you are still speaking He will say, “Behold, I am present.” If you remove from yourself a bond, finger-pointing, and a word of grumbling,
58:10 and give bread to the hungry from your heart and satisfy a humbled life, then your light will rise in darkness, and your darkness be like noon.
58:11 Your God will be with you continually, and you will be filled as your life desires. Your bones will grow rich; you will be like a drenched garden and a spring whose water never ran out. Your bones will sprout like grass and grow rich, and they will inherit generations upon generations.
58:12 Your ancient deserted places will be rebuilt, and your foundations will endure through generations upon generations. You will be called Builder of Fences, and you will put an end to the paths between.
58:13 If you turn your foot away from the Sabbaths, from doing your wishes on the holy day, and call the Sabbaths delightful, holy to your God, you will not lift your foot for work or speak a word in anger from your mouth,
58:14 and you will trust in the Lord. He will bring you up to earth’s good things and feed you your father Jacob’s inheritance, for the Lord’s mouth has spoken these things.’”

ISAIAH 59

59:1 Is the Lord’s hand unable to save? Or has His ear grown heavy so that He cannot hear?
59:2 Instead, your offenses stand between you and God, and because of your sins He has turned His face away from you so as not to show mercy.
59:3 For your hands are stained with blood and your fingers with sins. Your lips spoke lawlessness, and your tongue meditates on injustice.
59:4 No one speaks what is just, nor is there true judgment. They trust vain things and speak emptiness, for they conceive trouble and give birth to lawlessness.
59:5 They broke open asps’ eggs and weave a spider’s web. Whoever was about to eat their eggs broke one and found a wind-egg, and in it a basilisk.
59:6 Their web will not become clothing, nor will they clothe themselves from their works, for their works are works of lawlessness.
59:7 Their feet run to wickedness, quick to shed blood. Their thoughts are the thoughts of fools; destruction and misery lie in their ways.
59:8 They do not know the way of peace, and there is no justice in their ways. For the paths they travel are twisted, and they do not know peace.
59:9 Therefore judgment withdrew from them, and righteousness will not reach them. While they waited for light, darkness came to them; waiting for dawn, they walked in an untimely darkness.
59:10 They will grope along a wall like the blind, and as though without eyes they will grope. They will fall at noon as at midnight; like people dying, they will groan.
59:11 Like a bear and like a dove they will travel together. We waited for judgment, but there is none; salvation has withdrawn far from us.
59:12 For our lawlessness is great before Thee, and our sins have opposed us. Our lawless deeds are within us, and we have recognized our wrongs:
59:13 We acted impiously and lied and withdrew from following our God. We spoke unjust things and disobeyed; we conceived and meditated on unjust words from our heart.
59:14 We drove judgment backward, and righteousness has withdrawn far away, for truth was consumed in their ways, and they could not go straight through.
59:15 Truth has been taken away, and they turned their mind away from understanding. The Lord saw, and it did not please Him that there was no judgment.
59:16 He saw, and there was no man; He considered, and there was no one to help. He defended them with His arm and steadied Himself by mercy.
59:17 He put on righteousness like a breastplate, placed a helmet of salvation on His head, and wrapped Himself in a garment of vengeance and the cloak,
59:18 as one who will repay recompense—reproach to His adversaries.
59:19 Those from the west will fear the Lord’s name, and those from sunrise the glorious name. For anger from the Lord will come like a violent river; it will come with wrath.
59:20 The deliverer will come for Zion’s sake and turn ungodliness away from Jacob.
59:21 “And this is My covenant with them,” said the Lord: “My spirit that is upon you and the words I put into your mouth will never fail from your mouth or your offspring’s mouth,” for the Lord said, “from now on and for ever.”

ISAIAH 60

60:1 Shine, shine, Jerusalem, for your light has come, and the Lord’s glory has risen upon you.
60:2 Behold, darkness and gloom will cover earth over nations, but the Lord will appear upon you, and His glory will be seen over you.
60:3 Kings will walk by your light, and nations by your radiance.
60:4 Lift your eyes around you and see your children gathered. Behold, all your sons have come from far away, and your daughters will be lifted upon shoulders.
60:5 Then you will see and fear and be astonished in heart, for the wealth of sea, nations, and peoples will turn toward you. There will come to you
60:6 herds of camels, and camels from Madiam and Gaipha will cover you. They will all come from Saba, bringing gold and carrying frankincense, and they will proclaim the Lord’s salvation as good news.
60:7 All Kedar’s sheep will gather to you, and Nabaioth’s rams will come to you. They will be offered acceptably upon My altar, and My house of prayer will be glorified.
60:8 Who are these flying like clouds and like doves with their young?
60:9 Islands waited for Me, and Tharsis ships among the first, to bring your children from far away, with silver and gold, because of the Lord’s holy name and because Israel’s Holy One is glorious.
60:10 Foreigners will build your walls, and their kings will attend you. For because of My anger I struck you, and because of mercy I loved you.

60:18 Injustice will no longer be heard in your land, nor destruction and misery within your borders. Your walls will be called Salvation, and your gates Engraving.
60:19 The sun will not be your light by day, nor will the rising moon light your night. Instead, the Lord will be your everlasting light and God your glory.
60:20 For your sun will not set, and your moon will not fail. The Lord will be your everlasting light, and your days of mourning will be fulfilled.
60:21 Your people will all be righteous, and they will inherit the land for ever, guarding the planting, the works of His hands, for glory.
60:22 The fewest will become thousands, and the least a great nation. I, the Lord, will gather them at the proper time.

65:2 I spread out My hands all day to a disobedient, contradicting people who did not walk in a true way but followed their sins.
65:3 This people continually provokes Me to My face. They sacrifice in gardens and burn incense on bricks to demons that do not exist,
65:4 and sleep in tombs and caves for dreams. They eat pig’s flesh and sacrificial broth; all their vessels are defiled.
65:5 They say, ‘Far away from me! Do not approach me, for I am clean.’ This is smoke of My wrath; fire burns in it all the days.

65:10 There will be shelters for flocks in the woodland, and the ravine of Achor will be a resting place for cattle, for My people who sought Me.
