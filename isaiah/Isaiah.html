Isaiah 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Vision of Isaiah Son of Amos

1:1The vision that Isaiah son of Amos saw, which he saw against Judea and against Jerusalem during the reign of Oziah, Joatham, Achaz and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judea.

1:2Listen, heaven, and give ear, earth, because the Lord has spoken: “I begot sons and raised them high, but they rejected Me.

1:3An ox knew the one who acquired it, and a donkey its lord’s manger; but Israel did not know Me, and the people did not understand Me.”

1:4Woe, sinful nation, people full of sins, evil offspring, lawless sons! You abandoned the Lord and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger.

1:5Why should you be struck again as you add lawlessness? Every head is in pain, and every heart in grief.

1:6From feet to head: for neither a wound nor a bruise nor an inflamed blow is there a poultice to apply, nor oil nor bandages.

1:7Your land is deserted, your cities burned by fire. Your country—foreigners consume it before you; it has been deserted, overturned by foreign peoples.

1:8Daughter Zion will be left like a tent in a vineyard, like a fruit-watcher’s shelter in a cucumber field, like a besieged city.

1:9If the Lord Sabaoth had not left us seed, we would have become like Sodom and been made like Gomorrah.

1:10Hear the Lord’s word, rulers of Sodom! Attend to God’s law, people of Gomorrah!

What Is the Multitude of Your Sacrifices?

1:11“What is the multitude of your sacrifices to Me?” says the Lord. “I am full of burnt offerings of rams; fat of lambs and blood of bulls and male goats I do not desire,

1:12not even if you come to appear before Me. For who demanded these things from your hands? To trample My court,

1:13you will no longer continue. If you bring fine flour, it is futile. Incense is an abomination to Me. Your new moons and sabbaths and a great day I cannot endure; fasting and idleness,

1:14and your new moons and your festivals My soul hates. You have become more than enough for Me; I will no longer let your sins go.

1:15Whenever you stretch your hands toward Me, I will turn My eyes away from you. Even if you multiply supplication, I will not listen to you, for your hands are full of blood.

1:16Wash yourselves, become clean. Remove the evils from your souls, before My eyes. Stop your evil deeds.

Learn to Do Good, Seek Judgment

1:17Learn to do good, seek judgment, rescue the wronged. Give judgment for the orphan and vindicate the widow.

1:18Come, let us reason together, says the Lord. Though your sins are like purple-red, I will whiten them like snow; though they are like scarlet, I will whiten them like wool. [1]

1:19If you are willing and listen to Me, you will eat the land’s good things.

1:20But if you are unwilling and do not listen to Me, a sword will devour you, for the Lord’s mouth has spoken these things.”

How the Faithful City Has Become a Prostitute

1:21How the faithful city Zion has become a prostitute, full of judgment, in which righteousness lay at rest, but now murderers!

1:22Your silver is unapproved; your retailers mix the wine with water. [1]

1:23Your rulers disobey, partners of thieves, loving gifts and pursuing repayment, not judging for orphans and not attending to widows’ judgment.

1:24Therefore thus says the Master, the Lord Sabaoth: “Woe, mighty ones of Israel! For My anger will not cease among My opponents, and I will exact judgment from My enemies.

1:25I will bring My hand against you and burn you to purity. I will destroy the disobedient, remove all lawless people from you and humble all the arrogant.

1:26I will establish your judges as before and your counselors as at the beginning. After these things you will be called City of Righteousness, Faithful Metropolis Zion.

Her Captivity Saved Through Judgment

1:27For her captivity will be saved through judgment and through mercy.

1:28The lawless and sinners will be shattered together, and those abandoning the Lord will be brought to an end.

1:29For they will be ashamed of their idols, which they themselves wanted, and they were disgraced by their gardens, which they desired.

1:30For they will be like a terebinth that has shed its leaves, like a garden that has no water.

1:31Their strength will become a stalk of tow and their works sparks of fire. The lawless and sinners will be burned together, with no one to quench.”

Isaiah 2

The Mountain of the Lord in the Last Days

2:1The word that came from the Lord to Isaiah son of Amos concerning Judea and Jerusalem.

2:2In the last days the Lord’s mountain and God’s house will be clearly visible on the summits of the mountains and be raised above the hills; all the nations will come to it,

2:3and many nations will go and say, “Come, let us go up to the Lord’s mountain and to the house of Jacob’s God. He will announce His way to us, and we will walk in it.” For law will go out from Zion, and the Lord’s word from Jerusalem.

2:4He will judge between the nations and reprove a numerous people. They will beat their swords into plows and their spears into sickles. Nation will no longer take sword against nation, and they will certainly no longer learn to wage war. [1]

2:5And now, house of Jacob, come, let us walk by the Lord’s light!

2:6For He abandoned His people, Israel’s house, because their country was filled, as from the beginning, with omens like the foreigners’ country, and many foreign children were born to them.

Their Country Was Filled with Silver and Gold

2:7For their country was filled with silver and gold, and their treasures had no number; the land was filled with horses, and their chariots had no number.

2:8The land was filled with abominations, the works of their hands, and they bowed before what their fingers made.

2:9A human being bowed down, and a man was humbled; I will certainly not let them go.

2:10And now enter the rocks and hide in the earth from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.

The Lord's Eyes Are High

2:11For the Lord’s eyes are high, but the human being is low; humanity’s height will be humbled, and the Lord alone will be exalted on that day.

2:12For the Lord Sabaoth has a day against every insolent and arrogant person, against everyone high and elevated; they will be humbled,

2:13against every cedar of Lebanon among the high and elevated, and against every oak tree of Bashan,

2:14against every mountain and every high hill,

2:15against every high tower and every high wall,

2:16against every ship of the sea and every beautiful display of ships.

2:17Every human being will be humbled, humanity’s height will fall, and the Lord alone will be exalted on that day.

2:18They will hide away all the handmade things,

Into the Caves and the Clefts of the Rocks

2:19carrying them into caves, into clefts in the rocks and into holes in the earth, from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.

2:20For on that day a person will cast out his silver and gold abominations, which they made to worship, to the worthless ones and the bats,

2:21to enter holes in the solid rock and clefts in the rocks from the Lord’s terror and the glory of His strength, whenever He rises to shatter the earth.

Isaiah 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Master Removes Support from Jerusalem

3:1Look now, the Master, the Lord Sabaoth, will remove from Judea and Jerusalem strong man and strong woman, the strength of bread and the strength of water,

3:2giant and strong man and warrior and judge and prophet and conjecturer and elder,

3:3commander of fifty and admirable counselor and wise master builder and discerning listener.

3:4I will establish young men as their rulers, and mockers will lord it over them.

3:5The people will clash, person against person and each against his neighbor. The child will collide with the elder, the dishonored with the honored.

3:6For a man will seize his brother or a member of his father’s household, saying, “You have a cloak; become our leader, and let my food be under you!”

3:7Answering on that day, he will say, “I will not be your leader, for there is neither bread nor cloak in my house. I will not be leader of this people.”

3:8For Jerusalem has been abandoned and Judea has collapsed; their tongues accompany lawlessness, disobeying what concerns the Lord, because now their glory has been humbled,

The Shame of Their Face Stood Against Them

3:9and the shame of their face stood against them. They announced their sin like Sodom’s and made it manifest. Woe to their soul, for they have planned an evil plan against themselves,

3:10saying, “Let us bind the righteous person, because he is troublesome to us.” Therefore they will eat the produce of their works.

3:11Woe to the lawless! Evils according to the works of his hands will happen to him.

3:12My people, your collectors glean you, and those demanding payment lord it over you. My people, those pronouncing you blessed lead you astray and disturb the path of your feet.

The Lord Establishes Himself for Judgment

3:13But now the Lord will establish Himself for judgment and will set His people before judgment.

3:14The Lord Himself will come to judgment with the people’s elders and its rulers: “Why have you burned My vineyard, while the poor person’s plunder is in your houses?

3:15Why do you wrong My people and shame the face of the poor?”

3:16Thus says the Lord: “Because Zion’s daughters were exalted and walked with high neck and glancing eyes, dragging their tunics as their feet moved and playing with their feet,

Zion's Ruling Daughters Humbled

3:17God will humble Zion’s ruling daughters, and the Lord will uncover their appearance

3:18on that day, and the Lord will remove the glory of their clothing and their ornaments, hair braids, fringes and crescents,

3:19the necklace and the adornment of their face,

3:20the arrangement of glorious adornment, armlets, bracelets, a hair braid, armbands, rings and earrings,

3:21the garments bordered with purple and those purple at the center,

3:22the coverings used about the house and the transparent Laconian garments,

3:23the fine-linen, blue-purple and scarlet garments, and fine linen interwoven with gold and blue-purple, and trailing summer wraps.

3:24Instead of sweet fragrance there will be dust; instead of a belt you will gird yourself with a cord; instead of golden head adornment you will have baldness because of your deeds; instead of the tunic with purple at its center you will gird yourself with sackcloth.

3:25Your most beautiful son, whom you love, will fall by the sword, and your strong men will fall by the sword.

3:26The cases of your adornment will be humbled and mourn; you will be left alone and dashed to the ground.”

Isaiah 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Song of the Vineyard

5:1I will sing now to the beloved a song of the loved one for my vineyard. The beloved had a vineyard on a horn, in a fertile place.

5:2I put a fence around it, surrounded it with a palisade, planted a Sorech vine, built a tower in its midst and dug a wine vat in it. I waited for it to produce grapes, but it produced thorns.

5:3Now, man of Judah and residents of Jerusalem, judge My case and the matter of My vineyard.

5:4What more shall I do for My vineyard that I did not do for it? For I waited for it to produce grapes, but it produced thorns.

5:5Now I will announce to you what I will do to My vineyard: I will remove its fence, and it will be plundered; I will pull down its wall, and it will be trampled,

5:6and I will abandon My vineyard. It will certainly not be pruned or dug, and thorns will rise in it as in fallow ground. I will command the clouds not to rain upon it.

The Vineyard of the Lord Sabaoth Is Israel

5:7For the vineyard of the Lord Sabaoth is Israel’s house, and the man of Judah a beloved new planting. I waited for it to produce judgment, but it produced lawlessness, not righteousness but an outcry.

5:8Woe to those joining house to house and bringing field beside field, so that they may take something from their neighbor! Surely you will not dwell alone upon the earth?

5:9For these things were heard in the ears of the Lord Sabaoth: “If many houses come to be, great and beautiful, they will become deserted, and no one will inhabit them.

5:10For where ten pairs of oxen work, it will yield one jar, and the one sowing six artabas will yield three measures.”

Woe to Those Rising Early for Strong Drink

5:11Woe to those rising in the morning and pursuing strong drink, lingering into the evening, for wine will burn them up!

5:12For with lyre and harp, drums and flutes they drink wine, but they do not look at the Lord’s works or consider the works of His hands.

5:13Therefore My people became captive because they did not know the Lord, and there was a multitude of dead through famine and thirst for water.

5:14Hades enlarged its appetite and opened its mouth without ceasing, and her glorious ones, great ones, rich ones and pestilent ones will go down.

5:15A human being will be humbled, a man dishonored, and lofty eyes humbled.

5:16The Lord Sabaoth will be exalted through judgment, and the holy God will be glorified through righteousness.

5:17Those who were plundered will be pastured like bulls, and lambs will feed upon the deserted places of those wiped out.

5:18Woe to those drawing sins as with a long cord and lawless deeds as with the strap of a heifer’s yoke,

5:19who say, “Let what He will do approach quickly, so that we may see, and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come, so that we may know!”

5:20Woe to those calling evil good and good evil, making darkness light and light darkness, making bitter sweet and sweet bitter!

Woe to Those Discerning in Themselves

5:21Woe to those who are discerning in their own judgment and knowledgeable in their own view!

5:22Woe to your strong ones who drink wine and the powerful who mix strong drink,

5:23who justify the ungodly for gifts and take away the righteous person’s right!

5:24Therefore, as a stalk is burned by a fiery coal and consumed by a rising flame, their root will become like chaff and their blossom rise like dust. For they did not desire the law of the Lord Sabaoth, but provoked the utterance of the Holy One of Israel.

The Lord Was Enraged with Wrath

5:25The Lord Sabaoth was enraged with wrath against His people, brought His hand against them and struck them. The mountains were roused, and their carcasses became like dung in the middle of the road. In all these things the anger did not turn away, but the hand is still high.

5:26Therefore He will raise a signal among the distant nations and whistle to them from the earth’s end. Behold, they come quickly and lightly!

5:27They will not hunger or grow weary, slumber or sleep, nor loosen the belts from their waists; their sandal straps will certainly not break.

5:28Their arrows are sharp and their bows drawn. Their horses’ feet were reckoned like solid rock, and their chariot wheels like a storm.

5:29They rush like lions and have taken their stand like a lion’s cub. It will seize and cry out as a beast does and cast out, and no one will rescue them.

5:30It will cry out because of them on that day like the sound of a surging sea. They will look to the earth, and behold, harsh darkness in their perplexity.

Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Sabaoth;
all the earth is full of His glory. [1] [2]

Wretched Am I, for I Am Stricken
