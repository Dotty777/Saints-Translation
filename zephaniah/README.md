## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Ordinary third-person says and has replace obsolete forms; the perfect preparation and consecration remain. The repeated disappear, names, army of heaven, openly, the singular lamp, repeated near and day clauses, appointed mighty, third-person He pouring blood and flesh, and completion and haste are retained. No explanatory worship-versus-social-violence reading is inserted at 1:9.

Pending: Review appointed mighty, safeguards, the battered place and the interpretation of the Lord’s house at 1:9. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary address uses you and verbs use comes, says and has. Ashkelon will disappear makes the shared future explicit; waterless wasteland and a wasteland express the ruined place naturally at 2:13 and 2:15. The passing flower, seek repetitions, answer these things, possibility of shelter, Cretans/Crete, has visited, away from the sons of Judah, Damascus, distinct Lord of powers/Almighty, islands, Ethiopians, chameleons, hedgehogs and cedar are retained.

Pending: Review passing flower, the lodging clause, Damascus, animal identifications and ruin imagery. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Ordinary address to the city uses you/your; third-person reporting uses says and has. Removed remains past while has redeemed makes the perfect explicit at 3:15. Conspicuous and redeemed dove-city, Arabian wolves, spirit bearers, the unusual exaction/strife clauses, number changes in 3:7, tongue for its generation and one yoke, repeated daughter Jerusalem, renew in His love and the feast in 3:17, the crushed and Alas, and the they/you distinction at 3:20 remain. The remnant clause at 3:13 still continues 3:12.

Pending: Review redeemed dove-city, spirit bearers, the exaction/strife clauses, number and pronoun shifts, tongue for its generation, renew and the crushed. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
