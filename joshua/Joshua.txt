JOSHUA 1

1:1 After the death of Moses, the Lord spoke to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses’ attendant, saying:
1:2 “Moses My servant has died. Now therefore arise and cross the Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving them.
1:3 Every place where you tread with the sole of your feet I will give you, as I have spoken to Moses.
1:4 The wilderness and Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river, the River Euphrates, and as far as the furthest sea toward the setting sun shall be your borders.
1:5 No man shall withstand you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you; I will not forsake you or disregard you.
1:6 Be strong and courageous, for you shall apportion to this people the land which I swore to your fathers to give them.
1:7 Be strong and courageous, then, to keep and do what Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from these commands to the right or to the left, so that you may have understanding in all that you do.
1:8 This book of the law shall not depart from your mouth. Meditate on it day and night, so that you may understand how to do all that is written. Then you shall prosper and make your ways prosper, and then you shall understand.
1:9 Behold, I have commanded you: be strong and courageous. Do not be fainthearted or afraid, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go.”
1:10 Joshua commanded the scribes of the people, saying:
1:11 “Go into the midst of the people's camp and command the people, saying, ‘Prepare provisions; for in three days you are crossing this Jordan, to enter and possess the land that the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving you.’”
1:12 Joshua said to Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh:
1:13 “Remember the word of the Lord that Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded you: ‘The Lord your God has given you rest and has given you this land.’
1:14 Let your wives and your children and your livestock dwell in the land he gave you; but you, every man of strength, shall cross equipped before your brothers and fight alongside them,
1:15 until the Lord your God gives your brothers rest as He has given you, and they also inherit the land that the Lord our God is giving them. Then each of you shall return to his inheritance, which Moses gave you beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise.”
1:16 They answered Joshua, “We will do all that you command us, and we will go wherever you send us.
1:17 As we obeyed Moses in all things, so will we obey you. Only let the Lord our God be with you, as He was with Moses.
1:18 Whoever disobeys you or does not heed your words in all that you command him, let him die. Only be strong and courageous!”

JOSHUA 2

2:1 Joshua son of Nun sent two young men from Shittim to spy, saying, “Go up and look at the land and Jericho.” The two young men went into Jericho, entered the house of a harlot named Rahab, and lodged there.
2:2 The king of Jericho was informed, “Men from the sons of Israel have entered here to spy out the land.”
2:3 The king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, “Bring out the men who entered your house by night, for they have come to spy out the land.”
2:4 The woman took the men and hid them, and said to the messengers, “The men came to me.
2:5 As the gate was closing in the darkness, the men went out. I do not know where they have gone. Pursue them, to see whether you can overtake them.”
2:6 But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them among the flax stalks she had piled on the roof.
2:7 The men pursued them along the road to the Jordan, toward the crossings, and the gate was shut. After the pursuers had gone out after them,
2:8 and before the young men went to sleep, she went up to them on the roof
2:9 and said, “I know that the Lord has given you the land, for fear of you has fallen upon us.
2:10 For we have heard that the Lord God dried up the Red Sea before you when you came out of the land of Egypt, and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you destroyed.
2:11 When we heard, our hearts were astounded, and no spirit remained in any of us because of you. For the Lord your God is God in heaven above and on the earth below.
2:12 Now swear to me by the Lord God that, as I show you mercy, you also will show mercy to my father's house,
2:13 and keep alive my father’s household, my mother, my brothers, my whole household, and all who belong to them, and rescue my life from death.”
2:14 The men said to her, “Our life in place of yours, even to death.” And she said, “When the Lord delivers the city to you, you shall show me mercy and faithfulness.”
2:15 She lowered them through the window
2:16 and said to them, “Go into the hill country, so the pursuers do not encounter you. Hide there three days until those pursuing you return; afterward go on your way.”
2:17 The men said to her, “We are free from this oath of yours.
2:18 Behold, we are coming into part of the city. You shall set up the sign: bind this scarlet cord in the window through which you lowered us, and gather your father and your mother and your brothers and all your father’s house to you in your house.
2:19 Whoever goes out beyond the door of your house shall bear his own guilt; we shall be free from this oath of yours. For all who are with you in your house, we shall be answerable.
2:20 But if anyone wrongs us or makes known these words of ours, we shall be free from this oath of yours.”
2:21 She said to them, “Let it be according to your word.” Then she sent them away.
2:22 They went into the hill country and remained there three days. The pursuers searched all the roads but did not find them.
2:23 The two young men returned, came down from the mountain, crossed over to Joshua son of Nun, and related everything that had happened to them.
2:24 They said to Joshua, “The Lord has given all the land into our hand, and everyone inhabiting that land is cowering before us.”

JOSHUA 3

3:1 Joshua rose early in the morning. They set out from Shittim, came to the Jordan, and lodged there before crossing.
3:2 After three days, the scribes passed through the camp
3:3 and commanded the people, “When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord our God, and our priests and the Levites carrying it, leave your places and follow it.
3:4 Yet let there be a distance of about two thousand cubits between you and it. Keep your place, and do not draw near to it, so that you may know the way you are to go; for you have not passed this way before.”
3:5 Joshua said to the people, “Purify yourselves for tomorrow, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you.”
3:6 Joshua said to the priests, “Lift up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and go ahead of the people.” The priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and went before the people.
3:7 The Lord said to Joshua, “Today I begin to exalt you before all the sons of Israel, so that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.
3:8 Now command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant, saying, ‘When you enter the edge of the waters of the Jordan, stand in the Jordan.’”
3:9 Joshua said to the sons of Israel, “Come here and hear the word of the Lord our God.”
3:10 “By this you shall know that the living God is among you, and will surely destroy before us the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, Amorites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.
3:11 Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing the Jordan.
3:12 Choose twelve men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe.
3:13 When the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth rest in the Jordan’s water, the Jordan’s water shall fail, and the water coming down shall stand.”
3:14 The people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, and the priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord ahead of the people.
3:15 As the priests carrying the ark of the covenant entered the Jordan and the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord dipped into the edge of the Jordan’s water—the Jordan was overflowing its whole bank, as in the days of wheat harvest—
3:16 the waters coming down from above stood. One mass stood very, very far away, as far as the region of Kiriath-jearim. The water flowing down descended into the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, until it failed completely. The people stood opposite Jericho.
3:17 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All the sons of Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole people had finished crossing the Jordan.

JOSHUA 4

4:1 When the whole people had finished crossing the Jordan, the Lord said to Joshua:
4:2 “Take men from the people, one from each tribe,
4:3 and command them, ‘Take twelve ready stones from the midst of the Jordan, carry them over with you, and set them in your camp where you will camp tonight.’”
4:4 Joshua summoned twelve distinguished men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe,
4:5 and said to them, “Come forward before me, before the Lord, into the midst of the Jordan. Each of you shall take a stone from there and lift it upon his shoulders, according to the number of the twelve tribes of Israel,
4:6 so that these may remain a sign to you for ever. When your son asks you in time to come, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’
4:7 you shall declare to your son, ‘The River Jordan failed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth as it crossed.’ And these stones shall be a memorial to you, the sons of Israel, for ever.”
4:8 The sons of Israel did as the Lord commanded Joshua. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, just as the Lord directed Joshua when the sons of Israel finished crossing. They carried them with them into the camp and set them there.
4:9 Joshua also set up another twelve stones in the Jordan itself, in the place beneath the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord. They are there to this day.
4:10 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant stood in the Jordan until Joshua completed everything the Lord had commanded him to announce to the people. The people hurried and crossed.
4:11 When all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the covenant of the Lord crossed, and the stones were before them.
4:12 The sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed equipped before the sons of Israel, just as Moses had commanded them.
4:13 Forty thousand equipped for battle crossed before the Lord for war, toward the city of Jericho.
4:14 On that day the Lord exalted Joshua before the whole race of Israel. They feared him as they had Moses, as long as he lived.
4:15 The Lord said to Joshua:
4:16 “Command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the testimony of the Lord to come out of the Jordan.”
4:17 Joshua commanded the priests, “Come out of the Jordan.”
4:18 When the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord came out of the Jordan and set their feet on the land, the Jordan’s water rushed into its place and flowed along its whole bank as before.
4:19 The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month. The sons of Israel camped at Gilgal, in the region east of Jericho.
4:20 Joshua set up at Gilgal these twelve stones that he had taken from the Jordan,
4:21 saying, “When your sons ask you, ‘What are these stones?’
4:22 tell your sons, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground,
4:23 when the Lord our God dried up the Jordan’s water before them until they crossed, just as the Lord our God did to the Red Sea, which the Lord our God dried up before us until we passed through,’
4:24 that all the nations of the earth may know that the power of the Lord is mighty, and that you may revere the Lord your God at all times.”

JOSHUA 5

5:1 When the kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan and the kings of Phoenicia by the sea heard that the Lord God had dried up the River Jordan before the sons of Israel as they crossed, their minds melted; they were dismayed, and no understanding remained in them because of the sons of Israel.
5:2 At that time the Lord said to Joshua, “Make for yourself stone knives from sharp rock, and sit down and circumcise the sons of Israel.”
5:3 Joshua made sharp stone knives and circumcised the sons of Israel at the place called Hill of the Foreskins.
5:4 This is how Joshua purified the sons of Israel: those born along the way and those among the people who had left Egypt who were still uncircumcised—
5:5 Joshua circumcised all these.
5:6 For Israel had lived forty-two years in the wilderness of Madbaritis. Therefore most of the fighting men who had left the land of Egypt were uncircumcised, those who disobeyed God’s commands. He decreed that they should not see the land the Lord swore to their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.
5:7 In their place He appointed their sons, whom Joshua circumcised because they had remained uncircumcised along the way.
5:8 After being circumcised, they rested there in the camp until they recovered.
5:9 The Lord said to Joshua son of Nun, “Today I have removed the reproach of Egypt from you.” He called that place Gilgal.
5:10 The sons of Israel kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, from evening, west of Jericho, beyond the Jordan, in the plain.
5:11 They ate from the grain of the land: unleavened bread and new grain. On this day
5:12 the manna failed after they had eaten from the grain of the land. There was no longer manna for the sons of Israel; that year they harvested the country of the Phoenicians.
5:13 When Joshua was at Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and saw a man standing opposite him, with a drawn sword in his hand. Joshua drew near and said, “Are you on our side, or on the side of our adversaries?”
5:14 He said to him, “I have now come as chief captain of the host of the Lord.” Joshua fell on his face to the earth and said to him, “Master, what do you command your servant?”
5:15 The chief captain of the Lord said to Joshua, “Loosen your sandal from your feet, for the place where you stand is holy.”

JOSHUA 6

6:1 Jericho was shut up and fortified; no one went out of it or entered it.
6:2 And the Lord said to Joshua, “Behold, I am delivering Jericho, and its king within it, mighty in strength, into your power.
6:3 Station the fighting men all around it,
6:5 and when you sound the trumpet, let all the people cry out together. As they cry out, the walls of the city shall fall of themselves, and all the people shall rush into the city, each straight ahead of him.”
6:6 Joshua son of Nun went in to the priests
6:7 and said to them, “Instruct the people to go around and encircle the city. Let the fighting men pass armed before the Lord.
6:8 Let seven priests holding seven sacred trumpets likewise pass before the Lord and sound them vigorously, and let the ark of the covenant of the Lord follow.
6:9 Let the fighting men pass in front, and let the priests forming the rear guard go behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord, marching and blowing trumpets.”
6:10 Joshua commanded the people, “Do not cry out or let anyone hear your voice until the day he himself commands you to cry out. Then you shall cry out.”
6:11 After the ark of the covenant of God had gone around the city, it went straight back to the camp and rested there.
6:12 On the second day Joshua rose in the morning, and the priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord.
6:13 The seven priests carrying the seven trumpets went before the Lord. Afterward the fighting men and the rest of the crowd proceeded behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord. The priests sounded the trumpets, and all the rest of the crowd encircled the city close by
6:14 and returned to the camp. This was done for six days.
6:15 On the seventh day they rose at dawn and went around the city six times.
6:16 On the seventh circuit the priests sounded the trumpets, and Joshua said to the sons of Israel, “Cry out, for the Lord has handed the city over to you!
6:17 The city and everything in it shall be under the ban to the Lord Sabaoth. But preserve Rahab the harlot and everything in her house.
6:18 But guard yourselves carefully from what is under the ban, lest you covet it and take any of it, and bring the camp of the sons of Israel under the ban, and destroy us.
6:19 All silver, gold, bronze, and iron shall be holy to the Lord and shall be brought into the Lord’s treasury.”
6:20 The priests sounded the trumpets. When the people heard their sound, all the people together raised a great, mighty shout. The whole wall fell all around, and all the people went up into the city.
6:21 Joshua put the city and everything in it under the ban by the edge of the sword: man and woman, young and old, calf and beast of burden.
6:22 Joshua said to the two young men who had spied, “Enter the woman’s house and bring her out from there, with everything that belongs to her.”
6:23 The two young men who had spied out the city entered the woman’s house and brought out Rahab the harlot, her father, her mother, her brothers, everything belonging to her, and all her kin. They placed her outside Israel’s camp.
6:24 The city was burned with everything in it, except that the silver, gold, bronze, and iron were given to be brought into the Lord’s treasury.
6:25 Joshua kept alive Rahab the harlot and her entire paternal household. She has lived in Israel to this day, because she hid the spies Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.
6:26 On that day Joshua pronounced an oath before the Lord: “Cursed is the man who rebuilds that city! At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates.” Ozan of Bethel did so: at the cost of Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest surviving son he set up its gates.
6:27 The Lord was with Joshua, and his name was known throughout the land.

JOSHUA 7

7:1 The sons of Israel committed a great offence and misappropriated what was under the ban. Achar son of Carmi, son of Zambri, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of it, and the Lord became angry with the sons of Israel.
7:2 Joshua sent men to Ai, which is near Bethel, saying, “Spy out Ai.” The men went up and spied out Ai.
7:3 They returned to Joshua and said, “Do not let all the people go up. Let about two or three thousand men go up and capture the city by siege. Do not bring all the people there, for they are few.”
7:4 About three thousand men went up, but they fled before the men of Ai.
7:5 The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them, pursued them from the gate, and crushed them on the descent. The people’s heart was terrified and became like water.
7:6 Joshua tore his clothes and fell face down on the earth before the Lord until evening, he and the elders of Israel. They put dust on their heads.
7:7 And Joshua said, “I beseech Thee, Lord, why did Thy servant bring this people across the Jordan, to deliver them to the Amorites and destroy us? Would that we had remained and dwelt beside the Jordan!
7:8 What can I say, since Israel has turned its back before its enemy?
7:9 When the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land hear, they shall surround us and blot us out from the earth. And what wilt Thou do for Thy great name?”
7:10 The Lord said to Joshua, “Arise! Why have you fallen on your face like this?
7:11 The people have sinned and transgressed the covenant I made with them. They stole from what was under the ban and put it among their belongings.
7:12 The sons of Israel shall not be able to stand before their enemies. They shall turn their backs before their enemies, for they have become subject to the ban. I will no longer be with you, unless you remove that which is under the ban from among yourselves.
7:13 Arise, consecrate the people, and tell them to consecrate themselves for tomorrow. Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: ‘What is under the ban is among you. You cannot withstand your enemies until you remove it from among you.’
7:14 In the morning you shall all assemble by tribes. Bring forward by clans the tribe which the Lord indicates; bring forward by households the clan which the Lord indicates; and bring forward man by man the household which the Lord indicates.
7:15 Whoever is indicated shall be burned with fire, together with everything belonging to him, because he transgressed the Lord’s covenant and committed lawlessness in Israel.”
7:16 Joshua rose early and brought the people forward by tribes. The tribe of Judah was indicated.
7:17 They were brought forward by clans, and the Zerahite clan was indicated. They were brought forward man by man,
7:18 and Achar son of Zambri, son of Zerah, was indicated.
7:19 Joshua said to Achar, “Give glory today to the Lord, the God of Israel, and make confession. Tell me what you have done, and do not hide it from me.”
7:20 Achar answered Joshua, “Truly I have sinned before the Lord, the God of Israel. This is what I did:
7:21 I saw among the spoil a beautiful patterned carpet, two hundred didrachms of silver, and one tongue-shaped bar of gold weighing fifty didrachms. I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground in my tent, and the silver is hidden underneath them.”
7:22 Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent in the camp. The things were hidden in the tent, with the silver underneath them.
7:23 They brought the things out of the tent to Joshua and the elders of Israel and placed them before the Lord.
7:24 Joshua took Achar son of Zerah and brought him up to the ravine of Achor, with his sons, his daughters, his cattle, his beasts of burden, all his sheep, his tent, and everything belonging to him. All the people were with him, and he brought them up to Emek-achor.
7:25 Joshua said to Achar, “Why have you brought destruction upon us? May the Lord destroy you, as on this very day!” And all Israel stoned him with stones.
7:26 They set a great heap of stones over him. The Lord ceased from the heat of His anger. Therefore the place was named Emek-achor, as it is to this day.

JOSHUA 8

8:1 The Lord said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid or lose heart. Take all the men of war with you, arise, and go up to Ai. Behold, I have delivered the king of Ai and his land into your hands.
8:2 Do to Ai as you did to Jericho and its king, and take the livestock as spoil for yourself. Set an ambush behind the city.”
8:3 Joshua and all the fighting people arose to go up to Ai. Joshua selected thirty thousand mighty men and sent them away by night.
8:4 He commanded them, “Lie in ambush behind the city. Do not go far from the city, and all of you be ready.
8:5 I and everyone with me will approach the city. When Ai’s inhabitants come out to meet us as before, we shall flee before them.
8:6 When they come out after us, we shall draw them away from the city. They will say, ‘These men are fleeing before us as before.’
8:7 Then rise from the ambush and go into the city.
8:8 Do according to this word. Behold, I have commanded you.”
8:9 Joshua sent them away, and they went to the ambush and took their position between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai.
8:10 Joshua rose early in the morning and inspected the people. He and the elders went up before the people toward Ai.
8:11 All the fighting people with him went up and came opposite the city from the east,
8:12 while the ambush lay west of the city.
8:14 When the king of Ai saw, he hurried straight out to meet them in battle, he and all the people with him. He did not know there was an ambush behind the city.
8:15 Joshua saw and withdrew, and Israel withdrew before them.
8:16 They pursued the sons of Israel and moved away from the city.
8:17 No one remained in Ai who did not pursue Israel. They left the city open and pursued Israel.
8:18 The Lord said to Joshua, “Stretch out your hand with the javelin you hold toward the city, for I have delivered it into your hands. The ambush shall rise quickly from its place.” Joshua stretched out his hand, with the javelin, toward the city,
8:19 and the ambush rose quickly from its place. They came out when he stretched out his hand, went to the city, captured it, and hurried to burn it with fire.
8:20 Ai’s inhabitants looked behind them and saw smoke rising from the city toward heaven. They no longer had anywhere to flee, this way or that.
8:21 Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city and that its smoke rose toward heaven. They turned and struck Ai’s men.
8:22 The others came out of the city to meet them, and they were caught between the forces, some on this side and some on that. They struck them until no survivor or fugitive remained.
8:23 They captured the king of Ai alive and brought him to Joshua.
8:24 When the sons of Israel finished killing all Ai’s people in the plains and on the mountain, on the descent where they pursued them from it to the end, Joshua returned to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.
8:25 Those who fell that day, men and women, were twelve thousand, all Ai’s inhabitants,
8:27 except the livestock and the spoil in the city, all of which the sons of Israel took as plunder according to the Lord’s command, as the Lord had directed Joshua.
8:28 Joshua burned the city with fire and made it an uninhabited mound for ever, as it remains to this day.
8:29 He hanged the king of Ai on a forked tree, and he remained on the tree until evening. At sunset Joshua gave the command, and they took his body down from the tree. They threw him into the pit and set a heap of stones over him, which remains to this day.

JOSHUA 9

9:1 When the kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, in the hill country, the plain, along the whole coast of the Great Sea and near Anti-Lebanon, and the Hittites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, Amorites, Girgashites, and Jebusites heard,
9:2 they all assembled together to make war on Joshua and Israel.
9:2a Then Joshua built an altar to the Lord, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal,
9:2b as Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded the sons of Israel, as written in Moses’ law: an altar of whole stones on which no iron had been applied. He offered whole burnt offerings to the Lord there and a sacrifice of well-being.
9:2c Joshua wrote on the stones the second law, Moses’ law, which he wrote before the sons of Israel.
9:2d All Israel, their elders, judges, and scribes passed on either side opposite the ark. The priests and Levites carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord. Resident alien and native alike were there, half near Mount Gerizim and half near Mount Ebal, as Moses, the servant of the Lord, had first commanded the blessing of the people.
9:2e After this Joshua read all the words of this law, the blessings and the curses, according to everything written in Moses’ law.
9:2f There was not a word of everything Moses commanded Joshua that Joshua did not read in the hearing of the whole assembly of the sons of Israel: the men, women, children, and resident aliens travelling with Israel.
9:3 The inhabitants of Gibeon heard everything the Lord had done to Jericho and Ai.
9:4 They too acted craftily. They came, provisioned and prepared themselves, and took old sacks on their donkeys and old wineskins, torn and tied up,
9:5 with the soles of their footwear and their sandals old and patched on their feet, and worn clothing on them. Their bread for the journey was dry, mouldy, and eaten away.
9:6 They came to Joshua at Israel’s camp in Gilgal and said to Joshua and Israel, “We have come from a distant land. Now make a covenant with us.”
9:7 The sons of Israel said to the Horite, “Perhaps you live in my midst. How then could I make a covenant with you?”
9:8 They said to Joshua, “We are your servants.” Joshua said to them, “Where are you from, and where have you come from?”
9:9 They said, “Your servants have come from a very distant land, in the name of the Lord your God; for we have heard His name and all that He did in Egypt,
9:10 and everything He did to the kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei.
9:11 When our elders and all the inhabitants of our land heard, they told us, ‘Take provisions for the journey, go to meet them, and say to them, “We are your servants; now make a covenant with us.”’
9:12 These loaves were hot when we took them as provisions on the day we set out to come to you. Now they are dry and eaten away.
9:13 These wineskins were new when we filled them, and now they have torn. Our clothes and sandals have worn out because of the exceedingly long journey.”
9:14 The leaders took some of their provisions, but did not inquire of the Lord.
9:15 Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them to preserve them. The leaders of the congregation swore to them.
9:16 Three days after making the covenant with them, they heard that the people were nearby and lived among them.
9:17 The sons of Israel set out and came to their cities. Their cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and the city of Jearim.
9:18 The sons of Israel did not fight them, because all the leaders had sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel. The whole congregation grumbled against the leaders.
9:19 The leaders said to the whole congregation, “We have sworn to them by the Lord, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them.
9:20 This is what we shall do: keep them alive and preserve them, so that wrath does not come against us because of the oath we swore to them.
9:21 They shall live and become woodcutters and water-carriers for the whole congregation,” as the leaders had told them.
9:22 And Joshua called them and said, “Why did you deceive me, saying, ‘We are very far from you,’ when you are inhabitants of the land, dwelling among us?
9:23 Now you are cursed: a slave and a woodcutter shall never be lacking among you, for me and for my God.”
9:24 They answered Joshua, “We were told all that the Lord your God commanded His servant Moses: to give you this land, and to destroy us and all its inhabitants before you. We feared greatly for our lives because of you, and did this.
9:25 And now, behold, we are in your power. Do to us as pleases you and seems best to you.”
9:26 They did so for them, and Joshua rescued them that day from the hands of the sons of Israel, and they did not kill them.
9:27 That day Joshua appointed them woodcutters and water-carriers for the whole congregation and God’s altar. Therefore Gibeon’s inhabitants became woodcutters and water-carriers for God’s altar to this day, and for the place the Lord would choose.

JOSHUA 10

10:1 Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had taken Ai and destroyed it. As they had done to Jericho and its king, so they had done to Ai and its king. He also heard that Gibeon’s inhabitants had gone over to Joshua and Israel.
10:2 They were greatly afraid of them, for he knew that Gibeon was a great city, like one of the chief cities, and all its men were strong.
10:3 Adoni-bezek king of Jerusalem sent to Ailam king of Hebron, Pheidon king of Jarmuth, Jephtha king of Lachish, and Debir king of Adullam, saying:
10:4 “Come up to me and help me. Let us wage war on Gibeon, for they have gone over to Joshua and the sons of Israel.”
10:5 The five kings of the Jebusites—of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Adullam—went up with all their people. They surrounded Gibeon and besieged it.
10:6 The inhabitants of Gibeon sent to Joshua at the camp of Israel in Gilgal, saying, “Do not withdraw your hands from your servants. Come up to us quickly, and save us and help us; for all the kings of the Amorites dwelling in the hill country are gathered against us.”
10:7 Joshua went up from Gilgal with all the fighting people, every mighty man of strength.
10:8 The Lord said to Joshua, “Do not fear them, for I have delivered them into your hands. None of them shall remain before you.”
10:9 Joshua arrived against them suddenly, having travelled from Gilgal the whole night.
10:10 The Lord threw them into confusion before the sons of Israel, and the Lord crushed them with a great defeat at Gibeon. They pursued them along the ascent of Horonin and cut them down as far as Azekah and Makkedah.
10:11 As they fled before the sons of Israel down the descent of Horonin, the Lord hurled hailstones from heaven upon them as far as Azekah. More died from the hailstones than the sons of Israel killed with the sword in battle.
10:12 Then Joshua spoke to the Lord on the day God handed the Amorites over to Israel, when He crushed them at Gibeon and they were crushed before the sons of Israel. Joshua said, “Let the sun stand over Gibeon, and the moon over the ravine of Aijalon.”
10:13 The sun stood, and the moon remained still, until God repelled their enemies. The sun stood in the middle of heaven and did not advance toward sunset for a full day.
10:14 There was no such day before or afterward, when God listened to a human being, for the Lord fought alongside Israel.
10:16 These five kings fled and hid in the cave at Makkedah.
10:17 Joshua was informed, “The five kings have been found hiding in the cave at Makkedah.”
10:18 Joshua said, “Roll stones against the mouth of the cave and station men over them to keep watch.
10:19 Do not stop pursuing your enemies. Overtake their rear guard, and do not let them enter their cities; for the Lord our God has delivered them into our hands.”
10:20 When Joshua and every son of Israel had finished cutting them down in a very great slaughter to the end, those who escaped reached the fortified cities.
10:21 All the people returned safely to Joshua at Makkedah, and none of the sons of Israel muttered with his tongue.
10:22 Joshua said, “Open the cave and bring these five kings out of it.”
10:23 They brought the five kings out of the cave: the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Adullam.
10:24 When they brought them to Joshua, he summoned all Israel and the leaders in battle who had accompanied him, saying, “Come forward and put your feet on their necks.” They came forward and put their feet on their necks.
10:25 Joshua said to them, “Do not fear them or lose heart. Be courageous and strong, for this is what the Lord shall do to all your enemies against whom you wage war.”
10:26 Joshua killed them and hanged them on five trees. They remained hanging on the trees until evening.
10:27 At sunset Joshua gave the command, and they took the bodies down from the trees and threw them into the cave where they had fled. They rolled stones against the cave, which remain to this day.
10:28 That day they took Makkedah, slaughtered it with the edge of the sword, and destroyed everything breathing in it. No survivor or fugitive remained. They did to Makkedah’s king as they had done to Jericho’s king.
10:29 Joshua and all Israel with him went from Makkedah to Libnah and besieged it.
10:30 The Lord handed it into Israel’s hands. They took it and its king, struck it and everything breathing in it with the edge of the sword, and left no survivor or fugitive. They did to its king as they had done to Jericho’s king.
10:31 Joshua and all Israel with him went from Libnah to Lachish, surrounded it, and besieged it.
10:32 The Lord handed Lachish into Israel’s hands. He took it on the second day, and they struck it with the edge of the sword and destroyed it, as they had done to Libnah.
10:33 Then Ailam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish. Joshua struck him and his people with the edge of the sword until no survivor or fugitive remained.
10:34 Joshua and all Israel with him went from Lachish to Adullam, surrounded it, and besieged it.
10:35 The Lord handed it into Israel’s hand. He took it that day and slaughtered it with the edge of the sword, and they slaughtered everything breathing in it, as they had done to Lachish.
10:36 Joshua and all Israel with him went to Hebron and surrounded it.
10:37 He struck it and everything breathing in it with the edge of the sword. No survivor remained. As they had done to Adullam, they destroyed it and everything in it.
10:38 Joshua and all Israel turned toward Debir and surrounded it,
10:39 and took it, its king, and its villages. They struck it with the edge of the sword and destroyed it and everything breathing in it, leaving no survivor. As they had done to Hebron and its king, so they did to Debir and its king.
10:40 Joshua struck all the land of the hill country, the Negeb, the plain, and Asedoth, with its kings. They left no survivor, and he destroyed everything breathing with life, as the Lord, the God of Israel, commanded.
10:41 From Kadesh-barnea to Gaza, all Gosom as far as Gibeon.
10:42 Joshua struck all their kings and their land at one time, because the Lord, the God of Israel, fought alongside Israel.

JOSHUA 11

11:2 and to the kings near Sidon the Great, in the hill country, in the Raba opposite Chinneroth, in the plain, and in Napheddor,
11:3 and to the coastal Canaanites from the east, the coastal Amorites, the Hivites, Jebusites, and Perizzites in the hill country, and the Hittites beneath Hermon in the land of Mizpah.
11:4 They came out, with their kings—as numerous as the sand of the sea, with very many horses and chariots.
11:5 All these kings assembled, came together, and camped at the water of Marron to fight Israel.
11:6 The Lord said to Joshua, “Do not fear them, for tomorrow at this hour I am delivering them, routed, before Israel. You shall hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire.”
11:7 Joshua and all the fighting people came upon them suddenly at the water of Marron and fell upon them in the hill country.
11:8 The Lord handed them over to Israel. Striking them, they pursued them as far as Sidon the Great, Maseron, and the plains of Massoch toward the east. They cut them down until none of them was left alive.
11:9 Joshua did to them as the Lord had commanded him: he hamstrung their horses and set their chariots on fire.
11:10 Joshua turned back at that time and captured Hazor and its king; Hazor had formerly ruled all these kingdoms.
11:11 They killed everything that breathed in it with the sword and destroyed them all. Nothing that breathed was left in it, and they set Hazor on fire.
11:12 Joshua took all the cities of the kings and their kings and killed them with the edge of the sword. They destroyed them, as Moses the servant of the Lord had ordered.
11:13 But Israel did not burn any of the cities enclosed with earthworks; Hazor alone Joshua burned.
11:14 The sons of Israel took all its spoil for themselves. But they destroyed all the people with the edge of the sword until he had destroyed them; they left not even one of them breathing.
11:15 As the Lord ordered Moses His servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did. He transgressed none of all that Moses had ordered him.
11:16 Joshua took all the hill country, all the Negeb, all the land of Gosom, the plain, the western region, the mountain of Israel, and the low places,
11:17 those near the mountain, from Mount Achel and what rises toward Seir as far as Baal-gad and the plains of Lebanon beneath Mount Hermon. He took all their kings, did away with them, and killed them.
11:18 Joshua waged war against these kings for many days.
11:19 There was no city that Israel did not take; they took everything in war.
11:20 For through the Lord their hearts were strengthened to meet Israel in war, so that they might be destroyed, that no mercy might be given them, but that they might be destroyed, as the Lord had said to Moses.
11:21 At that time Joshua came and destroyed the Anakim from the hill country—from Hebron, Debir, Anaboth, all the stock of Israel, and all the hill country of Judah—with their cities. Joshua destroyed them.
11:22 None of the Anakim was left among the sons of Israel; only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod were they left.
11:23 Joshua took all the land, as the Lord had commanded Moses, and Joshua gave these as an inheritance to Israel, in a division according to their tribes. And the land ceased being at war.

14:6 The sons of Judah drew near to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, “You know the word that the Lord spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and you at Kadesh-barnea.

14:8 But my brothers who went up with me turned away the heart of the people; yet I gave myself to follow the Lord my God.

17:5 Their measured allotment fell from Anassa, and the plain of Labek from Gilead, which is beyond the Jordan,

17:9 The boundary will descend to the ravine of Karana toward the southwest, along the ravine of Jariel; a terebinth belongs to Ephraim amid a city of Manasseh. Manasseh’s border is on the north toward the torrent, and its endpoint will be the sea.

17:13 When the sons of Israel grew strong, they made the Canaanites subject to them, but did not utterly destroy them.

JOSHUA 22

22:1 Then Joshua summoned the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,
22:2 and said to them, “You have heeded all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, and have obeyed my voice in all that I commanded you.
22:3 You have not forsaken your brothers these many days, even to this day; you have kept the command of the Lord your God.
22:4 And now the Lord our God has given our brothers rest, as He told them. Turn back therefore, and go to your homes and to the land of your possession, which Moses gave you beyond the Jordan.
22:5 Only take great care to do the commandments and the law which Moses the servant of the Lord commanded us to do: to love the Lord your God, to walk in all His ways, to keep His commandments, to cling to Him, and to serve Him with all your mind and all your soul.”
22:6 Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their homes.
22:7 Moses had given an inheritance in Bashan to half the tribe of Manasseh, and Joshua had given one to the other half with their brothers beyond the Jordan toward the sea. When Joshua sent them away to their homes and blessed them,
22:8 they went to their homes with much wealth, very much livestock, silver, gold, iron, and many garments, and divided the spoil of the enemies with their brothers.
22:9 The sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of the sons of Manasseh went from the sons of Israel at Shiloh in the land of Canaan to the land of Gilead, the land of their possession, which they inherited by the Lord’s command through Moses.
22:10 They came to Galgala of the Jordan, which is in the land of Canaan, and the sons of Gad, the sons of Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there beside the Jordan, an altar great to behold.
22:11 The sons of Israel heard people saying, “Behold, the sons of Gad, the sons of Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar at the borders of the land of Canaan, on the Gilead of the Jordan, on the side of the sons of Israel.”
22:12 All the sons of Israel gathered at Shiloh to go up and wage war against them.
22:13 The sons of Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the high priest, to the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead,
22:14 and ten leaders with him, one leader from an ancestral house from each of the tribes of Israel; they were leaders of ancestral houses, commanders of thousands in Israel.
22:15 They came to the sons of Gad, the sons of Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead and spoke to them, saying,
22:16 “Thus says the whole congregation of the Lord: ‘What is this offence that you have committed before the God of Israel, turning away today from the Lord, in that you have built yourselves an altar and become apostates from the Lord?
22:17 Was the sin of Peor a small thing for us? We have not been cleansed of it to this day, and a plague came upon the congregation of the Lord.
22:18 And will you turn away today from the Lord? If you depart today from the Lord, tomorrow wrath shall come upon all Israel.
22:19 Now, if the land of your possession is too small for you, cross into the land of the Lord’s possession, where the tabernacle of the Lord dwells, and inherit among us. Do not become apostates from God or depart from the Lord by building yourselves an altar apart from the altar of the Lord our God.
22:20 Behold, did not Achar son of Zerah commit an offence concerning the devoted thing, and wrath came upon the whole congregation of Israel? He was only one man; was he alone the one who died for his own sin?’”
22:21 The sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the commanders of thousands in Israel, saying,
22:22 “God, God is Lord! And God, God the Lord, He knows, and Israel shall know. If in apostasy we offended before the Lord, may He not deliver us this day.
22:23 If we built ourselves an altar to depart from the Lord our God, to offer on it a sacrifice of whole burnt offerings or a sacrifice of well-being, the Lord will require an account.
22:24 We did this out of concern about this matter, saying, ‘Tomorrow your children might say to our children, “What do you have to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?
22:25 The Lord has made the Jordan a boundary between us and you, and you have no portion in the Lord.” Your sons would turn our sons away, so that they would not revere the Lord.’
22:26 We said that we should act in this way: build this altar, not for offerings and not for sacrifices,
22:27 but so that it may be a testimony between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we may perform service to the Lord before Him with our offerings and our sacrifices and our sacrifices of well-being. Then your children shall not say to our children tomorrow, ‘You have no portion in the Lord.’
22:28 We said, ‘If ever they speak to us and to our generations tomorrow, they shall say, “Behold the likeness of the Lord’s altar which our fathers made, not for offerings or sacrifices, but as a testimony between you and us and between our sons.”’
22:29 May it never be that we turn away from the Lord in these present days, departing from the Lord by building an altar for offerings, salamin sacrifices, and a sacrifice of well-being apart from the Lord’s altar before His tent.”
22:30 When Phinehas the priest and all the leaders of the congregation of Israel with him heard the words spoken by the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, they were pleased.
22:31 And Phinehas the priest said to the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, “Today we know that the Lord is with us, for you have committed no offence before the Lord, and you have delivered the sons of Israel from the hand of the Lord.”
22:32 Phinehas the priest and the leaders returned from the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, from the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the sons of Israel, and reported the words to them.
22:33 It pleased the sons of Israel. They spoke to the sons of Israel, blessed the God of the sons of Israel, and said they should no longer go up against them for war to destroy the land of the sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. They dwelt upon it.
22:34 Joshua named the altar of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and said, “It is a testimony between them that the Lord is their God.”

JOSHUA 23

23:3 You have seen all that the Lord your God did to all these nations before you; for the Lord your God is He who fought for you.
23:4 Behold, I have assigned these nations that remain to you in the lots for your tribes, from the Jordan, all the nations that I destroyed; and the boundary shall extend from the Great Sea toward the sunset.
23:5 The Lord your God Himself shall destroy them before you until they perish. He shall send wild beasts against them until He destroys them and their kings before you. Inherit their land, as the Lord your God told you.
23:6 Be very strong, then, to keep and do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, so that you do not turn aside to the right or to the left,
23:7 and do not enter among these nations that remain. Do not let the names of their gods be named among you, and do not bow down to them or serve them,
23:8 but cling to the Lord your God, as you have done to this day.
23:9 The Lord destroyed them before you, great and strong nations; no one has stood against you to this day.
23:10 One of you pursued a thousand, for the Lord your God was fighting for you, as He told you.
23:11 Take great care to love the Lord your God.
23:12 For if you turn back and cling to these nations that remain with you, and intermarry with them, and mingle with them and they with you,
23:13 know that the Lord shall surely no longer destroy these nations before you. They shall be traps and stumbling blocks to you, and nails in your heels and darts in your eyes, until you perish from this good land which the Lord your God has given you.
23:14 I am departing on the way of all who are upon the earth. You shall know in your heart and soul that not one word has failed of all the words which the Lord your God spoke concerning all that belongs to you; not one has proved untrue.
23:15 As all the good words which the Lord spoke to you have come upon you, so the Lord God shall bring upon you all the harmful words, until He destroys you from this good land which the Lord gave you,
23:16 when you transgress the covenant of the Lord your God which He commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them.”

JOSHUA 24

24:1 Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shiloh. He summoned their elders, scribes, and judges and made them stand before God.
24:2 Joshua said to all the people, “Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: ‘From the beginning your fathers lived beyond the River—Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor—and they served other gods.
24:3 I took your father Abraham from beyond the River, led him through all the land, multiplied his offspring, and gave him Isaac,
24:4 and to Isaac, Jacob and Esau. I gave Mount Seir to Esau as his inheritance, and Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt and there became a great, numerous, and mighty nation.’
24:5 The Egyptians afflicted them, and the Lord struck Egypt by what He did to them; afterward He brought you
24:6 out of Egypt, and you entered the Red Sea. And the Egyptians pursued your fathers with chariots and horses into the Red Sea,
24:7 and we cried to the Lord. And He set a cloud and thick darkness between us and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them and covered them. And your eyes saw all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt. And you were in the wilderness many days.
24:8 And He brought you into the land of the Amorites dwelling beyond the Jordan. And they arrayed themselves against you, and the Lord delivered them into your hands. And you inherited their land and destroyed them before you.
24:9 Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and arrayed himself against Israel. He sent and called Balaam to curse you.
24:10 But the Lord your God was unwilling to destroy you. He blessed you abundantly, rescued you from their hands, and handed them over.
24:11 And you crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho. And the inhabitants of Jericho fought against you, the Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, Jebusites, Hittites, and Girgashites; and the Lord delivered them into your hands.
24:12 He sent the swarm of wasps before you, and it drove them out from before you, twelve kings of the Amorites, not by your sword or by your bow.
24:13 He gave you a land for which you did not labour, and cities which you did not build, and you were settled in them. You shall eat of vineyards and olive groves which you did not plant.
24:14 Now fear the Lord and serve Him with uprightness and righteousness. Remove the foreign gods your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the Lord.
24:15 But if it does not please you to serve the Lord, choose for yourselves today whom you will serve: the gods of your fathers beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you dwell. But I and my house will serve the Lord, for He is holy.”
24:16 The people answered and said, “May it never be that we abandon the Lord to serve other gods.
24:17 The Lord our God—He is God. He brought us and our fathers up from Egypt and guarded us on all the way we travelled and among all the nations through whom we passed.
24:18 The Lord drove out before us the Amorites and all the nations living in the land. We too will serve the Lord, for He is our God.”
24:19 Joshua said to the people, “You cannot serve the Lord, for He is a holy God; and being jealous, He will not forgive your sins and your lawless deeds.
24:20 Whenever you forsake the Lord and serve other gods, He shall come upon you and afflict you and consume you in return for the good He has done you.”
24:21 The people said to Joshua, “No! We will serve the Lord.”
24:22 And Joshua said to the people, “You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the Lord to serve Him.
24:23 Now remove the foreign gods among you and direct your heart to the Lord, the God of Israel.”
24:24 The people said to Joshua, “The Lord we will serve, and His voice we will obey.”
24:25 Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day and gave them law and judgment at Shiloh before the tent of the God of Israel.
24:26 He wrote these words in a scroll, the law of God. Joshua took a great stone and set it beneath the terebinth before the Lord.
24:27 Joshua said to the people, “Behold, this stone shall be among you as a testimony, for it has heard all that was spoken to it by the Lord, what He spoke to us today. It shall be among you as a testimony at the last of the days, whenever you deal falsely with the Lord my God.”
24:28 Joshua sent the people away, and each went to his own place.
24:29 Israel served the Lord all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua and saw all the works the Lord had done for Israel.
24:30 After those things Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died at the age of one hundred ten years.
24:31 They buried him by the borders of his lot at Thamnathasachara in the mountain of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.
24:31a There they placed with him in the tomb in which they buried him the stone knives with which he circumcised the sons of Israel at Gilgal, when he brought them out of Egypt, as the Lord commanded them. They are there to this very day.
24:32 The sons of Israel brought Joseph’s bones up from Egypt and buried them at Shechem in the portion of the field that Jacob acquired from the Amorites living at Shechem for one hundred ewe lambs; he gave it to Joseph as a portion.
24:33 After these things Eleazar son of Aaron, the high priest, died and was buried at Gabaath of Phinehas his son, which he had given him in the mountain of Ephraim.
24:33a On that day the sons of Israel took the ark of God and carried it about among themselves. Phinehas served as priest in place of Eleazar his father until he died and was buried at his own Gabaath.
24:33b The sons of Israel went away, each to his own place and his own city. The sons of Israel revered Astarte and Ashtaroth and the gods of the nations around them. The Lord handed them into the hands of Eglom king of Moab, and he ruled over them for eighteen years.
