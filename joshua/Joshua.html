Joshua 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Lord Commissions Joshua

1:1After the death of Moses, the Lord spoke to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses’ attendant, saying:

1:2“Moses My servant has died. Now therefore arise and cross the Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving them.

1:3Every place where you tread with the sole of your feet I will give you, as I have spoken to Moses.

1:4The wilderness and Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river, the River Euphrates, and as far as the furthest sea toward the setting sun shall be your borders.

1:5No man shall withstand you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you; I will not forsake you or disregard you.

1:6Be strong and courageous, for you shall apportion to this people the land which I swore to your fathers to give them.

1:7Be strong and courageous, then, to keep and do what Moses My servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from these commands to the right or to the left, so that you may have understanding in all that you do.

1:8This book of the law shall not depart from your mouth. Meditate on it day and night, so that you may understand how to do all that is written. Then you shall prosper and make your ways prosper, and then you shall understand.

1:9Behold, I have commanded you: be strong and courageous. Do not be fainthearted or afraid, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go.”

Orders to Prepare for the Crossing

1:10Joshua commanded the scribes of the people, saying:

1:11“Go into the midst of the people's camp and command the people, saying, ‘Prepare provisions; for in three days you are crossing this Jordan, to enter and possess the land that the Lord, the God of your fathers, is giving you.’”

The Eastern Tribes Pledge Their Warriors

1:12Joshua said to Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh:

1:13“Remember the word of the Lord that Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded you: ‘The Lord your God has given you rest and has given you this land.’

1:14Let your wives and your children and your livestock dwell in the land he gave you; but you, every man of strength, shall cross equipped before your brothers and fight alongside them,

1:15until the Lord your God gives your brothers rest as He has given you, and they also inherit the land that the Lord our God is giving them. Then each of you shall return to his inheritance, which Moses gave you beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise.”

1:16They answered Joshua, “We will do all that you command us, and we will go wherever you send us.

1:17As we obeyed Moses in all things, so will we obey you. Only let the Lord our God be with you, as He was with Moses.

1:18Whoever disobeys you or does not heed your words in all that you command him, let him die. Only be strong and courageous!”

Joshua 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Spies Hidden in Rahab’s House

2:1Joshua son of Nun sent two young men from Shittim to spy, saying, “Go up and look at the land and Jericho.” The two young men went into Jericho, entered the house of a harlot named Rahab, and lodged there.

2:2The king of Jericho was informed, “Men from the sons of Israel have entered here to spy out the land.”

2:3The king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, “Bring out the men who entered your house by night, for they have come to spy out the land.”

2:4The woman took the men and hid them, and said to the messengers, “The men came to me.

2:5As the gate was closing in the darkness, the men went out. I do not know where they have gone. Pursue them, to see whether you can overtake them.”

2:6But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them among the flax stalks she had piled on the roof.

2:7The men pursued them along the road to the Jordan, toward the crossings, and the gate was shut. After the pursuers had gone out after them,

Rahab’s Confession and Request

2:8and before the young men went to sleep, she went up to them on the roof

2:9and said, “I know that the Lord has given you the land, for fear of you has fallen upon us.

2:10For we have heard that the Lord God dried up the Red Sea before you when you came out of the land of Egypt, and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you destroyed.

2:11When we heard, our hearts were astounded, and no spirit remained in any of us because of you. For the Lord your God is God in heaven above and on the earth below.

2:12Now swear to me by the Lord God that, as I show you mercy, you also will show mercy to my father's house,

2:13and keep alive my father’s household, my mother, my brothers, my whole household, and all who belong to them, and rescue my life from death.”

2:14The men said to her, “Our life in place of yours, even to death.” And she said, “When the Lord delivers the city to you, you shall show me mercy and faithfulness.”

The Scarlet Cord and the Terms of the Oath

2:15She lowered them through the window

2:16and said to them, “Go into the hill country, so the pursuers do not encounter you. Hide there three days until those pursuing you return; afterward go on your way.”

2:17The men said to her, “We are free from this oath of yours.

2:18Behold, we are coming into part of the city. You shall set up the sign: bind this scarlet cord in the window through which you lowered us, and gather your father and your mother and your brothers and all your father’s house to you in your house.

2:19Whoever goes out beyond the door of your house shall bear his own guilt; we shall be free from this oath of yours. For all who are with you in your house, we shall be answerable.

2:20But if anyone wrongs us or makes known these words of ours, we shall be free from this oath of yours.”

2:21She said to them, “Let it be according to your word.” Then she sent them away.

The Spies Report to Joshua

2:22They went into the hill country and remained there three days. The pursuers searched all the roads but did not find them.

2:23The two young men returned, came down from the mountain, crossed over to Joshua son of Nun, and related everything that had happened to them.

2:24They said to Joshua, “The Lord has given all the land into our hand, and everyone inhabiting that land is cowering before us.”

Joshua 3

Read this chapter on staging

Orders for the Crossing

3:1Joshua rose early in the morning. They set out from Shittim, came to the Jordan, and lodged there before crossing.

3:2After three days, the scribes passed through the camp

3:3and commanded the people, “When you see the ark of the covenant of the Lord our God, and our priests and the Levites carrying it, leave your places and follow it.

3:4Yet let there be a distance of about two thousand cubits between you and it. Keep your place, and do not draw near to it, so that you may know the way you are to go; for you have not passed this way before.”

3:5Joshua said to the people, “Purify yourselves for tomorrow, for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you.”

3:6Joshua said to the priests, “Lift up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and go ahead of the people.” The priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord and went before the people.

The Lord Begins to Exalt Joshua

3:7The Lord said to Joshua, “Today I begin to exalt you before all the sons of Israel, so that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.

3:8Now command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant, saying, ‘When you enter the edge of the waters of the Jordan, stand in the Jordan.’”

3:9Joshua said to the sons of Israel, “Come here and hear the word of the Lord our God.”

3:10“By this you shall know that the living God is among you, and will surely destroy before us the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, Amorites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.

3:11Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing the Jordan.

3:12Choose twelve men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe.

3:13When the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth rest in the Jordan’s water, the Jordan’s water shall fail, and the water coming down shall stand.”

The Waters Stand and Israel Crosses

3:14The people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, and the priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord ahead of the people.

3:15As the priests carrying the ark of the covenant entered the Jordan and the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord dipped into the edge of the Jordan’s water—the Jordan was overflowing its whole bank, as in the days of wheat harvest—

3:16the waters coming down from above stood. One mass stood very, very far away, as far as the region of Kiriath-jearim. The water flowing down descended into the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, until it failed completely. The people stood opposite Jericho.

3:17The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All the sons of Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole people had finished crossing the Jordan.

Joshua 4

Read this chapter on staging

Twelve Stones from the Middle of the Jordan

4:1When the whole people had finished crossing the Jordan, the Lord said to Joshua:

4:2“Take men from the people, one from each tribe,

4:3and command them, ‘Take twelve ready stones from the midst of the Jordan, carry them over with you, and set them in your camp where you will camp tonight.’”

4:4Joshua summoned twelve distinguished men from the sons of Israel, one from each tribe,

4:5and said to them, “Come forward before me, before the Lord, into the midst of the Jordan. Each of you shall take a stone from there and lift it upon his shoulders, according to the number of the twelve tribes of Israel,

4:6so that these may remain a sign to you for ever. When your son asks you in time to come, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’

4:7you shall declare to your son, ‘The River Jordan failed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth as it crossed.’ And these stones shall be a memorial to you, the sons of Israel, for ever.”

4:8The sons of Israel did as the Lord commanded Joshua. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, just as the Lord directed Joshua when the sons of Israel finished crossing. They carried them with them into the camp and set them there.

4:9Joshua also set up another twelve stones in the Jordan itself, in the place beneath the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord. They are there to this day.

The People Complete the Crossing

4:10The priests carrying the ark of the covenant stood in the Jordan until Joshua completed everything the Lord had commanded him to announce to the people. The people hurried and crossed.

4:11When all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the covenant of the Lord crossed, and the stones were before them.

4:12The sons of Reuben, the sons of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed equipped before the sons of Israel, just as Moses had commanded them.

4:13Forty thousand equipped for battle crossed before the Lord for war, toward the city of Jericho.

4:14On that day the Lord exalted Joshua before the whole race of Israel. They feared him as they had Moses, as long as he lived.

The Priests Come Up and the River Returns

4:15The Lord said to Joshua:

4:16“Command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the testimony of the Lord to come out of the Jordan.”

4:17Joshua commanded the priests, “Come out of the Jordan.”

4:18When the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the Lord came out of the Jordan and set their feet on the land, the Jordan’s water rushed into its place and flowed along its whole bank as before.

The Stones Set Up at Gilgal

4:19The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month. The sons of Israel camped at Gilgal, in the region east of Jericho.

4:20Joshua set up at Gilgal these twelve stones that he had taken from the Jordan,

4:21saying, “When your sons ask you, ‘What are these stones?’

4:22tell your sons, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground,

4:23when the Lord our God dried up the Jordan’s water before them until they crossed, just as the Lord our God did to the Red Sea, which the Lord our God dried up before us until we passed through,’

4:24that all the nations of the earth may know that the power of the Lord is mighty, and that you may revere the Lord your God at all times.”

Circumcision at the Hill of the Foreskins

5:1When the kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan and the kings of Phoenicia by the sea heard that the Lord God had dried up the River Jordan before the sons of Israel as they crossed, their minds melted; they were dismayed, and no understanding remained in them because of the sons of Israel.

5:2At that time the Lord said to Joshua, “Make for yourself stone knives from sharp rock, and sit down and circumcise the sons of Israel.”

5:3Joshua made sharp stone knives and circumcised the sons of Israel at the place called Hill of the Foreskins.

5:4This is how Joshua purified the sons of Israel: those born along the way and those among the people who had left Egypt who were still uncircumcised—

5:5Joshua circumcised all these.

5:6For Israel had lived forty-two years in the wilderness of Madbaritis. Therefore most of the fighting men who had left the land of Egypt were uncircumcised, those who disobeyed God’s commands. He decreed that they should not see the land the Lord swore to their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.

5:7In their place He appointed their sons, whom Joshua circumcised because they had remained uncircumcised along the way.

5:8After being circumcised, they rested there in the camp until they recovered.

5:9The Lord said to Joshua son of Nun, “Today I have removed the reproach of Egypt from you.” He called that place Gilgal.

Passover at Gilgal and the End of the Manna

5:10The sons of Israel kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, from evening, west of Jericho, beyond the Jordan, in the plain.

5:11They ate from the grain of the land: unleavened bread and new grain. On this day

5:12the manna failed after they had eaten from the grain of the land. There was no longer manna for the sons of Israel; that year they harvested the country of the Phoenicians.

The Commander of the Lord’s Host

5:13When Joshua was at Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and saw a man standing opposite him, with a drawn sword in his hand. Joshua drew near and said, “Are you on our side, or on the side of our adversaries?”

5:14He said to him, “I have now come as chief captain of the host of the Lord.” Joshua fell on his face to the earth and said to him, “Master, what do you command your servant?”

5:15The chief captain of the Lord said to Joshua, “Loosen your sandal from your feet, for the place where you stand is holy.”

Joshua 6

Read this chapter on staging

The Lord Gives Jericho into Joshua’s Hand

6:1Jericho was shut up and fortified; no one went out of it or entered it.

6:2And the Lord said to Joshua, “Behold, I am delivering Jericho, and its king within it, mighty in strength, into your power.

6:3Station the fighting men all around it,

6:5and when you sound the trumpet, let all the people cry out together. As they cry out, the walls of the city shall fall of themselves, and all the people shall rush into the city, each straight ahead of him.”

The Order of the Procession

6:6Joshua son of Nun went in to the priests

6:7and said to them, “Instruct the people to go around and encircle the city. Let the fighting men pass armed before the Lord.

6:8Let seven priests holding seven sacred trumpets likewise pass before the Lord and sound them vigorously, and let the ark of the covenant of the Lord follow.

6:9Let the fighting men pass in front, and let the priests forming the rear guard go behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord, marching and blowing trumpets.”

6:10Joshua commanded the people, “Do not cry out or let anyone hear your voice until the day he himself commands you to cry out. Then you shall cry out.”

6:11After the ark of the covenant of God had gone around the city, it went straight back to the camp and rested there.

Six Days of Circling the City

6:12On the second day Joshua rose in the morning, and the priests lifted up the ark of the covenant of the Lord.

6:13The seven priests carrying the seven trumpets went before the Lord. Afterward the fighting men and the rest of the crowd proceeded behind the ark of the covenant of the Lord. The priests sounded the trumpets, and all the rest of the crowd encircled the city close by

6:14and returned to the camp. This was done for six days.

6:15On the seventh day they rose at dawn and went around the city six times.

The Shout, the Ban, and the Fall of the Walls

6:16On the seventh circuit the priests sounded the trumpets, and Joshua said to the sons of Israel, “Cry out, for the Lord has handed the city over to you!

6:17The city and everything in it shall be under the ban to the Lord Sabaoth. But preserve Rahab the harlot and everything in her house.

6:18But guard yourselves carefully from what is under the ban, lest you covet it and take any of it, and bring the camp of the sons of Israel under the ban, and destroy us.

6:19All silver, gold, bronze, and iron shall be holy to the Lord and shall be brought into the Lord’s treasury.”

6:20The priests sounded the trumpets. When the people heard their sound, all the people together raised a great, mighty shout. The whole wall fell all around, and all the people went up into the city.

6:21Joshua put the city and everything in it under the ban by the edge of the sword: man and woman, young and old, calf and beast of burden.

Rahab Spared and the City Burned

6:22Joshua said to the two young men who had spied, “Enter the woman’s house and bring her out from there, with everything that belongs to her.”

6:23The two young men who had spied out the city entered the woman’s house and brought out Rahab the harlot, her father, her mother, her brothers, everything belonging to her, and all her kin. They placed her outside Israel’s camp.

6:24The city was burned with everything in it, except that the silver, gold, bronze, and iron were given to be brought into the Lord’s treasury.

6:25Joshua kept alive Rahab the harlot and her entire paternal household. She has lived in Israel to this day, because she hid the spies Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

Joshua’s Oath over Jericho

6:26On that day Joshua pronounced an oath before the Lord: “Cursed is the man who rebuilds that city! At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates.” Ozan of Bethel did so: at the cost of Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest surviving son he set up its gates.

6:27The Lord was with Joshua, and his name was known throughout the land.

Joshua 7

Read this chapter on staging

Achar Takes What Is Under the Ban

7:1The sons of Israel committed a great offence and misappropriated what was under the ban. Achar son of Carmi, son of Zambri, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of it, and the Lord became angry with the sons of Israel.

Defeat at Ai

7:2Joshua sent men to Ai, which is near Bethel, saying, “Spy out Ai.” The men went up and spied out Ai.

7:3They returned to Joshua and said, “Do not let all the people go up. Let about two or three thousand men go up and capture the city by siege. Do not bring all the people there, for they are few.”

7:4About three thousand men went up, but they fled before the men of Ai.

7:5The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them, pursued them from the gate, and crushed them on the descent. The people’s heart was terrified and became like water.

Joshua’s Complaint and the Lord’s Answer

7:6Joshua tore his clothes and fell face down on the earth before the Lord until evening, he and the elders of Israel. They put dust on their heads.

7:7And Joshua said, “I beseech Thee, Lord, why did Thy servant bring this people across the Jordan, to deliver them to the Amorites and destroy us? Would that we had remained and dwelt beside the Jordan!

7:8What can I say, since Israel has turned its back before its enemy?

7:9When the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land hear, they shall surround us and blot us out from the earth. And what wilt Thou do for Thy great name?”

7:10The Lord said to Joshua, “Arise! Why have you fallen on your face like this?

7:11The people have sinned and transgressed the covenant I made with them. They stole from what was under the ban and put it among their belongings.

7:12The sons of Israel shall not be able to stand before their enemies. They shall turn their backs before their enemies, for they have become subject to the ban. I will no longer be with you, unless you remove that which is under the ban from among yourselves.

7:13Arise, consecrate the people, and tell them to consecrate themselves for tomorrow. Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: ‘What is under the ban is among you. You cannot withstand your enemies until you remove it from among you.’

7:14In the morning you shall all assemble by tribes. Bring forward by clans the tribe which the Lord indicates; bring forward by households the clan which the Lord indicates; and bring forward man by man the household which the Lord indicates.

7:15Whoever is indicated shall be burned with fire, together with everything belonging to him, because he transgressed the Lord’s covenant and committed lawlessness in Israel.”

The Lot Falls on Achar

7:16Joshua rose early and brought the people forward by tribes. The tribe of Judah was indicated.

7:17They were brought forward by clans, and the Zerahite clan was indicated. They were brought forward man by man,

7:18and Achar son of Zambri, son of Zerah, was indicated.

Confession, Judgment, and the Valley of Achor

7:19Joshua said to Achar, “Give glory today to the Lord, the God of Israel, and make confession. Tell me what you have done, and do not hide it from me.”

7:20Achar answered Joshua, “Truly I have sinned before the Lord, the God of Israel. This is what I did:

7:21I saw among the spoil a beautiful patterned carpet, two hundred didrachms of silver, and one tongue-shaped bar of gold weighing fifty didrachms. I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground in my tent, and the silver is hidden underneath them.”

7:22Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent in the camp. The things were hidden in the tent, with the silver underneath them.

7:23They brought the things out of the tent to Joshua and the elders of Israel and placed them before the Lord.

7:24Joshua took Achar son of Zerah and brought him up to the ravine of Achor, with his sons, his daughters, his cattle, his beasts of burden, all his sheep, his tent, and everything belonging to him. All the people were with him, and he brought them up to Emek-achor.

7:25Joshua said to Achar, “Why have you brought destruction upon us? May the Lord destroy you, as on this very day!” And all Israel stoned him with stones.

7:26They set a great heap of stones over him. The Lord ceased from the heat of His anger. Therefore the place was named Emek-achor, as it is to this day.

Joshua 8

Read this chapter on staging

The Lord Gives Ai into Joshua’s Hand

8:1The Lord said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid or lose heart. Take all the men of war with you, arise, and go up to Ai. Behold, I have delivered the king of Ai and his land into your hands.

8:2Do to Ai as you did to Jericho and its king, and take the livestock as spoil for yourself. Set an ambush behind the city.”

The Ambush Set Behind the City

8:3Joshua and all the fighting people arose to go up to Ai. Joshua selected thirty thousand mighty men and sent them away by night.

8:4He commanded them, “Lie in ambush behind the city. Do not go far from the city, and all of you be ready.

8:5I and everyone with me will approach the city. When Ai’s inhabitants come out to meet us as before, we shall flee before them.

8:6When they come out after us, we shall draw them away from the city. They will say, ‘These men are fleeing before us as before.’

8:7Then rise from the ambush and go into the city.

8:8Do according to this word. Behold, I have commanded you.”

The Land Divided by Lot

14:1These are the sons of Israel who received an inheritance in the land of Canaan. Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the sons of Israel assigned it to them.

14:2They inherited by lots, as the Lord commanded through Joshua for the nine tribes and the half-tribe,

14:3from beyond the Jordan. And he gave the Levites no lot among them.

14:4For the sons of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. No portion in the land was given to the Levites, except cities to live in, their allotted lands for the livestock, and their livestock.

14:5As the Lord had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they divided the land.

Caleb Asks for the Hill Country

14:6The sons of Judah drew near to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, “You know the word that the Lord spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and you at Kadesh-barnea.

14:7For I was forty years old when Moses the servant of God sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land, and I answered him with a report according to his mind. †

14:8But my brothers who went up with me turned away the heart of the people; yet I gave myself to follow the Lord my God.

14:9Moses swore on that day, saying, ‘The land on which you have trodden shall be a lot for you and your children for ever, for you gave yourself to follow the Lord our God.’

14:10Now the Lord has sustained me, as He said. This is the forty-fifth year since the Lord spoke this word to Moses and Israel journeyed in the wilderness. And now, look: today I am eighty-five years old.

14:11I am still strong today, as when Moses sent me; I am just as strong now to go out and come in for war.

14:12Now I ask you for this mountain, as the Lord said on that day, for you heard this word on that day. The Anakim are now there, and great, fortified cities. If the Lord is with me, I shall destroy them, as the Lord said to me.”

Hebron Given to Caleb

14:13Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh son of Kenaz as a lot.

14:14Therefore Hebron has belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as a lot to this day, because he followed the command of the Lord, the God of Israel.

14:15The former name of Hebron was the city of Arbok; it was the mother-city of the Anakim. And the land grew quiet from war.

24:3I took your father Abraham from beyond the River, led him through all the land, multiplied his offspring, and gave him Isaac,

24:4and to Isaac, Jacob and Esau. I gave Mount Seir to Esau as his inheritance, and Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt and there became a great, numerous, and mighty nation.’

24:6out of Egypt, and you entered the Red Sea. And the Egyptians pursued your fathers with chariots and horses into the Red Sea,

24:8And He brought you into the land of the Amorites dwelling beyond the Jordan. And they arrayed themselves against you, and the Lord delivered them into your hands. And you inherited their land and destroyed them before you.

24:9Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and arrayed himself against Israel. He sent and called Balaam to curse you.

24:12He sent the swarm of wasps before you, and it drove them out from before you, twelve kings of the Amorites, not by your sword or by your bow.

24:13He gave you a land for which you did not labour, and cities which you did not build, and you were settled in them. You shall eat of vineyards and olive groves which you did not plant.

Choose Whom You Will Serve

24:14Now fear the Lord and serve Him with uprightness and righteousness. Remove the foreign gods your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the Lord.

The People Answer

24:16The people answered and said, “May it never be that we abandon the Lord to serve other gods.

24:21The people said to Joshua, “No! We will serve the Lord.”

24:22And Joshua said to the people, “You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the Lord to serve Him.

24:23Now remove the foreign gods among you and direct your heart to the Lord, the God of Israel.”

24:24The people said to Joshua, “The Lord we will serve, and His voice we will obey.”

The Covenant and the Stone of Witness

24:25Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day and gave them law and judgment at Shiloh before the tent of the God of Israel.

24:26He wrote these words in a scroll, the law of God. Joshua took a great stone and set it beneath the terebinth before the Lord.

24:27Joshua said to the people, “Behold, this stone shall be among you as a testimony, for it has heard all that was spoken to it by the Lord, what He spoke to us today. It shall be among you as a testimony at the last of the days, whenever you deal falsely with the Lord my God.”

24:28Joshua sent the people away, and each went to his own place.

24:30After those things Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the Lord, died at the age of one hundred ten years.

24:31They buried him by the borders of his lot at Thamnathasachara in the mountain of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

24:33After these things Eleazar son of Aaron, the high priest, died and was buried at Gabaath of Phinehas his son, which he had given him in the mountain of Ephraim.

24:33aOn that day the sons of Israel took the ark of God and carried it about among themselves. Phinehas served as priest in place of Eleazar his father until he died and was buried at his own Gabaath.
