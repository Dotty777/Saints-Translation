# Joshua — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 24 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 659 existing verse units received an English editorial review: 161 changed and 498 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition, with independent specialist approval still pending.

The newer *Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf* governs and supersedes the older *Saints English Founding Style Guide* where their pronoun policies conflict. Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your. Accepted Genesis 1–11 is the working English benchmark. Successful earlier wording is retained.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 is the user-authorized provisional base. Selected running-text passages were inspected in the [German Bible Society edition](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/JOS.1), with the exact chapter-by-chapter scope recorded in source-checks.json. Göttingen and the full apparatus were not obtained or collated. No complete new Greek verification or human read-aloud review is claimed.

## Preserved distinctions and corrections

Every original verse ID, link, chapter-navigation destination, image, dagger and existing review note is preserved. Greek-based gaps and additions remain, including 9:2a–2f, 15:59a, 19:47a/48a, 21:42a–42d and 24:31a/33a–33b. No missing verse was invented or renumbered. Ancient names, measures and quantities remain distinct.

Joshua 3:16 restores the doubled intensifier as “very, very far.” Joshua 15:7 restores the future verbs in its final sentence. Joshua 24:5 removes an accidentally duplicated “out” across the 24:5–6 boundary; 24:13 restores the plural “them” referring to the cities. Other documented changes improve ordinary address, verb forms, syntax and spoken rhythm.

The existing disputed readings remain at 14:7 (“his mind”), 19:48 (Judah, Lachish and Lasendak), and 22:29 (“salamin sacrifices”), each with its original dagger and note. Further priorities include 6:2 and 6:10, 8:18 and 8:24, 14:2–4, 17:5–18, 21:11–12/20/27, 22:20–29, 23:4/14 and the ending of chapter 24. Full name, quantity, boundary and legal-language review is pending.

Saints Translation literal-smooth and all six study/comparison pages were left unchanged and passed content-hash checks. No live-site update was made. This is a book-level archive, not a full-site backup.

Joshua is the first completed book of the Judgement Division in this Bespoke pass. Judges is next; the other fourteen books of the division remain pending. Repository-wide progress is maintained in [progress.json](../progress.json); the earlier Law book records are preserved.
