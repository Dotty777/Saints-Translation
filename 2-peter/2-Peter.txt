2 PETER 1

1:1 Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have received a faith of equal honor with ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:
1:2 May grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.
1:3 Since His divine power has granted us everything pertaining to life and godliness through the knowledge of the One who called us by His own glory and excellence,
1:4 through these He has granted us the precious and very great promises, so that through them you may become participants in the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through desire.
1:5 And for this very reason, bringing every effort to bear, supply excellence in your faith, and knowledge in excellence,
1:6 and self-control in knowledge, and endurance in self-control, and godliness in endurance,
1:7 and brotherly love in godliness, and love in brotherly love.
1:8 For if these things are yours and abound, they make you neither idle nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:9 For the person who lacks these things is blind and shortsighted, having forgotten the cleansing of his former sins.
1:10 Therefore, brothers, be all the more diligent to make your calling and election firm, for if you do these things, you will certainly never stumble.
1:11 For in this way entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplied to you.
1:12 Therefore, I will always be ready to remind you of these things, even though you know them and are firmly established in the truth that is present with you.
1:13 And I consider it right, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up by reminding you,
1:14 knowing that I will soon put off my tent, as our Lord Jesus Christ also made clear to me.
1:15 And I will also make every effort so that after my departure you may be able at any time to call these things to mind.
1:16 For we did not follow cleverly devised myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we became eyewitnesses of His majesty.
1:17 For He received honor and glory from God the Father when a voice such as this was borne to Him by the Majestic Glory: “This is My Son, My Beloved, in whom I have taken pleasure”—
1:18 and we ourselves heard this voice borne from heaven when we were with Him on the holy mountain.
1:19 And we have the prophetic word more firmly established, to which you do well to pay attention as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts,
1:20 knowing this first: that no prophecy of Scripture arises from one’s own interpretation.
1:21 For prophecy was never borne along by human will, but human beings spoke from God as they were borne along by the Holy Spirit.

2 PETER 2

2:1 But false prophets also arose among the people, just as there will also be false teachers among you. They will secretly bring in destructive factions, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing swift destruction upon themselves.
2:2 And many will follow their debaucheries, and because of them the way of truth will be slandered.
2:3 And in greed they will exploit you with fabricated words. Their judgment has not been idle since long ago, and their destruction does not sleep.
2:4 For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into Tartarus and handed them over to chains of gloom, kept for judgment,
2:5 and did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness, as one of eight, when He brought a flood upon a world of ungodly people,
2:6 and reduced the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes, condemning them to overthrow, having made them an example for those who would live ungodly lives,
2:7 and rescued righteous Lot, who was distressed by the lawless people’s conduct in debauchery—
2:8 for by what he saw and heard, that righteous man, living among them, tormented his righteous soul day after day over their lawless deeds—
2:9 then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trial, and to keep the unrighteous under punishment for the day of judgment,
2:10 especially those who go after flesh in defiling desire and despise lordship. Reckless and self-willed, they do not tremble as they slander glorious beings,
2:11 whereas angels, though greater in strength and power, do not bring a slanderous judgment against them before the Lord.
2:12 But these people, like irrational animals born as creatures of instinct for capture and destruction, slandering things they do not understand, will also be destroyed in their destruction,
2:13 suffering harm as the wages of wrongdoing. They consider indulgence in the daytime a pleasure. They are spots and blemishes, reveling in their deceptions while feasting with you,
2:14 having eyes full of an adulteress and unable to stop sinning, enticing unstable souls, having a heart trained in greed—children of a curse!
2:15 Leaving the straight way, they wandered off, following the way of Balaam son of Bosor, who loved the wages of wrongdoing,
2:16 but received a rebuke for his own transgression: a speechless beast of burden spoke with a human voice and restrained the prophet’s madness.
2:17 These people are waterless springs and mists driven by a storm, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept.
2:18 For, uttering inflated words of emptiness, they entice through fleshly desires and debaucheries those who are truly escaping from people who live in error,
2:19 promising them freedom while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by whatever someone has been overcome, to this he has also been enslaved.
2:20 For if, after escaping the world’s defilements through the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, they are again entangled in them and overcome, their last state has become worse than their first.
2:21 For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than, after knowing it, to turn back from the holy commandment handed down to them.
2:22 What the true proverb says has happened to them: “A dog has returned to its own vomit,” and, “A sow, after washing, returns to wallowing in mud.”

2 PETER 3

3:1 Beloved, this is now the second letter I am writing to you. In these letters I stir up your sincere mind by reminding you,
3:2 so that you may remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Savior through your apostles,
3:3 knowing this first: in the last days scoffers will come with scoffing, walking according to their own desires,
3:4 and saying, “Where is the promise of His coming? For ever since the fathers fell asleep, everything continues as it was from the beginning of creation.”
3:5 For they willingly overlook this: that heavens existed long ago, and earth was formed out of water and through water by the word of God,
3:6 through which the world that then existed was flooded with water and perished.
3:7 But the present heavens and earth have been stored up by the same word for fire, kept for the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly people.
3:8 But do not let this one thing escape you, beloved: with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.
3:9 The Lord is not slow concerning the promise, as some consider slowness, but is patient toward you, not wishing anyone to perish but all to come to repentance.
3:10 But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On that day the heavens will pass away with a rushing roar, and the elements will be dissolved by burning heat, and the earth and the works in it will not be found.
3:11 Since all these things are being dissolved in this way, what sort of people ought you to be in holy acts of conduct and godliness,
3:12 waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be dissolved by fire and the elements melt in burning heat?
3:13 But according to His promise we await new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.
3:14 Therefore, beloved, as you await these things, be diligent to be found by Him spotless and blameless, in peace,
3:15 and consider the patience of our Lord to be salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you according to the wisdom given to him,
3:16 as also in all his letters, speaking in them about these things. In them are some things hard to understand, which the unlearned and unstable will distort, just as they will distort the other Scriptures, to their own destruction.
3:17 Therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, guard yourselves so that you are not carried away by the error of lawless people and fall from your own firm position.
3:18 But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity.
