## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Simeon and the joined our God and Savior Jesus Christ remain. Modern verbs and ordinary you restored. Participants in the divine nature retains the strong participation wording without a doctrinal expansion. The faith/excellence/knowledge/self-control/endurance/godliness/brotherly-love/love chain and its repetitions remain. Tent, putting off, departure, eyewitness, lamp and morning star images remain. The third-person Son quotation is not changed into direct address.

Pending: Review participants in divine nature (1:4), the blind/shortsighted relation (1:9), swift versus soon in 1:14, the comparative more firmly established in 1:19 and whose own interpretation is meant in 1:20. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Modern third-person verbs restored. The consulted NA28 wording is reflected in those who would live ungodly lives (2:6), before the Lord restored in 2:11, and truly escaping in 2:18 replacing barely escaping. Tartarus and chains of gloom remain distinct from other judgment terms. Bosor is retained rather than harmonized to Beor. The personified unsleeping destruction, springs/mists, freedom/slavery, vomit and sow/mud images remain.

Pending: Review the recorded variants at 2:6,11,18 against the full apparatus/ECM. Review destructive factions (2:1), chains/pits (2:4), glorious beings and their judgment (2:10–12), deceptions (2:13), and eyes full of an adulteress (2:14). Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** The day/thousand-years comparison remains two-sided. Patience toward you, not wishing anyone to perish and all coming to repentance remain. The negative not be found in 3:10 is retained from the consulted NA28; will replaces shall only as English register. In 3:16 will distort reflects the displayed future form. Day of eternity remains in the closing doxology, with no final Amen, as in the consulted running text.

Pending: Priority full-apparatus review of not be found (3:10) and will distort (3:16). The singular masculine relative at 3:6 may refer to the word/God in 3:5; inherited through which is retained provisionally. Review elements (3:10–12), hastening (3:12) and the difficult participial construction of the chapter’s warning. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
