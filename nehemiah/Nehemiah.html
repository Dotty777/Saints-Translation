Nehemiah 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Words of Neemias

1:1The words of Neemias son of Achalia. In the month of Chaseelou, in the twentieth year, I was in Sousan abira,

1:2and Anani, one of my brothers, came, he and men of Judah. I asked them about those who had been saved, who remained from the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

1:3They said to me, “The remaining people who were left from the captivity there in the country are in great distress and disgrace. Jerusalem’s walls have been pulled down, and its gates have been set ablaze with fire.”

Neemias Mourns and Prays

1:4When I heard these words, I sat down and wept, and mourned for days. I was fasting and praying before the God of heaven,

1:5and said, “No, I pray, Lord, God of heaven, the Mighty One, the Great and Fearsome, who dost keep the covenant and mercy for those who love Him and keep His commandments:

1:6let Thine ear be attentive and Thine eyes opened to hear Thy servant’s prayer, which I pray before Thee today, day and night, for the sons of Israel, Thy servants. I confess concerning the sins of the sons of Israel that we have sinned against Thee. I and my father’s house have sinned.

1:7We have utterly broken faith with Thee, and have not kept the commandments, ordinances and judgments that Thou didst command Moses Thy servant.

1:8Remember, I pray, the word that Thou didst command Moses Thy servant, saying, ‘If you break covenant, I will scatter you among the peoples;

1:9and if you turn back to Me, keep My commandments and do them, even if your dispersed people are at the farthest edge of heaven, I will gather them from there and bring them into the place I chose for My name to dwell there.’

1:10They are Thy servants and Thy people, whom Thou didst redeem by Thy great power and Thy mighty hand.

1:11No, I pray, Lord! But let Thine ear be attentive to Thy servant’s prayer and to the prayer of Thy servants who wish to fear Thy name. Grant, I pray, Thy servant success today, and give him compassion before this man.” Now I was the king’s cupbearer.

Nehemiah 2

Read this chapter on staging

Neemias Before King Arthasastha

2:1In the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Arthasastha, the wine was before me. I took the wine and gave it to the king, and there was no other person before him.

2:2The king said to me, “Why is your face distressed when you are not unwell? This is nothing but distress of heart.” I became very greatly afraid.

2:3I said to the king, “May the king live for ever! Why should my face not be distressed, since the city, the home of my fathers’ tombs, has been laid waste and its gates have been consumed by fire?”

2:4The king said to me, “What are you asking for?” I prayed to the God of heaven,

2:5and said to the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant finds favor before you, send him to Judah, to the city of my fathers’ tombs, and I will rebuild it.”

2:6The king and the concubine sitting beside him said to me, “How long will your journey last, and when will you return?” It pleased the king, and he sent me, and I gave him a time limit.

2:7I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, let him give me letters to the governors beyond the river, so that they may conduct me through until I reach Judah,

2:8and a letter to Asaph, keeper of the king’s park, so that he may give me timber to roof the gates, for the city wall, and for the house I will enter.” The king granted it to me, according to the good hand of God.

The Journey Beyond the River

2:9I came to the governors beyond the river and gave them the king’s letters. The king had sent army commanders and horsemen with me.

2:10Sanaballat the Aroni and Tobia the Ammoni servant heard, and it displeased them that a man had arrived to seek good for the sons of Israel.

The Inspection of the Wall by Night

2:11I came to Jerusalem and was there for three days.

2:12I arose at night, with a few men accompanying me. I told no one what God was putting into my heart to do with Israel. No animal was with me except the animal on which I was riding.

2:13I went out through the gate of Golela, toward the mouth of the Spring of Figs and to the Dung Gate. I was breaking at Jerusalem’s wall, which they were pulling down, and its gates had been consumed by fire.

2:14I passed on to the gate of Ain and the King’s Pool, but there was no room for the animal beneath me to pass.

2:15I went up along the wall of the torrent at night, breaking at the wall. I was at the Valley Gate, and returned.

2:16The guards did not know why I had gone or what I was doing. Until then I had told nothing to the Jews, the priests, the honored men, the commanders, or the remaining people who were doing the works.

Let Us Build the Wall of Jerusalem

2:17I said to them, “You see the distress we are in, how Jerusalem is desolate and its gates have been given to fire. Come, let us rebuild Jerusalem’s wall, and we will no longer be a disgrace.”

2:18I told them about the hand of God, which was good upon me, and the king’s words that he had spoken to me. I said, “Let us arise and build,” and their hands were strengthened for good.

The Scorn of the Adversaries

2:19Sanaballat the Aroni, Tobia the Ammoni servant, and Gesam the Arabi heard. They laughed at us and came against us, and said, “What is this thing you are doing? Are you revolting against the king?”

2:20I answered them and said, “The God of heaven, He will grant us success. We are His pure servants, and we will build. But you have no share, right, or memorial in Jerusalem.”

Nehemiah 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Sheep Gate and the Wall Rebuilt

3:1Elisoub the high priest and his brothers the priests arose and built the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors, and consecrated as far as the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Tower of Ananeel,

3:2and alongside the sons of the men of Jericho, and alongside the sons of Zakchour son of Amari.

3:3The sons of Asana built the Fish Gate. They roofed it and set up its doors, locks and bars.

3:4Next to them, from Ramoth, a son of Ouria son of Akos took charge. Next to them Mosollam son of Barachias son of Masezebel took charge. Next to them Sadok son of Baana took charge.

3:5Next to them the Thekoin took charge, but the adoreem did not bring their necks into their service.

3:6Ioida son of Phasek and Mesoulam son of Basodia repaired the gate of Isana. They roofed it and set up its doors, locks and bars.

3:8Next to them Ananias son of Rokeim repaired, and they left Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

3:9Next to them Raphaia, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired.

3:10Next to them Iedaia son of Eromaph repaired, opposite his house. Next to him Atous son of Asbania repaired.

3:11As second, Melchias son of Eram and Asoub son of Phaathmoab repaired, as far as the Tower of the Thannourim.

3:12Next to him Saloum son of Alloes, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, repaired, he and his daughters.

The Valley Gate and the Dung Gate

3:13Anoun and the inhabitants of Zano repaired the Valley Gate. They built it and set up its doors, locks and bars, and a thousand cubits of wall as far as the Dung Gate.

3:14Melchia son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Bethacharm, repaired the Dung Gate, he and his sons. They covered it and set up its doors, locks and bars,

3:15and the wall of the Pool of Fleeces, for the king’s shearing, and as far as the steps descending from the City of David.

The Levites and the Rulers Repair

3:16After him Neemias son of Azabouch, ruler of half the district of Bethsour, repaired as far as the garden of David’s tomb, as far as the pool that had been made, and as far as Bethaggabarim.

3:17After him the Levites—Raoum son of Bani—repaired. Next to him Asabia, ruler of half the district of Keila, repaired for his district.

3:18After him their brothers—Beni son of Enadad, ruler of half the district of Keila—repaired.

3:19Next to him Azour son of Iesous, ruler of Masphe, repaired a second section of the tower of the ascent adjoining the corner.

3:20After him Barouch son of Zabou repaired a second section, from the corner to the entrance of Bethelisoub of the high priest.

3:21After him Meramoth son of Ouria son of Akos repaired a second section, from the entrance of Bethelisoub to the end of Bethelisoub.

3:22After him the priests, men of Achechar, repaired.

3:23After him Benjamin and Asoub repaired opposite their house. After him Azaria son of Maasea son of Anania repaired beside his house.

3:24After him Bani son of Enadad repaired a second section, from Bethazaria to the corner and to the bend

3:25of Phalal son of Euzai, opposite the corner, and the upper tower projecting from the king’s house, belonging to the court of the guard. After him was Phadaia son of Phoros.

The Nathinim on the Ophal

3:26The Nathinim were living on the Ophal, as far as the garden of the Water Gate toward the east, and the projecting tower.

3:27After him the Thekoin repaired a second section opposite the great projecting tower, as far as the wall of the Ophla.

From the Horse Gate to the Sheep Gate

3:28Above the Horse Gate the priests repaired, each opposite his house.

3:29After him Saddouk son of Emmer repaired opposite his house. After him Samaia son of Sechenia, keeper of the East Gate, repaired.

3:30After him Anania son of Selemia and Anoum, the sixth son of Seleph, repaired a second section. After him Mesoulam son of Barchia repaired opposite his treasury chamber.

3:31After him Melchia son of Saraphi repaired as far as Bethanathinim; and the dealers in small wares, opposite the gate of Maphekad and as far as the ascent of the bend.

3:32In the intervening space at the ascent of the Sheep Gate, the metalworkers and dealers in small wares repaired.

The Anger of Sanaballat

3:33When Sanaballat heard that we were building the wall, it displeased him. He became very angry and laughed at the Jews.

3:34He said before his brothers, “Is this the strength of Somoron, that these Jews are building their own city?”

3:35Tobias the Ammanite came beside him, and they said to one another, “Surely they will not sacrifice or eat in their place? Will not a fox climb up and pull down their stone wall?”

A Prayer Against the Mockers

3:36Hear, our God, for we have become an object of mockery. Turn their reproach back upon their own heads, and give them over to mockery in a land of captivity,

3:37and do not cover over lawlessness.

Nehemiah 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Adversaries Gather to Fight

4:1When Sanaballat, Tobia, the Arabs and the Ammanites heard that growth had risen on Jerusalem’s walls, that the breaches were beginning to be blocked, it seemed very bad to them.

4:2They all gathered together to come and draw up for battle in Jerusalem.

4:3We prayed to our God and stationed outposts against them day and night because of them.

The Strength of the Bearers Fails

4:4Judah said, “The strength of the enemies has been crushed. There is much rubble, and we will not be able to build the wall.”

4:5Those who were afflicting us said, “They will neither know nor see until we come among them, murder them, and bring the work to a stop.”

4:6When the Jews who lived beside them came, they told us, “They are coming up against us from every place.”

Swords, Spears and Bows Set at the Wall

4:7I stationed people in the lowest parts of the place behind the wall, in the sheltered areas. I stationed the people by local groups, with their swords, their spears and their bows.

4:8I looked, arose, and said to the honored men, the commanders and the rest of the people, “Do not be afraid of them. Remember our God, great and fearsome, and draw up for battle for your brothers, your sons and daughters, your wives and your houses.”

4:9When our enemies heard that it had become known to us, and God had scattered their counsel, we all returned to the wall, each to his work.

Half at the Work, Half with Spears

4:10From that day on, half of those who had been shaken out did the work, and half of them held firm. Spears, shields, bows and breastplates were there, and the leaders were behind all the house of Judah,

4:11those building on the wall. Those carrying with lifting gear were armed; with one hand each did the work itself, and the other held a dart.

4:12The builders worked with each man’s sword girded at his waist, and the one sounding the horn was beside him.

Our God Will Fight for Us

4:13I said to the honored men, the leaders and the rest of the people, “The work is widespread and great, and we are scattered along the wall, each far from his brother.

4:14At whatever place you hear the sound of the horn, gather to us there, and our God will wage war for us.”

4:15We continued doing the work, while half of them held spears from the rising of dawn until the stars came out.

A Watch by Night and Work by Day

4:16At that time I told the people, “Lodge within Jerusalem, and let the night be a watch for you, and the day, work.”

4:17I was there, with the men of the guard behind me, and not one of us took off his clothes.

Nehemiah 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Outcry of the People Against Their Brothers

5:1A great outcry arose from the people and their wives against their Jewish brothers.

5:2Some said, “With our sons and daughters we are many. We must obtain grain, eat, and live.”

5:3Others said, “Our fields, our vineyards and our houses—we are pledging them. We must obtain grain and eat.”

5:4Others said, “We borrowed silver for the king’s taxes—our fields, vineyards and houses are at stake.

5:5Now our flesh is like our brothers’ flesh, and our sons like their sons. Yet we are forcing our sons and daughters into slavery, and some of our daughters are being subjected. There is no power in our hands, and our fields and vineyards belong to the honored men.”

Neemias Contends with the Honored Men

5:6I was deeply grieved when I heard their outcry and these words.

5:7My heart took counsel within me. I contended with the honored men and leaders and said to them, “You are making demands, each against his brother!” I brought a great assembly against them,

5:8and said to them, “We have bought our Jewish brothers who were being sold to the nations, of our own free will. And are you selling your brothers?” They fell quiet and found no answer.

5:9I said, “What you are doing is not good. Not so! In the fear of our God you shall depart from the reproach of the nations, our enemies.

5:10My brothers, my acquaintances and I have also put silver and grain at their disposal. Let us, I pray, relinquish this exaction.

Restore to Them This Very Day

5:11Please restore to them this very day their fields, vineyards, olive trees and houses; and from the silver, bring out for them the grain, wine and oil.”

5:12They said, “We will return it and ask nothing from them. We will do as you say.” I called the priests and made them swear to act according to this word.

5:13I shook out my wrap and said, “So may God shake out from his house and his labor every man who does not uphold this word. So shall he be shaken out and empty.” All the assembly said, “Amen,” and praised the Lord. The people carried out this word.

Neemias Takes No Governor's Portion

5:14From the day he appointed me to be their ruler in the land of Judah—from the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of Arthasastha, twelve years—I and my brothers did not eat their coerced provision,

5:15and as for the former exactions, which before me they laid heavily upon them: they took from them bread and wine, and, at the last, silver—forty didrachms. Their shaken-out men exercise authority over the people, but I did not act that way, because of the fear of God.

5:16I did not take charge of the work on their wall; I acquired no field, and all those gathered there were for the work.

5:17At my table were the Jews, one hundred and fifty men, and those who came to us from the nations around us.

5:18Each day one young bull, six choice sheep and a he-goat were prepared for me. Every ten days there was wine in abundance for everyone. Along with these things, I did not demand bread from coercion, because the service weighed heavily on this people.

5:19Remember me for good, God, for everything I have done for this people.

Nehemiah 6

Read this chapter on staging

The Summons to the Plain of Ono

6:1When Sanaballat, Tobia, Gesam the Arabi and the rest of our enemies heard that I had built the wall, no breath was left in them. Up to that time I had not set doors in the gates.

6:2Sanaballat and Gesam sent to me, saying, “Come, let us gather together in the villages in the plain of Ono.” They were planning to do me harm.

6:3I sent messengers to them, saying, “I am doing a great work and will not be able to come down, lest the work stop. When I finish it, I will come down to you.”

6:4They sent me this same message, and I sent them an answer accordingly.

The Open Letter of Sanaballat

6:5Sanaballat sent his servant to me with an open letter in his hand.

6:6In it was written: “It has been reported among the nations that you and the Jews are planning to revolt. That is why you are building the wall, and you are becoming their king.

6:7Besides this, you have appointed prophets for yourself so that you may sit in Jerusalem as king in Judah. Now these words will be reported to the king. Come now, let us take counsel together.”

6:8I sent to him, saying, “Nothing like these words you say has happened. You are falsely devising them from your own heart.”

6:9For they are all frightening us, saying, “Their hands will grow slack from this work, and it will not be done.” Now I strengthened my hands.

The Hired Prophecy of Semei

6:10I entered the house of Semei son of Dalaia son of Meetabel, who was confined. He said, “Let us gather in the house of God, inside it, and shut its doors, for they are coming at night to murder you.”

6:11I said, “Who is the man who could enter the house and live?”

6:12I recognized that God had not sent him, because the prophecy was a word against me, and Tobias and Sanaballat had hired

6:13a crowd against me, so that I might be afraid, act in that way and sin, and become a bad name for them to reproach me.

6:14Remember, God, Tobia and Sanaballat according to these deeds of his, and Noadia the prophet and the rest of the prophets who were frightening me.

The Wall Completed in Fifty-Two Days

6:15The wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Eloul, in fifty-two days.

6:16When all our enemies heard, all the nations around us became afraid. Great fear fell upon them before their eyes, and they knew that it was by our God that this work had been brought to completion.

The Letters of Tobia

6:17In those days letters from many honored men of Judah went to Tobia, and Tobia’s letters came to them,

6:18because many in Judah were bound to him by oath, since he was the son-in-law of Sechenia son of Erae, and his son Ioanan had taken the daughter of Mesoulam son of Barachia as wife.

6:19They also told me his words and carried my words out to him, and Tobias sent letters to frighten me.

You Chose Abram

9:8“Thou didst find his heart faithful before Thee and make a covenant with him to give him the land of the Chananites, Chettites, Amorrites, Pherezites, Jebousites and Gergesites, and to his offspring. Thou didst fulfil Thy words, for Thou art righteous.

You Saw the Humiliation in Egypt

9:15“Thou gavest them bread from heaven for their shortage of food and didst bring water from a rock for their thirst. Thou didst tell them to enter and inherit the land that Thou hadst stretched out Thy hand to give them.

Our Fathers Stiffened Their Necks

9:16“But they and our fathers acted arrogantly, stiffened their necks and did not listen to Thy commandments.

9:17“They refused to listen and did not remember Thy wonders that Thou hadst done among them. They stiffened their necks and appointed leadership to return to their slavery in Egypt. But Thou art a merciful and compassionate God, longsuffering and abundant in mercy, and Thou didst not abandon them.

9:18“Even when they made a cast calf for themselves and said, ‘These are the gods who brought us out of Egypt,’ and committed great provocations,

9:19“Thou, in Thine abundant compassion, didst not abandon them in the wilderness. Thou didst not turn the pillar of cloud away from them by day, to guide them on the way, or the pillar of fire by night, to light for them the way they should travel.

9:20“Thou gavest Thy good Spirit to give them understanding. Thou didst not withhold Thy manna from their mouths, and Thou gavest them water for their thirst.

9:21“For forty years Thou didst nourish them in the wilderness; they lacked nothing. Their garments did not grow old, and their feet did not split open.

Kingdoms Given and the Land Inherited

9:22“Thou gavest them kingdoms and peoples and didst apportion them to them. They inherited the land of Seon king of Esebon and the land of Og king of Basan.

9:23“Thou didst multiply their sons like the stars of heaven and bring them into the land that Thou hadst spoken of to their fathers, and they inherited it.

9:24“Thou didst wipe out before them those inhabiting the land of the Chananites and give them into their hands, with their kings and the peoples of the land, to do with them as they pleased.

9:25“They took lofty cities and inherited houses full of every good thing, hewn cisterns, vineyards, olive groves and every kind of fruit tree in abundance. They ate, were filled, grew fat and lived in luxury in Thy great goodness.

They Cast Your Law Behind Them

9:26“But they changed and withdrew from Thee. They cast Thy law behind their bodies and killed Thy prophets who testified among them to turn them back to Thee. They committed great provocations.

9:27“Thou gavest them into the hand of those oppressing them, and they oppressed them. In the time of their distress they cried out to Thee, and Thou didst hear from Thy heaven. In Thy great compassion Thou gavest them saviors, and Thou didst save them from the hand of those oppressing them.

9:28“When they rested, they returned to doing evil before Thee. Thou didst abandon them into the hands of their enemies, who ruled over them. They again cried out to Thee, and Thou didst hear from heaven and rescue them in Thine abundant compassion.

9:29“Thou didst testify to them to turn them back to Thy law, but they did not listen. Instead, they sinned against Thy commandments and judgments, which, if a person does them, that person will live by them. They presented a disobedient back, stiffened their necks and did not listen.

9:30“Thou didst prolong Thy dealings with them for many years and testify to them by Thy Spirit through Thy prophets, but they did not give ear. Thou gavest them into the hand of the peoples of the land.

9:31“In Thine abundant compassion Thou didst not make an end of them or abandon them, for Thou art mighty, merciful and compassionate.

Now, Our God, Keeper of the Covenant

Those Appointed over the Treasuries

Nehemiah 13

Read this chapter on staging

The Reading of the Law and the Separation

13:1That day a reading was made from a book of Moses in the hearing of the people. It was found written in it that Ammanites and Moabites should not enter God’s assembly for ever,

13:2because they did not meet the sons of Israel with bread and water, and they hired Balaam against Israel to curse him. But our God turned the curse into a blessing.

13:3When they heard the law, all the mixed population in Israel was separated.

Tobia in the Chamber of God's House

13:4Before this, Eliasib the priest, who lived in a treasury chamber of the house of our God, was drawing close to Tobia,

13:5and he made a large treasury chamber for him. There they had formerly put the manaa, frankincense, vessels, the tithe of grain, wine and oil prescribed for the Levites, singers and gatekeepers, and the priests’ first offerings.

13:6During all this I was not in Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Arthasastha king of Babylon I had gone to the king. After some days had passed I made a request of the king

13:7and came to Jerusalem. I understood the evil Elisoub had done for Tobia by making him a treasury chamber in the court of God’s house.

13:8It appeared exceedingly evil to me, and I threw all the goods of Tobia’s house out of the treasury chamber.

13:9I gave orders, and they purified the treasury chambers. I brought back there the vessels of God’s house, the manaa and frankincense.

The Portions of the Levites Restored

13:10I learned that the Levites’ portions had not been given, and the Levites and singers doing the work had fled, each to his field.

13:11I contended with the commanders and said, “Why has God’s house been abandoned?” I gathered them and stationed them at their posts.

13:12All Jouda brought the tithe of wheat, wine and oil into the stores,

13:13into the charge of Selemia the priest, Saddouk the scribe and Phadaia from the Levites, and beside them Anan son of Zakchour, son of Mathania, for they were considered trustworthy; distribution to their brothers rested upon them.

13:14Remember me, O God, in this, and do not let my kindness be blotted out, which I practiced in the house of the Lord God.

The Sabbath Profaned by Trade

13:15In those days I saw people in Jouda treading winepresses on the Sabbath, bringing sheaves and loading them on donkeys, with wine, grapes, figs and every kind of burden, bringing them to Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I warned them on the day of their selling.

13:16People lived there bringing fish and every kind of merchandise, selling on the Sabbath to the sons of Jouda and in Jerusalem.

13:17I contended with the free men of Jouda and said to them, “What is this evil thing you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day?

13:18“Did not your fathers do this, so that our God brought all these evils upon them and upon us and upon this city? Yet you add wrath against Israel by profaning the Sabbath.”

The Gates Shut Before the Sabbath

13:19When the gates of Jerusalem were at rest before the Sabbath, I gave orders and they closed the gates. I ordered that they should not be opened until after the Sabbath. I stationed some of my young servants at the gates so that no burdens would be carried on the Sabbath day.

13:20They all lodged and conducted sales outside Jerusalem once or twice.

13:21I testified against them and said, “Why are you lodging opposite the wall? If you do it a second time, I will stretch out my hand against you.” From that time they did not come on the Sabbath.

13:22I told the Levites who were purifying themselves and coming to guard the gates to sanctify the Sabbath day. In these things remember me, O God, and spare me according to the abundance of Thy mercy.

The Foreign Marriages Rebuked

13:23In those days I also saw Jews who had established households with Azotian, Ammanite and Moabite wives,

13:24and half their children spoke Azotian and did not know how to speak Judean.

13:25I contended with them, cursed them, struck some of their men and pulled out their hair. I made them swear by God: “You shall not give your daughters to their sons or take any of their daughters for your sons.

13:26“Did not Salomon king of Israel sin in this way? Among many nations there was no king like him, and he was loved by God, and God made him king over all Israel. Yet foreign women turned even him aside.

13:27“Are we not to hear of you doing all this evil, breaking faith with our God by establishing households with foreign wives?”

The Son-in-Law of Sanaballat Driven Out

13:28From the sons of Joada of Elisoub, the high priest, the son-in-law of Sanaballat the Oronite—and I drove him away from me.

13:29Remember it against them, O God, concerning disqualification by descent from the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood—and remember the Levites.

Remember Me, O God, for Good

13:30I purified them from all foreign association and established service divisions for the priests and Levites, each according to his work,

13:31and the gift of the wood carriers at intervals from time to time, and at the bakchouria. Remember me, our God, for goodness.
