# Nehemiah — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 13 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 392 verse units received an English editorial review: 37 changed and 355 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/EZR.11) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Retained Thou/Thee/Thy in direct prayer and modernized the quoted divine address to the people. Used Fearsome for Terrible and dost keep for direct address; retained the Greek shift to Him/His, No I pray, the covenant repetition and the mighty-hand image.

Pending: 1:1 abira, 1:5 and 1:11 petition formula, 1:5 change of grammatical person and full apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 2.** Modernized ordinary royal address; smoothed the king’s question, if it pleases the king and time limit. Preserved no other person, the concubine’s participation, repeated breaking at the wall, Spring of Figs, God’s hand and pure servants.

Pending: 2:1–2 sequence, 2:6 concubine and time limit, 2:13–15 breaking terminology and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Retained every English unit, repeated repair formula, name, abbreviated passage and numbered gap. Preserved necks, Pool of Fleeces/king’s shearing, place-like Bethelisoub and the short imprecatory prayer.

Pending: 3:4 Ramoth, 3:5 adoreem, 3:15 fleeces/shearing, list segmentation, missing verse numbers and full textual collation need specialist review.

**Chapter 4.** Modernized the third-person verb and ordinary plural address. Preserved growth on walls, enemies’ strength, shaken-out men, lifting gear, dart and the shortened concluding account.

Pending: 4:4 enemies reading, 4:10 shaken-out expression, 4:11 lifting gear and 4:12 horn-blower referent require specialist review.

**Chapter 5.** Modernized ordinary address, third-person does and my acquaintances. Preserved grief, voluntary purchase, debt/exaction language, shaken wrap and men, forty didrachms, the negative wall-work statement and brief prayer.

Pending: 5:9 syntax, 5:11 commodity repayment, 5:14–18 coerced provision, 5:15 present tense and 5:16 negation require full apparatus and specialist review.

**Chapter 6.** Modernized ordinary address in the letters and warning. Preserved no breath, the promise to come down after finishing, I strengthened my hands, the hired crowd across verses, singular his deeds and fifty-two days. The publisher’s page was read in the browser after retrieval attempts failed.

Pending: 6:9 declarative versus Hebrew imperative, 6:11 shortened reply, 6:12–14 crowd/pronoun syntax and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Retained the complete English roster, its numbers and names, including both Anania figures, Jesus, the additional names, separate Bethlehem/Netopha counts, the priest giving light, gifts to Neemia and garment totals.

Pending: 7:3 command-person shift, full name and numerical collation, 7:64 status wording and 7:70–72 gifts/units require specialist review.

**Chapter 8.** Retained all English units, three named teachers, Esdras’s teaching, it is your strength without an added subject, trumpet signal, foliage, tents and closing observance.

Pending: 8:8 knowledge/explanation syntax, 8:10 implied strength referent, 8:15 festival instruction and full apparatus require specialist review.

**Chapter 9.** Preserved the sustained direct prayer with Thou/Thee/Thy and its second-person verb forms. Smoothed unto to to, modernized third-person does/has/belongs, and used dost keep in direct address. Retained Esdras as speaker, plural gods, split-open feet, bodies/back/neck images, repeated compassion and the cross-verse land clause.

Pending: 9:5 shift into prayer, 9:18 plural gods, 9:24 wipe out, 9:32 keeping Thy covenant and 9:35–37 kingdom/land syntax require specialist and apparatus review.

**Chapter 10.** Retained all English units, source numbering, Sedekias son of Saraia, the divided sons-of clause, prevailed/cursed, the demand of every hand, third of a didrachm and tithe of the tithe.

Pending: 10:2–3 lineage segmentation, 10:15–16 list division, 10:30 social action, 10:32 release wording and full apparatus require specialist review.

**Chapter 11.** Retained the shortened roster and all numbered gaps. Kept Saraia opposite the house, surviving names/counts, the king’s hand, farmsteads and the final Levite portions without importing longer witnesses.

Pending: Full roster and place-name collation, 11:22 grammatical grouping, 11:25–31 curtailed settlement list and 11:36 portions need specialist review.

**Chapter 12.** Retained all English wording and gaps, over the hands, at times, I gathered, thodatha, two great ones for praise, repeated gladness, names and the difficult storehouse appointment clause.

Pending: 12:8–9 hands/service divisions, 12:17 at times, 12:31 companies, 12:42 appointment, 12:44 syntax and complete apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 13.** Modernized ordinary plural address and third-person has; smoothed after some days had passed and do not let in prayer. Kept Thy in direct prayer, the repeated remembrance petitions, Eliasib/Elisoub forms, anonymous traders, acts of violence, lineage fragment and bakchouria.

Pending: 13:6 elapsed-days phrase, 13:14 kindness, 13:19 gates at rest, 13:28–29 lineage/disqualification and 13:31 bakchouria require specialist review.
