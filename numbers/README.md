# Numbers — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 36 chapters and the book index were revised, saved and read back on WordPress staging on 14 October 2026. All 1,287 existing verse units received an English editorial review: 505 changed and 782 retained. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist review remains pending.

The newer Bespoke policy and the Genesis–Exodus manner govern this pass. Ordinary address uses you/your. Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine remain in direct address to God, including Moses' prayers. The blessing in 6:24–26 addresses human recipients and therefore uses you. Modern verb forms and restrained changes to grammar and punctuation improve the English while preserving agents, imagery, repetitions and inherited source-specific readings.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. [source-checks.json](source-checks.json) records selected main-text checks and their limits. No full Göttingen or apparatus collation, independent Greek specialist review, or human read-aloud review is claimed. Earlier source and reception notes remain historical records; the new Bespoke notes explain the superseding English convention.
