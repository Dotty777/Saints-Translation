Numbers 1

1:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the Tent of Testimony, on the first day of the second month of the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt, saying:
1:2 Take the sum of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, by their families, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of their names, head by head, every male
1:3 from twenty years old and upward, every one who goes out in Israel’s army: register them with their army; you and Aaron shall register them.
1:4 And one of the rulers from each tribe shall be with you; they shall be according to their ancestral houses.
1:5 And these are the names of the men who shall stand with you. For Reuben: Elizur son of Shedeur.
1:6 For Simeon: Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.
1:7 For Judah: Nahshon son of Amminadab.
1:8 For Issachar: Nethanel son of Zuar.
1:9 For Zebulun: Eliab son of Helon.
1:10 For the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.
1:11 For Benjamin: Abidan son of Gideoni.
1:12 For Dan: Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.
1:13 For Asher: Pagiel son of Ocran.
1:14 For Gad: Eliasaph son of Reuel.
1:15 For Naphtali: Ahira son of Enan.
1:16 These are the men called from the congregation, rulers of the tribes by ancestral houses; they are commanders of Israel’s thousands.
1:17 And Moses and Aaron took these men who had been called by name.
1:18 And they assembled the whole congregation on the first day of the month of the second year and registered them by their descent and ancestral houses, counting their names, from twenty years old and upward, every male, head by head.
1:19 As the Lord instructed Moses, they were registered in the wilderness of Sinai.
1:20 And the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, were reckoned by their families, by their divisions, by their ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, every one who goes out in the army:
1:21 The enrolment from the tribe of Reuben was forty-six thousand five hundred.
1:22 For the sons of Simeon: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:23 The enrolment from the tribe of Simeon was fifty-nine thousand three hundred.
1:24 For the sons of Judah: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:25 The enrolment from the tribe of Judah was seventy-four thousand six hundred.
1:26 For the sons of Issachar: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:27 The enrolment from the tribe of Issachar was fifty-four thousand four hundred.
1:28 For the sons of Zebulun: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:29 The enrolment from the tribe of Zebulun was fifty-seven thousand four hundred.
1:30 For the sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:31 The enrolment from the tribe of Ephraim was forty thousand five hundred.
1:32 For the sons of Manasseh: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:33 The enrolment from the tribe of Manasseh was thirty-two thousand two hundred.
1:34 For the sons of Benjamin: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:35 The enrolment from the tribe of Benjamin was thirty-five thousand four hundred.
1:36 For the sons of Gad: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:37 The enrolment from the tribe of Gad was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.
1:38 For the sons of Dan: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:39 The enrolment from the tribe of Dan was sixty-two thousand seven hundred.
1:40 For the sons of Asher: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:41 The enrolment from the tribe of Asher was forty-one thousand five hundred.
1:42 For the sons of Naphtali: by their families, divisions, and ancestral houses, by the number of their names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, everyone who goes out in the army:
1:43 The enrolment from the tribe of Naphtali was fifty-three thousand four hundred.
1:44 This was the enrolment made by Moses, Aaron, and the rulers of Israel, twelve men, one man for each tribe, according to the tribe of their ancestral houses.
1:45 And the whole enrolment of the sons of Israel with their army, from twenty years old and upward, every one who goes out to battle in Israel,
1:46 was six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.
1:47 But the Levites, from their ancestral tribe, were not registered among the sons of Israel.
1:48 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
1:49 See that you do not register the tribe of Levi together with them, neither shall you take their number among the sons of Israel.
1:50 And you shall appoint the Levites over the Tent of Testimony, and over all its furnishings, and over all that is therein. They shall carry the Tent and all its furnishings, and they shall minister therein, and shall camp round about the Tent.
1:51 And when the Tent sets out, the Levites shall take it down; when it camps, they shall erect it. Let an outsider who approaches die.
1:52 And the sons of Israel shall camp, each in his own formation and each according to his own command, with their army.
1:53 But let the Levites camp before the Lord round about the Tent of Testimony, and there shall be no offence among the sons of Israel. And the Levites themselves shall keep the charge of the Tent of Testimony.
1:54 And the sons of Israel did everything that the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron; so they did.

Editorial note: The census order, names and quantities remain. “Head by head” and the distinction between the army and the Levites are retained. Ordinary address now uses you/your.

Numbers 2

2:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:
2:2 Let the sons of Israel camp, each beside his neighbour, by formation and by the signs of their ancestral houses. The sons of Israel shall camp opposite, around the Tent of Testimony.
2:3 And those camping first toward the east shall be the formation of Judah’s camp, with their army. The ruler of the sons of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab.
2:4 His army, those registered, numbered seventy-four thousand six hundred.
2:5 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Issachar; the ruler of the sons of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar.
2:6 His army, those registered, numbered fifty-four thousand four hundred.
2:7 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Zebulun; the ruler of the sons of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon.
2:8 His army, those registered, numbered fifty-seven thousand four hundred.
2:9 All those registered in the camp of Judah, with their army, numbered one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred. They shall set out first.
2:10 The formation of the camp of Reuben, with their army, shall be toward the southwest. The ruler of the sons of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur.
2:11 His army, those registered, numbered forty-six thousand five hundred.
2:12 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Simeon; the ruler of the sons of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.
2:13 His army, those registered, numbered fifty-nine thousand three hundred.
2:14 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Gad; the ruler of the sons of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel.
2:15 His army, those registered, numbered forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.
2:16 All those registered in the camp of Reuben, with their army, numbered one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty. They shall set out second.
2:17 And the Tent of Testimony and the camp of the Levites shall be raised in the middle of the camps. As they camp, so they shall set out, each beside his neighbour according to command.
2:18 The formation of the camp of Ephraim, with their army, shall be toward the sea. The ruler of the sons of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud.
2:19 His army, those registered, numbered forty thousand five hundred.
2:20 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Manasseh; the ruler of the sons of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.
2:21 His army, those registered, numbered thirty-two thousand two hundred.
2:22 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Benjamin; the ruler of the sons of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni.
2:23 His army, those registered, numbered thirty-five thousand four hundred.
2:24 All those registered in the camp of Ephraim, with their army, numbered one hundred eight thousand one hundred. They shall set out third.
2:25 The formation of the camp of Dan, with their army, shall be toward the north. The ruler of the sons of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.
2:26 His army, those registered, numbered sixty-two thousand seven hundred.
2:27 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Asher; the ruler of the sons of Asher is Pagiel son of Ocran.
2:28 His army, those registered, numbered forty-one thousand five hundred.
2:29 And those camping next shall be the tribe of Naphtali; the ruler of the sons of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan.
2:30 His army, those registered, numbered fifty-three thousand four hundred.
2:31 All those registered in the camp of Dan numbered one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last, by their formation.
2:32 This is the enrolment of the sons of Israel by their ancestral houses. The whole enrolment of the camps, with their armies, was six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.
2:33 But the Levites were not registered together with them, as the Lord commanded Moses.
2:34 And the sons of Israel did everything that the Lord instructed Moses. So they camped by their formations, and so they set out, each beside his neighbour, by their divisions and ancestral houses.

Editorial note: The camp order, directions, named leaders and totals remain. The inherited southwest and seaward language has not been replaced with a different geographical system.

Numbers 3

3:1 And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day when the Lord spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.
3:2 And these are the names of Aaron’s sons: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
3:3 These are the names of Aaron’s sons, the anointed priests whose hands were consecrated for priestly service.
3:4 And Nadab and Abihu died before the Lord when they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children. And Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests with Aaron their father.
3:5 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
3:6 Take the tribe of Levi, and you shall set them before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister unto him;
3:7 And they shall keep his charges and the charges of the sons of Israel before the Tent of Testimony, performing the work of the Tent.
3:8 And they shall keep all the furnishings of the Tent of Testimony and the charges of the sons of Israel, according to all the work of the Tent.
3:9 And you shall give the Levites unto Aaron and unto his sons, the priests. These are given unto Me as a gift from the sons of Israel.
3:10 And you shall appoint Aaron and his sons over the Tent of Testimony, and they shall keep their priesthood, and all that concerns the altar and is within the veil. And the outsider who touches shall die.
3:11 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
3:12 And I, behold, I have taken the Levites from among the sons of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb among the sons of Israel. They shall be their ransom, and the Levites shall be Mine.
3:13 For every firstborn is Mine. On the day when I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated unto Myself every firstborn in Israel, from man unto livestock. They shall be Mine; I am the Lord.
3:14 And the Lord spoke unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying:
3:15 Register the sons of Levi by their ancestral houses, by their divisions, by their families. You shall register every male from one month old and upward.
3:16 And Moses and Aaron registered them by the voice of the Lord, as the Lord instructed them.
3:17 And these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
3:18 And these are the names of Gershon’s sons by their divisions: Libni and Shimei.
3:19 And the sons of Kohath by their divisions: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.
3:20 And the sons of Merari by their divisions: Mahli and Mushi. These are the divisions of the Levites by their ancestral houses.
3:21 To Gershon belonged the division of Libni and the division of Shimei. These are the divisions of Gershon.
3:22 Those registered, counting every male from one month old and upward, numbered seven thousand five hundred.
3:23 And the sons of Gershon shall camp behind the Tent, toward the sea.
3:24 And the ruler of the ancestral house of the division of Gershon is Eliasaph son of Lael.
3:25 And the charge of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Testimony is the Tent, the covering, and the covering at the door of the Tent of Testimony,
3:26 and the hangings of the court, the curtain at the gate of the court by the Tent, and the remaining things for all its work.
3:27 To Kohath belonged the Amramite, Izharite, Hebronite, and Uzzielite divisions. These are the divisions of Kohath.
3:28 Counting every male from one month old and upward, there were eight thousand six hundred, keeping charge of the holy things.
3:29 The divisions of the sons of Kohath shall camp beside the Tent toward the southwest.
3:30 And the ruler of the ancestral house of the divisions of Kohath is Elizaphan son of Uzziel.
3:31 And their charge is the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the holy utensils with which they minister, the covering, and all their work.
3:32 And the ruler over the rulers of the Levites is Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, appointed to keep charge of the holy things.
3:33 To Merari belonged the division of Mahli and the division of Mushi. These are the divisions of Merari.
3:34 Those registered, counting every male from one month old and upward, numbered six thousand fifty.
3:35 And the ruler of the ancestral house of the division of Merari is Zuriel son of Abihail. They shall camp beside the Tent toward the north.
3:36 The appointed charge of the sons of Merari is the capitals of the Tent, its bars, pillars, and bases, all their furnishings, and their work,
3:37 and the pillars around the court, their bases, pegs, and cords.
3:38 And those camping before the Tent of Testimony on the east are Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charges of the sons of Israel. And the outsider who touches shall die.
3:39 All those registered among the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord by their divisions, every male from one month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.
3:40 And the Lord said unto Moses, saying: Register every firstborn male among the sons of Israel from one month old and upward, and take their number by name.
3:41 And you shall take the Levites for Me, I am the Lord, in place of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the sons of Israel.
3:42 And Moses registered every firstborn among the sons of Israel, as the Lord commanded.
3:43 And all the firstborn males, counted by name from one month old and upward, numbered twenty-two thousand two hundred seventy-three.
3:44 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
3:45 Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the sons of Israel, and the Levites’ livestock in place of their livestock. The Levites shall be Mine. I am the Lord.
3:46 And for the ransom of the two hundred seventy-three firstborn sons of Israel who exceed the number of Levites,
3:47 you shall take five shekels head by head; you shall take them according to the sacred didrachma, twenty obols to the shekel.
3:48 And you shall give the silver unto Aaron and unto his sons, as the ransom of those who exceed their number.
3:49 And Moses took the silver, the ransom of those in excess, for the redemption of the Levites.
3:50 From the firstborn of the sons of Israel he took the silver: one thousand three hundred sixty-five sanctuary shekels.
3:51 And Moses gave the ransom of those in excess unto Aaron and his sons by the voice of the Lord, as the Lord instructed Moses.

Editorial note: The Levite divisions and firstborn redemption remain. Numerical discrepancies, including 3:34 and the total in 3:39, have not been silently harmonized. Specialist review of these figures remains pending.

Numbers 4

4:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:
4:2 Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, by their divisions, by their ancestral houses,
4:3 from twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering to minister and do all the work in the Tent of Testimony.
4:4 And this is the work of the sons of Kohath in the Tent of Testimony: the most holy things.
4:5 And when the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall enter, and take down the overshadowing veil, and cover the ark of testimony with it.
4:6 And they shall put over it a covering of blue skin, lay a cloth entirely of blue above it, and insert the carrying poles.
4:7 And over the table set forth they shall spread a cloth entirely of purple, and the dishes, and the incense vessels, and the cups, and the vessels wherein he pours the drink offerings; and the continual loaves shall be upon it.
4:8 And they shall spread a scarlet cloth over it, cover it with a covering of blue leather, and insert its carrying poles through it.
4:9 And they shall take a blue cloth and cover the illuminating lampstand, its lamps, tongs, snuffers, and all the oil vessels used in ministering with them.
4:10 And they shall put it and all its furnishings into a covering of blue leather and place it on carrying poles.
4:11 And over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, cover it with a covering of blue leather, and insert its carrying poles.
4:12 And they shall take all the ministering utensils used in the holy place, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with a covering of blue leather, and place them on carrying poles.
4:13 And he shall put the cover upon the altar, and they shall spread a cloth entirely of purple over it.
4:14 And they shall put on it all the utensils used in its ministry: the firepans, meat forks, bowls, the cover, and all the altar utensils. They shall spread a covering of blue leather over it and insert its carrying poles. They shall take a purple cloth, cover the basin and its stand together, put them into a covering of blue leather, and place them on carrying poles.
4:15 And when the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy things and all the holy utensils. Afterward the sons of Kohath shall enter to carry them, and they shall not touch the holy things, so that they do not die. These are what the sons of Kohath shall carry in the Tent of Testimony.
4:16 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is overseer of the oil for light, the compounded incense, the daily sacrifice, and the anointing oil: oversight of the whole Tent and everything in it, in the holy place, in all the work.
4:17 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:
4:18 Do not destroy the division of Kohath’s tribe from among the Levites.
4:19 Do this for them, and they shall live and shall in no wise die when they approach the most holy things: let Aaron and his sons approach, and they shall appoint each man according to his burden.
4:20 And they shall in no wise enter to behold the holy things suddenly, lest they die.
4:21 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
4:22 Take the sum of the sons of Gershon also, by their ancestral houses, by their divisions.
4:23 Register them from twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering to minister and perform his work in the Tent of Testimony.
4:24 This is the ministry of the division of Gershon, in ministering and carrying:
4:25 And it shall carry the skin curtains of the Tent, the Tent of Testimony, its covering, the blue covering above it, and the covering at the door of the Tent of Testimony,
4:26 And the court hangings by the Tent of Testimony, the remaining things, and all the ministering utensils used with them. They shall do the work.
4:27 The ministry of the sons of Gershon shall be at the mouth of Aaron and his sons, in all their ministries and all that they carry. And you shall register them by name, with all that they carry.
4:28 This is the ministry of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Testimony. Their charge is under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
4:29 You shall register the sons of Merari by their divisions, by their ancestral houses.
4:30 You shall register them from twenty-five years old and upward unto fifty years old, every one who enters to minister in the work of the Tent of Testimony.
4:31 And these are the things entrusted to their carrying for all their work in the Tent of Testimony: the capitals of the Tent, its bars, pillars, and bases, the covering, their bases and pillars, and the covering at the door of the Tent,
4:32 And the pillars around the court and their bases, the pillars of the curtain at the court gate and their bases, their pegs and cords, all their utensils, and all their ministries. Register them by name, with all the utensils entrusted to their carrying.
4:33 This is the ministry of the division of the sons of Merari, all their work in the Tent of Testimony, under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
4:34 And Moses, Aaron, and the rulers of Israel registered the sons of Kohath by their divisions and ancestral houses,
4:35 From twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering to minister and work in the Tent of Testimony.
4:36 And their enrolment by their divisions was two thousand seven hundred fifty.
4:37 This is the enrolment of the division of Kohath, everyone ministering in the Tent of Testimony, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord through Moses.
4:38 And the sons of Gershon were registered by their divisions and ancestral houses,
4:39 From twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering to minister and do the work in the Tent of Testimony.
4:40 And their enrolment by their divisions and ancestral houses was two thousand six hundred thirty.
4:41 This is the enrolment of the division of the sons of Gershon, everyone ministering in the Tent of Testimony, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord through Moses.
4:42 The division of the sons of Merari was also registered by their divisions and ancestral houses,
4:43 From twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering to minister in the work of the Tent of Testimony.
4:44 And their enrolment by their divisions and ancestral houses was three thousand two hundred.
4:45 This is the enrolment of the division of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron registered by the voice of the Lord through Moses.
4:46 All those registered among the Levites by Moses, Aaron, and the rulers of Israel, by their divisions and ancestral houses,
4:47 From twenty-five years old and upward, up to fifty years old, everyone entering for the work of service and the work of carrying in the Tent of Testimony,
4:48 And those registered numbered eight thousand five hundred eighty.
4:49 By the voice of the Lord, through Moses, each man was appointed to his work and what he carried. They were registered as the Lord instructed Moses.

Editorial note: The age limits, objects, coverings, ritual agents and totals remain. The negative purpose clause in 4:15 and the prohibition in 4:18 have clearer English grammar.

Numbers 5

5:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
5:2 Command the sons of Israel to send out of the camp everyone with leprosy, everyone with a seminal discharge, and everyone unclean through a dead person.
5:3 From male unto female, send them outside the camp, and they shall in no wise defile their camps, wherein I dwell among them.
5:4 And the sons of Israel did so and sent them outside the camp. As the Lord spoke unto Moses, so the sons of Israel did.
5:5 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
5:6 Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying: If a man or woman commits any of the sins of men, and acts with utter disregard, and that soul incurs guilt,
5:7 that person shall confess the sin committed, and repay the principal of the offence, and add a fifth part thereto, and give it unto the one who was wronged.
5:8 But if the man has no next of kin to receive repayment for the offence, the repayment made unto the Lord shall be the priest’s, besides the ram of atonement, wherewith he shall make atonement for him.
5:9 And every first portion from all the things consecrated among the sons of Israel that they bring unto the Lord, unto the priest, shall belong to him.
5:10 And each man’s consecrated things shall be his; whatsoever a man gives unto the priest shall be his.
5:11 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
5:12 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: If any man’s wife transgresses and utterly disregards him,
5:13 and anyone lies with her with an emission of semen, and it escapes the eyes of her husband, and she conceals it, though she is defiled, and there is no witness concerning her, and she is not caught,
5:14 and a spirit of jealousy comes upon him, and he is jealous of his wife, and she is defiled; or a spirit of jealousy comes upon him, and he is jealous of his wife, and she is not defiled:
5:15 then the man shall bring his wife unto the priest, and shall present the gift on her behalf, a tenth of an oiphi of barley flour. He shall pour no oil upon it, neither shall he put frankincense upon it; for it is a sacrifice of jealousy, a memorial sacrifice bringing sin to remembrance.
5:16 And the priest shall bring her near and stand her before the Lord.
5:17 And the priest shall take clean, living water in an earthenware vessel. He shall take some earth from the floor of the Tent of Testimony and put it into the water.
5:18 And the priest shall stand the woman before the Lord, uncover her head, and put the memorial sacrifice—the sacrifice of jealousy—on her hands. In the priest’s hand shall be this curse-bringing water of testing.
5:19 And the priest shall put her under oath, and say unto the woman: If no one has lain with you, and you have not transgressed into defilement while under your own husband, be unharmed by this curse-bringing water of testing.
5:20 But if you have transgressed while under a husband, or have become defiled, and someone other than your husband has lain with you:
5:21 then the priest shall put the woman under the oaths of this curse, and the priest shall say unto the woman: The Lord make you a curse and an oath among your people, when the Lord makes your thigh collapse and your belly swell;
5:22 and this curse-bringing water shall enter your belly, to make your belly swell and your thigh collapse. And the woman shall say: So be it, so be it.
5:23 And the priest shall write these curses in a scroll and wipe them off into the curse-bringing water of testing.
5:24 And he shall make the woman drink the curse-bringing water of testing, and the curse-bringing water of testing shall enter her.
5:25 And the priest shall take the sacrifice of jealousy from the woman’s hand, place the sacrifice before the Lord, and bring it near the altar.
5:26 And the priest shall take a handful from the sacrifice as its memorial portion and offer it up on the altar. Afterward he shall make the woman drink the water.
5:27 And it shall be, if she is defiled and has concealed it from her husband, that the curse-bringing water of testing shall enter her, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall collapse, and the woman shall become a curse among her people.
5:28 But if the woman is not defiled, and is clean, she shall be unharmed, and shall bring forth seed.
5:29 This is the law of jealousy, when a wife transgresses while under a husband and becomes defiled,
5:30 or when a spirit of jealousy comes upon a man and he is jealous of his wife. He shall set his wife before the Lord, and the priest shall perform all this law for her.
5:31 And the man shall be free from sin, and that woman shall bear her sin.

Editorial note: The distinctions among uncleanness, restitution, accusation and testing remain. Conditional clauses use ordinary present-tense English. The bodily language is retained without substituting a modern medical interpretation.

Numbers 6

6:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
6:2 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: If a man or woman solemnly vows a vow of consecration unto the Lord, to be set apart
6:3 from wine and strong drink, he shall be purified from wine. He shall drink neither vinegar of wine, nor vinegar of strong drink, nor anything prepared from grapes; and he shall eat neither fresh grapes nor raisins.
6:4 All the days of his vow he shall consume nothing made from the vine: wine, from pressed grape skins unto the seed.
6:5 All the days of the vow of purification no razor shall come upon his head. Until the days which he vowed unto the Lord are fulfilled, he shall be holy, letting the hair of his head grow long.
6:6 All the days of his vow unto the Lord he shall not enter where any dead person is.
6:7 For his father, or his mother, or his brother, or his sister, he shall not defile himself when they die; for the vow of his God is upon him, upon his head.
6:8 All the days of his vow he shall be holy unto the Lord.
6:9 But if anyone dies suddenly beside him, the head of his vow shall be defiled forthwith. He shall shave his head on the day when he is cleansed; on the seventh day it shall be shaved.
6:10 And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons unto the priest, at the doors of the Tent of Testimony.
6:11 And the priest shall prepare one for sin and one for a whole burnt offering. And the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin in connection with the dead person, and shall consecrate his head on that day,
6:12 whereon he was consecrated unto the Lord for the days of the vow. And he shall bring a year-old lamb for a guilt offering; and the former days shall not be reckoned, for the head of his vow was defiled.

Numbers 19

19:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses and Aaron, saying:

19:4 And Eleazar shall take some of its blood and sprinkle some of its blood seven times toward the front of the Tent of Testimony.
19:5 And they shall burn it before him; its skin, flesh, and blood, together with its dung, shall be burned.
19:6 And the priest shall take cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet material, and they shall throw them into the burning heifer.
19:7 And the priest shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. Afterward he may enter the camp, but the priest shall be unclean until evening.
19:8 And the one who burns it shall wash his clothes and bathe his body, and he shall be unclean until evening.
19:9 And a clean person shall gather the heifer's ashes and deposit them outside the camp in a clean place. They shall be kept for the congregation of the sons of Israel as water of sprinkling; it is a means of purification.
19:10 And the one who gathers the heifer's ashes shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening. This shall be an everlasting ordinance for the sons of Israel and the attached sojourners.
19:11 Whosoever touches a dead person, any human being who has died, shall be unclean for seven days.
19:12 That person shall be purified on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall be clean. If he is not purified on the third day and on the seventh day, he shall not be clean.
19:13 Whosoever touches the dead body of any human being who has died, and is not purified, has defiled the Tent of the Lord; that soul shall be wiped out from Israel. Because water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him, he is unclean; his uncleanness is still in him.
19:14 And this is the law: If a person dies in a house, everyone who enters the house and everything in the house shall be unclean for seven days.
19:15 And every open vessel without a fastening tied upon it is unclean.
19:16 And anyone who touches, on the open plain, one slain, a dead person, a human bone, or a tomb shall be unclean for seven days.
19:17 And for the unclean person they shall take some ashes from what was burned for purification, and they shall pour living water over them into a vessel.
19:18 And a clean man shall take hyssop, dip it in the water, and sprinkle it around the house, the vessels, and the people who were there, and on whoever touched the human bone, the slain person, the dead person, or the tomb.
19:19 And the clean person shall sprinkle the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day. He shall be purified on the seventh day, wash his clothes, and bathe in water, and he shall be unclean until evening.
19:20 And if a man is defiled, and is not purified, that soul shall be destroyed from the midst of the congregation, because he has defiled the holy things of the Lord; because water of sprinkling was not sprinkled upon him, he is unclean.
19:21 And this shall be an everlasting ordinance for you. The one who sprinkles the water of sprinkling shall wash his clothes, and whosoever touches the water of sprinkling shall be unclean until evening.
19:22 And whatever the unclean person touches shall be unclean, and the person who touches it shall be unclean until evening.

Editorial note: The ritual agents, ashes, living water, contact rules and third/seventh-day sequence remain. The inherited statement in 19:19 is retained without silently reconciling it with another reading.

Numbers 35

35:1 And the Lord spoke unto Moses on the western side of Moab, beside the Jordan opposite Jericho, saying:
35:2 Instruct the sons of Israel to give the Levites towns to live in from their allotted possessions. They shall also give the Levites the outlying lands around the towns.
35:3 And the towns shall be theirs to live in, and their designated lands shall be for their livestock and all their four-footed animals.
35:4 And the lands adjoining the towns that you shall give the Levites shall extend two thousand cubits outward from the town wall all around.
35:5 And you shall measure outside the town two thousand cubits on the eastern side, and two thousand cubits on the southwestern side, and two thousand cubits on the seaward side, and two thousand cubits on the northern side; and the town shall be in the midst thereof for you, together with the adjoining lands of the towns.
35:6 And you shall give the Levites the six cities of refuge that you shall provide for the person who has killed someone to flee to, and in addition to these, forty-two towns.
35:7 All the towns you shall give the Levites shall be forty-eight towns, together with their outlying lands.
35:8 And for the towns that you shall give from the possession of the sons of Israel, take many from those with much and fewer from those with less. Each shall give towns to the Levites according to the inheritance he receives.
35:9 And the Lord spoke unto Moses, saying:
35:10 Speak unto the sons of Israel, and you shall say unto them: You are crossing the Jordan into the land of Canaan,
35:11 and you shall set apart towns for yourselves. They shall be refuges for the killer to flee there, anyone who has struck a person unintentionally.
35:12 And the towns shall be refuges for you from the kinsman who avenges blood, so that the killer shall in no wise die before standing before the congregation for judgment.
35:13 And the towns you shall provide, the six towns, shall be refuges for you.
35:14 You shall provide three towns across the Jordan, and you shall provide three towns in the land of Canaan.
35:15 There shall be a refuge for the sons of Israel; for the sojourner and the resident stranger among you, these towns shall also be refuges, so that anyone who strikes a person unintentionally may flee there.
35:16 But if he strikes someone with an iron object and that person dies, he is a murderer. Let the murderer surely be put to death.
35:17 If he strikes someone with a stone from his hand capable of causing death and that person dies, he is a murderer. Let the murderer surely be put to death.
35:18 If he strikes someone with a wooden object from his hand capable of causing death and that person dies, he is a murderer. Let the murderer surely be put to death.
35:19 The kinsman who avenges blood shall himself kill the murderer. When he encounters him, he shall kill him.
35:20 If he pushes someone out of enmity and throws an object at him from ambush and that person dies,
35:21 or strikes him with his hand out of wrath and he dies, let the one who struck him surely be put to death; he is a murderer. Let the murderer surely be put to death. The kinsman who avenges blood shall kill the murderer when he encounters him.
35:22 But if he suddenly pushes someone without enmity, or throws any object at him without ambush,
35:23 or, without his knowing, a stone capable of causing death falls upon him and he dies, though he was not his enemy and was not seeking to harm him,
35:24 And then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the kinsman who avenges blood according to these judgments.
35:25 And the congregation shall rescue the killer from the kinsman who avenges blood and restore him to the city of refuge to which he fled. He shall live there until the high priest dies, whom they anointed with the holy oil.
35:26 But if the killer goes outside the boundaries of the city to which he fled,
35:27 and the kinsman who avenges blood finds him outside the boundaries of his city of refuge and kills the killer, the avenger is not liable.
35:28 For he must live in the city of refuge until the high priest dies. After the death of the high priest, the killer may return to the land of his possession.
35:29 And these shall be an ordinance of judgment for you throughout your generations in all your settlements.
35:30 In every case of a person being struck down, you shall put the killer to death through witnesses. A single witness shall not testify against a person so as to cause his death.
35:31 And you shall not accept a ransom for a life from the killer who is guilty and liable to be killed; he shall surely be put to death.
35:32 You shall not accept a ransom concerning flight to a city of refuge, allowing him to live in the land again before the high priest dies.
35:33 And you shall in no wise stain with murder the land wherein you dwell; for this blood stains the land with murder, and atonement shall not be made for the land for the blood shed upon it, except by the blood of him who shed it.
35:34 And you shall not defile the land in which you dwell, in which I will dwell among you, for I am the Lord, dwelling among the sons of Israel.

Editorial note: The distinctions among unintentional killing, murder, refuge, witnesses and ransom remain. Conditional verbs now use ordinary present-tense English. The inherited distances and high-priest provisions remain.
