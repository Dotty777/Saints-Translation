## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize ordinary address and has; smooth the cargo-lightening purpose and the sequence of origin questions; render righteous blood as innocent blood in the sailors’ plea, keeping the blood image. Preserve Thou/hast/didst in their direct prayer, repeated Tarshish and from the Lord’s presence, equipment, sleeping/snoring, lot/calamity repetitions, servant of the Lord, worship, repeated surges, great fear, sacrifice and vows.

Pending: Review the ship officer’s title, each with their god, the hollow of the ship and the grammar of the cargo-lightening purpose. Innocent blood states the sailors’ plea and does not adjudicate Jonah’s entire character. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Use attributive my for Jonah’s affliction and thanksgiving with praise. Retain Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine in direct address to God, all eleven source-numbered units, commanded sea creature and three days/three nights, belly of Hades, rivers/billows/waves, question about the sanctuary, mountain clefts, everlasting restraints, the imperative let at 2:7 and may at 2:8, past abandoned, and the difficult final deliverance clause.

Pending: Review the 2:5 question’s force, life/ruin syntax at 2:6–7 and the unexpanded deliverance-to-the-Lord phrase at 2:10. Do not convert the requests into accomplished rescue, identify a modern sea-creature species, or replace the difficult ending with a familiar translation. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize ordinary you and knows and smooth the three-day journey phrase. Retain the former proclamation, great before God, the accepted Three more days announcement and shall, separate proclaimed/said verbs, greatest/least, king/nobles, people and livestock/cattle/sheep, narrated sackcloth and turning, deeds, God’s relenting and the final He did not do it.

Pending: Review the city’s journey measure and the singular each/his with plural their hands at 3:8. The selected Greek says three days in 3:4; forty days remains a comparison reading. Relenting describes the announced action without adding confession-of-sin language. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize address to Jonah as Are you/You and attributive my own land; smooth did not toil. Preserve Thou art in direct prayer to God, repeated great grief/great joy, merciful and compassionate, patient and abundant in mercy, Master Lord, repeated preference for death, shelter/gourd/worm, commanded and struck repetitions, burning/scorching wind, night/night, spare/spare, more than 120,000, right/left, livestock and the final question.

Pending: Review the force of grief versus anger, botanical identification of the gourd, and the spare/concern vocabulary. No diagnosis of Jonah, modern plant species or identification of the inhabitants as infants is supplied. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
