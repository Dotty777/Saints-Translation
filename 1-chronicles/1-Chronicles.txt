1 CHRONICLES 1

1:1 Adam, Seth, Enosh,
1:2 Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,
1:3 Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,
1:4 Noah. Noah’s sons: Shem, Ham, Japheth.
1:5 Japheth’s sons: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elishah, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
1:6 Gomer’s sons: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.
1:7 Javan’s sons: Elishah, Tharsis, the Kitians, and the Rhodians.
1:8 Ham’s sons: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.
1:9 Cush’s sons: Saba, Euilat, Sabatha, Regma, and Sebekatha. Regma’s sons: Saba and Oudadan.
1:10 Cush begot Nimrod. He began to be a giant hunter on the earth.
1:17 Shem’s sons: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad,
1:24 Sala,
1:25 Eber, Peleg, Ragau,
1:26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,
1:27 Abraham.
1:28 Abraham’s sons: Isaac and Ishmael.
1:29 These are the generations of Ishmael’s firstborn: Nabaioth, Kedar, Nabdeel, Mabsan,
1:30 Masma, Idouma, Masse, Choddad, Thaiman,
1:31 Jettour, Naphes, and Kedma. These are Ishmael’s sons.
1:32 The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore him Zembran, Jexan, Madan, Midian, Sobak, and Soe. Jexan’s sons: Saba and Daidan.
1:33 Midian’s sons: Gaipha, Opher, Enoch, Abida, and Eldaa. All these were Keturah’s sons.
1:34 Abraham begot Isaac. Isaac’s sons: Esau and Jacob.
1:35 Esau’s sons: Eliphaz, Ragouel, Jeoul, Jeglom, and Kore.
1:36 Eliphaz’s sons: Teman, Omar, Sophar, Gootham, Kenaz, and, from Timna, Amalek.
1:37 Ragouel’s sons: Nacheth, Zare, Some, and Moze.
1:38 Seir’s sons: Lotan, Sobal, Sebegon, Ana, Deson, Osar, and Daison.
1:39 Lotan’s sons: Chorri, Aiman, Ailath, and Namna.
1:40 Sobal’s sons: Golam, Manachath, Gaibel, Sob, and Onam. Sebegon’s sons: Aia and Ana.
1:41 Ana’s sons: Daison. Deson’s sons: Emeron, Eseban, Jethran, and Charran.
1:42 Osar’s sons: Balaan, Zoukan, and Jokan. Daison’s sons: Os and Arran.
1:43 These are their kings: Balak son of Beor, whose city was named Dennaba.
1:44 Balak died, and Jobab son of Zara from Bosorra became king in his place.
1:45 Jobab died, and Asom from the land of the Thaimans became king in his place.
1:46 Asom died, and Adad son of Barad, who struck Midian in Moab’s plain, became king in his place. His city was named Geththaim.
1:47 Adad died, and Samaa from Masekka became king in his place.
1:48 Samaa died, and Saul from Rooboth beside the river became king in his place.
1:49 Saul died, and Balaennon son of Achobor became king in his place.
1:50 Balaennon son of Achobor died, and Adad son of Barad became king in his place. His city was named Phogor.
1:51 Adad died. Edom’s chiefs were Chief Thamana, Chief Gola, Chief Jetheth,
1:52 Chief Elibamas, Chief Elas, Chief Phinon,
1:53 Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mabsar,
1:54 Chief Megediel, Chief Heram. These were Edom’s chiefs.

1 CHRONICLES 2

2:1 These are the names of Israel’s sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,
2:2 Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.
2:3 Judah’s sons: Er, Onan, Shelon, three. They were born to him by Shua’s daughter, the Canaanite woman. Er, Judah’s firstborn, was evil before the Lord, and He killed him.
2:4 Tamar his daughter-in-law bore him Phares and Zara. Judah’s sons were five in all.
2:5 Phares’s sons: Arson and Jemouel.
2:6 Zara’s sons: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Chalchal, and Dara, five in all.
2:7 Charmi’s sons: Achar, Israel’s obstructer, who broke faith concerning the devoted thing.
2:8 Ethan’s sons: Azariah.
2:9 Eseron’s sons who were born to him: Jirameel, Ram, Caleb, and Aram.
2:10 Aram begot Amminadab, and Amminadab begot Naasson, ruler of Judah’s house,
2:11 Naasson begot Salmon, and Salmon begot Boaz,
2:12 Boaz begot Obed, and Obed begot Jesse,
2:13 and Jesse begot his firstborn Eliab; Amminadab was the second, Samaa the third,
2:14 Nathanael the fourth, Raddai the fifth,
2:15 Asom the sixth, David the seventh.
2:16 Their sister: Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah’s sons were Abessa, Joab, and Asahel, three.
2:17 Abigail bore Amessa. Amessa’s father was Jothor the Ishmaelite.
2:18 Caleb son of Eseron begot Gazouba, a wife, and Jerioth. These were her sons: Joasar, Sobab, and Orna.
2:19 Gazouba died, and Caleb took Ephrath for himself. She bore him Or,
2:20 and Or begot Uri, and Uri begot Bezalel.
2:21 After these things Eseron went in to the daughter of Machir, Galaad’s father. He took her when he was sixty years old, and she bore him Segub.
2:22 Segub begot Jair. He had twenty-three cities in Galaad.
2:23 Gedsour and Aram took Jair’s villages from them, Kanath and its villages, sixty cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir, Galaad’s father.
2:24 After Eseron died, Caleb came to Ephratha. Eseron’s wife Abia bore him Aschod, Thekoe’s father.
2:25 The sons of Jeremeel, Eseron’s firstborn, were Ram the firstborn, Baana, Aran, and Asom his brother.
2:26 Jeremeel had another wife, whose name was Atara. She was Ozom’s mother.
2:27 The sons of Ram, Jeremeel’s firstborn, were Maas, Jamin, and Akor.
2:28 Ozom’s sons were Samai and Jadae. Samai’s sons were Nadab and Abisour.
2:29 Abisour’s wife was named Abichail. She bore him Achabar and Molid.
2:30 Nadab’s sons: Salad and Apphaim. Salad died without children.
2:31 Apphaim’s sons: Isemiel. Isemiel’s sons: Sosan. Sosan’s sons: Achlai.
2:32 Jadae’s sons: Achisamai, Jether, Jonathan. Jether died without children.
2:33 Jonathan’s sons: Phaleth and Ozaza. These were Jeremeel’s sons.
2:34 Sosan had no sons, only daughters. Sosan had an Egyptian servant named Jochel,
2:35 and Sosan gave his daughter to his servant Jochel as a wife, and she bore him Eththi.
2:36 Eththi begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabed,
2:37 and Zabed begot Aphalel, and Aphalel begot Obed,
2:38 and Obed begot Jehu, and Jehu begot Azariah,
2:39 and Azariah begot Chelles, and Chelles begot Eleasa,
2:40 and Eleasa begot Sosomai, and Sosomai begot Salloum,
2:41 and Salloum begot Jechemias, and Jechemias begot Elisama.
2:42 The sons of Caleb, Jeremeel’s brother: Marisa his firstborn, who was Ziph’s father, and the sons of Marisa, Hebron’s father.
2:43 Hebron’s sons: Kore, Thapous, Rekom, and Semaa.
2:44 Semaa begot Raem, Jerkaan’s father, and Jerkaan begot Samai,
2:45 and his son was Maon, and Maon was Baithsour’s father.
2:46 Gaipha, Caleb’s concubine, bore Arran, Mosa, and Gezoue. Arran begot Gezoue.
2:47 Jadai’s sons: Ragem, Jotham, Gersom, Phalet, Gaipha, and Sagaph.
2:48 Caleb’s concubine Mocha bore Saber and Tharchna.
2:49 She bore Sagaph, Marmena’s father, and Saou, Machabena’s father and Gaibaa’s father. Caleb’s daughter was Ascha.
2:50 These were Caleb’s sons. The sons of Or, Ephratha’s firstborn: Sobal, Kariathiarim’s father,
2:51 Solomon, Bethlehem’s father, and Arim, Baithgedor’s father.
2:52 Sobal, Kariathiarim’s father, had sons: Araa, Esi, Ammanith,
2:53 Emospheos, the city of Jair, Aithalim, Miphithim, Hesamathim, and Hemasaraim. From these came the Sarathaians and the Esthaolaians.
2:54 Solomon’s sons: Bethlehem, Netophathi, Ataroth of Joab’s house, half the Manathi, Hesarei,
2:55 the families of scribes dwelling at Jabes, Thargathiim, Samathiim, Sokathiim. These were the Kinaians who came from Mesema, father of Rechab’s house.

1 CHRONICLES 3

3:2 the third, Absalom son of Mocha daughter of Tholmai king of Gedsour; the fourth, Adonijah son of Aggith;
3:3 the fifth, Saphatias, by Abital; the sixth, Jethraam, by his wife Agla.
3:4 Six were born to him in Hebron. He reigned there seven years and six months, and he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem.
3:5 These were born to him in Jerusalem: Samaa, Sobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bersabee daughter of Amiel,
3:6 and Ibaar, Elisama, and Eliphalet,
3:7 Nage, Naphag, and Janoue,
3:8 Elisama, Eliada, and Eliphalet, nine.
3:9 All these were David’s sons, except for the sons of the concubines; Themar was their sister.
3:10 Solomon’s sons: Rehoboam; Abia his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,
3:11 Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,
3:12 Amasias his son, Azariah his son, Joathan his son,
3:13 Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,
3:14 Amon his son, Josiah his son.
3:15 Josiah’s sons: Joanan the firstborn, Joakim the second, Sedekias the third, Salloum the fourth.
3:16 Joakim’s sons: Jechonias his son, Sedekias his son.
3:17 The sons of Jechonia-asir: Salathiel his son,
3:18 Melchiram, Phadaias, Sanesar, Jekemia, Osamo, and Denethi.
3:19 Salathiel’s sons: Zerubbabel and Semei. Zerubbabel’s sons: Mosollamos and Ananias, and Salomith their sister,
3:20 Asoube, Ool, Barachias, Asadias, and Asobaesd, five.
3:21 Ananias’s sons: Phalletia, and Isaiah his son, Raphaia his son, Orna his son, Abdia his son, Sechenia his son.
3:22 Sechenia’s son: Samaia. Samaia’s sons: Chattous, Joel, Mari, Noadia, and Saphat, six.
3:23 Noadia’s sons: Elithenan, Hezekiah, and Ezrikam, three.
3:24 Elithenan’s sons: Odouia, Eliasib, Phalaia, Akoun, Joanan, Dalaia, and Anani, seven.

1 CHRONICLES 4

4:1 Judah’s sons: Phares, Arson, Charmi, Or, Soubal,
4:2 and Raia his son. Soubal begot Jeth, and Jeth begot Achimi and Laad. These were the generations of the Sarathi.
4:3 These were Aitam’s sons: Jezrael, Ragma, and Jabas. Their sister’s name was Eselebbōn.
4:4 Phanouel was Gedor’s father, and Azer was Osan’s father. These were the sons of Or, Ephratha’s firstborn, Bethlehem’s father.
4:5 Saour, Thekoe’s father, had two wives, Aoda and Thoada.
4:6 Aoda bore him Ochazam, Hephad, Thaiman, and Astheran. All these were Aoda’s sons.
4:7 Thoada’s sons: Sareth, Saar, and Ethnan.
4:8 Kos begot Enob and Sabeba, and the generations of the brother of Rechab son of Jarim.
4:9 Igabes was more renowned than his brethren. His mother called his name Igabes, saying, “I bore as gabes.”
4:10 Igabes called upon the God of Israel, saying, “If Thou dost indeed bless me and enlarge my borders, and Thy hand is with me, Thou wilt bring about knowledge, so that I am not humbled.” And God brought about all that he asked.
4:11 Caleb, Ascha’s father, begot Machir, who was Assathon’s father.
4:12 Assathon begot Bathrephan, Phessee, and Thana, father of the city of Naas, brother of Eselon the Kenazite. These were the men of Repha.
4:13 Kenaz’s sons: Othniel and Saraia. Othniel’s sons: Athath.
4:14 Manathi begot Gophera. Saraia begot Joab, father of Ageaddair, for they were craftsmen.
4:15 The sons of Caleb son of Jephonne: Hera, Ala, and Noom. Ala’s sons: Kenaz.
4:16 His son: Geseel, Ameachi, Zapha, Zaira, and Eserael.
4:17 Esri’s sons: Jether, Morad, Apher, and Jalon. Jether begot Maron, Semai, and Mareth, Esthemon’s father.
4:18 His wife—she was Adia—bore Jared, Gedor’s father, Aber, Sochon’s father, and Jekthiel, Zano’s father. These were the sons of Gelia daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mored took.
4:19 The sons of the woman Idouia, Nachem’s sister. Dalia was Keila’s father, and Semeion was Joman’s father. The sons of Naem, Keila’s father: Agarmi and Esthemoe Machathi.
4:20 Semion’s sons: Amnon and Rana, Anan’s son, and Thilon. Isei’s sons: Zoath and Zoath’s sons.
4:21 The sons of Selom son of Judah: Er, Lecha’s father, Laada, Maresa’s father, and the families of houses of ephrath abak belonging to Esoba’s house,
4:22 Joakim, the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph, who dwelt in Moab; and he brought them back—abedirin athoukiin.
4:23 These were potters who dwelt in Nataim and Gadera. They grew strong with the king in his kingdom and dwelt there.
4:24 Simeon’s sons: Namouel, Jamin, Jarib, Zare, and Saul,
4:25 Salem his son, Mabasam his son, Masma his son,
4:26 Amouel his son, Saboud his son, Zakchour his son, Semei his son.
4:27 Semei had sixteen sons and three daughters, but their brethren did not have many sons. All their families did not multiply as much as Judah’s sons.
4:28 They dwelt in Beersheba, Sama, Molada, and Esersoual,
4:29 Balaa, Boasom, and Thoulad,
4:30 Bathouel, Erma, and Seklag,
4:31 Baithmarchaboth, half of Sosim, and the house of Baroumseorim. These were their cities until King David.
4:32 Their farmsteads were Aitam, Enremmon, Thokkan, and Aisan, five cities,
4:33 with all their farmsteads around these cities as far as Baal. This was their possession and their enrollment.
4:34 Mosobab, Jemoloch, and Josia son of Amasia,
4:35 Joel—and this one was Isabia’s son—son of Saraia, son of Asiel,
4:36 Elioenai, Jakaba, Jasouia, Asaia, Ediel, Ishmael, and Banaia,
4:37 Zouza son of Sephei son of Allon son of Jedia son of Samari son of Samaios.
4:38 These passed through under the names of rulers in their generations, and in their ancestral houses they multiplied into a multitude.
4:39 They went as far as the approach to Gerara, as far as the east of Gai, to seek pastures for their livestock.
4:40 They found rich, good pastures. The land was broad before them, with peace and quiet, for those who formerly dwelt there were from Ham’s sons.
4:41 These who are recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. They struck their houses and the Minaioi found there, devoted them to destruction to this day, and settled in their place, because there were pastures for their livestock there.
4:42 Some of them, from Simeon’s sons, went to Mount Seir, five hundred men, with Phalettia, Noadia, Raphaia, and Oziel, Jesi’s sons, as their rulers.
4:43 They struck those who remained of Amalek and dwelt there to this day.

1 CHRONICLES 5

5:1 The sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn—for he was the firstborn, but when he went up upon his father’s bed, he gave his blessing to his son Joseph, Israel’s son, and he was not entered in the genealogy for the firstborn rights.
5:2 For Judah was mighty in strength among his brethren, and from him came a leader, but the blessing belonged to Joseph.
5:3 The sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn: Enoch, Phallous, Arson, and Charmi.
5:4 Joel’s sons: Semei and Banaia his son. The sons of Goug, Semei’s son:
5:5 his son Micha, his son Recha, his son Baal,
5:6 his son Beera, whom Thaglathphalnasar king of Assour deported. He was ruler of the Reubenites.
5:7 His brethren by his ancestral family, in their enrollments according to their generations: the ruler Joel, Zacharia,
5:8 and Balek son of Ozouz son of Sama son of Joel. He dwelt in Aroer, and toward Nabau and Beelmaon,
5:9 and eastward he lived as far as the approaches of the wilderness from the River Euphrates, because their livestock were numerous in Galaad’s land.
5:10 In Saul’s days they made war against the resident aliens, and they fell into their hands, dwelling in tents as far as all the east of Galaad.
5:11 Gad’s sons lived opposite them in Basan as far as Selcha.
5:12 Joel was the firstborn, Sapham the second, and Jani the scribe in Basan.
5:13 Their brethren by their ancestral houses were Michael, Mosollam, Sebee, Joree, Jachan, Zoue, and Obed, seven.
5:14 These were the sons of Abichail son of Uri son of Jidai son of Galaad son of Michael son of Isai son of Jouri son of Zaboucham,
5:15 son of Abdiel son of Gouni, ruler of an ancestral house.
5:16 They dwelt in Galaad, in Basan and their villages, and throughout Sharon’s surrounding region as far as its outlet.
5:17 Their enrollment, all of them, was in the days of Joatham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.
5:18 The sons of Reuben and Gad and half Manasseh’s tribe had men of strength who carried shields and the sword, drew the bow, and were taught war: forty-four thousand seven hundred sixty who went out to the battle line.
5:19 They made war against the Hagarenes, Ituraeans, Naphisaeans, and Nadabaeans,
5:20 and they prevailed over them, and the Hagaraeans and all their tents were given into their hands. For they cried to God in the battle, and He listened to them because they hoped in Him.
5:21 They captured their baggage: five thousand camels, two hundred fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand human lives.
5:22 Many fell wounded, for the war was from God. They settled in their place until the deportation.
5:23 The half of Manasseh’s tribe dwelt in the land from Basan to Baalermon, Sanir, and Mount Aermon, and they multiplied in Lebanon.
5:24 These were the chiefs of their ancestral houses: Opher, Isei, Eliel, Esdriel, Jermia, Odouia, and Jediel, men mighty in strength, renowned men, rulers of their ancestral houses.
5:25 They broke faith with their fathers’ God and went whoring after the gods of the peoples of the land whom God had removed from before them.
5:26 Israel’s God stirred up the spirit of Phaloch king of Assour and the spirit of Thaglathphalnasar king of Assour. He deported Reuben, the Gaddite, and half Manasseh’s tribe, and brought them to Chalach, Chabor, and the River Gozan, to this day.
5:27 Levi’s sons: Gedson, Kaath, and Merari.
5:28 Kaath’s sons: Ambram, Issaar, Hebron, and Oziel.
5:29 Ambram’s sons: Aaron, Moses, and Mariam. Aaron’s sons: Nadab, Abioud, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
5:30 Eleazar begot Phinees, and Phinees begot Abisou.
5:31 Abisou begot Bokai, and Bokai begot Ozi.
5:32 Ozi begot Zaraia, and Zaraia begot Mariel.
5:33 Mariel begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Achitob.
5:34 Achitob begot Sadok, and Sadok begot Achimaas.
5:35 Achimaas begot Azariah, and Azariah begot Joanan.
5:36 Joanas begot Azariah, who served as priest in the house Solomon built in Jerusalem.
5:37 Azariah begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Achitob.
5:38 Achitob begot Sadok, and Sadok begot Salom.
5:39 Salom begot Chelkias, and Chelkias begot Azariah.
5:40 Azariah begot Saraias, and Saraias begot Josadak.
5:41 Josadak went in the deportation with Judah and Jerusalem by Nabouchodonosor’s hand.

1 CHRONICLES 6

6:1 Levi’s sons: Gedson, Kaath, and Merari.
6:2 These are the names of Gedson’s sons: Lobeni and Semei.
6:3 Kaath’s sons: Ambram, Issaar, Hebron, and Oziel.
6:4 Merari’s sons: Mooli and Omousi. These were Levi’s ancestral families according to their ancestral families:
6:5 For Gedson: for Lobeni his son, Jeeth his son, Zemma his son,
6:6 Joach his son, Addi his son, Zara his son, Jethri his son.
6:7 Kaath’s sons: Amminadab his son, Kore his son, Asir his son,
6:8 Elkanah his son, Abiasaph his son, Asir his son,

6:10 Elkanah’s sons: Amasi and Achimoth,
6:11 Elkanah his son, Souphi his son, and Naath his son,
6:12 Eliab his son, Jidaer his son, Elkanah his son.
6:13 Samuel’s sons: the firstborn, Sani, and Abia.
6:14 Merari’s sons: Mooli; Lobeni his son, Semei his son, Oza his son,
6:15 Somea his son, Aggia his son, Asaia his son.
6:16 These are those David appointed over the work of the singers in the Lord’s house when the ark came to rest,
6:17 and they ministered with instruments before the tent of the house of testimony until Solomon built the Lord’s house in Jerusalem. They took their places for their ministries according to their prescribed order.
6:18 These are those who took their places, with their sons, from Kaath’s sons: Heman the psalm singer, son of Joel son of Samuel,
6:19 son of Elkanah son of Edad son of Eliel son of Thie,
6:20 son of Souph son of Elkanah son of Meth son of Amasias,
6:21 son of Elkanah son of Joel son of Azariah son of Saphania,
6:22 son of Thaath son of Asir son of Abiasaph son of Kore,
6:23 son of Issaar son of Kaath son of Levi son of Israel.
6:24 His brother Asaph stood at his right: Asaph son of Barachia son of Samaa,
6:25 son of Michael son of Maasia son of Melchia,
6:26 son of Athani son of Zarai son of Adia,
6:27 son of Ethan son of Zamma son of Semei,
6:28 son of Hecha son of Gedson son of Levi.
6:29 The sons of Merari their brother were on the left: Ethan son of Kisai son of Abdi son of Maloch,
6:30 son of Asebi son of Amessia son of Chelkios,
6:31 son of Amasai son of Bani son of Semmer,
6:32 son of Mooli son of Mousi son of Merari son of Levi.
6:33 Their brethren, according to their ancestral houses, the Levites, were assigned to all the work of ministry of the tent of God’s house.
6:34 Aaron and his sons burned incense on the altar of whole burnt offerings and on the incense altar, for all the work of the Holy of Holies and to make atonement for Israel, according to everything Moses, God’s servant, commanded.
6:35 These were Aaron’s sons: Eleazar his son, Phinees his son, Abisou his son,
6:36 Bokai his son, Ozi his son, Zaraia his son,
6:37 Mariel his son, Amariah his son, Achitob his son,
6:38 Sadok his son, Achimaas his son.
6:39 These were their dwelling places in their villages within their boundaries, for Aaron’s sons of the Kaathi ancestral family, for the lot fell to them.
6:40 They gave them Hebron in Judah’s land and its surrounding lands all around it.
6:41 They gave the city’s plains and its villages to Caleb son of Jephonne.
6:42 To Aaron’s sons they gave the cities of refuge: Hebron, Lobna and its surrounding lands, Selna and its surrounding lands, and Esthamo and its surrounding lands,
6:43 Jeththar and its surrounding lands, Dabir and its surrounding lands,
6:44 Asan and its surrounding lands, Attan and its surrounding lands, Basamys and its surrounding lands,
6:45 and from Benjamin’s tribe, Gabee and its surrounding lands, Galemeth and its surrounding lands, Anchoch and its surrounding lands. All their cities were thirteen cities according to their ancestral families.
6:46 For Kaath’s remaining sons, from the ancestral families, from the tribe, from half Manasseh’s tribe, there were ten cities by lot.
6:47 For Gedson’s sons according to their ancestral families, from Issachar’s tribe, Asher’s tribe, Naphtali’s tribe, and Manasseh’s tribe in Basan, there were thirteen cities.
6:48 For Merari’s sons according to their ancestral families, from Reuben’s tribe, Gad’s tribe, and Zebulun’s tribe, there were twelve cities by lot.
6:49 Israel’s sons gave the Levites the cities and their surrounding lands,
6:50 and they gave by lot, from the tribe of Judah’s sons and the tribe of Simeon’s sons, these cities which he called by name.
6:51 Some of the ancestral families of Kaath’s sons had the cities of their boundaries from Ephraim’s tribe.
6:52 They gave him the cities of refuge: Sychem and its surrounding lands in Mount Ephraim, and Gazer and its surrounding lands,
6:53 Jekmaam and its surrounding lands, Baithoron and its surrounding lands,
6:54 Eglam and its surrounding lands, Gethremmon and its surrounding lands,
6:55 and from half Manasseh’s tribe, Anar and its surrounding lands, Jeblaam and its surrounding lands, according to the ancestral family of Kaath’s remaining sons.
6:56 For Gedson’s sons, from the ancestral families of half Manasseh’s tribe: Golan in Basan and its surrounding lands, Aseroth and its surrounding lands,

1 CHRONICLES 29

29:1 King David said to the whole assembly, “Solomon my son, the one whom the Lord has chosen, is young and tender, and the work is great, for the building is for the Lord God, not for a human being.
29:2 With all my strength I have prepared for my God’s house gold, silver, bronze, iron, timber, soom stones and filling stones, costly and variegated stones, every precious stone, and much Parian marble.
29:3 Moreover, because I delight in my God’s house, I have gold and silver that I have acquired for myself. Behold, I give it to my God’s house in addition, apart from what I have prepared for the house of holy things:
29:4 three thousand talents of gold from Souphir and seven thousand talents of tested silver to coat the sanctuary’s walls with them,
29:5 by the craftsmen’s hand. Who is eager to fill his hands today for the Lord?”
29:6 The ancestral rulers, the rulers of Israel’s sons, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, the overseers of the works, and the king’s stewards offered eagerly.
29:7 They gave for the works of the Lord’s house five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand gold coins, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and a hundred thousand talents of iron.
29:8 Those who had a stone gave it to the storerooms of the Lord’s house through Jiiel the Gersoni.
29:9 The people rejoiced because of their eagerness, for they offered eagerly to the Lord with a full heart. King David rejoiced greatly.
29:10 King David blessed the Lord before the assembly, saying, “Blessed art Thou, O Lord, the God of Israel, our Father, from everlasting to everlasting.
29:11 Thine, O Lord, are greatness, power, glory, victory, and strength; for Thou rulest all things in heaven and upon earth. Before Thee every king and nation is shaken.
29:12 From Thee come wealth and glory. Thou governest all, O Lord, Ruler of every rule. In Thy hand are strength and dominion; and it is in Thy hand, O Almighty, to make all things great and strong.
29:13 Now, O Lord, we give thanks unto Thee, and praise the name of Thy glory.
29:14 Who am I, and who are my people, that we had strength to offer willingly unto Thee in this manner? All things are Thine, and of Thine own have we given unto Thee.
29:15 For we are resident strangers before Thee, and sojourners, as all our fathers were: our days upon earth are as a shadow, and there is no endurance.

29:17 I know, Lord, that Thou examinest hearts and lovest righteousness. In simplicity of heart I eagerly offered all these things. Now I have seen Thy people found here offer eagerly to Thee in gladness.
29:18 Lord, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel our fathers, preserve these things for ever in the thought of Thy people’s heart, and direct their hearts toward Thee.

29:24 The rulers, the powerful men, and all the sons of King David his father submitted to him.

29:26 David son of Jessai reigned over Israel

29:28 He died in a good old age, full of days, wealth, and glory. Solomon his son reigned in his place.
