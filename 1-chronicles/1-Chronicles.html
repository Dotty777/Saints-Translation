1 Chronicles 1

Read this chapter on staging

From Adam to Noah

1:1Adam, Seth, Enosh,

1:2Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

1:3Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

1:4Noah. Noah’s sons: Shem, Ham, Japheth.

The Sons of Japheth

1:5Japheth’s sons: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Elishah, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

1:6Gomer’s sons: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

1:7Javan’s sons: Elishah, Tharsis, the Kitians, and the Rhodians.

The Sons of Ham

1:8Ham’s sons: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

1:9Cush’s sons: Saba, Euilat, Sabatha, Regma, and Sebekatha. Regma’s sons: Saba and Oudadan.

1:10Cush begot Nimrod. He began to be a giant hunter on the earth.

The Sons of Shem

1:17Shem’s sons: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad,

1:24Sala,

1:25Eber, Peleg, Ragau,

1:26Serug, Nahor, Terah,

1:27Abraham.

The Sons of Abraham

1:28Abraham’s sons: Isaac and Ishmael.

1:29These are the generations of Ishmael’s firstborn: Nabaioth, Kedar, Nabdeel, Mabsan,

1:30Masma, Idouma, Masse, Choddad, Thaiman,

1:31Jettour, Naphes, and Kedma. These are Ishmael’s sons.

1:32The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore him Zembran, Jexan, Madan, Midian, Sobak, and Soe. Jexan’s sons: Saba and Daidan.

1:33Midian’s sons: Gaipha, Opher, Enoch, Abida, and Eldaa. All these were Keturah’s sons.

Esau and Israel

1:34Abraham begot Isaac. Isaac’s sons: Esau and Jacob.

1:35Esau’s sons: Eliphaz, Ragouel, Jeoul, Jeglom, and Kore.

1:36Eliphaz’s sons: Teman, Omar, Sophar, Gootham, Kenaz, and, from Timna, Amalek.

1:37Ragouel’s sons: Nacheth, Zare, Some, and Moze.

The Sons of Seir

1:38Seir’s sons: Lotan, Sobal, Sebegon, Ana, Deson, Osar, and Daison.

1:39Lotan’s sons: Chorri, Aiman, Ailath, and Namna.

1:40Sobal’s sons: Golam, Manachath, Gaibel, Sob, and Onam. Sebegon’s sons: Aia and Ana.

1:41Ana’s sons: Daison. Deson’s sons: Emeron, Eseban, Jethran, and Charran.

1:42Osar’s sons: Balaan, Zoukan, and Jokan. Daison’s sons: Os and Arran.

The Kings of Edom

1:43These are their kings: Balak son of Beor, whose city was named Dennaba.

1:44Balak died, and Jobab son of Zara from Bosorra became king in his place.

1:45Jobab died, and Asom from the land of the Thaimans became king in his place.

1:46Asom died, and Adad son of Barad, who struck Midian in Moab’s plain, became king in his place. His city was named Geththaim.

1:47Adad died, and Samaa from Masekka became king in his place.

1:48Samaa died, and Saul from Rooboth beside the river became king in his place.

1:49Saul died, and Balaennon son of Achobor became king in his place.

1:50Balaennon son of Achobor died, and Adad son of Barad became king in his place. His city was named Phogor.

The Chiefs of Edom

1:51Adad died. Edom’s chiefs were Chief Thamana, Chief Gola, Chief Jetheth,

1:52Chief Elibamas, Chief Elas, Chief Phinon,

1:53Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mabsar,

1:54Chief Megediel, Chief Heram. These were Edom’s chiefs.

1 Chronicles 2

The Sons of Israel

2:1These are the names of Israel’s sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

2:2Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, Asher.

The Sons of Judah

2:3Judah’s sons: Er, Onan, Shelon, three. They were born to him by Shua’s daughter, the Canaanite woman. Er, Judah’s firstborn, was evil before the Lord, and He killed him.

2:4Tamar his daughter-in-law bore him Phares and Zara. Judah’s sons were five in all.

2:5Phares’s sons: Arson and Jemouel.

2:6Zara’s sons: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Chalchal, and Dara, five in all.

2:7Charmi’s sons: Achar, Israel’s obstructer, who broke faith concerning the devoted thing.

2:8Ethan’s sons: Azariah.

The House of Eseron

2:9Eseron’s sons who were born to him: Jirameel, Ram, Caleb, and Aram.

2:10Aram begot Amminadab, and Amminadab begot Naasson, ruler of Judah’s house,

2:11Naasson begot Salmon, and Salmon begot Boaz,

2:12Boaz begot Obed, and Obed begot Jesse,

The Sons of Jesse

2:13and Jesse begot his firstborn Eliab; Amminadab was the second, Samaa the third,

2:14Nathanael the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

2:15Asom the sixth, David the seventh.

2:16Their sister: Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah’s sons were Abessa, Joab, and Asahel, three.

2:17Abigail bore Amessa. Amessa’s father was Jothor the Ishmaelite.

The Sons of Caleb Son of Eseron

2:18Caleb son of Eseron begot Gazouba, a wife, and Jerioth. These were her sons: Joasar, Sobab, and Orna.

2:19Gazouba died, and Caleb took Ephrath for himself. She bore him Or,

2:20and Or begot Uri, and Uri begot Bezalel.

2:21After these things Eseron went in to the daughter of Machir, Galaad’s father. He took her when he was sixty years old, and she bore him Segub.

2:22Segub begot Jair. He had twenty-three cities in Galaad.

2:23Gedsour and Aram took Jair’s villages from them, Kanath and its villages, sixty cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir, Galaad’s father.

2:24After Eseron died, Caleb came to Ephratha. Eseron’s wife Abia bore him Aschod, Thekoe’s father.

The Sons of Jeremeel

2:25The sons of Jeremeel, Eseron’s firstborn, were Ram the firstborn, Baana, Aran, and Asom his brother.

2:26Jeremeel had another wife, whose name was Atara. She was Ozom’s mother.

2:27The sons of Ram, Jeremeel’s firstborn, were Maas, Jamin, and Akor.

2:28Ozom’s sons were Samai and Jadae. Samai’s sons were Nadab and Abisour.

2:29Abisour’s wife was named Abichail. She bore him Achabar and Molid.

2:30Nadab’s sons: Salad and Apphaim. Salad died without children.

2:31Apphaim’s sons: Isemiel. Isemiel’s sons: Sosan. Sosan’s sons: Achlai.

2:32Jadae’s sons: Achisamai, Jether, Jonathan. Jether died without children.

2:33Jonathan’s sons: Phaleth and Ozaza. These were Jeremeel’s sons.

2:34Sosan had no sons, only daughters. Sosan had an Egyptian servant named Jochel,

2:35and Sosan gave his daughter to his servant Jochel as a wife, and she bore him Eththi.

2:36Eththi begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabed,

2:37and Zabed begot Aphalel, and Aphalel begot Obed,

2:38and Obed begot Jehu, and Jehu begot Azariah,

2:39and Azariah begot Chelles, and Chelles begot Eleasa,

2:40and Eleasa begot Sosomai, and Sosomai begot Salloum,

2:41and Salloum begot Jechemias, and Jechemias begot Elisama.

The Sons of Caleb, Jeremeel's Brother

2:42The sons of Caleb, Jeremeel’s brother: Marisa his firstborn, who was Ziph’s father, and the sons of Marisa, Hebron’s father.

2:43Hebron’s sons: Kore, Thapous, Rekom, and Semaa.

2:44Semaa begot Raem, Jerkaan’s father, and Jerkaan begot Samai,

2:45and his son was Maon, and Maon was Baithsour’s father.

2:46Gaipha, Caleb’s concubine, bore Arran, Mosa, and Gezoue. Arran begot Gezoue.

2:47Jadai’s sons: Ragem, Jotham, Gersom, Phalet, Gaipha, and Sagaph.

2:48Caleb’s concubine Mocha bore Saber and Tharchna.

2:49She bore Sagaph, Marmena’s father, and Saou, Machabena’s father and Gaibaa’s father. Caleb’s daughter was Ascha.

The Sons of Or

2:50These were Caleb’s sons. The sons of Or, Ephratha’s firstborn: Sobal, Kariathiarim’s father,

2:51Solomon, Bethlehem’s father, and Arim, Baithgedor’s father.

2:52Sobal, Kariathiarim’s father, had sons: Araa, Esi, Ammanith,

2:53Emospheos, the city of Jair, Aithalim, Miphithim, Hesamathim, and Hemasaraim. From these came the Sarathaians and the Esthaolaians.

2:54Solomon’s sons: Bethlehem, Netophathi, Ataroth of Joab’s house, half the Manathi, Hesarei,

2:55the families of scribes dwelling at Jabes, Thargathiim, Samathiim, Sokathiim. These were the Kinaians who came from Mesema, father of Rechab’s house.

The Sons of Judah

4:1Judah’s sons: Phares, Arson, Charmi, Or, Soubal,

4:2and Raia his son. Soubal begot Jeth, and Jeth begot Achimi and Laad. These were the generations of the Sarathi.

4:3These were Aitam’s sons: Jezrael, Ragma, and Jabas. Their sister’s name was Eselebbōn.

4:4Phanouel was Gedor’s father, and Azer was Osan’s father. These were the sons of Or, Ephratha’s firstborn, Bethlehem’s father.

4:5Saour, Thekoe’s father, had two wives, Aoda and Thoada.

4:6Aoda bore him Ochazam, Hephad, Thaiman, and Astheran. All these were Aoda’s sons.

4:7Thoada’s sons: Sareth, Saar, and Ethnan.

4:8Kos begot Enob and Sabeba, and the generations of the brother of Rechab son of Jarim.

The Prayer of Igabes

4:9Igabes was more renowned than his brethren. His mother called his name Igabes, saying, “I bore as gabes.” †

4:10Igabes called upon the God of Israel, saying, “If Thou dost indeed bless me and enlarge my borders, and Thy hand is with me, Thou wilt bring about knowledge, so that I am not humbled.” And God brought about all that he asked. †

The Generations of Caleb and Kenaz

4:11Caleb, Ascha’s father, begot Machir, who was Assathon’s father.

4:12Assathon begot Bathrephan, Phessee, and Thana, father of the city of Naas, brother of Eselon the Kenazite. These were the men of Repha.

4:13Kenaz’s sons: Othniel and Saraia. Othniel’s sons: Athath.

4:14Manathi begot Gophera. Saraia begot Joab, father of Ageaddair, for they were craftsmen.

4:15The sons of Caleb son of Jephonne: Hera, Ala, and Noom. Ala’s sons: Kenaz.

4:16His son: Geseel, Ameachi, Zapha, Zaira, and Eserael.

4:17Esri’s sons: Jether, Morad, Apher, and Jalon. Jether begot Maron, Semai, and Mareth, Esthemon’s father.

4:18His wife—she was Adia—bore Jared, Gedor’s father, Aber, Sochon’s father, and Jekthiel, Zano’s father. These were the sons of Gelia daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mored took.

4:19The sons of the woman Idouia, Nachem’s sister. Dalia was Keila’s father, and Semeion was Joman’s father. The sons of Naem, Keila’s father: Agarmi and Esthemoe Machathi.

4:20Semion’s sons: Amnon and Rana, Anan’s son, and Thilon. Isei’s sons: Zoath and Zoath’s sons.

The Sons of Selom

4:21The sons of Selom son of Judah: Er, Lecha’s father, Laada, Maresa’s father, and the families of houses of ephrath abak belonging to Esoba’s house, †

4:22Joakim, the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph, who dwelt in Moab; and he brought them back—abedirin athoukiin. †

4:23These were potters who dwelt in Nataim and Gadera. They grew strong with the king in his kingdom and dwelt there.

The Sons of Simeon

4:24Simeon’s sons: Namouel, Jamin, Jarib, Zare, and Saul,

4:25Salem his son, Mabasam his son, Masma his son,

4:26Amouel his son, Saboud his son, Zakchour his son, Semei his son.

4:27Semei had sixteen sons and three daughters, but their brethren did not have many sons. All their families did not multiply as much as Judah’s sons.

The Dwellings of Simeon

4:28They dwelt in Beersheba, Sama, Molada, and Esersoual,

4:29Balaa, Boasom, and Thoulad,

4:30Bathouel, Erma, and Seklag,

4:31Baithmarchaboth, half of Sosim, and the house of Baroumseorim. These were their cities until King David.

4:32Their farmsteads were Aitam, Enremmon, Thokkan, and Aisan, five cities,

4:33with all their farmsteads around these cities as far as Baal. This was their possession and their enrollment.

The Rulers of Simeon

4:34Mosobab, Jemoloch, and Josia son of Amasia,

4:35Joel—and this one was Isabia’s son—son of Saraia, son of Asiel,

4:36Elioenai, Jakaba, Jasouia, Asaia, Ediel, Ishmael, and Banaia,

4:37Zouza son of Sephei son of Allon son of Jedia son of Samari son of Samaios.

4:38These passed through under the names of rulers in their generations, and in their ancestral houses they multiplied into a multitude.

Simeon Seeks Pasture Toward Gerara

4:39They went as far as the approach to Gerara, as far as the east of Gai, to seek pastures for their livestock.

4:40They found rich, good pastures. The land was broad before them, with peace and quiet, for those who formerly dwelt there were from Ham’s sons.

4:41These who are recorded by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. They struck their houses and the Minaioi found there, devoted them to destruction to this day, and settled in their place, because there were pastures for their livestock there.

4:42Some of them, from Simeon’s sons, went to Mount Seir, five hundred men, with Phalettia, Noadia, Raphaia, and Oziel, Jesi’s sons, as their rulers.

4:43They struck those who remained of Amalek and dwelt there to this day.

1 Chronicles 5

The Sons of Reuben

5:1The sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn—for he was the firstborn, but when he went up upon his father’s bed, he gave his blessing to his son Joseph, Israel’s son, and he was not entered in the genealogy for the firstborn rights.

5:2For Judah was mighty in strength among his brethren, and from him came a leader, but the blessing belonged to Joseph.

5:3The sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn: Enoch, Phallous, Arson, and Charmi.

5:4Joel’s sons: Semei and Banaia his son. The sons of Goug, Semei’s son:

5:5his son Micha, his son Recha, his son Baal,

5:6his son Beera, whom Thaglathphalnasar king of Assour deported. He was ruler of the Reubenites.

5:7His brethren by his ancestral family, in their enrollments according to their generations: the ruler Joel, Zacharia,

5:8and Balek son of Ozouz son of Sama son of Joel. He dwelt in Aroer, and toward Nabau and Beelmaon,

5:9and eastward he lived as far as the approaches of the wilderness from the River Euphrates, because their livestock were numerous in Galaad’s land.

5:10In Saul’s days they made war against the resident aliens, and they fell into their hands, dwelling in tents as far as all the east of Galaad.

The Sons of Gad

5:11Gad’s sons lived opposite them in Basan as far as Selcha.

5:12Joel was the firstborn, Sapham the second, and Jani the scribe in Basan.

5:13Their brethren by their ancestral houses were Michael, Mosollam, Sebee, Joree, Jachan, Zoue, and Obed, seven.

5:14These were the sons of Abichail son of Uri son of Jidai son of Galaad son of Michael son of Isai son of Jouri son of Zaboucham,

5:15son of Abdiel son of Gouni, ruler of an ancestral house.

5:16They dwelt in Galaad, in Basan and their villages, and throughout Sharon’s surrounding region as far as its outlet.

5:17Their enrollment, all of them, was in the days of Joatham king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.

War Against the Hagarenes

5:18The sons of Reuben and Gad and half Manasseh’s tribe had men of strength who carried shields and the sword, drew the bow, and were taught war: forty-four thousand seven hundred sixty who went out to the battle line.

5:19They made war against the Hagarenes, Ituraeans, Naphisaeans, and Nadabaeans,

5:20and they prevailed over them, and the Hagaraeans and all their tents were given into their hands. For they cried to God in the battle, and He listened to them because they hoped in Him.

5:21They captured their baggage: five thousand camels, two hundred fifty thousand sheep, two thousand donkeys, and one hundred thousand human lives.

5:22Many fell wounded, for the war was from God. They settled in their place until the deportation.

The Half-Tribe of Manasseh

5:23The half of Manasseh’s tribe dwelt in the land from Basan to Baalermon, Sanir, and Mount Aermon, and they multiplied in Lebanon.

5:24These were the chiefs of their ancestral houses: Opher, Isei, Eliel, Esdriel, Jermia, Odouia, and Jediel, men mighty in strength, renowned men, rulers of their ancestral houses.

Breaking Faith and the Deportation

5:25They broke faith with their fathers’ God and went whoring after the gods of the peoples of the land whom God had removed from before them.

5:26Israel’s God stirred up the spirit of Phaloch king of Assour and the spirit of Thaglathphalnasar king of Assour. He deported Reuben, the Gaddite, and half Manasseh’s tribe, and brought them to Chalach, Chabor, and the River Gozan, to this day.

The Sons of Levi

5:27Levi’s sons: Gedson, Kaath, and Merari.

5:28Kaath’s sons: Ambram, Issaar, Hebron, and Oziel.

5:29Ambram’s sons: Aaron, Moses, and Mariam. Aaron’s sons: Nadab, Abioud, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

The Line of the High Priests

5:30Eleazar begot Phinees, and Phinees begot Abisou.

5:31Abisou begot Bokai, and Bokai begot Ozi.

5:32Ozi begot Zaraia, and Zaraia begot Mariel.

5:33Mariel begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Achitob.

5:34Achitob begot Sadok, and Sadok begot Achimaas.

5:35Achimaas begot Azariah, and Azariah begot Joanan.

5:36Joanas begot Azariah, who served as priest in the house Solomon built in Jerusalem.

5:37Azariah begot Amariah, and Amariah begot Achitob.

5:38Achitob begot Sadok, and Sadok begot Salom.

5:39Salom begot Chelkias, and Chelkias begot Azariah.

5:40Azariah begot Saraias, and Saraias begot Josadak.

5:41Josadak went in the deportation with Judah and Jerusalem by Nabouchodonosor’s hand.
