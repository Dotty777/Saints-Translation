1 KINGS 1

1:1 King David was old and advanced in days; and they wrapped him in garments, but he did not grow warm.
1:2 His attendants said, “Let them seek a young virgin for our lord the king. She shall attend the king and keep him warm; she shall lie with him, and our lord the king will become warm.”
1:3 And they sought a fair young woman throughout all the territory of Israel, and found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.
1:4 The young woman was very fair; she kept the king warm and served him, but the king did not know her.
1:5 Adonijah son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, “I will reign!” He prepared chariots and horsemen for himself, and fifty men to run ahead of him.
1:6 His father had never restrained him by saying, “Why have you done this?” He too was very handsome in appearance, and his mother bore him after Absalom.
1:7 He conferred with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest, and they helped by following Adonijah.
1:8 But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David’s mighty men were not following Adonijah.
1:9 Adonijah sacrificed sheep, calves, and lambs beside the stone of Zoheleth, near the spring of Rogel. He invited all his brothers and all the prominent men of Judah, the king’s attendants.
1:10 But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.
1:11 Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother, “Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, while our lord David does not know it?
1:12 Now come, let me give you counsel, and rescue your life and the life of your son Solomon.
1:13 Come, enter King David’s presence and say to him, ‘Did you not, my lord the king, swear to your servant, saying, “Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne”? Why, then, has Adonijah become king?’
1:14 Behold, while you are still speaking there with the king, I will come in after you and complete your words.”
1:15 Bathsheba entered the king’s chamber. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was ministering to the king.
1:16 Bathsheba bowed and prostrated herself before the king. The king said, “What is your concern?”
1:17 She said, “My lord the king, you swore by the Lord your God to your servant, saying, ‘Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’
1:18 Now behold, Adonijah has become king, and you, my lord the king, do not know it.
1:19 He has sacrificed calves, lambs, and sheep in abundance. He has invited all the king’s sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the force, but he has not invited your servant Solomon.
1:20 As for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are directed toward you, for you to tell them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.
1:21 When my lord the king lies down with his fathers, my son Solomon and I will be sinners.”
1:22 While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.
1:23 The king was told, “Behold, Nathan the prophet!” He came before the king and prostrated himself before the king, face to the ground.
1:24 Nathan said, “My lord the king, you said, ‘Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’
1:25 For he went down this day and sacrificed calves, lambs, and sheep in abundance. He invited all the king’s sons, the commanders of the force, and Abiathar the priest. Behold, they are eating and drinking before him and have said, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’
1:26 But me—me, your servant—and Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and your servant Solomon, he did not invite.
1:27 Has this matter come about through my lord the king without your informing your servant who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?”
1:28 David answered, “Call Bathsheba to me.” She came before the king and stood before him.
1:29 The king swore, saying, “As the Lord lives, who redeemed my life from every affliction,
1:30 just as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, ‘Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne in my place,’ so I will indeed do this very day.”
1:31 Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground and prostrated herself before the king. She said, “May my lord King David live for ever!”
1:32 King David said, “Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me.” They came before the king.
1:33 The king said to them, “Take your lord’s servants with you. Mount my son Solomon on my own mule, and bring him down to Gihon.
1:34 There let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him king over Israel. Sound the horn and say, ‘Long live King Solomon!’
1:35 He shall sit on my throne and reign in my place; I have commanded that he be leader over Israel and Judah.”
1:36 Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, “So be it! May the Lord, the God of my lord the king, confirm it so.
1:37 As the Lord has been with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David.”
1:38 Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down. They seated Solomon on King David’s mule and led him away to Gihon.
1:39 Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon. He sounded the horn, and all the people said, “Long live King Solomon!”
1:40 All the people went up after him. They danced in choruses and rejoiced with great joy, and the earth burst at their sound.
1:41 Adonijah and all his guests heard it, and they finished eating. Joab heard the sound of the horn and said, “What is this sound of the echoing city?”
1:42 While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, “Come in, for you are a man of ability; bring good news!”
1:43 Jonathan answered, “Indeed, our lord King David has made Solomon king!
1:44 The king sent with him Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites. They seated him on the king’s mule.
1:45 Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king at Gihon. They came up from there rejoicing, and the city echoed. That is the sound you heard.
1:46 Solomon has sat on the royal throne.
1:47 The king’s servants came to bless our lord King David, saying, ‘May God make the name of your son Solomon better than your name and make his throne greater than your throne.’ The king prostrated himself on his bed.
1:48 The king also said this: ‘Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who this day has given one from my offspring to sit on my throne while my eyes see it!’”
1:49 All Adonijah’s guests were astonished and rose. Each went his own way.
1:50 Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He rose, went away, and took hold of the horns of the altar.
1:51 Solomon was told, “Behold, Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon. He is holding fast to the altar’s horns, saying, ‘Let King Solomon swear to me this day whether he will not put his servant to death with the sword.’”
1:52 Solomon said, “If he becomes a man of ability, not one of his hairs shall fall to the ground! But if wickedness is found in him, he shall be put to death.”
1:53 King Solomon sent and brought him down from the altar. He came and prostrated himself before King Solomon. Solomon said to him, “Come now, go to your house.”

1 KINGS 2

2:1 When David’s days drew near for him to die, he commanded his son Solomon, saying,
2:2 “I myself am going the way of all the earth. Be strong, and be a man,
2:3 and keep the charge of the Lord your God: walk in His ways, keeping His commandments, statutes, and judgments written in Moses’ law, so that you may understand what you will do in everything that I command you,
2:4 so that the Lord may establish His word which He spoke, saying, ‘If your sons guard their way, walking before Me in truth with all their heart and all their soul,’ saying, ‘A man shall not be cut off for you from Israel’s throne.’
2:5 You also know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of Israel’s forces, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them, setting the blood of war in peace, and put innocent blood on the belt around his waist and the sandal on his foot.
2:6 Act according to your wisdom, and do not bring his gray hair down to Hades in peace.
2:7 Show mercy to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for they came near to me in this way when I fled from your brother Absalom.
2:8 Behold, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a son of the Benjaminite from Bahurim. He cursed me with a painful curse on the day I went to Camps. He came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, ‘I will not put you to death with the sword.’
2:9 You must certainly not hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man. You will know what to do to him, and you shall bring his gray hair down to Hades with blood.”
2:10 David lay down with his fathers and was buried in the City of David.
2:11 David’s reign over Israel lasted forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.
2:12 Solomon sat on the throne of his father David when he was twelve years old, and his kingdom was firmly prepared.
2:13 Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother, and prostrated himself before her. She said, “Is your coming peaceful?” He said, “Peaceful.
2:14 I have a word for you.” She said to him, “Speak.”
2:15 He said to her, “You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel set its face toward me to reign. But the kingdom turned and became my brother’s, for it became his from the Lord.
2:16 Now I have one request to ask of you; do not turn away your face.” Bathsheba said to him, “Speak.”
2:17 He said to her, “Please speak to King Solomon—for he will not turn his face away from you—and he will give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife.”
2:18 Bathsheba said, “Very well. I will speak to the king about you.”
2:19 Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him about Adonijah. The king rose to meet her and kissed her, then sat on his throne. A throne was placed for the king’s mother, and she sat at his right.
2:20 She said to him, “I have one small request to ask of you; do not turn away your face.” The king said to her, “Ask, my mother, for I will not turn you away.”
2:21 She said, “Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to your brother Adonijah as a wife.”
2:22 King Solomon answered his mother, “Why do you ask for Abishag for Adonijah? Ask for the kingdom for him too, for he is my elder brother! Abiathar the priest is his, and Joab son of Zeruiah, the commander-in-chief, is his companion!”
2:23 King Solomon swore by the Lord, “May God do thus to me and add more, for Adonijah has spoken this word against his own life!
2:24 Now, as the Lord lives, who prepared me and placed me on the throne of my father David—He made a house for me, as the Lord spoke—Adonijah shall be put to death this day.”
2:25 King Solomon sent through Benaiah son of Jehoiada and had Adonijah killed. Adonijah died that day.
2:26 The king said to Abiathar the priest, “Go quickly to Anathoth, to your field. You are a man deserving death this day, but I will not put you to death, because you did carry the ark of the Lord’s covenant before my father and did suffer all the hardships my father suffered.”
2:27 Solomon expelled Abiathar from being priest to the Lord, fulfilling the Lord’s word which He had spoken concerning Eli’s house at Shiloh.
2:28 The report reached Joab son of Zeruiah, for Joab had followed Adonijah but had not followed Solomon. Joab fled to the Lord’s dwelling and held fast to the horns of the altar.
2:29 Solomon was told, “Joab has fled to the Lord’s tent and is holding fast to the altar’s horns.” Solomon sent to Joab, asking, “What has happened to you, that you have fled to the altar?” Joab said, “I was afraid of you, and I fled to the Lord.” King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, “Go, kill him and bury him.”
2:30 Benaiah son of Jehoiada came to Joab in the Lord’s tent and said to him, “Thus says the king: Come out.” Joab said, “I am not coming out, for I will die here.” Benaiah son of Jehoiada returned and said to the king, “This is what Joab has said, and this is how he has answered me.”
2:31 The king said to him, “Go and do to him as he has said. Kill him and bury him, and this day remove from me and my father’s house the blood Joab shed without cause.
2:32 The Lord returned the blood of his injustice upon his own head, because he struck two men more righteous and better than himself and killed them with the sword, while my father David did not know of their blood: Abner son of Ner, commander-in-chief of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander-in-chief of Judah.
2:33 Their blood was returned upon his head and the head of his offspring for ever. But for David, his offspring, his house, and his throne may there be peace from the Lord for ever.”
2:34 Benaiah son of Jehoiada came upon Joab, put him to death, and buried him at his house in the wilderness.
2:35 The king put Benaiah son of Jehoiada in the military command in Joab’s place. The kingdom was being established in Jerusalem, and the king appointed Zadok the priest as first priest in Abiathar’s place.
2:35a The Lord gave Solomon discernment, exceedingly great wisdom, and breadth of heart like the sand beside the sea.
2:35b Solomon’s discernment greatly surpassed the discernment of all the sons of old and all the discerning men of Egypt.
2:35c He took Pharaoh’s daughter and brought her into the City of David until he had first finished his own house, the Lord’s house, and Jerusalem’s surrounding wall. He built and completed them in seven years.
2:35d Solomon had seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country.
2:35e Solomon made the sea, the supports, the great washbasins, the pillars, the courtyard’s fountain, and the bronze sea.
2:35f He built the citadel and its battlements and cut through the City of David. Thus Pharaoh’s daughter would go up from the City of David to her house, which he built for her. Then he built the citadel.
2:35g Solomon offered three whole burnt offerings and peace offerings in the year on the altar he built for the Lord, and burned incense before the Lord. He completed the house.
2:35h These were the officials appointed over Solomon’s works: three thousand six hundred overseers of the people doing the works.
2:35i He built Assour, Megiddo, Gezer, Upper Beth-horon, and Baalath.
2:35k Only after he had built the Lord’s house and Jerusalem’s surrounding wall—after these things—did he build these cities.
2:35l While David was still alive, he commanded Solomon: “Behold, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a descendant of the Benjaminite, from Hebron.
2:35m He cursed me with a painful curse on the day I was going to Camps.
2:35n He was coming down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, ‘He shall not be put to death with the sword.’
2:35o Now do not hold him guiltless, for you are a discerning man. You will know what to do to him, and you shall bring his gray hair down to Hades with blood.”
2:36 The king summoned Shimei and said to him, “Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and stay there. You shall not go out from there anywhere.
2:37 On the day you go out and cross the Wadi Kidron, know for certain that you shall surely die. Your blood shall be upon your own head.” The king made him swear that day.
2:38 Shimei said to the king, “The word you have spoken is good, my lord the king. Your servant will do so.” Shimei stayed in Jerusalem three years.
2:39 After three years, two of Shimei’s slaves fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, “Behold, your slaves are in Gath.”
2:40 Shimei rose, saddled his donkey, and went to Achish at Gath to seek out his slaves. Shimei went and brought his slaves back from Gath.
2:41 Solomon was told, “Shimei went from Jerusalem to Gath and brought back his slaves.”
2:42 The king sent for Shimei and said to him, “Did I not make you swear by the Lord and solemnly warn you, ‘On the day you leave Jerusalem and go to the right or left, know for certain that you shall surely die’?
2:43 Why have you not kept the Lord’s oath and the command I laid upon you?”
2:44 The king said to Shimei, “You know all your wickedness which your heart knew, the things you did to my father David. The Lord has repaid your wickedness upon your head.
2:45 But King Solomon is blessed, and David’s throne shall be prepared before the Lord for ever.”
2:46 King Solomon commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada. He went out and killed Shimei, and Shimei died.
2:46a King Solomon was exceedingly discerning and wise. Judah and Israel were exceedingly numerous, like the sand beside the sea in multitude, eating, drinking, and rejoicing.
2:46b Solomon ruled among all the kingdoms. They brought gifts and served Solomon all the days of his life.
2:46c Solomon began to open up the resources of Lebanon,
2:46d and he built Thermai in the wilderness.
2:46e This was Solomon’s midday meal: thirty kors of fine flour, sixty kors of pounded meal, ten choice calves, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and one hundred sheep, besides deer, gazelles, and choice pasture-fed birds.
2:46f For he ruled throughout the region beyond the River, from Raphi to Gaza, among all the kings beyond the River.
2:46g He had peace on every side around him. Judah and Israel lived securely, each beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, eating and drinking, from Dan to Beersheba, all Solomon’s days.
2:46h These were Solomon’s officials: Azariah son of Zadok the priest; Ornia son of Nathan, chief of the supervisors; Edram over his house; Souba the scribe; Basa son of Achithalam, the recorder; Abi son of Joab, commander-in-chief; Achire son of Edrai over the burdens; Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the court administration and the brickworks; and Zachour son of Nathan, the counselor.
2:46i Solomon had forty thousand broodmares for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen.
2:46k He ruled among all the kings from the River to the land of foreigners and to Egypt’s borders.
2:46l Solomon son of David reigned over Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

1 KINGS 3

3:2 The people, however, were burning incense at the high places, because no house had yet been built for the Lord’s name.
3:3 Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the ordinances of his father David; however, he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.
3:4 He rose and went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for it was the highest place and a great one. Solomon offered a thousand whole burnt offerings on the altar at Gibeon.
3:5 The Lord appeared to Solomon in sleep at night. The Lord said to Solomon, “Ask a request for yourself.”
3:6 Solomon said, “Thou showedst great mercy to Thy servant David my father, as he walked before Thee in truth, righteousness, and uprightness of heart with Thee. Thou keptest this great mercy for him, placing his son on his throne, as it is this day.
3:7 Now, Lord my God, Thou hast placed Thy servant in place of my father David. I am a little child and do not know my going out or my coming in.
3:8 Thy servant is among Thy people whom Thou didst choose, a numerous people who cannot be numbered.
3:9 Give Thy servant a heart to hear and to judge Thy people in righteousness, discerning between good and evil; for who can judge this weighty people of Thine?”
3:10 It pleased the Lord that Solomon had asked this.
3:11 The Lord said to him, “Because you have asked this of Me and have not asked for many days for yourself, or wealth, or the lives of your enemies, but have asked for understanding to hear judgment,
3:12 behold, I have done according to your word. Behold, I have given you a discerning and wise heart, so that no one like you has been before you, and no one like you shall arise after you.
3:13 I have also given you what you did not ask: both wealth and glory, so that no man like you has been among the kings.
3:14 If you walk in My way, keeping My commandments and ordinances as your father David walked, I will multiply your days.”
3:15 Solomon awoke: it was a dream. He rose and came to Jerusalem, and stood before the altar that was before the ark of the Lord’s covenant in Zion. He offered whole burnt offerings and peace offerings, and made a great feast for himself and all his attendants.
3:16 Then two women who were prostitutes appeared before the king and stood before him.
3:17 One woman said, “Please, my lord, this woman and I live in one house, and we gave birth in the house.
3:18 On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were together, with no one in the house except the two of us.
3:19 This woman’s son died during the night when she lay on him.
3:20 She rose in the middle of the night and took my son from my arms. She laid him in her bosom, and laid her dead son in my bosom.
3:21 I rose in the morning to nurse my son, and that child was dead. I looked carefully at him in the morning, and he was not my son whom I had borne.”
3:22 The other woman said, “No! My son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.” Thus they spoke before the king.
3:23 The king said to them, “You say, ‘This is my son, the living one, and her son is the dead one.’ And you say, ‘No! My son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.’”
3:24 The king said, “Bring me a sword.” They brought the sword before the king.
3:25 The king said, “Divide the living nursing child in two. Give half of it to this woman and half of it to this woman.”
3:26 The woman whose son was alive answered the king, for her womb was stirred for her son. She said, “Please, my lord, give her the child! You must not put him to death!” But the other said, “He shall belong to neither me nor her. Divide him.”
3:27 The king answered, “Give the child to the woman who said, ‘Give it to her,’ and you must not put him to death. She is his mother.”
3:28 All Israel heard this judgment the king had given, and they feared the king, for they saw that God’s discernment was within him to carry out justice.

1 KINGS 4

4:1 King Solomon reigned over Israel.
4:2 These were his officials: Azariah son of Zadok;
4:3 Elihoreph and Ahijah son of Saba were scribes; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;
4:4 Zadok and Abiathar were priests;
4:5 Ornia son of Nathan was over the appointed officers; Zabud son of Nathan was the king’s companion;
4:6 Achiel was household steward; Eliab son of Saph was over the clan; Adoniram son of Ephra was over the tributes.
4:7 Solomon had twelve appointed officers over all Israel to provide for the king and his household. Each was responsible for providing one month in the year.
4:8 These were their names: Benor, in Ephraim’s hill country—one;
4:9 the son of Rechab, in Machemas, Bethalamin, Beth-shemesh, and Ailon as far as Beth-hanan—one;
4:10 the son of Esoth, in Berbethnema, Lousamencha, and Rhesphara;
4:11 Chinanadab and Anaphathi, husband of Tableth; a daughter of Solomon was his wife—one;
4:12 Baccha son of Ahilud, over Taanach, Megiddo, and all the house of San beside Sesathan below Esrae, and from Baisaphoud, Abel-meholah, as far as Maeber Loukam—one;
4:13 the son of Geber, in Remath-gilead; Eregaba’s district in Bashan belonged to his charge, sixty great walled cities with bronze bars—one;
4:14 Ahinadab son of Achel, in Mahanaim—one;
4:15 Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; he too took Solomon’s daughter Basemath as a wife—one;
4:16 Baana son of Hushai, in Maalath—one;
4:17 Shammah son of Elah, in Benjamin;
4:18 Geber son of Adai, in Gad’s land, the land of Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan; and one officer in Judah’s land;
4:19 Jehoshaphat son of Phouasoud, in Issachar.

1 KINGS 5

5:1 The appointed officers thus provided for King Solomon and all the orders for the king’s table, each in his month; they did not depart from an instruction. They brought barley and straw for the horses and chariots to the place where the king would be, each according to his assignment.
5:2 These were Solomon’s requirements for one day: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal,
5:3 ten choice calves, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and a hundred sheep, besides deer, gazelles, and choice birds—fattened provisions.
5:4 For he was ruler beyond the River, and he had peace on every side around him.
5:9 The Lord gave Solomon discernment, very great wisdom, and abundance of heart like the sand beside the sea.
5:10 Solomon increased greatly beyond the discernment of all ancient people and beyond all the discerning people of Egypt.
5:11 He became wiser than all people; he became wiser than Gaithan the Ezraite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mal.
5:12 Solomon spoke three thousand parables, and his songs numbered five thousand.
5:13 He spoke about trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop growing through the wall. He spoke about livestock, birds, creeping things, and fish.
5:14 All the peoples came to hear Solomon’s wisdom, and he received gifts from all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom.
5:14a Solomon took Pharaoh’s daughter as his wife and brought her into the City of David until he finished the Lord’s house, his own house, and Jerusalem’s wall.
5:14b Then Pharaoh king of Egypt went up, captured Gezer beforehand, and burned it and the Canaanite who lived in Mergab. Pharaoh gave them as parting gifts to his daughter, Solomon’s wife; and Solomon built Gezer.
5:15 Hiram king of Tyre sent his attendants to anoint Solomon in place of David his father, for Hiram had always loved David.
5:16 Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,
5:17 “You know that my father David could not build a house for the name of the Lord my God because of the wars that surrounded him, until the Lord put them beneath the footsteps of his feet.
5:18 But now the Lord my God has given me rest on every side; there is no plotter and no evil encounter.
5:19 Behold, I intend to build a house for the name of the Lord my God, as the Lord God told my father David: ‘Your son, whom I shall place on your throne in your place, shall build the house for My name.’
5:20 Now command that they cut timber from Lebanon for me. Behold, my servants are with your servants, and I will pay you the wages of your service according to whatever you say; for you know that no one among us knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians.”
5:21 When Hiram heard Solomon’s words, he rejoiced greatly and said, “Blessed be God this day, who has given David a discerning son over this great people!”
5:22 He sent to Solomon, saying, “I have heard about everything you did send to me. I will fulfill all your wishes: cedar and pine timber.
5:23 My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea. I will make them into rafts as far as the place you send me word about, and shake them apart there; you shall take them away. You shall fulfill my wish by providing loaves for my household.”
5:24 Hiram supplied Solomon with cedars and all that he desired.
5:25 Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat and machir for his household, and twenty thousand beth of beaten oil. Solomon gave this to Hiram each year.
5:26 The Lord gave Solomon wisdom, as He had promised him. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between themselves.
5:27 The king raised a levy from all Israel; the levy was thirty thousand men.
5:28 He sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month in shifts: one month they were in Lebanon and two months at their home. Adoniram was over the levy.
5:29 Solomon had seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country,
5:30 besides the chiefs appointed over Solomon’s works, three thousand six hundred overseers who performed the works.
5:32 They prepared the stones and timber for three years.

1 KINGS 6

6:1 In the four hundred fortieth year of the exodus of the sons of Israel from Egypt, in the fourth year, in the second month, when King Solomon reigned over Israel,
6:1a the king commanded, and they carried great, costly stones for the house’s foundation, and unhewn stones.
6:1b Solomon’s sons and Hiram’s sons hewed them and laid them down.
6:1c In the fourth year he laid the foundation of the Lord’s house in the month of Niso, the second month.
6:1d In the eleventh year, in the month of Baal, which is the eighth month, the house was completed in every detail and according to its entire arrangement.
6:2 The house that King Solomon built for the Lord was forty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty-five cubits high.
6:3 The porch in front of the temple was twenty cubits long across the house’s width, and ten cubits wide in front of the house. He built the house and completed it.
6:4 He made recessed, concealed windows for the house.
6:5 He placed beams all around against the house’s wall, for the temple and the inner sanctuary; he made side chambers all around.

22:21 A spirit came forward, stood before the Lord, and said, ‘I will deceive him.’ The Lord said to it, ‘By what means?’
22:22 It said, ‘I will go out and become a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.’ He said, ‘You will deceive him and indeed prevail. Go out and do so.’
22:23 Now behold, the Lord has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, and the Lord has spoken calamities against you.”
22:24 Zedekiah son of Chenaanah approached, struck Micaiah on the cheek, and said, “What sort of spirit of the Lord was it that spoke in you?”
22:25 Micaiah said, “Behold, you will see on that day, when you enter an inner chamber within an inner chamber to hide.”
22:26 The king of Israel said, “Take Micaiah and return him to Emer the city ruler; and to Joash the king’s son
22:27 say to put this man in prison and have him eat bread of affliction and water of affliction until I return in peace.”
22:28 Micaiah said, “If you do return in peace, the Lord has not spoken in me.”
22:29 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah with him went up to Remmath-gilead.
22:30 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, “I will conceal myself and enter the battle, but you must put on my clothing.” The king of Israel concealed himself and entered the battle.
22:31 The king of Syria had commanded his thirty-two chariot commanders, “Fight neither small nor great, but only the king of Israel.”
22:32 When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat king of Judah, they said, “This appears to be the king of Israel!” They surrounded him to fight, and Jehoshaphat cried out.
22:33 When the chariot commanders saw that this was not the king of Israel, they turned away from him.
22:34 One man drew his bow with good aim and struck the king of Israel between the lung and the breastplate. He said to his charioteer, “Turn your hands and take me out of the battle, for I am wounded.”
22:35 The battle turned into a rout that day. The king stood on the chariot facing Syria from morning until evening, and the blood from the wound poured into the hollow of the chariot. He died in the evening, and the blood of the rout ran into the hollow of the chariot.
22:36 At sunset the army herald stood and said, “Each man to his own city and his own land,
22:37 for the king is dead.” They came to Samaria and buried the king in Samaria.
22:38 They washed the chariot at Samaria’s spring. Swine and dogs licked the blood, and prostitutes bathed in the blood, according to the Lord’s word which He had spoken.
22:39 The rest of Ahab’s acts, all he did, the ivory house he built, and all the cities he made—behold, are these not written in the Book of the Annals of the Kings of Israel?
22:40 Ahab slept with his fathers, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.
22:41 Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king over Judah. In the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel he became king.
22:42 Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king and reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Azubah daughter of Selei.
22:43 He walked in all the way of Asa his father and did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the Lord’s eyes.
22:44 Yet he did not remove the high places. The people still sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.
22:45 Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.
22:46 The rest of Jehoshaphat’s acts and his mighty deeds, all he did—behold, are these not written in the Book of the Annals of the Kings of Judah?
22:51 Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers and was buried beside his fathers in the City of David his father. Joram his son reigned in his place.
22:52 Ahaziah son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned in Israel two years.
22:53 He did evil before the Lord and walked in the way of Ahab his father, the way of Jezebel his mother, and the sins of the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.
22:54 He served the Baalim, bowed down to them, and provoked the Lord, the God of Israel, according to all that had happened before him.
