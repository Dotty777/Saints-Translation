1 Kings 1

Read this chapter on staging

Abishag the Shunammite

1:1King David was old and advanced in days; and they wrapped him in garments, but he did not grow warm.

1:2His attendants said, “Let them seek a young virgin for our lord the king. She shall attend the king and keep him warm; she shall lie with him, and our lord the king will become warm.”

1:3And they sought a fair young woman throughout all the territory of Israel, and found Abishag the Shunammite, and brought her to the king.

1:4The young woman was very fair; she kept the king warm and served him, but the king did not know her.

Adonijah Exalts Himself

1:5Adonijah son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, “I will reign!” He prepared chariots and horsemen for himself, and fifty men to run ahead of him.

1:6His father had never restrained him by saying, “Why have you done this?” He too was very handsome in appearance, and his mother bore him after Absalom.

1:7He conferred with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest, and they helped by following Adonijah.

1:8But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David’s mighty men were not following Adonijah.

1:9Adonijah sacrificed sheep, calves, and lambs beside the stone of Zoheleth, near the spring of Rogel. He invited all his brothers and all the prominent men of Judah, the king’s attendants.

1:10But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.

Nathan and Bathsheba Before the King

1:11Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother, “Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, while our lord David does not know it?

1:12Now come, let me give you counsel, and rescue your life and the life of your son Solomon.

1:13Come, enter King David’s presence and say to him, ‘Did you not, my lord the king, swear to your servant, saying, “Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne”? Why, then, has Adonijah become king?’

1:14Behold, while you are still speaking there with the king, I will come in after you and complete your words.”

1:15Bathsheba entered the king’s chamber. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was ministering to the king.

1:16Bathsheba bowed and prostrated herself before the king. The king said, “What is your concern?”

1:17She said, “My lord the king, you swore by the Lord your God to your servant, saying, ‘Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’

1:18Now behold, Adonijah has become king, and you, my lord the king, do not know it.

1:19He has sacrificed calves, lambs, and sheep in abundance. He has invited all the king’s sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander of the force, but he has not invited your servant Solomon.

1:20As for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are directed toward you, for you to tell them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.

1:21When my lord the king lies down with his fathers, my son Solomon and I will be sinners.”

1:22While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.

1:23The king was told, “Behold, Nathan the prophet!” He came before the king and prostrated himself before the king, face to the ground.

1:24Nathan said, “My lord the king, you said, ‘Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.’

1:25For he went down this day and sacrificed calves, lambs, and sheep in abundance. He invited all the king’s sons, the commanders of the force, and Abiathar the priest. Behold, they are eating and drinking before him and have said, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’

1:26But me—me, your servant—and Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and your servant Solomon, he did not invite.

1:27Has this matter come about through my lord the king without your informing your servant who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?”

David Swears That Solomon Shall Reign

1:28David answered, “Call Bathsheba to me.” She came before the king and stood before him.

1:29The king swore, saying, “As the Lord lives, who redeemed my life from every affliction,

1:30just as I swore to you by the Lord, the God of Israel, saying, ‘Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne in my place,’ so I will indeed do this very day.”

1:31Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground and prostrated herself before the king. She said, “May my lord King David live for ever!”

Solomon Anointed at Gihon

1:32King David said, “Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me.” They came before the king.

1:33The king said to them, “Take your lord’s servants with you. Mount my son Solomon on my own mule, and bring him down to Gihon.

1:34There let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him king over Israel. Sound the horn and say, ‘Long live King Solomon!’

1:35He shall sit on my throne and reign in my place; I have commanded that he be leader over Israel and Judah.”

1:36Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, “So be it! May the Lord, the God of my lord the king, confirm it so.

1:37As the Lord has been with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon, and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David.”

1:38Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went down. They seated Solomon on King David’s mule and led him away to Gihon.

1:39Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon. He sounded the horn, and all the people said, “Long live King Solomon!”

1:40All the people went up after him. They danced in choruses and rejoiced with great joy, and the earth burst at their sound.

The News Reaches Adonijah

1:41Adonijah and all his guests heard it, and they finished eating. Joab heard the sound of the horn and said, “What is this sound of the echoing city?”

1:42While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, “Come in, for you are a man of ability; bring good news!”

1:43Jonathan answered, “Indeed, our lord King David has made Solomon king!

1:44The king sent with him Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites. They seated him on the king’s mule.

1:45Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king at Gihon. They came up from there rejoicing, and the city echoed. That is the sound you heard.

1:46Solomon has sat on the royal throne.

1:47The king’s servants came to bless our lord King David, saying, ‘May God make the name of your son Solomon better than your name and make his throne greater than your throne.’ The king prostrated himself on his bed.

1:48The king also said this: ‘Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who this day has given one from my offspring to sit on my throne while my eyes see it!’”

1:49All Adonijah’s guests were astonished and rose. Each went his own way.

Adonijah Takes Hold of the Altar

1:50Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He rose, went away, and took hold of the horns of the altar.

1:51Solomon was told, “Behold, Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon. He is holding fast to the altar’s horns, saying, ‘Let King Solomon swear to me this day whether he will not put his servant to death with the sword.’”

1:52Solomon said, “If he becomes a man of ability, not one of his hairs shall fall to the ground! But if wickedness is found in him, he shall be put to death.”

1:53King Solomon sent and brought him down from the altar. He came and prostrated himself before King Solomon. Solomon said to him, “Come now, go to your house.”

1 Kings 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Charge of David to Solomon

2:1When David’s days drew near for him to die, he commanded his son Solomon, saying,

2:2“I myself am going the way of all the earth. Be strong, and be a man,

2:3and keep the charge of the Lord your God: walk in His ways, keeping His commandments, statutes, and judgments written in Moses’ law, so that you may understand what you will do in everything that I command you,

2:4so that the Lord may establish His word which He spoke, saying, ‘If your sons guard their way, walking before Me in truth with all their heart and all their soul,’ saying, ‘A man shall not be cut off for you from Israel’s throne.’

2:5You also know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of Israel’s forces, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them, setting the blood of war in peace, and put innocent blood on the belt around his waist and the sandal on his foot.

2:6Act according to your wisdom, and do not bring his gray hair down to Hades in peace.

2:7Show mercy to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for they came near to me in this way when I fled from your brother Absalom.

2:8Behold, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a son of the Benjaminite from Bahurim. He cursed me with a painful curse on the day I went to Camps. He came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, ‘I will not put you to death with the sword.’

2:9You must certainly not hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man. You will know what to do to him, and you shall bring his gray hair down to Hades with blood.”

The Death of David

2:10David lay down with his fathers and was buried in the City of David.

2:11David’s reign over Israel lasted forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

2:12Solomon sat on the throne of his father David when he was twelve years old, and his kingdom was firmly prepared.

Adonijah Asks for Abishag

2:13Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother, and prostrated himself before her. She said, “Is your coming peaceful?” He said, “Peaceful.

2:14I have a word for you.” She said to him, “Speak.”

2:15He said to her, “You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel set its face toward me to reign. But the kingdom turned and became my brother’s, for it became his from the Lord.

2:16Now I have one request to ask of you; do not turn away your face.” Bathsheba said to him, “Speak.”

2:17He said to her, “Please speak to King Solomon—for he will not turn his face away from you—and he will give me Abishag the Shunammite as a wife.”

2:18Bathsheba said, “Very well. I will speak to the king about you.”

2:19Bathsheba came to King Solomon to speak to him about Adonijah. The king rose to meet her and kissed her, then sat on his throne. A throne was placed for the king’s mother, and she sat at his right.

2:20She said to him, “I have one small request to ask of you; do not turn away your face.” The king said to her, “Ask, my mother, for I will not turn you away.”

2:21She said, “Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to your brother Adonijah as a wife.”

2:22King Solomon answered his mother, “Why do you ask for Abishag for Adonijah? Ask for the kingdom for him too, for he is my elder brother! Abiathar the priest is his, and Joab son of Zeruiah, the commander-in-chief, is his companion!”

2:23King Solomon swore by the Lord, “May God do thus to me and add more, for Adonijah has spoken this word against his own life!

2:24Now, as the Lord lives, who prepared me and placed me on the throne of my father David—He made a house for me, as the Lord spoke—Adonijah shall be put to death this day.”

2:25King Solomon sent through Benaiah son of Jehoiada and had Adonijah killed. Adonijah died that day.

Abiathar Expelled from the Priesthood

2:26The king said to Abiathar the priest, “Go quickly to Anathoth, to your field. You are a man deserving death this day, but I will not put you to death, because you did carry the ark of the Lord’s covenant before my father and did suffer all the hardships my father suffered.”

2:27Solomon expelled Abiathar from being priest to the Lord, fulfilling the Lord’s word which He had spoken concerning Eli’s house at Shiloh.

Joab Slain at the Altar

2:28The report reached Joab son of Zeruiah, for Joab had followed Adonijah but had not followed Solomon. Joab fled to the Lord’s dwelling and held fast to the horns of the altar.

2:29Solomon was told, “Joab has fled to the Lord’s tent and is holding fast to the altar’s horns.” Solomon sent to Joab, asking, “What has happened to you, that you have fled to the altar?” Joab said, “I was afraid of you, and I fled to the Lord.” King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, “Go, kill him and bury him.”

2:30Benaiah son of Jehoiada came to Joab in the Lord’s tent and said to him, “Thus says the king: Come out.” Joab said, “I am not coming out, for I will die here.” Benaiah son of Jehoiada returned and said to the king, “This is what Joab has said, and this is how he has answered me.”

2:31The king said to him, “Go and do to him as he has said. Kill him and bury him, and this day remove from me and my father’s house the blood Joab shed without cause.

2:32The Lord returned the blood of his injustice upon his own head, because he struck two men more righteous and better than himself and killed them with the sword, while my father David did not know of their blood: Abner son of Ner, commander-in-chief of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander-in-chief of Judah.

2:33Their blood was returned upon his head and the head of his offspring for ever. But for David, his offspring, his house, and his throne may there be peace from the Lord for ever.”

2:34Benaiah son of Jehoiada came upon Joab, put him to death, and buried him at his house in the wilderness.

2:35The king put Benaiah son of Jehoiada in the military command in Joab’s place. The kingdom was being established in Jerusalem, and the king appointed Zadok the priest as first priest in Abiathar’s place.

The Wisdom and the Works of Solomon

2:35aThe Lord gave Solomon discernment, exceedingly great wisdom, and breadth of heart like the sand beside the sea.

2:35bSolomon’s discernment greatly surpassed the discernment of all the sons of old and all the discerning men of Egypt.

2:35cHe took Pharaoh’s daughter and brought her into the City of David until he had first finished his own house, the Lord’s house, and Jerusalem’s surrounding wall. He built and completed them in seven years.

2:35dSolomon had seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country.

2:35eSolomon made the sea, the supports, the great washbasins, the pillars, the courtyard’s fountain, and the bronze sea.

2:35fHe built the citadel and its battlements and cut through the City of David. Thus Pharaoh’s daughter would go up from the City of David to her house, which he built for her. Then he built the citadel.

2:35gSolomon offered three whole burnt offerings and peace offerings in the year on the altar he built for the Lord, and burned incense before the Lord. He completed the house.

2:35hThese were the officials appointed over Solomon’s works: three thousand six hundred overseers of the people doing the works.

2:35iHe built Assour, Megiddo, Gezer, Upper Beth-horon, and Baalath.

2:35kOnly after he had built the Lord’s house and Jerusalem’s surrounding wall—after these things—did he build these cities.

David's Charge Concerning Shimei

2:35lWhile David was still alive, he commanded Solomon: “Behold, with you is Shimei son of Gera, a descendant of the Benjaminite, from Hebron.

2:35mHe cursed me with a painful curse on the day I was going to Camps.

2:35nHe was coming down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, ‘He shall not be put to death with the sword.’

2:35oNow do not hold him guiltless, for you are a discerning man. You will know what to do to him, and you shall bring his gray hair down to Hades with blood.”

Shimei Confined to Jerusalem

2:36The king summoned Shimei and said to him, “Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and stay there. You shall not go out from there anywhere.

2:37On the day you go out and cross the Wadi Kidron, know for certain that you shall surely die. Your blood shall be upon your own head.” The king made him swear that day.

2:38Shimei said to the king, “The word you have spoken is good, my lord the king. Your servant will do so.” Shimei stayed in Jerusalem three years.

2:39After three years, two of Shimei’s slaves fled to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, “Behold, your slaves are in Gath.”

2:40Shimei rose, saddled his donkey, and went to Achish at Gath to seek out his slaves. Shimei went and brought his slaves back from Gath.

2:41Solomon was told, “Shimei went from Jerusalem to Gath and brought back his slaves.”

2:42The king sent for Shimei and said to him, “Did I not make you swear by the Lord and solemnly warn you, ‘On the day you leave Jerusalem and go to the right or left, know for certain that you shall surely die’?

2:43Why have you not kept the Lord’s oath and the command I laid upon you?”

2:44The king said to Shimei, “You know all your wickedness which your heart knew, the things you did to my father David. The Lord has repaid your wickedness upon your head.

2:45But King Solomon is blessed, and David’s throne shall be prepared before the Lord for ever.”

2:46King Solomon commanded Benaiah son of Jehoiada. He went out and killed Shimei, and Shimei died.

The Greatness of Solomon's Kingdom

2:46aKing Solomon was exceedingly discerning and wise. Judah and Israel were exceedingly numerous, like the sand beside the sea in multitude, eating, drinking, and rejoicing.

2:46bSolomon ruled among all the kingdoms. They brought gifts and served Solomon all the days of his life.

2:46cSolomon began to open up the resources of Lebanon,

2:46dand he built Thermai in the wilderness.

2:46eThis was Solomon’s midday meal: thirty kors of fine flour, sixty kors of pounded meal, ten choice calves, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and one hundred sheep, besides deer, gazelles, and choice pasture-fed birds.

2:46fFor he ruled throughout the region beyond the River, from Raphi to Gaza, among all the kings beyond the River.

2:46gHe had peace on every side around him. Judah and Israel lived securely, each beneath his vine and beneath his fig tree, eating and drinking, from Dan to Beersheba, all Solomon’s days.

2:46hThese were Solomon’s officials: Azariah son of Zadok the priest; Ornia son of Nathan, chief of the supervisors; Edram over his house; Souba the scribe; Basa son of Achithalam, the recorder; Abi son of Joab, commander-in-chief; Achire son of Edrai over the burdens; Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the court administration and the brickworks; and Zachour son of Nathan, the counselor.

2:46iSolomon had forty thousand broodmares for chariots and twelve thousand horsemen.

2:46kHe ruled among all the kings from the River to the land of foreigners and to Egypt’s borders.

2:46lSolomon son of David reigned over Israel and Judah in Jerusalem.

Sacrifice at the High Places

3:2The people, however, were burning incense at the high places, because no house had yet been built for the Lord’s name.

3:3Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the ordinances of his father David; however, he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

The Dream at Gibeon

3:4He rose and went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for it was the highest place and a great one. Solomon offered a thousand whole burnt offerings on the altar at Gibeon.

3:5The Lord appeared to Solomon in sleep at night. The Lord said to Solomon, “Ask a request for yourself.”

3:6Solomon said, “Thou showedst great mercy to Thy servant David my father, as he walked before Thee in truth, righteousness, and uprightness of heart with Thee. Thou keptest this great mercy for him, placing his son on his throne, as it is this day.

3:7Now, Lord my God, Thou hast placed Thy servant in place of my father David. I am a little child and do not know my going out or my coming in.

3:8Thy servant is among Thy people whom Thou didst choose, a numerous people who cannot be numbered.

3:9Give Thy servant a heart to hear and to judge Thy people in righteousness, discerning between good and evil; for who can judge this weighty people of Thine?”

3:10It pleased the Lord that Solomon had asked this.

3:11The Lord said to him, “Because you have asked this of Me and have not asked for many days for yourself, or wealth, or the lives of your enemies, but have asked for understanding to hear judgment,

3:12behold, I have done according to your word. Behold, I have given you a discerning and wise heart, so that no one like you has been before you, and no one like you shall arise after you.

3:13I have also given you what you did not ask: both wealth and glory, so that no man like you has been among the kings.

3:14If you walk in My way, keeping My commandments and ordinances as your father David walked, I will multiply your days.”

3:15Solomon awoke: it was a dream. He rose and came to Jerusalem, and stood before the altar that was before the ark of the Lord’s covenant in Zion. He offered whole burnt offerings and peace offerings, and made a great feast for himself and all his attendants.

The Judgment Between the Two Women

3:16Then two women who were prostitutes appeared before the king and stood before him.

3:17One woman said, “Please, my lord, this woman and I live in one house, and we gave birth in the house.

3:18On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were together, with no one in the house except the two of us.

3:19This woman’s son died during the night when she lay on him.

3:20She rose in the middle of the night and took my son from my arms. She laid him in her bosom, and laid her dead son in my bosom.

3:21I rose in the morning to nurse my son, and that child was dead. I looked carefully at him in the morning, and he was not my son whom I had borne.”

3:22The other woman said, “No! My son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.” Thus they spoke before the king.

3:23The king said to them, “You say, ‘This is my son, the living one, and her son is the dead one.’ And you say, ‘No! My son is the living one, and your son is the dead one.’”

3:24The king said, “Bring me a sword.” They brought the sword before the king.

3:25The king said, “Divide the living nursing child in two. Give half of it to this woman and half of it to this woman.”

3:26The woman whose son was alive answered the king, for her womb was stirred for her son. She said, “Please, my lord, give her the child! You must not put him to death!” But the other said, “He shall belong to neither me nor her. Divide him.”

3:27The king answered, “Give the child to the woman who said, ‘Give it to her,’ and you must not put him to death. She is his mother.”

3:28All Israel heard this judgment the king had given, and they feared the king, for they saw that God’s discernment was within him to carry out justice.

1 Kings 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Daily Provision of Solomon's Table

5:1The appointed officers thus provided for King Solomon and all the orders for the king’s table, each in his month; they did not depart from an instruction. They brought barley and straw for the horses and chariots to the place where the king would be, each according to his assignment.

5:2These were Solomon’s requirements for one day: thirty kors of fine flour and sixty kors of pounded meal,

5:3ten choice calves, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and a hundred sheep, besides deer, gazelles, and choice birds—fattened provisions.

5:4For he was ruler beyond the River, and he had peace on every side around him.
