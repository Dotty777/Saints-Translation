# 1 Kings — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 22 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 822 verse units received an English editorial review: 285 changed and 537 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/1KI.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernized ordinary royal address and finite verbs; kept the knowing euphemism, sinners, the repeated invitations, and the earth bursting at the people's sound. Rendered the dismissal at 1:53 as 'Come now, go to your house.'

Pending: Independent review of the oath's negative syntax at 1:51 and the contextual sense of sinners at 1:21; full critical apparatus review.

**Chapter 2.** Retained Solomon's twelve years, Camps, Hades, the differing Bahurim/Hebron locations, repeated Shimei account, and all lettered additions and skipped letter labels. Ordinary address now uses you/your; source-specific past tenses and figures remain.

Pending: Independent review of the expanded and repeated accounts, administrative titles and figures, and oath syntax; Göttingen and apparatus comparison.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved direct prayer Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine in 3:6–9, but ordinary you/your in God's reply. Clarified the highest place at Gibeon, retaining the superlative. Kept going out/coming in, hearing heart, weighty people, and the mother's womb imagery. The inherited absence of verse 1 remains.

Pending: Independent lexical review of the highest place, weighty people, and womb idiom; full apparatus and human read-aloud review.

**Chapter 4.** Retained the official list, repeated 'one', source spellings and unusual order without harmonizing names or inserting familiar Hebrew names.

Pending: Independent grammatical review of 4:11 and geographical/name identification.

**Chapter 5.** Retained five thousand songs, machir, beth, twenty thousand measures of oil and 3,600 overseers; modernized ordinary address and clarified the place specified for the timber rafts.

Pending: Machir and beth, provisioning syntax and the raft verb remain provisional; historical notes consulted for 5:25.

**Chapter 6.** Retained 440 years, source temple measurements, the unworked/sharply cut stone tension, and the sentence continuing from 6:17 into 6:19 across the numbered gap. Existing English retained throughout.

Pending: Architectural terminology, the stone wording, and dimensions require independent specialist review; historical note on 6:7 consulted.

**Chapter 7.** Retained source sequence, father/Hiram grammatical attachment, fourteen-cubit pillar circumference, thirty-three-cubit Sea circumference, choes and all figures. Existing English retained.

Pending: Technical architectural terms and the attachment in 7:2 remain provisional; historical note on 7:2 consulted.

**Chapter 8.** Preserved direct divine address in the prayer and 8:59, but ordinary you/your inside the quoted promise at 8:25. Modernized third-person verbs within the prayer. Preserved consecrated things, singular/plural shifts, repeated divine name, and the complete 8:53a poem including its unanchored continuation paragraph.

Pending: The poem's speaker/punctuation and My/yourself relationship remain provisional, as in the historical notes; full apparatus review pending.

**Chapter 9.** Modernized God's ordinary address to Solomon; kept My for divine self-reference. Retained disappearance, Boundary, Sophira, 120 talents, and the gap between 9:14 and 9:26.

Pending: Independent review of disappearance and the maritime syntax; no harmonization of the gold figure.

**Chapter 10.** Modernized conversation and independent possessive yours. Preserved women, spears, hewn stones, four thousand mares, trading prices, and the continuing 10:22a–b clause.

Pending: The spoil/undertaking clause, technical trade vocabulary and source figures need independent review.

**Chapter 11.** Changed man of work to hard worker. Retained the single grouped 11:5–8 unit with four internal labels, two scepters beside one elsewhere, God's opposition wording, and the repeated closing notice.

Pending: Historical note on 11:34 consulted; political metaphor and difficult reason clause remain provisional.

**Chapter 12.** Preserved the alternate account in 12:24a–z, skipped letter labels, age sixteen/reign twelve, names, female heifers, collar/scorpion imagery, and little part without supplying finger. Modernized ordinary address.

Pending: Independent comparison of the alternate accounts and little part idiom; historical note on 12:10 consulted.

**Chapter 13.** Modernized ordinary address while retaining the altar address, fat, the ambiguous he in 13:6 and the warning narrative. Preserved numbered gap 13:27.

Pending: Independent review of the ambiguous agent in 13:6, 13:31 and the complete apparatus.

**Chapter 14.** Retained all existing English, including age forty-one/reign seventeen, conspiracy and the guards placing shields on the king. The chapter still begins at 14:21.

Pending: Conspiracy and the active shield construction remain provisional; historical notes on 14:24, 27 consulted.

**Chapter 15.** Modernized ordinary address and spoke. Retained six years, Ana, initiatory rites, gathering, hiding places and repeated pillars rather than harmonizing cult terms.

Pending: Independent review of cult terminology and hiding places; historical notes on 15:12–15 consulted.

**Chapter 16.** Retained cave, unions, nasib, source chronological placement and the your/my distinction in 16:28g. Modernized ordinary address and dies.

Pending: The compressed nasib clause, cult vocabulary and agency of the self-destruction clause need specialist review.

**Chapter 17.** Kept Thou/Thee in Elijah's direct prayers; ordinary requests now use please and you. Preserved children plural, water jar, Witness of the widow, breathing into the child and the child's cry.

Pending: Independent review of Witness, breathing and the soul/life expression; full apparatus review.

**Chapter 18.** Preserved direct prayer in 18:36–37, repeated Hear me, burned kingdom, knees, rain's footsteps, footprint-shaped cloud and Ahab's weeping. Modernized ordinary conversation.

Pending: Lexical and textual review of burned, turned backward, footprint and wept; no harmonization to familiar Hebrew-based wording.

**Chapter 19.** Retained divine address in 19:4, 10 and 14; ordinary you in God's replies. Supplied natural English finite verbs in the wind/fire sequence while preserving repetition, the gentle breeze and 'there was the Lord'. Retained 'You will leave' in 19:18.

Pending: Independent review of the breeze expression, cloak terminology, 19:18's second person and the Elisha farewell.

**Chapter 20.** Retained Naboth's story here in Greek chapter order, blessed in quotation marks, the swine/dogs/prostitutes wording and Ahab's narrated mourning. Modernized ordinary address, possessives and finite verbs.

Pending: Independent review of the blessed euphemism, in vain and the difficult sackcloth chronology; source order remains distinct from Hebrew.

**Chapter 21.** Kept the war narrative in Greek chapter order, foxes, bent/upright proverb, sixty thousand and other source figures, continuous 21:19–20 sentence, and the unusual ambush/murder reply. Improved ordinary requests and possessives.

Pending: Independent linguistic review of 21:10–11, the agent in 21:19–20 and the king's reply at 21:40; full apparatus review.

**Chapter 22.** Modernized ordinary address and requests; retained Micaiah's repetitions, lying spirit, my clothing, good aim/lung wording, swine and prostitutes, source order and numbered gaps.

Pending: Independent lexical and syntactic review of Micaiah's opening, the wound description and the battle/rout repetition.
