## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved command/stewardship, love/heart/conscience/faith, law/lawfully, every item of the vice list, sound teaching, grace overflowing, foremost, ages, the military campaign and shipwreck. Smoothed urged, attention, sincere faith, the trustworthy saying and Timothy’s address. The interrupted opening at 1:3–4 remains; the shorter only God at 1:17 is retained.

Pending: Review stewardship at 1:4, father/mother striking versus killing at 1:9, the historical scope of men who bed males and enslavers at 1:10, and the intended relation of the entrusted command to the prophecies in 1:18. The vice-list wording is not a modern identity label. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved four distinct prayer terms, all people, two occurrences of one, explicit humanity of Christ, ransom, proper times, holy hands, dress/good works, quietness/submission, Adam/Eve, and the singular she/plural they in 2:15. Smoothed urge/petitions/people. Retained the bracketed also at 2:9 and the inherited provisional authority wording at 2:12.

Pending: Review exercise authority/domineer/usurp at 2:12, woman/wife and man/husband in context, quietness, and salvation through childbearing at 2:15. Preserve the singular/plural shift and examine the perfect verb at 2:14; do not derive a medical guarantee or settle modern offices by one gloss. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved oversight/good work, all qualifications, one wife, household/Church, newly planted, devil/snare, double-tongued, conscience, testing, women, standing, household/pillar/foundation and all six passive confession clauses. Modernized desires/does and smoothed trustworthy, wine and common confession. Retained He who at 3:16 rather than substituting God, and retained the inherited lowercase spirit provisionally.

Pending: Review the attachment of the trustworthy saying, one-wife expressions, women/wives at 3:11, newly planted and devil’s judgment at 3:6, pillar/foundation at 3:15, and spirit/Spirit and the relative-pronoun variant at 3:16. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved apostasy, spirits/demons, seared conscience, marriage/foods/thanksgiving, nourishment, training, promise of life, prophecy/hands/elders and save yourself/hearers. Smoothed several phrases and used the same trustworthy-saying wording as 1:15. At 4:10, struggle replaces inherited suffer reproach following consulted NA28 agonizometha; the older wording is archived. The shorter virtues list at 4:12 remains.

Pending: Review seared/branded conscience, the implied require abstinence in 4:3, the range of little in 4:8 including limited duration, Savior of all people especially believers in 4:10, public reading at 4:13 and the wording of salvation in 4:16. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserved father/brother/mother/sister relations, true widows, children/grandchildren, living/dead, sixty years, one husband, each if, washed feet, household care, double honor, wages, two or three witnesses, impartiality, hands and hidden/evident works. Modernized third-person forms, petitions and word order. At 5:11, their desires turn them against Christ makes the inherited wanton expression readable; the difficult meaning remains flagged. Retained believing woman alone at 5:16 and the shorter Christ Jesus title in 5:21.

Pending: Review widow enrollment and first faith/pledge in 5:9–16, the desire expression in 5:11, double honor, the scope of those who sin in 5:20, and the laying-on-of-hands context in 5:22. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Preserved slave/yoke, brothers, sound/sick, gain/contentment, coverings, snare/plunging, root/love of money, pierced pains, pursuit/fight, good confession, Pontius Pilate, King/Lord, unapproachable light, treasure/foundation and entrusted deposit. Smoothed ordinary verbs and several phrases; retained the repeated fight words. You at 6:13 reflects bracketed NA28 wording. The final relative construction is now two clear sentences; the final you is plural, and no Amen is added.

Pending: Review the causal connection at 6:7, coverings at 6:8, a root and all evils at 6:10, preserves alive/gives life at 6:13, before/under Pontius Pilate, and the scope of the wealth instructions. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
