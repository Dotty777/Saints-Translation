## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** The repeated what clauses, seeing/hearing/touching, manifested life, fellowship, walking, light/darkness and confession remain. Our joy in 1:4 and Jesus His Son without an added Christ in 1:7 follow the consulted wording. Cleanses is modern third-person English; every sin and plural our sins remain distinct. All unrighteousness in 1:9 uses natural English for the mass noun.

Pending: Review the relative-clause structure in 1:1–3, the word-of-life expression and the aspects of seen/heard and cleansing; no once-only chronology is inferred from the present tense. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary you and modern verbs restored. Advocate and propitiation remain provisional inherited terms, with the whole world clause kept intact. The old/new commandment, repeated I write/I have written, little children/children/fathers/young men, last hour/antichrists, and abiding/anointing language remain. All of you know in 2:20 is not changed to you know all things. The long 2:27 sentence is repunctuated for spoken clarity without deleting its truth/no-lie contrast.

Pending: Review hilasmos/propitiation as atoning means or sacrifice (2:2), the love-of-God/Father genitives, the scope of not all in 2:19, indicative/imperative readings in 2:27,29, and the referents of He/Him in 2:28–29. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modern verbs restored while the children, birth, seed, abiding, death/life and closed-heart images remain. Children in 3:7 reflects paidia, distinguished from little children/teknia in 3:18. This world’s goods smooths the means-of-living expression in 3:17. Future we will know/reassure is preserved in 3:19. The singular our heart remains in 3:19–21. Does not sin, does not practice sin and cannot sin are retained without inserting habitually as an automatic tense gloss.

Pending: Priority review of the sin statements in 3:6,9 alongside 1:8–2:2; the birth/seed language and the punctuation/causal relationship of 3:19–20. Practice sin is a contextual rendering of doing sin, not a claim that present tense always means habitual action. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modern verbs restored. In 4:8 has not come to know expresses the failure to know in natural English, retaining its past/result orientation rather than replacing it with a simple present does not know. God remains the subject of God is love. Only Son, world, abiding, Spirit, complete love and punishment remain. We love in 4:19 has no supplied Him, matching the consulted shorter wording.

Pending: Review the monogenes/only expression (4:9), hilasmos/propitiation (4:10), the among us/for us expression (4:9,16), the completion language and the pronoun referents in 4:17. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Modern verbs restored; birth/begotten, victory, water/blood and witness repetitions remain. Verses 5:7 and 5:8 keep separate anchors and the consulted shorter wording without the heavenly-witness expansion. The difficult prayer clause in 5:16 is smoothed by committing a sin, retaining its pronouns and distinction of sin leading/not leading to death. All unrighteousness is natural English for the mass noun in 5:17. Keeps himself at 5:18 follows the consulted NA28 reflexive heauton, replacing inherited keeps him. The closing idols warning remains without an added Amen.

Pending: Priority apparatus/ECM review of 5:18’s reflexive and the referent of the one born of God. Review the agents in 5:16, the sin/death distinction, the water/blood witness language, and the referent of This in 5:20. The heavenly-witness variant belongs in separate comparative notes, not in the NA28-based reading text. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
