PSALMS — BESPOKE SAINTS ENGLISH TRANSLATION
14 October 2026
Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
Plain text Scripture only. Separated editorial notes and unresolved readings are in editorial-notes.json, source-checks.json and the HTML reader.


PSALMS 1

1:1 Blessed is the man who did not walk in the counsel of the ungodly,
and did not stand in the way of sinners,
and did not sit in the seat of pestilent men;
1:2 but his will is in the Lord’s law,
and on His law he will meditate day and night.
1:3 He will be like the tree planted beside the outlets of waters,
which will give its fruit in its season,
and whose leaf will not fall off;
and everything he does will be prospered.
1:4 Not so the ungodly, not so,
but like chaff which the wind flings away from the face of the earth.
1:5 Therefore the ungodly will not rise in judgment,
nor sinners in the council of the righteous;
1:6 for the Lord knows the way of the righteous,
and the way of the ungodly will perish.

PSALMS 2

2:1 Why did nations rage,
and peoples meditate on empty things?
2:2 The kings of the earth stood ready,
and the rulers gathered together,
against the Lord and against His anointed.
Diapsalma.
2:3 “Let us tear apart their bonds
and cast their yoke away from us.”
2:4 The One dwelling in heaven will laugh at them,
and the Lord will sneer at them.
2:5 Then He will speak to them in His anger
and trouble them in His fury:
2:6 “But I was appointed king by Him
on Zion, His holy mountain,
2:7 announcing the Lord’s decree:
The Lord said to me, ‘You are My son;
today I have begotten you.
2:8 Ask of Me, and I will give you nations as your inheritance,
and the ends of the earth as your possession.
2:9 You will shepherd them with an iron rod;
you will crush them like a potter’s vessel.’”
2:10 And now, kings, understand;
be instructed, all who judge the earth.
2:11 Serve the Lord with fear,
and exult in Him with trembling.
2:12 Take hold of instruction, lest the Lord become angry
and you perish from a righteous way.
When His fury burns quickly,
blessed are all who have trusted in Him.

PSALMS 3

3:1 A psalm of David, when he was fleeing from the presence of Absalom his son.
3:2 Lord, why have those who afflict me multiplied?
Many rise up against me.
3:3 Many say to my soul,
“There is no salvation for him in his God.”
Diapsalma.
3:4 But Thou, Lord, art my helper,
my glory and the One who lifts my head.
3:5 With my voice I cried to the Lord,
and He heard me from His holy mountain.
Diapsalma.
3:6 I lay down and slept;
I was awakened, for the Lord will help me.
3:7 I will not fear tens of thousands of people
who together attack me all around.
3:8 Rise, Lord! Save me, my God!
For Thou didst strike all who were my enemies without cause;
Thou didst crush sinners’ teeth.
3:9 Salvation belongs to the Lord,
and Thy blessing is upon Thy people.

PSALMS 4

4:1 For the end, among psalms. A song of David.
4:2 When I called, the God of my righteousness heard me.
In affliction Thou madest room for me.
Have compassion on me, and hear my prayer.
4:3 Sons of men, how long will you be dull of heart?
Why do you love vanity and seek falsehood?
Diapsalma.
4:4 Know that the Lord has made His holy one wondrous;
the Lord will hear me when I cry to Him.
4:5 Be angry, and do not sin;
speak in your hearts,
and upon your beds be pierced with remorse.
Diapsalma.
4:6 Offer a sacrifice of righteousness,
and hope in the Lord.
4:7 Many say, “Who will show us good things?”
The light of Thy face was marked upon us, Lord.
4:8 Thou didst put gladness in my heart;
from the season of their grain and wine and oil, they were increased.
4:9 In peace I will both lie down and sleep,
for Thou, Lord, madest me dwell alone in hope.

PSALMS 5

5:1 For the end, concerning her who inherits. A psalm of David.
5:2 Give ear to my words, Lord;
understand my cry.
5:3 Attend to the voice of my petition,
my King and my God,
for unto Thee I will pray, Lord.
5:4 In the morning Thou wilt hear my voice;
in the morning I will stand before Thee and watch.
5:5 For Thou art not a God who desires lawlessness,
nor will an evildoer sojourn with Thee.
5:6 The lawless will not remain before Thine eyes;
Thou didst hate all who work lawlessness.
5:7 Thou wilt destroy all who speak falsehood;
the Lord abhors a man of bloodshed and deceit.
5:8 But I, through the multitude of Thy mercy, will enter Thy house;
I will bow toward Thy holy temple in fear of Thee.
5:9 Lord, guide me in Thy righteousness because of my enemies;
direct Thy way straight before me.
5:10 For there is no truth in their mouth;
their heart is vain.
Their throat is an open tomb;
with their tongues they practiced deceit.
5:11 Judge them, God!
Let them fall away from their counsels.
According to the multitude of their ungodly deeds, drive them out,
for they provoked Thee bitterly, Lord.
5:12 Let all who hope in Thee rejoice;
they will exult for ever, and Thou wilt dwell among them.
All who love Thy name will boast in Thee.
5:13 For Thou wilt bless the righteous one;
Lord, Thou didst crown us as with a weapon of favor.

PSALMS 6

6:1 For the end, among hymns, concerning the eighth. A psalm of David.
6:2 Lord, do not rebuke me in Thy fury,
nor discipline me in Thine anger.
6:3 Have mercy on me, Lord, for I am weak;
heal me, Lord, for my bones were troubled,
6:4 and my soul was greatly troubled.
And Thou, Lord—how long?
6:5 Return, Lord; rescue my soul.
Save me for the sake of Thy mercy.
6:6 For in death there is no one who remembers Thee;
and in Hades, who will give Thee thanks?
6:7 I grew weary with my groaning;
every night I will bathe my bed,
with my tears I will wet my resting place.
6:8 My eye was troubled from anger;
I grew old among all my enemies.
6:9 Stand away from me, all who work lawlessness,
for the Lord heard the voice of my weeping.
6:10 The Lord heard my petition;
the Lord accepted my prayer.
6:11 May all my enemies be ashamed and greatly troubled;
may they turn back and be greatly put to shame quickly.

PSALMS 7

7:1 A psalm of David, which he sang to the Lord concerning the words of Chousi son of Iemeni.
7:2 Lord my God, in Thee I hoped.
Save me from all who pursue me, and rescue me,
7:3 lest he seize my soul like a lion,
with no one to redeem or save.
7:4 Lord my God, if I did this,
if there is injustice in my hands,
7:5 if I repaid those who repay me evils,
then may I fall away from my enemies empty,
7:6 then may the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it,
trample my life into the earth,
and make my glory dwell in dust.
Diapsalma.
7:7 Rise, Lord, in Thine anger;
be exalted within the boundaries of my enemies.
Awake, Lord my God, in the ordinance which Thou didst command,
7:8 and an assembly of peoples will surround Thee;
and for its sake return on high.
7:9 The Lord will judge peoples.
Judge me, Lord, according to my righteousness
and according to my innocence upon me.
7:10 Let sinners’ wickedness come to an end,
and Thou wilt direct the righteous one,
God who tests hearts and kidneys.
7:11 Righteous is my help from God,
who saves those upright in heart.
7:12 God is a righteous judge, strong and patient,
not bringing wrath every day.
7:13 If you do not return, He will polish His sword;
He bent His bow and prepared it,
7:14 and in it He prepared instruments of death;
He fashioned His arrows for those being burned.
7:15 Behold, he was in labor with injustice;
he conceived trouble and gave birth to lawlessness.
7:16 He dug a pit and hollowed it out,
and he will fall into the hole he made.
7:17 His trouble will return upon his head,
and his injustice will descend upon his crown.
7:18 I will give thanks to the Lord according to His righteousness,
and sing praise to the name of the Lord Most High.

PSALMS 8

8:1 For the end, concerning the winepresses. A psalm of David.
8:2 Lord our Lord, how wondrous is Thy name in all the earth,
for Thy magnificence was raised above the heavens.
8:3 From the mouth of infants and nursing children Thou didst prepare praise,
because of Thine enemies,
to bring down the enemy and the avenger.
8:4 For I will see the heavens, works of Thy fingers,
the moon and stars which Thou didst found:
8:5 what is a human being, that Thou rememberest him,
or a son of man, that Thou visitest him?
8:6 Thou madest him a little lower than angels;
Thou didst crown him with glory and honor,
8:7 and didst appoint him over the works of Thy hands;
Thou didst subject everything beneath his feet:
8:8 all sheep and cattle,
and also the animals of the plain,
8:9 the birds of heaven and the fish of the sea,
those that travel along the paths of seas.
8:10 Lord our Lord,
how wondrous is Thy name in all the earth!

PSALMS 9

9:1 For the end, concerning the hidden things of the son. A psalm of David.
9:2 I will give thanks unto Thee, Lord, with my whole heart;
I will recount all Thy wonders.
9:3 I will rejoice and exult in Thee;
I will sing praise to Thy name, Most High.
9:4 When my enemy turns back,
they will grow weak and perish before Thee,
9:5 for Thou hast upheld my judgment and my cause;
Thou sattest on a throne, Thou who judgest righteousness.
9:6 Thou didst rebuke nations, and the ungodly one perished;
Thou didst erase their name for ever and for the age of the age.
9:7 The enemy’s swords failed utterly, and Thou didst pull down cities;
their memory perished with a noise.
9:8 And the Lord remains for ever;
He prepared His throne for judgment,
9:9 and He will judge the inhabited world in righteousness;
He will judge peoples with uprightness.
9:10 And the Lord became a refuge for the needy,
a helper at fitting times in distress.
9:11 Let those who know Thy name hope in Thee,
for Thou hast not abandoned those who seek Thee, Lord.
9:12 Sing praise to the Lord, who dwells in Zion;
announce His deeds among the nations,
9:13 for He who calls bloodshed to account remembered them;
He did not forget the cry of the needy.
9:14 Have mercy on me, Lord;
see my humiliation from my enemies,
Thou who raisest me from the gates of death,
9:15 so that I may proclaim all Thy praises
in the gates of daughter Zion;
I will exult in Thy salvation.
9:16 Nations became fixed in the destruction they brought about;
in this trap they concealed, their foot was caught.
9:17 The Lord is known as He executes judgments;
the sinner was caught in the works of his own hands.
A song of Diapsalma.
9:18 Let sinners turn back to Hades,
all nations that forget God.
9:19 For the poor will not be forgotten utterly;
the endurance of the needy will not perish for ever.
9:20 Arise, Lord; do not let a human being grow strong;
let nations be judged before Thee.
9:21 Appoint a lawgiver over them, Lord;
let nations know that they are human beings.
Diapsalma.
9:22 Why, Lord, hast Thou stood far away,
overlooking us at fitting times in distress?
9:23 As the ungodly man exalts himself, the poor man is set ablaze;
they are caught in the counsels they devise.
9:24 For the sinner is praised for the desires of his soul,
and the one who acts unjustly is blessed.
9:25 The sinner provoked the Lord.
According to the abundance of his anger, he will not seek out;
God is not before him.
9:26 His ways are profaned at every time;
Thy judgments are removed from before him;
he will lord it over all his enemies.
9:27 For he said in his heart, “I will certainly not be shaken,
from generation to generation, without adversity.”
9:28 His mouth is full of cursing, bitterness, and deceit;
under his tongue are toil and trouble.
9:29 He sits in ambush with rich people,
to kill an innocent person in hiding places;
his eyes look toward the needy.
9:30 He lurks in hiding like a lion in its enclosure;
he lurks to seize a poor man,
to seize a poor man as he drags him away.
9:31 In his trap he will humble him;
he will bow down and fall as he lords it over the needy.
9:32 For he said in his heart, “God has forgotten;
He turned His face away so as never to see.”
9:33 Arise, Lord God; let Thy hand be raised;
do not forget the needy.
9:34 For what reason did the ungodly provoke God?
For he said in his heart, “He will not call to account.”
9:35 Thou seest, for Thou perceivest trouble and anger,
to hand them over into Thy hands.
To Thee, therefore, the poor man has been left;
Thou wast helping the orphan.
9:36 Crush the arm of the sinful and evil man;
his sin will be sought out, and he will certainly not be found because of it.
9:37 The Lord will reign for ever and for the age of the age;
you will perish, nations, from His land.
9:38 The Lord heard the desire of the needy;
Thine ear attended to the preparation of their heart,
9:39 to judge for the orphan and the lowly,
so that a human being on the earth may no longer boast greatly.

PSALMS 10

10:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
In the Lord I have trusted.
How will you say to my soul,
“Move away to the mountains like a little bird”?
10:2 For behold, sinners bent a bow;
they prepared arrows for a quiver,
to shoot down in the moonless dark the upright in heart.
10:3 For what Thou didst establish, they pulled down;
but what did the righteous person do?
10:4 The Lord is in His holy temple;
the Lord’s throne is in heaven.
His eyes look toward the needy;
His eyelids examine the sons of men.
10:5 The Lord examines the righteous and the ungodly;
but the one who loves injustice hates his own soul.
10:6 He will rain traps upon sinners;
fire, sulfur, and a storm wind are the portion of their cup.
10:7 For the Lord is righteous and loved righteous deeds;
His face beheld uprightness.

PSALMS 11

11:1 For the end, concerning the eighth. A psalm of David.
11:2 Save me, Lord, for the holy one is gone;
for truths have become few among the sons of men.
11:3 Each spoke empty things to his neighbor;
deceitful lips spoke with a heart and a heart.
11:4 May the Lord destroy all deceitful lips
and a boastful tongue—
11:5 those who said, “We will magnify our tongue;
our lips are our own; who is our lord?”
11:6 “Because of the misery of the poor
and because of the groaning of the needy,
now I will arise,” says the Lord.
“I will set him in salvation; I will speak openly in him.”
11:7 The sayings of the Lord are pure sayings,
silver tried by fire, tested in the earth,
purified sevenfold.
11:8 Thou, Lord, wilt keep us
and preserve us from this generation and for ever.
11:9 The ungodly walk around;
according to Thy height, Thou heldest the sons of men in high regard.

PSALMS 12

12:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
12:2 How long, Lord, wilt Thou forget me utterly?
How long wilt Thou turn Thy face away from me?
12:3 How long shall I place counsels within my soul,
pains within my heart by day?
How long will my enemy be raised above me?
12:4 Look; hear me, Lord my God.
Give light to my eyes, lest I fall asleep into death,
12:5 lest my enemy say, “I prevailed against him.”
Those who afflict me will exult if I am shaken.
12:6 But I have hoped in Thy mercy;
my heart will exult in Thy salvation.
I will sing to the Lord, who has benefited me,
and I will sing praise to the name of the Lord Most High.

PSALMS 13

13:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
A fool said in his heart, “There is no God.”
They acted corruptly and became abominable in their practices;
there is no one who shows kindness; there is not even one.
13:2 The Lord looked down from heaven upon the sons of men,
to see whether there is anyone who understands or seeks God.
13:3 All turned aside; together they became useless;
there is no one who shows kindness, not even one.
Their throat is an open tomb;
with their tongues they practiced deceit.
The venom of asps is under their lips;
their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.
Their feet are swift to shed blood;
brokenness and misery are in their ways,
and they did not know a way of peace.
There is no fear of God before their eyes.
13:4 Will all those who practice lawlessness not know?
Those who devour my people as a meal of bread did not call upon the Lord.
13:5 There they cowered in fear where there was no fear,
for God is with a righteous generation.
13:6 You put a poor man’s counsel to shame,
because the Lord is his hope.
13:7 Who will give Israel’s salvation from Zion?
When the Lord turns back His people’s captivity,
let Jacob exult and let Israel rejoice.

PSALMS 14

14:1 A psalm of David.
Lord, who will sojourn in Thy dwelling,
and who will encamp on Thy holy mountain?
14:2 One who walks blamelessly and works righteousness,
speaking truth in his heart,
14:3 who did not act deceitfully with his tongue,
nor do evil to his neighbor,
and did not take up reproach against those nearest him.
14:4 One who acts wickedly is held in contempt before him,
but he honors those who fear the Lord;
one who swears to his neighbor and does not set it aside.
14:5 He did not lend his silver at interest
and did not take gifts against innocent people.
The one who does these things will never be shaken.

PSALMS 15

15:1 An inscription of David.
Guard me, Lord, for I have hoped in Thee.
15:2 I said to the Lord, “Thou art my Lord,
for Thou hast no need of my good things.”
15:3 For the holy ones who are in His land,
He made all His purposes wonderful among them.
15:4 Their weaknesses multiplied;
after these things they hurried.
I will certainly not gather their gatherings of bloodshed,
nor will I remember their names through my lips.
15:5 The Lord is the portion of my inheritance and my cup;
Thou art the One who restores my inheritance to me.
15:6 Measuring cords fell for me among the best places;
indeed, my inheritance is the best for me.
15:7 I will bless the Lord, who gave me understanding;
indeed, even until night my kidneys disciplined me.
15:8 I kept seeing the Lord before me continually,
because He is at my right hand, so that I may not be shaken.
15:9 Therefore my heart rejoiced, and my tongue exulted;
indeed, even my flesh will dwell in hope,
15:10 for Thou wilt not abandon my soul to Hades,
nor let Thy holy one see corruption.
15:11 Thou madest known to me ways of life;
Thou wilt fill me with gladness in Thy presence;
pleasures are at Thy right hand for ever.

PSALMS 16

16:1 A prayer of David.
Hear my righteousness, Lord;
attend to my petition;
give ear to my prayer, not from deceitful lips.
16:2 May my judgment come forth from Thy presence;
let my eyes see upright things.
16:3 Thou didst test my heart; Thou didst visit by night;
Thou didst try me by fire, and no injustice was found in me,
16:4 so that my mouth might not speak human deeds.
Because of the words of Thy lips, I kept hard paths.
16:5 Set my steps firmly in Thy paths,
so that my steps may not be shaken.
16:6 I cried out, for Thou heardest me, God;
incline Thine ear to me and hear my words.
16:7 Make Thy mercies wonderful,
Thou who savest those hoping in Thee
from those who oppose Thy right hand.
16:8 Guard me as the pupil of an eye;
in the shelter of Thy wings Thou wilt shelter me,
16:9 from the ungodly who made me miserable.
My enemies surrounded my soul.
16:10 They shut up their fat;
their mouth spoke arrogance.
16:11 Casting me out, they now surrounded me;
they set their eyes to turn aside toward the earth.
16:12 They took me up like a lion ready for the hunt
and like a young lion dwelling in hiding places.
16:13 Arise, Lord; get ahead of them and trip them up;
rescue my soul from an ungodly man,
Thy sword from the enemies of Thy hand.
16:14 Lord, separate them from the few, from the earth,
during their life.
Their belly was filled with Thy hidden things;
they were satisfied with sons
and left the remains to their infants.
16:15 But I, in righteousness, will appear before Thy face;
I will be satisfied when Thy glory appears.

PSALMS 17

17:1 For the end. Of the Lord’s servant David, who spoke the words of this song to the Lord on the day the Lord rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.
17:2 And he said:
I will love Thee, Lord, my strength.
17:3 The Lord is my firm foundation, my refuge, and my rescuer;
my God is my helper, and I will hope in Him;
my protector, the horn of my salvation, my supporter.
17:4 Praising, I will call upon the Lord,
and I will be saved from my enemies.
17:5 The birth pangs of death encompassed me,
and torrents of lawlessness threw me into turmoil.
17:6 The birth pangs of Hades surrounded me;
the traps of death overtook me.
17:7 And when I was afflicted, I called upon the Lord
and cried out to my God.
He heard my voice from His holy temple,
and my cry before Him will enter His ears.
17:8 And the earth was shaken and began to tremble;
the foundations of the mountains were troubled
and shaken because God was angry with them.
17:9 Smoke rose in His anger,
and fire from His face set things ablaze;
coals were kindled from Him.
17:10 And He bent heaven and came down,
and gloom was beneath His feet.
17:11 And He mounted cherubim and flew;
He flew on the wings of winds.
17:12 And He made darkness His hiding place;
His tent was around Him,
dark water in clouds of the air.
17:13 From the far-shining radiance before Him, the clouds passed—
hail and coals of fire.
17:14 And the Lord thundered from heaven,
and the Most High gave His voice.
17:15 And He sent out arrows and scattered them;
He multiplied lightning flashes and threw them into turmoil.
17:16 And the springs of the waters appeared,
and the foundations of the inhabited world were uncovered
at Thy rebuke, Lord,
at the blast of the spirit of Thine anger.
17:17 He sent from on high and took me;
He took me to Himself out of many waters.
17:18 He will rescue me from my powerful enemies
and from those who hate me, for they grew stronger than I.
17:19 They overtook me in the day of my affliction,
and the Lord became my support,
17:20 and He brought me out into a spacious place.
He will rescue me because He desired me.
He will rescue me from my powerful enemies
and from those who hate me.
17:21 And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness,
and according to the purity of my hands He will repay me,
17:22 because I kept the Lord’s ways
and did not act impiously by turning from my God,
17:23 because all His judgments are before me,
and I did not put His ordinances away from me.
17:24 And I will be blameless with Him
and guard myself from my lawlessness.
17:25 And the Lord will repay me according to my righteousness
and according to the purity of my hands before His eyes.
17:26 With a holy one Thou wilt show Thyself holy,
and with an innocent man Thou wilt be innocent,
17:27 and with a chosen one Thou wilt be chosen,
and with a twisted one Thou wilt twist.
17:28 For Thou wilt save a lowly people
and humble the eyes of the arrogant.
17:29 For Thou wilt light my lamp, Lord;
my God, Thou wilt light my darkness.
17:30 For by Thee I will be rescued from a raiding band,
and by my God I will cross over a wall.
17:31 My God’s way is blameless;
the Lord’s sayings are tried by fire;
He is a protector of all who hope in Him.
17:32 For who is God except the Lord?
And who is God except our God?
17:33 God girds me with power
and made my way blameless,
17:34 He sets my feet firmly like a deer’s
and makes me stand upon the heights,
17:35 training my hands for war;
and Thou madest my arms a bronze bow.
17:36 And Thou gavest me the protection of my salvation;
Thy right hand supported me,
and Thy discipline set me upright completely,
and Thy discipline itself will teach me.
17:37 Thou didst widen my steps beneath me,
and my footprints did not grow weak.
17:38 I will pursue my enemies and overtake them,
and I will not turn back until they fail.
17:39 I will press them down, and they will certainly be unable to stand;
they will fall beneath my feet.
17:40 And Thou didst gird me with power for war;
Thou didst fetter all those who rose against me beneath me,
17:41 and Thou madest my enemies turn their backs to me,
and Thou didst destroy those who hated me.
17:42 They cried out, and there was no one to save;
they cried to the Lord, and He did not hear them.
17:43 And I will grind them fine like dust before the wind;
I will smooth them away like mud in the streets.
17:44 Thou wilt rescue me from the people’s disputes;
Thou wilt appoint me head of nations;
a people I did not know served me.
17:45 At the hearing of the ear, it obeyed me;
foreigners lied to me.
17:46 Foreigners grew old
and limped away from their paths.
17:47 The Lord lives! Blessed be my God!
Let the God of my salvation be exalted,
17:48 the God who gives me acts of vengeance
and subjected peoples beneath me,
17:49 my rescuer from my wrathful enemies.
Thou wilt raise me above those who rise against me;
Thou wilt rescue me from an unjust man.
17:50 Therefore I will thank Thee among the nations, Lord,
and sing praise to Thy name,
17:51 magnifying the salvations of His king
and showing mercy to His anointed,
to David and to his seed for ever.

PSALMS 18

18:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
18:2 The heavens recount God’s glory,
and the firmament announces the work of His hands.
18:3 Day pours forth an utterance to day,
and night announces knowledge to night.
18:4 There are no utterances or words
whose voices are not heard.
18:5 Their sound went out into all the earth,
and their utterances to the limits of the inhabited world.
In the sun He set His dwelling.
18:6 And he is like a bridegroom coming out of his bridal chamber;
he will exult like a giant to run his way.
18:7 His going out is from heaven’s edge,
and his arrival reaches heaven’s edge;
there is no one who will be hidden from his heat.
18:8 The Lord’s law is blameless, turning souls;
the Lord’s testimony is trustworthy, making infants wise.
18:9 The Lord’s ordinances are upright, rejoicing the heart;
the Lord’s commandment shines afar, giving light to the eyes.
18:10 The fear of the Lord is pure, enduring for the age of the age;
the Lord’s judgments are true, justified together,
18:11 desirable beyond gold and much precious stone,
and sweeter than honey and honeycomb.
18:12 For indeed, Thy servant keeps them;
in keeping them there is great recompense.
18:13 Who will understand transgressions?
Cleanse me of my hidden things.
18:14 And spare Thy servant from others;
if they do not rule over me, then I will be blameless
and cleansed from great sin.
18:15 And the sayings of my mouth will be pleasing,
and the meditation of my heart will be before Thee continually,
Lord, my helper and my redeemer.

PSALMS 19

19:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
19:2 May the Lord hear you in the day of affliction;
may the name of Jacob’s God protect you.
19:3 May He send you help from the sanctuary
and support you from Zion.
19:4 May He remember every sacrifice of yours
and make your whole burnt offering fat.
Diapsalma.
19:5 May He give you according to your heart
and fulfill all your counsel.
19:6 We will exult in your salvation,
and in our God’s name we will be magnified.
May the Lord fulfill all your petitions.
19:7 Now I know that the Lord saved His anointed;
He will hear him from His holy heaven;
in mighty acts is the salvation of His right hand.
19:8 These rely on chariots, and those on horses,
but we will be magnified in the name of the Lord our God.
19:9 They were fettered and fell,
but we rose and were set upright.
19:10 Lord, save Thy king,
and hear us on whatever day we call upon Thee.

PSALMS 20

20:1 For the end. A psalm of David.
20:2 Lord, the king will rejoice in Thy power
and exult greatly in Thy salvation.
20:3 Thou gavest him the desire of his soul
and didst not deprive him of the wish of his lips.
Diapsalma.
20:4 For Thou didst meet him with blessings of kindness;
Thou didst place upon his head a crown of precious stone.
20:5 He asked Thee for life, and Thou gavest it to him,
length of days for the age of the age.
20:6 His glory is great in Thy salvation;
Thou wilt bestow glory and magnificence upon him.
20:7 For Thou wilt give him blessing for the age of the age;
Thou wilt gladden him with joy in Thy presence.
20:8 For the king hopes in the Lord,
and through the Most High’s mercy he will certainly not be shaken.
20:9 May your hand be found by all your enemies;
may your right hand find all who hate you.
20:10 You will make them like a fiery furnace at the time of your presence;
the Lord in His anger will throw them into turmoil,
and fire will devour them.
20:11 You will destroy their fruit from the earth
and their seed from the sons of men,
20:12 because they directed evils against you;
they devised a counsel they will certainly be unable to establish.
20:13 For you will make them turn their backs;
among your remaining ones you will prepare their face.
20:14 Be exalted, Lord, in Thy power;
we will sing and make music of Thy mighty acts.

PSALMS 21

21:1 For the end, concerning the help of the morning. A psalm of David.
21:2 God, my God, attend to me; why didst Thou abandon me?
Far from my salvation are the words of my transgressions.
21:3 My God, I will cry by day, and Thou wilt not hear,
and by night, and not to folly for me.
21:4 But Thou dwellest among holy ones,
the praise of Israel.
21:5 In Thee our fathers hoped;
they hoped, and Thou didst rescue them.
21:6 To Thee they cried out and were saved;
in Thee they hoped and were not put to shame.
21:7 But I am a worm and not a human being,
a human reproach and an object of the people’s contempt.
21:8 All who watch me sneered at me;
they spoke with their lips and shook their heads:
21:9 “He hoped in the Lord; let Him rescue him;
let Him save him, since He desires him.”
21:10 For Thou art the One who drew me from the belly,
my hope from my mother’s breasts.
21:11 Upon Thee I was cast from the womb;
from my mother’s belly Thou art my God.
21:12 Do not stand away from me, for affliction is near,
for there is no one to help.
21:13 Many calves surrounded me;
fat bulls encompassed me.
21:14 They opened their mouth against me
like a lion seizing prey and roaring.
21:15 I was poured out like water,
and all my bones were scattered.
My heart became like wax
melting in the midst of my belly.
21:16 My strength dried up like a potsherd;
my tongue is stuck to my throat,
and Thou broughtest me down into the dust of death.
21:17 For many dogs surrounded me;
a gathering of evildoers encompassed me;
they dug into my hands and feet.
21:18 I counted all my bones;
but they observed and looked upon me.
21:19 They divided my garments among themselves,
and cast a lot for my clothing.
21:20 But Thou, Lord, do not put my help far away;
attend to my support.
21:21 Rescue my soul from the sword
and my only one from the dog’s hand.
21:22 Save me from a lion’s mouth
and my lowliness from the horns of one-horned animals.
21:23 I will recount Thy name to my brothers;
in the midst of the assembly I will sing hymns unto Thee:
21:24 You who fear the Lord, praise Him!
All Jacob’s seed, glorify Him!
Let all Israel’s seed fear Him,
21:25 because He did not despise or resent the poor man’s petition,
nor turn His face away from me,
and when I cried out to Him, He heard me.
21:26 From Thee comes my praise in a great assembly;
I will repay my vows before those who fear Him.
21:27 The needy will eat and be filled,
and those who seek the Lord will praise Him;
their hearts will live for the age of the age.
21:28 All the limits of the earth will remember and turn to the Lord,
and all the families of the nations will bow down before Thee,
21:29 because kingship belongs to the Lord,
and He is master of the nations.
21:30 All the prosperous of the earth ate and bowed down;
before Him all who descend into the earth will fall,
and my soul lives for Him.
21:31 And my seed will serve Him;
the coming generation will be announced to the Lord,
21:32 and they will announce His righteousness
to a people yet to be born,
because the Lord has done it.

PSALMS 22

22:1 A psalm of David.
The Lord shepherds me, and nothing shall fail me.
22:2 He made me dwell in a place of green growth;
He nourished me beside water of rest.
22:3 He turned my soul back;
He guided me upon paths of righteousness
for His name’s sake.
22:4 For even if I walk in the midst of death’s shadow,
I will fear no evils, for Thou art with me;
Thy rod and Thy staff comforted me.
22:5 Thou didst prepare a table before me opposite those who afflict me;
Thou didst enrich my head with oil,
and Thy cup is intoxicating—how excellent!
22:6 And Thy mercy will pursue me all the days of my life,
and I will dwell in the Lord’s house for length of days.

PSALMS 23

23:1 A psalm of David, for the first day of the week.
The earth and its fullness belong to the Lord,
the inhabited world and all who dwell in it.
23:2 He founded it upon seas
and prepared it upon rivers.
23:3 Who will ascend the Lord’s mountain,
and who will stand in His holy place?
23:4 One innocent in hands and pure in heart,
who did not take up his soul for emptiness
and did not swear deceitfully to his neighbor.
23:5 This one will receive blessing from the Lord
and mercy from God his Savior.
23:6 This is the generation of those who seek Him,
who seek the face of Jacob’s God.
Diapsalma.
23:7 Your rulers—lift up gates!
And be raised, eternal gates,
and the King of glory will enter.
23:8 Who is this King of glory?
The Lord, strong and powerful;
the Lord, powerful in war.
23:9 Your rulers—lift up gates!
And be raised, eternal gates,
and the King of glory will enter.
23:10 Who is this King of glory?
The Lord of powers—He is the King of glory.

PSALMS 24

24:1 A psalm of David.
To Thee, Lord, I lifted my soul, my God.
24:2 In Thee I have trusted; may I not be put to shame,
and let my enemies not laugh at me.
24:3 For indeed, all who wait for Thee will certainly not be put to shame;
let all who act lawlessly without cause be ashamed.
24:4 Make Thy ways known to me, Lord,
and teach me Thy paths.
24:5 Guide me into Thy truth
and teach me, for Thou art God my Savior,
and I waited for Thee the whole day.
24:6 Remember Thy compassions, Lord,
and Thy mercies, for they are from eternity.
24:7 Do not remember the sins of my youth or my acts of ignorance;
according to Thy mercy, remember me Thyself,
for Thy kindness’ sake, Lord.
24:8 The Lord is kind and upright;
therefore He will give sinners instruction in the way.
24:9 He will guide the meek in judgment;
He will teach the meek His ways.
24:10 All the Lord’s ways are mercy and truth
for those who seek His covenant and His testimonies.
24:11 For Thy name’s sake, Lord,
Thou wilt be gracious to my sin, for it is great.
24:12 Who is the human being who fears the Lord?
He will give him instruction in a way he chose.
24:13 His soul will lodge among good things,
and his seed will inherit land.
24:14 The Lord is the stronghold of those who fear Him,
and the Lord’s name belongs to those who fear Him,
and His covenant is to make things known to them.
24:15 My eyes are continually toward the Lord,
for He will pull my feet out of a trap.
24:16 Look upon me and have mercy on me,
for I am alone and poor.
24:17 The afflictions of my heart widened;
bring me out of my distresses.
24:18 See my humiliation and my toil,
and forgive all my sins.
24:19 See my enemies, for they multiplied
and hated me with unjust hatred.
24:20 Guard my soul and rescue me;
may I not be put to shame, for I hoped in Thee.
24:21 Innocent and upright people clung to me,
for I waited for Thee, Lord.
24:22 Redeem Israel, God,
from all its afflictions.

PSALMS 25

25:1 Of David.
Judge me, Lord, for I walked in my innocence,
and hoping in the Lord, I will certainly not grow weak.
25:2 Test me, Lord, and try me;
try my kidneys and my heart by fire.
25:3 For Thy mercy is before my eyes,
and I acted pleasingly in Thy truth.
25:4 I did not sit with a council of emptiness,
and I will certainly not enter with transgressors.
25:5 I hated an assembly of evildoers,
and I will certainly not sit with the ungodly.
25:6 I will wash my hands among the innocent
and go around Thine altar, Lord,
25:7 to hear a voice of praise
and recount all Thy wonders.
25:8 Lord, I loved the beauty of Thy house
and the place of Thy glory’s dwelling.
25:9 Do not destroy my soul together with the ungodly,
or my life with men of bloodshed,
25:10 in whose hands are lawless deeds;
their right hand was filled with gifts.
25:11 But I walked in my innocence;
redeem me and have mercy on me.
25:12 For my foot stood in uprightness;
in assemblies I will bless Thee, Lord.

PSALMS 26

26:1 Of David, before being anointed.
The Lord is my light and my Savior; whom will I fear?
The Lord is the protector of my life; of whom will I be afraid?
26:2 When those doing evil approached me to eat my flesh,
those who afflict me and my enemies,
they grew weak and fell.
26:3 Even if an army draws up against me, my heart will not fear;
even if war rises against me, in this I hope.
26:4 One thing I asked from the Lord; this I will seek:
that I may dwell in the Lord’s house all the days of my life,
that I may behold the Lord’s loveliness
and visit His temple.
26:5 For He hid me in a tent in the day of my troubles;
He sheltered me in the hiding place of His tent;
He raised me upon a rock.
26:6 And now, behold, He raised my head above my enemies;
I went around and sacrificed in His tent a sacrifice of shouting;
I will sing and make music to the Lord.
26:7 Hear, Lord, my voice with which I cried;
have mercy on me and hear me.
26:8 My heart said unto Thee, “My face sought Thee.”
Thy face, Lord, I will seek.
26:9 Do not turn Thy face away from me;
do not turn aside in anger from Thy servant.
Become my helper; do not reject me
or abandon me, God my Savior.
26:10 For my father and my mother abandoned me,
but the Lord took me to Himself.
26:11 Give me instruction in Thy way, Lord,
and guide me on an upright path because of my enemies.
26:12 Do not hand me over to the souls of those who afflict me,
for unjust witnesses rose against me,
and injustice lied to itself.
26:13 I believe that I will see the Lord’s good things in the land of the living.
26:14 Wait for the Lord;
act courageously, and let your heart be strong,
and wait for the Lord.

PSALMS 27

27:1 Of David.
To Thee, Lord, I cried;
my God, do not keep silence toward me,
lest Thou keep silence toward me
and I become like those descending into a pit.
27:2 Hear the voice of my petition as I petition Thee,
as I lift my hands toward Thy holy temple.
27:3 Do not drag my soul away together with sinners,
and do not destroy me together with those working injustice,
who speak peace with their neighbors
while evils are in their hearts.
27:4 Give them according to their works
and according to the wickedness of their practices;
give them according to the works of their hands;
repay their recompense to them.
27:5 Because they did not understand the Lord’s works
or the works of His hands,
Thou wilt pull them down and certainly not build them up.
27:6 Blessed be the Lord,
because He heard the voice of my petition.
27:7 The Lord is my helper and my protector;
in Him my heart hoped, and I was helped,
and my flesh blossomed anew;
from my own will I will thank Him.
27:8 The Lord is the stronghold of His people
and the protector of the salvations of His anointed.
27:9 Save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance;
shepherd them and lift them up for ever.

and didst not gladden my enemies over me.
29:3 Lord my God, I cried out unto Thee,
and Thou didst heal me.
29:4 Lord, Thou broughtest my soul up from Hades;
Thou didst save me from those descending into a pit.
29:5 Sing praise to the Lord, His holy ones,
and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness,
29:6 because anger is in His wrath,
and life in His will;
weeping will lodge in the evening,
and exultation comes toward morning.
29:7 But I said in my prosperity,
“I will certainly never be shaken.”

but Thou didst turn Thy face away, and I became troubled.

and to my God I will make my petition:

PSALMS 30

30:1 For the end. A psalm of David, of alarm.
30:2 In Thee, Lord, I hoped; may I never be put to shame;
in Thy righteousness rescue me and bring me out.
30:3 Incline Thine ear to me;
hasten to bring me out;
be for me a protecting God
and a house of refuge to save me.
30:4 For Thou art my stronghold and my refuge,
and for Thy name’s sake Thou wilt guide me and nourish me.
30:5 Thou wilt bring me out of this trap they hid for me,
for Thou art my protector.
30:6 Into Thy hands I will entrust my spirit;
Thou didst redeem me, Lord, God of truth.
30:7 Thou didst hate those who guard vanities to no purpose;
but I hoped in the Lord.
30:8 I will exult and rejoice in Thy mercy,
because Thou didst look upon my humiliation;
Thou didst save my soul from constraints
30:9 and didst not enclose me in an enemy’s hands;
Thou didst set my feet in a spacious place.
30:10 Have mercy on me, Lord, for I am afflicted;
my eye was troubled with anger,
my soul and my belly too.
30:11 For my life failed in pain
and my years in groaning;
my strength grew weak in poverty,
and my bones were troubled.
30:12 Beyond all my enemies I became a reproach,
and greatly so to my neighbors,
and a fear to my acquaintances;
those who watched me outside fled from me.
30:13 I was forgotten from the heart like a dead person;
I became like a lost vessel.
30:14 For I heard the reproach of many dwelling all around;
as they gathered together against me,
they planned to take my soul.
30:15 But I hoped in Thee, Lord;
I said, “Thou art my God.”
30:16 My seasons are in Thy hands;
rescue me from the hand of my enemies
and from those who pursue me.
30:17 Make Thy face shine upon Thy servant;
save me in Thy mercy.
30:18 Lord, may I not be put to shame, for I called upon Thee;
may the ungodly be ashamed and brought down to Hades.
30:19 Let deceitful lips become speechless,
those that speak lawlessness against the righteous
in arrogance and contempt.
30:20 How great is the multitude of Thy kindness, Lord,
which Thou didst conceal for those who fear Thee,
which Thou didst accomplish for those who hope in Thee
before the sons of men!
30:21 Thou wilt hide them in the hiding place of Thy presence from human turmoil;
Thou wilt shelter them in a tent from the contradiction of tongues.
30:22 Blessed be the Lord,
because He made His mercy wonderful in an enclosed city.
30:23 But I said in my alarm,
“So I have been cast away from before Thine eyes.”
For this reason Thou heardest the voice of my petition when I cried unto Thee.
30:24 Love the Lord, all His holy ones,
because the Lord seeks truths
and repays those who act with abundant arrogance.
30:25 Act courageously, and let your heart be strong,
all who hope in the Lord.

PSALMS 31

31:1 Of David. Of understanding.
Blessed are those whose lawless deeds are forgiven
and whose sins are covered.
31:2 Blessed is the man whose sin the Lord will certainly not reckon,
and in whose mouth there is no deceit.
31:3 Because I kept silent, my bones grew old
from my crying out all day long.
31:4 For day and night Thy hand weighed heavily upon me;
I was turned to misery when a thorn became lodged. Diapsalma.
31:5 I made my sin known,
and I did not cover my lawlessness.
I said, “I will confess my lawlessness against myself to the Lord,”
and Thou forgavest the impiety of my sin. Diapsalma.
31:6 For this every holy one will pray unto Thee at a fitting time;
yet in a flood of many waters they will not approach him.
31:7 Thou art my refuge from the affliction surrounding me;
my exultation, redeem me from those who have encircled me. Diapsalma.

31:11 Rejoice in the Lord and exult, righteous ones;

PSALMS 32

32:1 Of David.
Exult in the Lord, righteous ones;
praise is fitting for the upright.
32:2 Give thanks to the Lord with the lyre;
make music to Him with a ten-stringed psaltery.
32:3 Sing Him a new song;
make music well with a shout of triumph.
32:4 For the Lord’s word is upright,
and all His works are in faithfulness.
32:5 He loves mercy and judgment;
the earth is full of the Lord’s mercy.
32:6 By the Lord’s word the heavens were established,
and by the spirit of His mouth all their power.
32:7 He gathers the sea’s waters like a wineskin;
He places the deeps in storehouses.
32:8 Let all the earth fear the Lord;
let all who inhabit the world be shaken before Him.
32:9 For He spoke, and they came to be;
He commanded, and they were created.
32:10 The Lord scatters the counsels of nations;
He nullifies the plans of peoples
and nullifies the counsels of rulers.
32:11 But the Lord’s counsel remains for ever,
the plans of His heart from generation to generation.
32:12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord,
the people He chose as an inheritance for Himself.
32:13 From heaven the Lord looked down;
He saw all the sons of men.
32:14 From His prepared dwelling place He looked
upon all who inhabit the earth.
32:15 He formed their hearts individually;
He understands all their deeds.
32:16 A king is not saved through a great force,
and a giant will not be saved by the abundance of his strength.
32:17 A horse is false for salvation,
and by the abundance of its power it will not be saved.
32:18 Behold, the Lord’s eyes are upon those who fear Him,
those who hope in His mercy,
32:19 to rescue their souls from death
and nourish them in famine.
32:20 Our soul waits for the Lord,
for He is our helper and defender.
32:21 For our heart will rejoice in Him,
and we have hoped in His holy name.
32:22 May Thy mercy, Lord, be upon us,
just as we have hoped in Thee.

PSALMS 34

34:1 Of David.
Judge, Lord, those who wrong me;
make war on those who make war on me.
34:2 Take hold of weapon and shield,
and rise to my aid.
34:3 Draw the sword and close the way against those pursuing me;
say to my soul, “I am your salvation.”
34:4 Let those who seek my soul be ashamed and abashed;
let those devising evils for me turn backward and be put to shame.
34:5 Let them become like dust before the wind,
with an angel of the Lord pressing them out.
34:6 Let their way become darkness and a slippery path,
with an angel of the Lord pursuing them.
34:7 For without cause they hid for me the destruction of their trap;
without purpose they reproached my soul.
34:8 Let a trap they do not know come upon them;
let the snare they hid catch them,
and into that trap they will fall.
34:9 But my soul will exult in the Lord;
it will delight in His salvation.
34:10 All my bones will say,
“Lord, who is like Thee,
rescuing the poor from the hand of those stronger than he is,
and the poor and needy from those plundering him?”
34:11 Unjust witnesses rose up;
they questioned me about what I did not know.
34:12 They repaid me evils for good things,
and childlessness to my soul.
34:13 But when they troubled me, I put on sackcloth
and humbled my soul with fasting;
and my prayer will return to my bosom.
34:14 As for a neighbor, as for our brother, so I sought to please;
like one mourning and downcast, so I humbled myself.
34:15 But against me they rejoiced and gathered;
scourges gathered against me, and I did not know;
they were torn apart and felt no remorse.
34:16 They tested me and mocked me with mockery;
they gnashed their teeth at me.
34:17 Lord, when wilt Thou look upon this?
Restore my soul from their wickedness,
my only life from lions.
34:18 I will thank Thee, Lord, in a great assembly;
among a weighty people I will praise Thee.
34:19 Let those unjustly hostile to me not rejoice over me,
those who hate me without cause and signal with their eyes.
34:20 For to me they spoke peaceful things,
but in anger they devised deceits.
34:21 They opened their mouth wide against me;
they said, “Well done! Well done! Our eyes have seen!”
34:22 Thou hast seen, Lord; do not remain silent.
Lord, do not depart from me.
34:23 Awake, Lord, and attend to my judgment,
my God and my Lord, to my case.
34:24 Judge me according to Thy righteousness, Lord my God;
let them not rejoice over me.
34:25 Let them not say in their hearts, “Well done! Well done for our soul!”
Nor let them say, “We have swallowed him.”
34:26 May those rejoicing at my troubles be ashamed and abashed together;
let those boasting against me wear shame and humiliation.
34:27 May those who desire my righteousness exult and rejoice;
let them always say, “May the Lord be magnified”—
those who desire His servant’s peace.
34:28 My tongue will meditate on Thy righteousness,
on Thy praise all day long.

PSALMS 35

35:1 For the end. Of the Lord’s servant, of David.
35:2 The lawless person speaks within himself of sinning;
there is no fear of God before his eyes.
35:3 For he acted deceitfully in his sight,
as to finding his lawlessness and hating it.
35:4 The words of his mouth are lawlessness and deceit;
he was unwilling to understand so as to do good.
35:5 He devised lawlessness upon his bed;
he stood by every way that was not good,
and he was not offended by wickedness.

151:1 This psalm is personally written concerning David and is outside the number, when he fought Goliath in single combat.
I was small among my brothers
and younger in my father’s house;
I was shepherding my father’s sheep.
151:2 My hands made an instrument;
my fingers fitted a psaltery.
151:3 And who shall tell my Lord?
He Himself is Lord; He Himself hears.
151:4 He Himself sent out His angel
and took me from my father’s sheep
and anointed me with the oil of His anointing.
151:5 My brothers were handsome and tall,
but the Lord took no pleasure in them.
151:6 I went out to meet the foreigner,
and he cursed me by his idols.
151:7 But I drew the sword he carried,
cut off his head,
and removed reproach from the sons of Israel.
