## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved every name, prisoner and fellow-soldier images, thanksgiving and prayer, love/faith, fellowship, repeated hearts/refreshment, old man, fatherhood in chains, useless/useful, voluntary rather than compelled action, perhaps and temporary/forever, slave/brother, the conditional wrong/debt and account, Paul’s own hand, lodging and final grace. Address to Philemon uses you/your. You all at 1:3 makes the plural greeting explicit. Both at 1:11 reflects the bracketed first kai in the consulted Greek. Among us at 1:6 and in Christ at 1:20 follow the consulted text; no final Amen is added.
