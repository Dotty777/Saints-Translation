Hebrews 1

Read this chapter on staging

God Has Spoken by His Son

1:1In many portions and in many ways, God spoke long ago to the fathers in the prophets,

1:2but in these last days He spoke to us in a Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom He also made the ages.

1:3He is the radiance of His glory and the imprint of His being, upholding all things by the word of His power. Having made purification of sins, He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,

1:4having become so much greater than the angels as the name He has inherited is more excellent than theirs.

The Son Superior to the Angels

1:5For to which of the angels did He ever say, “Thou art My Son; today I have begotten Thee”? And again, “I will be a Father to Him, and He will be a Son to Me”?

1:6And when He again brings the firstborn into the inhabited world, He says, “And let all God’s angels worship Him.”

1:7And concerning the angels He says, “He makes His angels spirits, and His ministers a flame of fire.”

1:8But concerning the Son, “Thy throne, O God, is for the age of the age, and the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of Thy kingdom.

1:9Thou didst love righteousness and hate lawlessness; for this reason God, Thy God, anointed Thee with the oil of exultation beyond Thy companions.”

1:10And, “Thou, Lord, didst lay the foundation of the earth in the beginning, and the heavens are the works of Thy hands.

1:11They will perish, but Thou remainest; and all will grow old like a garment,

1:12and like a cloak Thou wilt roll them up; like a garment they will also be changed. But Thou art the same, and Thy years will not fail.”

1:13But to which of the angels has He ever said, “Sit at My right hand until I make Thine enemies a footstool for Thy feet”?

1:14Are they not all ministering spirits, sent out for service for the sake of those who are to inherit salvation?

Hebrews 2

Read this chapter on staging

A Warning Against Drifting Away

2:1For this reason we must pay all the more attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away.

2:2For if the word spoken through angels became firm, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompense,

2:3how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation? It was first spoken through the Lord and was confirmed to us by those who heard,

2:4God bearing witness together with signs and wonders and various mighty deeds and distributions of the Holy Spirit according to His own will.

The World to Come Subjected to the Son

2:5For He did not subject to angels the inhabited world to come, of which we speak.

2:6But someone testified somewhere, saying, “What is a human being, that Thou rememberest him, or a son of man, that Thou carest for him?

2:7Thou didst make him a little lower than angels; Thou didst crown him with glory and honor;

2:8Thou didst subject all things beneath his feet.” For in subjecting all things to him, He left nothing unsubjected to him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to him.

2:9But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than angels, crowned with glory and honor because of the suffering of death, so that by the grace of God He might taste death on behalf of everyone.

2:10For it was fitting for Him, for whom are all things and through whom are all things, in bringing many sons into glory, to perfect the pioneer of their salvation through sufferings.

2:11For both the One who sanctifies and those being sanctified are all from one. For this reason He is not ashamed to call them brothers,

2:12saying, “I will announce Thy name to My brothers; in the midst of the assembly I will sing praise to Thee.”

2:13And again, “I will put My trust in Him.” And again, “Behold, I and the children whom God gave Me.”

Made Like His Brothers

2:14Therefore, since the children have shared in blood and flesh, He Himself likewise partook of the same, so that through death He might render powerless the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil,

2:15and release those who through fear of death were subject to slavery throughout their whole lives.

2:16For surely it is not angels He takes hold of, but He takes hold of Abraham’s seed.

2:17Therefore He had to be made like His brothers in all things, so that He might become a merciful and faithful high priest in matters concerning God, to make propitiation for the sins of the people.

2:18For because He Himself suffered when tested, He is able to help those who are being tested.

Hebrews 3

Read this chapter on staging

Jesus Greater Than Moses

3:1Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the apostle and high priest of our confession, Jesus,

3:2who is faithful to the One who appointed Him, as Moses also was in all His house.

3:3For He has been considered worthy of greater glory than Moses, just as the one who builds a house has greater honor than the house.

3:4For every house is built by someone, but the One who built all things is God.

3:5And Moses indeed was faithful in all His house as an attendant, for a testimony of the things to be spoken,

3:6but Christ as a Son over His house. We are that house if indeed we hold fast our confidence and the boast of our hope.

Today If You Hear His Voice

3:7Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear His voice,

3:8do not harden your hearts as in the provocation, on the day of testing in the wilderness,

3:9where your fathers tested Me with a trial and saw My works

3:10for forty years. Therefore I was indignant with this generation and said, ‘They always wander in their heart, and they did not know My ways,’

3:11as I swore in My wrath, ‘They shall certainly not enter My rest.’”

An Unbelieving Heart

3:12Watch out, brothers, so that none of you has an evil, unbelieving heart that turns away from the living God.

3:13But encourage one another each day, as long as it is called “Today,” so that none of you may be hardened by the deceit of sin.

3:14For we have become partakers of Christ if indeed we hold firm the beginning of our confidence until the end,

3:15while it is said, “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation.”

3:16For who, having heard, provoked Him? Was it not all who came out of Egypt through Moses?

3:17And with whom was He indignant for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?

3:18And to whom did He swear that they would not enter His rest, except those who disobeyed?

3:19And we see that they could not enter because of unbelief.

Hebrews 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Promise of Rest

4:1Therefore let us fear, lest, while a promise remains of entering His rest, any of you should seem to have fallen short.

4:2For we too have received good news just as they did, but the word they heard did not benefit them, because they had not been united by faith with those who heard.

4:3For we who believed enter the rest, just as He has said, “As I swore in My wrath, ‘They shall certainly not enter My rest,’” although the works came into being from the foundation of the world.

4:4For He has said somewhere about the seventh day in this way, “And God rested on the seventh day from all His works.”

4:5And in this passage again, “They shall certainly not enter My rest.”

4:6Since, then, it remains for some to enter it, and those who formerly received the good news did not enter because of disobedience,

4:7He again appoints a certain day, “Today,” saying in David after so long a time, just as has been said before, “Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts.”

4:8For if Joshua had given them rest, He would not have spoken after these things about another day.

4:9So then, a Sabbath rest remains for the people of God.

4:10For the one who entered His rest also rested from his own works, just as God did from His own.

The Living and Active Word

4:11Therefore let us be diligent to enter that rest, so that no one may fall in the same pattern of disobedience.

4:12For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword, penetrating as far as the division of soul and spirit, of joints and marrow, and able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.

4:13And no created thing is hidden before Him, but all things are naked and laid open to His eyes, to whom we must give account.

A Great High Priest

4:14Therefore, having a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast the confession.

4:15For we do not have a high priest unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but One who has been tested in every way as we are, yet without sin.

4:16Therefore let us approach the throne of grace with boldness, so that we may receive mercy and find grace for help at the right time.

The High Priest Appointed by God

5:1For every high priest taken from among people is appointed on behalf of people in matters concerning God, to offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins.

5:2He can deal gently with those who are ignorant and wandering, since he too is surrounded by weakness,

5:3and because of it he is obliged to make offerings for sins on his own behalf, just as on behalf of the people.

5:4And no one takes the honor for himself, but receives it when called by God, just as Aaron was.

5:8Although He was a Son, He learned obedience from the things He suffered,

5:9and, having been perfected, He became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey Him,

5:10having been designated by God high priest according to the order of Melchizedek.

Milk and Solid Food

5:11Concerning this we have much to say, and it is hard to explain, since you have become slow to hear.

5:13For everyone who partakes of milk is inexperienced in the word of righteousness, for he is an infant.

5:14But solid food belongs to the mature, whose faculties have been trained by practice to distinguish both good and evil.

Hebrews 6

Read this chapter on staging

Pressing On to Maturity

6:1Therefore, leaving the elementary teaching about Christ, let us be carried on to maturity, without laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and faith toward God,

6:2of instruction about washings and the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.

6:3And this we will do, if indeed God permits.

6:4For it is impossible to renew again to repentance those who were once enlightened, tasted the heavenly gift, became partakers of the Holy Spirit,

6:5and tasted God’s good word and the powers of the coming age,

6:6and fell away, since they are crucifying the Son of God again for themselves and exposing Him to public disgrace.

6:7For land that drinks the rain often coming upon it, and produces vegetation useful to those for whose sake it is cultivated, shares in blessing from God.

6:8But if it bears thorns and thistles, it is rejected and near a curse; its end is burning.

Better Things Concerning You

6:9But concerning you, beloved, we are convinced of better things—things accompanying salvation—even though we speak this way.

6:10For God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love you showed toward His name by serving the saints, as you still serve them.

6:11And we desire each of you to show the same diligence toward the full assurance of hope until the end,

6:12so that you may not become sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

The Certainty of God’s Promise

6:13For when God made a promise to Abraham, since He had no one greater by whom to swear, He swore by Himself,

6:14saying, “Surely, blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you.”

6:15And so, having waited patiently, he obtained the promise.

6:16For people swear by one greater, and among them an oath for confirmation ends every dispute.

9:6With these things so arranged, the priests enter the first tent continually, carrying out the acts of worship.

9:8By this the Holy Spirit indicates that the way into the holy places has not yet been disclosed while the first tent still has its standing.

9:9This is a figure for the present time, according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered that cannot perfect the worshiper with respect to conscience.

9:10They concern only foods, drinks, and various washings: regulations for the flesh, imposed until a time of setting things right.

The Blood of Christ

9:11But Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come to be, through the greater and more perfect tent, not made by hands—that is, not of this creation—

9:12and entered the holy places once for all, not through the blood of goats and calves but through His own blood, having obtained eternal redemption.

9:13For if the blood of goats and bulls, and the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who have been defiled, sanctify for the cleanness of the flesh,

9:14how much more will the blood of Christ, who through an eternal Spirit offered Himself unblemished to God, cleanse our conscience from dead works to serve the living God!

9:15And for this reason He is mediator of a new covenant, so that those who have been called might receive the promise of the eternal inheritance, since a death has occurred for redemption from the transgressions under the first covenant.

The Testament Ratified by Death

9:16For where there is a testament, the death of the one who made it must be established.

9:17For a testament takes effect at death, since it is never in force while the one who made it lives.

9:18Therefore even the first covenant was not inaugurated without blood.

9:19For when Moses had spoken every commandment according to the law to all the people, he took the blood of the calves and the goats, with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled both the book itself and all the people,

9:20saying, “This is the blood of the covenant that God commanded for you.”

9:21And he likewise sprinkled the tent and all the vessels of ministry with the blood.

9:22And according to the law almost everything is cleansed with blood, and without the shedding of blood no forgiveness takes place.

The Heavenly Things Purified

9:23Therefore it was necessary for the copies of the things in the heavens to be cleansed with these, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.

9:24For Christ did not enter holy places made by hands, counterparts of the true ones, but heaven itself, now to appear before the face of God on our behalf.

9:25Nor did He enter to offer Himself many times, as the high priest enters the holy places year by year with another’s blood,

9:27And just as it is appointed for people to die once, and after this judgment,

9:28so also Christ, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will appear a second time, apart from sin, to those eagerly awaiting Him, for salvation.

12:6For the Lord disciplines the one He loves, and scourges every son He receives.”

12:7Endure for discipline. God deals with you as sons; for what son is there whom a father does not discipline?

12:8But if you are without discipline, in which all have become partakers, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.

12:9Furthermore, we had our fathers in the flesh to discipline us, and we respected them. But shall we not much more submit to the Father of spirits and live?

12:10For they disciplined us for a few days according to what seemed right to them, but He for our benefit, so that we may share in His holiness.

12:11Now every discipline seems for the present to be a matter of sorrow, not joy, but afterward it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those trained through it.

Pursue Peace and Holiness

12:12Therefore straighten the drooping hands and the weakened knees,

12:13and make straight tracks for your feet, so that what is lame may not be dislocated, but rather healed.

12:14Pursue peace with everyone, and sanctification, without which no one will see the Lord.

12:15Watch lest anyone fall short of the grace of God, lest any root of bitterness grow up and cause trouble, and through it many be defiled,

12:16lest anyone be sexually immoral or profane like Esau, who sold his own birthright for a single meal.

12:17For you know that even afterward, when he desired to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for repentance, although he sought it with tears.

Mount Zion and the Unshakable Kingdom

12:18For you have not approached what can be touched, and a blazing fire, and gloom and darkness and storm,

12:19and the sound of a trumpet and a voice speaking words, whose hearers begged that no further word be spoken to them.

12:20For they could not bear the command: “Even if an animal touches the mountain, it shall be stoned.”

12:21And so fearful was the sight that Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling.”

12:22But you have approached Mount Zion and the city of the living God, heavenly Jerusalem, and myriads of angels in festive gathering,

12:23and the assembly of firstborn ones enrolled in the heavens, and God the judge of all, and the spirits of righteous ones who have been perfected,

12:24and Jesus the mediator of a new covenant, and sprinkled blood that speaks better than Abel.

See That You Do Not Refuse the One Speaking

12:25See that you do not refuse the One who speaks. For if those who refused the One instructing them on earth did not escape, much less shall we, if we turn away from the One from the heavens.

12:26His voice shook the earth then, but now He has promised, saying, “Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heaven.”

12:27Now “yet once more” indicates the removal of the things shaken, as things that have been made, so that the things not shaken may remain.

12:28Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us be grateful, and through this gratitude serve God acceptably with reverence and awe,

12:29for indeed our God is a consuming fire.
