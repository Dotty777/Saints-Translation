# Hebrews — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 13 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 303 verse units received an English editorial review: 107 changed and 196 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

NA28 is the user-authorized provisional New Testament base. Focused checks of the German Bible Society’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not complete critical-text or apparatus verification. The newer Bespoke PDF’s preferred UBS6 text has not been obtained; UBS6/ECM comparison, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/HEB.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 13 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. ## Chapter decisions and review queue

Direct address to the Son and Lord now uses Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine; third-person has is modern. In these last days and lay the foundation make the temporal and building expressions readable.

Pending: Review in a Son and ages, radiance/imprint/being, again at 1:6, spirits/winds at 1:7, the throne vocative, the quotation context and referents of divine pronouns, and the relationship of 1:10–12 to its scriptural source. The shorter purification clause already present follows the consulted text; no through Himself has been restored. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved the drifting image, angels, witness/signs/wonders/Spirit distribution, the Psalm quotation, everything/subjected repetitions, glory/honor/death, pioneer and suffering, sanctification, shared blood and flesh, slavery, seed, and priestly action. Direct address to God uses Thou/Thy/Thee. At 2:9 by the grace of God replaces inherited apart from God to follow the consulted NA28 main text. To him at 2:8 reflects bracketed Greek wording.

Pending: The apart from God reading is retained in the before/after archive for textual review. Review the degree versus short-duration sense of a little in 2:7,9; the pronoun referents in 2:8; all from one at 2:11; takes hold of at 2:16; and propitiation/atonement at 2:17. No complete apparatus collation is claimed. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved apostle/high priest, Moses/Christ, all house comparisons, confidence/hope, repeated Today, testing/works/forty years, heart/ways/rest, the oath and every rhetorical question. All in 3:2 and indeed in 3:6 reflect bracketed NA28 wording. Modernized has, built and the warning/encouragement syntax. Forty years remains at the beginning of 3:10 as displayed in the consulted source.

Pending: Review appointed/made at 3:2, house/household referents, the bracketed wording, the oath idiom at 3:11 and the confidence expression at 3:14. **Chapter 4.** Preserved the warning, promise/rest and repeated oath, faith and hearing, seventh day, Today, Joshua, Sabbath rest, works, sword/soul/spirit/joints/marrow, nakedness and exposure, confession and high priest, weakness/testing/sin, and throne/grace/mercy.
