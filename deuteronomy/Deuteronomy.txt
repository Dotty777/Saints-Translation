Bespoke Saints English — Deuteronomy
Provisional editorial edition · 14 October 2026
Ordinary address uses you/your; Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine are reserved for direct address to God. Divine capitals are editorial. Full independent Greek review, Göttingen and apparatus collation, and human read-aloud review remain pending.


Deuteronomy 1

1:1 These are the words which Moses spoke unto all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness toward the west, near the Red Sea, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Aulon, and Katachrysea.
1:2 It is a journey of eleven days from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir unto Kadesh-barnea.
1:3 And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke unto all the sons of Israel, according to all that the Lord had commanded him concerning them.
1:4 After he had smitten Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,
1:5 beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to make this law plain, saying:
1:6 The Lord our God spoke unto us in Horeb, saying: Let it suffice you to have dwelt in this mountain.
1:7 Turn and set forth, and enter the hill country of the Amorites and all the neighbouring places of Arabah, the hill country and the plain, the southwest and the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites and Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river Euphrates.
1:8 Behold, I have delivered the land before you. Enter and inherit the land which I sware unto your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give unto them and unto their seed after them.
1:9 And I spoke unto you at that time, saying: I shall not be able to bear you alone.
1:10 The Lord your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are this day as the stars of heaven in multitude.
1:11 The Lord, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you, as He has spoken unto you.
1:12 How shall I alone be able to bear your trouble, and your resistance, and your disputes?
1:13 Choose for yourselves wise men, men of knowledge and understanding, for your tribes, and I will appoint them to rule over you.
1:14 And you answered me and said: The thing which you have spoken is good to do.
1:15 And I took from among you wise men, men of knowledge and understanding, and appointed them to rule over you, captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds, and captains of fifties, and captains of tens, and recording officers for your judges.
1:16 And I commanded your judges at that time, saying: Hear the matters between your brethren, and judge justly between a man and his brother and the sojourner with him.
1:17 You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall judge the small and the great alike. You shall not shrink before the face of man, for judgment belongs unto God. And you shall bring unto me the matter which is too hard for you, and I will hear it.
1:18 And I commanded you at that time all the things which you should do.
1:19 And we departed from Horeb, and went through all that great and fearful wilderness which you saw, by the way of the hill country of the Amorite, as the Lord our God commanded us; and we came unto Kadesh-barnea.
1:20 And I said unto you: You have come unto the hill country of the Amorite, which the Lord our God gives you.
1:21 Behold, the Lord your God has delivered the land before you. Go up and inherit it, as the Lord, the God of your fathers, has spoken unto you; fear not, neither be dismayed.
1:22 And you all came unto me, and said: Let us send men before us, and let them search out the land for us, and bring us word of the way by which we shall go up, and of the cities into which we shall enter.
1:23 And the saying pleased me; and I took twelve men from among you, one man from each tribe.
1:24 And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came unto the Valley of the Cluster, and searched it out.
1:25 And they took of the fruit of the land in their hands, and brought it down unto us, and said: The land which the Lord our God gives us is good.
1:26 And you would not go up, but disobeyed the word of the Lord your God.
1:27 And you murmured in your tents, and said: Because the Lord hates us, He brought us forth out of the land of Egypt, to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites, to destroy us.
1:28 Whither do we go up? Your brethren have made your heart fail, saying: The nation is great and numerous and mightier than we; and the cities are great and walled up to heaven. Moreover, we have seen sons of giants there.
1:29 And I said unto you: Neither cower nor fear them.
1:30 The Lord your God, who goes before you, shall Himself fight against them with you, according to all that He did for you in the land of Egypt,
1:31 and in this wilderness which you saw, how the Lord your God nurtured you as a man nurtures his son, in all the way which you went, until you came unto this place.
1:32 And in this matter you did not trust the Lord your God,
1:33 who goes before you in the way to choose a place for you, guiding you in fire by night, to show you the way wherein you should go, and in a cloud by day.
1:34 And the Lord heard the voice of your words, and, being provoked to anger, sware, saying:
1:35 Surely none of these men shall see this good land which I sware unto their fathers,
1:36 save Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and I will give unto him and unto his sons the land whereon he trod, because he cleaved unto the things of the Lord.
1:37 And the Lord was angry with me also for your sakes, saying: You shall not enter there either.
1:38 Joshua son of Nun, who stands beside you, he shall enter there. Strengthen him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.
1:39 And every young child who this day knows neither good nor evil shall enter there; and I will give it unto them, and they shall inherit it.
1:40 But you, turn and encamp in the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.
1:41 And you answered me and said: We have sinned before the Lord our God. We will go up and fight, according to all that the Lord our God commanded us. And you took up, every man, his weapons of war, and gathered yourselves together, and went up into the hill country.
1:42 And the Lord said unto me: Say unto them, You shall not go up, neither shall you fight, for I am not with you; and you shall not be crushed before your enemies.
1:43 And I spoke unto you, but you did not hearken unto me; and you transgressed the word of the Lord, and forced your way up into the hill country.
1:44 And the Amorites who dwelt in that hill country came out to meet you, and pursued you as bees do, and wounded you from Seir unto Hormah.
1:45 And you sat down and wept before the Lord; but the Lord did not hearken unto your voice, neither gave He heed unto you.
1:46 And you dwelt in Kadesh many days, as many days as you dwelt there.

Editorial note: The recollection, named places and judicial commands retain their order. Ordinary address uses you/your; earlier notes preserve observations about singular and plural Greek forms.

Deuteronomy 2

2:1 And we turned and departed into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea, as the Lord had spoken unto me; and we compassed Mount Seir many days.
2:2 And the Lord said unto me:
2:3 Let it suffice you to have compassed this mountain; turn therefore northward.
2:4 And command the people, saying: You are passing through the borders of your brethren, the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir; and they shall fear you, and be exceedingly wary of you.
2:5 Do not join battle with them, for I will give you none of their land, no, not so much as a footstep; for I have given Mount Seir unto the sons of Esau for a possession.
2:6 Buy food from them for silver, and eat; and you shall take water from them by measure for silver, and drink.
2:7 For the Lord our God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. Consider how you passed through that great and fearful wilderness. Behold, these forty years the Lord your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing.
2:8 And we passed by our brethren, the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, along the way of Arabah, from Ailon and from Ezion-geber; and we turned and passed along the wilderness way of Moab.
2:9 And the Lord said unto me: Do not deal as enemies with the Moabites, neither join battle with them; for I will give you none of their land for a possession, for I have given Seir unto the sons of Lot to inherit.
2:10 The Emim dwelt there aforetime, a nation great and numerous and strong as the Anakim.
2:11 These also are reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim; and the Moabites call them Emim.
2:12 And the Horites dwelt in Seir aforetime; but the sons of Esau destroyed them, and blotted them out from before them, and dwelt in their stead, as Israel did in the land of its inheritance, which the Lord gave unto them.
2:13 Now therefore arise, and set forth, and pass through the Ravine Zered. And we passed through the Ravine Zered.
2:14 And the time wherein we journeyed from Kadesh-barnea until we passed through the Ravine Zered was thirty and eight years, until the whole generation of men of war had fallen, dying out of the camp, as God had sworn unto them.
2:15 And the hand of God was upon them, to consume them out of the camp, until they had fallen.
2:16 And it came to pass, when all the men of war had fallen, dying out from among the people,
2:17 that the Lord spoke unto me, saying:
2:18 You shall pass this day through the borders of Moab, even Seir,
2:19 and you shall draw near unto the sons of Ammon. Do not deal as enemies with them, neither join battle with them; for I will give you none of the land of the sons of Ammon for a possession, because I have given it unto the sons of Lot for a possession.
2:20 It is reckoned a land of Rephaim; for the Rephaim dwelt there aforetime, and the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,
2:21 a nation great and numerous and mightier than you, like the Anakim. And the Lord destroyed them from before the Ammonites, who inherited the land and dwelt in their stead unto this day,
2:22 even as they did for the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, when they blotted out the Horites from before them, and inherited the land, and dwelt in their stead unto this day.
2:23 And the Hivites who dwelt in settlements as far as Gaza, the Cappadocians who came forth from Cappadocia blotted them out, and dwelt in their stead.
2:24 Now therefore arise, and set forth, and pass through the Ravine Arnon. Behold, I have delivered into your hands Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to inherit it; join battle with him.
2:25 This day begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you before all the nations under heaven, who, when they hear your name, shall be troubled and shall suffer pangs before you.
2:26 And I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth unto Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying:
2:27 I will pass through your land. I will pass along the way; I will turn neither to the right nor to the left.
2:28 You shall sell me food for silver, and I will eat; and you shall sell me water for silver, and I will drink. Only let me pass through on foot,
2:29 as the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, and the Moabites who dwell in Aroer, did for me, until I pass over the Jordan into the land which the Lord our God gives us.
2:30 And Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through his land; for the Lord our God hardened his spirit and strengthened his heart, that he might be delivered into your hands, as at this day.
2:31 And the Lord said unto me: Behold, I have begun to deliver before you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to inherit his land.
2:32 And Sihon king of Heshbon came out to meet us, he and all his people, unto battle at Jahaz.
2:33 And the Lord our God delivered him before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.
2:34 And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every city in succession, with their women and their children; we left none alive as a captive.
2:35 Only the cattle we took as plunder, and the spoil of the cities we took.
2:36 From Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, and the city which is in the ravine, even unto the hill country of Gilead, there was no city that escaped us; the Lord our God delivered them all into our hands.
2:37 Only unto the land of the sons of Ammon we did not draw near, all that borders upon the torrent Jabbok, and the cities in the hill country, as the Lord our God commanded us.

Editorial note: Names, territories, measures and the inherited Seir readings remain. The commands concerning neighbouring peoples and the reported violence have not been harmonized or softened.

Deuteronomy 3

3:1 And we turned and went up by the way unto Bashan; and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, unto battle at Edrei.
3:2 And the Lord said unto me: Do not fear him, for I have delivered him, and all his people, and all his land into your hands; and you shall do unto him as you did unto Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon.
3:3 And the Lord our God delivered him into our hands, even Og king of Bashan and all his people; and we smote him until none of his seed remained.
3:4 And we took all his cities at that time; there was no city which we did not take from them, sixty cities, all the region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.
3:5 All these were fortified cities, with high walls, gates, and bars, besides very many cities of the Perizzites.
3:6 We utterly destroyed them, as we did unto Sihon king of Heshbon; and we utterly destroyed every city in succession, with the women and the children.
3:7 And all the cattle and the spoil of the cities we took as plunder for ourselves.
3:8 And at that time we took the land from the hands of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the torrent Arnon unto Hermon.
3:9 The Phoenicians call Hermon Sanior, and the Amorites call it Senir.
3:10 All the cities of Misor, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, as far as Salcah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.
3:11 For only Og king of Bashan remained of the Rephaim. Behold, his bed was a bed of iron; behold, it is in the citadel of the sons of Ammon, nine cubits in length and four cubits in breadth, by the cubit of a man.
3:12 And we inherited that land at that time. From Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead, with its cities, I gave unto Reuben and unto Gad.
3:13 And the rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave unto the half tribe of Manasseh, with all the region of Argob, all that Bashan; it is reckoned a land of Rephaim.
3:14 And Jair son of Manasseh took all the region of Argob, unto the borders of Gargasi and the Maacathites; and he called those places of Bashan after his own name, Havvoth-jair, unto this day.
3:15 And unto Machir I gave Gilead.
3:16 And unto Reuben and unto Gad I gave from Gilead unto the torrent Arnon, the midst of the torrent being the border, and unto Jabbok, the torrent which is the border of the sons of Ammon;
3:17 and the Arabah, and the Jordan as the border, Machanareth, and as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, below Asedoth-Pisgah toward the east.
3:18 And I commanded you at that time, saying: The Lord your God has given you this land for a possession. Go armed before your brethren, the sons of Israel, every able man.
3:19 Only let your wives, and your children, and your cattle, for I know that you have much cattle, dwell in your cities which I have given you,
3:20 until the Lord your God gives rest unto your brethren, as unto you, and they also inherit the land which the Lord our God gives them beyond the Jordan; then you shall return, every man unto his inheritance which I have given you.
3:21 And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying: Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God has done unto these two kings; so shall the Lord our God do unto all the kingdoms whither you are passing over.
3:22 You shall not fear, for the Lord our God Himself shall fight for you.
3:23 And I besought the Lord at that time, saying:
3:24 O Lord, Lord, Thou hast begun to show Thy servant Thy strength, and Thy power, and Thy mighty hand, and Thine uplifted arm; for what god is there in heaven or upon earth who shall do as Thou hast done, and according to Thy strength?
3:25 Let me therefore pass over and see this good land beyond the Jordan, this good hill country and Anti-Lebanon.
3:26 And the Lord disregarded me for your sakes, and did not hearken unto me; and the Lord said unto me: Let it suffice you; speak no more of this matter.
3:27 Go up unto the summit of the Hewn Place, and lift up your eyes toward the sea, and northward, and toward the southwest, and eastward, and behold with your eyes; for you shall not pass over this Jordan.
3:28 And command Joshua, and strengthen him, and encourage him; for he shall pass over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land which you have seen.
3:29 And we dwelt in the valley near the house of Peor.

Editorial note: Moses’ prayer in 3:24 retains Thou/Thy/Thine. God’s reply and commands to human recipients use you/your. The geographical names, iron bed and stated dimensions remain.

Deuteronomy 4

4:1 And now, O Israel, hear the ordinances and the judgments which I teach you this day to do, that you may live and be multiplied, and enter and inherit the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, gives you.
4:2 You shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall you take away from it; you shall keep the commandments of the Lord your God, which I command you this day.
4:3 Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did unto Baal-peor; for every man who went after Baal-peor, the Lord your God blotted him out from among you.
4:4 But you who cleave unto the Lord your God are all alive this day.
4:5 Behold, I have shown you ordinances and judgments, as the Lord commanded me, that you should do so in the land whither you go to inherit it.
4:6 And you shall keep and do them; for this is your wisdom and understanding before all the nations, who shall hear all these ordinances and say: Behold, this great nation is a wise and understanding people.
4:7 For what great nation is there whose god draws near unto them, as the Lord our God in all things wherein we call upon Him?
4:8 And what great nation is there that has ordinances and judgments as righteous as all this law which I set before you this day?
4:9 Take heed unto yourself, and guard your soul diligently, lest you forget all the things which your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life. And you shall teach your sons and your sons’ sons
4:10 the day when you stood before the Lord your God in Horeb, in the day of the assembly, when the Lord said unto me: Assemble the people unto Me, and let them hear My words, that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live upon the earth, and may teach their sons.
4:11 And you drew near and stood beneath the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire unto heaven, with darkness, gloom, tempest, and a great sound.
4:12 And the Lord spoke unto you out of the midst of the fire; you heard the sound of words, but saw no likeness, only a voice.
4:13 And He declared unto you His covenant, which He commanded you to do, even the ten words; and He wrote them upon two tablets of stone.
4:14 And the Lord commanded me at that time to teach you ordinances and judgments, that you should do them in the land whither you go to inherit it.
4:15 And you shall guard your souls diligently; for you saw no likeness in the day when the Lord spoke unto you in Horeb, on the mountain, out of the midst of the fire,
4:16 lest you act lawlessly and make for yourselves a graven likeness, any image, the likeness of male or female,
4:17 a likeness of any animal on the earth, a likeness of any winged bird flying beneath heaven,
4:18 the likeness of any creeping thing that creeps upon the earth, the likeness of any fish that is in the waters beneath the earth.
4:19 And lest you lift up your eyes unto heaven, and see the sun, and the moon, and the stars, and all the adornment of heaven, and be led astray to bow down unto them and serve them, which the Lord your God has allotted unto all the nations under heaven.
4:20 But God took you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be unto Him a people of inheritance, as at this day.
4:21 And the Lord was angry with me concerning the things spoken by you, and sware that I should not pass over this Jordan, neither enter the land which the Lord God gives you for a possession.
4:22 For I die in this land, and do not pass over this Jordan; but you pass over, and shall inherit this good land.
4:23 Take heed, lest you forget the covenant of the Lord your God, which He made with you, and make for yourselves a graven likeness of anything concerning which the Lord your God has given commandment.
4:24 For the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.
4:25 And if you beget sons and sons’ sons, and you have remained long upon the land, and act lawlessly, and make a graven likeness of anything, and do evil before the Lord your God, to provoke Him to anger,
4:26 I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that you shall surely perish from the land whither you pass over the Jordan to inherit it. You shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall be utterly blotted out.
4:27 And the Lord shall scatter you among all the nations, and you shall be left few in number among the nations whither the Lord shall bring you.
4:28 And there you shall serve other gods, the works of men’s hands, wood and stone, which shall neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.
4:29 And there you shall seek the Lord your God, and shall find Him, when you seek Him with all your heart and with all your soul, in your distress.
4:30 And all these things shall find you in the latter days, and you shall return unto the Lord your God, and hearken unto His voice.
4:31 For the Lord your God is a merciful God; He will not forsake you, neither will He destroy you, nor forget the covenant of your fathers which He sware unto them.
4:32 Ask now of the former days that were before you, from the day when God created man upon the earth, and from one end of heaven unto the other: has there been anything like this great matter, or has such a thing been heard?
4:33 Has a nation heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as you have heard, and lived?
4:34 Or has God attempted to go and take unto Himself a nation from the midst of a nation, by trial, and by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand, and by an uplifted arm, and by great visions, according to all that the Lord our God did in Egypt before your eyes?
4:35 That you might know that the Lord your God, He is God, and there is none beside Him.
4:36 Out of heaven His voice was made heard, to instruct you; and upon earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words out of the midst of the fire.
4:37 Because He loved your fathers, He chose their seed after them, even you, and Himself brought you forth out of Egypt by His great strength,
4:38 to destroy nations great and mightier than you from before you, to bring you in, and to give you their land to inherit, as you have this day.
4:39 And you shall know this day, and lay it to mind, that the Lord your God, He is God in heaven above and upon earth beneath; there is none beside Him.
4:40 And you shall keep His ordinances and His commandments which I command you this day, that it may be well with you and with your sons after you, and that you may prolong your days upon the land which the Lord your God gives you for ever.
4:41 Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise,
4:42 that the slayer might flee thither, who has slain his neighbour unintentionally, having borne him no hatred aforetime; and he shall flee unto one of these cities, and live:
4:43 Bezer in the wilderness, in the plain country, for Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead for Gad; and Golan in Bashan for Manasseh.
4:44 This is the law which Moses set before the sons of Israel.
4:45 These are the testimonies, and the ordinances, and the judgments which Moses spoke unto the sons of Israel in the wilderness, when they came forth out of the land of Egypt,
4:46 beyond the Jordan, in the ravine near the house of Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel smote when they came forth out of the land of Egypt.
4:47 And they inherited his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise,
4:48 from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon torrent as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,
4:49 all the Arabah beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise, below Asedoth, the hewn height.

Editorial note: The images of fire, the iron furnace, voice and likeness remain. Negatives and the question in 4:34 have clearer English. Earlier notes retain the source’s changes between singular and plural address.

Deuteronomy 5

5:1 And Moses called all Israel, and said unto them: Hear, O Israel, the ordinances and the judgments which I speak in your ears this day; and you shall learn them, and take heed to do them.
5:2 The Lord your God made a covenant with you in Horeb.
5:3 The Lord did not make this covenant with your fathers, but with you, even you who are all here alive this day.
5:4 The Lord spoke with you face to face on the mountain, from the midst of the fire.
5:5 And I stood between the Lord and you at that time, to declare unto you the words of the Lord; for you were afraid of the fire, and did not go up into the mountain. He said:
5:6 I am the Lord your God, who brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.
5:7 You shall have no other gods before Me.
5:8 You shall not make unto yourself an idol, nor any likeness of anything in heaven above, or upon the earth beneath, or in the waters under the earth.
5:9 You shall not bow down unto them, neither shall you serve them; for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate Me,
5:10 and showing mercy unto thousands, unto them that love Me and keep My ordinances.
5:11 You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain; for the Lord will not hold him guiltless who takes His name in vain.
5:12 Keep the Sabbath day, to sanctify it, as the Lord your God commanded you.
5:13 You shall labour six days, and do all your works;
5:14 but the seventh day is a Sabbath unto the Lord your God. You shall do no work therein, you, nor your sons, nor your daughter, nor your male slave, nor your female slave, nor your ox, nor your beast of burden, nor any of your cattle, nor the sojourner dwelling with you; that your male slave and your female slave may rest as you do.
5:15 And you shall remember that you were a household slave in the land of Egypt, and that the Lord your God brought you forth thence with a mighty hand and with an uplifted arm; therefore the Lord your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day, and to sanctify it.
5:16 Honour your father and your mother, as the Lord your God commanded you, that it may be well with you, and that your days may be long upon the land which the Lord your God gives you.
5:17 You shall not commit adultery.
5:18 You shall not murder.
5:19 You shall not steal.
5:20 You shall not bear false witness against your neighbour.
5:21 You shall not covet your neighbour’s wife. You shall not covet your neighbour’s house, nor his field, nor his male slave, nor his female slave, nor his ox, nor his beast of burden, nor any of his cattle, nor anything that is your neighbour’s.
5:22 These words the Lord spoke unto all your assembly on the mountain, out of the midst of the fire, with darkness, gloom, tempest, and a great voice; and He added no more. And He wrote them upon two tablets of stone, and gave them unto me.
5:23 And it came to pass, when you heard the voice out of the midst of the fire, and the mountain burned with fire, that you drew near unto me, all the heads of your tribes and your elders,
5:24 and said: Behold, the Lord our God has shown us His glory, and we have heard His voice out of the midst of the fire. This day we have seen that God speaks with man, and he lives.
5:25 And now let us not die, for this great fire will consume us; if we hear the voice of the Lord our God any more, we shall die.
5:26 For what flesh has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as we have, and shall live?
5:27 Draw near, and hear all that the Lord our God shall say; and speak unto us all that the Lord our God shall speak unto you, and we will hear and do it.
5:28 And the Lord heard the voice of your words as you spoke unto me; and the Lord said unto me: I have heard the voice of the words of this people, which they have spoken unto you; all that they have spoken is right.
5:29 O that their heart were thus in them, to fear Me and to keep My commandments all their days, that it might be well with them and with their sons for ever!
5:30 Go, say unto them: Return unto your houses.
5:31 But you, stand here with Me, and I will speak unto you the commandments, and the ordinances, and the judgments which you shall teach them; and let them do them in the land which I give them for a possession.
5:32 And you shall take heed to do as the Lord your God commanded you; you shall turn neither to the right nor to the left,
5:33 but walk in all the way which the Lord your God commanded you, that He may give you rest, and that it may be well with you, and that you may prolong your days upon the land which you shall inherit.

Editorial note: The inherited commandment order is retained: adultery precedes murder in 5:17–18. Commands address people and use you/your; the quoted divine speaker is not the addressee. The source’s repeated covenant language remains.

Deuteronomy 6

6:1 And these are the commandments, and the ordinances, and the judgments which the Lord our God commanded me to teach you, that you should do them in the land whither you go to inherit it,
6:2 that you may fear the Lord your God, and keep all His ordinances and His commandments which I command you this day, you, and your sons, and your sons’ sons, all the days of your life, that you may prolong your days.
6:3 And hear, O Israel, and take heed to do them, that it may be well with you, and that you may multiply greatly, as the Lord, the God of your fathers, has spoken, to give you a land flowing with milk and honey.

Deuteronomy 14

14:2 For you are a holy people unto the Lord your God; and the Lord your God has chosen you to be unto Him a people for His own possession, out of all the nations upon the face of the earth.
14:3 You shall eat no abominable thing.
14:4 These are the beasts which you may eat: a calf of the cattle, and a lamb of the sheep, and a young male goat of the goats,
14:5 deer, gazelle, bubalos, goat-stag, white-rumped antelope, oryx, and giraffe.
14:6 Every beast that divides the hoof, and cleaves its hoof into two parts, and brings up the cud among the beasts, these you may eat.
14:7 And these you shall not eat of those that bring up the cud, and of those that divide the hoof and cleave its parts: the camel, and the hare, and the rock hyrax; for they bring up the cud, but do not divide the hoof; they are unclean unto you.
14:8 And the swine, for it divides the hoof and cleaves its parts, but does not chew the cud, is unclean unto you. Of their flesh you shall not eat, neither shall you touch their carcasses.
14:9 And these you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever has fins and scales, you may eat.
14:10 And whatsoever does not have fins and scales, you shall not eat; they are unclean unto you.
14:11 Every clean bird you may eat.
14:12 And these you shall not eat of them: the eagle, and the griffon vulture, and the sea-eagle,
14:13 and the vulture, and the kite, and those like it,
14:14 and every raven, and those like it,
14:15 and the ostrich, and the owl, and the gull,
14:16 and the heron, and the swan, and the ibis,
14:17 and the diving bird, and the hawk, and those like it, and the hoopoe, and the night-raven,
14:18 and the pelican, and the plover, and those like it, and the purple swamphen, and the bat.
14:19 All creeping things among the flying creatures are unclean unto you; you shall not eat of them.
14:20 Every clean flying creature you may eat.
14:21 You shall not eat anything that dies of itself; it shall be given unto the resident alien within your cities, and he shall eat it, or you may sell it unto a foreigner; for you are a holy people unto the Lord your God. You shall not boil a lamb in its mother’s milk.
14:22 You shall surely tithe all the produce of your seed, the yield of your field, year by year.
14:23 And you shall eat it before the Lord your God, in the place which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there. 14:24 And if the way is too long for you, so that you cannot carry them, because the place which the Lord your God shall choose for His name to be called upon there is far from you, when the Lord your God has blessed you,
14:25 then you shall sell them for silver, and take the silver in your hands, and go unto the place which the Lord your God shall choose.
14:26 And you shall spend the silver for whatsoever your soul desires, for cattle, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatsoever your soul desires; and you shall eat there before the Lord your God, and rejoice, you and your house,
14:27 and the Levite within your cities; for he has neither portion nor inheritance with you.
14:28 After three years you shall bring forth all the tithe of your produce; in that year you shall lay it up within your cities.
14:29 And the Levite, for he has neither portion nor inheritance with you, and the sojourner, and the orphan, and the widow within your cities shall come, and eat, and be filled; that the Lord your God may bless you in all the works which you do.

Editorial note: Animal and bird names, purification wording and the lamb in its mother’s milk remain as inherited provisional renderings. The tithes and provisions for the sojourner, orphan and widow remain.

Deuteronomy 15

15:2 And this is the ordinance of the release: you shall release every debt of yours which your neighbour owes you; and you shall not exact it of your brother, for a release unto the Lord your God has been proclaimed.
15:3 Of a foreigner you may exact whatsoever he owes you; but you shall release your brother’s debt unto you.
15:4 For there shall be none in need among you; for the Lord your God shall surely bless you in the land which the Lord your God gives you for a possession, to inherit it,
15:5 if you diligently hearken unto the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and do all these commandments which I command you this day.
15:6 For the Lord your God has blessed you, as He has spoken unto you; and you shall lend unto many nations, but you shall not borrow; and you shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.
15:7 And if there is among you one of your brethren in need, within any of your cities in the land which the Lord your God gives you, you shall not withhold the affection of your heart, neither shall you close your hand against your needy brother.
15:8 You shall freely open your hands unto him, and shall surely lend him as much as he needs, according to his want.
15:9 Take heed unto yourself, lest there be a hidden, lawless thought in your heart, saying: The seventh year, the year of release, draws near; and your eye be evil toward your needy brother, and you give him nothing, and he cry against you unto the Lord, and there be great sin upon you.
15:10 You shall freely give unto him, and shall surely lend him as much as he needs; and your heart shall not be grieved when you give unto him. For because of this the Lord your God shall bless you in all your works, and in all that you put your hand unto.
15:11 For the needy shall never cease out of the land; therefore I command you to do this thing, saying: You shall freely open your hands unto your poor brother and unto the needy in your land.
15:12 And if your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, is sold unto you, he shall serve you six years; and in the seventh year you shall send him forth free from you.
15:13 And when you send him forth free from you, you shall not send him away empty.
15:14 You shall furnish him generously out of your sheep, and out of your grain, and out of your winepress; as the Lord your God has blessed you, you shall give unto him.
15:15 And you shall remember that you were a household slave in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God redeemed you thence; therefore I command you to do this thing.
15:16 And if he says unto you: I will not go forth from you, because he loves you and your house, and because it is well with him beside you,
15:17 then you shall take an awl, and pierce his ear against the door, and he shall be your household slave for ever; and unto your female slave you shall do likewise.
15:18 It shall not seem hard unto you when they are sent forth free from you, for he served you six years for the yearly wage of a hired worker; and the Lord your God shall bless you in all that you do.
15:19 Every firstborn male that is born among your cattle and among your sheep you shall sanctify unto the Lord your God. You shall do no work with your firstborn calf, neither shall you shear the firstborn of your sheep.
15:20 You shall eat it before the Lord, year by year, in the place which the Lord your God shall choose, you and your house.
15:21 And if there is a blemish in it, lameness or blindness or any grievous blemish, you shall not sacrifice it unto the Lord your God.
15:22 You shall eat it within your cities; the unclean among you and the clean shall eat it alike, as the gazelle or the deer.
15:23 Only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it upon the earth as water.

Editorial note: Release, lending, service, gifts and firstborn provisions retain their distinctions. Attributive your and possessive yours are distinguished. The inherited yearly-wage wording in 15:18 remains provisional.

Deuteronomy 16

16:3 You shall eat no leaven with it; you shall eat unleavened bread with it for seven days, the bread of affliction, for you came forth out of Egypt in haste; that you may remember the day of your departure out of the land of Egypt all the days of your life.

Deuteronomy 19

19:6 lest the kinsman who avenges blood pursue the slayer while his heart is hot, and overtake him, if the way is long, and strike him mortally, so that he dies, though no sentence of death is due unto him, for he bore his neighbour no hatred aforetime.

19:13 Your eye shall not spare him; and you shall cleanse the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, and it shall be well with you.
19:14 You shall not remove your neighbour’s boundaries, which your fathers set in your inheritance, which was allotted unto you in the land which the Lord your God gives you for a possession.

Deuteronomy 22

22:1 You shall not see your brother’s calf or his sheep wandering in the way, and disregard them; you shall surely bring them back unto your brother, and restore them unto him.
22:2 And if your brother is not near you, and you do not know him, you shall gather them into your house, and they shall be with you until your brother seeks them; and you shall restore them unto him.
22:3 So you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do with his garment, and so you shall do with every lost thing of your brother, whatsoever he has lost and you have found; you may not disregard it.
22:4 You shall not see your brother’s donkey or his calf fallen in the way, and disregard them; you shall surely raise them up with him.
22:5 A woman shall not have a man’s equipment upon her, neither shall a man put on a woman’s robe; for everyone who does these things is an abomination unto the Lord your God.
22:6 And if you come upon a bird’s nest before you in the way, in any tree or upon the ground, with young birds or eggs, and the mother sitting upon the young or upon the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the young.
22:7 You shall surely let the mother go, but the young you may take unto yourself, that it may be well with you, and that your days may be long.
22:8 And when you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, that you do not cause bloodshed in your house, if anyone falls from it.
22:9 You shall not sow your vineyard with a different crop, lest the produce and the seed which you sow, together with the produce of your vineyard, become consecrated.
22:10 You shall not plough with a calf and a donkey together.
22:11 You shall not wear a mingled fabric, wool and linen together.
22:12 You shall make twisted tassels upon the four edges of your outer garments wherewith you cover yourself.
22:13 And if a man takes a wife, and dwells with her as husband, and hates her,
22:14 and brings charges against her as a pretext, and gives her an evil name, and says: I took this woman, and when I came unto her, I did not find the tokens of her virginity,
22:15 then the father of the young woman and her mother shall take and bring forth the tokens of her virginity unto the elders at the gate.
22:16 And the father of the young woman shall say unto the elders: I gave this my daughter unto this man for a wife, and he has hated her;
22:17 and behold, he brings charges against her as a pretext, saying: I did not find the tokens of your daughter’s virginity. Yet these are the tokens of my daughter’s virginity. And they shall spread the garment before the elders of the city.
22:18 And the elders of that city shall take that man and chasten him.
22:19 And they shall fine him a hundred shekels, and give them unto the father of the young woman, because he has brought an evil name upon a virgin of Israel; and she shall be his wife, and he may not put her away all his days.
22:20 But if this matter is true, and the tokens of the young woman’s virginity are not found,
22:21 then they shall bring forth the young woman unto the doors of her father’s house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones, and she shall die; for she has wrought folly among the sons of Israel, bringing harlotry into her father’s house. And you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.
22:22 And if a man is found lying with a woman married unto a husband, you shall put both of them to death, the man who lay with the woman, and the woman; and you shall remove the evil one from Israel.
22:23 And if a young woman, a virgin, is betrothed unto a man, and a man finds her in the city and lies with her,
22:24 you shall bring both of them forth unto the gate of their city, and they shall be stoned with stones and shall die: the young woman, because she did not cry out in the city, and the man, because he violated his neighbour’s wife. And you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.
22:25 But if a man finds the betrothed young woman in the field, and forces her and lies with her, you shall put to death only the man who lay with her.
22:26 And unto the young woman you shall do nothing; there is in her no sin deserving death. For as when a man rises against his neighbour and slays him, so is this matter.
22:27 For he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, and there was none to help her.
22:28 And if a man finds a young woman, a virgin who is not betrothed, and forces her and lies with her, and he is found out,
22:29 the man who lay with her shall give unto the father of the young woman fifty silver didrachmas, and she shall be his wife, because he violated her; he may not put her away all his days.

Editorial note: Lost property, roof parapet, mixtures, marriage and sexual-violence provisions retain their distinctions. Conditional verbs and negatives are clearer; no modern legal interpretation is substituted for the text.

Deuteronomy 23

23:1 A man shall not take his father’s wife or uncover his father’s covering.
23:2 He whose testicles are crushed, or whose organ is cut off, shall not enter the assembly of the Lord.
23:3 One born of a prostitute shall not enter the assembly of the Lord.
23:4 An Ammonite or a Moabite shall not enter the assembly of the Lord; even unto the tenth generation he shall not enter the assembly of the Lord, even for ever,
23:5 because they did not meet you with bread and water in the way, when you came forth out of Egypt, and because they hired against you Balaam son of Beor, from Mesopotamia, to curse you.
23:6 And the Lord your God would not hearken unto Balaam; and the Lord your God turned the curses into a blessing, because the Lord your God loved you.
23:7 You shall not speak peace unto them, nor seek their advantage, all your days for ever.
23:8 You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother; you shall not abhor an Egyptian, for you were a resident alien in his land.
23:9 The sons born unto them shall enter the assembly of the Lord in the third generation.
23:10 And when you go forth to encamp against your enemies, you shall keep yourself from every evil thing.
23:11 If there is among you a man who is not clean by reason of a discharge in the night, he shall go forth outside the camp, and shall not enter the camp.
23:12 And it shall be, toward evening, that he shall wash his body with water; and when the sun is set, he shall enter the camp.
23:13 And you shall have a place outside the camp, whither you shall go forth.
23:14 And you shall have a peg upon your belt; and when you squat outside, you shall dig therewith, and turn and cover your excrement with it.
23:15 For the Lord your God walks in the midst of your camp, to deliver you, and to give your enemy before you; therefore your camp shall be holy, that no indecent thing be seen among you, and He turn away from you.
23:16 You shall not deliver unto his master a slave who has come unto you from his master.
23:17 He shall dwell with you; he shall dwell among you in any place that pleases him. You shall not afflict him.
23:18 There shall be no prostitute among the daughters of Israel, neither shall there be one who practises prostitution among the sons of Israel. There shall be no woman who performs initiation rites among the daughters of Israel, neither shall there be an initiated man among the sons of Israel.
23:19 You shall not bring the hire of a prostitute, nor the price of a dog, into the house of the Lord your God for any vow; for both are an abomination unto the Lord your God.
23:20 You shall not charge your brother interest on silver, nor interest on food, nor interest on anything which you lend.
23:21 Unto a foreigner you may lend at interest, but unto your brother you shall not lend at interest; that the Lord your God may bless you in all your works upon the land whither you go to inherit it.
23:22 And if you vow a vow unto the Lord your God, you shall not delay to pay it; for the Lord your God shall surely require it of you, and there shall be sin upon you.
23:23 But if you do not desire to vow, there is no sin upon you.
23:24 That which proceeds through your lips you shall keep and do, as you have vowed a gift unto the Lord your God, which you have spoken with your mouth.
23:25 And if you enter your neighbour’s harvest, you may gather heads of grain with your hands; but you shall not put a sickle unto your neighbour’s harvest.
23:26 And if you enter your neighbour’s vineyard, you may eat grapes until your soul is filled; but you shall put none in a vessel.

Editorial note: All 26 source-numbered units remain, including the opening father’s-wife prohibition and the grain/vineyard order. Assembly, camp, escaped-slave, interest and vow provisions retain their inherited wording.

Deuteronomy 24

24:1 And if a man takes a wife and dwells with her as husband, and she finds no favour in his sight, because he has found some unseemly thing in her, and he writes her a certificate of divorce, and gives it into her hands, and sends her forth out of his house,
24:2 and she departs and becomes another man’s wife,
24:3 and the latter husband hates her, and writes her a certificate of divorce, and gives it into her hands, and sends her forth out of his house, or if the latter husband who took her to be his wife dies,
24:4 her former husband, who sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife, after she has been defiled; for it is an abomination before the Lord your God. And you shall not defile the land which the Lord your God gives you for a possession.
24:5 And if a man has newly taken a wife, he shall not go forth unto war, neither shall any duty be laid upon him. He shall be free in his house one year, and shall gladden his wife whom he has taken.
24:6 You shall not take a mill or its upper stone in pledge, for he who does so takes a life in pledge.
24:7 And if a man is caught stealing one of his brethren, of the sons of Israel, and he subjugates him and sells him, that thief shall die; and you shall remove the evil one from among yourselves.
24:8 Take heed unto yourself concerning the affliction of leprosy. You shall take diligent heed to do according to all the law which the Levitical priests shall declare unto you; as I commanded you, take heed to do.
24:9 Remember what the Lord your God did unto Miriam in the way, when you came forth out of Egypt.
24:10 If your neighbour owes a debt, any debt whatsoever, you shall not enter his house to take his pledge.
24:11 You shall stand outside, and the man who owes you the loan shall bring forth the pledge unto you.
24:12 And if the man is poor, you shall not sleep with his pledge in your keeping.
24:13 You shall surely restore his pledge at the setting of the sun, that he may sleep in his garment and bless you; and it shall be mercy unto you before the Lord your God.
24:14 You shall not withhold the wages of the poor and needy, whether of your brethren or of the sojourners within your cities.
24:15 On the same day you shall pay his wages; the sun shall not go down upon them, for he is poor and sets his hope upon them; lest he cry against you unto the Lord, and there be sin upon you.
24:16 Fathers shall not die for children, and sons shall not die for fathers. Each shall die for his own sin.
24:17 You shall not pervert the judgment of the sojourner, or of the orphan, or of the widow; neither shall you take a widow’s garment in pledge.
24:18 And you shall remember that you were a household slave in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God redeemed you thence; therefore I command you to do this thing.
24:19 And when you reap your harvest in your field, and forget a sheaf in your field, you shall not return to take it. It shall be for the poor, and for the sojourner, and for the orphan, and for the widow; that the Lord your God may bless you in all the works of your hands.
24:20 And when you gather your olives, you shall not return to glean what remains behind you. It shall be for the sojourner, and for the orphan, and for the widow. And you shall remember that you were a household slave in the land of Egypt; therefore I command you to do this thing.
24:21 And when you gather the grapes of your vineyard, you shall not glean it afterward; it shall be for the sojourner, and for the orphan, and for the widow.
24:22 And you shall remember that you were a household slave in the land of Egypt; therefore I command you to do this thing.

34:11 in all the signs and wonders which the Lord sent him to do in the land of Egypt unto Pharaoh, and unto his servants, and unto all his land,
34:12 the great wonders and the mighty hand which Moses wrought before all Israel.

Editorial note: The inherited plural “they buried him”, Gai, cheeks and spirit of understanding remain after selected main-text inspection. Moses’ death and Joshua’s succession keep their distinct agents and wording.
