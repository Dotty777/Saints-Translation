# Deuteronomy — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 34 chapters and the book index were revised, saved and read back on WordPress staging on 14 October 2026. All 959 existing verse units received an English editorial review: 725 changed and 234 retained. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist review remains pending.

The newer Bespoke policy and the Genesis–Exodus manner govern this pass. Ordinary address uses you/your. Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine remain only in direct address to God, including the mixed prayer/instruction passages in chapter 26 and changes of addressee in chapter 33. Modern verb forms, clearer negatives, conditional grammar and restrained changes to word order improve the English while preserving source readings, imagery, agents and repetitions.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. [source-checks.json](source-checks.json) records the selected main-text checks and their limits. No full Göttingen or apparatus collation, independent Greek specialist review, or human read-aloud review is claimed. Earlier notes remain historical records and preserve observations about singular/plural source forms; the newer Bespoke convention governs the English.

## Preservation and review limits

Every verse ID and the inherited numbering remain, including the commandment order in 5:17–18, the preface in 6:4, the 19 units in chapter 13, the 26 units in chapter 23, the covenant heading at 28:69 and the 28 units in chapter 29. All 123 inline note links, earlier note paragraphs, navigation, images, scripts, styles and serialized block comments were preserved. The different page formats used by chapters 1–10 and 11–34 were both retained on staging. The combined reading export has unique IDs and valid internal links.

All 1,538 study rows match the revised reading. Only the matching phrase field changed in 209 rows; Greek, explanations, themes, colours, references and other fields were preserved. A browser sample confirmed the revised “do not forsake the Levite” highlight opens the correct study and that the current note appears in the Translation notes workspace.

Source-specific readings remain, including angels of God in 32:8, the expanded 32:43 and plural burial agents in 34:6. Difficult wording, animal and bird identifications, legal and ritual terminology, geographical names and syntax require independent specialist review. The inherited interpretation of 29:18 and difficult clauses in chapters 32–33 remain provisional. This pass does not renew all earlier source or reception claims.

The separate literal-smooth Saints Translation and comparison streams were not edited. No production-site changes were made. These archives cover Deuteronomy, not the whole website.

Genesis through Deuteronomy now have English editorial passes and staging verification: the five-book Law division, 187 chapters and 5,801 verse units.
