Deuteronomy 1

Command to Leave Horeb

1:1 These are the words which Moses spoke unto all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness toward the west, near the Red Sea, between Paran, Tophel, Laban, Aulon, and Katachrysea.

1:2 It is a journey of eleven days from Horeb by the way of Mount Seir unto Kadesh-barnea.

1:3 And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke unto all the sons of Israel, according to all that the Lord had commanded him concerning them.

1:4 After he had smitten Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei,

1:5 beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to make this law plain, saying:

1:6 The Lord our God spoke unto us in Horeb, saying: Let it suffice you to have dwelt in this mountain.

1:7 Turn and set forth, and enter the hill country of the Amorites and all the neighbouring places of Arabah, the hill country and the plain, the southwest and the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites and Anti-Lebanon, as far as the great river Euphrates.

1:8 Behold, I have delivered the land before you. Enter and inherit the land which I sware unto your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give unto them and unto their seed after them.

1:9 And I spoke unto you at that time, saying: I shall not be able to bear you alone.

1:10 The Lord your God has multiplied you, and behold, you are this day as the stars of heaven in multitude.

1:11 The Lord, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you, as He has spoken unto you.

1:12 How shall I alone be able to bear your trouble, and your resistance, and your disputes?

1:13 Choose for yourselves wise men, men of knowledge and understanding, for your tribes, and I will appoint them to rule over you.

1:14 And you answered me and said: The thing which you have spoken is good to do.

1:15 And I took from among you wise men, men of knowledge and understanding, and appointed them to rule over you, captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds, and captains of fifties, and captains of tens, and recording officers for your judges.

1:16 And I commanded your judges at that time, saying: Hear the matters between your brethren, and judge justly between a man and his brother and the sojourner with him.

1:17 You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall judge the small and the great alike. You shall not shrink before the face of man, for judgment belongs unto God. And you shall bring unto me the matter which is too hard for you, and I will hear it. 1

1:18 And I commanded you at that time all the things which you should do.

1:19 And we departed from Horeb, and went through all that great and fearful wilderness which you saw, by the way of the hill country of the Amorite, as the Lord our God commanded us; and we came unto Kadesh-barnea.

1:20 And I said unto you: You have come unto the hill country of the Amorite, which the Lord our God gives you.

1:21 Behold, the Lord your God has delivered the land before you. Go up and inherit it, as the Lord, the God of your fathers, has spoken unto you; fear not, neither be dismayed.

1:22 And you all came unto me, and said: Let us send men before us, and let them search out the land for us, and bring us word of the way by which we shall go up, and of the cities into which we shall enter.

1:23 And the saying pleased me; and I took twelve men from among you, one man from each tribe.

1:24 And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came unto the Valley of the Cluster, and searched it out.

1:25 And they took of the fruit of the land in their hands, and brought it down unto us, and said: The land which the Lord our God gives us is good.

1:26 And you would not go up, but disobeyed the word of the Lord your God.

1:27 And you murmured in your tents, and said: Because the Lord hates us, He brought us forth out of the land of Egypt, to deliver us into the hands of the Amorites, to destroy us.

1:28 Whither do we go up? Your brethren have made your heart fail, saying: The nation is great and numerous and mightier than we; and the cities are great and walled up to heaven. Moreover, we have seen sons of giants there.

1:29 And I said unto you: Neither cower nor fear them.

1:30 The Lord your God, who goes before you, shall Himself fight against them with you, according to all that He did for you in the land of Egypt,

1:31 and in this wilderness which you saw, how the Lord your God nurtured you as a man nurtures his son, in all the way which you went, until you came unto this place. 2

1:32 And in this matter you did not trust the Lord your God,

1:33 who goes before you in the way to choose a place for you, guiding you in fire by night, to show you the way wherein you should go, and in a cloud by day.

1:34 And the Lord heard the voice of your words, and, being provoked to anger, sware, saying:

1:35 Surely none of these men shall see this good land which I sware unto their fathers,

1:36 save Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and I will give unto him and unto his sons the land whereon he trod, because he cleaved unto the things of the Lord.

1:37 And the Lord was angry with me also for your sakes, saying: You shall not enter there either.

1:38 Joshua son of Nun, who stands beside you, he shall enter there. Strengthen him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.

1:39 And every young child who this day knows neither good nor evil shall enter there; and I will give it unto them, and they shall inherit it.

1:40 But you, turn and encamp in the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.

1:41 And you answered me and said: We have sinned before the Lord our God. We will go up and fight, according to all that the Lord our God commanded us. And you took up, every man, his weapons of war, and gathered yourselves together, and went up into the hill country.

1:42 And the Lord said unto me: Say unto them, You shall not go up, neither shall you fight, for I am not with you; and you shall not be crushed before your enemies.

1:43 And I spoke unto you, but you did not hearken unto me; and you transgressed the word of the Lord, and forced your way up into the hill country.

1:44 And the Amorites who dwelt in that hill country came out to meet you, and pursued you as bees do, and wounded you from Seir unto Hormah.

1:45 And you sat down and wept before the Lord; but the Lord did not hearken unto your voice, neither gave He heed unto you.

1:46 And you dwelt in Kadesh many days, as many days as you dwelt there.

Bespoke editorial notes

The recollection, named places and judicial commands retain their order. Ordinary address uses you/your; earlier notes preserve observations about singular and plural Greek forms.

Earlier translation notes

1. Deuteronomy 1:17 • Wording. “Show partiality” states the judicial sense more clearly than “respect persons,” which can now suggest ordinary courtesy. The singular commands to the judge give way to plural address in the difficult-case clause; Saints preserves that shift. ↩ Verse 1:17

2. Deuteronomy 1:31 • Meaning. The Greek describes past care, rendered “nurtured thee.” Brenton’s future “will bear thee” is not adopted. The change from plural travellers to singular Israel belongs to the source, rather than to an inconsistent English voice. ↩ Verse 1:31

Deuteronomy 2

Journey Past Seir

2:1 And we turned and departed into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea, as the Lord had spoken unto me; and we compassed Mount Seir many days.

2:2 And the Lord said unto me:

2:3 Let it suffice you to have compassed this mountain; turn therefore northward.

2:4 And command the people, saying: You are passing through the borders of your brethren, the sons of Esau, who dwell in Seir; and they shall fear you, and be exceedingly wary of you. 3

2:5 Do not join battle with them, for I will give you none of their land, no, not so much as a footstep; for I have given Mount Seir unto the sons of Esau for a possession.

2:6 Buy food from them for silver, and eat; and you shall take water from them by measure for silver, and drink.

2:7 For the Lord our God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. Consider how you passed through that great and fearful wilderness. Behold, these forty years the Lord your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing.

2:8 And we passed by our brethren, the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, along the way of Arabah, from Ailon and from Ezion-geber; and we turned and passed along the wilderness way of Moab.

2:9 And the Lord said unto me: Do not deal as enemies with the Moabites, neither join battle with them; for I will give you none of their land for a possession, for I have given Seir unto the sons of Lot to inherit. 4

2:10 The Emim dwelt there aforetime, a nation great and numerous and strong as the Anakim.

2:11 These also are reckoned as Rephaim, like the Anakim; and the Moabites call them Emim.

2:12 And the Horites dwelt in Seir aforetime; but the sons of Esau destroyed them, and blotted them out from before them, and dwelt in their stead, as Israel did in the land of its inheritance, which the Lord gave unto them.

2:13 Now therefore arise, and set forth, and pass through the Ravine Zered. And we passed through the Ravine Zered.

2:14 And the time wherein we journeyed from Kadesh-barnea until we passed through the Ravine Zered was thirty and eight years, until the whole generation of men of war had fallen, dying out of the camp, as God had sworn unto them.

2:15 And the hand of God was upon them, to consume them out of the camp, until they had fallen.

2:16 And it came to pass, when all the men of war had fallen, dying out from among the people,

2:17 that the Lord spoke unto me, saying:

2:18 You shall pass this day through the borders of Moab, even Seir,

2:19 and you shall draw near unto the sons of Ammon. Do not deal as enemies with them, neither join battle with them; for I will give you none of the land of the sons of Ammon for a possession, because I have given it unto the sons of Lot for a possession.

2:20 It is reckoned a land of Rephaim; for the Rephaim dwelt there aforetime, and the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

2:21 a nation great and numerous and mightier than you, like the Anakim. And the Lord destroyed them from before the Ammonites, who inherited the land and dwelt in their stead unto this day,

2:22 even as they did for the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, when they blotted out the Horites from before them, and inherited the land, and dwelt in their stead unto this day.

2:23 And the Hivites who dwelt in settlements as far as Gaza, the Cappadocians who came forth from Cappadocia blotted them out, and dwelt in their stead. 5

2:24 Now therefore arise, and set forth, and pass through the Ravine Arnon. Behold, I have delivered into your hands Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to inherit it; join battle with him.

2:25 This day begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you before all the nations under heaven, who, when they hear your name, shall be troubled and shall suffer pangs before you.

2:26 And I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth unto Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying:

2:27 I will pass through your land. I will pass along the way; I will turn neither to the right nor to the left.

2:28 You shall sell me food for silver, and I will eat; and you shall sell me water for silver, and I will drink. Only let me pass through on foot,

2:29 as the sons of Esau who dwell in Seir, and the Moabites who dwell in Aroer, did for me, until I pass over the Jordan into the land which the Lord our God gives us.

2:30 And Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through his land; for the Lord our God hardened his spirit and strengthened his heart, that he might be delivered into your hands, as at this day.

2:31 And the Lord said unto me: Behold, I have begun to deliver before you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land; begin to inherit his land.

2:32 And Sihon king of Heshbon came out to meet us, he and all his people, unto battle at Jahaz.

2:33 And the Lord our God delivered him before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.

2:34 And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every city in succession, with their women and their children; we left none alive as a captive.

2:35 Only the cattle we took as plunder, and the spoil of the cities we took.

2:36 From Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, and the city which is in the ravine, even unto the hill country of Gilead, there was no city that escaped us; the Lord our God delivered them all into our hands.

2:37 Only unto the land of the sons of Ammon we did not draw near, all that borders upon the torrent Jabbok, and the cities in the hill country, as the Lord our God commanded us.

Deuteronomy 3

Defeat of Og King of Bashan

3:1 And we turned and went up by the way unto Bashan; and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, unto battle at Edrei.

3:2 And the Lord said unto me: Do not fear him, for I have delivered him, and all his people, and all his land into your hands; and you shall do unto him as you did unto Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon.

3:3 And the Lord our God delivered him into our hands, even Og king of Bashan and all his people; and we smote him until none of his seed remained.

3:4 And we took all his cities at that time; there was no city which we did not take from them, sixty cities, all the region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

3:5 All these were fortified cities, with high walls, gates, and bars, besides very many cities of the Perizzites. 6

3:6 We utterly destroyed them, as we did unto Sihon king of Heshbon; and we utterly destroyed every city in succession, with the women and the children.

3:7 And all the cattle and the spoil of the cities we took as plunder for ourselves.

3:8 And at that time we took the land from the hands of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the torrent Arnon unto Hermon.

3:9 The Phoenicians call Hermon Sanior, and the Amorites call it Senir.

3:10 All the cities of Misor, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, as far as Salcah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

3:11 For only Og king of Bashan remained of the Rephaim. Behold, his bed was a bed of iron; behold, it is in the citadel of the sons of Ammon, nine cubits in length and four cubits in breadth, by the cubit of a man. 7

3:12 And we inherited that land at that time. From Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead, with its cities, I gave unto Reuben and unto Gad.

3:13 And the rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave unto the half tribe of Manasseh, with all the region of Argob, all that Bashan; it is reckoned a land of Rephaim.

3:14 And Jair son of Manasseh took all the region of Argob, unto the borders of Gargasi and the Maacathites; and he called those places of Bashan after his own name, Havvoth-jair, unto this day.

3:15 And unto Machir I gave Gilead.

3:16 And unto Reuben and unto Gad I gave from Gilead unto the torrent Arnon, the midst of the torrent being the border, and unto Jabbok, the torrent which is the border of the sons of Ammon;

3:17 and the Arabah, and the Jordan as the border, Machanareth, and as far as the Sea of Arabah, the Salt Sea, below Asedoth-Pisgah toward the east.

3:18 And I commanded you at that time, saying: The Lord your God has given you this land for a possession. Go armed before your brethren, the sons of Israel, every able man.

3:19 Only let your wives, and your children, and your cattle, for I know that you have much cattle, dwell in your cities which I have given you,

3:20 until the Lord your God gives rest unto your brethren, as unto you, and they also inherit the land which the Lord our God gives them beyond the Jordan; then you shall return, every man unto his inheritance which I have given you.

3:21 And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying: Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God has done unto these two kings; so shall the Lord our God do unto all the kingdoms whither you are passing over.

3:22 You shall not fear, for the Lord our God Himself shall fight for you.

3:23 And I besought the Lord at that time, saying:

3:24 O Lord, Lord, Thou hast begun to show Thy servant Thy strength, and Thy power, and Thy mighty hand, and Thine uplifted arm; for what god is there in heaven or upon earth who shall do as Thou hast done, and according to Thy strength?

3:25 Let me therefore pass over and see this good land beyond the Jordan, this good hill country and Anti-Lebanon.

3:26 And the Lord disregarded me for your sakes, and did not hearken unto me; and the Lord said unto me: Let it suffice you; speak no more of this matter.

3:27 Go up unto the summit of the Hewn Place, and lift up your eyes toward the sea, and northward, and toward the southwest, and eastward, and behold with your eyes; for you shall not pass over this Jordan. 8

3:28 And command Joshua, and strengthen him, and encourage him; for he shall pass over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land which you have seen.

3:29 And we dwelt in the valley near the house of Peor.

Deuteronomy 4

Moses Calls Israel to Obedience

4:1 And now, O Israel, hear the ordinances and the judgments which I teach you this day to do, that you may live and be multiplied, and enter and inherit the land which the Lord, the God of your fathers, gives you.

4:2 You shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall you take away from it; you shall keep the commandments of the Lord your God, which I command you this day.

4:3 Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did unto Baal-peor; for every man who went after Baal-peor, the Lord your God blotted him out from among you.

4:4 But you who cleave unto the Lord your God are all alive this day.

4:5 Behold, I have shown you ordinances and judgments, as the Lord commanded me, that you should do so in the land whither you go to inherit it.

4:6 And you shall keep and do them; for this is your wisdom and understanding before all the nations, who shall hear all these ordinances and say: Behold, this great nation is a wise and understanding people.

4:7 For what great nation is there whose god draws near unto them, as the Lord our God in all things wherein we call upon Him?

4:8 And what great nation is there that has ordinances and judgments as righteous as all this law which I set before you this day?

4:9 Take heed unto yourself, and guard your soul diligently, lest you forget all the things which your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life. And you shall teach your sons and your sons’ sons

4:10 the day when you stood before the Lord your God in Horeb, in the day of the assembly, when the Lord said unto me: Assemble the people unto Me, and let them hear My words, that they may learn to fear Me all the days that they live upon the earth, and may teach their sons.

4:11 And you drew near and stood beneath the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire unto heaven, with darkness, gloom, tempest, and a great sound.

4:12 And the Lord spoke unto you out of the midst of the fire; you heard the sound of words, but saw no likeness, only a voice.

4:13 And He declared unto you His covenant, which He commanded you to do, even the ten words; and He wrote them upon two tablets of stone. 9

4:14 And the Lord commanded me at that time to teach you ordinances and judgments, that you should do them in the land whither you go to inherit it.

4:15 And you shall guard your souls diligently; for you saw no likeness in the day when the Lord spoke unto you in Horeb, on the mountain, out of the midst of the fire,

4:16 lest you act lawlessly and make for yourselves a graven likeness, any image, the likeness of male or female,

4:17 a likeness of any animal on the earth, a likeness of any winged bird flying beneath heaven,

4:18 the likeness of any creeping thing that creeps upon the earth, the likeness of any fish that is in the waters beneath the earth.

4:19 And lest you lift up your eyes unto heaven, and see the sun, and the moon, and the stars, and all the adornment of heaven, and be led astray to bow down unto them and serve them, which the Lord your God has allotted unto all the nations under heaven.

4:20 But God took you, and brought you forth out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be unto Him a people of inheritance, as at this day.

4:21 And the Lord was angry with me concerning the things spoken by you, and sware that I should not pass over this Jordan, neither enter the land which the Lord God gives you for a possession.

4:22 For I die in this land, and do not pass over this Jordan; but you pass over, and shall inherit this good land.

4:23 Take heed, lest you forget the covenant of the Lord your God, which He made with you, and make for yourselves a graven likeness of anything concerning which the Lord your God has given commandment.

4:24 For the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God. 10

4:25 And if you beget sons and sons’ sons, and you have remained long upon the land, and act lawlessly, and make a graven likeness of anything, and do evil before the Lord your God, to provoke Him to anger,

4:26 I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that you shall surely perish from the land whither you pass over the Jordan to inherit it. You shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall be utterly blotted out.

4:27 And the Lord shall scatter you among all the nations, and you shall be left few in number among the nations whither the Lord shall bring you.

4:28 And there you shall serve other gods, the works of men’s hands, wood and stone, which shall neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.

4:29 And there you shall seek the Lord your God, and shall find Him, when you seek Him with all your heart and with all your soul, in your distress. 11

4:30 And all these things shall find you in the latter days, and you shall return unto the Lord your God, and hearken unto His voice.

4:31 For the Lord your God is a merciful God; He will not forsake you, neither will He destroy you, nor forget the covenant of your fathers which He sware unto them.

4:32 Ask now of the former days that were before you, from the day when God created man upon the earth, and from one end of heaven unto the other: has there been anything like this great matter, or has such a thing been heard?

4:33 Has a nation heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as you have heard, and lived?

4:34 Or has God attempted to go and take unto Himself a nation from the midst of a nation, by trial, and by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand, and by an uplifted arm, and by great visions, according to all that the Lord our God did in Egypt before your eyes?

4:35 That you might know that the Lord your God, He is God, and there is none beside Him.

4:36 Out of heaven His voice was made heard, to instruct you; and upon earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words out of the midst of the fire.

4:37 Because He loved your fathers, He chose their seed after them, even you, and Himself brought you forth out of Egypt by His great strength,

4:38 to destroy nations great and mightier than you from before you, to bring you in, and to give you their land to inherit, as you have this day.

4:39 And you shall know this day, and lay it to mind, that the Lord your God, He is God in heaven above and upon earth beneath; there is none beside Him.

4:40 And you shall keep His ordinances and His commandments which I command you this day, that it may be well with you and with your sons after you, and that you may prolong your days upon the land which the Lord your God gives you for ever.

4:41 Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise,

4:42 that the slayer might flee thither, who has slain his neighbour unintentionally, having borne him no hatred aforetime; and he shall flee unto one of these cities, and live:

4:43 Bezer in the wilderness, in the plain country, for Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead for Gad; and Golan in Bashan for Manasseh.

4:44 This is the law which Moses set before the sons of Israel.

4:45 These are the testimonies, and the ordinances, and the judgments which Moses spoke unto the sons of Israel in the wilderness, when they came forth out of the land of Egypt,

4:46 beyond the Jordan, in the ravine near the house of Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel smote when they came forth out of the land of Egypt.

4:47 And they inherited his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise,

4:48 from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon torrent as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,

4:49 all the Arabah beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise, below Asedoth, the hewn height.

Deuteronomy 5

The Covenant at Horeb

5:1 And Moses called all Israel, and said unto them: Hear, O Israel, the ordinances and the judgments which I speak in your ears this day; and you shall learn them, and take heed to do them.

5:2 The Lord your God made a covenant with you in Horeb.

5:3 The Lord did not make this covenant with your fathers, but with you, even you who are all here alive this day.

5:4 The Lord spoke with you face to face on the mountain, from the midst of the fire.

5:5 And I stood between the Lord and you at that time, to declare unto you the words of the Lord; for you were afraid of the fire, and did not go up into the mountain. He said:

5:6 I am the Lord your God, who brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

5:7 You shall have no other gods before Me.

5:8 You shall not make unto yourself an idol, nor any likeness of anything in heaven above, or upon the earth beneath, or in the waters under the earth.

5:9 You shall not bow down unto them, neither shall you serve them; for I am the Lord your God, a jealous God, repaying the sins of fathers upon children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate Me,

5:10 and showing mercy unto thousands, unto them that love Me and keep My ordinances.
