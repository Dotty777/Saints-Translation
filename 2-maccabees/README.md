All 15 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 555 verse units received an English editorial review: 75 changed and 480 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. ## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernized ordinary address in the festival instructions. Preserved Thy/Thou in the prayer and made the accompanying direct-address verb forms consistent: dost preserve and didst choose/sanctify. Retained repeated Lord and priest expressions, the thick-water account, narrative first-person shift, divine titles, and Nephthar/Nephthai distinction.

Pending: Prayer-title syntax at 1:24–25, the battle clause at 1:11, the narrative voice at 1:20, Jonathan/Nehemiah syntax at 1:23, flame/light relation at 1:32, and complete critical collation.

**Chapter 2.** Modernized human address and third-person gathers/becomes/involves/belongs. Preserved the distinctive Moses/fire statement, the unfinished construction spanning 2:17–18, kingship/priesthood/sanctification, sweat and sleeplessness, numerical-material expression and architectural/painting comparison.

Pending: Syntax at 2:17–18, historical-scriptural relation of 2:10–11, figures at 2:24, encaustic vocabulary at 2:29, and full apparatus review. Historical notes remain separate.

**Chapter 3.** Modernized human address and third-person verbs; replaced aforesaid with clear that references. Clarified the collapse into faintness/cowardice and construed 3:31 as asking Onias to call on the Most High to grant life. Preserved the Lord as heavenly Overseer, Benjamin, monetary totals and the theophany’s horse, armour and repeated young men.

Pending: Market-administration terminology at 3:4, agency/infinitive relation in the intercession at 3:31, propitiation terminology and complete critical collation.

**Chapter 4.** Modernized the backward reference at 4:1 and rendered the awkward good order phrase as admirable conduct at 4:37. Kept all names, amounts, fifth-year festival wording, right-hand pledge and violent imagery.

Pending: 4:14 athletic provision/discus terminology, 4:34 subject sequence, 4:37 ethical vocabulary and full apparatus require specialist review.

**Chapter 5.** Retained the existing English throughout. Preserved visions, repeated profane hands, casualty and slave counts, nation/place contrast, reconciliation language, land/sea inversion and the company of approximately ten.

Pending: 5:14 numerical construction, 5:24 Mysarch title and full textual collation remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Modernized third-person verbs and ordinary possessives. Smoothed the narrator’s transition at 6:17 while retaining its limited reminder and return to narrative. Preserved discipline/destruction contrast, Hades, bodily torment and soul language.

Pending: 6:1 Athenian identification, 6:19–20 syntax/torture terminology and full apparatus require specialist review.

**Chapter 7.** Modernized ordinary speech to the king and third-person verbs; smoothed the concluding narrative formula. Preserved resurrection distinctions, the creation phrase not from things that were, gendered imagery, covenant wording and anger coming to rest without doctrinal expansion.

Pending: 7:6 consolation wording, 7:36 covenant syntax, 7:42 sacrificial-eating term and full apparatus require specialist review.

**Chapter 8.** Retained the English text, including crying blood, nights as allies, repeated bodies for sale, God’s nod, military numbers, reconciliation and the Defender claim.

Pending: 8:20 military totals, 8:22–23 Eleazar and reading syntax, 8:33 burning clause and complete collation need specialist review.

**Chapter 9.** Modernized ordinary plural address in the king’s letter. Kept divine judgment imagery, the indirect vows, body details and the awkward opening of the letter without supplying a missing clause.

Pending: 9:20–21 letter syntax, illness narration and full apparatus review remain pending.

**Chapter 10.** Retained the chapter wording. Kept heated stones, two-year interval, paired enemy/opponent language, five horsemen, lightning, casualty figures and the named people killed.

Pending: 10:3 fire-making wording, 10:13 syntax, 10:29–30 appearance and subject sequence, and textual/historical problems require independent review.

**Chapter 11.** Modernized ordinary singular/plural letter address, third-person verbs and my own. Retained five stadia, ancient dates, royal apotheosis language, named Roman envoys and pledge terminology.

Pending: 11:5 distance, 11:10 ally’s referent, letter dates/names and full apparatus remain pending; no chronological harmonization.

**Chapter 12.** Modernized third-person verbs. Preserved ancient units and names, difficult hostage wording, bodily violence, two thousand drachmas, prayer for the dead, resurrection reasoning and atonement wording without importing later theology.

Pending: 12:24 hostage threat, 12:35 injury wording, 12:43–45 syntax, textual variants and doctrinal reception require specialist review.

**Chapter 13.** Retained all English units. Preserved each force’s numbers, tower and ashes imagery, military watchword, pledge exchange, named officials and the rapid succession of actions.

Pending: 13:2 each force wording, 13:5 tower mechanism, 13:15 elephant rider and 13:24 geographical range require apparatus and specialist review.

**Chapter 14.** Modernized ordinary address to the king, possessives, backward reference and third-person verbs. Retained Thou/Thy in direct prayer, sudden silence, separation language and the entire Razis account without softening or adding commentary.

Pending: 14:3,38 separation wording, 14:17 sudden silence, 14:26 successor claim and full textual/reception review remain pending.

**Chapter 15.** Modernized ordinary and third-person forms; used will for the promise to Judas. Preserved Thou/Thine/Thy in direct prayer, Onias and Jeremiah’s intercession, the golden sword, body imagery, casualty count, ancient festival date and wine-water closing image.

Pending: 15:11 dream/waking vision, 15:17 campaign/charge contrast, 15:21 victory syntax and complete apparatus and reception review remain pending.
