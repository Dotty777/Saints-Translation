# Wisdom of Solomon — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 19 chapters across 19 reading sections and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 435 verse units received an English editorial review: 119 changed and 316 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/WIS.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 19 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize the plural address to rulers and third-person verb forms. Makes Himself known expresses the manifestation in 2 naturally. Preserve simplicity (sincerity or undivided intention), righteousness/injustice connections, the indebted body, spirit language, kidneys/heart/tongue, the inhabited world, utterance without added every, the ear of jealousy, death and Hades. In 16 him remains the personified death figure, not a newly capitalized divine reference. No allegorical or doctrinal explanation is inserted into the Scripture.

Pending: Review the force and subject of put to shame in 5, spirit/Wisdom relationships in 5–7, voice/utterance in 7 and the personification and portion in 16. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserve the quotation across 1–20 as the ungodly speakers’ reasoning, including the mortality argument, oppressive plans, child of the Lord in 13 and son of God in 18. Modernize verbs; has reckoned in 16 reflects the past passive reckoning with the same agents in natural active English before the ongoing clauses. Retain upbringing distinct from law, the departure/visitation language, incorruption distinct from immortality, the image of God’s eternity in the accessed text, the devil’s envy and the experience/portion clause.

Pending: Review child/servant in 13, the reckoning tense in 16, visitation in 20, the eternity reading and its variants in 23, and the referents in 24. Do not collapse quoted speech and narrator assertion. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 3.** Keep the souls in God’s hand, emphatic denial of torment, appearance of death versus peace, conditional punishment, gold/crucible and whole burnt offering, visitation and sparks, judgment/reign and grace/mercy. Will surely not in 1 preserves the emphatic future denial in modern English. Preserve the singular despiser followed by plural their, barren woman and eunuch, fruit/root, the transgressive-bed contrasts, and both long-life and early-death conditions. No qualification softens the stated judgments or promises.

Pending: Review conditional punishment in 4, visitation terminology, the singular/plural transition in 11 and the social and theological implications of 12–19 separately from the wording. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize ordinary verbs and the opening word order; dies early preserves the conditional premature death, and many years of an unrighteous person’s old age unfolds the compact expression. Preserve virtue/childlessness and their remembrance, the tree imagery, completion versus calendar age, transferred without inserting Enoch, the uncertain subject of hastened, grace/mercy/visitation and the plural persons becoming a singular dishonored corpse.

Pending: Review the opening referents, transfer/Enoch echo in 10, subject of hastened in 14 and corpse image in 19. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 5.** Keep the ungodly speakers’ confession distinct from the narrator and preserve salvation, sons of God, saints, ship/bird/arrow/dust/frost/smoke images. Reshape the bird comparison into natural English with identical six-line structure and both absence-of-trace clauses; the bird becomes grammatical subject of crossed without changing the depicted action. Past flowed back and was dispersed retain the completed actions in 12 and 14. Keep unfeigned judgment, armor and creation at war, sea water singular, hail, winnowing and the final social judgment.

Pending: Review grammatical transitions and simile tenses in 9–14, royal crown in 16, hail/catapult construction in 22 and unfeigned in 18. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 6.** Use you for plural royal address and modern ordinary verbs. Goes ahead … making herself known first preserves Wisdom’s initiative and precedence without the modern ambiguity of anticipate. Keep severe judgment, powerful/powerfully repetition, faces, holy things/holiness/made holy, feminine Wisdom and the discipline/love/laws/incorruption/nearness/kingship chain. Preserve the emphatic refusal to pass truth by and the rejection of envy.

Pending: Review the force of the least in 6, the holiness sequence in 10, Wisdom’s precedence in 13 and the chain of reasoning in 17–20. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 7.** Modernize third-person verbs while retaining the sculpted flesh, ten months, birth equality, Wisdom as mother, learned/share tense contrast, natural-world knowledge and the full list of spirit qualities. Preserve unique without importing a filial begetting claim, sleepless radiance, breath/outflow/radiance/mirror/image as distinct images, activity rather than a comparison stream’s power or majesty, and friends of God and prophets.

Pending: Review friendship tense in 14, winds/spirits in 20, all quality-list lexical choices in 22–23, breath/outflow in 25 and radiance/reflection in 26. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 8.** Modernize ordinary verbs and make the final comparison in 7 natural English without changing its four virtues. Keep the bridal and household imagery, governing well, initiated knowledge and chooser of works, anticipated future honors, hand upon mouth, immortality/remembrance, shared exercise of companionship, good soul/undefiled body and the correction or rather. Retain gaining possession of Wisdom in 21 rather than importing the Latin self-control interpretation.

Pending: Review chooser in 4, the compact companionship/exercise clause in 18, soul/body implications in 19–20 and possession/self-control in 21. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 9.** Keep Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine and didst in direct prayer to God; modernize verbs describing Wisdom or speaking about the Lord, and my actions replaces mine actions. Preserve plural thrones, slave and slave woman, sons and daughters, temple/altar/tent, the repeated sending, her glory, corruptible body/soul/earthly tent, uncertain mortal reasoning and the prayer’s final saving by wisdom. No theological explanation enters the prayer.

Pending: Review servant/child distinction in 4–5, thrones in 4 and 12, bodily and tent imagery in 15 and the Holy Spirit and salvation clauses in 17–18. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 10.** Keep the historical figures unnamed, the repeated she for Wisdom, the first-formed father, humble wood, compassion for a child, Five Cities, salt pillar and fruit, holy people, singular oppressive nation, devotion to God, rescue from sin, pit/bonds, Red Sea and the direct address Thy in 20. Modernize stands and reaches in 7 without replacing the images or importing comparison-stream word choices.

Pending: Review the unnamed historical allusions without adding names to Scripture, compassion in 5, life/memorial in 8, the contest and devotion in 12 and flame of stars in 17. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 11.** Retain direct divine address and its second-person verb forms; modernize the ordinary third-person sins, has fallen and loves. Preserve the water/blood contrast and infant-killing decree, these/those and father/king contrasts, the position of the thirst clause in 14, unformed matter, hypothetical strange beasts, breath and winnowing, repeated measure and number and weight, scale/dew images, mercy and love for all existing things.

Pending: Review referents and exposure wording in 11–14, unformed matter in 17, hypothetical beasts and their imagery in 18–19, the scale image in 22 and the love/hatred argument in 23–26. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 12.** Preserve the long 3–6 sentence and the difficult ritual-initiates phrase, the repeated little by little and repentance, incorruptible Spirit, hornets, sovereignty and gentle judgment. Keep direct divine pronouns and verbs; modernize third-person cares, does and makes. Recast 27 within its existing four lines to identify clearly the punishing creatures, the mistaken gods, the sufferers’ indignation and recognition, followed by condemnation. Preserve those who know in 17, not the opposite reading in the Latin comparison.

Pending: Review the construction and textual difficulties in 3–6, strong claims about origin and repentance in 10–11, the avenger clause in 12, the those-who-know reading in 17 and the compressed causality in 27. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 13.** Modernize ordinary verbs and the indirect question in 4. Introduce the carver’s illustration with Suppose and use past verbs for his completed actions in 11–16, preserving the shift to the present prayers in 17–19. Keep every stage of useful vessel, cooking and eating, worthless scrap, carving, paint, fastening and dependence, followed by the ironic petitions. **Chapter 14.** Keep Wisdom as the ship’s artisan, Father and Thy in direct address, ordinary steers for providence, present entrusting and past rescue, raft/wood imagery and the unnamed flood figures. Preserve the history of images, grief and royal power, the unshareable name, sexual immorality without added spiritual, the full vice list, and the distinction between powerless idols and justice pursuing sin.

Pending: Review Wisdom/craftsman textual variants in 2, the blessed wood in 7, visitation in 11, royal image history and name in 15–21, alteration of natural relations in 26 and the causal claims in 27–31. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 15.** Keep Thou/Thine and the resolve we will not sin alongside the preceding condition, full righteousness and the root of immortality. **Chapter 16.** Retain direct divine pronouns and second-person forms while modernizing ordinary third-person verbs. Pending: Review the subject of bring back in 14, healing/saving distinctions, manna substance and sweetness in 20–21, tense shifts in 18–25 and the world-fighting and creation-serving imagery. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.
