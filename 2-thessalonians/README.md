## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved the distinct growth of faith and mutual love, affliction/afflict/afflicted, relief, angels of power, flame, vengeance, eternal destruction, presence/glory/strength, and glory/faith repetitions. Included our in 1:2 from bracketed NA28 wording and smoothed bringing vengeance on. Retained the shorter Lord Jesus title at 1:8.

Pending: Review eternal destruction and from the presence in 1:9, including source/separation interpretations, the relationship of suffering and worthiness in 1:5, and the syntax of our God and Lord Jesus Christ in 1:12. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved gathering, shaking, the three communication channels, lawlessness, son of destruction, sanctuary, neuter what restrains versus masculine the one who restrains, breath/mouth, signs/wonders, firstfruits and oral/written traditions. Modernized ordinary you and verbs. Included also at 2:14 from bracketed NA28 wording; retained bracketed Jesus at 2:8. The restrainer remains unidentified.

Pending: Review the supplied English completion that day will not come at 2:3, the elliptical restrainer clause in 2:7, breath/spirit at 2:8, firstfruits versus from the beginning at 2:13, and the genitive relations in sanctification by the Spirit and faith in the truth. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved the running word, faith/faithful, love/endurance, walking, bread, labor/toil/night/day, willingness to work, working/other people’s business contrast, brother rather than enemy, peace/all and the handwritten greeting. Smoothed disorderly constructions and the right/example sentence; modernized forms and ordinary address. Retained bracketed our at 3:6 and the plural they received. No final Amen was added.

Pending: Review evil/evil one in 3:3, love of God/endurance of Christ in 3:5, disorderly/idle in its work context and the relation of the discipline commands to the brotherly instruction in 3:15. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
