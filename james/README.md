## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Ordinary plural address and modern third-person verbs restored. Trials and temptation remain distinguished by context (1:2,12–14); 1:13 is rendered cannot be tempted by evil. Complete is retained across 1:4,17,25; act of giving distinguishes the giving from the gift in 1:17. The divine Father-of-lights image, conception and birth, firstfruits, mirror, bridled tongue and unstained religion remain. Become doers in 1:22 restores the imperative’s becoming language. Past narrative forms in 1:11,24 are retained; 1:25 is smoothed without removing doing/hearing contrast.

Pending: Review the nuance of apeirastos in 1:13, shadow from turning in 1:17, the imperative/indicative force of iste in 1:19 and the completion vocabulary. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary human address and third-person verbs modernized. The Lord-of-glory expression, partiality, royal law and mercy/judgment are retained. That faith in 2:14 is an interpretive rendering of the article, not a Greek demonstrative. In 2:19 the opening is declarative, matching the displayed NA28 punctuation. Idle in 2:20 follows arge, rather than substituting the variant dead. Justified by works and not by faith alone (2:24) remains without harmonizing James with Paul.

Pending: Review the genitive expressions in 2:1, the speaker boundaries in 2:18, the article in 2:14, and the argument about justification in its full context. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modern third-person verbs restored. The pilot intends in 3:4 renders the pilot’s directing impulse naturally. The bridle, rudder, tongue/fire, wheel of life, poison, spring and fruit images remain, as do Lord and Father (3:9), sweet/bitter water, complete man (3:2), and the ordered qualities of wisdom.

Pending: Review the wheel of life expression (3:6), psychike/merely natural (3:15), impartial versus unwavering (3:17), and the agency/beneficiary dative in 3:18. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** The two opening where questions and the pleasures that wage war are preserved in natural English. Adulteresses is retained without adding a masculine doublet. The difficult spirit/yearning clause (4:5) retains the inherited God-as-subject interpretation provisionally. Ordinary human imperatives remain you. The life-as-vapor image, conditional future life and action, and condemnation of omission remain.

Pending: Priority specialist review: 4:5–6 has multiple possible subjects and quotation boundaries; the inherited English question ends in 4:5, whereas displayed NA28 punctuation carries it into greater grace in 4:6. This interpretive punctuation has not been silently certified. Review also the indefinite city expression in 4:13. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** The fire simile in 5:3 now modifies devouring flesh, matching displayed NA28 punctuation, rather than treasure storage. In 5:6 He does not oppose you replaces the inherited question, matching displayed NA28 declarative punctuation. Modern verbs and ordinary you address restored. Lord Sabaoth, the day of slaughter, early/late rain, doors, Job, the sick person, oil, confession and prayer remain. The prayer participle in 5:16 stays when it is at work without adding emotional fervor; Elijah’s emphatic prayer and three years six months remain.

Pending: Review 5:6’s righteous one and interpretation, 5:7’s implied rain, telos kyriou in 5:11, the prayer/healing and forgiveness clauses in 5:14–16, the participle energoumene in 5:16 and the soul pronoun in 5:20. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
