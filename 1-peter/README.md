## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** New birth and living hope replace the older begotten/unto register without losing the birth image. Ordinary you address and judges restored. In 1:13 fully now qualifies set your hope rather than sober, while gird up the loins of your mind remains. Pure in 1:22 is restored from the consulted NA28 wording. The imperishable inheritance/seed, fire-tested faith, lamb/blood and grass/flower images remain; quoted human address in 1:16 uses you.

Pending: Review the time/person interpretation in 1:11, the adverb attachment in 1:13, and the word/God attachment in 1:23. The restored pure in 1:22 needs apparatus review. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary address and modern verbs restored, with a smoother living-stones clause and conduct clause. The stone/head-of-corner images, royal priesthood, holy nation, possession, darkness/light, household slaves and crooked masters remain. Awareness of God smooths 2:19 without changing the conscience/awareness relation. In 2:24 tree and singular wound are retained; sins remains plural. The four short commands in 2:17 remain distinct.

Pending: Review logikon/spiritual milk (2:2), indicative/imperative force in 2:5, institution/creation in 2:13, grace/favor in 2:19–20 and the unexpressed object of entrusted in 2:23; His cause is an inherited explanatory supplement. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** The weaker-vessel image and fellow-heir dignity remain together; women replaces the awkward substantival female, with the weaker-vessel image still explicit. Sarah calls Abraham lord, with no divine-address archaism introduced. Christ as Lord in 3:15 follows the consulted wording. Christ suffered once for sins to bring you to God (3:18) remains, as do flesh/spirit, spirits in prison, Noah, eight souls and baptism saves you. Also is included in 3:21.

Pending: Priority review of 3:18–21: flesh/spirit, the spirits and proclamation, the water/baptism correspondence, and eperotema as appeal or pledge. Review the vessel comparison in 3:7 and fear their fear in 3:14; no doctrinal harmonization has been inserted. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modern third-person verbs restored. The vice list retains its distinct items, and the flood image remains in flood of reckless living. Prayers remains plural in 4:7. Words from God renders logia naturally in 4:11. In this matter at 4:16 follows the consulted NA28 merei rather than inherited in this name. The proclamation even to the dead (4:6), fiery ordeal, Christian, judgment beginning at God’s house, and faithful Creator remain.

Pending: Priority review of 4:1’s cessation from sin and 4:6’s proclamation to the dead. The name/matter variant at 4:16 requires apparatus/ECM review. Review the referent of to whom in 4:11 and the rare meddler term in 4:15. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** The initial therefore in 5:1 is removed because it is absent from the consulted NA28 running text. Shepherding, oversight, willingly according to God, the flock and all paired contrasts remain. Humility as clothing, mighty hand, casting anxiety and the roaring lion are preserved. The four future verbs in 5:10 remain distinct. Forever in 5:11 represents the shorter to the ages formula, distinguished from ages of ages in 4:11. Silvanus, Babylon, Mark, kiss of love and the final peace greeting remain; no final Amen is added.

Pending: Review the oversight/according to God readings in the apparatus, the imperative stand in 5:12, the role of Silvanus, and the identity of she in Babylon. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
