1 Peter 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen resident aliens of the dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

1:2according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification by the Spirit, for obedience and sprinkling with the blood of Jesus Christ: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.

A Living Hope

1:3Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who, according to His great mercy, has given us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

1:4into an inheritance imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you,

1:5who are guarded by the power of God through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

1:6In this you rejoice, though now, for a little while, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials,

1:7so that the tested genuineness of your faith—more precious than gold that perishes, though tested by fire—may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

1:8Though you have not seen Him, you love Him; though you do not see Him now, yet believing in Him, you rejoice with joy inexpressible and full of glory,

1:9receiving the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

1:10Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace for you searched and investigated,

1:11investigating what time or what kind of time the Spirit of Christ within them was indicating, as He testified beforehand to the sufferings destined for Christ and the glories after them.

1:12It was revealed to them that they were serving these things for you, and not for themselves—things now announced to you through those who proclaimed the good news to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, things into which angels long to look.

Be Holy in All Your Conduct

1:13Therefore, gird up the loins of your mind; be sober, and set your hope fully on the grace being brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

1:14As children of obedience, do not conform yourselves to the former desires of your ignorance,

1:15but, in accordance with the Holy One who called you, become holy yourselves in all your conduct,

1:16because it is written, “You shall be holy, because I am holy.”

1:17And if you call upon as Father the One who judges impartially according to each person’s work, conduct yourselves in fear during the time of your sojourn,

1:18knowing that you were ransomed from the futile conduct handed down from your ancestors, not with perishable things, silver or gold,

1:19but with precious blood, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot—the blood of Christ,

1:20who was foreknown before the foundation of the world but was manifested at the end of the times for your sake.

1:21Through Him you are believers in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.

Born Again Through the Living Word

1:22Having purified your souls by obedience to the truth for sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from a pure heart,

1:23having been born anew, not from perishable seed but from imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.

1:24For “All flesh is like grass, and all its glory like a flower of grass. The grass withered, and the flower fell,

1:25but the word of the Lord remains forever.” And this is the word that was proclaimed to you as good news.

1 Peter 2

Read this chapter on staging

Pure Spiritual Milk

2:1Therefore, putting away all malice and all deceit and hypocrisies and envies and all slanders,

2:2like newborn infants, long for the pure spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow into salvation,

2:3if you have tasted that the Lord is good.

The Living Stone and a Holy People

2:4Coming to Him, a living stone rejected by human beings but chosen and precious before God,

2:5you yourselves, like living stones, are also being built into a spiritual house, for a holy priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

2:6For it stands in Scripture: “Look, I am laying in Zion a stone, a chosen, precious cornerstone, and the one who believes in Him will certainly not be put to shame.”

2:7Therefore, the honor is for you who believe; but for those who do not believe, “The stone that the builders rejected has become the head of the corner,”

2:8and “a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense.” They stumble, disobeying the word; to this they were also appointed.

2:9But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s possession, so that you may proclaim the excellences of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.

2:10Once you were not a people, but now you are God’s people; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.

Beloved, I Urge You, as Aliens and Exiles

2:11Beloved, I urge you, as aliens and sojourners, to abstain from fleshly desires that wage war against the soul,

2:12keeping your conduct good among the Gentiles, so that, in the very matter for which they slander you as evildoers, they may observe your good works and glorify God on the day of visitation.

Submission to Human Institutions

2:13Submit yourselves to every human institution for the Lord’s sake, whether to a king as supreme,

2:14or to governors as sent by him for the punishment of evildoers and the praise of those who do good.

2:15For this is the will of God: that by doing good you should silence the ignorance of foolish people.

2:16Live as free people, yet without using freedom as a covering for evil, but as slaves of God.

2:17Honor everyone. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king.

Slaves and the Example of Christ

2:18Household slaves, submit yourselves to your masters with all fear, not only to the good and considerate but also to the crooked.

2:19For this is grace: if, out of awareness of God, someone bears sorrows while suffering unjustly.

2:20For what credit is there if, when you sin and are beaten, you endure? But if, when you do good and suffer, you endure, this is grace before God.

2:21For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in His footsteps.

2:22He committed no sin, nor was deceit found in His mouth.

2:23When He was reviled, He did not revile in return; when He suffered, He did not threaten, but entrusted His cause to the One who judges justly.

2:24He Himself bore our sins in His body upon the tree, so that, having died to sins, we might live to righteousness. By His wound you were healed.

2:25For you were wandering like sheep, but now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.

1 Peter 3

Read this chapter on staging

Wives and Husbands

3:1Likewise, wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands, so that even if some disobey the word, they may be won without a word through the conduct of their wives,

3:2when they observe your pure conduct in fear.

3:3Let your adornment not be the outward kind—braiding hair, putting on gold ornaments, or dressing in garments—

3:4but the hidden person of the heart, in the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is very precious before God.

3:5For this is how the holy women of former times, who hoped in God, also adorned themselves, submitting themselves to their own husbands,

3:6as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord. You have become her children if you do good and do not fear any terror.

Likewise, Husbands, Live with Understanding

3:7Likewise, husbands, live with your wives with understanding, honoring them as women—the weaker vessel—and also as fellow heirs of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered.

Blessing for Insult

3:8Finally, all of you, be of one mind, sympathetic, loving as brothers, tenderhearted, and humble-minded,

3:9not repaying evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but, on the contrary, blessing, because you were called for this, so that you might inherit a blessing.

3:10For “The one who wishes to love life and see good days must keep his tongue from evil and his lips from speaking deceit.

3:11He must turn away from evil and do good; he must seek peace and pursue it.

3:12For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears attend to their petition, but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”

Suffering for Righteousness

3:13And who will harm you if you become zealous for what is good?

3:14But even if you should suffer for righteousness, you are blessed. Do not fear their fear, and do not be troubled,

3:15but sanctify Christ as Lord in your hearts, always ready to give a defense to everyone who asks you for an account of the hope within you,

3:16yet with gentleness and fear, keeping a good conscience, so that, in the very matter for which you are slandered, those who abuse your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame.

3:17For it is better, if the will of God should will it, to suffer while doing good than while doing evil.

Christ Suffered Once for Sins

3:18For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, so that He might bring you to God, being put to death in flesh but made alive in spirit,

3:19in which He also went and proclaimed to the spirits in prison,

3:20who were once disobedient, when the patience of God waited in the days of Noah while the ark was being prepared, in which a few—that is, eight souls—were brought safely through water.

3:21Corresponding to this, baptism also now saves you—not a removal of dirt from the flesh, but an appeal to God for a good conscience—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,

3:22who is at the right hand of God, having gone into heaven, with angels and authorities and powers subjected to Him.

1 Peter 4

Read this chapter on staging

Living for the Will of God

4:1Therefore, since Christ suffered in flesh, arm yourselves also with the same thought, because the one who has suffered in flesh has ceased from sin,

4:2so as to live the remaining time in flesh no longer for human desires but for the will of God.

4:3For you have spent enough time doing what the Gentiles want, walking in debaucheries, desires, bouts of drunkenness, revelries, drinking parties, and lawless acts of idolatry.

4:4They are surprised that you do not run with them into the same flood of reckless living, and they slander you.

4:5They will give an account to the One who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

4:6For this is why the good news was proclaimed even to the dead: so that they might be judged according to human beings in flesh, but live according to God in spirit.

The End of All Things Is Near

4:7But the end of all things has drawn near. Therefore, be clear-minded and sober for your prayers.

4:8Above all, maintain earnest love for one another, because love covers a multitude of sins.

4:9Be hospitable to one another without grumbling.

4:10As each has received a gift, use it in serving one another, as good stewards of God’s varied grace.

4:11If anyone speaks, let it be as words from God; if anyone serves, let it be with the strength God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom belong the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.

Sharing the Sufferings of Christ

4:12Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery ordeal among you, which comes upon you for testing, as though something strange were happening to you.

4:13But insofar as you share in the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, so that you may also rejoice with exultation at the revelation of His glory.

4:14If you are reviled for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests upon you.

4:15For let none of you suffer as a murderer, or a thief, or an evildoer, or as a meddler in others’ affairs.

4:16But if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify God in this matter.

4:17For it is time for judgment to begin from the house of God; and if it begins first from us, what will be the outcome for those who disobey the good news of God?

4:18And “If the righteous one is saved with difficulty, where will the ungodly person and sinner appear?”

4:19Therefore, let those who suffer according to the will of God entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good.

1 Peter 5

Read this chapter on staging

Shepherd the Flock of God

5:1I exhort the elders among you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a participant in the glory about to be revealed:

5:2Shepherd the flock of God among you, exercising oversight, not under compulsion but willingly, according to God; not for shameful gain but eagerly;

5:3not as lording it over those allotted to you, but becoming examples to the flock.

5:4And when the Chief Shepherd is manifested, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.

Humility and Watchfulness

5:5Likewise, you who are younger, submit yourselves to the elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.”

5:6Therefore, humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, so that He may exalt you in due time,

5:7casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He cares for you.

5:8Be sober; be watchful. Your adversary, the devil, walks about like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour.

5:9Resist him, firm in the faith, knowing that the same kinds of sufferings are being experienced by your brotherhood in the world.

5:10And the God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Christ, after you have suffered a little while, will Himself restore, establish, strengthen, and firmly ground you.
