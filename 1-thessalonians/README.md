## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved Silvanus, the shorter greeting, every/all, the three work/faith labor/love endurance/hope pairings, Spirit, sounded out/gone out, idols and the living/true God. Modernized has and smoothed mention and coming. The participial connection across 1:2–3 and the singular example at 1:7 remain.

Pending: Review the attachment of unceasingly across 1:2–3, election and assurance, and the senses of the entry/coming at 1:9. Bracketed articles and prepositions in 1:4–5, 1:8 and 1:10 require apparatus review. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved the little-children reading at 2:7 and the distinct nursing-mother and father comparisons, repeated denials and labor/toil, the kingdom/glory, bereavement/face/heart and crown images, and once and twice. Smoothed coming and modernized verbs; linked the nursing comparison to so, longing for you at 2:8. Kept the exact scope and sequence of 2:14–16 without adding a historical identification.

Pending: Review the little children/gentle variant, weight/burden in 2:7, the scope and polemical context of 2:14–16, and to the end/at last/fully at 2:16. No particular historical event is assigned to the wrath clause. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved we/I shifts, afflictions and the tempter/tempted repetition, labor, faith/love, night/day, face, overflowing love and the prayer before God. Smoothed willing, fear and remembrance; modernized has. Retained God’s fellow worker at 3:2 and the shorter Lord Jesus titles. Added Amen at 3:13 from bracketed NA28 wording, with the shorter inherited form archived.

Pending: Review the fellow worker/servant variants at 3:2, the nuanced shaken expression at 3:3, the scope of saints/holy ones at 3:13, and the bracketed Amen. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved walk/abound, sanctification, vessel, the three holiness clauses, brotherly love, ambition to be quiet, sleep/death, the emphatic not preceding, cry/voice/trumpet, clouds, air and together/always with the Lord. Modernized ordinary you and future will and smoothed urge/unaware. Added own at 4:11 from bracketed NA28 wording; retained bracketed also at 4:8.

Pending: Review acquire/possess/control one’s vessel at 4:4, including body and spouse interpretations, the scope of the matter at 4:6, need of no one/nothing at 4:12, and the attachment of through Jesus at 4:14. The rendering adds no eschatological timetable. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserved times/seasons, thief/night, childbirth, light/day, wake/sleep, sobriety, breastplate/helmet, building, quenching, the testing commands, every/all, spirit/soul/body and the holy kiss. Smoothed several phrases and ordinary commands while retaining distinct wholeness expressions in 5:23. Included bracketed also at 5:25. The bracketed conjunction at 5:15 is represented by the two coordinated recipients. No final Amen is added at 5:28.

Pending: Review the sleep/waking senses in 5:6–10, disorderly versus idle at 5:14, form/kind of evil in 5:22 and the syntax of the wholeness prayer at 5:23. No technical anthropology is supplied by the three nouns. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
