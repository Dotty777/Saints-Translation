## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved the promise, pure conscience, night/day, rekindling, cowardice/power/love/self-discipline, pre-age grace, appearing, death and incorruptibility, both deposits, chains and the repeated Lord at 1:18. Ordinary Timothy address and third-person verbs are modernized. Christ Jesus at 1:10 follows the displayed NA28 order; the inherited Jesus Christ remains archived. Sincere faith replaces unhypocritical faith.

Pending: Review the temporal expression at 1:9, the ownership or direction of my deposit in 1:12, the pattern at 1:13, the force of the comparative in 1:18, and the petitions concerning Onesiphorus without asserting his life/death status. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved soldier/athlete/farmer, fruit, seed, chains, death/life/endurance/denial/faithfulness, gangrene, foundation/seal, vessels, youthful desires and snare/capture. Before God at 2:14 follows the displayed NA28 reading in place of inherited before the Lord. At 2:15 the straight-course image is restored from the earlier record. The at 2:18 reflects the bracketed Greek article. Commands to Timothy use ordinary address.

Pending: Review through many witnesses at 2:2, the precise straight-cutting image at 2:15, the pronoun subject in 2:16, these things/these people in 2:21, and the competing pronoun referents and capture/sobriety syntax in 2:26. Escape supplies the force of the Greek out-of construction. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Preserved each vice-list item and repeated lovers, godliness/power, captivity, the Jannes/Jambres names, the entire sequence of conduct and sufferings, deceiving/being deceived, infancy and sacred writings, God-breathed and all four uses of Scripture. Those from whom in 3:14 makes the consulted plural explicit. Natural English replaces difficult seasons, human beings, unapproved and advance toward the worse.

Pending: Review the pejorative diminutive rendered weak women at 3:6 in its local context, disqualified at 3:8, the bracketed article in 3:15, all/every Scripture and predicate/attributive possibilities at 3:16, and person of God at 3:17. The text does not generalize 3:6 to all women or insert an inspiration theory. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved the charge, living/dead, appearing/kingdom, five commands, season pairing, sound teaching, heaping teachers and itching-ear image, pouring out, contest/course/faith, crown/Judge, all names and places, cloak/books/parchments, first defense, lion mouth and heavenly kingdom. Modernized Timothy address and has, and smoothed make every effort, award and did much harm. The final singular your spirit and plural you all remain distinct; no final Amen is added.

Pending: Review the force of the heaping and itching-ear expression, the pouring-out image, the good contest and course, award/repay, Galatia textual alternatives, cloak versus document-case, and save into the heavenly kingdom. The lion is not identified beyond the text. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
