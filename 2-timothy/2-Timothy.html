2 Timothy 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, according to the promise of life in Christ Jesus,

1:2to Timothy, a beloved child: grace, mercy, peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

Thanksgiving for Timothy’s Faith

1:3I thank God, whom I serve, as my forebears did, with a pure conscience, as I remember you unceasingly in my petitions night and day,

1:4longing to see you, remembering your tears, so that I may be filled with joy,

1:5having been reminded of the sincere faith in you, which dwelt first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it dwells also in you.

1:6For this reason I remind you to rekindle God’s gift, which is in you through the laying on of my hands.

1:7For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power and love and self-discipline.

Not Ashamed of the Testimony

1:8Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord or of me, His prisoner, but share suffering for the gospel according to the power of God,

1:9who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before the times of the ages,

1:10but has now been revealed through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and incorruptibility to light through the gospel.

1:11For this I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher.

1:12For this reason I also suffer these things, but I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that He is able to guard my deposit for that day.

1:13Keep the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in faith and love in Christ Jesus.

1:14Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

Onesiphorus

1:15You know this, that all those in Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.

1:16May the Lord give mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain,

1:17but when he came to Rome, he earnestly searched for me and found me.

1:18May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day. And you know very well how much service he gave in Ephesus.

2 Timothy 1:7 · self-discipline

Meaning. Sound judgment and disciplined conduct contrasted with cowardice.

Decision. Retain the three terms and the preceding cowardice, which is more specific than every kind of fear. Explain sound mind and sobriety without making a medical claim.

Excluded. all fear treated as personal failure

2 Timothy 2

Read this chapter on staging

A Good Soldier of Christ Jesus

2:1You, then, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus,

2:2and the things you heard from me through many witnesses, entrust these to faithful people who will be able to teach others also.

2:3Share suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

2:4No one serving as a soldier entangles himself in the affairs of ordinary life, so that he may please the one who enlisted him.

2:5And if someone competes as an athlete, he is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.

2:6The farmer who labors must be first to share in the fruits.

2:7Consider what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all things.

Remember Jesus Christ

2:8Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, from David’s seed, according to my gospel,

2:9for which I suffer hardship even to chains as a criminal. But the word of God has not been chained.

2:10For this reason I endure all things for the chosen ones, so that they too may obtain the salvation in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.

2:11The saying is trustworthy: for if we died with Him, we will also live with Him;

2:12if we endure, we will also reign with Him; if we deny Him, He also will deny us;

2:13if we are unfaithful, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself.

A Worker Approved by God

2:14Remind them of these things, charging them before God not to quarrel over words, which does no good and leads to the ruin of those who hear.

2:15Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, a worker with no cause for shame, cutting a straight course for the word of truth.

2:16But avoid profane empty talk, for they will advance into further ungodliness,

2:17and their word will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

2:18who missed the mark concerning truth, saying that the resurrection has already happened, and overturn the faith of some.

2:19Nevertheless, the firm foundation of God stands, having this seal: “The Lord knew those who are His,” and, “Let everyone who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.”

Vessels for Honor

2:20Now in a great house there are vessels not only of gold and silver but also of wood and clay, some for honor and some for dishonor.

2:21Therefore if someone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.

2:22But flee youthful desires, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call upon the Lord from a pure heart.

2:23And reject foolish and ignorant disputes, knowing that they give birth to fights.

2:24But a slave of the Lord must not fight, but must be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient with wrong,

2:25instructing opponents with gentleness, in case God may give them repentance toward the knowledge of truth,

2:26and they may regain their sobriety and escape the devil’s snare, having been captured by him to do his will.

Excluded. a theological partition inferred from the English dividing

2 Timothy 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Last Days

3:1But know this: in the last days difficult times will come.

3:2For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, blasphemous, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,

3:3without natural affection, irreconcilable, slanderers, without self-control, savage, not loving the good,

3:4traitors, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

3:5having a form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from these also.

3:6For among these are those who slip into houses and take captive weak women, laden with sins, led by various desires,

3:7always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of truth.

3:8And just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these too oppose the truth, people corrupted in mind, disqualified concerning the faith.

3:9But they will make no further progress, for their folly will be clear to all, as the folly of those men also became.

Paul’s Example and Persecutions

3:10But you followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love, endurance,

3:11persecutions, sufferings, such as happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra, what persecutions I endured; and out of all of them the Lord rescued me.

3:12And all who want to live godly lives in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.

3:13But evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.

The Sacred Writings

3:14But you, remain in the things you learned and became convinced of, knowing those from whom you learned,

3:15and that from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.

3:16All Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness,

3:17so that the person of God may be capable, fully equipped for every good work.

2 Timothy 4

Read this chapter on staging

Proclaim the Word

4:1I charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is to judge the living and the dead, and by His appearing and His kingdom:

4:2proclaim the word; be ready in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.

4:3For a time will come when they will not endure sound teaching, but with itching ears they will heap up teachers for themselves according to their own desires,

4:4and they will turn their hearing away from the truth and turn aside to myths.

4:5But you, be sober in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your service.

The Crown of Righteousness

4:6For I am already being poured out, and the time of my departure has arrived.

4:7I have fought the good fight; I have finished the course; I have kept the faith.

4:8From now on the crown of righteousness is laid up for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award me on that day, and not to me alone but also to all who have loved His appearing.

Personal Instructions

4:9Make every effort to come to me soon,

4:10for Demas deserted me, having loved the present age, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia; Titus to Dalmatia.

4:11Luke alone is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service.

4:12But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

4:13When you come, bring the cloak which I left in Troas with Carpus, and the books, especially the parchments.

4:14Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm; the Lord will repay him according to his works.

4:15You too must guard yourself against him, for he strongly opposed our words.

4:16At my first defense no one came to my side, but all deserted me. May it not be counted against them.

4:17But the Lord stood beside me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fulfilled and all the nations might hear, and I was rescued from a lion’s mouth.

4:18The Lord will rescue me from every evil work and save me into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be glory for the ages of ages. Amen.

Final Greetings

4:19Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.

4:20Erastus remained in Corinth, but I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.

4:21Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers.

4:22The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.
