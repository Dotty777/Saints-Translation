# Judges — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 21 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 618 existing verse units received an English editorial review: 213 changed and 405 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer *Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf* governs and supersedes the older *Saints English Founding Style Guide* where their pronoun policies conflict. Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your. Accepted Genesis 1–11 is the working English benchmark. Successful earlier wording is retained.

The existing selected A form of Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Selected running-text passages were inspected in the [German Bible Society edition](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/JDG.1), with exact scope in source-checks.json. Göttingen, the full apparatus and A/B witness collation were not undertaken. No complete new Greek verification or human read-aloud review is claimed.

## Preserved distinctions and editorial decisions

Every original verse ID, link, navigation destination, image, dagger and existing review note is preserved. All 18 existing dagger notes remain linked. The revision does not harmonize the selected Greek form with Hebrew, a B-form presentation, or a familiar English version.

The browser review found 34 inherited unnumbered fragments in chapter 5 that repeated material already present in its complete numbered verses. Each was checked and removed from display. [layout-decisions.json](layout-decisions.json) records their wording and removal separately from the 213 verse changes; the original blocks remain in the backup. No verse, note or source clause was removed.

Natural questions and requests replace strained inversions and ordinary archaic pronouns. Judges 6:26 retains the future “will cut down”; 7:7 uses “save you all” to distinguish the plural recipients from Gideon’s singular “your hand.” Judges 13:16 uses plural “loaves.” Judges 14:16 retains completed action with present force. Flax fibre clarifies “tow” in 15:14 while preserving the smell-of-fire image. The command and question across 4:6–7 receive clearer punctuation without moving any clause across its verse boundary.

Distinctive readings remain: redemption of water and Rechab (chapter 1); fifty years, handsome Eglon and the calves phrase (chapter 3); the difficult song of Deborah (chapter 5); thirteen servants (6:27); barkonnim/barakēnim and uncertain ornaments (chapter 8); thirty-two counts and the difficult divine response (chapter 10); Jephthah’s vow and its fulfillment (chapter 11); Password (12:6); the wound of the jawbone (15:19); all night and one vengeance for two eyes (chapter 16); Moses in the genealogy (18:30); the explicit death statement (19:28); differing casualty totals and offerings of deliverance (chapter 20); visitation, comforted, Lebanon/Lebona and the difficult offence argument (chapter 21).

The existing 18 review notes and the chapter-specific pending questions are the specialist review queue. Unclear referents, messenger identity, source distinctions and opaque expressions remain provisional. Ordinary address to a messenger does not decide the messenger’s identity. Narrative violence, sexuality, coercion and servitude have neither been softened nor intensified.

Saints Translation literal-smooth and all six study/comparison pages were left unchanged and passed content-hash checks. No live-site update was made. This is a book-level archive, not a full-site backup.

Joshua and Judges have completed this division’s Bespoke English/staging pass. Ruth is next; thirteen books in the division remain pending. [Repository progress](../progress.json) supersedes next-book statements in earlier snapshots.
