Zechariah 1

Read this chapter on staging

Return to Me, Says the Lord Almighty

1:1In the eighth month of the second year under Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, Berechiah’s son, son of Addo, the prophet, saying:

1:2“The Lord was angry with your fathers with great anger.

1:3You shall say to them: Thus says the Lord Almighty: Return to Me, and I will return to you, says the Lord.

1:4Do not become like your fathers, against whom the former prophets brought charges, saying, ‘Thus says the Lord Almighty: Turn away from your evil ways and your evil practices.’ But they did not listen attentively to Me, says the Lord.

1:5Your fathers—where are they? And the prophets—surely they will not live forever?

1:6Yet receive My words and My ordinances, all that I, in My Spirit, command My servants the prophets, who overtook your fathers.” And they answered and said, “As the Lord Almighty has arrayed Himself to act according to your ways and practices, so He acted toward you.”

The First Vision: The Man Among the Myrtles

1:7On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month—which is the month Sabat—in the second year under Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah, Berechiah’s son, son of Addo, the prophet, saying:

1:8I saw in the night: look, a man mounted on a flame-red horse! He stood between the two shaded mountains, and behind him were flame-red, gray, dappled and white horses.

1:9I said, “What are these, lord?” The angel speaking within me said to me, “I will show you what these are.”

1:10The man standing between the mountains answered and said to me, “These are the ones the Lord has sent out to travel around the earth.”

1:11They answered the angel of the Lord standing between the mountains, “We have traveled around all the earth, and look, all the earth is inhabited and quiet.”

1:12The angel of the Lord answered, “Lord Almighty, how long wilt Thou refuse mercy to Jerusalem and Judah’s cities, which Thou hast overlooked this seventieth year?”

1:13The Lord Almighty answered the angel speaking within me with good words and comforting speeches.

I Will Return to Jerusalem in Compassion

1:14The angel speaking within me said to me, “Cry out: Thus says the Lord Almighty: I have been jealous for Jerusalem and Zion with great jealousy,

1:15and I am angry with great anger at the nations joining in assault, because I was a little angry, but they joined in attacking to bring evils.

1:16Therefore thus says the Lord: I will return to Jerusalem in compassion, and My house will be rebuilt in her, says the Lord Almighty, and a measure will again be extended over Jerusalem.”

1:17The angel speaking within me said to me, “Cry out: Thus says the Lord Almighty: Cities will again overflow with good things; the Lord will again have mercy on Zion and again choose Jerusalem.”

Zechariah 2

Read this chapter on staging

The Second Vision: Four Horns and Four Craftsmen

2:1I lifted my eyes and saw: look, four horns!

2:2I said to the angel speaking within me, “What are these, lord?” He said to me, “These are the horns that scattered Judah and Israel.”

2:3The Lord showed me four craftsmen.

2:4I said, “What are these coming to do?” He said to me, “These horns that scattered Judah also shattered Israel, and none of them lifted a head. These entered to sharpen them for their hands—the four horns, the nations lifting a horn against the Lord’s land to scatter it.”

The Third Vision: The Measuring Line

2:5I lifted my eyes and saw: look, a man with a land-measuring cord in his hand!

2:6I said to him, “Where are you going?” He said to me, “To measure Jerusalem, to see how wide it is and how long.”

2:7Look, the angel speaking within me was standing, and another angel was going out to meet him,

2:8and said to him, “Run and speak to that young man: Jerusalem will be inhabited fruitfully because of the multitude of people and animals within her.

2:9I will be a wall of fire around her, says the Lord, and I will be glory within her.”

Flee from the Northern Land

2:10“Oh! Oh! Flee from the northern land,” says the Lord, “because I will gather you from the four winds of heaven,” says the Lord.

2:11Escape safely to Zion, you who dwell in daughter Babylon!

2:12For thus says the Lord Almighty: “After glory He has sent me against the nations that plundered you, for whoever touches you is like one touching the pupil of his eye.

2:13Look, I am bringing My hand against them, and they will become spoils for those serving them; you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me.”

Delight and Rejoice, Daughter Zion

2:14“Delight and rejoice, daughter Zion, for look, I am coming and will dwell within you,” says the Lord.

2:15Many nations will take refuge with the Lord on that day and become His people. They will dwell within you, and you will recognize that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you.

2:16The Lord will inherit Judah as His portion on the holy land and again choose Jerusalem.

2:17Let all flesh be reverent before the Lord, for He has risen from His holy clouds.

Zechariah 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Fourth Vision: Joshua the High Priest

3:1He showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, with the slanderer standing at his right hand to oppose him.

3:2The Lord said to the slanderer, “May the Lord rebuke you, slanderer! May the Lord, who chose Jerusalem, rebuke you! Look, is this not like a brand pulled from fire?”

3:3Joshua was clothed in filthy garments and standing before the angel.

3:4He answered and said to those standing before him, “Remove the filthy garments from him.” He said to him, “Look, I have taken away your lawless deeds; clothe him in a robe reaching to the feet,

3:5and put a clean turban on his head.” They clothed him in garments and put a clean turban on his head, while the angel of the Lord stood by.

The Charge to Joshua

3:6The angel of the Lord solemnly testified to Joshua, saying:

3:7“Thus says the Lord Almighty: If you walk in My ways and keep My commands, you will govern My house. If you also guard My court, I will give you those who move about among these standing here.

The Servant Who Is Coming

3:8Listen now, Joshua the high priest, you and your neighbors sitting before you, for they are men who watch for portents. Look, I am bringing My servant, Rising.

3:9For the stone I set before Joshua—on the one stone are seven eyes. Look, I am digging a pit, says the Lord Almighty, and I will feel out all the injustice of that land in one day.

3:10On that day, says the Lord Almighty, each of you will invite his neighbor beneath a vine and beneath a fig tree.”

Zechariah 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Fifth Vision: The Lampstand and the Olive Trees

4:1The angel speaking within me returned and roused me as when someone is awakened from sleep,

4:2and said to me, “What do you see?” I said, “I see a lampstand entirely of gold, with its lamp-bowl above it, seven lamps upon it, and seven pouring channels for the lamps above it.

4:3Two olive trees stand over it, one to the right of the lamp-bowl and one to the left.”

4:4I asked the angel speaking within me, “What are these, lord?”

4:5The angel speaking within me answered me, “Do you not know what these are?” I said, “No, lord.”

Not by Power, but by My Spirit

4:6He answered me, “This is the Lord’s word to Zerubbabel: Not by great power or by strength, but by My Spirit, says the Lord Almighty.

4:7Who are you, great mountain, before Zerubbabel, to set things straight? I will bring out the stone of inheritance—an equality of grace, its grace.”

4:8The word of the Lord came to me, saying:

4:9“Zerubbabel’s hands laid this house’s foundation, and his hands will complete it. You will recognize that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you.

4:10For who treated the small days with contempt? They will rejoice and see the tin stone in Zerubbabel’s hand. These seven are the Lord’s eyes, looking over all the earth.”

The Two Sons of Richness

4:11I answered him, “What are these two olive trees to the lampstand’s right and left?”

4:12I asked a second time, “What are the two olive branches in the hands of the two golden nozzles that pour upon and draw up the golden pouring channels?”

4:13He said to me, “Do you not know what these are?” I said, “No, lord.”

4:14He said, “These two sons of richness stand beside the Lord of all the earth.”

Zechariah 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Sixth Vision: The Flying Sickle

5:1I turned and lifted my eyes and saw: look, a flying sickle!

5:2He said to me, “What do you see?” I said, “I see a flying sickle, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”

5:3He said to me, “This is the curse going out over the whole earth, because every thief will be punished by this with death, and every false swearer will be punished by this with death.

5:4I will bring it out, says the Lord Almighty. It will enter the thief’s house and the house of whoever swears falsely by My name; it will lodge within his house and destroy it completely, its timbers and its stones.”

The Seventh Vision: The Measure and the Woman

5:5The angel speaking within me came out and said to me, “Look up with your eyes and see what this is that is coming out.”

5:6I said, “What is it?” He said, “This is the measure coming out.” He said, “This is their injustice throughout the earth.”

5:7Look, a talent of lead was being lifted out, and look, one woman was sitting inside the measure!

5:8He said, “This is Lawlessness.” He threw her into the measure and threw the lead stone into her mouth.

5:9I lifted my eyes and saw: look, two women coming out, with wind in their wings! They had wings like a hoopoe’s wings, and they lifted the measure between earth and heaven.

5:10I said to the angel speaking within me, “Where are they carrying the measure?”

5:11He said to me, “To build a house for it in Babylon’s land and prepare it; they will place it there on its prepared setting.”

Read this chapter on staging

The Eighth Vision: The Four Chariots

6:1I turned and lifted my eyes and saw: look, four chariots coming out from between two mountains! The mountains were bronze mountains.

6:2The first chariot had flame-red horses, and the second chariot black horses,

6:3the third chariot white horses, and the fourth chariot dappled, gray horses.

6:4I answered and said to the angel speaking within me, “What are these, lord?”

6:5The angel speaking within me answered, “These are the four winds of heaven; they go out to stand beside the Lord of all the earth.

6:6The one with the black horses was going out toward the northern land; the white ones were going out behind them, and the dappled ones were going out toward the southern land,

6:7and the gray ones were going out and looking to go and travel around the earth.” He said, “Go and travel around the earth!” And they traveled around the earth.

6:8He cried out and said to me, “Look, those going out toward the northern land have brought My wrath to rest in the northern land.”

The Crowns for Joshua

6:9The word of the Lord came to me, saying:

6:10“Take the things from the captivity, from its rulers, from its useful people and from those who have known it; you shall go that day into the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah, who has come from Babylon,

6:11take silver and gold, make crowns and put them on the head of Joshua son of Josedek, the high priest,

The Man Who Will Build the Lord's House

6:12and say to him: Thus says the Lord Almighty: Look, a man whose name is Rising! From beneath him he will rise, and he will build the Lord’s house.

6:13He himself will receive excellence, sit and rule on his throne; the priest will be at his right hand, and peaceful counsel will be between both.

6:14The crown will be for those who endure, for its useful people and those who have known it, for the favor of Zephaniah’s son and for a psalm in the Lord’s house.

6:15Those far away from them will come and build in the Lord’s house, and you will know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you. This will happen if you truly listen to the voice of the Lord your God.”

Zechariah 7

Read this chapter on staging

The Question About Fasting

7:1In the fourth year under King Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Chaseleu,

7:2and Sarasar, King Arbeseer and his men sent to Bethel to appease the Lord,

7:3saying to the priests in the house of the Lord Almighty and to the prophets, “The holy offering has come here in the fifth month, as I have already done for many years.”

7:4The word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying:

7:5“Say to all the land’s people and to the priests: If you fast or mourn at the fifth or seventh observances—and look, seventy years—surely you have not fasted a fast for Me?

7:6If you eat or drink, are you not yourselves eating and yourselves drinking?

7:7Are these not the words the Lord spoke through the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and flourishing, with her cities around her, and the hill country and plain were inhabited?”

Render Righteous Judgment

7:8The word of the Lord came to Zechariah, saying:

7:9“Thus says the Lord Almighty: Render righteous judgment; show mercy and compassion, each to his brother.

7:10Do not oppress widow, orphan, newcomer or poor person, and let no one harbor resentment for his brother’s wrong in your hearts.”

They Disobeyed and Turned a Senseless Back

7:11But they refused to pay attention, turned a senseless back and made their ears heavy so as not to listen,

7:12and made their heart disobedient, so as not to listen to My law and the words the Lord Almighty sent by His Spirit through the former prophets. Great anger came from the Lord Almighty.

7:13It will be as He spoke and they did not listen to Him: so they will cry out, and I will certainly not listen, says the Lord Almighty.

7:14I will cast them out among all the nations they did not know, and the land behind them will be obliterated, without anyone passing through or returning. They made a choice land a desolation.

Zechariah 8

Read this chapter on staging

I Have Been Jealous for Jerusalem

8:1The word of the Lord Almighty came, saying:

8:2“Thus says the Lord Almighty: I have been jealous for Jerusalem and Zion with great jealousy; with great wrath I have been jealous for her.

8:3Thus says the Lord: I will return to Zion and dwell within Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the True City, and the mountain of the Lord Almighty a Holy Mountain.

8:4Thus says the Lord Almighty: Old men and old women will again sit in Jerusalem’s squares, each holding a staff in hand because of many days.

8:5The city’s squares will be filled with boys and girls playing in her squares.

8:6Thus says the Lord Almighty: If it is impossible before this people’s remnant in those days, surely it will not also be impossible before Me? says the Lord Almighty.

8:7Thus says the Lord Almighty: Look, I am rescuing My people from the eastern land and from the western land,

8:8and I will bring them in and dwell within Jerusalem. They will be My people, and I will be their God in truth and righteousness.

Let Your Hands Be Strong

8:9Thus says the Lord Almighty: Let your hands be strong, you who hear these words in these days from the prophets’ mouth, from the day when the house of the Lord Almighty was founded, and from when the temple was built.

8:10Before those days people’s wages will bring no benefit, and animals’ hire will not exist; those going out or coming in will have no peace because of affliction. I will send everyone out against his neighbor.

8:11Now I do not act toward this people’s remnant as in the former days, says the Lord Almighty,

8:12but I will show peace: the vine will give its fruit, the earth its produce, and heaven its dew. I will give My people’s remnant all these as an inheritance.

8:13As you were under a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will preserve you, and you will be in blessing. Take courage and be strong in your hands.

8:14For thus says the Lord Almighty: As I intended to harm you when your fathers provoked Me, says the Lord Almighty, and I did not change My mind,

8:15so I have arrayed Myself and intended in these days to do good to Jerusalem and Judah’s house. Take courage.

8:16These are the things you shall do: speak truth, each to his neighbor, and render peaceful judgment in your gates.

8:17Do not reckon your neighbor’s wrong in your hearts, and do not love a false oath, for I hate all these things, says the Lord Almighty.”

The Fasts Will Become Gladness

8:18The word of the Lord Almighty came to me, saying:

8:19“Thus says the Lord Almighty: The fourth fast, the fifth fast, the seventh fast and the tenth fast will become joy, gladness and good festivals for Judah’s house. You will rejoice; love truth and peace.

Many Peoples Will Come to Seek the Lord

8:20Thus says the Lord Almighty: Many peoples and residents of many cities will yet come.

8:21Residents of five cities will come together into one city, saying, ‘Let us go to entreat the Lord’s presence and seek the presence of the Lord Almighty. I too will go.’

8:22Many peoples and many nations will come to seek the presence of the Lord Almighty in Jerusalem and appease the Lord’s presence.

8:23Thus says the Lord Almighty: In those days, when ten men from all the nations’ languages take hold and grasp the fringe of a Jewish man’s garment, saying, ‘We will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you all.’”

Zechariah 9

Read this chapter on staging

An Oracle Against the Nations

9:1An oracle of the Lord’s word: in Sedrach’s land and Damascus is His sacrifice, because the Lord watches human beings and all Israel’s tribes,

9:2and Emath within its borders, Tyre and Sidon, because they were exceedingly wise.

9:3Tyre built herself fortifications, stored silver like dust and gathered gold like the mud of the roads.

9:4Therefore the Lord will inherit her and strike her strength into the sea; she will be consumed by fire.

9:5Ashkelon will see and fear; Gaza will suffer greatly, and Akkaron too, because she was ashamed at her downfall. A king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will certainly not be inhabited.

9:6Foreigners will dwell in Azotus, and I will bring down the foreigners’ arrogance.

9:7I will remove their blood from their mouth and their detestable things from between their teeth. This one too will remain for our God; they will be like a commander of a thousand in Judah, and Akkaron like the Jebusite.

9:8I will take a stand for My house to prevent passing through or returning. No one driving them out will come upon them again, for now I have seen with My own eyes.

Rejoice Greatly, Daughter Zion

9:9Rejoice greatly, daughter Zion; proclaim, daughter Jerusalem. Behold, your king comes to you, righteous and saving, gentle, and mounted upon a beast of burden and a young colt.

9:10He will destroy chariots from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem; the battle bow will be destroyed, and abundance and peace from the nations. He will rule waters as far as the sea and the earth’s river outlets.

By Covenant Blood I Released Your Prisoners

9:11You, by covenant blood, released your prisoners from a pit with no water.

9:12You will settle in a stronghold, prisoners of the gathering; in exchange for one day of your sojourn I will repay you double.

9:13For I bent you, Judah, as a bow for Myself; I filled Ephraim, and I will rouse your children, Zion, against the Greeks’ children and handle you like a fighter’s sword.

The Lord Shields and Saves His People

9:14The Lord will be over them; a missile will go out like lightning. The Lord Almighty will sound a trumpet and go in the tumult of His threat.

9:15The Lord Almighty will shield them. They will consume them, bury them with sling stones and drink them down like wine; they will fill the altar like bowls.

9:16The Lord will save them on that day, His people like sheep, because holy stones roll over His land.
