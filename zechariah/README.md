# Zechariah — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 14 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 211 verse units received an English editorial review: 90 changed and 121 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/ZEC.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 14 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Ordinary address to the prophet and third-person verbs are modernized; Thou and hast/wilt remain in the angel’s direct address to God at 1:12. Attributive My ordinances replaces Mine ordinances. Listening attentively and the ordering of the command clause improve flow, while return, great anger and jealousy repetitions, My Spirit, your rather than our at 1:6, two shaded mountains, four horse colors, speaking within me and this seventieth year remain. Has sent out makes the completed commission explicit at 1:10.

Pending: Review the syntax and antecedents in 1:6, the mountains and horse colors, and the seventieth-year expression. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Ordinary address uses you/your; the dimensions become how wide it is and how long, and completed sending is expressed as has sent. The four horns and craftsmen remain at 2:1–4 in the inherited numbering. The difficult sharpening clause, fruitful inhabitation, wall of fire/glory, gathering from four winds, pupil of his eye, plural they will dwell, holy land and holy clouds are retained without harmonization.

Pending: Review the sharpening clause at 2:4, fruitful inhabitation, after glory and the pupil-of-his-eye pronoun. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Ordinary address to Joshua and the slanderer uses you/your; reporting says is modernized. Slanderer and oppose, the robe reaching to the feet, plural command to clothe him, conditional clauses, men watching for portents, Rising, one stone with seven eyes, digging a pit and feeling out injustice are retained. No interpretive identification replaces these source expressions.

Pending: Review portents, Rising, the pit and feeling out injustice, including the relation between enacted sign and promise. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Questions and ordinary address to the prophet and mountain use modern forms. Has sent me expresses the completed sending at 4:9. The seven lamps and seven channels, two olive trees, great power/strength/My Spirit distinction, stone of inheritance and grace repetition, tin stone, difficult nozzles/channels and sons of richness remain provisional source-based wording.

Pending: Review stone of inheritance, equality of grace, tin stone, nozzles/channels and sons of richness. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Ordinary pronouns and verbs are modernized. Punished by this with death keeps the repeated judgment clauses; destroy it completely clarifies the house’s destruction with its timbers and stones. Flying sickle, twenty by ten cubits, measure, talent of lead, Lawlessness, her mouth, hoopoe wings, wind and Babylon remain distinct from other witnesses.

Pending: Review measure terminology, the lead stone/her mouth, hoopoe identification and the curse’s death formula. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Ordinary address uses you and verbs use says/has; has sent me preserves the completed commission. Bronze mountains, all horse colors and directions, four winds, wrath brought to rest, descriptive groups rather than imported personal names, plural crowns followed by singular crown, Rising/rise, the priest at his right hand and counsel between both remain. Josiah, Zephaniah and Josedek retain the site’s inherited names.

Pending: Review the descriptive groups, crown/crowns, Rising and the distinction between ruler and priest. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Ordinary address and verbs are modernized; refused to pay attention smooths the opening of 7:11. The date, names, holy offering report, distinct Lord of hosts, fifth/seventh observances, seventy years and fasted a fast remain. Mercy, compassion and the protected groups remain explicit; senseless back, heavy ears, My law/His Spirit and the concluding change from future to past remain.

Pending: Review the names and holy offering report, observance labels, senseless back and changes of speaker. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 8.** Ordinary address and reporting verbs are modernized; the temple clause at 8:9 is reconnected to its temporal phrase. The repeated jealousy, True City/Holy Mountain, old people and playing children, divine I will dwell at 8:8, unusual future verbs after before those days, peace and inheritance, strong hands, four named fasts, five cities into one, ten men, garment fringe and singular you/plural you all distinction remain.

Pending: Review the temporal construction at 8:9–10, emphatic impossibility question and number transitions. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 9.** Ordinary address to Zion/Judah uses you/your, attributive My own replaces Mine own, and mud of the roads and a pit with no water improve spoken flow. The 9:9 benchmark keeps righteous and saving, gentle, beast of burden and young colt, with your king comes to you. Sedrach, Damascus sacrifice, Emath, Akkaron/Azotus, inherited number shifts, the active second-person release at 9:11, prisoners of the gathering, Greeks’ children, holy stones and fragrant wine remain.

Pending: Review the sacrifice clause, pronouns and number shifts, animal expressions, holy stones and 9:17’s difficult wording. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 10.** The reporting verb says is modernized; the other successful English wording is retained. Early and late rain, appearances and winter rain, no healing, lambs, the difficult repeated from him clauses, Joseph/Judah distinction, sowing among peoples, not even one left behind, narrow sea, plural rivers and boasting in His name remain without explanatory substitutions.

Pending: Review appearances, no healing, lambs, the ambiguous subjects of 10:4 and the narrow sea. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 11.** Ordinary address and third-person forms are modernized. The fallen cedar and suffering images, sheep for slaughter, Canaan’s land and Canaanites, future/past shifts, Beauty and Measuring Cord, three shepherds in one month, thirty silver pieces and the smelting furnace/testing language remain. The inexperienced shepherd, ankle bones and plural address shifting to singular arm/eye remain.

Pending: Review Canaan/Canaanites, staff terminology, furnace testing, identities of shepherds and number changes. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 12.** Modern reporting and descriptive verbs and attributive My eyes replace obsolete forms. Shaking entranceways, trampled stone and mockery repetition, commanders of thousands, house of David/God’s house, spirit of grace and compassion, danced in mockery, the shift from toward Me to over him, pomegranate grove, and every repeated tribe/wives clause remain. Pierced is not substituted into the selected running text.

Pending: Review entranceways, mockery grammar and dance nuance, Me/him referents and pomegranate grove. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 13.** Ordinary address and prophesies/says are modernized; refine them with fire preserves the fire action in natural English. Every place opened, feet bound, the positive wearing of hairy skin, begot me, between your hands, beloved house, all three plural shepherds, fellow citizen, two parts/third and the final singular He/him remain. Later reception is kept separate.

Pending: Review opened place, binding feet, positive hairy-skin wording, begot me, beloved house, fellow citizen and plural shepherds. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.

**Chapter 14.** Ordinary address and verbs are modernized; will strike makes the future explicit at 14:12 and 14:18. Days, half/remnant distinctions, battle among the nations, blocked and joined ravine, Iasol/Oziah, holy ones with Him, no light/cold/ice, one day and evening light, summer and spring, inherited names, repeated downfall, these added to those, Tent-Pitching, horse’s bridle and no Canaanite remain.

Pending: Review the blocked ravine and place names, light/cold/ice, temporal expressions, downfall and these added to those. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud reviews remain pending.
