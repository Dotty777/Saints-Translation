# Ezra — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 10 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 280 verse units received an English editorial review: 48 changed and 232 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/EZR.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernized third-person verbs. Preserved the future promise in 1:3, take-him-up wording, Persian title, rare vessel terms and the itemized figures alongside the stated total.

Pending: 1:4 syntax, 1:8 gasbaren, 1:9–10 vessel terminology and numerical totals require full apparatus and specialist review.

**Chapter 2.** Retained every entry, family name, repeated sons-of formula and number. Selected running-text checks support keeping Bagoui/Bagoi, Eram/Erem and the three Nathin- forms distinct here. Preserved minas, methoesim and illuminating/perfect things.

Pending: Full name-list collation, Nebuchadnezzar/Nabouchodonosor consistency across chapters, 2:62 status wording and 2:69 units and kothonoi need specialist review.

**Chapter 3.** Modernized endures in the third-person praise. Kept it is good, prepared/prepared-place repetition, singular burnt offering, names, first-house founding phrase and the mingled song and weeping.

Pending: 3:3 terror clause, 3:9 grouping, 3:11 neuter good, 3:12 founding referent and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernized address to the king and officials and third-person verbs. Preserved the tax collector, slave-flight accusation, no-peace warning, horses and force, and Somoron/Samaria forms present in the consulted Greek.

Pending: 4:7 document syntax, 4:15 slave flights, 4:21–22 decree linkage and full critical comparison require specialist review.

**Chapter 5.** Modernized ordinary royal address and verb forms. Preserved prophesied a prophecy, city in 5:4, God’s eyes, the verse-crossing clause, treasury keeper and the absence of a new building command in 5:15.

Pending: 5:3,9 provision terminology, 5:5 dispatch to the tax collector, 5:14 office and full apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 6.** Modernized plural commands and third-person verbs. Kept the difficult now you shall give and negative instruction, timber penalty, according to me, Levites among builders and king of Assour.

Pending: 6:2 location, 6:3 elevation, 6:6–8 decree syntax, 6:11 punishment and full apparatus remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Modernized ordinary address and verbs; smoothed the supply instruction in 7:20 while keeping Ezra’s judgment, the house’s further need and the royal treasury houses. Preserved record-in-this-book, the enslavement ban, ancient units and discipline penalty. Address shifts: your God in 7:17–18 is plural; the final you in 7:25 is plural after singular address to Esdras.

Pending: 7:6 ready scribe, 7:12 formula, 7:14 third-person shift, 7:17 recording, 7:20 supply clause, 7:24–25 changes of number and complete apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 8.** Modernized runs and plural ordinary address. Retained names, Eui/Aoue distinction, silver of the place twice, singers, sachol, vessels as a count, rare inventory terms and hand/straight-way images.

Pending: 8:17–18 syntax and loanwords, 8:24 priest count, 8:27 inventory terms and complete name/number collation require specialist review.

**Chapter 9.** Kept Thee/Thou/Thy and gavest/hast/art for direct address to God. Used you for people addressed inside the quoted divine speech at 9:11–12. Modernized third-person verbs even within the prayer. Preserved the present-tense bend/stretch shift, sons, support/fence and mouth-to-mouth images, movement repetition, confession and plea.

Pending: 9:5 tense sequence, 9:8 support, 9:9 agency, 9:11 movement imagery, 9:13–14 broken syntax and full apparatus need specialist review.

**Chapter 10.** Modernized ordinary address and verbs. Smoothed 10:12 to This word of yours is a great thing for us to carry out. Preserved the repeated weeping, wives and children, frighten them, property ban, with me and the unusual and acted across 10:37–38.

Pending: 10:2 patient hope, 10:3 proposal and imperative, 10:12 force of great, 10:15–16 agency, 10:37–38 list/verb sequence and full apparatus/reception review remain pending.
