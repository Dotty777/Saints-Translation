# Ezekiel — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 48 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 1267 verse units received an English editorial review: 773 changed and 494 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/EZK.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 48 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.
