Ezekiel 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Vision by the River Chobar

1:1And it happened in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth of the month, while I was among the captives by the River Chebar: the heavens opened, and I saw visions of God.

1:2On the fifth of the month—this was the fifth year of King Jehoiakim’s captivity—

1:3the Lord’s word came to Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans by the River Chebar; and the Lord’s hand came upon me,

The Four Living Creatures

1:4and I looked, and behold, a sweeping wind was coming from the north, and a great cloud was within it, with brightness around it and fire flashing like lightning; within it was something like the appearance of electrum, within the fire, and brightness within it. [1]

1:5And within it was something like a likeness of four living creatures. This was their appearance: a human likeness was upon them,

1:6and each had four faces, and each had four wings.

1:7Their legs were straight, and their feet were winged, with sparks like flashing bronze; and their wings were light.

1:8A human hand was beneath their wings on their four sides; and the faces of all four of them

1:9did not turn as they walked; each went straight ahead.

1:10The likeness of their faces was a human face and a lion’s face on the right for all four, and a calf’s face on the left for all four, and an eagle’s face for all four.

1:11The wings of all four were stretched out above; each had two joined to one another, and two covered the top of their bodies.

1:12Each went straight ahead. Wherever the spirit was going, they went and did not turn.

1:13And amid the living creatures was an appearance like burning coals of fire, like the sight of torches whirling among the living creatures, and brightness of the fire; lightning came out of the fire.

The Wheels Beside the Living Creatures

1:15I looked, and behold, one wheel was on the earth adjoining the four living creatures;

1:16the appearance of the wheels was like the appearance of tharsis, and all four had one likeness; their workmanship was as though a wheel were within a wheel.

1:17They went toward their four sides; they did not turn as they went,

1:18nor did their backs; they were tall, and I saw them. The backs of all four were full of eyes all around.

1:19When the living creatures went, the wheels moved beside them; when the living creatures rose from the earth, the wheels rose.

1:20Wherever the cloud was, there was the spirit for movement; the living creatures and the wheels went and rose together with them, because a spirit of life was in the wheels.

1:21When they went, these went; when they stood, these stood; when they rose from the earth, these rose together with them, because a spirit of life was in the wheels.

The Firmament and the Throne

1:22Above the living creatures’ heads was a likeness as of a firmament, like the appearance of crystal, stretched out above their wings;

1:23and beneath the firmament their wings were stretched out, fluttering one toward another; each had two joined together, covering their bodies.

1:24As they went, I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of much water; when they stood, their wings came to rest.

1:25And behold, a voice came from above the firmament that was over their heads.

1:26Like the appearance of a sapphire stone was the likeness of a throne upon it; and above the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the form of a human being.

1:27I saw something like the sight of electrum from the appearance of a waist upward; and from the appearance of a waist down below I saw something like the appearance of fire, with its brightness all around.

1:28Like the appearance of a bow when it is in the cloud on a rainy day, so was the arrangement of the brightness all around. This was the appearance of the likeness of the Lord’s glory. I saw it, and I fall upon my face, and I heard the voice of One speaking.

Source and wording notes

Ezekiel 1:4 · Meaning and wording · electrum

A radiant material term in the vision, traditionally also rendered amber. Retain electrum with the historical amber comparison and the uncertainty of the material identification. Keep like the appearance of; the vision does not provide a chemical assay.

Historical comparison. Saints uses electrum; KJV, Brenton, and DRC use amber. The Greek noun can carry different material associations, so the lexicon must not impose a precise alloy ratio.

Son of Man, Stand on Your Feet

2:1He said to me, “Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak to you.” [1]

2:2A spirit came upon me, took me up, raised me, and set me on my feet; I heard Him speaking to me,

2:3and He said to me, “Son of man, I am sending you out to the house of Israel, those who embitter Me, who have embittered Me, they and their fathers, until this present day,

2:4and you will say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord.’

2:5If indeed they listen or are terrified—for it is an embittering house—they will know that you are a prophet among them.

2:6And you, son of man, do not fear them or be shaken before their faces, because they will rage and gather against you all around, and you dwell among scorpions. Do not fear their words or be shaken before their faces, for it is an embittering house.

2:7You will speak My words to them, if indeed they listen or are terrified, for it is an embittering house.

2:8And you, son of man, hear the One speaking to you. Do not become embittering like the embittering house. Open your mouth wide and eat what I give you.”

The Scroll Written on Both Sides

2:9I looked, and behold, a hand was stretched toward me, and in it was a scroll of a book;

2:10and He unrolled it before me. It had writing on the back and the front, and upon it were written a lament, a song and woe.

Ezekiel 3

Read this chapter on staging

Eat This Scroll

3:1He said to me, “Son of man, eat this scroll, then go and speak to the sons of Israel.”

3:2I opened my mouth wide, and He fed me the scroll.

3:3He said to me, “Son of man, your mouth will eat, and your belly will be filled with this scroll that has been given to you.” I ate it, and in my mouth it became like sweet honey.

Go to the House of Israel

3:4He said to me, “Son of man, go, enter the house of Israel, and speak My words to them;

3:5for you are not sent out to a people of deep speech and heavy tongue, but to the house of Israel,

3:6nor to many peoples of foreign speech or foreign tongues, nor those strong in tongue whose words you will not understand. If I had sent you to such people, they would have listened to you.

3:7But the house of Israel certainly will not be willing to listen to you, because they do not wish to listen to Me; the entire house of Israel is contentious and hard-hearted.

3:8Behold, I have made your face strong against their faces, and I will strengthen your contention against their contention,

3:9and it will always be stronger than rock. Do not fear them or be terrified before their faces, for it is an embittering house.”

3:10He said to me, “Son of man, take into your heart all the words that I have spoken with you, and hear with your ears,

3:11and go, enter the captivity, to the sons of your people; you will speak to them and say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord,’ if indeed they listen, if indeed they yield.”

The Spirit Takes Him to the Captivity

3:12A spirit took me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great shaking: “Blessed be the Lord’s glory from His place!”

3:13I saw the sound of the living creatures’ wings fluttering one toward another, and the sound of the wheels adjoining them, and the sound of the shaking.

3:14The spirit raised me and took me up. I went, impelled by my spirit, and the Lord’s hand came strongly upon me.

3:15I entered the captivity aloft and went around those dwelling by the River Chebar, those who were there; I sat there seven days, living among them.

A Watchman for the House of Israel

3:16After the seven days, the Lord’s word came to me, saying,

3:17“Son of man, I have appointed you a watchman for the house of Israel. You will hear a word from My mouth and convey My warning to them. [1]

3:18When I say to the lawless person, ‘You will surely be put to death,’ and you do not warn him or speak to warn the lawless person to turn from his ways so that he may live, that lawless person will die in his injustice; but I will require his blood from your hand.

3:19But if you warn the lawless person and he does not turn from his lawlessness and his way, that lawless person will die in his injustice, while you will rescue your life.

3:20When a righteous person turns from his righteous deeds and commits a transgression, and I place torment before his face, he will die because you did not warn him. He will die in his sins, because the righteous deeds he did certainly will not be remembered, and I will require his blood from your hand.

3:21But if you warn the righteous person not to sin, and he does not sin, the righteous person will surely live because you warned him; and you will rescue your own life.”

Bound and Made Mute

3:22The Lord’s hand came upon me, and He said to me, “Rise and go out to the plain, and you will be spoken to there.”

3:23I rose and went out to the plain, and behold, the Lord’s glory stood there, like the vision and like the glory I had seen by the River Chebar; and I fall upon my face.

3:24A spirit came upon me and set me on my feet. He spoke to me and said to me, “Go, be shut inside your house.

3:25And you, son of man, behold, bonds have been placed upon you, and they will bind you with them; you certainly will not go out from among them.

3:26I will bind your tongue, and you will become mute; you will not be a man rebuking them, for it is an embittering house.

3:27But when I speak to you, I will open your mouth, and you will say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord: Let the one who hears hear, and let the one who disobeys disobey,’ for it is an embittering house.”

Ezekiel 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Brick and the Siege of Jerusalem

4:1“And you, son of man, take a brick, set it before your face, and draw upon it a city—Jerusalem;

4:2lay siege to it, build forward defenses against it, throw up a palisade against it, set camps against it, and arrange missile positions all around.

4:3Take an iron pan and place it as an iron wall between you and the city. Prepare your face against it; it will be besieged, and you will besiege it. This is a sign for the sons of Israel.

Lying on the Left Side and the Right

4:4Lie on your left side and place the injustices of the house of Israel upon it, according to the number of days—one hundred fifty—that you lie upon it; bear their injustices.

4:5I have assigned you their two injustices as a number of days, one hundred ninety days; bear the injustices of the house of Israel

4:6and complete all these things. Lie on your right side and bear the injustices of the house of Judah forty days. I have appointed you a day for a year.

4:7Prepare your face against Jerusalem’s siege, strengthen your arm, and prophesy against it.

4:8Behold, I have placed bonds upon you; do not turn from side to side until the days of your confinement are completed.

The Bread of Siege

4:9Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet and spelt; put them in one earthen vessel and make loaves from them for yourself. According to the number of days you sleep on your side, you will eat them one hundred ninety days.

4:10The food you are to eat will be weighed out: twenty shekels a day; eat it from time to time.

4:11Drink water by measure, the sixth part of a hin; drink it from time to time.

4:12Eat it as a barley cake baked in embers; bake it in pellets of human dung before their eyes,

4:13and say, ‘Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: So the sons of Israel will eat unclean things among the nations.’”

4:14I said, “By no means, Lord, God of Israel! Behold, my life has not been defiled with uncleanness. From my birth until now I have eaten neither what died of itself nor what a wild animal killed, nor has any stale meat entered my mouth.”

4:15He said to me, “Behold, I have given you cattle dung instead of human dung; prepare your loaves over it.”

I Am Breaking the Support of Bread

4:16He said to me, “Son of man, behold, I am breaking the support of bread in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight and in need, and drink water by measure and in devastation,

4:17so that they become needy for bread and water. A man and his brother will disappear, and they will waste away in their injustices.

Ezekiel 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Sword and the Hair Divided

5:1“And you, son of man, take a sword sharper than a barber’s razor. Obtain it for yourself and pass it over your head and your beard. Take a balance with weights and divide the hairs;

5:2burn a quarter with fire inside the city when the days of siege are complete. Take a quarter and burn it up in its midst; cut down a quarter with the sword around it, and scatter a quarter to the wind. I will draw a sword after them.

5:3Take a few of them in number and gather them into your cloak.

5:4Take some of these again, cast them into the fire, and burn them in the fire. From it fire will go out. And you will say to the entire house of Israel,

This Is Jerusalem

5:5‘Thus says the Lord: This is Jerusalem. I have placed her amid the nations, and the lands around her.

5:6And you will speak My ordinances to the lawless one among the nations, and My laws among the lands around her; for they rejected My ordinances and did not walk in My laws.

5:7Therefore thus says the Lord: Because your impulse comes from the nations around you, and you have not walked in My laws or performed My ordinances—nor have you even acted according to the ordinances of the nations around you—

5:8therefore thus says the Lord: Behold, I am against you, and I will execute judgment in your midst before the nations,

5:9and I will do among you what I have not done and the like of which I will not do again, according to all your abominations.

Fathers Will Eat Children

5:10Therefore fathers will eat children in your midst, and children will eat fathers. I will execute judgments against you and scatter all your survivors to every wind.

5:11Therefore, as I live, says the Lord, surely, because you defiled My holy places with all your abominations, I too will reject you. My eye will not spare, and I too will have no mercy.

5:12A quarter of you will be consumed by death; a quarter of you will perish by famine within you; a quarter of you I will scatter to every wind; and a quarter of you will fall by the sword around you, and I will draw a sword after them.

5:13My wrath and My anger against them will be completed, and you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken in My jealousy when I complete My anger against them.

5:14I will make you a wilderness, and your daughters around you, before every passerby;

5:15you will be mourned and ravaged among the nations around you when I execute judgments against you in the vengeance of My wrath. I, the Lord, have spoken.

The Arrows of Famine

5:16When I send My arrows of famine against them, they will bring exhaustion, and I will break your support of bread.

5:17I will send famine and harmful wild animals against you, and I will punish you. Death and blood will pass over you, and I will bring a sword upon you all around. I, the Lord, have spoken.’”

Against the Mountains of Israel

6:1The Lord’s word came to me, saying,

6:2“Son of man, set your face firmly against the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them,

6:3and say, ‘Mountains of Israel, hear the Lord’s word. Thus says the Lord to the mountains, hills, ravines and wooded valleys: Behold, I am bringing a sword against you, and your high places will be destroyed,

6:4and your altars and your sacred precincts will be shattered; I will cast down your wounded before your idols,

6:5and I will scatter your bones around your altars.

6:6In all your dwelling territory the cities will be made desolate and the high places will disappear, so that your altars are destroyed, your idols shattered, and your sacred precincts removed;

The Survivors Will Remember Me

Clap Your Hand, Stamp Your Foot

6:11Thus says the Lord: Clap your hand, stamp your foot, and say, “Aha! Aha!” over all the abominations of the house of Israel; they will fall by sword, death and famine.

6:12The one near will fall by the sword, the one far away will die by death, and the one enclosed will perish by famine; I will complete My anger against them.

6:13You will know that I am the Lord when your wounded lie among your idols, around your altars, on every high hill and beneath a shady tree, where they offered a fragrant aroma to all their idols.

6:14I will stretch out My hand against them and reduce the land to ruin and destruction, from the wilderness of Deblatha, from every dwelling place; you will recognize that I am the Lord.’”

Ezekiel 7

Read this chapter on staging

The End Has Come upon the Land

7:1The Lord’s word came to me, saying,

7:2“And you, son of man, say, ‘Thus says the Lord to the land of Israel: An end has come; the end has come upon the four wings of the land.

7:3The end has come

7:4upon you, inhabitant of the land. The time has come; the day has drawn near, neither with tumult nor with birth pangs.

7:5Now soon I will pour My anger upon you and complete My wrath in you; I will judge you in your ways and bring all your abominations upon you.

7:6My eye will not spare, nor will I have mercy; I will bring your ways upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst. You will recognize that I am the Lord who strikes.

7:7Now the end is upon you. I Myself will send against you and exact retribution upon you in your ways; I will bring all your abominations upon you.

7:8My eye will not spare you, nor will I have mercy; I will bring your way upon you, and your abominations will be in your midst. You will recognize that I am the Lord.

7:9For thus says the Lord:

Behold, the Lord's Day

7:10Behold, the end has come; behold, the Lord’s day! Even if the rod has blossomed, insolence has risen.

7:11It will shatter the support of a lawless person, neither with tumult nor with haste.

7:12The time has come; behold, the day! Let the buyer not rejoice, nor the seller lament;

7:13for the buyer certainly will no longer return to the seller, and a person will not prevail in the eye of his life.

7:14Sound the trumpet and judge everything.

War Outside, Famine and Death Inside

7:15War by the sword is outside, and famine and death inside. The one in the plain will die by the sword, while famine and death will consume those in the city.

7:16Their survivors will be saved and be on the mountains; I will kill them all, each in his injustices.

7:17All hands will weaken, and all thighs will be defiled with moisture,

7:18and they will gird on sackcloth; amazement will cover them, shame will be upon every face among them, and baldness upon every head.

Their Silver Thrown into the Streets

7:19Their silver will be thrown into the broad streets, and their gold will be disregarded. Their lives certainly will not be satisfied, nor their bellies filled, because it became torment for their injustices.

7:20They made choice ornaments a source of arrogance and made images of their abominations from them. Therefore I have given them over to them as uncleanness.

7:21I will hand them into foreigners’ hands to plunder them, and to the pestilent people of the earth as spoils; they will profane them.

7:22I will turn My face away from them, and they will defile My oversight; they will enter them unguarded and profane them,

7:23and they will make a confused mixture, because the land is full of peoples and the city full of lawlessness.

7:24I will turn back the arrogance of their strength, and their holy places will be defiled.

7:25Propitiation will come and will seek peace, and there will be none.
