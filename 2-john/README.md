## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Ordinary human address is you to the lady, with you all marking the plural audience in 1:6 and 1:12. Modern verbs and to replace the older register. Chosen lady/children and chosen sister are retained without deciding personal versus ecclesial identity. Truth/love, commandment/walking and abiding repetitions remain. Coming in flesh in 1:7 is not converted to a completed participle. You must not lose what we have worked for (1:8) keeps the pronoun distinction. Our joy in 1:12 follows the consulted NA28; no final Amen is added.

Pending: Review the identity of the lady/sister, the teaching-of-Christ genitive and the practical scope of reception/greeting in 1:10–11. Face to face renders the literal mouth to mouth idiom in 1:12; the literal expression is preserved in notes.
