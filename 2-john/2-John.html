2 John 1

2 John 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone, but also all who have come to know the truth—

1:2because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:

1:3Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

Walking in the Truth

1:4I rejoiced greatly that I have found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a commandment from the Father.

1:5And now I ask you, lady—not as though writing you a new commandment, but the one we had from the beginning—that we love one another.

1:6And this is love: that we walk according to His commandments. This is the commandment, just as you all heard from the beginning: that you walk in it.

Deceivers and the Teaching of Christ

1:7For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.

1:8Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we have worked for, but receive a full reward.

1:9Everyone who goes ahead and does not abide in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever abides in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.

1:10If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into a house, and do not offer him a greeting,

1:11for whoever offers him a greeting shares in his evil works.

Final Greetings

1:12Although I have many things to write to you all, I did not wish to do so with paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that our joy may be made full.

1:13The children of your chosen sister greet you.

2 John 1:1 · elder

Meaning. The writer's self-designation and addressed household or community.

Decision. Keep elder, chosen lady, and children. A personal or ecclesial identity for the lady belongs in a note; do not substitute a reconstructed name or church.

Comparison. Saints and KJV elder; DRC Ancient. Chosen or elect translates the adjective without identifying the addressee.

Excluded. a disputed identity embedded as a proper name

2 John 1:5 · one another

Meaning. Reciprocal love in the command recalled from the beginning.

Decision. Keep singular thee to the lady and plural we in the love clause. Preserve the denial that this is a newly written command.

Comparison. All three retain reciprocal love. The switch in grammatical number should remain audible.

Excluded. singular address spread across the plural community clause

2 John 1:7 · antichrist

Meaning. A designation for the described denial concerning Jesus Christ.

Decision. Keep the present participle coming in flesh and the explicit failure to confess. The designation here must not automatically be turned into a named future figure.

Comparison. Saints coming in flesh; KJV and DRC is come. DRC seducer is historical deceiver language, not necessarily sexual.

Excluded. a contemporary person identified in the translation

2 John 1:9 · go ahead

Meaning. Going ahead characterized by failing to remain in the teaching.

Decision. Retain both linked actions so the warning does not condemn every kind of progress. Keep Father and Son as distinct named referents.

Comparison. Saints goes ahead; KJV transgresseth; DRC revolteth. The proposal follows the consulted proagon.

Excluded. all learning or development condemned by the verb alone

2 John 1:12 · face to face

Meaning. Direct personal speech, literally mouth to mouth.

Decision. Use the intelligible idiom face to face and record the literal expression. Correct inherited your joy to our joy for the consulted NA28 pronoun.

Comparison. Saints and DRC your joy; KJV our joy. All make the direct-speaking intention clear.

Excluded. our and your treated as interchangeable style choices

Current Bespoke editorial notes

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Ordinary human address is you to the lady, with you all marking the plural audience in 1:6 and 1:12. Modern verbs and to replace the older register. Chosen lady/children and chosen sister are retained without deciding personal versus ecclesial identity. Truth/love, commandment/walking and abiding repetitions remain. Coming in flesh in 1:7 is not converted to a completed participle. You must not lose what we have worked for (1:8) keeps the pronoun distinction. Our joy in 1:12 follows the consulted NA28; no final Amen is added.
