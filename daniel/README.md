# Daniel — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 12 numbered chapters, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 487 reading units (486 numbered verse units and the chapter-five preface) received an English editorial review: 154 changed and 333 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Old Greek Daniel in Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/DAG.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 14 reading sections, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Modernize ordinary address and the giving request at 1:12–13; legumes makes the food sense plain while retaining earth, ten days and water. Preserve Abiesdri as chief eunuch and the direct agent of the trial, the royal names, the three years, and the expanded education and appointment account at 1:20. Historical notes using pulse remain preserved.

Pending: Review the food term, royal-education vocabulary, individual names and the chief-eunuch agency. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 2.** Distinguish ordinary royal address from the direct prayer to God at 2:23, where Thee/Thou hast remain. Smooth Arioch’s introduction and the image’s appearance, preserving chief cook, the angel in 2:11, the difficult dwelling clause in 2:22, the stone that struck the whole earth in 2:35, iron cutting every tree, and the separate kingdom wording in 2:44. Keep the expanded list including fish of the sea and the source’s repeated last-days phrases.

Pending: Review dwelling at 2:22, the singular angel, the unusual negative kingdom clause at 2:44, gazarenes and the mixed plural/singular address at 2:47. Historical comparison-layer misattachments remain unchanged and require separate review. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 3.** Modernize ordinary royal address and third-person verbs while retaining Thou/Thee/Thy and matching verbs throughout direct prayer to God. Retain the full prayer and song, every repeated blessing, the eighteenth year, India-to-Ethiopia scope, measurements and instruments, repeated sevenfold heat, hand-and-foot binding and the forty-nine-cubit flame. Preserve the confident reply at 3:18 and angel of God at 3:92. The difficult repeated behind Thee clauses at 3:40 remain unresolved rather than replaced from another textual form.

Pending: Prioritize the syntax and textual history of 3:40, instrument names, the unfinished conditional at 3:15, the sequence of standing/rising at 3:91 and the preserved angel comparison. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 4.** Modernize address to the king and third-person verbs, repair is thou at 4:20, and distinguish the quotation boundaries as the account shifts between first-person report and third-person narration. Preserve the repeated tree appearance, thirty-stadia branches, sun and moon, the shift from tree to his body, seven years, custody and bronze bonds, the Temple-destruction statement, redeem/almsgiving, the replacement ruler and all lettered units. Render the present are rushing at 4:24 rather than silently treating it as future.

Pending: Review the extended Old Greek account, agency and tense in 4:14–17 and 4:24, the meaning of completed sins/ignorances at 4:34, time is full at 4:27 and the difficult tree-to-person shifts. The study page’s misplaced preface comparison remains historical. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 5.** Modernize ordinary royal address and verbs, preserve the unnumbered preface as a distinct anchored reading unit, and close Daniel’s speech after the combined 5:26–28 unit. Retain the two thousand guests in the preface, its Mane/Phares/Thekel sequence distinct from 5:17, the queen’s summons, authority over a third part, the grouped labels and gaps, and the ending that reports transfer of kingship without adding the king’s death.

Pending: Review the preface’s final interpretation term and the agent of clothing in 5:7. At 5:23 the address is chiefly singular to the king but the drinking, praising idols and failure to bless God involve his company; modern you does not display every number change. Historical ye notes remain preserved. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 6.** Repair did thou and Before thou, modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs, and smooth the sealed-pit and assembled-forces clauses. Preserve Artaxerxes/Darius in the opening, 127 satraps, two accusers, repeated three daily prayers and the thirty-day restriction, Daniel’s direct rebuke to the king, divine care closing the lions’ mouths, the deaths of the accusers with their families and the final succession notice.

Pending: Review the royal-name and chronology issues without harmonization, the unusual wording of the binding restriction, and Daniel’s righteousness/ignorant-fault distinction. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 7.** Retain the already natural wording through most of this chapter and smooth its closing time phrase. Preserve the lioness, a tongue given to the leopard, plans in the horns, three horns withering, watching the sound, and the source’s as an Ancient of Days at 7:13. Keep the unusual worship list, four kingdoms that will perish, the time/times/half-time period and the past gave at 7:27. No familiar-version substitutions or doctrinal glosses were added.

Pending: Prioritize the pronoun/figure relationship at 7:13–14, the worship-list syntax, the ambiguous subject in 7:5, the judgment/defiling clause at 7:26 and historical-present handling at 7:16. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 8.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs and smooth the human appearance and shameless-face descriptions. Preserve all four directions at 8:4, the horn being cast down and trampled by the stars, the chief commander rescuing captivity, ancient mountains, the mixed past/future sequence at 8:11, the 2,300 days, Ailam/Ulai/Phelmouni names, four shattered horns at 8:22 and the unresolved gathering of a hand clause at 8:25.

Pending: Prioritize 8:10–13 agency and syntax, the source’s unusual four-horn description at 8:22, the command at 8:16 and gathering of a hand and sell at 8:25. These are retained provisional readings, not claimed final interpretations. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 9.** Keep direct prayer in Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine with matching verbs, while using modern third-person belongs/has and ordinary you in Gabriel’s address. Preserve the prayer’s shifts between addressing God and speaking of Him, singular servant and plural servants, the city and people names, seventy weeks, gladdening a holy of holies, and the exact differing number formulas at 9:26–27. No chronology or familiar anointing formula was supplied.

Pending: Review the command-to-the-land clause at 9:2, commands to be answered at 9:25, the number sequences and referents at 9:26–27, the anointing/anointed distinction, and the scope of the covenant clauses. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 10.** Modernize ordinary address to Daniel and third-person has, smooth the linen around the waist, and clarify speech boundaries at 10:13 and 10:21. Preserve the first year of Cyrus, three weeks/twenty-one days, the repeated linen, light from the middle, the negative I did not hear, the hand and bodily images, worthy of pity, Michael left with the Persian general and the first things in a record of truth.

Pending: Review the strong-multitude clause at 10:1, spirit turning for destruction at 10:8, knees/soles at 10:10, the side/vision clause at 10:16, referents and speech boundaries across chapters 10–12. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 11.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs, open the renewed speech at 11:2, and clarify the carried-over clause and so will his son at 11:10 without moving its verse boundary. Preserve Egypt/north designations, all repeated roots, plant and arm images, the Romans at 11:30, my country at 11:41, and the clauses continuing across 11:28–29 and 11:38–39. No historical identities are inserted into the Scripture.

Pending: Review the many ambiguous agents and pronouns, son clause at 11:9–10, daughter of a human at 11:17, allotment language, defiling/harsh-people syntax at 11:32 and the foreign-god clause at 11:38–39. Independent Greek, Göttingen/apparatus and human read-aloud review pending.

**Chapter 12.** Modernize ordinary address and third-person verbs, while retaining the measured shall arise/unto cadence at 12:2 and opening the continued speech at 12:1. Preserve Michael passing by, exaltation of those inscribed, breadth of the earth, three outcome clauses including dispersion, strengthening my words, the many going mad, both hands raised, the difficult hands of release phrase, 1,290 and 1,335 days, and rising unto glory rather than substituting lot.

Pending: Review the scope of the resurrection clauses at 12:2, hands of release at 12:7, the figures/time wording at 12:8, and the durations and glory promise.
