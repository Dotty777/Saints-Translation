Daniel 3

Read this chapter on staging

The Golden Image on the Plain

3:1In the eighteenth year, King Nebuchadnezzar, who administered cities and countries and all who inhabited the earth from India to Ethiopia, made a golden image, sixty cubits high and six cubits wide. He set it up on the plain of the enclosure in the country of Babylonia.

3:2Nebuchadnezzar, king of kings and lord of the whole inhabited world, sent to gather all the nations, tribes, and languages, satraps, generals, district governors, consuls, administrators, those holding authority in each country, and everyone throughout the inhabited world, to come to the dedication of the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

3:3Those previously listed stood opposite the image.

3:4The herald proclaimed to the crowds, “You are commanded, nations and countries, peoples and languages:

3:5when you hear the sound of trumpet, panpipe, kithara, sambuca, psaltery, symphonia, and every kind of musical instrument, fall down and bow to the golden image that King Nebuchadnezzar has set up.

3:6They will throw into the blazing furnace anyone who does not fall down and bow.”

3:7At that time, when all the nations heard the sound of the trumpet and every musical sound, all the nations, tribes, and languages fell down and bowed to the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up before him.

The Chaldeans Accuse the Jews

3:8At that time Chaldean men came forward and accused the Jews.

3:9They spoke and said, “My lord king, live for ever!

3:10You, O king, commanded and decreed that everyone who hears the trumpet and every musical sound must fall down and bow to the golden image.

3:11Whoever does not fall down and bow will be thrown into the blazing furnace.

3:12There are certain Jewish men whom you appointed over the country of Babylonia—Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago. Those men have not feared your command; they have not served your idol or bowed to your golden image that you set up.”

We Neither Serve Your Idol nor Bow

3:13Then Nebuchadnezzar, angered with wrath, ordered Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago brought in. The men were brought to the king.

3:14When King Nebuchadnezzar saw them, he said to them, “Why, Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, do you not serve my gods or bow to the golden image I set up?

3:15Now, if you are ready, as soon as you hear the trumpet and every musical sound, to fall down and bow to the golden image I set up—but if not, know that unless you bow, you will immediately be thrown into the blazing furnace. What god will rescue you from my hands?”

3:16Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago answered King Nebuchadnezzar, “O king, we have no need to answer you concerning this command.

3:17For there is a God in the heavens, our one Lord, whom we fear, who is able to rescue us from the furnace of fire; and from your hands, O king, He will rescue us.

3:18Then it will be plain to you that we neither serve your idol nor bow to your golden image that you set up.”

The Furnace Heated Sevenfold

3:19Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with fury, and the appearance of his face changed. He ordered the furnace heated seven times beyond what it needed to be heated.

3:20He ordered the strongest men in his force to bind Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago hand and foot and throw them into the blazing furnace.

3:21Then those men were bound hand and foot, wearing their footwear and the tiaras on their heads, together with their clothing, and were thrown into the furnace.

3:22Because the king’s command was urgent and the furnace had been heated seven times beyond its earlier heat, the appointed men bound them hand and foot, brought them to the furnace, and threw them into it.

3:23The flame came out of the furnace and burned and killed the men who had bound Azariah and his companions; but they themselves were preserved.

The Prayer of Azariah

3:24Thus Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael prayed and sang to the Lord when the king ordered them thrown into the furnace.

3:25Azariah stood and prayed in this manner. Opening his mouth, he gave thanks to the Lord together with his companions amid the fire, while the Chaldeans were fiercely stoking the furnace. They said: [1]

3:26“Blessed art Thou, Lord, God of our fathers; Thy name is praised and glorified for ever.

3:27For Thou art righteous in everything Thou hast done to us; all Thy works are true, Thy ways upright, and all Thy judgments true.

3:28Thou hast carried out judgments of truth in everything Thou didst bring upon us and upon Thy holy city, our fathers’ Jerusalem. For in truth and judgment Thou didst all these things because of our sins.

3:29For we have sinned in everything and acted lawlessly by departing from Thee. We have sinned in everything and have not obeyed the commands of Thy law.

3:30We have neither kept nor done what Thou didst command us, so that it might go well with us.

3:31And now, everything Thou hast brought upon us and everything Thou hast done to us, Thou hast done in true judgment.

3:32Thou didst hand us over to our enemies, lawless and most hateful apostates, and to an unjust king, the most wicked in all the earth.

3:33Now we cannot open our mouths; shame and reproach have come upon Thy servants and those who revere Thee.

3:34Do not hand us over utterly, for Thy name’s sake; do not dissolve Thy covenant.

3:35Do not remove Thy mercy from us, for the sake of Abraham, beloved by Thee, and Isaac Thy servant, and Israel Thy holy one,

3:36as Thou didst speak to them, saying that Thou wouldst multiply their descendants like the stars of heaven and the sand along the shore of the sea.

3:37For, Master, we have been diminished beyond all nations, and today we are lowly throughout the earth because of our sins.

3:38At this time there is no ruler or prophet or leader, no burnt offering or sacrifice or offering or incense, no place to offer produce before Thee and find mercy.

3:39But with a crushed soul and humbled spirit may we be accepted, as with burnt offerings of rams and bulls, and as with tens of thousands of fat lambs.

3:40So may our sacrifice be before Thee today; and be propitious—“behind Thee”; for there is no shame for those who trust in Thee; and bring to completion—“behind Thee.”

3:41Now we follow with our whole heart; we fear Thee and seek Thy face. Do not put us to shame,

3:42but deal with us according to Thy gentleness and the abundance of Thy mercy.

3:43Rescue us according to Thy wondrous deeds, and give glory to Thy name, Lord.

3:44May all who inflict evil on Thy servants be ashamed; may they be humiliated out of all their power, and may their strength be crushed.

3:45Let them know that Thou alone art Lord, God, glorious over the whole inhabited world.”

The Angel of the Lord in the Furnace

3:46The king’s servants who threw them in did not stop heating the furnace. When they threw the three together into the furnace, the furnace was sevenfold ablaze in its heat. When they threw them in, those casting them were above them, while others stoked beneath them with naphtha, tow, pitch, and vine brushwood.

3:47The flame spread above the furnace for forty-nine cubits.

3:48It spread out and burned those Chaldeans whom it found around the furnace.

3:49An angel of the Lord descended into the furnace together with Azariah and his companions and shook the flame of fire out of the furnace.

3:50He made the middle of the furnace like a dew-laden breeze whistling through. The fire did not touch them at all; it neither distressed nor troubled them.

The Song of the Three Young Men

3:51Then the three took up the song as with one mouth, singing hymns, glorifying, blessing, and exalting God in the furnace, saying:

3:52“Blessed art Thou, Lord, God of our fathers, praised and highly exalted for ever. Blessed is the holy name of Thy glory, exceedingly praised and highly exalted through all ages.

3:53Blessed art Thou in the temple of Thy holy glory, exceedingly praised in hymns and exceedingly glorious for ever.

3:54Blessed art Thou upon the throne of Thy kingdom, praised in hymns and highly exalted for ever.

3:55Blessed art Thou who lookest upon the depths, seated upon cherubim, praised and glorified for ever.

3:56Blessed art Thou in the firmament, praised in hymns and glorified for ever.

All Works of the Lord, Bless the Lord

3:57All works of the Lord, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:58Angels of the Lord, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:59Heavens, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:60All waters above heaven, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:61All powers of the Lord, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:62Sun and moon, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:63Stars of heaven, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:64All rain and dew, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:65All winds, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:66Fire and heat, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:67Chill and cold, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:68Dews and snowfalls, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:69Ice and cold, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:70Hoarfrost and snow, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:71Nights and days, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:72Light and darkness, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:73Lightning and clouds, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:74Let the earth bless the Lord; let her sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:75Mountains and hills, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:76All things growing upon the earth, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:77Springs, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:78Seas and rivers, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:79Sea monsters and all things moving in the waters, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:80All birds of heaven, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:81Four-footed creatures and wild animals of the earth, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:82Sons of humankind, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:83Israel, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:84Priests, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:85Servants, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:86Spirits and souls of the righteous, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:87Holy ones and those humble in heart, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever.

3:88Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael, bless the Lord; sing praise to Him and exalt Him highly for ever. For He rescued us from Hades and saved us from the hand of death; He delivered us from the midst of the burning flame and redeemed us from the fire.

3:89Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good, for His mercy endures for ever.

3:90Bless, all who revere the God of gods; sing praise and give thanks, for His mercy endures for ever and for the age of ages.”

I See Four Men Unbound

3:91When the king heard them singing and, standing, observed them alive, King Nebuchadnezzar was astonished, rose in haste, and said to his friends:

3:92“Behold! I see four men unbound, walking in the fire, and no harm has come upon them. The fourth has the likeness of an angel of God.” [1]

3:93The king approached the door of the furnace blazing with fire and called them by name: “Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, servants of the Most High God of gods, come out of the fire!” Thus the men came out from the midst of the fire.

3:94The consuls, district governors, ancestral chiefs, and the king’s friends gathered. They observed those men: the fire had not touched their bodies, their hair had not burned, their trousers were unchanged, and there was no smell of fire on them.

Blessed Be the God of Sedrach, Misach and Abdenago

3:95King Nebuchadnezzar spoke and said, “Blessed be the Lord, the God of Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, who sent His angel and saved His servants who hoped in Him. They rejected the king’s command and surrendered their bodies to burning so that they would neither serve nor bow to another god except their own God.

3:96Now I decree: from every nation, every tribe, and every language, whoever blasphemes the Lord, the God of Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, will be dismembered and his house confiscated, because there is no other god who can rescue like this.”

3:97Thus the king gave Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago authority over the whole country and appointed them rulers.

Source and wording notes

Daniel 3:25 · Meaning and wording · give thanks

Praise or thankful acknowledgment within Azariah's prayer. Retain the acknowledgment, his companions, and the fierce stoking of the furnace in the Old Greek frame. Keep the prayer introduction separate from the prayer's next verse.

Historical comparison. Greek and Saints Daniel 3:25 are fuller than Brenton and DRC 3:25. KJV's corresponding prayer introduction is Prayer of Azariah 1:2 in its Apocrypha, not KJV Daniel 3:25.

Daniel 3:92 · Meaning and wording · angel of God

The fourth figure's appearance in the king's statement. Preserve angel of God and the comparison of appearance. Later identification of the figure belongs in reception notes; it must not displace the declared Old Greek reading.

Historical comparison. Saints follows Old Greek Daniel 3:92. Brenton and DRC at 3:92 and KJV at 3:25 have Son or son of God. This is a textual difference, not merely capitalization.
