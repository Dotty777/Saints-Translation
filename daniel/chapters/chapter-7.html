The Vision of the Four Beasts

7:1In the first year of Baltasar’s reign over the country of Babylonia, Daniel saw a vision at his head upon his bed. Then Daniel wrote the vision he had seen in principal points:

7:2“Upon my bed I was watching in dreams by night, and there were four winds of heaven falling upon the great sea.

7:3Four beasts came up from the sea, each differing from the other.

7:4The first was like a lioness with wings like an eagle’s. I watched until her wings were plucked out, she was lifted from the earth and stood on human feet, and a human heart was given her.

7:5After her there was another beast with the likeness of a bear. It stood on one side and had three ribs in its mouth. He spoke in this way: ‘Rise, devour much flesh!’

7:6After this I watched another beast, like a leopard. Four wings stretched over it, and the beast had four heads; a tongue was given it.

7:7After this I watched in the night vision a fourth beast, fearsome, its terror surpassing in strength. It had great iron teeth, devoured and pounded, trampled all around with its feet, and acted differently from all the beasts before it. It had ten horns,

7:8and many plans were in its horns. Another single horn sprouted among them, small among its horns, and three of the earlier horns were withered through it. This horn had eyes like human eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and it made war against the holy ones.

The Ancient of Days

7:9I watched until thrones were set in place and an Ancient of Days was seated, wearing a covering like snow, with the hair of His head like pure white wool. The throne was like a flame of fire,

7:10and a river of fire came out before Him. A thousand thousands attended Him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood beside Him. A court sat, and books were opened.

7:11Then I watched the sound of the great words the horn was speaking. The beast was beaten to death, its body perished, and it was given over to burning fire.

7:12He removed those around it from their authority, and a span of life was given them for a time and a season.

One Like a Son of Man upon the Clouds

7:13I watched in the night vision, and there, upon the clouds of heaven, one like a son of man was coming. He was present as an Ancient of Days, and those standing by were present with him. [1]

7:14Authority was given him, and all the nations of the earth according to their families, and all glory, worshipped him. His authority is everlasting authority that will never be taken away, and his kingdom will never be destroyed. [1]

Daniel Seeks the Interpretation

7:15I, Daniel, was disheartened by these things in the night vision,

7:16and approached one of those standing there, seeking exact understanding from him concerning all these things. He answered me and explained the judgment of the words:

7:17‘These great beasts are four kingdoms that will perish from the earth.

7:18The holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and hold the kingdom for ever, for the age of ages.’

The Fourth Beast and the Ten Horns

7:19Then I wanted exact understanding of the fourth beast, which destroyed everything and was exceedingly fearsome. Its teeth were iron and its claws bronze, devouring everyone all around and trampling with its feet,

7:20and of its ten horns upon its head and the single other that sprouted, through which three fell out. That horn had eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and its appearance surpassed the others.

7:21I observed that horn waging war against the holy ones and routing them,

7:22until the Ancient of Days came and gave judgment to the holy ones of the Most High. The time was given, and the holy ones took possession of the kingship.

7:23I was told concerning the fourth beast that a fourth kingdom would be upon the earth, surpassing the whole earth, overturning it and grinding it down.

7:24As for the kingdom’s ten horns, ten kings will arise, and another king will arise after them. He will surpass the earlier ones in evils and humble three kings.

7:25He will speak words against the Most High, wear down the holy ones of the Most High, and expect to alter seasons and law. Everything will be handed into his hands for a time, times, and half a time.

The Judgment and the Kingdom Given to the Holy People

7:26The judgment will sit; they will destroy the authority and plan to defile and destroy completely.

7:27He gave the kingdom, authority, their greatness, and rule over all the kingdoms under heaven to a holy people of the Most High, to reign with everlasting kingship; all authorities will be subject to him and obey him.

7:28Until the account ended, I, Daniel, was greatly overcome with astonishment; my condition changed, and I fixed the matter in my heart.”

Source and wording notes

Daniel 7:13 · Meaning and wording · Ancient of Days

The comparative designation in this Old Greek vision clause. Retain as and the being-present verb. Explain the pronoun and figure relationship as a difficult textual question; do not quietly convert as into to.

Historical comparison. Saints follows the Old Greek as an Ancient of Days. KJV, Brenton, and DRC describe coming to the Ancient of Days. The relation between the figures is materially different in the wording.

Daniel 7:14 · Meaning and wording · authority

Enduring rule granted to the figure in the vision. Keep authority distinct from kingdom and preserve the emphatic denial that it will be taken away. The preceding worship clause is syntactically unusual in Old Greek and remains separately flagged.

Historical comparison. Saints has authority; KJV and Brenton dominion; DRC power. The Old Greek's surrounding list differs from their peoples, tribes, and languages, so the whole verse must not be harmonized.
