Daniel 8

Read this chapter on staging

The Vision of the Ram

8:1In the third year of Baltasar’s reign came the vision that I, Daniel, saw after seeing the first one.

8:2I saw in the vision of my dream, while I was in the city of Susa in the country of Elymais, still beside the gate of Ailam,

8:3that, looking up, I saw a great ram standing opposite the gate. It had horns, one higher than the other, and the higher one was rising.

8:4After this I saw the ram charging eastward, northward, westward, and southward. No animals stood before it, and no one rescued from its hands. It did as it wished and was exalted.

The Male Goat from the West

8:5I was pondering, and a male goat came from the west over the earth’s surface without touching the earth. The goat had a single horn between its eyes.

8:6It came against the horned ram that I had seen standing beside the gate and ran at it in furious anger.

8:7I saw it approach the ram, become furious with it, strike it, and shatter its two horns. The ram no longer had strength to stand against the goat. It hurled the ram down upon the earth and crushed it, and no one could rescue the ram from the goat.

8:8The male goat grew exceedingly strong. When it became strong, its great horn was shattered, and four other horns rose behind it toward the four winds of heaven.

8:9From one of them a single strong horn sprouted. It grew strong and struck toward the south, toward the east, and toward the north.

8:10It was raised as high as heaven’s stars, but was dashed to the earth from the stars and trampled by them,

8:11until the chief commander rescues the captivity. Because of him the ancient mountains were dashed down; their place and sacrifice were removed. He set it down upon the earth, and it prospered and came to pass, and the sanctuary will be made desolate.

8:12Sins came upon the sacrifice; righteousness was thrown down to the ground, and he acted and prospered.

Two Thousand Three Hundred Evenings and Mornings

8:13I heard another holy one speaking, and the other said to the speaking Phelmouni, “How long will the vision stand, and the sacrifice that was removed, and the sin of desolation that was given, and the holy places be made desolate for trampling?”

8:14He said to him, “Until evening and morning, two thousand three hundred days; then the sanctuary will be cleansed.”

Gabriel Sent to Explain

8:15As I, Daniel, watched the vision, I sought to understand, and there stood opposite me someone with a human appearance.

8:16I heard a human voice in the midst of Ulai, calling, “Gabriel, give that one understanding of the vision.” The man cried out and said, “The vision concerns that command.”

8:17He came and stood beside where I stood. As he came, I was disturbed and fell upon my face. He said to me, “Understand, son of man, for this vision is still for an appointed hour.”

8:18As he spoke with me, I fell asleep face down on the ground. He touched me and raised me on the spot,

8:19and said to me, “Behold! I am reporting what will happen at the end of the wrath to the sons of your people; it will still remain until the hours of the appointed time of completion.

The Interpretation of the Vision

8:20The ram you saw with the horns is the king of the Medes and Persians.

8:21The male goat is the king of the Greeks, and the great horn between its eyes is the first king himself.

8:22As for the four horns that were shattered and rose behind it, four kings of his nation will arise, but not according to his strength.

8:23At the end of their kingship, when their sins reach fullness, a king with a shameless face who understands riddles will arise.

8:24His strength will be made firm, though not by his own strength. He will destroy astonishingly, prosper, and act; he will destroy rulers and a people of holy ones.

8:25His intent will be against the holy ones; falsehood will prosper in his hands, and his heart will be exalted. By deceit he will make many disappear, rise for the destruction of men, make a “gathering of a hand,” and sell.

8:26The evening-and-morning vision was found to be true. Now the vision is closed up, for it is still for many days.”

8:27I, Daniel, was weak for many days. Then I rose and again conducted royal affairs. I was undone by the vision, and there was no one who understood.
