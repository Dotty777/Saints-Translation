1 Corinthians 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, called as an apostle of Christ Jesus through God’s will, and Sosthenes the brother,

1:2to the Church of God in Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus, called as saints, together with all who call upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, theirs and ours:

1:3Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Thanksgiving

1:4I always thank my God for you because of the grace of God given to you in Christ Jesus,

1:5because in Him you were enriched in everything, in all speech and all knowledge,

1:6just as the testimony of Christ was confirmed among you,

1:7so that you lack no gift as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ.

1:8He will also keep you firm until the end, free from accusation on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

1:9God is faithful, through whom you were called into the fellowship of His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

Divisions in the Church

1:10Now I appeal to you, brothers, through the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all speak in agreement and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be restored to unity in the same mind and the same judgment.

1:11For Chloe’s people have made it clear to me, my brothers, that there are quarrels among you.

1:12What I mean is this: each of you says, “I belong to Paul,” or “I to Apollos,” or “I to Cephas,” or “I to Christ.”

1:13Has Christ been divided? Paul was not crucified for you, was he? Or were you baptized into Paul’s name?

1:14I give thanks to God that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,

1:15so that no one may say that you were baptized into my own name.

1:16I did also baptize the household of Stephanas; beyond that, I do not know whether I baptized anyone else.

1:17For Christ did not send me to baptize but to proclaim the good news, without wisdom of speech, so that the cross of Christ might not be emptied of its power.

The Word of the Cross

1:18For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is God’s power.

1:19For it is written, “I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and I will set aside the understanding of the discerning.”

1:20Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has not God made the world’s wisdom foolish?

1:21For since, in God’s wisdom, the world did not know God through wisdom, God was pleased through the foolishness of the proclamation to save those who believe.

1:22For Jews ask for signs and Greeks seek wisdom,

1:23but we proclaim Christ crucified: a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles,

1:24but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is God’s power and God’s wisdom.

1:25For God’s foolishness is wiser than human beings, and God’s weakness is stronger than human beings.

God Chose the Foolish Things

1:26For consider your calling, brothers: not many were wise according to the flesh, not many powerful, not many of noble birth.

1:27But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong;

1:28and God chose the lowborn things of the world and the despised things, the things that are not, to bring to nothing the things that are,

1:29so that no flesh may boast before God.

1:30But it is from Him that you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God, and righteousness and sanctification and redemption,

1:31so that, as it is written, “Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.”

1 Corinthians 2

Read this chapter on staging

Proclaiming Christ Crucified

2:1And when I came to you, brothers, I did not come with lofty speech or wisdom, proclaiming to you the mystery of God.

2:2For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.

2:3And I was with you in weakness and in fear and in much trembling.

2:4My speech and my proclamation were not in persuasive words of wisdom, but in a demonstration of Spirit and power,

2:5so that your faith might rest in God’s power rather than human wisdom.

Wisdom Revealed by the Spirit

2:6Yet we speak wisdom among the mature, a wisdom belonging neither to this age nor to the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing.

2:7Instead, we speak God’s wisdom in a mystery, the hidden wisdom that God appointed beforehand, before the ages, for our glory.

2:8None of the rulers of this age has known it; for if they had known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

2:9But as it is written, “What an eye has not seen and an ear has not heard, and what has not arisen in a human heart—these are the things God prepared for those who love Him.”

2:10But God revealed them to us through the Spirit, for the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.

2:11For who among human beings knows the things of a person except the person’s spirit within? So also no one has known the things of God except the Spirit of God.

2:12Now we did not receive the spirit of the world but the Spirit who is from God, so that we may know the things freely given to us by God.

2:13We also speak these things, not in words taught by human wisdom but in those taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things to spiritual people.

The Natural and the Spiritual Person

2:14But a natural person does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to such a person, who cannot know them because they are discerned spiritually.

2:15The spiritual person discerns all things, but is himself discerned by no one.

2:16For “Who has known the Lord’s mind, so as to instruct Him?” But we have Christ’s mind.

1 Corinthians 3

Read this chapter on staging

Still of the Flesh

3:1And I, brothers, could not speak to you as spiritual people, but as people of flesh, as infants in Christ.

3:2I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able to receive it. Indeed, even now you are still unable,

3:3for you are still fleshly. For where there are jealousy and quarrels among you, are you not fleshly and walking in a merely human way?

3:4For whenever someone says, “I belong to Paul,” and another, “I to Apollos,” are you not merely human?

Servants Through Whom You Believed

3:5What, then, is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, each as the Lord granted.

3:6I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.

3:7So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but God who gives the growth.

3:8The one who plants and the one who waters are one, but each will receive his own wages according to his own labor.

3:9For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s cultivated field, God’s building.

According to the Grace of God Given Me

3:10According to the grace of God given to me, I laid a foundation as a wise master builder, and another builds upon it. But let each one watch how he builds upon it.

3:11For no one can lay another foundation besides the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.

3:12Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, grass, or straw,

3:13each person’s work will become clear, for the day will disclose it, because it is revealed in fire; and the fire itself will test what sort of work each has done.

3:14If anyone’s work that he has built upon the foundation remains, he will receive wages.

3:15If anyone’s work is burned up, he will suffer loss, but he himself will be saved, though in this way: as through fire.

You Are God’s Temple

3:16Do you not know that you are God’s temple and that God’s Spirit dwells among you?

3:17If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy that person. For God’s temple is holy, and that is what you are.

The Wisdom of This World

3:18Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may become wise.

3:19For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,”

3:20and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile.”

3:21So let no one boast in human beings, for all things are yours,

3:22whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world or life or death, or present things or things to come—all are yours,

3:23and you are Christ’s, and Christ is God’s.

1 Corinthians 4

Read this chapter on staging

Stewards of God’s Mysteries

4:1This is how a person should regard us: as servants of Christ and stewards of God’s mysteries.

4:2Moreover, what is required of stewards is that each be found faithful.

4:3But to me it is a very small matter that I should be examined by you or by a human court; indeed, I do not even examine myself.

4:4For I am aware of nothing against myself, but I am not thereby justified. The One who examines me is the Lord.

4:5So judge nothing before the time, until the Lord comes. He will both bring to light the hidden things of darkness and reveal the purposes of hearts; then each one’s praise will come from God.

Fools for Christ

4:6Now, brothers, I have applied these things figuratively to myself and Apollos for your sake, so that in us you may learn, “Not beyond what is written,” so that none of you may be puffed up in favor of one against another.

4:7For who makes you different? And what do you have that you did not receive? But if you did receive it, why do you boast as though you had not received it?

4:8Already you have your fill! Already you have become rich! Without us you have become kings! And I wish indeed that you had become kings, so that we too might reign with you.

4:9For I think God has displayed us apostles last of all, as people condemned to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to human beings.

4:10We are foolish for Christ’s sake, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are honored, but we are dishonored.

4:11To this very hour we hunger and thirst, are poorly clothed, are beaten, and have no settled home,

4:12and we toil, working with our own hands. When insulted, we bless; when persecuted, we endure;

4:13when slandered, we appeal. We have become like the world’s refuse, everyone’s scrapings, until now.

A Father’s Admonition

4:14I write these things not to shame you, but to admonish you as my beloved children.

4:15For even if you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the good news.

4:16Therefore I appeal to you: imitate me.

4:17For this reason I sent Timothy to you, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, just as I teach everywhere in every Church.

The Kingdom of God in Power

4:18Some have become puffed up as though I were not coming to you.

4:19But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out the power of those who are puffed up, not their talk.

4:20For God’s kingdom is in power, not in talk.

4:21What do you want? Should I come to you with a rod, or in love and a spirit of gentleness?

Immorality in the Church

5:1Sexual immorality is actually reported among you, and such sexual immorality as does not occur even among the Gentiles: that someone has his father’s wife.

5:2And you are puffed up! Should you not instead have mourned, so that the one who did this deed might be removed from your midst?

5:3For although I am absent in body, I am present in spirit, and I have already passed judgment, as though present, on the one who acted in this way,

The Old Leaven

5:6Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump of dough?

5:8So let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, nor with the leaven of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

Judging Those Inside

5:12For what have I to do with judging those outside? Do you not judge those inside?

5:13But God will judge those outside. Remove the wicked person from among yourselves.

1 Corinthians 6

Read this chapter on staging

Lawsuits Among Believers

6:1Does any of you, having a case against another, dare to seek judgment before the unrighteous and not before the saints?

6:2Or do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is judged by you, are you unworthy of the smallest cases?

6:3Do you not know that we will judge angels, to say nothing of matters of ordinary life?

6:4If, then, you have cases concerning ordinary life, do you seat as judges those held in no regard in the Church?

6:5I say this to your shame. Is there really no wise person among you who will be able to decide his brother’s case,

6:6but brother goes to court against brother, and that before unbelievers?

6:7Indeed, then, it is already a complete defeat for you that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not instead be wronged? Why not instead be defrauded?

6:8But you yourselves do wrong and defraud—and do this to brothers.

Do You Not Know That the Unrighteous

10:4and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they drank from a spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.

10:5But God was not pleased with most of them, for they were strewn over the wilderness.

10:6Now these things became examples for us, so that we might not desire evil things as they also desired.

10:7Do not become idolaters, as some of them did, just as it is written: “The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose to play.”

10:8Nor let us commit sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day.

10:9Nor let us put Christ to the test, as some of them tested Him and were being destroyed by the serpents.

10:10Nor grumble, just as some of them grumbled and were destroyed by the destroyer.

10:12So let the one who thinks he stands take care that he does not fall.

10:13No temptation has seized you except what is common to humanity. But God is faithful: He will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you can bear, but with the temptation will also provide the way out, so that you may be able to endure it.

Flee Idolatry

10:14Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

10:15I speak as to wise people; judge for yourselves what I say.

10:16The cup of blessing that we bless—is it not participation in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break—is it not participation in the body of Christ?

10:17Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

10:18Consider Israel according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices participants in the altar?

10:19What, then, am I saying? That food offered to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything?

10:20Rather, that what they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God. And I do not want you to become participants with demons.

10:21You cannot drink the Lord’s cup and the cup of demons. You cannot partake of the Lord’s table and the table of demons.

10:22Or are we provoking the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?

10:23All things are permitted, but not all things are beneficial. All things are permitted, but not all things build up.

10:26for “The earth is the Lord’s, and all that fills it.”

10:27If any of the unbelievers invites you and you want to go, eat everything set before you without examining it for conscience’s sake.

10:28But if someone says to you, “This has been offered in sacrifice,” do not eat it, for the sake of the one who informed you and for conscience’s sake.

10:29I mean not your own conscience, but the other’s. For why is my freedom judged by another conscience?

10:30If I partake with thanksgiving, why am I slandered for what I give thanks for?

Whether You Eat or Drink

10:31Therefore, whether you eat or drink or do anything, do everything for God’s glory.

10:32Give no cause for stumbling to Jews or Greeks or the Church of God,

Imitators of Christ

Head Coverings

11:8For man is not from woman, but woman from man;

11:12For just as woman is from man, so also man is through woman; but all things are from God.

11:13Judge among yourselves: is it fitting for a woman to pray to God uncovered?

The Tradition of the Lord’s Supper

Examine Yourselves

11:30For this reason many among you are weak and ill, and a considerable number are asleep.

11:31But if we discerned ourselves, we would not be judged.

11:32Yet when we are judged by the Lord, we are disciplined, so that we may not be condemned with the world.

Wait for One Another

11:33Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.

11:34If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that you may not come together for judgment. I will arrange the remaining matters when I come.

One Body, Many Members

12:12For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the body’s members, though many, are one body, so also is Christ.

12:13For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, and we were all given one Spirit to drink.

12:14For the body is not one member but many.

12:15If the foot says, “Because I am not a hand, I am not part of the body,” that does not stop it from being part of the body, does it?

12:16And if the ear says, “Because I am not an eye, I am not part of the body,” that does not stop it from being part of the body, does it?

12:17If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?

12:18But as it is, God placed the members, each one of them, in the body just as He wished.

12:19If they were all one member, where would the body be?

12:20But as it is, there are many members, yet one body.

12:21The eye cannot say to the hand, “I have no need of you,” or again the head to the feet, “I have no need of you.”

12:22On the contrary, the members of the body that seem weaker are much more necessary,

12:23and those parts of the body we consider less honorable, we clothe with greater honor; and our unpresentable parts receive greater modesty,

12:25so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same care for one another.

12:26If one member suffers, all the members suffer together; if one member is honored, all the members rejoice together.

You Are Christ’s Body

12:27Now you are Christ’s body, and individually members of it.

12:28God has appointed in the Church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then acts of power, then gifts of healings, helps, forms of guidance, and kinds of tongues.

12:30Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

12:31But earnestly desire the greater gifts. And I show you a way beyond all comparison.

Prophecy and Tongues

14:1Pursue love, and earnestly desire spiritual things, especially that you may prophesy.

14:2For the one who speaks in a tongue speaks not to human beings but to God, for no one understands him; in spirit he speaks mysteries.

14:3But the one who prophesies speaks to human beings for building up, encouragement, and consolation.

14:4The one who speaks in a tongue builds himself up, but the one who prophesies builds up the Church.

14:5I want all of you to speak in tongues, but still more that you should prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, so that the Church may be built up.

Speech That Builds Up

14:6But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you unless I speak to you in revelation or knowledge or prophecy or teaching?

14:7Even lifeless things that give a sound, whether a flute or a lyre—if they do not produce distinct notes, how will what is played on the flute or lyre be recognized?

14:8For if a trumpet gives an unclear sound, who will prepare for battle?

14:9So also with you: unless you speak an intelligible word with your tongue, how will what is spoken be known? For you will be speaking into the air.

14:10There are, it may be, so many kinds of voices in the world, and none is voiceless.

14:11Therefore, if I do not know the meaning of the voice, I will be a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker a foreigner to me.

14:12So also with you: since you are zealous for spirits, seek to excel for the building up of the Church.

Let the One Who Speaks in a Tongue Pray

14:13Therefore let the one who speaks in a tongue pray that he may interpret.

14:14For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.

14:15What, then, is to be done? I will pray with the spirit, but I will also pray with the mind. I will sing with the spirit, but I will also sing with the mind.

14:16Otherwise, if you bless in spirit, how will the person in the place of the uninitiated say the Amen to your thanksgiving, since he does not know what you are saying?

14:17For you give thanks well, but the other person is not built up.

14:18I thank God; I speak in tongues more than all of you.

14:19But in Church I would rather speak five words with my mind, so that I may instruct others too, than ten thousand words in a tongue.

Brothers, Do Not Become Children in Thinking

14:20Brothers, do not become children in your thinking. Be infants in evil, but become mature in your thinking.

14:21In the law it is written, “Through people of other tongues and through the lips of others I will speak to this people, and even so they will not listen to Me,” says the Lord.

14:22So tongues are a sign for unbelievers, not for believers, while prophecy is for believers, not for unbelievers.

The Gospel of the Resurrection

15:1Now I make known to you, brothers, the good news that I proclaimed to you, which you also received, in which you also stand,

15:2through which you are also being saved, if you hold fast to the word in which I proclaimed it to you—unless you believed in vain.

15:3For I handed over to you as matters of first importance what I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,

15:4and that He was buried, and that He has been raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,

15:5and that He appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.

15:6After that He appeared to more than five hundred brothers at once, most of whom remain until now, though some have fallen asleep.

15:7After that He appeared to James, then to all the apostles.

15:8Last of all, as to one untimely born, He appeared also to me.

15:9For I am the least of the apostles, unworthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted God’s Church.

15:10But by God’s grace I am what I am, and His grace toward me was not empty. Instead, I labored more than all of them—yet not I, but God’s grace with me.

15:11So whether it was I or they, this is how we proclaim, and this is how you believed.

If the Dead Are Not Raised

15:12Now if Christ is proclaimed as raised from the dead, how can some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?

15:13But if there is no resurrection of the dead, neither has Christ been raised.

15:14And if Christ has not been raised, then our proclamation is also empty, and your faith is empty too.

15:15We are also found to be false witnesses concerning God, because we testified against God that He raised Christ, whom He did not raise if, after all, the dead are not raised.

15:16For if the dead are not raised, neither has Christ been raised.

15:17And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile; you are still in your sins.

15:18Then those who have fallen asleep in Christ have also perished.

15:19If in this life alone we have hoped in Christ, we are more to be pitied than all other people.

Christ the Firstfruits

15:20But as it is, Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.

15:21For since death came through a human being, resurrection of the dead also comes through a human being.

15:22For just as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.

15:23But each in his own order: Christ the firstfruits, then those who belong to Christ at His coming,

15:24then the end, when He hands over the kingdom to God the Father, when He brings to nothing every rule and every authority and power.

15:25For He must reign until He puts all the enemies under His feet.

15:26The last enemy to be brought to nothing is death,

15:27for “He subjected all things under His feet.” But when it says that all things have been subjected, it is clear that this excludes the One who subjected all things to Him.

15:28And when all things have been subjected to Him, then the Son Himself will also be subjected to the One who subjected all things to Him, so that God may be all in all.

Otherwise, What Will Those Do?

15:29Otherwise, what will those who are baptized for the dead do? If the dead are not raised at all, why are they even baptized for them?

15:30Why are we also in danger every hour?

15:31I die daily, brothers—I affirm it by my boasting in you, which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.

15:32If in a merely human way I fought with beasts in Ephesus, what benefit is there for me? If the dead are not raised, “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.”

15:33Do not be deceived: “Bad company corrupts good habits.”

15:34Sober up as you ought and do not sin, for some have no knowledge of God. I speak to your shame.

How Are the Dead Raised?

15:35But someone will say, “How are the dead raised? With what sort of body do they come?”

15:36Foolish one! What you sow is not made alive unless it dies.

15:37And what you sow is not the body that will come to be, but a bare grain, perhaps of wheat or of one of the other kinds.

15:38But God gives it a body just as He wished, and to each of the seeds its own body.

15:39Not all flesh is the same flesh: human flesh is one kind, the flesh of animals another, the flesh of birds another, and that of fish another.

15:40There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies, but the glory of the heavenly is one kind and that of the earthly another.

15:41The sun has one glory, the moon another glory, and the stars another glory; for star differs from star in glory.

The Resurrection Body

15:42So also is the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption; it is raised in incorruption.

15:43It is sown in dishonor; it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.

15:44It is sown a natural body; it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual one.

15:45So also it is written, “The first human being, Adam, became a living soul”; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.

15:46But the spiritual is not first; the natural is first, then the spiritual.

15:47The first human being is from the earth, made of dust; the second human being is from heaven.

15:48As is the one made of dust, so also are those made of dust; and as is the heavenly One, so also are the heavenly ones.

15:49And just as we bore the image of the one made of dust, we will also bear the image of the heavenly One.

The Mystery of the Change

15:50Now I say this, brothers: flesh and blood cannot inherit God’s kingdom, nor does corruption inherit incorruption.

15:51Look, I tell you a mystery: we will not all fall asleep, but we will all be changed,

15:52in an instant, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we will be changed.

15:53For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality.

15:54But when this corruptible puts on incorruption and this mortal puts on immortality, then the written saying will come to pass: “Death has been swallowed up in victory.”

15:55“Where, death, is your victory? Where, death, is your sting?”

15:56The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.

15:57But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

15:58Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the Lord’s work, knowing that your labor is not empty in the Lord.
