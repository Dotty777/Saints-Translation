2 CORINTHIANS 1

1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Timothy the brother, to the Church of God in Corinth, together with all the saints in the whole of Achaia:
1:2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
1:3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort,
1:4 who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those in every affliction through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.
1:5 For just as the sufferings of Christ abound toward us, so also our comfort abounds through Christ.
1:6 Now whether we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; or whether we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which is at work as you endure the same sufferings that we also suffer.
1:7 And our hope for you is firm, for we know that as you share in the sufferings, so also you share in the comfort.
1:8 For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of our affliction that occurred in Asia: we were burdened far beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of living.
1:9 Indeed, we ourselves had the sentence of death within us, so that we might rely not on ourselves but on God who raises the dead.
1:10 He delivered us from so great a death and will deliver us; in Him we have set our hope that He will deliver us yet again,
1:11 as you too help us by prayer, so that many people may give thanks on our behalf for the gift granted to us through many.
1:12 For our boast is this, the testimony of our conscience: that in simplicity and sincerity from God, and not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, we conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you.
1:13 For we write nothing to you other than what you read or also understand; and I hope that you will understand fully to the end,
1:14 just as you have also recognized us in part, that we are your boast, just as you also are ours in the day of our Lord Jesus.
1:15 And with this confidence I intended to come to you first, so that you might have a second grace,
1:16 and to visit you on my way to Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and to be sent onward by you to Judea.
1:17 Now when I intended this, was I then being fickle? Or do I plan what I plan according to the flesh, so that with me there is both “Yes, yes” and “No, no”?
1:18 But God is faithful: our word to you is not “Yes” and “No.”
1:19 For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was proclaimed among you through us—through me and Silvanus and Timothy—did not become “Yes” and “No,” but in Him “Yes” has come to be.
1:20 For however many God’s promises are, in Him is the “Yes.” Therefore also through Him is the “Amen” to God, for His glory through us.
1:21 Now the One who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,
1:22 who also sealed us and gave the pledge of the Spirit in our hearts.
1:23 But I call God as witness against my soul that it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth.
1:24 Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers for your joy, for in faith you stand.

2 CORINTHIANS 2

2:1 For I decided this for myself: not to come to you again in sorrow.
2:2 For if I cause you sorrow, who then makes me glad except the one made sorrowful by me?
2:3 And I wrote this very thing so that, when I came, I might not be grieved by those who ought to make me rejoice. I was confident about all of you that my joy is the joy of you all.
2:4 For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you might be made sorrowful, but that you might know the abundant love I have for you.
2:5 But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has caused sorrow not to me, but in part—to avoid pressing too heavily—to all of you.
2:6 This punishment by the majority is sufficient for such a person,
2:7 so that, on the contrary, you should rather forgive and comfort him, lest such a person be swallowed up by excessive sorrow.
2:8 Therefore I urge you to confirm your love toward him.
2:9 For I also wrote for this purpose: to see how you stood the test, whether you are obedient in everything.
2:10 Now whom you forgive anything, I also forgive; for what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, was for your sake in the presence of Christ,
2:11 so that we might not be taken advantage of by Satan, for we are not unaware of his designs.
2:12 Now when I came to Troas for the good news of Christ, and a door had been opened to me in the Lord,
2:13 I had no rest in my spirit because I did not find Titus my brother; but taking leave of them, I went out to Macedonia.
2:14 But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ and makes known through us the aroma of the knowledge of Him in every place.
2:15 For we are Christ’s fragrance to God among those being saved and among those perishing:
2:16 to the one an aroma from death to death, but to the other an aroma from life to life. And who is sufficient for these things?
2:17 For we are not like the many who peddle the word of God; but we speak in Christ before God, out of sincerity, as those sent from God.

2 CORINTHIANS 3

3:1 Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some do, letters of recommendation to you or from you?
3:2 You are our letter, inscribed in our hearts, known and read by all people.
3:3 You are clearly a letter of Christ, produced through our ministry, inscribed not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on tablets that are hearts of flesh.
3:4 And we have such confidence toward God through Christ.
3:5 Not that we are sufficient in ourselves to consider anything as coming from ourselves, but our sufficiency is from God,
3:6 who also made us sufficient as ministers of a new covenant, not of letter but of Spirit; for the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.
3:7 Now if the ministry of death, engraved in letters on stones, came in glory, so that the sons of Israel could not gaze at Moses’ face because of the glory of his face, which was being brought to an end,
3:8 how much more will the ministry of the Spirit be in glory?
3:9 For if there is glory in the ministry of condemnation, much more does the ministry of righteousness abound in glory.
3:10 For indeed what once had glory has no glory in this respect, because of the surpassing glory.
3:11 For if what is being brought to an end came through glory, how much greater is the glory of what remains!
3:12 Therefore, having such hope, we use great boldness,
3:13 and are not like Moses, who put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel might not gaze at the end of what was being brought to an end.
3:14 But their minds were hardened. For until this very day the same veil remains at the reading of the old covenant, not being unveiled, because in Christ it is brought to an end.
3:15 But to this day, whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their heart.
3:16 But whenever one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed.
3:17 Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.
3:18 And we all, with unveiled face, beholding as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, as from the Lord, the Spirit.

2 CORINTHIANS 4

4:1 For this reason, having this ministry, just as we received mercy, we do not lose heart.
4:2 But we have renounced the secret and shameful things, not walking in cunning or tampering with the word of God, but commending ourselves to every human conscience before God by making the truth known.
4:3 And even if our good news is veiled, it is veiled among those perishing,
4:4 among whom the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelieving, so that the light of the good news of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, might not shine upon them.
4:5 For we proclaim not ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for Jesus’ sake.
4:6 For God, who said, “Light will shine out of darkness,” is the One who shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.
4:7 But we have this treasure in clay jars, so that the surpassing power may be God’s and not from us.
4:8 We are afflicted in every way, but not confined; perplexed, but not utterly at a loss;
4:9 persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed;
4:10 always carrying in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.
4:11 For we who live are always being handed over to death for Jesus’ sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.
4:12 So death is at work in us, but life in you.
4:13 But having the same spirit of faith, according to what is written, “I believed, therefore I spoke,” we also believe, and therefore also speak,
4:14 knowing that the One who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and present us together with you.
4:15 For all things are for your sake, so that grace, having increased through the many, may cause thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.
4:16 Therefore we do not lose heart; but even if our outer person is wasting away, our inner person is being renewed day by day.
4:17 For the momentary lightness of our affliction produces for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all measure,
4:18 as we look not at the things seen but at the things not seen; for the things seen are temporary, but the things not seen are eternal.

2 CORINTHIANS 5

5:1 For we know that if our earthly house, this tent, is dismantled, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens.
5:2 For indeed in this we groan, longing to be clothed over with our dwelling from heaven,
5:3 if indeed, even after being unclothed, we shall not be found naked.
5:4 For indeed we who are in the tent groan, being burdened, because we do not wish to be unclothed but to be clothed over, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.
5:5 Now the One who prepared us for this very thing is God, who gave us the pledge of the Spirit.
5:6 Therefore we are always confident, knowing that while at home in the body we are away from the Lord—
5:7 for we walk by faith, not by sight—
5:8 we are confident, and prefer rather to be away from the body and at home with the Lord.
5:9 Therefore we also make it our aim, whether at home or away, to be pleasing to Him.
5:10 For we must all be revealed before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each may receive what is due for what he has done through the body, whether good or evil.
5:11 Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people; but we have been revealed to God, and I hope we have also been revealed in your consciences.
5:12 We are not commending ourselves to you again, but giving you an opportunity to boast on our behalf, so that you may have an answer for those who boast in appearance and not in heart.
5:13 For if we were beside ourselves, it was for God; if we are in our right mind, it is for you.
5:14 For the love of Christ holds us fast, since we have judged this: that One died for all; therefore all died.
5:15 And He died for all so that those who live might no longer live for themselves, but for the One who died and was raised on their behalf.
5:16 So from now on we know no one according to the flesh. Even if we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we no longer know Him in that way.
5:17 Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation. The old things passed away; behold, new things have come to be.
5:18 And all things are from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation:
5:19 namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and placing in us the word of reconciliation.
5:20 Therefore we serve as ambassadors on behalf of Christ, as God appeals through us. We beg on behalf of Christ: be reconciled to God.
5:21 On our behalf He made the One who knew no sin to be sin, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 CORINTHIANS 6

6:1 And working together, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain.
6:2 For He says, “At an acceptable time I heard you, and on a day of salvation I helped you.” Look, now is a most acceptable time; look, now is a day of salvation.
6:3 We give no occasion for stumbling in anything, so that the ministry may not be blamed,
6:4 but in everything commend ourselves as ministers of God: in much endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,
6:5 in beatings, in imprisonments, in disturbances, in labors, in sleepless nights, in fastings,
6:6 in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without hypocrisy,
6:7 in a word of truth, in the power of God; through the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and the left,
6:8 through glory and dishonor, through bad report and good report; as deceivers, and yet truthful;
6:9 as unknown, and yet recognized; as dying, and look, we live; as disciplined, and yet not put to death;
6:10 as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet enriching many; as having nothing, and yet possessing everything.
6:11 Our mouth has opened to you, Corinthians; our heart has opened wide.
6:12 You are not confined by us, but by your own affections.
6:13 Now in return—I speak as to children—open yourselves wide also.
6:14 Do not become unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what sharing is there between righteousness and lawlessness, or what fellowship between light and darkness?
6:15 And what harmony is there between Christ and Beliar, or what portion does a believer have with an unbeliever?
6:16 And what agreement is there between God’s temple and idols? For we are the temple of the living God, just as God said: “I will dwell in them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be My people.
6:17 Therefore come out from their midst and be separated, says the Lord, and do not touch anything unclean; and I will receive you.
6:18 And I will be a Father to you, and you will be sons and daughters to Me, says the Lord Almighty.”

2 CORINTHIANS 7

7:1 Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of flesh and spirit, bringing holiness to completion in the fear of God.
7:2 Make room for us. We wronged no one, corrupted no one, took advantage of no one.
7:3 I do not say this to condemn you, for I have said before that you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together.
7:4 Great is my boldness toward you; great is my boasting on your behalf. I am filled with comfort; I overflow exceedingly with joy in all our affliction.
7:5 For even when we came into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we were afflicted in every way: conflicts outside, fears within.
7:6 But God, who comforts the lowly, comforted us by the arrival of Titus,
7:7 and not only by his arrival, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted because of you. He reported to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal on my behalf, so that I rejoiced still more.
7:8 For even if I caused you sorrow by the letter, I do not regret it. Even if I did regret it—for I see that that letter caused you sorrow, even if for a time—
7:9 now I rejoice, not that you were made sorrowful, but that your sorrow led to repentance. For your sorrow was godly, so that you might suffer no loss from us in anything.
7:10 For godly sorrow produces repentance that leads to salvation without regret, but the sorrow of the world produces death.
7:11 For look at this very thing, your godly sorrow: what diligence it produced in you, what defense, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what vindication! In everything you showed yourselves to be pure in the matter.
7:12 So even if I wrote to you, it was not for the sake of the one who did wrong, nor for the sake of the one wronged, but so that your diligence on our behalf might be made clear to you before God.
7:13 For this reason we have been comforted. And beyond our comfort, we rejoiced much more at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by all of you.
7:14 For if I have boasted anything to him on your behalf, I was not put to shame; but as we spoke everything to you in truth, so also our boasting before Titus proved true.
7:15 And his affection for you is all the greater as he remembers the obedience of you all, how you received him with fear and trembling.
7:16 I rejoice that in everything I have confidence in you.

2 CORINTHIANS 8

8:1 Now we make known to you, brothers, the grace of God given among the Churches of Macedonia:
8:2 that in a great trial of affliction, the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty overflowed into the wealth of their generosity.
8:3 For I testify that they gave according to their ability, and beyond their ability, of their own choice,
8:4 begging us earnestly for the grace and the fellowship of the ministry to the saints.
8:5 And what they did was not as we expected: they gave themselves first to the Lord and to us through the will of God,
8:6 so we urged Titus to complete this grace among you, just as he had begun it beforehand.
8:7 But just as you abound in everything—in faith and word and knowledge and all diligence and the love from us in you—see that you abound also in this grace.
8:8 I do not speak as a command, but through the diligence of others I also test the genuineness of your love.
8:9 For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ: though He was rich, He became poor for your sake, so that you might become rich through His poverty.
8:10 And in this I give an opinion, for this is beneficial for you, who already began last year not only to do it but also to desire it.
8:11 But now finish doing it also, so that your eager desire may be matched by completing it out of what you have.
8:12 For if the readiness is present, it is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have.
8:13 For it is not that there should be relief for others and affliction for you, but on the basis of equality:
8:14 at the present time your abundance is to meet their lack, so that their abundance may also meet your lack, so that there may be equality.
8:15 As it is written, “The one with much had no excess, and the one with little had no shortage.”
8:16 But thanks be to God, who put the same diligence for you in Titus’ heart,
8:17 for he accepted the appeal, and being still more diligent, he went out to you of his own choice.
8:18 And we sent with him the brother whose praise in the good news is throughout all the Churches.
8:19 And not only that, but he was also appointed by the Churches as our traveling companion with this grace that we are administering, for the glory of the Lord Himself and to show our readiness.
8:20 We take this precaution so that no one may blame us concerning this abundant gift that we are administering,
8:21 for we take care to do what is honorable not only before the Lord but also before people.
8:22 And we sent with them our brother, whom we have often proved diligent in many things, but now much more diligent because of his great confidence in you.
8:23 As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker among you; as for our brothers, they are envoys of Churches, a glory of Christ.
8:24 Therefore show them the proof of your love and of our boasting on your behalf, in the presence of the Churches.

2 CORINTHIANS 9

9:1 For concerning the ministry to the saints, it is unnecessary for me to write to you,
9:2 for I know your readiness, of which I boast on your behalf to Macedonians, that Achaia has been prepared since last year; and your zeal stirred up the majority.
9:3 But I sent the brothers so that our boasting on your behalf might not prove empty in this respect, so that you might be prepared, just as I said,
9:4 lest, if Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we—to say nothing of you—should be put to shame in this confidence.
9:5 Therefore I considered it necessary to urge the brothers to go ahead to you and prepare beforehand your previously promised blessing, so that it might be ready in this way as a blessing and not as something extorted.
9:6 Now this is the point: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows in blessings will also reap in blessings.
9:7 Let each give as he has decided in his heart, not out of sorrow or compulsion; for God loves a cheerful giver.
9:8 And God is able to make every grace abound toward you, so that, always having all sufficiency in everything, you may abound in every good work.
9:9 As it is written, “He scattered abroad; he gave to the poor; his righteousness remains for the age.”
9:10 Now the One who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed and increase the fruits of your righteousness.
9:11 You are being enriched in everything for all generosity, which produces thanksgiving to God through us,
9:12 because the ministry of this service not only supplies what the saints lack but also overflows through many thanksgivings to God.
9:13 Because of the proof this ministry provides, they glorify God for your submission to the good news of Christ that you confess, and for your generous sharing with them and with all,
9:14 while they too, in prayer on your behalf, long for you because of the surpassing grace of God upon you.
9:15 Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift!

2 CORINTHIANS 10

10:1 Now I myself, Paul, appeal to you through the meekness and gentleness of Christ—I who am lowly among you face to face, but bold toward you when absent.
10:2 And I beg that, when I am present, I may not have to show the bold confidence with which I intend to confront some who think we walk according to the flesh.
10:3 For though we walk in flesh, we do not wage war according to flesh,
10:4 for the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful through God for the pulling down of strongholds. We pull down arguments
10:5 and every lofty thing raised against the knowledge of God, and take captive every thought into obedience to Christ,
10:6 and stand ready to punish every disobedience when your obedience is complete.
10:7 Look at what is right before you. If anyone is confident in himself that he belongs to Christ, let him consider this again for himself: just as he belongs to Christ, so also do we.
10:8 For even if I boast somewhat more abundantly concerning our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for pulling you down, I will not be put to shame.
10:9 I do not want to seem as though I would terrify you through the letters.
10:10 For someone says, “The letters are weighty and strong, but his bodily presence is weak and his speech is despised.”
10:11 Let such a person consider this: what we are in word through letters when absent, such we are also in deed when present.
10:12 For we do not dare to class or compare ourselves with some of those who commend themselves. But they, measuring themselves by themselves and comparing themselves with themselves, do not understand.
10:13 But we will not boast beyond measure, but according to the measure of the sphere that God apportioned to us, a measure reaching even as far as you.
10:14 For we are not overextending ourselves as though we did not reach you, for we came even as far as you in the good news of Christ.
10:15 We do not boast beyond measure in others’ labors, but have hope that, as your faith grows, we may be greatly enlarged among you according to our sphere,
10:16 so as to proclaim the good news in the regions beyond you, not to boast in another’s sphere over things already prepared.
10:17 But let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.
10:18 For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.

2 CORINTHIANS 11

11:1 I wish you would bear with me in a little foolishness. Indeed, do bear with me.
11:2 For I am jealous for you with God’s jealousy, for I betrothed you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ.
11:3 But I fear lest somehow, as the serpent deceived Eve by his cunning, your minds might be corrupted and drawn away from the simplicity and purity toward Christ.
11:4 For if the one who comes proclaims another Jesus whom we did not proclaim, or you receive a different spirit that you did not receive, or a different good news that you did not accept, you bear with it well!
11:5 For I reckon that I have fallen short in nothing of the exceedingly great apostles.
11:6 Even if I am untrained in speech, I am not in knowledge; but in every way we have made it clear to you in all things.
11:7 Or did I commit a sin by humbling myself so that you might be exalted, because I proclaimed God’s good news to you free of charge?
11:8 I plundered other Churches, taking wages for the ministry to you.
11:9 And when I was present with you and in need, I burdened no one, for the brothers who came from Macedonia supplied what I lacked. And in everything I kept myself from being a burden to you, and will keep myself so.
11:10 The truth of Christ is in me: this boasting will not be silenced in me in the regions of Achaia.
11:11 Why? Because I do not love you? God knows.
11:12 But what I do I will also continue to do, so that I may cut off the opportunity of those who desire an opportunity, so that in what they boast they may be found just as we are.
11:13 For such people are false apostles, deceitful workers, disguising themselves as apostles of Christ.
11:14 And no wonder, for Satan himself disguises himself as an angel of light.
11:15 Therefore it is no surprise if his ministers also disguise themselves as ministers of righteousness; their end will be according to their works.
11:16 Again I say, let no one think me foolish. But if you do, receive me even as a fool, so that I too may boast a little.
11:17 What I speak, I speak not according to the Lord, but as in foolishness, in this confident boasting.
11:18 Since many boast according to the flesh, I too will boast.
11:19 For you gladly bear with fools, being wise yourselves!
11:20 For you bear with it if someone enslaves you, if someone devours you, if someone takes from you, if someone exalts himself, if someone strikes you in the face.
11:21 I say this to my shame: as though we had been weak! But in whatever anyone dares—I speak in foolishness—I also dare.
11:22 Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they Abraham’s offspring? So am I.
11:23 Are they ministers of Christ? I speak as one out of his mind: I am more so—in more labors, in more imprisonments, in beatings beyond measure, often facing death.
11:24 From Jews five times I received forty lashes less one.
11:25 Three times I was beaten with rods, once I was stoned, three times I was shipwrecked; I have spent a night and a day in the deep.
11:26 On frequent journeys, in dangers of rivers, dangers of robbers, dangers from my own people, dangers from Gentiles, dangers in a city, dangers in a wilderness, dangers at sea, dangers among false brothers;
11:27 in labor and toil, in sleepless nights often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.
11:28 Apart from outward things, there is the pressure on me day by day, my concern for all the Churches.
11:29 Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn?
11:30 If I must boast, I will boast of the things of my weakness.
11:31 The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, who is blessed for the ages, knows that I am not lying.
11:32 In Damascus the ethnarch under King Aretas was guarding the city of the Damascenes to seize me,
11:33 and through a window I was lowered in a basket through the wall, and escaped his hands.

2 CORINTHIANS 12

12:1 I must boast. It is not beneficial, but I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.
12:2 I know a man in Christ. Fourteen years ago—whether in the body I do not know, or outside the body I do not know; God knows—such a one was caught up as far as the third heaven.
12:3 And I know such a man—whether in the body or apart from the body I do not know; God knows—
12:4 that he was caught up into Paradise and heard inexpressible words, which a human being is not permitted to speak.
12:5 On behalf of such a one I will boast, but on my own behalf I will not boast except in weaknesses.
12:6 For if I should desire to boast, I will not be a fool, for I will speak truth. But I refrain, lest anyone think more of me than what he sees me to be or anything he hears from me warrants.
12:7 And because of the surpassing revelations, therefore, so that I might not be exalted, a thorn was given me in the flesh, a messenger of Satan, to strike me, so that I might not be exalted.
12:8 Concerning this I appealed to the Lord three times, that it might depart from me.
12:9 And He has said to me, “My grace is sufficient for you, for power is made complete in weakness.” Therefore I will most gladly boast rather in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may pitch its tent over me.
12:10 Therefore I am content in weaknesses, in insults, in hardships, in persecutions and distresses, for Christ’s sake; for whenever I am weak, then I am strong.
12:11 I have become a fool; you compelled me. For I ought to have been commended by you, for I fell short in nothing of the exceedingly great apostles, even if I am nothing.
12:12 Indeed, the signs of the apostle were worked among you in all endurance, with signs and wonders and acts of power.
12:13 For in what were you treated as inferior to the other Churches, except that I myself did not burden you? Forgive me this wrong.
12:14 Look, for the third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not be a burden. For I seek not what is yours but you; for the children ought not to store up for the parents, but the parents for the children.
12:15 And I will most gladly spend and be spent for your souls. If I love you more abundantly, am I loved less?
12:16 But let it be so: I did not burden you. Yet, being crafty, I caught you by deceit!
12:17 Did I take advantage of you through anyone whom I sent to you?
12:18 I urged Titus, and sent the brother with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same spirit? In the same footsteps?
12:19 Have you been thinking all this time that we are defending ourselves to you? We speak before God in Christ. And all things, beloved, are for your building up.
12:20 For I fear lest somehow, when I come, I may find you not as I wish, and I may be found by you not as you wish; lest there be strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambitions, slanders, whisperings, inflations of pride, disorders;
12:21 lest, when I come again, my God may humble me before you, and I may mourn many of those who sinned before and did not repent of the impurity and sexual immorality and licentiousness they practiced.

2 CORINTHIANS 13

13:1 This is the third time I am coming to you. “By the mouth of two witnesses and three every matter will be established.”
13:2 I have warned those who sinned before and all the rest, and I warn them now while absent, as I did when present the second time: if I come again, I will not spare,
13:3 since you seek proof of Christ speaking in me. He is not weak toward you, but is powerful among you.
13:4 For indeed He was crucified in weakness, but lives by the power of God. For we too are weak in Him, but will live with Him by the power of God toward you.
13:5 Test yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; examine yourselves. Or do you not recognize this about yourselves—that Jesus Christ is in you? Unless indeed you fail the test.
13:6 But I hope you will know that we do not fail the test.
13:7 And we pray to God that you may do nothing evil, not that we may appear to pass the test, but that you may do what is honorable, even if we seem to fail it.
13:8 For we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.
13:9 For we rejoice whenever we are weak but you are strong; this we also pray for: your restoration.
13:10 For this reason I write these things while absent, so that when present I may not act sharply according to the authority the Lord gave me, for building up and not for pulling down.
13:11 Finally, brothers, rejoice, be restored, be encouraged, be of the same mind, be at peace; and the God of love and peace will be with you.
13:12 Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the saints greet you.
13:13 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.
