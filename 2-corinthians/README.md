# 2 Corinthians — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 13 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 15 October 2026. All 256 verse units received an English editorial review: 107 changed and 149 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

NA28 is the user-authorized provisional New Testament base. Focused checks of the German Bible Society’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not complete critical-text or apparatus verification. The newer Bespoke PDF’s preferred UBS6 text has not been obtained; UBS6/ECM comparison, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/NA28/2CO.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. The full displayed Greek running text was read for all 13 chapters, with focused comparison of the features named in the chapter records. Exhaustive critical-text, printed-apparatus and manuscript verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

**Chapter 1.** Preserved the repeated comfort/suffering language, the sentence of death, grace, yes/no, Amen, sealing and pledge images. Smoothed several clauses and restored modern has. At 1:12 changed inherited holiness to simplicity, following the displayed NA28 haploteti, and included its bracketed and. Retained second grace at 1:15 and our at 1:14, bracketed in NA28.

Pending: Review the simplicity/holiness variant at 1:12, the many and gift construction at 1:11, to the end at 1:13, second grace and the precise itinerary. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 2.** Preserved the repeated sorrow, forgiveness, swallowed-up, door and fragrance images. Modernized has and smoothed the testing and sincerity clauses. Retained in the presence of Christ at 2:10 and leads us in triumph at 2:14 without deciding every implication of the procession image.

Pending: Review the identity and scope of the offender passage, the emphases in 2:5, in the presence of Christ, the triumphal procession and the phrasing from God at 2:17. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 3.** Retained the letter/ink/tablet/heart images, repeated sufficiency and glory, the old/new covenant contrast, and the veil. Smoothed the ministry and glory syntax. Retained the inherited beholding as in a mirror at 3:18 and the final Lord, the Spirit construction rather than resolving them through paraphrase.

Pending: Review the exact force of ministry in 3:3, brought to an end in 3:7–14, the ambiguous unveiling construction in 3:14, the subject of turns in 3:16, beholding/reflecting and the Lord/Spirit relation in 3:18. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 4.** Preserved the treasure/container image, dying/life, affliction contrasts, inner/outer person, and lightness/weight. Rendered earthen vessels as clay jars and restored a sentence boundary after 4:7. Modernized has and smoothed illumination and manifestation. Followed displayed NA28 Jesus Christ order at 4:5, included bracketed Jesus at 4:6 and restored Lord at 4:14.

Pending: Review the god of this age phrase, name/title variants at 4:5–6 and 4:14, the paired affliction terms and the grace/thanksgiving construction at 4:15. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 5.** Preserved the mixed house/tent/clothing images, nakedness, swallowed-up mortality, pledge, home/away, new creation and reconciliation. Smoothed the judgment and modern pronoun clauses. At 5:3 followed displayed NA28 ekdusamenoi as even after being unclothed, replacing inherited having put it on. Retained new creation with supplied there is and no added all before new things. Preserved made sin rather than made sinful or sin offering.

Pending: Review the major clothed/unclothed variant and syntax in 5:3, the precise scope of Christ’s love, according to the flesh, new creation and made sin. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 6.** Preserved the repeated in/through/as contrasts, weapons and both hands, mouth/heart imagery, unequal yoke, temple, and sons and daughters. Modernized verb forms and smoothed hardships and reciprocal openness. Retained Beliar, the inherited singular collective mouth/heart and ordinary you in the quotation at 6:2 without supplying a doctrinal identification of its addressee.

Pending: Review the openness imagery and affections, exact force of unequal yoking, the quotation addressee and quotation combinations at 6:16–18. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 7.** Preserved comfort, sorrow, repentance, regret, fear/trembling and the sevenfold what sequence in 7:11. Rendered sorrow according to God as godly sorrow and smoothed surrounding clauses without removing the contrast with the sorrow of the world. Retained the bracketed for represented at 7:8 and modernized has at 7:13.

Pending: Review the scope of without regret at 7:10, defense/vindication at 7:11 and the historical identities in 7:12. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 8.** Preserved repeated grace, willingness, ability, abundance/lack and equality. Supplied gave at 8:3 to complete the English donation clause; smoothed the collection and administration syntax. Retained the NA28 love from us in you at 8:7 rather than reversing its direction, and Himself at 8:19, bracketed in the displayed text. Rendered the past giving of diligence at 8:16 with put.

Pending: Review grace/fellowship at 8:4, the love wording variant in 8:7, the readiness constructions in 8:11–12 and 8:19, epistolary past tenses and the scope of equality. The unnamed brother remains unnamed. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 9.** Preserved blessing, sowing/reaping, seed, righteousness, generosity and thanksgiving, as well as the repeated all/every sufficiency in 9:8. Modernized has/loves and smoothed supplying needs and the confession/sharing sentence. Retained for the age in the Psalm quotation and the future supply/multiply verbs.

Pending: Review blessing/extortion at 9:5–6, the Psalm wording, the confession/submission relation and the subject of glorifying in 9:13. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 10.** Preserved flesh/warfare, weapons, strongholds, pulling down, captive thoughts, building up and the repeated measure/sphere language. Smoothed the confrontation and consideration clauses. Rendered obedience of Christ as obedience to Christ while retaining the surrounding obedience/disobedience repetitions; kept the singular someone says in 10:10.

Pending: Review the imperative or alternative force of 10:7, the Christ genitive in 10:5, the reference of sphere and enlargement in 10:13–16 and rhetorical irony throughout. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 11.** Preserved the betrothal, serpent, plundering, disguise, devouring, burning and basket images; the ironic boasting and repeated dangers remain. Smoothed several clauses and completed elliptical So am I replies. Added the implicit lashes unit while retaining forty less one and every reported count. Retained and purity at 11:3, bracketed in the displayed NA28 text; kept ethnarch and Aretas.

Pending: Review the sense of simplicity/purity, the exceedingly great apostles and irony, the meaning of outward things in 11:28, ethnarch and the historical chronology. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 12.** Preserved fourteen years, the repeated uncertainty about body and the repeated God knows, Paradise, the thorn, repeated not be exalted, and spending/being spent. Modernized has, rendered the tent verb at 12:9 as pitch its tent over me and mighty works as acts of power. Kept ordinary you when the Lord addresses Paul. The inherited sentence boundary between 12:6 and 12:7 is retained provisionally; the displayed NA28 punctuation instead joins the opening revelation phrase in 12:7 to the preceding sentence.

Pending: Review the connection and punctuation of 12:6–7, the thorn without diagnosing it, the dwell/tent image, the grammar and bracketed letter at 12:15, and the rhetoric in 12:16–19. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.

**Chapter 13.** Preserved the site’s 13-verse numbering, matching the consulted NA28: the holy kiss and saints’ greeting share 13:12 and the blessing is 13:13. Smoothed warnings, weakness/power and the test/approval wordplay. Retained the witness counts and and of the displayed text, restoration/building up, holy kiss and the complete closing blessing without adding Amen.

Pending: Review the witness quotation and conjunction, the testing and approval nuances, the inclusive/exclusive force of we and you, and the final exhortations. Full UBS6/ECM and apparatus comparison, independent Greek and human read-aloud review remain pending.
