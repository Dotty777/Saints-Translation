2 Corinthians 1

Read this chapter on staging

Greeting

1:1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Timothy the brother, to the Church of God in Corinth, together with all the saints in the whole of Achaia:

1:2Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Comfort in Affliction

1:3Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort,

1:4who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those in every affliction through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

1:5For just as the sufferings of Christ abound toward us, so also our comfort abounds through Christ.

1:6Now whether we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; or whether we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which is at work as you endure the same sufferings that we also suffer.

1:7And our hope for you is firm, for we know that as you share in the sufferings, so also you share in the comfort.

1:8For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of our affliction that occurred in Asia: we were burdened far beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of living.

1:9Indeed, we ourselves had the sentence of death within us, so that we might rely not on ourselves but on God who raises the dead.

1:10He delivered us from so great a death and will deliver us; in Him we have set our hope that He will deliver us yet again,

1:11as you too help us by prayer, so that many people may give thanks on our behalf for the gift granted to us through many.

Sincerity and the Intended Visit

1:12For our boast is this, the testimony of our conscience: that in simplicity and sincerity from God, and not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, we conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you.

1:13For we write nothing to you other than what you read or also understand; and I hope that you will understand fully to the end,

1:14just as you have also recognized us in part, that we are your boast, just as you also are ours in the day of our Lord Jesus.

1:15And with this confidence I intended to come to you first, so that you might have a second grace,

1:16and to visit you on my way to Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and to be sent onward by you to Judea.

1:17Now when I intended this, was I then being fickle? Or do I plan what I plan according to the flesh, so that with me there is both “Yes, yes” and “No, no”?

God’s Yes in Christ

1:18But God is faithful: our word to you is not “Yes” and “No.”

1:19For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was proclaimed among you through us—through me and Silvanus and Timothy—did not become “Yes” and “No,” but in Him “Yes” has come to be.

1:20For however many God’s promises are, in Him is the “Yes.” Therefore also through Him is the “Amen” to God, for His glory through us.

1:21Now the One who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,

1:22who also sealed us and gave the pledge of the Spirit in our hearts.

Sparing the Community

1:23But I call God as witness against my soul that it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth.

1:24Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers for your joy, for in faith you stand.

Decision. Use pledge and retain sealing, giving, and hearts. The commercial image explains assurance without making the Spirit an impersonal object.

Comparison. Saints and DRC pledge; KJV earnest. Earnest is an old guarantee term, not merely sincerity.

2 Corinthians 2

Read this chapter on staging

2:1For I decided this for myself: not to come to you again in sorrow.

2:2For if I cause you sorrow, who then makes me glad except the one made sorrowful by me?

2:3And I wrote this very thing so that, when I came, I might not be grieved by those who ought to make me rejoice. I was confident about all of you that my joy is the joy of you all.

2:4For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you might be made sorrowful, but that you might know the abundant love I have for you.

Forgive and Reaffirm Love

2:5But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has caused sorrow not to me, but in part—to avoid pressing too heavily—to all of you.

2:6This punishment by the majority is sufficient for such a person,

2:7so that, on the contrary, you should rather forgive and comfort him, lest such a person be swallowed up by excessive sorrow.

2:8Therefore I urge you to confirm your love toward him.

2:9For I also wrote for this purpose: to see how you stood the test, whether you are obedient in everything.

2:10Now whom you forgive anything, I also forgive; for what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, was for your sake in the presence of Christ,

2:11so that we might not be taken advantage of by Satan, for we are not unaware of his designs.

Troas and the Aroma of Christ

2:12Now when I came to Troas for the good news of Christ, and a door had been opened to me in the Lord,

2:13I had no rest in my spirit because I did not find Titus my brother; but taking leave of them, I went out to Macedonia.

2:14But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ and makes known through us the aroma of the knowledge of Him in every place.

2:15For we are Christ’s fragrance to God among those being saved and among those perishing:

2:16to the one an aroma from death to death, but to the other an aroma from life to life. And who is sufficient for these things?

2:17For we are not like the many who peddle the word of God; but we speak in Christ before God, out of sincerity, as those sent from God.

2 Corinthians 3

Read this chapter on staging

A Letter on Hearts

3:1Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some do, letters of recommendation to you or from you?

3:2You are our letter, inscribed in our hearts, known and read by all people.

3:3You are clearly a letter of Christ, produced through our ministry, inscribed not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on tablets that are hearts of flesh.

3:4And we have such confidence toward God through Christ.

3:5Not that we are sufficient in ourselves to consider anything as coming from ourselves, but our sufficiency is from God,

3:6who also made us sufficient as ministers of a new covenant, not of letter but of Spirit; for the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.

The Glory of the New Ministry

3:7Now if the ministry of death, engraved in letters on stones, came in glory, so that the sons of Israel could not gaze at Moses’ face because of the glory of his face, which was being brought to an end,

3:8how much more will the ministry of the Spirit be in glory?

3:9For if there is glory in the ministry of condemnation, much more does the ministry of righteousness abound in glory.

3:10For indeed what once had glory has no glory in this respect, because of the surpassing glory.

3:11For if what is being brought to an end came through glory, how much greater is the glory of what remains!

The Unveiled Face

3:12Therefore, having such hope, we use great boldness,

3:13and are not like Moses, who put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel might not gaze at the end of what was being brought to an end.

3:14But their minds were hardened. For until this very day the same veil remains at the reading of the old covenant, not being unveiled, because in Christ it is brought to an end.

3:15But to this day, whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their heart.

3:16But whenever one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed.

3:17Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

3:18And we all, with unveiled face, beholding as in a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, as from the Lord, the Spirit.

2 Corinthians 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Light of the Good News

4:1For this reason, having this ministry, just as we received mercy, we do not lose heart.

4:2But we have renounced the secret and shameful things, not walking in cunning or tampering with the word of God, but commending ourselves to every human conscience before God by making the truth known.

4:3And even if our good news is veiled, it is veiled among those perishing,

4:4among whom the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelieving, so that the light of the good news of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, might not shine upon them.

4:5For we proclaim not ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for Jesus’ sake.

4:6For God, who said, “Light will shine out of darkness,” is the One who shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

Treasure in Earthen Vessels

4:7But we have this treasure in clay jars, so that the surpassing power may be God’s and not from us.

4:8We are afflicted in every way, but not confined; perplexed, but not utterly at a loss;

4:9persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed;

4:10always carrying in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.

4:11For we who live are always being handed over to death for Jesus’ sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.

4:12So death is at work in us, but life in you.

4:13But having the same spirit of faith, according to what is written, “I believed, therefore I spoke,” we also believe, and therefore also speak,

4:14knowing that the One who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and present us together with you.

4:15For all things are for your sake, so that grace, having increased through the many, may cause thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.

An Eternal Weight of Glory

4:16Therefore we do not lose heart; but even if our outer person is wasting away, our inner person is being renewed day by day.

4:17For the momentary lightness of our affliction produces for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all measure,

4:18as we look not at the things seen but at the things not seen; for the things seen are temporary, but the things not seen are eternal.

2 Corinthians 5

Read this chapter on staging

The Dwelling from Heaven

5:1For we know that if our earthly house, this tent, is dismantled, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens.

5:2For indeed in this we groan, longing to be clothed over with our dwelling from heaven,

5:3if indeed, even after being unclothed, we shall not be found naked.

5:4For indeed we who are in the tent groan, being burdened, because we do not wish to be unclothed but to be clothed over, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.

5:5Now the One who prepared us for this very thing is God, who gave us the pledge of the Spirit.

5:6Therefore we are always confident, knowing that while at home in the body we are away from the Lord—

5:7for we walk by faith, not by sight—

5:8we are confident, and prefer rather to be away from the body and at home with the Lord.

5:9Therefore we also make it our aim, whether at home or away, to be pleasing to Him.

5:10For we must all be revealed before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each may receive what is due for what he has done through the body, whether good or evil.

The Ministry of Reconciliation

5:11Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people; but we have been revealed to God, and I hope we have also been revealed in your consciences.

5:12We are not commending ourselves to you again, but giving you an opportunity to boast on our behalf, so that you may have an answer for those who boast in appearance and not in heart.

5:13For if we were beside ourselves, it was for God; if we are in our right mind, it is for you.

5:14For the love of Christ holds us fast, since we have judged this: that One died for all; therefore all died.

5:15And He died for all so that those who live might no longer live for themselves, but for the One who died and was raised on their behalf.

5:16So from now on we know no one according to the flesh. Even if we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we no longer know Him in that way.

5:17Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation. The old things passed away; behold, new things have come to be.

5:18And all things are from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation:

5:19namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and placing in us the word of reconciliation.

5:20Therefore we serve as ambassadors on behalf of Christ, as God appeals through us. We beg on behalf of Christ: be reconciled to God.

5:21On our behalf He made the One who knew no sin to be sin, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 Corinthians 6

Read this chapter on staging

The Acceptable Time and Faithful Service

6:1And working together, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain.

6:2For He says, “At an acceptable time I heard you, and on a day of salvation I helped you.” Look, now is a most acceptable time; look, now is a day of salvation.

6:3We give no occasion for stumbling in anything, so that the ministry may not be blamed,

6:4but in everything commend ourselves as ministers of God: in much endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,

6:5in beatings, in imprisonments, in disturbances, in labors, in sleepless nights, in fastings,

6:6in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without hypocrisy,

6:7in a word of truth, in the power of God; through the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and the left,

6:8through glory and dishonor, through bad report and good report; as deceivers, and yet truthful;

6:9as unknown, and yet recognized; as dying, and look, we live; as disciplined, and yet not put to death;

6:10as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet enriching many; as having nothing, and yet possessing everything.

Open Hearts

2 Corinthians 13

Read this chapter on staging

The Coming Visit and the Test

13:1This is the third time I am coming to you. “By the mouth of two witnesses and three every matter will be established.”

13:2I have warned those who sinned before and all the rest, and I warn them now while absent, as I did when present the second time: if I come again, I will not spare,

13:3since you seek proof of Christ speaking in me. He is not weak toward you, but is powerful among you.

13:4For indeed He was crucified in weakness, but lives by the power of God. For we too are weak in Him, but will live with Him by the power of God toward you.

13:5Test yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; examine yourselves. Or do you not recognize this about yourselves—that Jesus Christ is in you? Unless indeed you fail the test.

13:6But I hope you will know that we do not fail the test.

13:7And we pray to God that you may do nothing evil, not that we may appear to pass the test, but that you may do what is honorable, even if we seem to fail it.

13:8For we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.

13:9For we rejoice whenever we are weak but you are strong; this we also pray for: your restoration.

13:10For this reason I write these things while absent, so that when present I may not act sharply according to the authority the Lord gave me, for building up and not for pulling down.

Restoration and Blessing

13:11Finally, brothers, rejoice, be restored, be encouraged, be of the same mind, be at peace; and the God of love and peace will be with you.

13:12Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the saints greet you.

13:13The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.
