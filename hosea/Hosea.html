Hosea 1

Read this chapter on staging

The Word to Hosea Son of Beeri

1:1The word of the Lord that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

1:2The beginning of the Lord’s word to Hosea. The Lord said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a woman of prostitution and children of prostitution, for the land will utterly prostitute itself away from following the Lord.”

1:3He went and took Gomer daughter of Debelaim; she conceived and bore him a son.

Jezreel

1:4The Lord said to him, “Call him Jezreel, for in a little while I will avenge Jezreel’s blood upon Jehu’s house and put an end to the kingdom of Israel’s house.

1:5On that day I will shatter Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.”

Not Shown Mercy

1:6She conceived again and bore a daughter. He said to him, “Call her Not Shown Mercy, for I will certainly no longer show mercy to Israel’s house, but I will surely set Myself against them.

1:7But I will show mercy to Judah’s sons and save them by the Lord their God. I will not save them by bow, sword, war, chariots, horses or horsemen.”

Not My People

1:8She weaned Not Shown Mercy, conceived again and bore a son.

1:9He said, “Call him Not My People, for you are not My people, and I am not yours.”

Hosea 2

Read this chapter on staging

Like the Sand of the Sea

2:1The number of Israel’s sons was like the sand of the sea, which will not be measured or counted. In the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not My people,’ there they will be called sons of the living God.

2:2Judah’s sons and Israel’s sons will gather together, appoint one authority for themselves and come up from the land, for great is Jezreel’s day.

2:3Say to your brother, ‘My People,’ and to your sister, ‘Shown Mercy.’

Enter into Judgment with Your Mother

2:4“Enter into judgment with your mother, enter into judgment! For she is not My wife and I am not her husband. I will remove her prostitution from My face and her adultery from between her breasts,

2:5so that I may strip her naked and restore her as on the day of her birth. I will make her like a wilderness, set her like a waterless land and kill her with thirst.

2:6I will certainly show no mercy to her children, for they are children of prostitution.

2:7For their mother prostituted herself; she who bore them brought shame. She said, ‘I will follow my lovers, who give me my loaves and water, my garments and linen cloths, my oil and everything due to me.’

I Block Her Way with Thorns

2:8Therefore look, I block her way with thorns and will build up her roads, and she will certainly not find her path.

2:9She will pursue her lovers but certainly not catch them; she will seek them but certainly not find them. She will say, ‘I will go and return to my former husband, for it was better for me then than now.’

2:10She did not know that it was I who gave her grain, wine and oil and multiplied silver for her; but she made silver and gold objects for Baal.

2:11Therefore I will return and recover My grain at its season and My wine at its time; I will take away My garments and linen cloths so that they do not cover her shame.

2:12Now I will uncover her uncleanness before her lovers, and certainly no one will deliver her from My hand.

2:13I will turn away all her rejoicings, her festivals, new moons and Sabbaths, and all her festal assemblies.

2:14I will obliterate her vine and fig trees, of which she said, ‘These are my wages that my lovers gave me.’ I will make them a testimony, and the field’s beasts, heaven’s birds and the earth’s creeping things will devour them.”

2:15“I will exact vengeance upon her for the days of the Baalim, when she sacrificed to them, put on her earrings and necklaces and went after her lovers, but forgot Me,” says the Lord.

I Will Lead Her into the Wilderness

2:16“Therefore look, I am leading her astray; I will place her in a wilderness and speak to her heart,

2:17and from there I will give her her possessions and the Valley of Achor to open her understanding. There she will be humbled as in the days of her infancy and the days of her ascent from Egypt’s land.

2:18On that day,” says the Lord, “she will call Me ‘My Husband’ and no longer call Me ‘Baalim.’

2:19I will remove the names of the Baalim from her mouth, and they will certainly no longer remember their names.

2:20On that day I will establish a covenant for them with the field’s beasts, heaven’s birds and the earth’s creeping things. I will shatter bow, sword and war from the land and settle you in hope.

I Will Betroth You to Myself Forever

2:21I will betroth you to Myself forever; I will betroth you to Myself in righteousness and judgment, in mercy and compassion,

2:22and I will betroth you to Myself in faithfulness, and you will know the Lord.

2:23On that day,” says the Lord, “I will listen to heaven, and heaven will listen to the earth,

2:24and the earth will listen to grain, wine and oil, and they will listen to Jezreel.

2:25I will sow her for Myself upon the land, show mercy to Not Shown Mercy and say to Not My People, ‘You are My people.’ He will say, ‘Thou art the Lord my God!’”

Hosea 4

Read this chapter on staging

The Lord Has a Case Against the Land

4:1Hear the Lord’s word, sons of Israel, for the Lord has a case against the land’s inhabitants: there is no truth, no mercy and no recognition of God upon the land.

4:2Cursing, falsehood, murder, theft and adultery have poured over the land, and they mix bloodshed with bloodshed.

4:3Therefore the land will mourn and diminish with all who inhabit it, with the field’s beasts, the earth’s creeping things and heaven’s birds; the sea’s fish will disappear,

4:4so that no one brings a case and no one rebukes; but My people are like a priest being contradicted.

4:5You will grow weak by day, and a prophet will also grow weak with you; I likened your mother to night.

Likened to One Without Knowledge

4:6My people were likened to one without knowledge. Because you thrust away recognition, I too will thrust you away from serving Me as priest. You forgot your God’s law, and I too will forget your children.

4:7According to their multitude, so they sinned against Me; I will turn their glory into dishonor.

4:8They will eat My people’s sins and take their souls amid their injustices.

4:9It will be as with the people, so with the priest; I will exact punishment upon him for his ways and repay him for his schemes.

4:10They will eat but certainly not be filled; they prostituted themselves but will certainly not prosper, because they abandoned keeping faith with the Lord.

Prostitution, Wine and Intoxication

4:11My people’s heart welcomed prostitution, wine and intoxicating drink.

4:12They inquired by signs, and by his rods they announced things to him; they were led astray by a spirit of prostitution and prostituted themselves away from their God.

4:13On the mountaintops they sacrificed, and on the hills they offered sacrifice, beneath oak, poplar and a tree providing shade, because shelter is good. Therefore your daughters will prostitute themselves and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.

4:14I will certainly not call your daughters to account when they prostitute themselves, or your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery, for the men themselves mixed with prostitutes and sacrificed with initiates; the people who understood became entangled with a prostitute.

Israel, Do Not Be Ignorant

4:15But you, Israel, do not be ignorant; and Judah, do not enter Gilgal or go up to the house of On, and do not swear by the living Lord.

4:16For Israel has run wild like a heifer running wild. Now the Lord will pasture them like a lamb in an open place.

4:17Ephraim, a partner of idols, set stumbling blocks for himself.

4:18He chose Canaanites. They prostituted themselves thoroughly; they loved dishonor arising from their arrogance.

4:19You are a whirlwind within its wings, and they will be put to shame because of their altars.

Hosea 7

Read this chapter on staging

Ephraim's Injustice and Samaria's Wickedness

7:1when I heal Israel. Ephraim’s injustice and Samaria’s wickedness will be uncovered, because they practiced falsehood. A thief will enter upon him, a robber stripping him on his road,

7:2so that they sing together as people singing together with their heart. I remembered all their evils; now their schemes have surrounded them; they have come before My face.

7:3With their evils they gladdened kings, and with their falsehoods, rulers.

Like an Oven Burning

7:4All are adulterers, like an oven burning for the baking of something scorched by flame, from the kneading of fat until it ferments.

7:5On your kings’ days the rulers began to rage from wine; he stretched out his hand with pestilent men,

7:6because their hearts were kindled like an oven as they battered people. All night Ephraim was filled with sleep; morning came, and he was kindled like fire’s blaze.

7:7All grew hot like an oven and devoured their judges. All their kings fell; there was no one among them calling to Me.

A Cake Not Turned

7:8Ephraim mingled among his peoples; Ephraim became a cake baked in ashes and not turned.

7:9Foreigners devoured his strength, but he did not recognize it; gray hairs blossomed upon him, but he did not know it.

7:10Israel’s arrogance will be humbled before his face, yet they did not return to the Lord their God or seek Him in all these things.

7:11Ephraim was like a senseless dove without understanding; he called on Egypt, and they went to the Assyrians.

7:12Whenever they go, I will cast My net over them; I will bring them down like heaven’s birds. I will discipline them when their affliction is heard.

Woe to Them, Because They Sprang Away from Me

7:13Woe to them, because they sprang away from Me! They are miserable, because they acted impiously toward Me. I redeemed them, but they spoke falsehoods against Me.

7:14Their hearts did not cry to Me; they only howled on their beds. They cut themselves over grain and wine. They were disciplined through Me,

7:15and I strengthened their arms, but they devised evil against Me.

7:16They turned away toward nothing; they became like a drawn bow. Their rulers will fall by the sword because of their tongues’ lack of discipline. This is their contempt in Egypt’s land.

Hosea 9

Read this chapter on staging

Do Not Rejoice, Israel

9:1Do not rejoice, Israel, or be glad like the peoples, because you prostituted yourself away from your God; you loved gifts on every threshing floor of grain.

9:2Threshing floor and winepress did not know them, and wine deceived them.

9:3They did not dwell in the Lord’s land; Ephraim dwelt in Egypt, and among the Assyrians they will eat unclean things.

9:4They did not pour wine to the Lord or make it pleasant to Him. Their sacrifices are like bread of mourning to them; all who eat them will be defiled, because their loaves for their own lives will not enter the Lord’s house.

9:5What will you do on a day of assembly and on the day of the Lord’s festival?

9:6Therefore look, they will go away from Egypt’s misery; Memphis will receive them, and Machmas will bury them. Destruction will inherit their silver; thorns will be in their dwellings.

The Days of Vengeance Have Come

9:7The days of vengeance have come; the days of your repayment have come. Israel will be afflicted like the deranged prophet, a spirit-bearing man. Because your injustices were many, your madness increased.

9:8Ephraim’s watchman is with God; a prophet, a crooked trap upon all his ways. They fixed madness in the Lord’s house.
