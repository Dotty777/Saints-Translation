# 2 Kings — Bespoke Saints English Translation

All 25 chapters and the book index were saved and read back on the WordPress staging site on 14 October 2026. All 723 verse units received an English editorial review: 234 changed and 489 retained their wording. This is a provisional editorial edition; independent specialist approval remains pending.

The newer Bespoke Saints English (1).pdf governs, with accepted Genesis 1–11 as the English benchmark. It supersedes the older Founding Style Guide where their pronoun rules conflict: Thou/Thee/Thy address God directly; ordinary address uses you/your.

Rahlfs–Hanhart 2006 remains the user-authorized provisional base. Focused checks of the publisher’s Greek running text are recorded by chapter. This is an English editorial revision, not a complete critical-text or apparatus verification. Göttingen, full apparatus, independent Greek and human read-aloud reviews remain pending. The [German Bible Society running text](https://www.die-bibel.de/en/bible/LXX/2KI.1) was consulted in the scopes specified in source-checks.json. Only the selected passages named in those records were freshly consulted; complete Greek verification is not claimed. Existing textual and reception records are historical evidence, not a fresh verification claim. No manuscript-image inspection or independent scholarly approval is claimed.

## Chapter decisions and review queue

Pending: Independent review of Baal Fly, the historical present and source-specific accession notice.

**Chapter 2.** Modernized requests, ordinary address and verbs; rendered the repeated life oath as 'as you live'. Retained as into heaven, singular horseman, Appho, the first unsuccessful water strike, spirit wording, small children and forty-two.

Pending: Independent review of Appho, tumult/as into heaven, the spirit expression and source variants.

**Chapter 3.** Modernized ordinary speech; retained noqed, at the rising, possessions, source figures, the past 'had reigned' and 'great regret' without harmonizing the account.

Pending: Specialist review of noqed and the provisioning phrase, possessions, the son's reign tense and great regret.

**Chapter 4.** Modernized ordinary requests, verbs and address; rendered the life oath as 'as you live' and 'cook a cooked dish' as 'prepare a cooked dish'. Retained interest, the living-season expression, the woman's continued speech at 4:25, seven bendings and arioth.

Pending: Independent review of interest, season alive, 4:25's speaker boundary, the resuscitation actions and plant terminology.

**Chapter 5.** Retained feminine reporting subject, the great-word statement, red earth, repeated Remman references, debratha and dark place; modernized requests and ordinary verbs.

Pending: Independent review of the female referent, propitious wording, debratha and dark place; complete apparatus comparison.

**Chapter 6.** Kept direct prayer Thee in 6:18 and 6:20, ordinary you elsewhere, Elmoni, singular fiery chariot, fifty-shekel price and the king's negative wish. Modernized requests and finite verbs.

Pending: Independent review of Elmoni, the captive/striking clause and the king's negative wish; no harmonization of the famine price.

**Chapter 7.** Modernized ordinary address and the request in 7:13. Retained repeated price declarations, floodgates, lawlessness, two horse riders and the messenger in 7:17.

Pending: Independent review of the negative question, lawlessness and the rider/messenger wording.

**Chapter 8.** Modernized ordinary address and requests; retained manaa, machma, the second-person death prediction, unnamed agents and compressed face/shame wording, and king of the foreigners.

**Chapter 9.** Modernized ordinary address and please requests; kept garem, distinct driver/Jehu roles, the ambiguous deranged subject, Naboth's sons, third-person repayment and traces of hands.

Pending: Independent review of garem, the driving clause, 9:32's address and traces of hands; historical notes on 9:13, 20 consulted.

**Chapter 10.** Modernized finite verbs and possessives, including My in God's self-reference. Preserved Appho, Mesthaal, city of Baal's house, singular/plural pillars, mouth-to-mouth image and golden heifers.

Pending: Independent review of bows, the grave episode and the unusual succession clause; complete apparatus review.

**Chapter 21.** Modernized spoke/has/says and divine My. Kept plural sons, knowing ones, alabaster jar, measurement/weight and the repeated wiping image.

Pending: Independent review of divinatory terminology and the vessel/measurement images.

Pending: Independent review of monetary verbs, masena and prophetic discourse connection; full apparatus review.

Pending: Specialist review of 23:10's purpose/description, 23:11's title syntax and 23:35's assessment sentence; historical notes consulted. No negative was invented.

**Chapter 24.** Modernized spoke and shuts. Preserved repeated Joakim for father and son, Maththanias as his son, occupational shuts-up wording and the deportation figures.

Pending: Independent review of source names, kinship and occupational terminology; historical note on 24:17 consulted.

Pending: Independent review of chief cook, gabin/iamin/chothar/sabacha, second-rank wording and elliptical victims clause; historical notes on 25:10, 25 consulted.
